# Pentagram PET (Star Cross-Section Fibre) 五角星形截面 PET

> **Definition 定义**：A one-component polyester staple fibre whose cross-section is a **five-pointed star (pentagram)**, creating five open axial grooves that act as continuous capillary rails in a carded, air-through-bonded web.（单组分聚酯短纤，截面为**五角星形**，五个开口轴向沟槽在梳理—穿透热风粘合纤网中形成连续毛细导轨。）

## Why it is interesting 为何受关注

The star section maximises wettable surface and capillary edge count per gram. Mechanistically three things are claimed for it in a 25 gsm ATB liner:
星形截面把单位克重的可润湿表面积与毛细边缘数量最大化。在 25 gsm ATB 面层中，它被赋予三条机理：

1. **Capillary rails（毛细导轨）** — open grooves along the fibre act as low-volume liquid pathways once a hydrophilic finish lowers the contact angle; this favours spreading over local pooling.
2. **MD bias（机向偏置）** — carded webs already have partial machine-direction orientation; adding profiled PET to the second card can bias lateral urine distribution along MD.
3. **Loft support（蓬松支撑）** — PET is stiffer than PE/PET ES at bonding temperature, so a moderate addition helps preserve void volume under light pressure.

## The governing constraint 核心约束

> **One-component PET is not an ES binder.** 单组分 PET 不是 ES 粘结剂。

PE-sheath air-through bonding temperature does not bond PET; every bond point must still come from the ES fraction. Above ~15% total add-on, the risk shifts from useful capillary lanes to lint, tensile loss, rougher hand, rewet and process instability.
PE 皮层的热风粘合温度无法粘合 PET，所有粘合点仍须由 ES 组分提供。总添加超过约 15% 后，收益从「有用的毛细通道」转向起毛、强度损失、手感变粗、回渗与工艺不稳定。

## Add-on and denier readout 添加量—旦数读数

| Level 添加量 | Expected effect 预期效果 | Decision 决策 |
|---|---|---|
| 5% | Low-risk, modest MD gain | Include 纳入 |
| 10% | Best balance; strong MD gain with manageable risk | **Primary candidate 首选** |
| 15% | High MD gain; monitor rewet / lint / bond loss | Stress point 压力测试点 |
| 20% | Performance plateau; quality risk rises | Do not launch 不上线 |

Denier (at 10% add-on): **2D** raises fibre count and surface area but can reduce pore size, slow drainage under repeated insults and raise wetback risk; **3D is the best first denier** for a 25 gsm liner (good groove density, acceptable permeability, manageable tactile/bond risk); **4D** is safer for permeability and dryness but has lower capillary count per gram and may feel coarser.
旦数（10% 添加时）：**2D** 提高纤维数与表面积，但会缩小孔径、在重复冲击下降慢排水速率并抬高回渗风险；**3D 是 25 gsm 面层的最佳首试旦数**；**4D** 透气与干爽更稳，但单位克重毛细数量更低、手感可能更粗。

## Lessons learned 经验教训

> ✅ **Validation（2026-09-29）**：本批两份来源在同一对象上给出**方向相反的建议**——[[Pentagram PET Fiber Add-On for 25 gsm ATB Diaper Liner (Deep Dive)]] 以模拟为基础推荐 10% 五角星形（3D、仅下层）作为首选试点；[[Pentagram-Free Shaped PET Alternatives for 25 gsm Diaper Topsheet]] 从挤出/拉伸工艺出发判定五角星形**不适合作为首个商用截面**，建议改走圆角三叶形（15 wt%）或圆角四沟槽（12 wt%）。两者**不矛盾但取舍不同**：前者评"配方性能窗口"，后者评"截面可制造性"。本页并列登记，**不做取舍**；最终结论须由产线纺丝 + 转化 + 尿裤液体管理验证锁定。

- **要点**：五角星形的价值全在**沟槽几何是否被保住**——供应商批次若把星形塌成圆角/融合叶瓣，功能即归零；「旦数合格」不等于「截面合格」。
- **风险**：尖角截面带来的喷丝板压降、尖端损伤与拉伸不稳定属**工艺风险**，不会在实验室液体测试中暴露。
- **待补 `[待验证]`**：五角星形的供货可得性与价格带；量产级截面保真度实测数据。
