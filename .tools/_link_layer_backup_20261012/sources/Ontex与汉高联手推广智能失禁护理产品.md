# Ontex与汉高联手推广智能失禁护理产品

Ontex, Henkel Drive Roll Out of Smart Incontinence Product

Ontex与汉高 (Henkel) 联手推广智能失禁护理产品

Orizon eases workload in long-term care setting and offers residents greater comfort

Orizon 减轻了长期护理机构的工作负荷，并为居民提供更高的舒适度。

Henkel´s business segment for Digital Hygiene Solutions and absorbent products manufacturer Ontex are jointly driving the European rollout of the smart incontinence solution Orizon. This innovation significantly eases the daily workload in long-term care settings and provides residents of healthcare institutions like nursing homes and hospitals with greater comfort. The partners are combining their respective strengths: Ontex as an experienced manufacturer of hygiene products, and Henkel with its long-standing expertise in printed electronics and successful global implementations. Together, both companies share a common goal: to relieve professional caregivers and create more time for high-quality care.

汉高（Henkel）数字卫生解决方案部门与吸水用品制造商 Ontex 正在联手推动智能失禁解决方案 Orizon 在欧洲的推广。这一创新显著减轻了长期护理机构的日常工作负担，并为养老院、医院等医疗机构的居住者提供了更高的舒适度。合作伙伴双方各取所长：Ontex 拥有丰富的卫生用品制造经验，而汉高则在印刷电子技术及全球化项目实施方面拥有长期积累。双方的目标一致：减轻专业护理人员的压力，为高质量护理争取更多时间。

Orizon was developed by Ontex specifically for the needs of long-term care in close collaboration with caregivers. The solution automatically detects moisture in the incontinence product and displays the optimal time for a change via an intuitive app. Tasks that currently require routine checks, nighttime rounds and extensive experience are substantially simplified: caregivers receive a clear overview of when a change is truly necessary — and when it is not. This reduces unnecessary interruptions and provides noticeably more peace and security for everyone involved. Additionally, the reduction in changes has a positive impact on cost and waste reduction.

Orizon 是由 Ontex专门针对长期护理需求，并与护理人员紧密协作开发的。该方案能自动检测失禁用品中的水分，并通过直观的应用程序显示最佳更换时间。这使得原本需要例行检查、夜间巡视和依赖丰富经验的任务大为简化：护理人员可以清晰地判断何时真正需要更换。这减少了不必要的干扰，为所有相关人员带来了更多的安宁与安全感。此外，减少更换次数对降低成本和减少浪费也产生了积极影响。

The effects on day-to-day operations are clearly measurable: care facilities can save an average of around eight working hours per resident per month — time that directly benefits quality of care. At the same time, material consumption can be reduced by up to 20 percent because incontinence products are changed based on actual need. Changing too early leads to unnecessary waste, while changing too late can result in leakages that require significant additional effort: changing clothes, washing, cleaning, and replacing bedding all take at least 30 minutes of work — and add stress for residents. Orizon helps reduce such situations significantly. Residents are woken less frequently at night, preventing unnecessary disturbances and protecting their dignity.

其对日常运营的改善是显而易见的：护理机构平均每月每位居民可节省约 8 个工作小时，这些时间可以直接用于提升护理质量。同时，由于根据实际需求进行更换，材料消耗可降低高达 20%。过早更换会导致不必要的浪费，而更换过晚则可能导致渗漏，从而引发大量额外工作：更换衣服、清洗、清洁和更换床单等至少需要 30 分钟，且会增加居民的压力。Orizon 有助于显著减少此类情况，居民在夜间被惊醒的频率降低，防止了不必要的干扰并维护了他们的尊严。

The technology is based on a conductive, graphite-based, and skin-friendly ink developed by Henkel, which is printed on the exterior of the incontinence product as part of the sensor system. A reusable clip-on wearable is connected to this sensor element to detect moisture and the wearer’s position. These data are securely transmitted to an app or web interface. Caregivers can check the status of residents at any time — without having to enter each room.

该技术基于汉高（Henkel）开发的一种导电、石墨基且亲肤的油墨，这种油墨作为传感器系统的一部分印刷在失禁用品的外层。一个可重复使用的夹扣式可穿戴设备与该传感器元件连接，用于检测水分和佩戴者的体位。这些数据会被安全地传输到应用程序或网络界面。护理人员可以随时查看居住者的状态，而无需进入每个房间。

“Orizon gives caregivers a measure of relief and at the same time improves the care situation for residents,” says Ulrich Wagner, business development manager, Digital Hygiene at Henkel. “It’s a technology that doesn’t replace but supports — helping to use valuable time exactly where it’s needed most.”

“Orizon 为护理人员提供了一定程度的解脱，同时也改善了居住者的护理状况，”汉高数字卫生业务发展经理 Ulrich Wagner 表示，“这项技术不是为了取代人工，而是提供支持——帮助将宝贵的时间精准投入到最需要的地方。”

Laurent Nielly, CEO at Ontex, adds: “As a leading provider of hygiene solutions, we know how essential reliable and practical tools are for professional caregivers. By combining our expertise as a leader in Adult Incontinence products with Henkel’s proven implementation experience, we can scale this solution across Europe. We share many values and aspirations with the Henkel team and we both have a deep commitment to quality and safety for our customers and dignity and comfort for their patients.”

Ontex 首席执行官 Laurent Nielly 补充道：“作为领先的卫生解决方案供应商，我们深知可靠且实用的工具对专业护理人员是多么至关重要。通过将我们在成人失禁用品领域的领先专业知识与汉高成熟的实施经验相结合，我们可以将这一解决方案推广至全欧洲。我们与汉高团队拥有许多共同的价值观和抱负，并且都致力于为客户提供高品质与安全性，为患者带来尊严与舒适。”
