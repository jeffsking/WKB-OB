#   A Closer Look at Diaper Fluff Pulp

#   Fibers:

$\bullet$ Low cost versus synthetics

$\bullet$ Naturally wettable

$\bullet$ Average fiber length 3-5 mm (Long)

$\bullet$ 30-40-micron fiber diameter (Fat)

$\bullet$ Fiber absorbent capacity of .5 to 1 g/g

$\bullet$ Fiber becomes less stiff when wet

#   Air-formed Composite:

$\bullet$ Highly Wettable

$\bullet$ Absorbent capacity in fibers and interstitial space mean a total of 8 g/g benefit in composites

$\bullet$ Structure may collapse when wet

$\bullet$ Relatively low permeability compared to surge
