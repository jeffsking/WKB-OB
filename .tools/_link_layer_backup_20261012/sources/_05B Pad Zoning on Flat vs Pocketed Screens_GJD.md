#   Technology Differences

#   POCKETED SCREENS

$\bullet$ Control basis weight ratio and target zone capacity with screen design and scarfing

$\bullet$ Very sensitive – 0.25mm scarfing adjustment can mean 0.10 change in basis weight ratio

$\bullet$ Adjustment possible within limits set by pocket depth (pockets designed too deep to ensure ability to achieve target basis weight)

#   Zoned SEGMENTS

![](1/parseimages/figure0c00713aa32d40e99a5b491266700850_0_6.png)
$\bullet$ Control basis weight ratio and target zone capacity with segment design

- Less sensitive to process changes

- Adjustments often require new segments with a new pattern design

#   Technology Differences

#   Pocketed SCREENS

$\bullet$ High equipment cost
  - $50,000

$\bullet$ Grade change screens & segments

$\bullet$ Good for low SAM %
  - <50% SAM

$\bullet$ Scarfing damages SAM

$\bullet$ Metal contour segments

#   Form-On Screen

![](1/parseimages/figureb37daf3f7305474ca85fb35c494fdc56_0_8.png)
$\bullet$ Second generation of design

$\bullet$ Conveyor belt material used for segments

$\bullet$ Commercially used globally
