# 芯吸效应：水为什么自己往细缝里钻

## Summary 摘要

This article (材料有门道, 2026-09-07) gives a thermodynamics-and-hydrodynamics account of wicking in fibrous fabrics. Wicking is not "the fibre sucking water in" but spontaneous lowering of interfacial free energy inside narrow channels. It is governed by two variables — pore radius **r** (which sets capillary pressure) and contact angle **θ** (which sets wettability) — and these two act in **opposite directions** on "how high" versus "how fast". （中文对照：本文从界面热力学与流体动力学出发解释纤维集合体的芯吸行为，其控制变量为毛细半径与接触角，二者对"爬升高度"与"芯吸速率"的影响方向相反。）

The article proceeds from Young / Young–Laplace / Jurin / Lucas–Washburn relations to real-fabric effects (bimodal pores, dynamic contact angle, contact-angle hysteresis, Wenzel roughness, Concus–Finn groove condition), then to fibre-level moisture retention (cotton vs polyester) and finally to the GB/T 21655 quick-dry test standard. （中文对照：文章从基本方程推进到真实织物的双孔结构、动态接触角、滞后、粗糙度放大与沟槽 Concus–Finn 条件，再落到纤维本体吸湿与国标测试。）

## Key Equations & Numbers 关键公式与数字

| Quantity 量 | Relation 关系 | Implication 含义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Capillary pressure 毛细压力 | ΔP = γlv·cosθ/r (round tube); ΔP = γlv·cosθ·(C/A) general | θ<90° ⇒ suction; θ>90° ⇒ repulsion (water-repellent finishing) |
| Balance height 平衡高度 (Jurin) | h = 2γlv·cosθ/(ρ·g·r) | h ∝ 1/r — finer pore climbs higher |
| Wicking rate 芯吸速率 (Washburn) | L = √(γlv·r·cosθ·t / 2η) | dL/dt ∝ r — coarser pore wicks faster |
| Tortuous fabric 曲折织物 | L² ∝ γlv·reff·cosθ·t /(η·τ²) | twist/organisation affect reff and τ competitively |
| Dynamic angle 动态接触角 | θd rises with Ca = ηU/γ (Voinov–Hoffman, ~Ca^1/3) | early-stage L²∝t deviation |
| Roughness 粗糙度 (Wenzel) | cosθ* = rf·cosθ | hydrophilic⇒more hydrophilic; hydrophobic⇒more hydrophobic |
| Groove/wedge 沟槽 (Concus–Finn) | θ + α < 90° | geometry relaxes the surface-chemistry requirement |

Design trade-off table (from the article) — decreasing r raises capillary pressure (∝1/r) but raises flow resistance more (∝1/r²), so height rises while rate falls; decreasing θ raises both height and rate with no added resistance. （中文对照：减小半径提高毛细压力却牺牲速率；减小接触角同时改善高度与速率，是"不引入额外阻力"的优选变量。）

## Relevance to Absorbent Hygiene 对卫材的吸收相关性

- **Differential capillary effect 差动毛细效应**：细孔吸、粗孔送 —— directly relevant to acquisition-layer (ADL) vs core pore design, and to unidirectional top-sheet concepts.
- **Fibre swelling harms transport 纤维溶胀损害输运**：cotton swells >20% laterally in diameter when wet, narrowing channels — the same mechanism underpins why cellulose fluff/ SAP swelling closes pore networks in absorbent cores.
- **Wet-out vs dry-out 吸得进 vs 干得出**：high wicking height with low drying rate is the common failure mode — a reminder that acquisition and desorption must be evaluated as separate stages (links to rewet/desorption work in this KB).
