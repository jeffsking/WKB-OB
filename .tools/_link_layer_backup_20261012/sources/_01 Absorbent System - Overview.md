#   Absorbent system comprised of:

$\bullet$ Liner: Body-side material that covers the internal components of the diaper

$\bullet$ Surge - Acquisition & Distribution Layer (ADL): Lofty material under the liner to help fluid intake and transfer to the absorbent pad (LM+ NB-2 has a textured surge on top of the liner)

$\bullet$ Core Wrap/Spacer Layer: Wrap around the pad for help with skin health, fluid intake, and outer cover dampness

$\bullet$ Absorbent Pad: Made of SAM (superabsorbent material) and fluff (pulp) to absorb and distribute urine

#   Surge

* Fluid intake and dryness

- Resilient

- Fast intake and quick desorption to absorbent core

- Little/no retention capability

* Normally Through Air BCW (TABCW) or BCW

- Staple fibers (1-3" long)

- Primarily synthetic fibers (Polyester, Polypropylene, Bicomponent)

- Can contain natural fibers (cotton, wool)

- Fibers treated for wettability and/or bonding

$\bullet$ 50 – 130 gsm for BCC product forms

$\bullet$ Ships in Spools

$\bullet$ Internally and externally produced

- Berkeley, KC Mexico

- Shalag, TEXTOR, Freudenberg, PGI, Tenotex

$\bullet$ Recommended storage limit of ~1 year

#   Wrapsheet/Spacer Layer

* SAM containment and process aid

- Can aid in transferring the absorbent off the drum in 'form-on substrate' processes

* Minimal basis weight

- Provides wet and dry integrity to pad
