#   Intake Urine at the Rate of Insult:

#   Liquid Penetration

Absorbent materials should allow urine to enter quickly over a wide range of consumer conditions (velocity of insult, volumetric rate, user position, multiple insults, etc).

![](1/parseimages/figureb0595e08ba674d5daeeb885fb4561409_0_6.png)
![Kimberly-Clark Corporation\nConfidential\n](1/parseimages/figurea6b5a8e8ad47444b980a359a45724072_0_7.png)
#   Void Volume

There should be enough accessible void volume during the delivery of the insult to accommodate each consumer representative urination.

#   Temporary Containment of Urine:

#   Bucket Volume

There should be enough containment space above the body side absorbent materials to temporarily hold and contain urine in various product positions until it is absorbed.

![](1/parseimages/figure04a5e388b04a49308ad662449d116347_0_13.png)
#   Effective Barrier System

Material properties, placement, and adhesion to other components should prevent uncontrolled urine from escaping the product via a path of least resistance (tunnels, channels, holes, gutters, seams, etc).

#   Lock-up Urine:

#   Functional Capacity

There should be enough available capacity in the insult and surrounding area to lock-up urine in various consumer representative conditions pressures.

![](1/parseimages/figure6b5a0e0de99c4e48bbe924161b701aaa_0_19.png)
![](1/parseimages/figure8cf7d42573564096846168652219065a_0_20.png)
#   Design and Quality Considerations:

#   Target Zone

There should be effective positioning of the intake, absorbent, and barrier systems to adequately surround the gemitals. Consumer anthropometrics and product dimensions must be considered to ensure effective target zone positioning.

![](1/parseimages/figure406d60708e8d42d6a6dc7e3727a82626_0_24.png)
#   Material Variability/Process Capability

For each design principle, the variability and capability of the materials and process should be considered during development to ensure that a reliable commercial execution is delivered (example: through roll surge thickness, web weave to maintain needed spacing, treatment uniformity across deck, etc).
