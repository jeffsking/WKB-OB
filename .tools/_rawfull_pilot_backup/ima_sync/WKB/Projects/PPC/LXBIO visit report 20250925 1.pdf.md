# PPC-P price index and cost related to scale

## Some rough discussion on PPC-P pricing & cost

● Lianxin suggested price for PPC-P in 2025 is at RMB15 to 15.5 per kg once 25,000 ton capacity Taixing facility launched production (25 kg/ bag & 800 kg / bag). No reference in USD yet due to VAT rebate calculation.

· USD price will be ready by early 2026.

· By Mid 2027, once Zibo 40,000 ton capacity released for manufacturing, their target price for sell is around RMB 13.5 to 14 per kg

● If market has demand for PPC-P and they can launch 200,000 ton co-polymerization line by end of 2030, they will decrease the price of PPC-P to RMB10 per kg around.

● Just from raw material cost standpoint, including Co2 gas, PO, PA consumption and catalyst used, the cost per kg of PPC is around RMB 8, but they need to have room to amortize on capex and overheads.

· All price mentioned above includes VAT tax in China

According to Lianxin, the special Non-metal based catalyst they used for co-polymerization, they owned the patent and keep the catalyst production in house, where they think building up their competitive edges to keep the stabilization of PPC-P structure.

# Engineering modification of PPC-P with PBAT

Majority of current research from Lianxin is focused on engineering modification of PPC-P with PBAT

PPC-P + PBAT resin can be home composable

Blending process can be adding up to 35% of PPC-P into PBAT, which successfully has PPCT resin for fiber grade resin (reference price of PBAT : RMB10 per kg)

$$ \text{Assuming 65\%} \times \text{RMB 10 PBAT} + \text{35\%} \times \text{RMB 15 PPC-P} = \text{RMB11.75 per kg} $$

Copolymer research of PBAT / PPC-C is in lab test and can have 50%:50% PPCT resin for different features of fiber

Lianxin has strong interest to develop the Spunbond resin base on PPC-P/ PBAT copolymer. In case KC has special feature request, would like to take instruction from KC at beginning on what is a must list for polymers.

Blow film and casting film grade resin with 10% to 20% PPC-P + 80% to 90% PBAT is ready for agriculture field film. Research on adding CaCo3 powder is undergoing for breathable film resin development

Windeed film is working with Lianxin for hygiene breathable film resin development
