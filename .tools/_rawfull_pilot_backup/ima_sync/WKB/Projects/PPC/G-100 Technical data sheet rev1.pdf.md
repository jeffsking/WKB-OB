# Technical Data Sheet

MXD™ G-100

G-100 is biodegradable Polyglycolic acid resin, can be directly used in injection molding and extrusion processing. It can also be blended and modified with other degradable materials to enhance the mechanical strength, barrier performance and degradability of the modified materials.

## TECHNICAL DATA:

|Item|Test method|Standard|
|---|---|---|
|Moisture content, ppm|Karl-Fischer method|≤300|
|Density, g/cm³|ISO1183|1.5~1.6|

## PACKING & STORAGE:

This product is sealed in an aluminum foil bag. It is recommended to store it in a warehouse environment below 25°C.

## DRYING & PROCESSING:

G-100 is sensitive to moisture and needs to be dried before processing. It is recommended to use dehumidifying drying equipment or vacuum drying equipment for drying treatment. The drying temperature should be 120°C and the drying time is 4 hours.

# BIODEGRADATION:

G-100 exhibits excellent degradation performance and can be degraded in various environments, such as industrial composting, home composting, soil burial, and marine environments. G-100 has obtained certificates related to industrial composting and home composting.
