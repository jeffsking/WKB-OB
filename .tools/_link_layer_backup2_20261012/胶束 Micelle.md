# 胶束 Micelle

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 3 citing sources. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

Surfactant molecules above the CMC self-assemble into aggregates (micelles). 定义待补（引用来源未给出明确定义）。中文：超过临界胶束浓度后，表面活性剂分子自组装形成的聚集体。

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

- **Wetting dose window**: dose surfactant near CMC, never above it — extra surfactant above CMC is spent on micelles that cannot wet, a direct quantifiable formulation-cost overrun（源：[[The Role of Surfactants]]）.
- **Emulsion polymerization locus**: micelles are the main polymerization site, not monomer droplets; one growing radical per micelle gives low termination probability (high MW) while huge micelle numbers give high total rate; monomer is supplied droplet → water phase → micelle（源：[[Seymour Polymer Chemistry Ch08 Free Radical Chain Polymerization]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

Micelle formation sets the ceiling for useful wetting-agent dosing in absorbent core / topsheet treatments: above CMC the added surfactant forms micelles instead of improving wetting（源：[[The Role of Surfactants]]）.
