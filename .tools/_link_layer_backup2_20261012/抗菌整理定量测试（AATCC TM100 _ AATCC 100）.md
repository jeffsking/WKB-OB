# 抗菌整理定量测试（AATCC TM100 _ AATCC 100）

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 4 citing sources. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

The **quantitative test for antibacterial finishes on textile materials** issued by **AATCC** (American Association of Textile Chemists and Colorists). **AATCC 100** and **AATCC TM100-2019** are the same method family; the method quantifies antibacterial activity by inoculation, incubation, plate count and reduction %（源：[[AATCC 100 抗菌整理定量测试方法]]、[[AATCC TM100-2019 抗菌整理测试]]）.

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

- **Quantitative reduction measurement.** Finished textile is inoculated with test bacteria → incubated → plate-counted → bacterial reduction / survival is computed（源：[[AATCC 100 抗菌整理定量测试方法]]、[[AATCC TM100-2019 抗菌整理测试]]）.
- Both source files are standards-catalogue entries; the detailed method text is not included, so the mechanism described here is limited to the catalogue-level procedure（源：[[AATCC 100 抗菌整理定量测试方法]]、[[AATCC TM100-2019 抗菌整理测试]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- Used to assess the antibacterial finishing of hygiene fabrics and nonwovens (e.g. an antibacterial topsheet treatment)（源：[[AATCC 100 抗菌整理定量测试方法]]）.
- **Claim-risk lesson (chitosan).** Antibacterial rate and wash durability are high-risk claim areas for hygiene products and must be re-checked with a quantitative method of the AATCC 100 class — never taken directly from supplier numbers（源：[[Chitosan Fibre（壳聚糖纤维）]]、[[壳聚糖及壳聚糖纤维技术与应用总结报告]]）.
