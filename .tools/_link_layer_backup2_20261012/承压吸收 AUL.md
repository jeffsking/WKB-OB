## Definition 定义

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

AUL appears in [[Fluid Absorption in High Bulk Nonwovens]] as one of the concept cluster for high-bulk nonwoven absorption, together with absorption core, ADL, fluff pulp, SAP, Specific Air Volume, T/W thickness per gram, structural constant K, and rewet; the GATS gravimetric absorption test is the associated method in the same chapter（源：[[Fluid Absorption in High Bulk Nonwovens]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

Pressure during wear reduces the absorbent capacity of SAP in the core; AUL-type metrics capture that in-use behaviour for core formulation（关联概念来自 [[AUL 加压吸水率]] 与 [[Fluid Absorption in High Bulk Nonwovens]]）.
