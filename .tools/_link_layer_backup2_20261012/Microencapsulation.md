# Microencapsulation 微胶囊化

> **Definition 定义**：The encapsulation of an active chemistry (leuco dye + developer + solvent, a phase-change material, a fragrance, a conductive filler, etc.) inside a micron-scale shell — the **shared enabling technology** behind every chromic indicator route, and the actual axis of supplier differentiation.（把活性化学物质（leuco 染料+显色剂+溶剂、相变材料、香精、导电填料等）包封在微米级壳层内的技术——所有显色指示路线的**共同使能技术**，也是供应商之间真正的差异轴。）

## Why it decides the outcome 为何它决定结果

> **Microencapsulation decides colour stability, migration resistance and printability — the actual differentiator between suppliers, not the colour chemistry itself. Supplier selection should weight encapsulation maturity, not just colour range.**
> 决定色稳定性、抗迁移性与印刷适性的，是微胶囊化而非色化学本身——这才是供应商之间真正的差异。选型应评估**包封成熟度**，而非只看色域。

Four consequences follow directly:
1. **Reversibility is an encapsulation design choice** — hot-melt wetness glue is irreversible; a custom microcapsule can be engineered reversible for "real-time wetness level" cues.（可逆性是包封设计的选择：热熔尿显胶不可逆，而定制微胶囊可做成可逆以支持"实时湿量"提示。）
2. **Shell integrity maps to process risk** — in the PCM case, shell fragility causes rupture, lot variance, SAP contamination and wider process-control limits.（壳层完整性对应工艺风险：在相变材料案例中，壳脆导致破裂、批次波动、SAP 污染与控制限放宽。）
3. **Migration is the hard floor** — no bleed, no stain, no back-transfer, whether for thermochromic or hydrochromic layers.（迁移是硬底线：无论温敏或湿敏层，均须无渗色、无沾污、无回渗。）
4. **It adds a supply-chain tier** — specialty encapsulation brings a fragile, inventory- and QA-burdened tier into the chain, with petro-shell microplastic implications for PCM systems.（它增加一层供应链：专用封装环节脆弱、带库存与 QA 负担；相变体系的石化壳层还带来微塑料议题。）

## Lessons learned 经验教训

> ✅ **Validation（2026-09-29）**：本批两份来源在"微胶囊化是共同使能技术、且是供应商真实的差异轴"上互证（[[Thermochromic & Hydrochromic Materials for Diapers (Feasibility Review v3)]] 明确"Microencapsulation decides … the actual differentiator"；[[PCM-Free Thermoregulation with BNC Nanofiber Network]] 以壳脆性、批次波动、泄漏与 QA 负担论证"微胶囊税"）。同一技术在温敏/湿敏路线是**收益来源**，在调温路线则是**成本来源**——两页并列登记，不做取舍。

- **要点**：评估供应商时应把**包封成熟度**置于色域之上；RFQ 必须索取化学体系等级、添加量、可萃取物、气味/VOC 声明与迁移数据。
- **风险**：壳层破损是隐性失效——它污染 SAP、放宽控制限，而不一定立刻表现为颜色异常。
- **待补 `[待验证]`**：可逆微胶囊在洗涤/摩擦/长期储存下的循环寿命；低甲醛牌号的第三方检测口径。
