# Bacterial Cellulose Aerogel Drying（细菌纤维素气凝胶干燥）

## Definition 定义

Bacterial cellulose aerogel drying is the controlled removal of liquid from a bacterial cellulose (BC，细菌纤维素) hydrogel while managing capillary stress, ice templating, fiber bonding, and pore preservation to obtain a target thermal–mechanical property set.  
（中文：细菌纤维素气凝胶干燥是在液体脱除过程中控制毛细应力、冰模板、纤维结合与孔结构保持，以获得目标热—力性能组合的过程。）

## Mechanism and Structure 机理 / 结构拆解

1. **Capillary-stress route 毛细应力路线**: ambient, hot-air, and oven drying pass through a liquid–gas interface; capillary pressure can pull nanofibers together, increase density, and reduce swelling or gas permeability.
2. **Sublimation route 升华路线**: freeze-drying first immobilizes water as ice and then removes it by sublimation. Ice-crystal size and orientation template the final pore architecture, so freezing rate and direction matter.
3. **Critical-point route 临界点路线**: supercritical CO₂ drying suppresses the liquid–gas interface and can retain very fine pores, but requires pressure equipment and a more demanding process.
4. **Volumetric-heating route 体积加热路线**: microwave drying converts absorbed electromagnetic energy into internal heat, shortening drying time and promoting more uniform heating than surface convection in the cited film systems.
5. **Coupled variables 耦合变量**: bacterial strain, culture mode, crystallinity, fiber alignment, additives, oxidation, crosslinking, and post-treatment can reinforce or reverse an apparent drying-method effect.

## Selection Logic 选型逻辑

| Target 目标 | Preferred evidence direction 证据指向 | Main trade-off 主要权衡 |
|---|---|---|
| High porosity / insulation 高孔隙与隔热 | Freeze-drying or supercritical CO₂ drying | Equipment, cycle cost, and process control（设备、周期成本与过程控制） |
| Dense film / tensile response 致密膜与拉伸表现 | Oven or hot-air drying may be advantageous in some systems | Lower swelling and pore collapse risk（吸胀下降与孔塌陷风险） |
| Fast cycle 快速周期 | Microwave drying | Scale-up uniformity and dielectric response（放大均匀性与介电响应） |
| Directional heat transport 定向导热 | Bidirectional freezing plus freeze-drying | Architecture complexity（结构构建复杂度） |

**Key conclusion 关键结论：Drying is a structure-forming operation rather than a simple water-removal step.（干燥不是单纯除水，而是结构成形步骤。）**
