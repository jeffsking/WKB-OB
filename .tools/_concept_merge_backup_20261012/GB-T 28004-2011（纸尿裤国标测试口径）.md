# GB-T 28004-2011（纸尿裤国标测试口径）

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 7 citing sources in the vault. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced in the vault is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

GB-T 28004-2011 **《Disposable Diapers（纸尿裤 片、垫）》** is the **historical (superseded) Chinese national product standard** for disposable diapers — retained in this vault for historical version comparison, old-contract / inventory disputes, and lineage tracing of the penetration test method（源：[[GB-T 28004-2011 Disposable Diapers（纸尿裤 片、垫）]]）。It has been replaced as the current CN market baseline by **GB-T 28004.1-2021 Baby Diapers**, recorded as 生效中 with 2022-05-01 实施（源：[[GB-T 28004-2011 与 GB-T 28004.1-2021 婴儿纸尿裤指标对比]]）。The single most important methodological change in that succession is **「吸收速度取代滑渗量」** — absorption speed replaced the run-off amount（源：[[GB-T 28004.1-2021（纸尿裤国标）]]）。定义待补（引用来源未给出 GB-T 28004-2011 的完整适用范围条文）。

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

**The 2011 penetration口径 — 30° run-off（滑渗）.** The GB-T28004-2011 滑渗.doc test answers the question "**尿液冲击下有没有被及时吸收**" — urine has large volume and low viscosity, so the dominating issue is absorption speed（源：[[BM Run-off 与尿滑渗测试方法对比（TTM-00445 vs 30° 尿滑渗）]]）。Its intended use is **国标符合性与出厂放行** — a fixed caliber, robust to outliers (去极值), suited to external reports and cross-factory benchmarking（源：[[Run-off 30° 两法对比（GB-T28004-2011 vs TTM-00279）]]）。The 30° run-off method is contrasted with the in-house **TTM-00279** 30° run-off test, which is used for internal R&D and orientation-sensitivity analysis (to answer "前部漏还是侧部漏")（源：[[Run-off 30° 两法对比（GB-T28004-2011 vs TTM-00279）]]）。

**Version lineage (2011 → 2021).** 「吸收速度取代滑渗量」is the **largest methodological change** in the 2021 revision, so **cross-version data cannot be compared directly**（源：[[GB-T 28004.1-2021（纸尿裤国标）]]）。The change is confirmed across three independently extracted copies of the 2021 part-1 document, i.e. it is **not a single-extraction error**（源：[[GB-T 28004.1-2021 Baby Diapers（纸尿裤 第1部分 婴儿纸尿裤 · 副本2）]]）。

**Manufacturing / inbound checks in the family (2021).** Delivery moisture is measured by the **「恒重法」 (constant-weight method) rather than a fixed-time method** — the 0.1% criterion matters more than the 4 h period; desiccator cooling for 15 min with tinfoil covering are the two key anti-reabsorption steps（源：[[GBT28004.1-2021纸尿裤中交货水分的测定.doc]]）。Sampling representativeness is **2 包 × 1 片 × 2 组平行**（源：[[GBT28004.1-2021纸尿裤中交货水分的测定.doc]]）。

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **Reporting vs. R&D split.** GB-T28004-2011 滑渗.doc is the external / release口径 (national-standard compliance, factory release, cross-factory benchmarking); TTM-00279 is the internal structural-diagnosis口径（源：[[Run-off 30° 两法对比（GB-T28004-2011 vs TTM-00279）]]）。A REL-core engineer should route 符合性 claims through the GB-T 口径 and structural questions through the in-house one.
- **Finished-goods渗透红线.** The current infant red lines are 回渗量 ≤15.0 g and 渗漏量 ≤1.0 g（源：[[Rewet Test（回渗测试）]]）, with adult 回渗 thresholds of 30/40 g（源：[[Rewet Test（回渗测试）]]）— these are also cited as the practical anchor that differentiated GB-T 28004.1-2021 and GB-T 28004.2-2021 for cross-category referencing（源：[[GB-T 28004.2-2021 Adult Diapers（纸尿裤 第2部分 成人纸尿裤）]]）。
- **SAP escape is now a testable metric.** 面层附着物 ≤5 颗/片 turns top-sheet SAP residual into an inspectable index（源：[[GB-T 28004.1-2021（纸尿裤国标）]]）— directly relevant to REL core SAP containment.
- **Compliance stack.** Migratable fluorescent substances「无」plus four heavy-metal limits overlap with GB 15979-2024 Table 1（源：[[GB-T 28004.1-2021（纸尿裤国标）]]、[[Migratable Fluorescent Whitening Agents（可迁移性荧光增白剂）]]）。
- **Patent boundary on test apparatus.** The penetration test apparatus is covered by **4 patents**; a self-built test rig must respect the patent boundary（源：[[GB-T 28004.1-2021（纸尿裤国标）]]）。This affects any in-house replacement bench for a 2011-era method.
- **Reference-only items.** The SAP standard's 返黄值, though listed in Table 1, is explicitly a **reference indicator and not a判定依据** — it may not be used as an incoming-rejection clause（源：[[GB-T 22875-2018 SAP for Diapers and Sanitary Towels（纸尿裤和卫生巾用高吸收性树脂）]]）。
