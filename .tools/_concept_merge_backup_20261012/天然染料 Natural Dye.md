# 天然染料 Natural Dye

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 1 citing source, expanded with cross-referenced vault pages. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

**Natural Dye（天然染料）** in this vault is the **organic dye / pigment chemistry of flora- and fauna-sourced colorants** studied as an ancient chemical practice. The cited-by source frames it as **the advanced chemistry applied by ancient dyers to organic dyes and pigments from flora and fauna sources**, covering **ionic / covalent / intermolecular bonding, coordinate complexation, enzymatic hydrolysis, photochemical chromogenic precursor oxidation, anaerobic bacterial fermentative reduction, and redox reactions**; **some textile dyeings survive after six millennia**（源：[[Modern Chemistry of the Ancient Chemical Processing of Organic Dyes and Pigments]]）.

- **工作口径（材料学层面）**: 天然染料与颜料的化学本质是 **发色团（conjugated chromophore）+ 固色体系（mordant / 载体）**，与工业合成染料遵循**同一光学原理**（源：[[Ancient Dyes and Pigments]]）.

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

- **Ancient dyers were advanced empirical chemists**: controlling temperature and pH created coloured textile dyeings, some surviving six millennia（源：[[Modern Chemistry of the Ancient Chemical Processing of Organic Dyes and Pigments]]）.
- **Colour stability is a bond-energy / environment problem**: the differing stability of ancient pigments（some reds fade）stems from **chromophore bond energy and environmental sensitivity** — a mirror image of modern colour-changing ink's **"controlled instability"** design（源：[[Ancient Dyes and Pigments]]）.
- **Two opposite engineering goals from one chemistry**: ancient practice pursued **"不褪色"（no fading）**; hygiene colour-indicator inks pursue **"在特定条件下可控变色"（controlled colour change under specific conditions）**（源：[[Ancient Dyes and Pigments]]）.
