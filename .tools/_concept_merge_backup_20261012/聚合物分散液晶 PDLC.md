# 聚合物分散液晶 PDLC

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 3 citing sources. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

PDLC = **polymer-dispersed liquid crystal**. The source defines it as "immiscible mixtures of liquid crystal and polymer," whose materials "consist of micron sized nematic liquid crystal droplets dispersed in a polymer matrix"（源：[[Mechanochromic Elastomer from PDLC 5CB PDMS]]）.

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

- **Scattering ↔ transmission.** Nematic droplets dispersed in the polymer scatter light by refractive-index mismatch; reorienting the droplets (each droplet acts like a switchable domain) changes how light is transmitted, giving two switchable states（源：[[Mechanochromic Elastomer from PDLC 5CB PDMS]]）.
- **Electric → mechanical drive.** Conventional PDLC are actuated by an electric field; the mechanochromic route used in these vault sources reorients the droplets via applied mechanical strain instead（源：[[Mechanochromic Elastomer from PDLC 5CB PDMS]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **Stretch-visualisation indicator.** A 5CB–PDMS film that changes transmittance under strain could act as an indicator strip for diaper elastic-waistband tension or absorbent-core compression state（源：[[Elastic Tension Visualization Technology Assessment（拉力松紧可视化技术评估）]]）.
- **Open risk.** Stability of the 5CB–PDMS system in water/urine is stated as the largest unverified risk; 5CB is an environmentally sensitive chemical whose body-contact compliance and migration need assessment（源：[[Elastic Tension Visualization Technology Assessment（拉力松紧可视化技术评估）]]）.

## Open Questions 待补

- Water/urine environmental stability of the 5CB–PDMS film: `[待补]` (stated as the largest open risk)（源：[[Elastic Tension Visualization Technology Assessment（拉力松紧可视化技术评估）]]）.
- Repeated stretch-cycle fatigue life: existing data are compression / uniaxial-strain only and need re-testing.
- Skin-contact compliance and migration of 5CB: not assessed.
