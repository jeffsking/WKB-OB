# 热电现象 Thermoelectric

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 3 citing sources. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced in the vault is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

In this vault, **thermoelectricity (TE)** is framed as a **thermal-management + energy-harvesting function**: the **"conversion of waste heat into electrical energy"**, positioned as a contribution to reducing dependence on fossil fuels and greenhouse-gas emissions（源：[[Thermoelectric Polymer Materials ZT Figure of Merit and PGEC]]）. The same source states that **"over the past 10–15 years, there have been significant advances in the scientific understanding as well as in the performance of thermoelectric (TE) materials"**（源：[[Thermoelectric Polymer Materials ZT Figure of Merit and PGEC]]）.

Within the smart-textiles & wearables landscape, **thermoelectric harvesting** is one of the named function families — listed alongside stimuli-responsive polymers, wearable sensing/electronics, e-textiles, flexible electronics, and chromic / PCM / shape-memory functions（源：[[Smart Textiles & Wearables]]）. A dedicated survey on the topic is carried as [[Thermoelectric Materials and Applications Review]].

> A formal textbook definition of the physical effect（e.g. Seebeck / Peltier）is **not given** in the vault excerpts; the sourced definition above is the functional one. `[待补]`

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

- **Waste-heat → electricity.** The core rationale in the source is recovery of waste heat as electrical energy（源：[[Thermoelectric Polymer Materials ZT Figure of Merit and PGEC]]）.
- **Figure of merit (ZT) governs performance.** The source's key claim is that **ZT ≈ 1 is a practical, not theoretical, ceiling** — i.e. performance gains beyond that are hard, not impossible in principle（源：[[Thermoelectric Polymer Materials ZT Figure of Merit and PGEC]]）.
- **Material route named**: highly covalent semiconductor clathrates（Si / Ge / Sn）+ void structures formed by heavy elements, quoted as an approach that "works especially well"（源：[[Thermoelectric Polymer Materials ZT Figure of Merit and PGEC]]）.
- **Reversible thermal use**: the automotive examples span both **cooling**（climate-controlled cabin）and **energy recovery**（exhaust waste heat）（源：[[Thermoelectric Polymer Materials ZT Figure of Merit and PGEC]]）.
- **Flexible / wearable carrier**: TE is grouped under **热管理与能量采集** for smart textiles, with flexible/stretchable electronics as the integration path for flexible TE units（源：[[Smart Textiles & Wearables]]）.
