# 芯体通道花型设计

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 7 citing sources in the vault. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced in the vault is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

**核心通道花型（Core Channel Pattern）** is the deliberate forming of **channels / grooves and bosses** into the absorbent core; it is named in the vault as "the design object of the channels and bosses"（通道与凸台的设计对象）of the patterned SAP-core forming drum（源：[[Patterned SAP Core Forming Drum（印花SAP芯体成型鼓）]]）。It sits within the core-differentiation手段 list — **分区密度设计、毛细梯度设计、芯体通道花型设计** — used to raise SAP utilisation rather than to swap materials（源：[[ActivTech Spring Portfolio 2026（ActivTech 春季组合评审）]]）。定义待补（引用来源未给出几何定义或花型图样）。

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

**Spatial-dimension zoning.** Core Channel Pattern is listed as the **spatial-dimension** mitigation of gel blocking, against [[MD Liquid Distribution]] (the *inverse* distribution indicator) and [[SAP错峰膨胀]] (the *time-dimension* mitigation)（源：[[Gel Blocking（凝胶阻塞）]]）。It is likewise framed as the advanced form of zoning thinking — channel/void absorbent is the progression beyond flat vs pocketed forming（源：[[Pad Zoning：Flat vs Pocketed（成型分区：平区 vs 袋区）]]）。

**What channels can and cannot do.** A channel is a **「物理槽」** — it addresses physical routing but **cannot solve U-shaped anti-gravity diffusion**; that half depends on material capillarity（源：[[Online Core Competition & COCO V6 Strategy Workshop（在线新品竞争与策略）]]）。**Bonding is double-edged:** it preserves the wet-state channel but harms U-shaped capillary conduction, so bonds **must be made narrow**（源：[[Online Core Competition & COCO V6 Strategy Workshop（在线新品竞争与策略）]]）。

**Design constraints (list-level).** (1) Channels should be lengthened, not widened（源：[[COCO V6 Trial Round-2 Review（录音纪要 2026-09-16）]]）。(2) Reducing SAP while enlarging the open-groove area does **not** pay off — the saved SAP is returned as deteriorated cradle intake at the 2nd/3rd insult（源：[[COCO V6 Trial Round-2 Review（录音纪要 2026-09-16）]]）。(3) Structural channels raise utilisation more than a higher-GBP SAP grade does, so the GBP target is a downstream of structure（源：[[SAP Grade Strategy for Composite Core & Overseas Market Review — Nuoer Visit（复合芯体产品市场推广·诺尔交流）]]）。

**Structural counter-examples from models/minutes.** Model v8 flags that the two outermost slices are near-zero absorbing at 240 s — an edge-bypass route requiring barrier or edge-densified SAP（源：[[2D Absorption Model v8 Simulation Report（二维吸收模型 v8 仿真报告）]]）; and a "Code 10"-class observation notes that **centre densification can itself be detrimental** — a structural-zoning counter-example（源：[[Gel Blocking（凝胶阻塞）]]）。

**Related goal — shape stability.** Channel/pattern design shares its objective with shape-stability (SAF/RCF) and glue-free (ultrasonic bonding): **both exist to solve 起坨断层 (clumping / bulk failure)**（源：[[Diaper Structure & ADL-REL Compendium（纸尿裤结构·ADL与REL辨析）]]）。

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- This is a **direct REL/ultra-thin-core design theme**: raising real SAP utilisation (reported elsewhere as only 30–40%) by structure rather than by material swap（源：[[Online Core Competition & COCO V6 Strategy Workshop（在线新品竞争与策略）]]）。
- It is the **design object of the patterned SAP-core forming drum**, so pattern design is coupled to the [[Patterned SAP Core Forming Drum（印花SAP芯体成型鼓）]] hardware and its forming-drum space / 3-D SAP-array engineering gap（源：[[V6 Product Design & Process Optimization Workshop（V6产品设计与工艺优化 · ASR 纪要）]]）。
- Acceptance is still governed by the finished-diaper level, not the material level — the channel scheme must not degrade leakage or cradle vs the AT1.0 baseline（源：[[ActivTech Spring Portfolio 2026（ActivTech 春季组合评审）]]）。
