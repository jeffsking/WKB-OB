# 粘胶纤维 Viscose Fiber

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 5 citing sources. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

**Rayon is a regenerated cellulose fibre** — *"rayon, which is one of the oldest manufactured fibres, is a regenerated cellulose fibre"*, with *"a wide spectrum of properties"*（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch17 Rayon Fibres]]）。Its raw material is cellulose, *"abundantly available and a renewable source"*（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch17 Rayon Fibres]]）。The **viscose** route specifically *"resulted from research in the UK by Cross, Bevan and Beadle during the 1880s"*（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch17 Rayon Fibres]]）。

中文：粘胶（viscose）是人造丝（rayon）的一种——再生纤维素纤维；原料纤维素充足且可再生；粘胶工艺源自 1880 年代英国 Cross、Bevan、Beadle 的研究。

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

**Viscose process.** Chemistry is described by four mechanisms — **coagulation / neutralisation / desolvation / dexanthation（凝固/中和/脱溶/脱黄化）**; the manufacturing sequence is **steeping → shredding → ageing → xanthation → dissolution → filtration → mixing → ripening → spinning（浸渍→粉碎→老化→黄化→溶解→过滤→混合→熟成→纺丝）**（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch17 Rayon Fibres]]）。

**Zinc is structural.** *"over 95% of the rayon produced today is spun with zinc in the spin bath, the role of zinc in the structural regulation of viscose fibre is of paramount importance"*（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch17 Rayon Fibres]]）。Zinc regulates structure → **skin-core structure** → high strength; wet modulus can be raised stepwise (modifiers, low-temperature low-acid bath, formaldehyde) along the ladder **ordinary viscose → tyre yarn → HWM → polynosic → Ten-X**（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch17 Rayon Fibres]]）。

**Variants.** Named families: high-bulk/high-crimp and inflated fibres; **high-absorbency fibres (alloy rayon, 51 Fibre)**; **flame-retardant Tufban**; and **carbon-containing fibres** (conductive / adsorptive)（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch17 Rayon Fibres]]）。

**Alternative routes.** Cellulose-derivative solution spinning and direct solvent spinning (up to MMNO/Lyocell-Tencel) are catalogued as the successor routes（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch17 Rayon Fibres]]）。These direct-solvent systems are the process ancestor of today's cellulose dissolution–regeneration–nanostructuring work（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch17 Rayon Fibres]]）— see [[Nanocellulose Hygiene Application Roadmap（纳米纤维素卫材应用路线图）]]。

**Why the process is being abandoned.** *"The 'viscose process' has been largely given up in many of the more developed countries during the last two decades... it constitutes a health hazard due to toxic pollutants"*（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch17 Rayon Fibres]]）— tightening pollution rules weakened viscose's cost advantage and drove the solvent-route R&D wave since the late 1960s.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **Wet collapse is the design problem.** Viscose *collapses* when wet (low wet modulus), the inverse of cotton, which stiffens（源：[[Fibers and Fibrous Materials]]）— so wet strength, not dry strength, is the constraint for any core/wipe viscose.
- **Skin-core explains the fluff-pulp analogy.** The core-loses-50% / skin-loses-20% wet-strength split is the textbook analogy for why fluff pulp loses network strength when wet and why SAP plus bicomponent thermal-bond fibres are added for reinforcement（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch17 Rayon Fibres]]）。See [[绒毛浆（Fluff Pulp）]]、[[SAP（高吸水树脂）]]、[[吸收芯体]]。
- **Trilobal viscose for LSN hydroentanglement.** A real project use: **蓝鲸 is a trilobal viscose**, moved onto the spunlace line because trilobal increases specific surface area for distribution; the note also weighs replacing part of the 10% trilobal PET with it, price being the open issue, and remarks that viscose or trilobal PET can simply be carded in on the spunlace line（源：[[三叶形截面纤维（Trilobal Fibre）]]）。See [[水刺 Hydroentanglement]]。
- **High-absorbency viscose is the ancestor of the SAF route.** Alloy rayon and 51 Fibre are the early form of the high-absorbency-fibre line, adjacent to SAF（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch17 Rayon Fibres]]）。See [[Super Absorbent Fibre（超吸水纤维 SAF）]]、[[合金粘胶 Alloy Rayon]]。
