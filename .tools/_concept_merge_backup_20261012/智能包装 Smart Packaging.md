## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理
- **Packaging as a sensor-insertion point**：the wearable-sensors source frames smart packaging as a place to carry sensing alongside health monitoring and wetness indication（源：[[Wearable Sensors Fundamentals Implementation and Applications]]）.
- **Conductive-ink printed alarm 导电油墨报警**：紫华 demonstrated a smart package that alarms on urine via printed conductive ink; the mechanism, material system and performance are **not** recorded（源：[[EM Supplier Meetings 2026-06-02（紫华、恒碳、格菲林、Huayi 四场技术交流）]]）`[待补]`.
- **Colour/optical route 颜色/光学路线**：the chromic handbook names mica + TiO₂ lustre pigments as the actionable packaging material subset for hygiene（源：[[Chromic Phenomena]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联
- Smart packaging sits at the **packaging layer**, not the absorbent core; the vault's only demonstrated instance is a urine alarm carried by a film/backsheet supplier（源：[[EM Supplier Meetings 2026-06-02（紫华、恒碳、格菲林、Huayi 四场技术交流）]]）.
- Carried by [[紫华公司（Zihua, Zijiang Group）]], a PE backsheet-film supplier（源：同上）.
- Adjacent concepts in the vault: [[湿感指示条 Wetness Indicator]]、[[电子纺织品 E-textile]]、[[健康监测 Health Monitoring]]（源：[[Wearable Sensors Fundamentals Implementation and Applications]]）.
