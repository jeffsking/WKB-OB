# WRV 水保留值

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 2 citing sources in the vault. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced in the vault is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

**WRV（水保留值，Water Retention Value）** is the vault's name for a **water-retention metric used in pulp / fibre characterisation and acceptance**. In the vault it appears only as a **named metric** — it is listed among the required acceptance indicators for wood-pulp / viscose fibres, and among the concept set of the cross-linked-cellulose chapter — but **no operational definition (test geometry, load, time, units) is given in any read source**（定义待补（引用来源未给出明确定义））.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **Fibre/pulp acceptance metric.** In *Absorbent Technology* Ch.11 the vault places WRV in the four-metric acceptance block for wood-pulp / viscose fibres — **静态容量 + INDA 10.1 速率 + AUL + WRV + 湿态压缩模量** — with the explicit warning that capacity alone is not acceptable（源：[[Absorbent Product Testing]]）.
- **Cellulose-superabsorbent context.** WRV is registered as a concept of the cross-linked-cellulose chapter (Ch.7), alongside DS, cross-link density, HEC, MFC, grafting and gel blocking — i.e. it belongs to the cellulose water-retention family, even though that chapter's own text does not define it（源：[[Cross-linked Cellulose and Its Derivatives]]）.
