# 电致变色 Electrochromic

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 4 citing sources. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced in the vault is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

**Electrochromism（电致变色）** is colour change induced by an **applied voltage**（通电变色）— one of the chromic classes classified **by what induces the colour change**, alongside photochromic（light）and thermochromic（heat）（源：[[Smart Textiles and Their Applications]]）. It is counted among the **four commercialised chromic phenomena** — photochromism, thermochromism, **electrochromism**, ionochromism（源：[[Chromic Phenomena Introduction and Reversible Colour Change]]）— and among the **seven chromic mechanisms** named in the smart-fabric review（光/热/电/溶剂/酸碱 pH/摩擦/机械）（源：[[Smart Fabric Textiles Recent Advances and Challenges]]）.

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

- **Voltage-driven switching.** Colour appears/changes when a voltage is applied across the electrochromic element（源：[[Smart Textiles and Their Applications]]）.
- **Intrinsic reversibility.** Removal of the stimulus returns the colour to its resting state, because the original state is the more stable one（源：[[Smart Fabric Textiles Recent Advances and Challenges]]）.
- **Textile integration route.** The GRID report groups **woven optical fibre** and **electrochromic** as the two routes to a **textile flexible display**, together with their colour-change mechanisms（源：[[Smart Textile Visual Perception (GRID)]]、[[Smart Textiles & Wearables]]）.
- **Powered ↔ active architecture.** Electrochromic displays sit inside the **active smart-textile** class — textiles that sense *and* respond, deciding locally via embedded electronics or remotely via cloud/AI and acting through textile-based, flexible or miniaturised actuators（textile displays… `[原文残缺]`）（源：[[Smart Textiles and Their Applications]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **Powered indicators are a negative result for disposables.** The smart-textile community's own verdict is that **electrochromic routes are blocked on lifetime / reliability**; the source reads this as a warning to anyone proposing a **powered or electrically-switched indicator on a disposable**, and points to the **passive, power-free alternative** — [[US20070270773A1 Thermochromic Wetness Indicator]]（源：[[Smart Textiles and Their Applications]]）.
- **Family context**: it sits among the vault's chromic concepts — [[光致变色 Photochromic]]、[[热致变色 Thermochromic]]、[[机械变色 Mechanochromic]]、[[液晶 Liquid Crystal]]、[[离子变色 Ionochromic]]、[[变色油墨]]（源：[[Chromic Phenomena Introduction and Reversible Colour Change]]、[[Smart Textiles & Wearables]]）.
