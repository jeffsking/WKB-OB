## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

- **Design law of Chapter 6.** Because "the fiber surfaces constitute the pore walls" in capillary sorption, absorbency is a **surface-and-geometry problem**; the chapter proves that special-purpose fibres (alloy rayon, modified cellulose, deep-groove polyester, hollow chicken-feather) win by **engineering the pore wall and pore geometry rather than by changing the polymer**（源：[[Fibers and Fibrous Materials]]）。
- **Two sorption modes**：bulk adsorption/absorption into the fibre, and capillary sorption between fibres; the latter dominates nonwovens and is set by wetting and surface energy（源：[[Fibers and Fibrous Materials]]）。
- **"Buy the surface, not the resin."** Because fibre walls *are* the pore walls, surface energy and roughness dominate absorption; fibre chemistry matters mostly through wettability（源：[[Fibers and Fibrous Materials]]）。
- **Alloy ≠ blend is a semantic convention**（合金与共混的二分是语义约定而非热力学判据）：本章以 synergy（I > 0）定义 alloy，以 ≤ 算术平均定义 blend，二者并非截然两类，而是同一连续谱的两端（源：[[Ebewole Polymer Science Ch09 Polymer Additives and Reinforcements]]）。

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- Alloy rayon 属"工程化孔壁"这一纤维类别，用于吸收芯体与无纺布的孔结构调控；纤维化学主要通过润湿性起作用（源：[[Fibers and Fibrous Materials]]）。
- 湿态强度随结晶度反转：棉湿变硬、粘胶湿塌 —— 对湿巾与芯体层选材有直接含义（源：[[Fibers and Fibrous Materials]]）。
- 材料目录中的同类特种纤维：[[改性纤维素 Modified Cellulose]]、[[深沟槽涤纶 Deep-groove Polyester]]、[[鸡毛纤维 Chicken-feather Fibre]]。
