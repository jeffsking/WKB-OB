## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理
- The retention layer is the sink of the acquisition–distribution–retention chain; the surge layer's stated job is fast uptake + temporary hold, then release downstream（源：[[Products and Technology Perspective]]）.
- Design mechanism in the near/mid term is **gradient-based**: density gradient and capillary-pressure gradient across the three layers keep fluid moving into the retention layer（源：[[Products and Technology Perspective]]）.
- Retention performance is judged by under-load retention, not free swell — the compressed core regime is what matters（源：[[交联 CMC]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联
- Directly relevant to the REL ultra-thin core, where the retention layer is where SAP/fluff hold the fluid after acquisition and distribution（源：[[Products and Technology Perspective]]）.
- Retention is the property that resists [[回渗 Rewet]] under body pressure; the standard whole-product check is [[Retention Capacity Test（滞留能力测试）]] (STM-00033)（源：[[Preemie Diaper Absorption Test Methods & Parameters（早产儿纸尿裤吸收性能测试方法与参数）]]）.
- The material-level retention differentiator in the vault is under-load retention of modified CMC (16–21 %)（源：[[交联 CMC]]）.
