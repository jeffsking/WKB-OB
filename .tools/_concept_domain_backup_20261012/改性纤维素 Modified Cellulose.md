# 改性纤维素 Modified Cellulose

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 4 citing sources. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义
**Modified cellulose = cellulose derivatives obtained by controlled-substitution derivatisation** (esters / ethers). It exists as a materials class because cellulose itself is **infusible and insoluble**, and derivatisation with a controlled degree of substitution restores processability（源：[[Ebewole Polymer Science Ch05 Polymer Modification]]、[[Seymour Polymer Chemistry Ch10 Naturally Occurring Polymers]]）. In the absorbency literature it appears as the specialised absorbent fibre family "modified cellulose (CMC)"（源：[[Fibers and Fibrous Materials]]）.

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理
- **Two control variables.** DS fixes the solubility ceiling; cross-link density fixes the swelling ceiling — together they decide whether a cellulose derivative is a superabsorbent at all, and must be tuned jointly（源：[[Cross-linked Cellulose and Its Derivatives]]）.
- **Cross-linked CMC workhorse route.** Etherification with monochloroacetic acid (up to 8 mol/mol cellulose) + neutralisation with ~0.5 mol caustic/mol cellulose to reach DS ≥ 0.4; cross-linkers epichlorohydrin (~0.5 mol/mol), formaldehyde or glutaraldehyde（源：同上）.
- **Derivatisation restores processability.** Cellulose –OH substitution chemistry rests on hydrogen-bond theory; controlled DS converts an infusible/insoluble polymer into a processable derivative（源：[[Ebewole Polymer Science Ch05 Polymer Modification]]）.
- **Routes covered.** Cross-linked CMC, HEC, phosphonoalkylcellulose, sulfonated/oxidised cellulose, microfibrillated cellulose, and grafting — all unified under **Donnan osmotic swelling** ("almost completely governed by this mechanism")（源：[[Cross-linked Cellulose and Its Derivatives]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联
- **Wins where SAP loses.** Modified CMC's differentiator is **retention under compression** (16–21% vs 1.0% for plain cellulose), not free swell — the relevant metric for a compressed diaper core（源：[[Fibers and Fibrous Materials]]）.
- **Cellulose-based SAP alternative.** 10–25 g/g vs synthetic SAP 300–600 g/g is the number that justifies the 80/20–90/10 pulp+SAP composite core（源：[[Cross-linked Cellulose and Its Derivatives]]）.
- **Commercial anchors.** Hercules Aqualon / CLD (cross-linked CMC) and Kimberly-Clark's Bernardin phosphate fibre（源：同上）.
- **Regulatory watch.** Formaldehyde cross-linking is tightly restricted in infant products — epichlorohydrin or glutaraldehyde should be the default cross-linker in this space（源：同上）.
