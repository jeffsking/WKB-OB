# 珠光颜料 Lustre Pigment

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 4 citing sources. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced in the vault is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

**定义待补（引用来源未给出明确定义）** — the vault contains **no definition of a pearlescent / lustre pigment** as a material class（珠光颜料/珠光色素本体未在库内定义）.

What the vault *does* record are **two optics routes to a lustrous or colours appearance without a dye molecule**:

1. **Fibre-geometry lustre（纤维几何致光泽）**: for speciality PA/PET yarns, **"the non-circular cross-sections result in greater lustre and covering power and improved handle"**, with geometry set by the **spinneret hole shape**（trilobal, rectangular, hexalobal, hollow）and melt rheology（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch14 Speciality Polyamide and Polyester Yarns]]）.
2. **Structural colour without pigment（结构色）**: the Morphotex example produces **"colour from periodic layer thickness rather than from dye molecules"**（源：[[Intelligent Textiles and Sports Clothing]]）.

These sit within the vault's **five-mechanism classification of colour**, whose class **(V) geometric & physical optics** covers **interference, iridescence, diffraction and liquid crystals**（源：[[Chromic Phenomena Introduction and Reversible Colour Change]]）.

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

- **Geometry over formulation.** In the vault the sourced route to lustre is **non-circular fibre cross-section**, i.e. a spinneret-hole / melt-rheology design variable rather than an added pigment（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch14 Speciality Polyamide and Polyester Yarns]]）.
- **Periodic-layer optics.** Structural colour arises from **periodic layer thickness**, giving colour with no dye molecule — the physics shared with the vault's structural-colour strand（源：[[Intelligent Textiles and Sports Clothing]]、[[结构色 Structural Color]]）.
- **Interference / iridescence as class-V optics.** The chromic phenomenology places interference and iridescence in the geometric/physical optics class — the family a pearlescent pigment would belong to（源：[[Chromic Phenomena Introduction and Reversible Colour Change]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **Topsheet optics and hand.** The shaped-cross-section trade space（lustre × covering power × handle, p. 387）is named as relevant to **topsheet fibre selection**, though the source sentence tails off（`[原文残缺]`）（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch14 Speciality Polyamide and Polyester Yarns]]）.
- **Adjacency to the chromic programme.** Structural colour without pigment is described as "directly adjacent to our chromic programme" — i.e. the vault's functional-colour work is optics-led, not dye-led（源：[[Intelligent Textiles and Sports Clothing]]）.
