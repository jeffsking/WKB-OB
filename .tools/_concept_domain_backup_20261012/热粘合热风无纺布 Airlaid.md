# 热粘合热风无纺布 Airlaid

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 1 citing source, expanded with cross-referenced vault pages. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

**This heading is ambiguous in the vault**：it merges **two distinct materials / processes** —— **Airlaid（气铺 / 气流成网）**, a dry-laid web formed aerodynamically, and **热风无纺布（air-through / through-air bonded nonwoven，热粘合热风无纺布）**, a thermally bonded nonwoven. The vault treats this title as one of **≥4 overlapping headings** for the same material/process family（源：[[热风粘合无纺布]]）.

- **Airlaid（气铺/气流成网）**: the **dry-laid web-formation route based on aerodynamic web formation（气流成网）**; its raw-material science is **wood pulp, man-made fibres, superabsorbents (SAP) and bicomponent blends**（源：[[Dry-laid web formation]]）.
- **热风无纺布（air-through）**: a **thermally bonded nonwoven** in which the web is bonded by **hot air passing through it**（源：[[热风粘合无纺布]]）.
- 定义待补（引用来源未给出「热粘合热风无纺布 Airlaid」这一合并标题的单一明确口径定义）.

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

- **Aerodynamic web formation（气流成网）** is the endpoint of the dry-laid route（源：[[Dry-laid web formation]]）.
- **Thermal bonding（热粘合）**: heat a thermoplastic (low-melt sheath) component to its softening/melting point then cool, eliminating externally applied chemical adhesive — the dominant route for diaper topsheet/ADL and many wipes（源：[[Thermal bonding]]）.
- **Low-melt share is a channel-plug dial**: in ES/airlaid thermal bonding the 85/15 blend holds rate at 3.12 cc/g·s while 70/30 collapses to 1.07 cc/g·s — pushing strength with more low-melt destroys acquisition speed（源：[[Fluid Absorption in High Bulk Nonwovens]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **Airlaid paper（无尘纸）** commercial form: jumbo rolls or sheets for **wipes, diaper acquisition/absorption layers and absorbent cores**, split into 普通无尘纸（cellulose-only）and 合成无尘纸（with SAP）（源：[[GB-T 24292-2009 Airlaid Paper for Sanitary Products（卫生用品用无尘纸）]]）.
- **Core wrap 芯体包裹**: air-through nonwoven is a named core-wrap material（蓬松柔软、导流性好、包裹 SAP）（源：[[Composite Nonwoven Types Processes and Applications 2026（主流复合无纺布种类·工艺·应用）]]）; high-bulk air-through webs also act as the SAP-carrying substrate in multilayer SAP cores（源：[[Multilayer SAP Core（多层SAP芯体）]]）.
- **In-line airlaid core（fluff + SAP）is replacing pre-made airlaid cores** — a cross-category trend starting in femcare（源：[[High-Density Fluff Core（高密度绒毛浆芯体）]]）.
