# 植物基材料 Plant-Based Material
> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 6 citing sources in the vault. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

定义待补（引用来源未给出「植物基材料」的正式定义）。

The working sense used across the citing pages: the **"green track" material family that lowers petrochemical dependence and end-of-life impact in diapers** — **PLA（聚乳酸）, PPC（聚碳酸亚丙酯）, PHA（聚羟基脂肪酸酯）, bioSAP（生物基高吸水树脂）, nanocellulose（纳米纤维素）and natural fibers（竹 / 粘胶 / 棉）**（源：[[Biodegradable & Bio-based Diaper Materials（可降解与生物基卫材材料）]]）。

🔴 **Critical distinction（关键澄清）: 生物基 ≠ 可降解** — a bio-based acrylic SAP matches conventional SAP in performance and lifetime and does **not** degrade; the two concepts are orthogonal（源：[[生物基单体 Bio-based Monomer]]）。

The cited-by source [[Modern Chemistry of the Ancient Chemical Processing of Organic Dyes and Pigments]] names 「植物基材料」only as a **cross-reference point** with hygiene products (天然染料 / 植物基材料 / 植物提取物), without defining it（源：[[Modern Chemistry of the Ancient Chemical Processing of Organic Dyes and Pigments]]）。

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

- **Industrial path（工业路径）**: **生物基单体 → 生物基聚合物 → 可降解 SAP**；bio-based substitutes for the petroleum-based acrylic-acid route are **starch, cellulose, proteins and bio-based monomers**（源：[[生物基单体 Bio-based Monomer]]）。
- **PLA route**: plant starch → lactic acid（optical purity ≥99%）→ lactide（yield ≥90%, purity ≥99.5%）→ ring-opening polymerisation（Mw 70–100万）（源：[[生物基单体 Bio-based Monomer]]）。
- **Bio-based SAP certification route**: LG Chem SAPPYER uses **plant-based raw materials** and obtained the **world's first ISCC+ certification for SAP**; LG Chem states the **world's first commercialisation of BCB (Bio-Circular Balanced) SAP**（源：[[LG Chem SAPPYER SAP（Chinaplas Acrylates_SAP）]]）。ISCC+ is a **mass-balance** system certification that the input end contains plant-based feedstock（源：[[LG Chem（LG 化学）]]）。

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **Consumer value shift（消费价值迁移）**: Gen-Z parents want green + smart + premium, paying **20–30% premium** for biodegradable/plant-based（源：[[Diaper Tech Innovation Roadmap Next 5 Years（未来五年纸尿裤技术创新路径）]]）。
- **Supplier programme（采购侧动作）**: maintain a level of **bio-PE** to support the **Braskem** relationship and hedge future procurement risk; clarify **when bio-PP enters SBP**（源：[[Intake Systems Development Pathway 2023（摄入系统开发通路）]]）。
- ⚠️ **Documented risk — feminine care（女性护理已记录风险）**: 「**女性护理中多数生物基面层的经血摄入与回渗更差**」（源：[[Intake Systems Development Pathway 2023（摄入系统开发通路）]]）。相关采购风险：生物基 PE 需求下降可能妨碍未来获取；Cabka 回收对服装类废料的持续使用限制了可用替代成分（源：[[Intake Systems Development Pathway 2023（摄入系统开发通路）]]）。
- **Natural-fibre family（天然纤维族）**: 竹 / 粘胶 / 棉 are mature-application plant-based fibres for topsheet & ADL（源：[[Biodegradable & Bio-based Diaper Materials（可降解与生物基卫材材料）]]）；相关页面 [[粘胶纤维 Viscose Fiber]] · [[棉纤维 Cotton]] · [[PLA 玉米纤维]] · [[木浆 Pulp]]。
