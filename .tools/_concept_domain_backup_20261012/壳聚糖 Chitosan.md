# 壳聚糖 Chitosan

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 6 citing sources. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

**Chitosan（壳聚糖）** is positioned in the vault as a **bio-based functional material whose value is antibacterial + skin-friendly + biodegradable**, *rather than an absorbent*（源：[[壳聚糖及壳聚糖纤维技术与应用总结报告]]）.

For diapers, the source states the realistic entry point is the **skin-contact topsheet**（and active-care positioning）, **not the core** — consistent with its modest absorbency role in the cited literature（源：[[壳聚糖及壳聚糖纤维技术与应用总结报告]]）.

（中文：壳聚糖定位为**生物基功能材料**，价值在抗菌 + 亲肤 + 可降解，**而非吸水材料**；纸尿裤场景的现实切入点是**贴肤面层**（与主动护理定位），**不是芯体**。）

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

1. **Two recurring engineering routes**：(a) **gel-on-fibre** — chitosan gel **thermally spray-bonded onto PET fibre** to give fast uptake, lock-in, anti-rewet and antibacterial behaviour in one fibre; (b) **three-layer antibacterial superabsorbent** — breathable kapok/bamboo skins enclosing a **chitosan–acrylic acid/alginate microsphere** core, designed to fix the "wet → no breathability" failure mode（源：[[壳聚糖及壳聚糖纤维技术与应用总结报告]]）.
2. **Porous network + modifiability.** Chitosan is hygroscopic and forms a porous network（an absorption/moisture carrier）, and its **amino/hydroxyl groups are "molecular hooks"** that can be chemically modified to introduce polymerisable groups and graft acrylamide-type networks（源：[[Chitosan Fibre（壳聚糖纤维）]]）.
3. **Absorption comes from compositing, not from chitosan alone.** Chitosan fibre does not carry the load; uptake is achieved by **thermal spray onto PET fibre** or by **acrylic-acid polycondensation cross-linking + alginate** to form antibacterial superabsorbent microspheres（源：[[Chitosan Fibre（壳聚糖纤维）]]、[[壳聚糖及壳聚糖纤维技术与应用总结报告]]）.
4. **Cationic route for salt tolerance.** Quaternized chitosan's **–NH₃⁺** forms **ion pairs with AMPS's –SO₃H**, binding salt tolerance to network stability（源：[[AMPS（2-丙烯酰胺基-2-甲基丙磺酸）耐盐单体]]、[[季铵化壳聚糖复合芯层（PAM 微球+AMPS 体系）]]）.
5. **Hydrogel framework role.** Chitosan is one of the named **bio-based SAP backbones**（alongside cellulose, starch, calcium alginate）（源：[[水凝胶 Hydrogel]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **Positioning lesson — do not evaluate chitosan as an absorbent.** The correct question is *"which site needs natural antibacterial + skin-friendly + biodegradable"* — the answer is **topsheet and functional coating, not the core**（源：[[Chitosan Fibre（壳聚糖纤维）]]、[[Topsheet（面层）]]）.
- **Process lesson.** Chitosan rarely spun alone to carry structural load; it attaches to an existing fibre (**thermal spray onto PET**) or is made into **microspheres/microgels** embedded in a composite — manage it as a **functional additive, not a substrate**（源：[[Chitosan Fibre（壳聚糖纤维）]]）.
- **Claim-management lesson.** Antibacterial rate and wash durability are high-risk hygiene claims and must be re-verified by quantitative methods（e.g. AATCC 100 一类）, not quoted from vendors（源：[[Chitosan Fibre（壳聚糖纤维）]]）.
- **Core-recipe use.** Quaternized chitosan appears in a 2025 adult-diaper **hybrid-core配方**（with PAM microspheres + a ternary AMPS modifier）to attack salt-tolerance and gel-blocking at the recipe level; the DI-vs-saline column gives a direct **1/5 absorbency** anchor（源：[[CN 120285261 A（一种超吸收纸尿裤及其制备方法和应用·广东宜柔）]]、[[SAP性能指标（CRC AUL GBP Vortex）]]）.
- **Bio-based degradation track.** Chitosan also appears as a cationic/antibacterial partner in **biodegradable SAP** and cellulose-graft routes（源：[[两性离子高吸水树脂（纤维素接枝耐电解质）]]、[[Biodegradable SAP（可降解高吸水树脂）]]、[[纳米纤维素（CNC、CNF、BNC）]]）.
