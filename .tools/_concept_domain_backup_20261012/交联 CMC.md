# 交联 CMC

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 2 citing sources in the vault. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced in the vault is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

**交联 CMC（cross-linked CMC）** is the vault's most mature and most commercialised **cellulose-based superabsorbent route**, commercialised as **Hercules Aqualon / CLD**. It is made by etherifying cellulose (with **monochloroacetic acid**) and then cross-linking it, and it **lifts absorbency from pulp's 1–2 g/g to 10–25 g/g**（源：[[Cross-linked Cellulose and Its Derivatives]]）. It belongs to the "modified cellulose" family whose retention under load exceeds plain cellulose（源：[[Fibers and Fibrous Materials]]）. *(The English expansion "carboxymethyl cellulose" is not spelled out in the read sources; the vault uses "CMC" and gives the etherification reagent instead.)*

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

- **DS is the binary switch.** Below ~0.4 the material dissolves and is useless as a sheet; at ~0.4 it is insoluble and highly absorbent — so DS is the single most consequential specification to demand from a supplier（源：[[Cross-linked Cellulose and Its Derivatives]]）.
- **Cross-link density is a trade-off, judged in the load regime it will be used in.** It buys strength and insolubility at the price of capacity; because a real core is compressed, the optimum shifts higher than free-swell testing implies（源：[[Cross-linked Cellulose and Its Derivatives]]）.
- **Retention under compression is the relevant metric.** Modified CMC retains **16–21%** under a centrifugal load vs **1.0%** for plain cellulose — it "wins precisely where SAP loses"（源：[[Fibers and Fibrous Materials]]）.
- **Burst-fibre variant is a structural fix for gel blocking.** Fixing the swollen fibre state mechanically multiplies the wettable surface and removes the gel-blocking failure mode — no chemical change needed（源：[[Cross-linked Cellulose and Its Derivatives]]）.
- **Compliance caveat.** Formaldehyde cross-linking is tightly restricted in infant products in many markets; **epichlorohydrin or glutaraldehyde should be the default cross-linker** in this space（源：[[Cross-linked Cellulose and Its Derivatives]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **Cellulose-based SAP alternative.** The cellulose route delivers **10–25 g/g (5–10 g/g in brine)** against synthetic SAP's **300–600 g/g (20–50 g/g in brine)** — which is why commercial cores are pulp + SAP composites, typically **80/20 to 90/10**（源：[[Cross-linked Cellulose and Its Derivatives]]）.
- **Under-load retention is its differentiator.** Because a diaper core is compressed, the 16–21% under-load retention of modified CMC — not its free swell — is the figure to evaluate for a core application（源：[[Fibers and Fibrous Materials]]）.
- **Cost / traceability trade.** Cross-linked CMC is a low-value pulp by-product base and can partly integrate with existing paper lines, but etherification / cross-linking adds process cost and capital（源：[[Cross-linked Cellulose and Its Derivatives]]）.
