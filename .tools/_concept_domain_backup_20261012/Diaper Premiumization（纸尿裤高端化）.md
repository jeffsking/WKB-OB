# Diaper Premiumization（纸尿裤高端化）

> 一句话定义（Definition）：**Premiumization** is the strategy of trading consumers up tier-by-tier via **functional proof and brand equity** rather than price competition; in shrinking markets it is often the only sustainable growth path. A key execution lever is **occasion-based marketing（场景营销）** — sub-brands for summer, overnight, swim, premie, etc.（通过功能证据与品牌资产把消费者逐层上移、而非打价格战；在萎缩市场常是唯一的可持续增长路径。关键抓手是场景营销——夏季/夜用/游泳/早产等子品牌）.

## 机理 / 结构拆解 Mechanism

1. **Tier architecture（层级架构）**：offer a structured price ladder so consumers self-select trade-ups as willingness/ability to pay rises (e.g. Huggies' tier 4 → tier 8, with natural-skin and dry-comfort pillars).
2. **Functional proof（功能证据）**：premium claims must be demonstrable (skin-health tech, "3°C cooler" liner, overnight dryness) — brand equity is built on credibility, not slogans.
3. **Occasion-based marketing（场景营销）**：seasonal/sub-brand SKUs (summer +5~10% price) capture share and realize price without eroding the core brand.
4. **Brand equity as moat（品牌资产即护城河）**：in a shrinking category, the hard differentiator is consumer trust, not product spec — late entrants cannot easily replicate it.

## 区域印证 Regional Evidence

- **Korea（韩国）**：Huggies (Kaon) holds **~65% value share**; premiumization + occasion-based marketing sustained leadership through a decade of ~8%/yr decline. Chinese entrants (Kindle) reached only ~5% via a China-OEM model, hindered by consumer anti-China sentiment for baby products.
- **China（中国）**：consumers pay premiums for skincare/green/seamless; domestic brands trade up via probiotic topsheets, ultrafine fiber, and degradable materials.
