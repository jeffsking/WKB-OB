# 防水透气 Waterproof Breathable

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 10 citing sources. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

The vault gives the operating principle, not a textbook definition: waterproof-breathable pores are **20,000× smaller than a water drop** yet **700× larger than a water molecule** — so liquid water is blocked while water vapour passes（源：[[Smart Textiles Review]]）。It is listed as one function category in the smart-textile function map (chromic, thermoregulation, shape-memory, **waterproof-breathable**, wearable electronics, PCM)（源：[[Smart Textiles Review]]）。

中文：本库给出的是原理口径——防水透气孔径比水滴小约 20,000 倍、比水分子大约 700 倍，故阻液而透汽；它被列为智能纺织品功能版图中的一个类别。

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

**Microporous backsheet films.** A representative route is a **three-layer co-extruded blown film** with CaCO₃ masterbatch in each layer (middle layer 60–70 kg per batch, with PE and lubricant) giving an ultra-thin PE microporous film at 10–14 gsm（源：[[CN 111890767 A（一种超薄聚乙烯微孔透气膜及其制备方法）]]）。

**Zoned breathability.** A second route increases the cast film's single feed port to **multiple independently fed ports**, each controlling its own additive recipe, merged and secondarily drawn into one zoned breathable film — for adult-diaper backsheets that need differentiated breathability *and* leak-proofing（源：[[CN 112778606 A（一种分区透气底膜及其制备方法、纸尿裤）]]）。

**Film-free alternative (modelled).** An all-PP SMMMMS structure was studied as a physical-model feasibility question — replacing the breathable backsheet film — and the intended answer is a **zoning** decision (where the film can be dropped) rather than a yes/no（源：[[Laser-Assisted Meltblown Film-Free Backsheet（激光辅助熔喷无膜背层）]]）。

**Nanofibre membrane route.** Electrospun PU nanofibre membranes give very low basis weight with high hydrostatic pressure and very high WVTR; the vault is explicit that this is a **different material class, not a better film** — 4 gsm / 8 µm / 8,000 mmH₂O is not on the same scale as a PE drawn film（源：[[August R&D Meeting 2026（华峰研发会议）]]、[[Huafon 华峰 August R&D Meeting（氨纶与纳米纤维膜）]]）。

中文：机理三条——①微孔 PE 膜（三层共挤吹膜 + CaCO₃ 母粒，10–14 gsm）；②分区透气底膜（多独立进料口、独立配方后二级拉伸成一体膜，兼顾透气与防漏）；③无膜路线（全 PP SMMMMS 的物理建模可行性研究，答案按分区给）；④纳米纤维膜（PU 静电纺，4 gsm/8 µm/8,000 mmH₂O，属不同量纲）。

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **Backsheet is the primary application.** All three cited backsheet patents target baby-diaper or adult-diaper backsheets（源：[[CN 111890767 A（一种超薄聚乙烯微孔透气膜及其制备方法）]]、[[CN 112778606 A（一种分区透气底膜及其制备方法、纸尿裤）]]、[[US11612524B2 Breathable Diaper Backsheet (Colormasters)]]）。See [[底膜 Backsheet]]、[[SMMMS Film-Free vs Microporous Film Backsheet（无膜背层 vs 微孔膜背层）]]、[[Film-Free Backsheet（无膜背层）]]。
- **Structural (not material) breathability.** The waistband "air window" route opens **breathable holes like mini windows** (die-cut) rather than applying a coating — tying breathability to a cut process and its line-speed/safety validation（源：[[Airy Window（腰围透气窗）]]）。
- **Breathability is a cross-market claim.** *Breathable* is repeated as a headline claim across markets (e.g. Russia "100% Breathable…", Brazil/NA "Breathable Materials Keep You Feeling Cool And Dry"）（源：[[Global Overview - Intake Workshop（摄入系统研讨会·全球品牌与区域概览）]]）。See [[Nanofiber Waterproof-Breathable Membrane（纳米纤维防水透湿膜）]]。
- **Trade-off framing.** Breathable backsheets are engineered as a barrier-vs-permeability balance throughout（源：[[Smart Textiles Review]]）；the film-free study supplies the quantitative barrier/degradation limits behind any "drop the film" proposal（源：[[Laser-Assisted Meltblown Film-Free Backsheet（激光辅助熔喷无膜背层）]]）。
