# 活性炭除臭（DARCO）

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 5 citing sources. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced in the vault is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

**DARCO® KB-G** is a **powdered activated carbon (PAC)** made by **chemical activation of wood using the phosphoric acid process**（木基磷酸法化学活化）by **Cabot Norit Activated Carbon**（源：[[Cabot DARCO KB-G Powdered Activated Carbon (TDS)]]）. In this vault it anchors the **powdered activated carbon (PAC) option within the odour-control material family**（气味管理材料族中的「粉末活性炭」吸附型路线）（源：[[Cabot DARCO KB-G Powdered Activated Carbon (TDS)]]、[[Powdered Activated Carbon（粉末活性炭）]]）.

Per its technical datasheet (TDS, dated **6 April 2016**), it targets **purification of highly colored and/or foaming process liquids in the food, chemical and pharmaceutical industries**; it combines high adsorptive capacity with good filtration characteristics and is **especially effective for high-molecular-weight organics such as large color bodies and proteins at low carbon dosage**（源：[[Cabot DARCO KB-G Powdered Activated Carbon (TDS)]]）.

> ⚠️ **Scope note 口径说明**: the manufacturer TDS is written for **liquid purification**, not for a wearable odour application; the vault positions it as the **PAC route inside the odour-control family**（源：[[Cabot DARCO KB-G Powdered Activated Carbon (TDS)]]）.

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

- **Adsorption-type odour route**（吸附型除臭路线）: PAC removes odour molecules by adsorption rather than chemistry; the vault's odour-management family spans adsorption carriers（[[Powdered Activated Carbon（粉末活性炭）]]、[[Zeolite Odour Control（沸石除臭载体）]]）and fibre-based routes（[[咖啡炭纤维（异味吸附）]]）（源：[[Cabot Corporation（卡博特）]]）.
- **Low-dosage removal of large organics**: DARCO KB-G's stated differentiator is adsorbing **high-molecular-weight organics（大色体、蛋白质）at low carbon dosage**（源：[[Cabot Corporation（卡博特）]]、[[Cabot DARCO KB-G Powdered Activated Carbon (TDS)]]）.
- **Duplicate-carrier discipline**: the [[Cabot DARCO KB-G TDS（v3 归档同名件）]] is a **same-content archive copy**（v3 归档同名件）whose text, date and all parameter tables are identical to the main page; compilation does **not** re-extract — the main page is the single source of truth（源：[[Cabot DARCO KB-G TDS（v3 归档同名件）]]）.
