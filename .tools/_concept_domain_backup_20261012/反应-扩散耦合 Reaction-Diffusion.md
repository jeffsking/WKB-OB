## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

Crank's framework builds from Fick's laws (F = −D dC/dx; ∂C/∂t = D∂²C/∂x²) through analytical/numerical solutions, D(C)-dependent diffusion, moving boundaries (Ch.13) to reaction-diffusion coupling (Ch.14)（源：[[Mathematics of Diffusion]]）. The chapter-level page [[Diffusion and Chemical Reaction (Crank Ch.14)]] also references the Freundlich isotherm.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

Listed as part of the theoretical foundation for SAP swelling kinetics, absorbent core modeling, and wet migration simulation（源：[[Mathematics of Diffusion]]）.
