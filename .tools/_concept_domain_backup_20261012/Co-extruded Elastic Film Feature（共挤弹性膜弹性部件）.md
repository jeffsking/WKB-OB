# Co-extruded Elastic Film Feature（共挤弹性膜弹性部件）

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 3 citing sources in the vault（10 处引用）. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced in the vault is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

An **improved composite elastic feature（复合弹性部件）** for absorbent articles that is easier to handle during manufacture and subsequent processing, disclosed in [[EP0820747A1 Absorbent Article with Elastic Feature (P&G)]]（源：[[EP0820747A1 Absorbent Article with Elastic Feature (P&G)]]）. The feature comprises **at least one extruded elastomeric layer** and **at least one extruded, substantially non-elastomeric layer** which together form a film（至少一层挤出弹性体层 + 至少一层挤出、基本非弹性层，共同构成薄膜）. Before the film is elasticised by **activation（激活）**, the non-elastomeric layer inhibits the elastic properties of the elastomeric layer. The approach replaces the **nonwoven + elastomer laminate** route to diaper elastics with a **co-extruded elastomeric / non-elastomeric film** switched on by mechanical stretching — described as the earliest "activation" document in the library's elastic-feature cluster.

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

**Activation is the invention's switch（激活即开关）:** the film ships and stores in a non-elastic, easy-to-handle state and becomes elastic **only after mechanical stretching**（源：[[EP0820747A1 Absorbent Article with Elastic Feature (P&G)]]）. **Co-extrusion beats lamination** because it delivers the multi-layer structure in one process step from the same die head. Removing the nonwoven layer removes two manufacturing pains — weak nonwoven tensile strength and fibre/glue contamination.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

Directly relevant to **diaper/pant elastication** — waist, cuff and ear-panel elastic components. The co-extruded film feature is the material route behind the elastic-feature cluster that also covers [[Elastic Strand Snicking（弹性橡筋断丝失效化）]], [[Elastic Tension & Creep（橡筋张力与回缩率）]] and [[Waist Guard（腰围护挡）]], and it links to pant-type articles ([[Pant-Type Disposable Absorbent Article（裤型一次性吸收物品）]]、[[EP2413866B1 Disposable Wearing Article (Unicharm)]]）. Because the film must be adhesively attached, it is the attachment target of the OBC-based adhesive work in [[EP2456819B1 Olefin Block Copolymer Hot Melt Adhesive (Bostik)]]（[[Polyolefin Hot Melt Adhesive（聚烯烃热熔胶）]]）. The **activation** mechanism is the same family as the mechanically-activated elastic laminates used in [[US10905605 Pull-On Wearable Article with Informational Image]].
