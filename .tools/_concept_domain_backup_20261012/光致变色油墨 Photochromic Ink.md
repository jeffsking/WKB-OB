# 光致变色油墨 Photochromic Ink

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 5 citing sources. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

Photochromic ink is a colour-changing ("chromic") ink whose colour change is triggered by **light**. The vault classifies chromic materials **by the stimulus that induces the colour change**, and places photochromic = light-induced alongside thermochromic (heat) and electrochromic (applied voltage)（源：[[Smart Textiles and Their Applications]]）。It is counted among the **four commercialised colour-change families** — photochromism, thermochromism, electrochromism, ionochromism（源：[[Chromic Phenomena Introduction and Reversible Colour Change]]）。

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

- **Reversible by design.** Textile examples recorded are photochromics that notify UV radiation levels in solar protection, thermochromic wallpaper that "grows" flower buds when heated, and photochromic pillow covers revealing a motif in sunlight — "all reversing when the stimulus is removed"（源：[[Smart Textiles and Their Applications]]）。
- **Shared molecular mechanism.** Photochromic chemistry shares its molecular mechanism with mechano- and thermo-chromic chemistry; in the vault's absorption-hygiene scenarios photochromism is therefore treated as an **interference source rather than a function**（源：[[Chromism Taxonomy（变色现象分类）]]）。
- The reversibility physics (why the colour change reverses, why photochromics bleach inside a matrix) is treated in [[Molecular and Supramolecular Systems]]; that page is a mechanism reference, not a supplier or formulation source.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **Wetness / UV indication.** Photochromic novelty printing and textiles can connect directly to the **wetness / UV indication smart topsheet of adult-incontinence articles** (colour change on UV exposure → reminder to change)（源：[[Chromic Phenomena Introduction and Reversible Colour Change]]）；smart-textile records also include photochromics for UV-exposure notification in sun protection and sunlight-revealed pillow-cover motifs（源：[[Smart Textiles and Their Applications]]）。
- **Supplier base already serving hygiene.** CTI (Chromatic Technologies Inc., USA) covers Thermochromic / **Photochromic (UV light colour change)** / Hydro-chromic inks across the full range（源：[[Hygiene Chromic Ink Supplier Landscape（卫生护理变色油墨供应商调研 · 2026-06-12）]]）。Intellego Technologies AB (Sweden) builds its colour indicators on **photochromic technology** and sells a wetness indicator for diapers and UVC dose meters; it is expanding India plant capacity for the hygiene industry（源：同上）。Matsui Shikiso (松井, Japan) supplies a **Photochromic series** including BPA-free models（源：同上）。
