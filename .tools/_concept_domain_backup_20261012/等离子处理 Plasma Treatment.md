# 等离子处理 Plasma Treatment

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 3 citing sources. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

定义待补（引用来源未给出明确定义）。

The vault never defines plasma treatment as a process; it treats it functionally, as a **dry** route to fix polypropylene's poor wettability — *"Plasma fixes PP's poor wettability dryly"* and *"Plasma makes PP hydrophilic for hygiene without wet chemistry"*（源：[[Nonwoven fabric finishing]]）。It is grouped with the emerging/developing finishing technologies (alongside microencapsulation, laser etching, biomimetic lotus-leaf and electrochemical routes)（源：[[Nonwoven fabric finishing]]）。

中文：本库未给等离子处理的工艺定义，只把它作为**干法**改善 PP 润湿性的手段，并与微胶囊、激光蚀刻、仿生荷叶、电化学等并列为「新兴整理技术」。

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

**Why PP needs it.** Polypropylene fibres have low surface energy and are hard to bond; **corona/plasma treatment improves wetting**（源：[[Chemical bonding]]）。The finishing source restates this as PP's poor wettability being fixed by plasma *dryly*, i.e. without a wet-chemical finish（源：[[Nonwoven fabric finishing]]）。

**Demonstrated mechanism evidence.** The vault's only worked example is O₂-plasma on PP **blood filters**, where improved wetting gives *much higher* flow（源：[[Nonwoven fabric finishing]]）。

**Dry vs wet route.** Plasma is presented as the alternative to the wet-chemical finishes catalogued in the same source (antistatic, antimicrobial, lubricant, flameproof, waterproof, softener, UV finishes)（源：[[Nonwoven fabric finishing]]）。The adjacent spin-finish source adds the durability context that motivates a dry route: surfactant-type finishes are subject to **thermal migration** — the surfactant migrates to and enriches on the fibre surface on heating, so hydrophilicity decays over time（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch07 Spin Finishes]]）。

中文：机理为「PP 低表面能 → 难润湿/难粘合 → 电晕/等离子改善润湿」；唯一实证是 O₂ 等离子处理 PP 血液滤材使通量大幅提高。等离子被定位为湿法化学整理（抗静电/抗菌/润滑/阻燃/防水/柔软/抗UV）之外的**干法**替代；相邻的纺丝油剂来源给出动机——表面活性剂型整理剂受热会向纤维表面迁移富集，使亲水性随时间衰减。

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **Direct.** Plasma makes PP hydrophilic **for hygiene** without wet chemistry（源：[[Nonwoven fabric finishing]]）— this is the PP-based nonwoven in a topsheet/ADL role, where wettability, not chemistry, is the design target. See [[亲水整理 Hydrophilic Finish]]、[[Topsheet（面层）]]。
- **Bonding side.** Because it also improves wetting/bonding of low-energy PP, corona/plasma sits upstream of PP bonding operations（源：[[Chemical bonding]]）。See [[Thermal bonding]]、[[粘合剂 Binder]]。
- **Motivation — rewet durability.** The wet-chemistry hydrophilic finish has a thermal-migration durability problem（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch07 Spin Finishes]]）, and migration is one stated cause of rewet deterioration; a dry plasma route removes that specific failure channel. (This is a synthesis across two sources, not a statement made by either page.) See [[回渗 Rewet]]、[[亲水整理耐久性]]。
