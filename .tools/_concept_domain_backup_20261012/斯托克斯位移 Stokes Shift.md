## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

1. **Luminescence is the third dimension of colour chemistry** — energy absorption followed by light emission, distinct from the absorption/reflection of Chapter 1（源：[[Luminescence Fluorescence Phosphorescence and Electroluminescence]]）.
2. **Fluorescence vs phosphorescence reduces to excited-state lifetime**：1–10 ns prompt emission vs delayed emission with afterglow — this difference, not the chemistry family, decides the application（源：[[Luminescence Fluorescence Phosphorescence and Electroluminescence]]）.
3. **The azo exception is the governing constraint for paper.** *"A vast range of organic chromophoric ring systems exhibit fluorescence, with the notable exception of the ubiquitous azo chromophore"* — so **paper that must look fluorescent-white cannot use azo brighteners**; it needs stilbene-type or coumarin-type chemistry（源：[[Luminescence Fluorescence Phosphorescence and Electroluminescence]]、[[偶氮染料 Azo Dye]]）.
4. **Dye vs pigment trade-off**：*"where light fastness is not a problem, dyes take precedence because of brighter shade, better colour gamut and inherently more stable inks"*（源：[[Luminescence Fluorescence Phosphorescence and Electroluminescence]]）.
5. **Emission efficiency spans two orders of magnitude in practice**：luminol quantum yield ≈ **1%**（limits sensitivity）while AMPPD reaches **20–30%**（used for alkaline-phosphatase assays）（源：[[Luminescence Fluorescence Phosphorescence and Electroluminescence]]）.
6. **Electroluminescence context**：LED emission wavelengths GaP 550/700 nm, GaAsP 580/660 nm, GaAs 880 nm, Si 1100 nm, Ge 1810 nm; blue-green and UV emission stayed the hardest problem until early-1990s 3M reports of a **450 nm ZnSe-based laser**（源：[[Luminescence Fluorescence Phosphorescence and Electroluminescence]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **This page is a supplier-qualification instrument, not theory.** The stated key action: when evaluating **pulp optical-brightener suppliers**, require **three quantitative data — Stokes shift, quantum yield, light fastness**（标准见源文行 9829–9835）（源：[[Luminescence Fluorescence Phosphorescence and Electroluminescence]]）.
- **Pulp whitening route is constrained by the azo rule**：fluorescent-white paper requires **non-azo brighteners**（stilbene / coumarin classes）（源：[[Luminescence Fluorescence Phosphorescence and Electroluminescence]]、[[木浆 Pulp]]）.
- **Named vault neighbours**：[[荧光增白剂 Optical Brightener]]、[[香豆素 Coumarin]]、[[偶氮染料 Azo Dye]]、[[电致发光 Electroluminescence]]、[[光致变色 Photochromic]]、[[热致变色 Thermochromic]]（源：[[Luminescence Fluorescence Phosphorescence and Electroluminescence]]）.
- **Digital printing fit**：the source notes digital printing's fast growth aligns with hygiene products' many SKUs and strong customisation needs（源：[[Luminescence Fluorescence Phosphorescence and Electroluminescence]]）— relevant to wipes packaging / fluorescent marking.
- **Regulatory tie**：migratable fluorescent whitening agents are a controlled substance class for children's paper products（源：[[GB 43631-2023 General Safety Technical Specification for Infants and Children Paper Products（婴幼儿及儿童用纸品基本安全技术规范）]]）.
