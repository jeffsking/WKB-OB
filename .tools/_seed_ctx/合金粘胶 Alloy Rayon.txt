--- [[Fibers and Fibrous Materials]]  (4 句)
   • Chapter 6 (pp. 216–249; Ludwig Rebenfeld, TRI) is the materials catalog plus the one design law that governs all of it: because 'the fiber surfaces constitute the pore walls' in capillary sorption, absorbency is a surface-and-geometry problem — and the chapter proves that special-purpose fibres (alloy rayon, modified cellulose, deep-groove polyester, hollow chicken-feather) win by engineering the pore wall and pore geometry rather than by changing the polymer.（中文：第 6 章确立"毛细吸收中纤维表面即孔壁"这一设计律，并用合金粘
   • **Fibrous material, defined.** A substrate of fibres in which 'the spaces between the component fibers, referred to as pores, play a key role in their physical properties', with porosity typically 0.6–0.9 or higher.
- **Two sorption modes.** Bulk adsorption/absorption into the fibre, and capillary sorption between fibres; the latter dominates nonwovens and is set by wetting and surface energy, since 'the fiber surfaces constitute the pore walls'.
- **The dual-dependence principle.** Performance 
   • **Buy the surface, not the resin.** Because fibre walls *are* the pore walls, surface energy and roughness dominate absorption; fibre chemistry matters mostly through wettability.
- **Density and geometry substitute for hydrophilicity.** Polyolefin and PET absorb well through fine denier and non-circular cross-section — the pore network, not the polymer, is the absorber.

   • **棉花 vs Rayon**（line 235-237）：化学组成相同（都是纤维素），但物理结构不同——棉花为天然高结晶度 α-cellulose、纤维形态规则；Rayon 结晶度与取向度较低，**湿态强度低**。
   • **绒毛浆就是纤维素，本章是其材料化学基础**：绒毛浆（fluff pulp）由针叶木硫酸盐浆制得，其 DP（3,500–36,000）、α-cellulose 含量与结晶度直接决定纤维的**刚度、成网性与毛细吸液速率**；line 190 的氢键网络解释了绒毛浆「不溶于水但高度亲水」——吸液发生在纤维间毛细空隙与无定形区羟基，而非溶解。这与 GB-T 21331-2021 绒毛浆标准的指标口径可直接对照。
- **再生纤维素纤维（Rayon/粘胶、Lyocell 类）用于面层与导流层**：Rayon 结晶度与取向度低于棉、湿态强度低（line 237），这既是其柔软亲水的来源，也是**湿态掉毛/强度衰减**的机理依据；设计导流层时必须权衡其湿强。
