--- [[GB 43631-2023 General Safety Technical Specification for Infants and Children Paper Products（婴幼儿及儿童用纸品基本安全技术规范）]]  (3 句)
   • China's **mandatory chemical-safety floor**（强制国标）for all paper products for children ≤6 — the single table that wipes, diapers and tissue SKUs must clear on heavy metals, FWAs, acrylamide, formaldehyde, solvents, preservatives, phthalates and aromatic amines; it is the standard that GB/T 27728.2-2024 delegates its "safety indicators" clause to.
   • GB 43631-2023 (ICS 85.010 / CCS Y30, 工信部) is a mandatory national standard applying to infant/children paper products manufactured or sold in China. It classifies products into 生活用纸品 (tissue, wipes, diapers) and 文化用纸品 (paper/board/books) and sets one shared chemical baseline plus category-specific rows. The shared floor: four heavy metals, migratable fluorescent substances "无", acrylamide ≤0.1 mg/kg, and a 24-species carcinogenic aromatic amine list (Annex A) at ≤20 mg/kg for printed/dyed items.
   • 概念：GB 43631-2023（婴幼儿及儿童纸品化学安全总门槛）、Migratable Fluorescent Whitening Agents（可迁移性荧光增白剂）、锑（Antimony, Sb）测试方法三口径：可迁移·消解·燃烧
- 来源：GB-T 27728.2-2024 Wipes Part 2 Baby and Child Wipes（湿巾 第2部分 婴童湿巾专用要求）

--- [[GB-T 27728.2-2024 Wipes Part 2 Baby and Child Wipes（湿巾 第2部分 婴童湿巾专用要求）]]  (3 句)
   • China's voluntary national standard (GB/T) setting **particular requirements for baby & child wet wipes**（婴童湿巾专用要求）— the compliance baseline for any wipes SKU targeting children ≤6 in the CN market.
   • Part 2 of the GB/T 27728 series (ICS 85.080 / CCS Y39), drafted under SAC/TC 141 (全国造纸工业标准化技术委员会), proposed by 中国轻工业联合会. Drafting units include 中国制浆造纸研究院、好孩子、维达、恒安、金佰利（中国）、广州宝洁、花王（中国）研发中心、露乐健康、赛得利（九江）等 — i.e. essentially the whole CN wipes/hygiene value chain. The standard layers baby-wipe requirements on top of the general requirements of GB/T 27728.1: raw-material minimization and long-safe-use-history principle, fragrance per GB/T 22731, production lotion per the 2015 Cosmetics Safety Technic
   • 概念：GB 43631-2023 General Safety Technical Specification for Infants and Children Paper Products（婴幼儿及儿童用纸品基本安全技术规范）、化妆品限用-准用组分体系（中国 2015 技术规范）
- 来源：GB 15979-2024 Hygienic Requirements for Disposable Sanitary Products（一次性使用卫生用品卫生要求）、GB 38598-2020 General Requirements for Labels and Instructions of Disinfection Products（消毒产品标签说明书通用要求）
- 同族标准：GB-T 27728-2011 Wipes（湿巾）、GB-T 27728.3-2024 Wipes Part 3 Disinfectant Wipes（湿巾 第3部分 消毒湿巾专用要求）

--- [[GB 43631-2023（婴幼儿及儿童纸品化学安全总门槛）]]  (2 句)
   • 上位体系：GB 15979-2024（一次性使用卫生用品卫生要求）（卫生指标维度与本规范化学安全维度互补）

- 平行限用物质：Migratable Fluorescent Whitening Agents（可迁移性荧光增白剂）、化妆品限用-准用组分体系（中国 2015 技术规范）（湿巾生产用液与防腐剂的上位口径）
- 元素迁移口径：锑（Antimony, Sb）测试方法三口径：可迁移·消解·燃烧

--- [[GB 5296.3-1995 Instructions for Use of Products of Consumer Interest General Labelling of Cosmetics（消费品使用说明 化妆品通用标签）]]  (2 句)
   • Historical CN standard for **cosmetic labels**（化妆品通用标签，1995 版）— defines the two-mode date marking and the no-medical-claims principle that survives (in updated form) in today's cosmetic labeling regime; relevant to wipes because baby-wipe lotion labeling borrows cosmetic vocabulary.
   • **「日期双模式 + 无医疗用语」是 CN 化妆品标签 30 年不变的骨架**，湿巾生产用液成分标注（GB/T 27728.2 的 INCI 降序要求）是其现代延伸 —— 支撑页：GB-T 27728.2-2024 Wipes Part 2 Baby and Child Wipes（湿巾 第2部分 婴童湿巾专用要求）、中国化妆品卫生标准体系（GB 7916 表1-表6 清单结构）

--- [[Industrial Drying Ch40 Drying of Coated Webs]]  (2 句)
   • **Hygiene implication:** Emulsion/lotion coatings on wipes behave like latex — a longer benign constant-rate before the defect-prone falling-rate zone.
   • **Hygiene implication:** For skin-contact hygiene films and wipes, 100% solids UV/EB is the regulatory-friendly route — no solvent VOC, no extractable cross-linker residuals.
