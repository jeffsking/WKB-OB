--- [[GB-T 21331-2021 Fluff Pulp（绒毛浆）]]  (4 句)
   • China's product standard for **fluff pulp**（绒毛浆）used as raw material for disposable hygiene products — the CN-market acceptance baseline for fluff pulp lots (quality, chemical safety, microbial).
   • GB/T 21331-2021 (ICS 85.040 / CCS Y31, SAC/TC 141) is the first revision of the 2008 fluff pulp standard. It drops the old product-grade split and instead classifies pulp by chemical treatment: **treated**（处理浆，耐破度显著降低，利于散纤）vs **untreated**（未处理浆）. The 2021 revision's main technical changes: migratable fluorescent substances, AOX, and four heavy metals (Pb/As/Cd/Hg) are now specified with test methods; 二氯甲烷抽出物 is replaced by 丙酮抽出物; dry bulk, absorption time/capacity methods are reworked in normati
   • **绒毛浆入厂验收的化学安全四件套（荧光物质/AOX/重金属/丙酮抽出物）是 2021 版新增** —— 支撑页：绒毛浆（Fluff Pulp）、Migratable Fluorescent Whitening Agents（可迁移性荧光增白剂）
- **处理浆 vs 未处理浆的取舍本质是「蓬松/吸速 vs 强度」**：处理浆蓬松度门槛更高（17.0 vs 15.0 cm³/g）但耐破度更低 —— 支撑页：绒毛浆（Fluff Pulp）
   • 概念：绒毛浆（Fluff Pulp）、Migratable Fluorescent Whitening Agents（可迁移性荧光增白剂）、TCF Bleaching（全无氯漂白）（AOX 与漂白工艺相关）

   • **Scope.** "The use of surfactants provides one of the most powerful means available to the practitioner for altering absorbency, either to promote it or to suppress it."

   • **Dose near CMC, never above it.** Extra surfactant above CMC is spent on micelles that cannot wet — a direct, quantifiable overrun in formulation cost.

- **Don't sell cellulose a wetting agent.** At 0° contact angle the fibre is already saturated; any measured gain is impurity removal

--- [[交联 CMC]]  (3 句)
   • **交联 CMC（cross-linked CMC）** is the vault's most mature and most commercialised **cellulose-based superabsorbent route**, commercialised as **Hercules Aqualon / CLD**. It is made by etherifying cellulose (with **monochloroacetic acid**) and then cross-linking it, and it **lifts absorbency from pulp's 1–2 g/g to 10–25 g/g**（源：Cross-linked Cellulose and Its Derivatives）. It belongs to the "modified cellulose" family whose retention under load exceeds plain cellulose（源：Fibers and Fibrous Materials）
   • **Cellulose-based SAP alternative.** The cellulose route delivers **10–25 g/g (5–10 g/g in brine)** against synthetic SAP's **300–600 g/g (20–50 g/g in brine)** — which is why commercial cores are pulp + SAP composites, typically **80/20 to 90/10**（源：Cross-linked Cellulose and Its Derivatives）.

   • Cross-linked Cellulose and Its Derivatives — cross-linked CMC synthesis, DS/cross-link trade-off, Hercules Aqualon / CLD
