--- [[交联 CMC]]  (4 句)
   • **交联 CMC（cross-linked CMC）** is the vault's most mature and most commercialised **cellulose-based superabsorbent route**, commercialised as **Hercules Aqualon / CLD**. It is made by etherifying cellulose (with **monochloroacetic acid**) and then cross-linking it, and it **lifts absorbency from pulp's 1–2 g/g to 10–25 g/g**（源：Cross-linked Cellulose and Its Derivatives）. It belongs to the "modified cellulose" family whose retention under load exceeds plain cellulose（源：Fibers and Fibrous Materials）
   • **DS is the binary switch.** Below ~0.4 the material dissolves and is useless as a sheet; at ~0.4 it is insoluble and highly absorbent — so DS is the single most consequential specification to demand from a supplier（源：Cross-linked Cellulose and Its Derivatives）.
- **Cross-link density is a trade-off, judged in the load regime it will be used in.** It buys strength and insolubility at the price of capacity; because a real core is compressed, the optimum shifts higher than free-swell testing impli
   • **Cellulose-based SAP alternative.** The cellulose route delivers **10–25 g/g (5–10 g/g in brine)** against synthetic SAP's **300–600 g/g (20–50 g/g in brine)** — which is why commercial cores are pulp + SAP composites, typically **80/20 to 90/10**（源：Cross-linked Cellulose and Its Derivatives）.
- **Under-load retention is its differentiator.** Because a diaper core is compressed, the 16–21% under-load retention of modified CMC — not its free swell — is the figure to evaluate for a core applicati
   • Cross-linked Cellulose and Its Derivatives — cross-linked CMC synthesis, DS/cross-link trade-off, Hercules Aqualon / CLD

--- [[三叶形截面纤维（Trilobal Fibre）]]  (3 句)
   • 1. **截面决定功能**：圆形截面纤维之间只有"点接触"，液体靠纤维间孔隙毛细力输运；三叶形截面的三个凹槽在纤维表面形成**轴向沟道**，纤网中相邻纤维的沟道叠加即成为**准连续的低阻导流路径**。
2. **方向性 = MD 沟槽**：梳理成网时纤维沿机向（MD）取向，沟道也随之沿 MD 排列 → 液体被**沿机向拉长铺展**，而非在冲击点堆积。这是"MD distribution / MD 铺展"指标的物理来源。
3. **两种载体**：
   - **PE/PET 皮芯型三叶形**（面层用）：三叶形截面 + 皮芯结构并存，兼顾导流与热粘合；COCO V6 面层上层仅放 10%、下层放 50%。
   - **三叶形莱赛尔（Lyocell）粘胶纤维**（芯包/水刺用）：用于 LSN 水刺非织造，主打湿态强度与亲水通道——对应 OI-08「Trilobal Lyocell for LSN」。
4. **配比的两难**：三叶形越多导流越好，但贴肤侧三叶形**手感偏硬**。COCO V6 的解法是 **Z 梯度分层**——整体 30%（15 gsm），但皮肤侧只放 5%。详见 ATB双
   • 实例：**蓝鲸** = **三叶形粘胶纤维**（原用于「碳矿」[待验证]，现移到水刺布上）；原理同样是**三叶形增加比表面积以增加导流**。
- 可考虑**把 10% 的三叶形 PET 换成蓝鲸粘胶** —— 关键仍是价格。
- 水刺路线的便利：粘胶也好、PET 三叶形也好，**在水刺机上直接梳理进去就行**。
