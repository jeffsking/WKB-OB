   • The commercial product form is **airlaid paper（无尘纸）**, defined by the Chinese product standard as the material supplied as **jumbo rolls or sheets** for disposable hygiene products, used in **wipes, diaper acquisition/absorption layers and absorbent cores**, and split into **普通无尘纸 (cellulose-only)** and **合成无尘纸 (with SAP)**（源：GB-T 24292-2009 Airlaid Paper for Sanitary Products（卫生用品用无尘纸））.
