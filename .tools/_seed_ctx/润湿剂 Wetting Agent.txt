--- [[The Role of Surfactants]]  (4 句)
   • Chapter 5 (pp. 166–215) is the formulation chapter: it builds the full causal chain surfactant → interfacial adsorption → free-energy change → wetting/penetration kinetics → macroscopic absorbency, and it separates the two most-confused product roles — wetting agents and rewetting agents — whose optimum molecular structures are actively opposed. Directly actionable for fluff-pulp treatment and core-layer chemistry.（中文：第 5 章建立"表面活性剂→界面吸附→界面自由能→润湿与渗透动力学→宏观吸液性"的完整因果链，并把最易混淆的润湿剂与再润湿剂做了严格区分，直接对应绒毛浆与芯
   • **Scope.** "The use of surfactants provides one of the most powerful means available to the practitioner for altering absorbency, either to promote it or to suppress it."
- **Definition by function, not by structure.** High interfacial adsorption at low bulk concentration is what makes a molecule a surfactant; not every amphiphile qualifies.
- **CMC is the operating window.** Below CMC, linear monomer activity; near CMC, saturated interfacial adsorption (best free-energy reduction) — saturation 
   • **Dose near CMC, never above it.** Extra surfactant above CMC is spent on micelles that cannot wet — a direct, quantifiable overrun in formulation cost.
- **Design the hydrophile position, not just the chain length.** Central hydrophilic group (C7–C7) beats the straight C14 chain on wetting time; the molecular topology argument is worth more than the carbon count.
- **Don't sell cellulose a wetting agent.** At 0° contact angle the fibre is already saturated; any measured gain is impurity removal
   • 父书：Absorbent Technology

--- [[Differentiable CFD and AI Surrogate Solvers（可微分 CFD 与 AI 代理求解器）]]  (2 句)
   • 1. **可微分路径（differentiable path）**：求解器本身由 PyTorch/JAX/Julia AD 承载，PDE 离散与数值格式进入计算图 → 损失函数可对求解结果求导 → 神经网络（闭合模型、源项、边界模型）可用物理梯度训练。工程价值在于**训练目标与物理一致**，而不是只拟合数据。
   - 后验训练（a posteriori training）：把 NN 闭合模型嵌入 LES 后按实际模拟结果训练，而非先离线拟合再插入——避免训练/部署分布不一致。
2. **嵌入路径（embedding path）**：保留 OpenFOAM 等成熟求解器作计算核心，把 DNN 插到特定环节——替代 RANS 的线性动量耗散项、预测 LES 动态 Smagorinsky 系数，或在求解器运行中在线训练（IN_SITU）。
3. **神经算子路径（neural operator path）**：FNO / KNO 等学习的是**函数到函数的映射**（与离散分辨率无关），PINNs 以 PDE 残差作损失，PDE-Net 走机理融合；优势是推理快，代价是外推与守恒性需额外约束
   • **教训一（选型按需求分叉）**：要梯度反传与模型优化 → 可微分求解器（JAX-Fluids / PhiFlow）；要复用 OpenFOAM 生态 → 嵌入路线（DeepFlame / TensorFlowFoam）；要全栈 AI 与极速推理 → 神经算子（MindSpore Flow / DeepCFD）；要自主仿真与物理发现 → AI Agent + VLM 验证。
- **教训二（加速倍数是口径陷阱）**：「3 个数量级」类数字的比较基线不同（稳态层流 vs 通用求解器、网格数、硬件），**跨项目倍率不可比**。
- **教训三（守恒性优先）**：代理模型进入工程流前必须检查**质量守恒与稳定性**；NeuralDEM 明确宣称单 GPU 实时且守恒，说明守恒是被当作卖点而非默认属性。
- **待补 **：各框架的开源许可证与维护活跃度；对多孔介质/可变孔隙率/毛细驱动的支持程度；与现有吸收模型的耦合路径。
