--- [[Industrial Drying Ch1 Principles and Classification]]  (3 句)
   • Drying is governed by two simultaneous subprocesses:
- **Process 1 (External):** Transfer of energy (heat) from environment to evaporate surface moisture
- **Process 2 (Internal):** Transfer of internal moisture to the surface and its evaporation
   • **Hygiene implication:** In diaper cores, internal moisture transport (capillary/vapor diffusion through fluff pulp and SAP) is usually the rate-limiting step at low final moisture — surface air conditions matter far less than residence time and bed depth.
   • **Hygiene implication:** Bound moisture sets the equilibrium moisture content and hence the floor of achievable dryness in cellulose fluff and SAP.

--- [[Preemie Diaper Absorption Test Methods & Parameters（早产儿纸尿裤吸收性能测试方法与参数）]]  (3 句)
   • 文件整合 GB/T 28004.1-2021、Cradle（TTM-00148）、Surface Moisture（TTM-00302）、Low/High Pressure Rewet（TTM-00623）、Retention Capacity（STM-00033）五法在早产儿场景下的要求，并附北美早产儿详细参数与可查到的滞留能力数值。
   • 逐法结论：**GB/T 28004.1**——标准范围虽含低出生体重尿不湿，但**不考核渗透与防侧漏性能**，无直接适用的早产儿注液参数。**TTM-00148 Cradle**——有，注液量/速度/注液点/次数按尺码与市场预定义。**TTM-00302 Surface Moisture**——注液量继承自 TTM-00148，不单独定义早产儿表，方法流速 7.5 mL/s。**TTM-00623 Low/High Rewet**——推荐用于早产儿回渗，常与 Cradle 组合支撑 Micro/Nano 干爽宣称。**STM-00033 Retention**——推荐用于早产儿滞留能力，是总吸收容量的「金标准」，适用整片产品。
   • 回渗实测（TTM-00623，Huggies 早产儿，新参数）：Nano Preemie 低压 0.03 g / 高压 0.05 g；Micro Preemie 0.03 / 0.06 g；Preemie 0.04 / **0.99 g**——**Preemie 档高压回渗跳升约 25 倍**，是全表最刺眼的异常点，提示该尺码在高压段存在容量瓶颈。Surface Moisture 未检到北美早产儿专项实测值。

   • 上下两片 MPM 均为 **Unikorn、20 gsm**，差异只在宽度与位置；两者是本批**供应链最稳**的物料（唯二提前到货）。真正的隐忧有两个：**① 试产 20 gsm 高于验收规格 15–18 gsm，原文要求确认适用性；② 本轮只复测含水率与爬坡，基重/STT/返渗缺测**——所以"MPM 够不够格"这个问题，**目前既没有结论、也没有完整数据**。水刺 LSN 是一个方向明确（导流强）但代价明确（返渗高）的替代项。

--- [[Fibers and Fibrous Materials]]  (2 句)
   • **Fibrous material, defined.** A substrate of fibres in which 'the spaces between the component fibers, referred to as pores, play a key role in their physical properties', with porosity typically 0.6–0.9 or higher.
- **Two sorption modes.** Bulk adsorption/absorption into the fibre, and capillary sorption between fibres; the latter dominates nonwovens and is set by wetting and surface energy, since 'the fiber surfaces constitute the pore walls'.
- **The dual-dependence principle.** Performance

--- [[Handbook of Industrial Drying]]  (2 句)
   • 1. **机械脱水优先**：蒸发 1kg 水需 2260kJ，机械脱水仅 1-70kJ/kg——能压掉的水不要去蒸发
2. **结合水/自由水二分**：残湿下限受物料湿分结合态决定，不是想设多低就多低
3. **恒速-降速段与临界含水率**：选型、放大与缺陷诊断都绕不开'谁在控速'
4. **干燥改变材料结构**：角质化/结壳——高温/过干不可逆改变柔软度、溶胀度、强度
5. **中试放大必须**：任何凭文献值定干燥器的行为都是最高风险动作
6. **能耗与成本最终裁决**：一切选型在 kJ/kg 水与 $/t 干品上同台竞技
   • **控制角质化**：flash-dryer 下限含水率以下过热→热交联、难解纤；打浆/解纤能耗（kWh/BDt）与 CSF 作为隐藏角质化 KPI
- **设定热窗**：非粘合蓬松木浆控温 <150°C 保溶胀性；>150°C 才用于湿法/粘合芯体
- **SAP 终段温度**：须低于（水塑化）Tg 以防过交联杀溶胀

--- [[Industrial Drying Ch50 Solid-Liquid Separation]]  (2 句)
   • **Flocculation/conditioning** (polyelectrolytes, pH, temperature) to enlarge particles is the **cheapest way** to lower residual cake moisture before the dryer.
   • **Key insight:** Expression pressures of 0.6–2.5 MPa and the membrane filter-press (two-step: filter then consolidate) are the mechanical dewatering tools that get cake moisture below what filtration alone achieves — **the last, cheapest water before the dryer**.
