--- [[Development of the nonwovens industry]]  (1 句)
   • Nonwovens resist a single clean definition — the field is convergent and boundary-crossing.（无纺布无单一洁净定义，属跨界聚合产业。）
- Three process families trace to three mother industries: drylaid→textiles, wetlaid→papermaking, polymer-laid→polymer extrusion.（三工艺族分别源自纺织、造纸、高分子挤出。）
- Hygiene is the dominant end-use, justifying the book's hygiene-centred lens.（卫生用品是主导终端。）
