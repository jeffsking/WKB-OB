--- [[US5197958 Wetness Indicating Diaper]]  (8 句)
   • **Thermal-mediation wetness indication for diapers（以温度为媒介的尿布湿态指示）** — a *reusable cloth diaper* carrying a thermochromic ink on its **outer surface**, triggered by the heat of the excreted liquid conducted through a layer whose thermal conductivity changes on wetting.
   • **This is the thermochromic route, not hydrochromic** — the trigger is *temperature*, and the chromic material is **thermochromic ink**（Claim 8）.
   • Ink source: "The thermochromic ink that is used is similar to the thermochromic ink of **U.S. Pat. No. 4,826,550, assigned to Sakura Color Corporation**."
   • **Microencapsulation（微胶囊化）** — required because the fatty acid is liquid and would otherwise be lost: "In-situ polymerization with **urea formalin resin** used as wall material"; the TC slurry is an aqueous dispersion containing about **"45% wt. of capsule particles"**, then formulated with a **water-based binder** into a water-based thermochromic ink. --- [[US20070270773A1 Thermochromic Wetness Indicator]]  (7 句)
   • **Thermochromic wetness indication on absorbent articles/garments（吸收制品与服装上的热致变色湿态指示）**.
   • Background art cited in the source: US 4,834,733（liquid-shrinkage mechanical wetness meter）, US 6,603,403（remote wetness signalling）, US 4,484,573（wetness alarm）, US 6,635,797（liquid-soluble ink + interactive toilet-training graphic）, US 6,657,099（urine-soluble sheet）, **and directly US 5,197,958 and 5,389,093 to Howell**（"reusable diapers with thermally sensitive indicators comprised of thermochromic ink(s) using conventional silk screening techniques"）.
   • **Thermal activation by the temperature of the excreted liquid（以排泄物温度热激活）** — a *thermochromic*, not hydrochromic, route:
   • Mechanism chain（机理链）: urine/body waste contacts the article → **local temperature rises** → the thermochromic compound crosses its transition → **visual colour change**. Claim 8 requires the activation to occur "upon exposure to **temperature change generated by urine, and/or bodily wastes and/or bodily excretions**, either with **direct or indirect contact** of the absorbent article and/or garment and/or the thermochromic wetness indicator(s)."

--- [[US8273939 Windowed Removable Sensor Absorbent Article]]  (6 句)
   • **Non-electronic, visually-read health indication integrated into a disposable absorbent article（集成到一次性吸收制品的非电子目视健康指示）** — a **window** in the article's outer surface allowing a **removable thermochromic sensor** to be read from outside.
   • **Thermochromic clearing-point transition read through a window（透过窗口读取的热致变色 clearing point 转变）** — two distinct chromic mechanisms are described:
   • 1. **Cholesteric liquid crystal, clearing-point type（胆甾型液晶·清亮点型，温度不敏感型）** — "常温有色、升温变透明": at normal temperature the LC reflects a colour; on reaching its **clearing point** it becomes **transparent**. This is the mechanism used in the worked EXAMPLE.
2. **Temperature-sensitive liquid crystal（温度敏感型液晶）** — colour sequence "无色→红→橙→黄→绿→蓝→紫→无色" as temperature rises.
3. **Thermochromic inks / leuco dyes（热致变色油墨 / 隐色染料）** — leuco dyes "可直接混入薄膜、无纺布和弹性体"（may be incorporated directly into films, nonwovens 
   • Source note: the preamble's "for visually detecting health-related conditions" is a **use/intended-purpose limitation**; the source observes that infringing determination normally still requires the structural features. The claim is a five-element combination: **article + window + removable + thermochromic + ~99.5–102 °F + closed material list**.

--- [[变色油墨]]  (5 句)
   • 1. **热致变色（温敏）**：主流为 **leuco 染料微胶囊体系**——供电子体（leuco 染料）: 受电子体 : 脂肪酸 ≈ 1:5:50 的配比，通过熔融/结晶的可逆转变实现显色/褪色。典型用于尿湿指示（体温或尿温触发）。
2. **力致变色（力敏）**：机械力改变分子排列或光子晶体结构导致颜色变化。代表应用为**拉伸变色**（K-C US7704589B2，已失效）与液晶弹性体变色纤维（可机洗应变可视化）。
3. **湿敏/化学比色**：pH 或离子强度变化引发比色反应（如 CN114209501A 的 pH 纳米微胶囊纳米纤维膜）。
4. **载体形态要求**：印刷油墨/涂层（丝网印刷于底膜外侧、弹性侧片等），或作为薄膜/纤维基材的负载相。**功能层多在非皮肤面**，对材料安全性要求相对低。
5. **与本库其他方向的接口**：变色油墨可作为「视觉反馈」层与压力传感（力敏）、湿态指示（温敏/湿敏）耦合；BNC 膜/纤维可作比色指示基材。
   • （详见 Thermochromic Microcapsule (Leuco Dye System)、Wetness Indicator Glue）
   • **MVP A（温敏，P1）**：外层印刷/标签；非结构功能；门＝30–37 ℃ 内颜色稳定变化 + **>70%** 消费者正确解读"过冷/舒适/过热"。
- **MVP B（湿敏，P1）**：与现有尿显**相邻**的湿致变色窗口/贴片；门＝冲击后 ≤60 s 稳定显色 + 与传统尿显**可区分**；4 周包装后无异常变色。
- **MVP C（复合，P2）**：双区显示；**学习型原型**；若解读失败或错误率高，立即回退单功能方案。
- **排序规则**：**不替代**现有尿显；复合方案只在单功能验证后启动；台架必须**干态与湿态双测并量化误触发率**（最重要的 MVP 出口门）。
