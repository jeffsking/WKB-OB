--- [[聚氨酯 Polyurethane]]  (3 句)
   • **Waterborne shift.** Waterborne PU adhesives emit **no VOC** and are nonflammable, hence "more environmentally friendly"; the shift was driven by rising solvent prices（源：Adhesive Handbook Part 3 Adhesive Classes）。
