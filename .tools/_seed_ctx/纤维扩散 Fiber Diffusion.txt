--- [[Fick 定律]]  (6 句)
   • Diffusion is the process by which matter is transported from one part of a system to another as a result of **random molecular motion**（扩散是分子随机运动驱动的物质从系统一部分传输到另一部分的过程）（源：Diffusion Equations (Crank Ch.1)）. **Fick's first law** states the transfer rate is proportional to the concentration gradient, , by analogy with heat conduction (Fourier's law). **Fick's second law**, （一维恒 D）, is described as **the single core PDE** of the whole Mathematics of Diffusion reference, on which every chapter builds.
   • **Derivation chain:** physical motivation → molecular random walk → statistical averaging gives net flux → Fick assumption → differential mass conservation → conservative form → simplification under constant D（源：Diffusion Equations (Crank Ch.1)）.
   • **SAP swelling is not plain Fickian.** The **Alfrey-Gurnee-Lloyd three-way classification** splits behaviour by the ratio of diffusion to relaxation rate: **Case I（Fickian）** when diffusion << relaxation; **Case II** when diffusion >> relaxation; **non-Fickian** when they are comparable. SAP maps onto the sequence 干态玻璃态 → Case II → 非菲克 → 橡胶态 Case I, which is the physical root of the **S-shaped sorption curve**（源：Non-Fickian Diffusion (Crank Ch.11)）.    • **Scope limits:** Fick's law applies only to isotropic media — a composite core's MD/CD difference requires a **diffusion tensor **; forced-convection scenarios (e.g. wet-wipe wiping) need an added convection term（源：Diffusion Equations (Crank Ch.1)）.
