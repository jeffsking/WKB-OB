--- [[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch09 Heat-setting of Thermoplastic Fibres]]  (7 句)
   • Drawn but unset filaments do not possess **dimensional stability 尺寸稳定性**: they tend to shrink, crease and wrinkle. This chapter explains how hot dry air, steam or hot water — "by the action of heat in the absence or presence of swelling agents, with or without tension" (p.188) — give the fibre its **set 定形**. It classifies set into three classes, identifies **partial melting and recrystallization 部分熔融与重结晶** as the dominant mechanism of permanent set, analyses the two-stage nature of PET shrinkag
   • Mechanism of permanent set (p.190–191): "The most important mechanism of permanent setting in crystalline polymers is through partial melting and recrystallization, whereby partial melting of small, imperfect crystals takes place below the melting point to allow the growth of larger more perfect and regular crystalline units."
   • Why it cannot be undone (p.191): "the mechanism of heat-setting is to lead the structure to a position of lower minimum energy from the state prior to heat-setting." Hearle's crystal-size vs. melting-frequency diagram is cited with the plain statement that "small or imperfect crystals will melt and larger crystals will grow. This is a typical process of annealing" (p.192).
   • Source wording (p.196): "shrinkage in PET is a two-stage process involving a rapid initial stage in which shrinkage is associated with disorientation in the amorphous zones, followed by a crystalline stage during which chain folding... occurs." Chain folding is evidenced by a specific IR absorption peak after high-temperature annealing (Koenig & Hannon; Wilson) (pp.195–196). 因此收缩与伸长之间没有唯一的一一对应关系 — no unique shrinkage/elongation correspondence exists.

--- [[Ebewole Polymer Science Ch04 Thermal Transitions in Polymers]]  (4 句)
   • Chapter 4 is the **thermal–structure–property bridge 热-结构-性能桥梁**: it takes the glass transition temperature **Tg** and the crystalline melting point **Tm** as its two axes and explains why polymer thermal behaviour differs fundamentally from that of simple small molecules.
   • Three-state model for an amorphous polymer: **hard glassy solid → soft rubbery material → viscous liquid 硬玻璃态 → 软橡胶态 → 粘性液体**. For a semicrystalline polymer, between Tg and Tm the material "is composed of rigid crystals and a rubbery amorphous phase" — 半结晶聚合物在 Tg 与 Tm 之间是"刚性晶粒 + 橡胶态无定形相"的复合体。
   • Other factors enumerated: **branching 支化**, **crystallinity 结晶度**, **plasticization 塑化**, plus external factors (pressure, test rate 压力与测试速率) and **copolymerization 共聚** as an industrial Tg-tuning tool (Ch.8).
