--- [[交联 CMC]]  (2 句)
   • **交联 CMC（cross-linked CMC）** is the vault's most mature and most commercialised **cellulose-based superabsorbent route**, commercialised as **Hercules Aqualon / CLD**. It is made by etherifying cellulose (with **monochloroacetic acid**) and then cross-linking it, and it **lifts absorbency from pulp's 1–2 g/g to 10–25 g/g**（源：Cross-linked Cellulose and Its Derivatives）. It belongs to the "modified cellulose" family whose retention under load exceeds plain cellulose（源：Fibers and Fibrous Materials）
   • Cross-linked Cellulose and Its Derivatives — cross-linked CMC synthesis, DS/cross-link trade-off, Hercules Aqualon / CLD
- Fibers and Fibrous Materials — modified CMC retention 16–21% vs 1.0%; "wins precisely where SAP loses"

--- [[Fibers and Fibrous Materials]]  (1 句)
   • **Fibrous material, defined.** A substrate of fibres in which 'the spaces between the component fibers, referred to as pores, play a key role in their physical properties', with porosity typically 0.6–0.9 or higher.
- **Two sorption modes.** Bulk adsorption/absorption into the fibre, and capillary sorption between fibres; the latter dominates nonwovens and is set by wetting and surface energy, since 'the fiber surfaces constitute the pore walls'.
- **The dual-dependence principle.** Performance
