--- [[ATB Topsheet with Bico Spunbond for 600 mpm Ultrasonic Lamination]]  (3 句)
   • A literature-based **fibre-mix strategy for a laminated topsheet**: a 25 gsm carded ATB web on a 12 gsm PE/PP bico spunbond carrier, ultra-sonically bonded and 3D-pattern-formed in-line at 600 m/min. It is the batch's "**bonding and pattern reliability constraint**" page.
本页是**层压面层的纤维配比策略**：25 gsm 梳理 ATB 网 + 12 gsm PE/PP 双组分纺粘载体，在 600 m/min 车速下在线超声粘合与 3D 花型成型，回答「**粘合与花型可靠性如何约束配比**」。
   • The working hypothesis is a three-fibre blend in the 25 gsm ATB web — **55–65 wt% 2D ES** (softness, fine touch, more bondable surface), **20–30 wt% 4D eccentric ES** (bulk, void volume, pattern-depth support, resilience under compression) and **12–18 wt% 2D rounded trilobal mono-PET** (capillary guidance, faster strike-through, better urine spreading) — with the 12 gsm PE/PP bico spunbond kept as the ultrasonic carrier/support layer.

   • 1. **无纺布（§17.1）**：spunbond/meltblown 是 melt spinning 的工程化延伸——PP 熔体经过 spinneret 后被高速气流牵伸成细纤维。本章给出熔纺的物理框架（熔融 → 过滤 → 喷丝板 → 牵伸取向），但**未给出 meltblown 纤维直径、气流速度、驻极工艺**等参数，这些需另查 [待验证]。
