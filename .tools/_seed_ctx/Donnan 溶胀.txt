--- [[Industrial Drying Ch28 Osmotic Dehydration and SAP Physics]]  (2 句)
   • This chapter is the **single most direct bridge to superabsorbent-polymer (SAP) science** in the Handbook. The osmotic pressure framework, semipermeable membrane analogy, and electrolyte ion effect are exactly the physics family of SAP swelling/deswelling governed by Flory-Rehner equilibrium and Donnan osmotic pressure.
   • **SAP transfer:** This is the seed of Donnan equilibrium — mobile ions distributed across a charged, semipermeable matrix raise Π. It is precisely why SAP (a fixed-charge polyelectrolyte) swells in water but **deswells in saline**: the fixed –COO⁻ groups plus counterions create a Donnan osmotic pressure that is suppressed by external salt.

--- [[毛细压力 Capillary Pressure]]  (1 句)
   • **毛细压力法（Capillary Pressure Method）**：试样置于与储液池恒连通的载物台上，垂直移动载物台改变**静水压头**，同步称量持液量并归一化为**饱和度 saturation（g/g）**，得 **height–saturation** 曲线；**下降台面得吸收支（absorption），上升台面得解吸支（desorption）**（源：Capillary Pressure Method（毛细压力法·解吸毛细压力与解吸能量））。
- **读数规则**：**毛细压越高 → 孔越想把液体留住**；**解吸能量越低 → 纤维越容易"晾干"**。设计原则：**摄入层（面层/导流层）的毛细性与润湿性必须低于液体最终被锁住的地方（芯体）**（源：Capillary Pressure Method（毛细压力法·解吸毛细压力与解吸能量））。
- **速率与容量解耦**：**孔径控制吸收速率（经 Hagen–Poiseuille ∝ r²），孔体积控制容量** —— 优化其一必罚另一个，需中值最优化（源：Liquid Flow in Porous Structures）。
