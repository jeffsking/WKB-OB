--- [[莱赛尔 Lyocell]]  (5 句)
   • **Lyocell / Tencel is a regenerated cellulose fibre spun from a direct solvent, NMMO (N-methylmorpholine-N-oxide)** — a route that **omits xanthation and ripening**, such that *viscose's* fibre strength and wet modulus are exceeded, while it **retains a propensity for fibrillation**, verbatim *"propensity for some degree of fibrillation"*（源：Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch17 Rayon Fibres）。Property comparisons are tabulated there as Table 17.12 (Camilon) and Table 17.14 (Tencel)（源：Manufactured Fi
   • **Solvent route.** Lyocell sits under **direct solvent spinning (MMNO/NMMO)**, which needs no derivatisation — unlike the viscose process — and that is why its strength and wet modulus exceed viscose's; the same property set brings the fibrillation liability（源：Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch17 Rayon Fibres）。
   • **Fibrillation mechanism and its two fixes.** The cause is intrinsic: the fibre has **no skin layer** and high crystallinity/orientation, so **water plus friction peels the surface open into fine fibrils**; it is most visible **wet** — invisible dry — so validation must be a **post-wash fabric comparison**（源：原纤化）。Two routes exist: (a) **intrinsic** cross-linking to lock the structure (Lenzing **A100** is the cross-linked type); (b) **process-side management** — tension/friction control in spinni
   • **Hydroentanglement behaviour.** Viscose/Lyocell have **low wet modulus and entangle cheaply**, whereas PP/PET need hydrophilic finishes; and **high jet impact force unlocks the Lyocell-fibrillation microfibre route**（源：Mechanical bonding）。

--- [[Fibers and Fibrous Materials]]  (2 句)
   • **Fibrous material, defined.** A substrate of fibres in which 'the spaces between the component fibers, referred to as pores, play a key role in their physical properties', with porosity typically 0.6–0.9 or higher.
- **Two sorption modes.** Bulk adsorption/absorption into the fibre, and capillary sorption between fibres; the latter dominates nonwovens and is set by wetting and surface energy, since 'the fiber surfaces constitute the pore walls'.
