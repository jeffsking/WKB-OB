--- [[交联密度 Crosslink Density]]  (7 句)
   • **交联密度 ν_c** is the density of cross-link junction points in a three-dimensional polymer network, and it is the **pivotal control parameter** of SAP / hydrogel design（源：Acrylic-Based Superabsorbent Polymers）。Its governing relationship is **Q_max ∝ 1/ν_c** — swelling extent falls as cross-link density (and the chain-elasticity constraint) rises（源：Smart Polymers Ch14 Hydrogels in Microfluidics）。For cellulose derivatives, **cross-link density fixes the swelling ceiling while DS fixes the solubility
   • **The double-sided effect — there is an optimum.** Cross-link density too low → gel exudation (凝胶层析出), swelling collapse and rising system viscosity; too high → too many cross-link points, insufficient porosity and inadequate absorbency — **存在最优值**（源：Acrylic-Based Superabsorbent Polymers）。Stated independently for hydrogels: low cross-linking → high absorbency but poor structural stability; high cross-linking → good retention but restricted absorbency（源：Hydrogel-based Superabsorbent Polymers）。
   • **Cross-linking routes.** Chemical cross-linking (bifunctional/multifunctional cross-linkers, chain-transfer cross-linking) plus radiation cross-linking (β-ray, UV)（源：Acrylic-Based Superabsorbent Polymers）。For hydrogel SAP the routes are chemical cross-linking, **physical cross-linking** (hydrogen bonding; e.g. chitosan with calcium alginate, cellulose) and composite modification with nanomaterials（源：Hydrogel-based Superabsorbent Polymers）。**Green-synthesis note:** microwave polymerisation gives
   • **Negotiate against load.** Cross-link density buys strength and insolubility **at the price of capacity**; because a real absorbent core is compressed, the optimum shifts **higher** than free-swell testing implies（源：Cross-linked Cellulose and Its Derivatives）。

--- [[达西定律 Darcy's Law]]  (5 句)
   • Darcy's law（达西定律）is the governing relation for flow in porous media as used in the vault: **q = −K·∇P，K = k/μ**（流率正比于压力梯度，K 为传导系数 = 渗透率 k / 流体黏度 μ）（源：Liquid Flow in Porous Structures）。In absorbent systems the absorption driving force is not an external pressure but **capillary pressure**: **p_c = 2γcosθ/r**，外加重力或压力只起次要作用（源：Liquid Flow in Porous Structures）。
   • **Capillary-driven absorption 毛细驱动的吸收.** 「The driving force for absorption of the bulk of the liquid into an absorbing material ... is the capillary pressure」，重力或外压为次要项（源：Porous Structure and Liquid Flow Models）。
