--- [[Modern Chemistry of the Ancient Chemical Processing of Organic Dyes and Pigments]]  (4 句)
   • **EN**: A scholarly review of the advanced chemistry applied by ancient dyers to organic dyes and pigments from flora and fauna sources. Covers ionic/covalent/intermolecular bonding, coordinate complexation, enzymatic hydrolysis, photochemical chromogenic precursor oxidation, anaerobic bacterial fermentative reduction, and redox reactions. Some textile dyeings survive after six millennia. Cross-references with hygiene products in natural dyes, plant-based materials, and botanical extracts.
   • **中文**: 古代染料师应用于植物/动物源有机染料与颜料的高级化学综述——涵盖离子/共价/分子间键合、配位络合、酶促水解、光化学变色前体氧化、厌氧细菌还原、氧化还原反应。部分纺织染色存活六千年。与卫生用品的交叉点在于天然染料、植物基材料与植物提取物。
   • 1. **古代染色化学机理**——离子键/共价键/分子间键合、配位络合、酶促水解、光化学、厌氧还原、氧化还原
2. **植物源染料**——黄/红/蓝色素的分子结构与染色机理
3. **动物源染料**——鳞虫红、海蜗牛紫的化学机理
4. **古代工艺控制**——温度与酸碱 pH 控制、染料浴条件优化
