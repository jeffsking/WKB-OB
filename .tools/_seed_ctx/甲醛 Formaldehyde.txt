--- [[Cross-linked Cellulose and Its Derivatives]]  (3 句)
   • **The framework.** DS fixes the solubility ceiling, cross-link density fixes the swelling ceiling; together they define whether a cellulose derivative is a superabsorbent at all. Both must be tuned jointly.
- **Cross-linked CMC — the workhorse.** Etherification with monochloroacetic acid (up to 8 mol/mol cellulose) plus neutralisation with '0.5 mol caustic/mol cellulose to insure attainment of a DS (carboxyalkyl substituents) of about 0.4 or above'. Cross-linkers: epichlorohydrin (~0.5 mol/mol),
   • **DS is the binary switch.** Below ~0.4 the material dissolves and is useless as a sheet; at ~0.4 it is insoluble and highly absorbent. DS is the single most consequential specification to demand from a supplier.
- **Cross-link density has to be negotiated against load.** It buys strength and insolubility at the price of capacity; because a real core is compressed, the optimum shifts higher than free-swell testing implies.

--- [[GB 43631-2023 General Safety Technical Specification for Infants and Children Paper Products（婴幼儿及儿童用纸品基本安全技术规范）]]  (2 句)
   • China's **mandatory chemical-safety floor**（强制国标）for all paper products for children ≤6 — the single table that wipes, diapers and tissue SKUs must clear on heavy metals, FWAs, acrylamide, formaldehyde, solvents, preservatives, phthalates and aromatic amines; it is the standard that GB/T 27728.2-2024 delegates its "safety indicators" clause to.
   • GB 43631-2023 (ICS 85.010 / CCS Y30, 工信部) is a mandatory national standard applying to infant/children paper products manufactured or sold in China. It classifies products into 生活用纸品 (tissue, wipes, diapers) and 文化用纸品 (paper/board/books) and sets one shared chemical baseline plus category-specific rows. The shared floor: four heavy metals, migratable fluorescent substances "无", acrylamide ≤0.1 mg/kg, and a 24-species carcinogenic aromatic amine list (Annex A) at ≤20 mg/kg for printed/dyed items.

--- [[GB-T 34448-2017 Determination of Formaldehyde in Tissue Paper and Disposable Products（生活用纸及纸制品 甲醛含量的测定）]]  (2 句)
   • The **method standard** for formaldehyde in tissue/wipes/diapers — its HPLC branch (LOD 6.0 mg/kg) is the enforcement method behind the ≤6 mg/kg formaldehyde limits in GB 43631-2023 and GB/T 28004.1-2021, and its spectrophotometry branch (LOD 10 mg/kg) serves tissue-class limits.
   • GB/T 34448-2017 (SAC/TC 141) defines water extraction of free and partially hydrolysable formaldehyde from disposable paper products, with two quantification routes. The acetylacetone method derivatizes formaldehyde to a yellow chromophore read at 412 nm (LOD 10 mg/kg, double-acetone/dimedone confirmation test available for colored samples); the HPLC method derivatizes with 2,4-dinitrophenylhydrazine at 60 ℃ for 30 min and reads the hydrazone at 355 nm on a C18 column (LOD 6.0 mg/kg).
