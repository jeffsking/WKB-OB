--- [[Nonwoven fabric finishing]]  (3 句)
   • Chapter 8 (A. Ahmed) addresses the finishing stage where nonwoven substrates are transformed into value-added, fitness-for-purpose products via wet/dry finishing, chemical finishes and lamination — the chapter where hygiene function (softening, rewetting, barrier, lotion delivery) is actually engineered.（中文：第 8 章讲后整理——通过湿/干整理、化学整理与复合层压，把无纺布基材转化为功能化成品，是卫生用品功能（柔软、回渗、阻隔、加液）真正被设计的环节。）

- Plasma fixes PP's poor wettability dryly; lamination engineers backsheet barrier/breathability.（等离子干法改善 PP 润湿；层压设计底膜阻隔/透气。）

--- [[Co-extruded Elastic Film Feature（共挤弹性膜弹性部件）]]  (1 句)
   • **Activation is the invention's switch（激活即开关）:** the film ships and stores in a non-elastic, easy-to-handle state and becomes elastic **only after mechanical stretching**（源：EP0820747A1 Absorbent Article with Elastic Feature (P&G)）. **Co-extrusion beats lamination** because it delivers the multi-layer structure in one process step from the same die head. Removing the nonwoven layer removes two manufacturing pains — weak nonwoven tensile strength and fibre/glue contamination.
