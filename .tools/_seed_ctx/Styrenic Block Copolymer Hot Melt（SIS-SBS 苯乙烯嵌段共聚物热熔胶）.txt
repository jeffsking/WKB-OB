--- [[BM Indicator Design — Viscosity & Cost（便指示·流变与成本）]]  (7 句)

   • Rheology is specified as near-Newtonian on purpose: 3,200 mPa·s @ 120 °C (2,100 @ 130 °C, 1,400 @ 140 °C), Cross index n = 0.88 (spec ≥ 0.85), λ ≤ 2 × 10⁻⁴ s, flow activation energy 56 kJ/mol, no high-MW styrenic block copolymer.    • Thermal load is the binding constraint: the contact interface reaches 69 °C at a 125 °C melt (comfortable), but the bulk equilibrium temperature reaches 85 °C at 125 °C and 90 °C at 135 °C — and a microporous CaCO₃-filled PE film loses WVTR irreversibly once the drawn structure anneals above ~90 °C. --- [[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch09 Heat-setting of Thermoplastic Fibres]]  (6 句)
   • Drawn but unset filaments do not possess **dimensional stability 尺寸稳定性**: they tend to shrink, crease and wrinkle. This chapter explains how hot dry air, steam or hot water — "by the action of heat in the absence or presence of swelling agents, with or without tension" (p.188) — give the fibre its **set 定形**. It classifies set into three classes, identifies **partial melting and recrystallization 部分熔融与重结晶** as the dominant mechanism of permanent set, analyses the two-stage nature of PET shrinkag
   • Mechanism of permanent set (p.190–191): "The most important mechanism of permanent setting in crystalline polymers is through partial melting and recrystallization, whereby partial melting of small, imperfect crystals takes place below the melting point to allow the growth of larger more perfect and regular crystalline units."
   • Why it cannot be undone (p.191): "the mechanism of heat-setting is to lead the structure to a position of lower minimum energy from the state prior to heat-setting." Hearle's crystal-size vs. melting-frequency diagram is cited with the plain statement that "small or imperfect crystals will melt and larger crystals will grow. This is a typical process of annealing" (p.192).
   • Strength assessment from the source itself: the melting–recrystallization mechanism is strongly supported by multiple independent experiments; the tension effect on Tg/tenacity relies mostly on one group (Valk) and is self-consistent but single-sourced; the triacetate section rests on only two early studies and is rated medium-to-weak.

--- [[Stretch-Triggered Colour Indicator for Diaper Pant Side Panels]]  (6 句)
   • The engineering deep-dive behind the pull-strain fit meter on elastic side panels — it fixes the seven hard requirements, screens seven mechanochromic families, and lands on a single-pass slot-coated **geometric optical mask** with cost, safety and FTO closed out. This is the most implementation-complete document of the Mechanochromic block.
   • Part Two screens seven families against applicator, cycle life, cost and tox. Only the geometric optical mask survives (TRL 8, native slot-coatable, 1.0× cost index, no new tox package); craze/cavitation whitening is Phase 2 (1.4×), interference pigment is backup (3×); polymer opal (25×), stretchable DBR (15×), cholesteric LCE (40×) and molecular mechanophores (60×) are rejected — the latter because ~240 pN per bond demands covalent incorporation into a cross-linked network, fades after ~3 cycle
   • Part Three specifies the selected design: the elastic laminate's own corrugation (2.2× bonded, 1.2 mm relaxed pitch) acts as a mechanical shutter under a rigid, CD-zoned opaque hot melt (TB-A). One equation, φ(ε) = 1 − 2a/[λ₀(1+ε)], sets all three thresholds, and the three coat weights (7.5 / 9.0 / 11.0 g/m²) come from one shim whose land lengths (18.0 / 15.0 / 12.0 mm) give a 1.00 : 1.20 : 1.50 flow ratio — within 2 % of the required 1.00 : 1.20 : 1.47.
   • Part Four covers the process: contact slot die, 200 µm shim (a 100 µm gap would have driven 165 bar and stalled the pump), 400 m/min, 12 °C chill roll, 0.4 s dwell, and a full setpoint table. Sensitivity analysis concludes that three variables matter (panel lamination pre-stretch, coat weight, web tension) and melt viscosity is self-compensating.

--- [[BM Urine Indicator Concept（尿+便双指示概念）]]  (5 句)
   • Signal selection compares urine and infant BM across seven properties. pH is a WEAK discriminator (ranges overlap); titratable acidity is GOOD (buffer capacity, not pH); bile pigment is BEST (binary presence); active enzyme is BEST but needs an aqueous-coated substrate; rheology (urine ≈1 mPa·s vs BM 3–60 Pa·s) is GOOD and drives sampling geometry; temperature is NONE; head-space volatiles are BLOCKED by the granular.
   • Mechanism options are ranked by cost per unit of certainty: **A titratable-acidity lane** (buffered halochromic hot melt, ~US$0.4/1,000, Phase 1, sensitivity ~65–80 %, misses low-VFA formula stool), **B bile-pigment mordant lane** (quaternised cationic polymer as an anion-exchange lane, ~US$0.3/1,000, binary marker independent of feeding mode, but meconium and firm stool release little liquor) and **C protease-cleavage film** (crosslinked gelatin 12–20 µm over a dark printed field, ~US$2.5/1,000
   • The architecture is sample → isolate → react → read: a 10 mm hydrophilic wick at topsheet level in the rear zone; an 18 gsm PE barrier under the wick to stop SAP stealing the sample water and to keep the granular ≥20 mm away in MD; a 90 mm MD travel to the two hot-melt lanes; and read-out under an unprinted clear channel in the backsheet artwork at the rear waist. Two jobs are kept separate — the pH ladder is a load gauge ("change at the right time"), the BM lanes override it ("change now").
   • Phase-1 total capex 合计：US$38–60 k；无车速损失、无新放卷
- Watch item 注意项：cationic-loaded hot melt can char or pre-trip in a hot tank → screen every candidate for **72 h at 150 °C** tank residence before line trial
