--- [[Industrial Drying Ch1 Principles and Classification]]  (5 句)
   • Drying is governed by two simultaneous subprocesses:
- **Process 1 (External):** Transfer of energy (heat) from environment to evaporate surface moisture
- **Process 2 (Internal):** Transfer of internal moisture to the surface and its evaporation
   • **Hygiene implication:** In diaper cores, internal moisture transport (capillary/vapor diffusion through fluff pulp and SAP) is usually the rate-limiting step at low final moisture — surface air conditions matter far less than residence time and bed depth.
   • **Constant-rate period:** Surface evaporation dominates; drying rate independent of internal structure
- **Falling-rate period:** Internal diffusion/capillarity becomes rate-controlling; time scales with square of thickness
   • **Key constraint:** The wet-bulb temperature of the drying air sets the minimum achievable product surface temperature during constant-rate drying (evaporative cooling).

--- [[Industrial Drying Ch15 Impingement Drying]]  (5 句)
   • **Hygiene relevance:** Tissue Yankee hoods, through-air nonwoven bonding ovens, and lotion/coating cure on wipes are all impingement applications.
   • **Key insight:** A 350°C jet can keep a tissue surface far below ignition because the latent heat sink dominates. Once bone-dry, surface temperature climbs toward air temperature — degradation window opens.
   • 1. Choose nozzle configuration (round or slot array)
2. Determine geometry (nozzle size, spacing, distance)
3. Set velocity/temperature/spacing (interrelated; Re > 2000)
4. Compute constant-rate drying rate via empirical correlations with corrections
5. Check surface temperature against degradation/ignition limits
6. Determine recycle ratio via enthalpy balance
   • **Array of Round Nozzles (ARN):** Valid over stated Re/f/H/d ranges, ±15% accuracy
- **Array of Slot Nozzles (ASN):** Similar validity
- Corrections needed for: crossflow, semiconfinement, mass flux, large ΔT, suction, surface motion, oblique impingement, swirl

--- [[Skin Health Treatment on Nonwoven（无纺布护肤处理）]]  (4 句)
   • 1. **四类成分对应四条不同的作用路径**：
   - **ZnO（氧化锌）** —— FDA OTC 专论的**皮肤保护剂成分**；抑制粪便酶与尿液酶、促进伤口愈合，并在低 pH 下与乳酸协同解离出 Zn²⁺，生成乳酸锌（NMF 的已知组分、生物可利用锌）。
   - **pH 调节剂（乳酸 / 苹果酸 / 柠檬酸）** —— 对抗尿液与粪便引起的 pH 上升，把皮肤维持在**目标 pH 4.1–5.8（约 5.5）**；走化妆品路径，是"温和"型皮肤健康宣称的主力。
   - **凡士林（Petrolatum）** —— FDA OTC 专论皮肤保护剂成分，在皮肤表面形成保护层阻隔刺激物。
   - **调理剂 + 益生类（Conditioners + Biotics）** —— 改善皮肤屏障、减少水分流失与炎症，机制多样，多源自化妆品行业。
2. **收益来自"对抗刺激源"而非"增加吸收"**：损伤三要素是**摩擦、过度水合、尿液/粪便+粪便酶**。护肤处理只解决第三项（与部分第二项），因此**它不能替代干爽性与低摩擦**——源资料明确要求皮肤健康收益必须建立在透气、锁液/干爽
   • ✅ **验证记录（2026-09-29）**：三圈失败与可视化放大失败均为**内部实测/试产结论**，但具体数据（摩擦系数、滑顺度数值）未在转写中给出，标 ；供应商名与配方细节属商务敏感，见源摘要页 🔴 标注。参见 Botanical Extract Efficacy Verification（植物提取物功效验证）、Hot-Melt Slot Coating（热熔狭缝涂布）、Skin Contact Pressure（皮肤接触压力）。
   • 供 **KC 卫生巾面层**的**维他命 B5 泛醇**添加量仅 **100–200 ppm** —— "非常少"。
- 供应商口径：**护肤型添加在纤维上，添加量很少，效果有限**。
- 结论：**要真实起效，仍需在布上做 coating** —— 纤维端添加量不足以支撑功效。
   • 1. **纤维端功能化 ≠ 功效**：添加量受纺丝工艺约束（通常几十到几百 ppm），**宣称价值 > 实测功效**；把"宣称"当"功效"是本类项目最大的认知风险。
2. **凉感是一场神经刺激，不是热管理** —— 与真正的相变/温感材料不是同一件事，评估时必须区分。
3. **"越底层越直接"的判断需要代价**：纤维端改性的确定性强，但**开发周期长、起订量大（≥30 吨）、切换废料多**，与布端 coating 的灵活性正好互补。
