   • **The framework.** DS fixes the solubility ceiling, cross-link density fixes the swelling ceiling; together they define whether a cellulose derivative is a superabsorbent at all. Both must be tuned jointly.
- **Cross-linked CMC — the workhorse.** Etherification with monochloroacetic acid (up to 8 mol/mol cellulose) plus neutralisation with '0.5 mol caustic/mol cellulose to insure attainment of a DS (carboxyalkyl substituents) of about 0.4 or above'. Cross-linkers: epichlorohydrin (~0.5 mol/mol),
   • **DS is the binary switch.** Below ~0.4 the material dissolves and is useless as a sheet; at ~0.4 it is insoluble and highly absorbent. DS is the single most consequential specification to demand from a supplier.
- **Cross-link density has to be negotiated against load.** It buys strength and insolubility at the price of capacity; because a real core is compressed, the optimum shifts higher than free-swell testing implies.

--- [[Cross-linked Cellulose and Derivatives]]  (3 句)
   • Chapter VII covers molecular-level chemical modification of cellulose into superabsorbent derivatives — cross-linked CMC (the most commercialized cellulosic route), HEC, phosphonoalkyl/phosphorylated cellulose, sulfonated/oxidized cellulose, microfibrillated cellulose (MFC) and lignin-retaining CTMP — and unifies them under Donnan gel-swelling theory.
   • 本章在分子层面论述纤维素的化学改性超吸水路线——交联 CMC（最成熟的纤维素基路线）、HEC、磷酸化纤维素、磺化/氧化纤维素、微纤丝化纤维素（MFC）及含木质素 CTMP，并以 Donnan 凝胶溶胀理论统一解释。
   • **Cross-linked CMC** is the core technology: etherification (monochloroacetic acid, up to 8 mol/mol cellulose) followed by cross-linking (epichlorohydrin, formaldehyde, glutaraldehyde). **Water-insolubility threshold at low DS ≈ 0.35–0.40**; cross-linked CMC gives 5–25 g/g vs 1–2 g/g for unmodified pulp (p. 278).
