--- [[交联 CMC]]  (5 句)
   • **交联 CMC（cross-linked CMC）** is the vault's most mature and most commercialised **cellulose-based superabsorbent route**, commercialised as **Hercules Aqualon / CLD**. It is made by etherifying cellulose (with **monochloroacetic acid**) and then cross-linking it, and it **lifts absorbency from pulp's 1–2 g/g to 10–25 g/g**（源：Cross-linked Cellulose and Its Derivatives）.    • Chapter 10 is the largest chapter in the book and brings biopolymers into the unified polymer-chemistry framework: **carbohydrates（cellulose → regenerated cellulose → cellulose esters/ethers → starch → heteropolysaccharides）→ proteins → nucleic acids → natural rubber → renewable resources**. 对卫材的直接意义：**绒毛浆（fluff pulp）就是纤维素**，本章给出其 DP、结晶密度、氢键网络与制浆工艺的化学基础；**粘胶纤维、醋酸纤维、Lyocell 类再生纤维素纤维**是面层/导流层原料；**CMC 等纤维素衍生物**是 SAP 的辅助吸水剂。
