--- [[Fick 定律]]  (1 句)
   • **SAP swelling is not plain Fickian.** The **Alfrey-Gurnee-Lloyd three-way classification** splits behaviour by the ratio of diffusion to relaxation rate: **Case I（Fickian）** when diffusion << relaxation; **Case II** when diffusion >> relaxation; **non-Fickian** when they are comparable.
