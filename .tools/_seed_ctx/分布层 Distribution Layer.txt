The feature comprises **at least one extruded elastomeric layer** and **at least one extruded, substantially non-elastomeric layer** which together form a film（至少一层挤出弹性体层 + 至少一层挤出、基本非弹性层，共同构成薄膜）. Before the film is elasticise
   • **Activation is the invention's switch（激活即开关）:** the film ships and stores in a non-elastic, easy-to-handle state and becomes elastic **only after mechanical stretching**（源：EP0820747A1 Absorbent Article with Elastic Feature (P&G)）. **Co-extrusion beats lamination** because it delivers the multi-layer structure in one process step from the same die head. Removing the nonwoven layer removes two manufacturing pains — weak nonwoven tensile strength and fibre/glue contamination.
