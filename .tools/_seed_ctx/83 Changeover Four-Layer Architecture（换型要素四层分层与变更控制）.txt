--- [[US2022-0339044A1 Pant-Type Absorbent Article with Waist Guard (P&G)]]  (2 句)

   • **Blowout leakage is quantified, not asserted** — the patent supplies a test method (artificial BM on a tared paper towel) and a numeric gate. → Blowout Leakage Test（爆漏测试）
- **Decoupled elastic zones are the mechanism** — the waist guard elastic portion and the correlated belt portion must have different tensile stress, otherwise the pocket cannot form/act. → Waist Guard（腰围护挡）
- **The pocket is a geometric feature with a bounded location** (5%–60% of LS) — this is the claim-critical limitation.
