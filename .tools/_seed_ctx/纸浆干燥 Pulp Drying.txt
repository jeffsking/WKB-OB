--- [[Industrial Drying Ch1 Principles and Classification]]  (9 句)
   • Drying is governed by two simultaneous subprocesses:
- **Process 1 (External):** Transfer of energy (heat) from environment to evaporate surface moisture
- **Process 2 (Internal):** Transfer of internal moisture to the surface and its evaporation
   • **Hygiene implication:** In diaper cores, internal moisture transport (capillary/vapor diffusion through fluff pulp and SAP) is usually the rate-limiting step at low final moisture — surface air conditions matter far less than residence time and bed depth.
   • **Hygiene implication:** Hot-air convection is the default for pulp, tissue and nonwoven through-dryers.
   • **Constant-rate period:** Surface evaporation dominates; drying rate independent of internal structure
- **Falling-rate period:** Internal diffusion/capillarity becomes rate-controlling; time scales with square of thickness

--- [[GB-T 21331-2021 Fluff Pulp（绒毛浆）]]  (4 句)
   • China's product standard for **fluff pulp**（绒毛浆）used as raw material for disposable hygiene products — the CN-market acceptance baseline for fluff pulp lots (quality, chemical safety, microbial).
   • GB/T 21331-2021 (ICS 85.040 / CCS Y31, SAC/TC 141) is the first revision of the 2008 fluff pulp standard. It drops the old product-grade split and instead classifies pulp by chemical treatment: **treated**（处理浆，耐破度显著降低，利于散纤）vs **untreated**（未处理浆）. The 2021 revision's main technical changes: migratable fluorescent substances, AOX, and four heavy metals (Pb/As/Cd/Hg) are now specified with test methods; 二氯甲烷抽出物 is replaced by 丙酮抽出物; dry bulk, absorption time/capacity methods are reworked in normati
   • **绒毛浆入厂验收的化学安全四件套（荧光物质/AOX/重金属/丙酮抽出物）是 2021 版新增** —— 支撑页：绒毛浆（Fluff Pulp）、Migratable Fluorescent Whitening Agents（可迁移性荧光增白剂）
- **处理浆 vs 未处理浆的取舍本质是「蓬松/吸速 vs 强度」**：处理浆蓬松度门槛更高（17.0 vs 15.0 cm³/g）但耐破度更低 —— 支撑页：绒毛浆（Fluff Pulp）
   • 概念：绒毛浆（Fluff Pulp）、Migratable Fluorescent Whitening Agents（可迁移性荧光增白剂）、TCF Bleaching（全无氯漂白）（AOX 与漂白工艺相关）
- 来源：GB-T 27741-2018 Determination of Migratable Fluorescent Whitening Agents in Paper and Board（纸和纸板 可迁移性荧光增白剂的测定）

--- [[Industrial Drying Ch35 Drying of Pulp and Paper]]  (4 句)
   • **This is THE most actionable sentence in the Handbook for fluff-pulp qualification.** Over-drying market pulp in the final stage welds fibres via thermal cross-linking (hornification), raising defibration energy and hurting pad integrity. Suppliers control pulp temperature °C precisely to avoid this.
   • 1. Claims the lower moisture bound is critical and names the failure mode — thermal cross-linking → poor reconstitution
2. Operational rule: pulp temperature held below 70°C across both stages despite 400/170°C inlet air
3. Energy integration — second-stage exhaust preheats first-stage inlet
4. Staging — bulk water off first (~30% MC), delicate finish at low T
5. Finishing — cool before baling to lock in quality
   • **Fluff implication:** High-temperature drying history means collapsed pores and lost swellability.
