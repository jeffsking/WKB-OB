--- [[毛细压力 Capillary Pressure]]  (5 句)
   • **毛细压力是吸收的主要驱动力**：吸收由毛细压力驱动，外部施加的重力/压力只起次要作用 —— "Absorption is driven primarily by **capillary pressure** (p_c = 2γcosθ/r); externally imposed gravity or pressure plays only a secondary role"（源：Liquid Flow in Porous Structures）。
- **物理基础是 Young-LaPlace 方程**：ΔP = −2γcosθ / R（ΔP 毛细压、γ 表面张力、θ 接触角、R 孔隙半径）；**毛细压由润湿性（θ）与孔径（R）共同决定**（源：Capillary Pressure Method（毛细压力法·解吸毛细压力与解吸能量））。因此测到的毛细压差异**只能归因于这两者之一** —— 这是用它做根因诊断（润湿性 vs 孔径二分）的根据。
   • **毛细压力法（Capillary Pressure Method）**：试样置于与储液池恒连通的载物台上，垂直移动载物台改变**静水压头**，同步称量持液量并归一化为**饱和度 saturation（g/g）**，得 **height–saturation** 曲线；**下降台面得吸收支（absorption），上升台面得解吸支（desorption）**（源：Capillary Pressure Method（毛细压力法·解吸毛细压力与解吸能量））。
- **读数规则**：**毛细压越高 → 孔越想把液体留住**；**解吸能量越低 → 纤维越容易"晾干"**。设计原则：**摄入层（面层/导流层）的毛细性与润湿性必须低于液体最终被锁住的地方（芯体）**（源：Capillary Pressure Method（毛细压力法·解吸毛细压力与解吸能量））。
- **速率与容量解耦**：**孔径控制吸收速率（经 Hagen–Poiseuille ∝ r²），孔体积控制容量** —— 优化其一必罚另一个，需中值最优化（源：Liquid Flow in Porous Structures）。

   • **回渗根因诊断的逐层流程**：① 确认结果指标（Closed Cradle / Cradle 回渗）由**解吸**驱动；② 逐层测毛细压力与解吸能量，找出哪一层偏向竞品；③ 该层按**润湿性 vs 孔径**二分定位根因，孔径路径下再细分平均孔径 vs PSD；④ 改材料 → 回产品台架验回渗，形成闭环（源：Capillary Pressure Method（毛细压力法·解吸毛细压力与解吸能量））。

--- [[Residual Fluid & Pore Stranding（残液与孔隙滞留）]]  (4 句)
   • 1. **残液的两个出口条件由 Young-LaPlace 方程给出**：**ΔP = −2γcosθ / R**。毛细压由**润湿性（θ）**与**孔径（R）**共同决定。**毛细压越高 → 孔越想把液体留住；解吸能越高 → 纤维越难"晾干"**。因此降低残液只有两条路：**降低润湿性（提高 θ）**或**加大孔径 R**（以及让孔径分布收敛）。
2. **孔径分布（PSD）比平均孔径更重要**：假说是**液体滞留在极小孔里，因为旁边没有足够大的孔把它拉出来**。双峰/宽分布会造成"抽不出来"的死角；对策是**让纤维尺寸趋同以收窄分布**，并**减少孔的总数以减少可拘留液体的位置**。

--- [[达西定律 Darcy's Law]]  (4 句)
   • Darcy's law（达西定律）is the governing relation for flow in porous media as used in the vault: **q = −K·∇P，K = k/μ**（流率正比于压力梯度，K 为传导系数 = 渗透率 k / 流体黏度 μ）（源：Liquid Flow in Porous Structures）。In absorbent systems the absorption driving force is not an external pressure but **capillary pressure**: **p_c = 2γcosθ/r**，外加重力或压力只起次要作用（源：Liquid Flow in Porous Structures）。
   • **Capillary-driven absorption 毛细驱动的吸收.** 「The driving force for absorption of the bulk of the liquid into an absorbing material ... is the capillary pressure」，重力或外压为次要项（源：Porous Structure and Liquid Flow Models）。

--- [[Industrial Drying Ch28 Osmotic Dehydration and SAP Physics]]  (4 句)
   • This chapter is the **single most direct bridge to superabsorbent-polymer (SAP) science** in the Handbook. The osmotic pressure framework, semipermeable membrane analogy, and electrolyte ion effect are exactly the physics family of SAP swelling/deswelling governed by Flory-Rehner equilibrium and Donnan osmotic pressure.
   • **SAP transfer:** This is the swelling/driving pressure set by the solvent chemical-potential difference. Inside an SAP, this is the swelling pressure of the crosslinked polyelectrolyte network.
   • **SAP transfer:** This is the seed of Donnan equilibrium — mobile ions distributed across a charged, semipermeable matrix raise Π. It is precisely why SAP (a fixed-charge polyelectrolyte) swells in water but **deswells in saline**: the fixed –COO⁻ groups plus counterions create a Donnan osmotic pressure that is suppressed by external salt.
   • **Cross-link:** The same osmotic-pressure number that drives SAP uptake also sets the antimicrobial window of a hypertonic brine — useful for preservative-free stabilization.
