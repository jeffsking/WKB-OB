--- [[防水透气 Waterproof Breathable]]  (6 句)
   • **Microporous backsheet films.** A representative route is a **three-layer co-extruded blown film** with CaCO₃ masterbatch in each layer (middle layer 60–70 kg per batch, with PE and lubricant) giving an ultra-thin PE microporous film at 10–14 gsm（源：CN 111890767 A（一种超薄聚乙烯微孔透气膜及其制备方法））。
   • **Zoned breathability.** A second route increases the cast film's single feed port to **multiple independently fed ports**, each controlling its own additive recipe, merged and secondarily drawn into one zoned breathable film — for adult-diaper backsheets that need differentiated breathability *and* leak-proofing（源：CN 112778606 A（一种分区透气底膜及其制备方法、纸尿裤））。
   • **Film-free alternative (modelled).** An all-PP SMMMMS structure was studied as a physical-model feasibility question — replacing the breathable backsheet film — and the intended answer is a **zoning** decision (where the film can be dropped) rather than a yes/no（源：Laser-Assisted Meltblown Film-Free Backsheet（激光辅助熔喷无膜背层））。
   • **Nanofibre membrane route.** Electrospun PU nanofibre membranes give very low basis weight with high hydrostatic pressure and very high WVTR; the vault is explicit that this is a **different material class, not a better film** — 4 gsm / 8 µm / 8,000 mmH₂O is not on the same scale as a PE drawn film（源：August R&D Meeting 2026（华峰研发会议）、Huafon 华峰 August R&D Meeting（氨纶与纳米纤维膜））。
