# 2025.08.28 - GRIFFIN Regroup（GRIFFIN 重整与再聚焦）

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 9 citing sources. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

**GRIFFIN** is a project code in the **IPC BCC OI (open-innovation) portfolio**（源：[[Depend Rewet Update 2025-04-04（LUGH 与 GRIFFIN 回渗进展）]]）, carried in the vault as the **GRIFFIN ultra-low-rewet programme**（源：[[GRIFFIN Rewet Program（GRIFFIN 超低回渗项目）]]）.

**This page** covers the specific **regroup review authored 2025-08-25 and updated 2025-08-28**（K. Clemmer, ADT）（源：[[GRIFFIN Regroup - Depend Rewet Update（GRIFFIN 重整更新 · 2025-08-28）]]）. Per that source, it is the **first time "rewet cannot be measured reliably" was raised as a stand-alone problem** — batch-to-batch and test-request variation coupled with dry thickness — and it records three parallel progress lines: LUGH liner / GRIFFIN liner / competitor swap.

（中文：本页不是 GRIFFIN 项目的总定义，而是 **2025-08-25 撰写、8-28 更新**的那次**重整复盘**；该件首次把"回渗测不准"单列为问题。）

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

1. **The flap is the switch for the low-wettability route.** No flap（ATACAMA）⇒ leakage rises; flap present（MOJAVE）⇒ leakage does not rise. This converts the earlier vague warning "lower wettability → leakage risk" into a bounded criterion: **flap available ⇒ usable; no flap ⇒ not usable**（源：[[亲水整理耐久性]]）.

2. **The rewet/leakage trade-off is a system problem, not a material problem.** The same treatment（no wetting finish）gives opposite results on two product structures; a liner variable must therefore be judged together with the containment structure（源：[[亲水整理耐久性]]）.

3. **Mechanism of reduced wettability, stated in the source**：not "repelling liquid" but **"lowering capillarity + not letting liquid stay in the top layer"**; the criterion should be **top-layer residual fluid**, not contact angle（源：[[亲水整理耐久性]]）.

4. **ATH re-defined as a system-boundary change**：in ATH, **fluff changes from "absorbent material" to "part of the intake system"**, so more layers ⇒ more retention points. Remedy direction: **add a small amount of SAM into the fluff layer to desorb it** rather than continuing to change the liner（源：[[ADT Brain Trust - Fluff and Rewet（绒毛浆与回渗 DOE 复盘）]]）.

5. **2025-07 capillary testing（旁证）**：on SAM-free GRIFFIN samples, with fluff present the **desorption energy is higher and the sample is harder to dry, trapping more residual fluid** — consistent under both 12 gsm dSB and 20 gsm BCW wraps（源：[[ADT Brain Trust - Fluff and Rewet（绒毛浆与回渗 DOE 复盘）]]）.

6. **Two rewet pathways（本件反复引用的机理底座）**：closed-cradle rewet is **desorption-driven**（residual fluid in the intake system）; high-pressure rewet is **flowback-driven**（loosely held fluid in the core）（源：[[ADT Brain Trust - Fluff and Rewet（绒毛浆与回渗 DOE 复盘）]]、[[Rewet Mechanisms Desorption and Flowback（回渗双机理：解吸与回流）]]）.

7. **Declared next steps at the time**：Aug BUFF BABY deep-dive → Sep competitive analysis → Sep interface trial → **Oct GRIFFIN HUT results** → Oct SAM impact and other core drivers（DML trial）（源：[[Rewet Quarterly Update 2025-08（设计原则落地与五个项目战绩）]]）. GRIFFIN timeline in the regroup source: **HUT results mid-October**; **untreated 12 gsm Fibertex ordered for testing**（源：[[GRIFFIN Regroup - Depend Rewet Update（GRIFFIN 重整更新 · 2025-08-28）]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- Directly concerns the **Depend（adult incontinence）吸收系统**：liner + surge + core, i.e. the layer stack a REL-core engineer works with（源：[[GRIFFIN Regroup - Depend Rewet Update（GRIFFIN 重整更新 · 2025-08-28）]]）.
- The **low-wettability liner route was formally taken out of GRIFFIN scope** on HUT evidence, alongside "open bond pattern = no improvement"（源：[[亲水整理耐久性]]）.
- Forward-looking framing aligns GRIFFIN with the **ActivTech Hybrid** core: that core had never been used in such a severe ultra-low-rewet scenario and early data showed higher AT-hybrid rewet, but the code **lacked the liner-surge embossing（LSE）** critical to ATACAMA rewet improvement（源：[[GRIFFIN Rewet Technical Problem Statement（GRIFFIN 回渗技术问题描述 · 2025-01-31）]]）.
- Ties into **LUGH** (liner) and **GRIFFIN** joint purchasing of a "14 gsm soft + lower-wetting-treatment" liner, with the explicit warning that **without the GRIFFIN flap, leakage risk exists — bench first, then HUT**（源：[[Depend Rewet Update 2025-04-04（成功标准 · LUGH-GRIFFIN-ATH 进展 · Walmart NWSP 070.9 方法对比）]]）.
