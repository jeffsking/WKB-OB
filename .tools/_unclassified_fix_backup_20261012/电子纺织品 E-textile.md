## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

**Fibre form as a complementary route.** The vault source frames thin-film and fibre as the two mainstream paths of wearable electronics, not competitors but complements — the fibre path reduces two dimensions relative to a bulk device（源：[[Wearable Electronics (National Science Review 2023)]]）。

**Washability as the differentiator.** Because the device is carried *in* a textile, laundering durability rather than electrical function alone is what separates it from conventional electronics; the source states fibre-form devices are washable hundreds of times（源：[[Wearable Electronics (National Science Review 2023)]]）。

**Landscape placement.** In this vault, e-textiles sit inside the broader smart-textiles & wearables landscape, which spans stimuli-responsive polymers, wearable sensing/electronics, flexible electronics, chromic/PCM/shape-memory functions and thermoelectric harvesting（源：[[Smart Textiles & Wearables]]）。The Wearable Sensors book preview places e-textile as the first link of its chain — e-textile → energy harvesting → algorithms → WBAN full stack（源：[[Wearable Sensors Fundamentals Implementation and Applications]]）。

中文：机理要点为——纤维化是可穿戴电子的两条互补主线之一；可水洗性是其区别于刚性电子器件的判据；在本库中它被归入「智能纺织品与可穿戴」知识域，并被《可穿戴传感器》专著预览列为全栈的第一环。
