## Definition 定义

No textbook definition sentence exists in the citing sources `[待补]`. The vault records it as a **special-purpose absorbent fibre** whose declared function is **capillary distribution + storage in grooves**（源：[[Fibers and Fibrous Materials]]）, and — in the wetlaid fibre palette — as a PET fibre with an **eight-leg cross-section** whose grooves **spontaneously transport fluid within the web**（源：[[Wet-laid web formation]]）.

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

1. **Geometry instead of chemistry.** The chapter's governing law is that in capillary sorption *"the fiber surfaces constitute the pore walls"*, so absorbency is a **surface-and-geometry** problem. Special-purpose fibres — alloy rayon, modified cellulose, **deep-groove polyester**, hollow chicken-feather — **win by engineering the pore wall and pore geometry rather than by changing the polymer**（源：[[Fibers and Fibrous Materials]]）.
2. **Why a hydrophobic PET can still absorb**：*"it is possible to produce small diameter (fine) fibers and also to produce fibers with irregular or noncircular cross-sectional shapes. Both these fiber characteristics contribute to the formation of effective interfiber capillaries"*（源：[[Fibers and Fibrous Materials]]）.
3. **Fluid transport moves from web design to fibre design.** The wetlaid source states that **deep-grooved PET moves fluid transport from a web-design problem to a fibre-design problem**（源：[[Wet-laid web formation]]）.
4. **How the geometry is made**：non-circular cross-sections are set by the **spinneret hole shape**（trilobal, rectangular, hexalobal, hollow）and by **melt rheology**; the same design lever gives *"greater lustre and covering power and improved handle"*（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch14 Speciality Polyamide and Polyester Yarns]]）.
5. **Sibling shaped-fibre routes in the vault**：[[三叶形截面纤维（Trilobal Fibre）]]、[[Pentagram PET vs Rounded Trilobal PET (Topsheet Shaped Fibre Selection)]]、[[双组分纤维 Bicomponent]]（island-in-sea / radial split-pie configurations as the ancestors of splittable nonwovens and high-coverage coverstock / wipe webs）（源：[[双组分纤维 Bicomponent]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **Wetlaid nonwovens are the named carrier**：deep-grooved PET sits in the wetlaid fibre palette（cellulose、Tencel/Lyocell、SWP、deep-grooved PET、polyolefins）alongside cellulose H-bonding, latex, thermal bicomponent (PP/PE or PET/CoPET), PVA and hydroentanglement bonding（源：[[Wet-laid web formation]]）.
- **Fibre selection for the absorbent web, not the polymer**：this is the closest vault analogue to designing an acquisition/distribution layer by **fibre cross-section** rather than by chemistry — same logic as the REL / distribution-layer pages built on PET resilience（源：[[Fibers and Fibrous Materials]]、[[Wet-laid web formation]]）.
- **Topsheet optics and hand**：the shaped-cross-section trade space（lustre × covering power × handle, p.387）is named as relevant to **topsheet fibre selection**（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch14 Speciality Polyamide and Polyester Yarns]]）.
- **PET base polymer context**：PET is *"the most outstanding member of the polyester fibre family"*（ISO: ≥85% by mass of an ester of a diol and terephthalic acid）, commercialised by **DuPont (1953)** and **ICI (1954)**; spinning speed is a designable orientation lever, with draw headroom disappearing above **~1500 m/min**（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch12 Polyethylene Terephthalate Fibres]]）.
- **Material-set neighbours**：[[中空纤维 Hollow Fibre]]、[[合金粘胶 Alloy Rayon]]、[[改性纤维素 Modified Cellulose]]、[[粘胶纤维 Viscose Fiber]]、[[莱赛尔 Lyocell]]、[[绒毛浆（Fluff Pulp）]]（源：[[Fibers and Fibrous Materials]]）.
