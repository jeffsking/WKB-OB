# Grafted Cellulose and Starch

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 5 citing sources. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义
**Grafting cellulose and starch = covalently attaching vinyl polymer chains (acrylic acid, acrylamide) onto polysaccharide backbones to form superabsorbent networks.** The vault frames grafting as a **post-polymerisation modification** route — "the postpolymerization reaction of preformed polymers … include chain extensions, cross-linking, and graft and block copolymer formation"（源：[[Ebewole Polymer Science Ch05 Polymer Modification]] p.125）. Ch.7 of *Absorbent Technology* names "Grafted Cellulose and Starch" as its neighbouring chapter and treats grafting as the bridge to synthetic-SAP performance（源：[[Cross-linked Cellulose and Its Derivatives]]）. ## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理
- **Post-polymerisation grafting.** Reactive sites dispersed in the polymer main chain allow chain extension, cross-linking and graft/block copolymer formation after the backbone is made（源：[[Ebewole Polymer Science Ch05 Polymer Modification]] p.125）.
- **Cellulose-side grafts.** HEC (hydroxyethyl cellulose) is made superabsorbent "by cross-linking or by grafting polyacrylamide, PMMA, PDA or sodium polyacrylate"（源：[[Cross-linked Cellulose and Its Derivatives]]）.
- **Structure model.** Marchessault's "constrained paracrystalline gels" describe grafted superabsorbent fibres, held together by weak mechanical forces（源：同上）.
- **Starch backbone chemistry.** Amylose is a linear polysaccharide with α-1,4 linkages; amylopectin adds α-1,6 branch points — the α- vs β-linkage difference is why starch can be metabolised where cellulose cannot（源：[[Seymour Polymer Chemistry Ch10 Naturally Occurring Polymers]]）.
- **Classic route.** Starch/cellulose backbone grafted with acrylic acid then hydrolysed to introduce sodium carboxylate — the classic biodegradable-SAP route（源：[[Hydrophilic SAP Wrapping Adhesive（亲水SAP包裹胶）]]）.
- **ATRP variant.** Nanocellulose carboxylated → macroinitiator → acrylamide polymerised in situ so PAM chains are covalently grafted, not physically blended（源：[[CN 112851860 A（可降解的纤维素-聚丙烯酰胺复合保水剂和制备方法·杭州所予科技）]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联
- **Grafting is the bridge to synthetic-SAP performance.** 3000 g/g in water on a cellulose backbone is the highest cellulosic figure and the natural place to look before committing fully to polyacrylate SAP（源：[[Cross-linked Cellulose and Its Derivatives]]）.
- **Partially-degradable bio-SAP route.** Starch-grafted copolymer (200–1000×) is the **only degradable route already industrialised** among the four/five degradable SAP routes（源：[[Degradable SAP Routes（可降解 SAP 四路线对比）]]、[[Biodegradable SAP（可降解高吸水树脂）]]）.
- Registered as the **接枝改性 Grafting** route in the adhesive-materials landscape（源：[[Hydrophilic SAP Wrapping Adhesive（亲水SAP包裹胶）]]）.
