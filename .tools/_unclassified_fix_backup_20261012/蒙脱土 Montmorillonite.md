# 蒙脱土 Montmorillonite

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 5 citing sources. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

Montmorillonite (MMT) is used in the vault as an **inorganic nanolayered silicate clay** serving two distinct roles: a rigid nano-reinforcement / physical cross-link point in superabsorbent resins（源：[[CN 101787101 A（一种高吸水性纳米树脂的制备方法·陕西科技大学）]]、[[SAP性能指标（CRC AUL GBP Vortex）]]）, and an **inorganic silicate porous carrier** for odour control（源：[[Zeolite Odour Control（沸石除臭载体）]]）. A single formal definition is not stated — `定义待补（引用来源未给出明确定义）`.

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

- **In-situ intercalation grafting.** Organically modified MMT (quaternary-ammonium-organophilised) is mixed with a CMC solution; acrylic monomers polymerise in the interlayer gaps to form an organic–inorganic nanocomposite network（源：[[蒙脱土纳米复合高吸水树脂（CMC-AA-OMMT）]]）.
- **Rigid reinforcement.** The rigidity of the MMT platelets is the stated cause of the multi-fold gel-strength gain（源：[[蒙脱土纳米复合高吸水树脂（CMC-AA-OMMT）]]）.
- **Cost & degradation.** CMC + clay substitutes part of the acrylic acid monomer; the cellulose backbone is microbially degraded and the network disintegrates in **3–4 months** — an early cellulose-based-SAP economics template（源：[[蒙脱土纳米复合高吸水树脂（CMC-AA-OMMT）]]、[[Biodegradable SAP（可降解高吸水树脂）]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **Absorbency uplift lever.** Doping MMT as a physical cross-link point is one of the routes cited to raise AUL / gel strength of SAP for absorbent cores（源：[[SAP性能指标（CRC AUL GBP Vortex）]]、[[AUL 加压吸水率]]）.
- **Odour control.** MMT is a candidate inorganic silicate carrier for odour-management platforms in hygiene products（源：[[Zeolite Odour Control（沸石除臭载体）]]）.
- **Cellulose-based SAP.** The CMC/acrylic/MMT route is the earliest "clay-reinforced biodegradable SAP" anchor in its source batch, with direct reference value for core-facing cellulose SAP（源：[[CN 101787101 A（一种高吸水性纳米树脂的制备方法·陕西科技大学）]]）.
