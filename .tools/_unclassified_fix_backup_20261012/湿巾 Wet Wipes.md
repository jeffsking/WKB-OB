定义待补（引用来源未给出明确定义）。

What the digest *does* evidence is the **regulatory scope boundary**（口径边界），not a definition: in the CN market, wet wipes are governed in layers — general requirements in GB/T 27728.1, baby & child particular requirements in GB/T 27728.2-2024（for children ≤6, the compliance baseline for any baby-wipe SKU in China）（源：[[GB-T 27728.2-2024 Wipes Part 2 Baby and Child Wipes（湿巾 第2部分 婴童湿巾专用要求）]]）；disinfectant wipes must follow GB/T 27728.3-2024 and **may not cite only the 2011 edition**（源：[[GB-T 27728-2011 Wipes（湿巾）]]）。

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

**Compliance layers（合规层级，CN）**
- GB 43631-2023 is the **mandatory chemical-safety floor**（强制国标，工信部归口）for children's paper products; it covers wipes, diapers and tissue（生活用纸品）and sets one shared floor table across heavy metals, FWAs, acrylamide, formaldehyde, solvents, preservatives, phthalates and aromatic amines（源：[[GB 43631-2023 General Safety Technical Specification for Infants and Children Paper Products（婴幼儿及儿童用纸品基本安全技术规范）]]）。
- GB/T 27728.2-2024 layers baby-wipe requirements on top of GB/T 27728.1: raw-material minimisation and the long-safe-use-history principle, fragrance per GB/T 22731, production lotion per the 2015 Cosmetics Safety Technical Specification; its clause 4.2.3 **delegates to GB 43631**（源：[[GB-T 27728.2-2024 Wipes Part 2 Baby and Child Wipes（湿巾 第2部分 婴童湿巾专用要求）]]、[[GB 43631-2023（婴幼儿及儿童纸品化学安全总门槛）]]）。
- The upper-tier system is GB 15979-2024（一次性使用卫生用品卫生要求），complementary to GB 43631's chemical-safety dimension（源：[[GB 43631-2023（婴幼儿及儿童纸品化学安全总门槛）]]）。

**Coating and drying（涂层与干燥，制造侧）**
- Emulsion / lotion coatings on wipes behave like latex — a longer benign constant-rate period before the defect-prone falling-rate zone（源：[[Industrial Drying Ch40 Drying of Coated Webs]]）。
- For skin-contact hygiene films and wipes, **100 % solids UV/EB** is the regulatory-friendly route — no solvent VOC, no extractable cross-linker residuals（源：[[Industrial Drying Ch40 Drying of Coated Webs]]）。

**Labelling and lotion wording（标签与用液表述）**
- "Two-mode date marking + no medical claims"（日期双模式 + 无医疗用语）is the 30-year-stable skeleton of CN cosmetic labelling; the wipes-lotion ingredient declaration（GB/T 27728.2 INCI descending order）is its modern extension（源：[[GB 5296.3-1995 Instructions for Use of Products of Consumer Interest General Labelling of Cosmetics（消费品使用说明 化妆品通用标签）]]）。

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- Formulation cross-over: the skincare-formulation reference covers formulation design, ingredient selection, processing and stability, and cross-references hygiene products in **moisturising / care wet-wipes formulation** and **nonwoven–skin interface comfort assessment**（源：[[Cosmetic Formulation of Skin Care Products]]）。
- Related concepts: [[Nonwoven 非织造布]]、[[乳化剂 Emulsifier]]、[[保湿剂 Humectant]]、[[防腐剂 Preservative]]（源：[[Cosmetic Formulation of Skin Care Products]]）。
- Shared chemical floor with diapers: GB/T 28004.1-2021 Table 2 matches GB 43631 Table 1 in the diaper column（源：[[GB 43631-2023（婴幼儿及儿童纸品化学安全总门槛）]]）。
- Disinfectant-wipe compliance vs hygienic-wipe definitions: see [[GB-T 27728-2011 与 GB-T 27728.3-2024 湿巾标准对比]]、[[湿巾 vs 卫生湿巾（中国 GB 15979-2024 口径）]]。
