## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

- **Bonding determines flushability, and bonding is a chemical choice.** The vault's only mechanism-level anchor is that the flushable-wipe route is solved in **binder chemistry**, alongside incontinence binder chemistry, within the chemical-bonding family（源：[[Chemical bonding]]）.
- **Transport limit（数学旁证）**：Fick's law applies only to isotropic media; **forced-convection scenarios (e.g. wet-wipe wiping) require an added convection term**（源：[[Fick 定律]]）. Flushable-wipe dispersion is therefore outside the plain diffusion description.
- **Binder polymer palette available to the designer**（same source, Ch.7）：vinyl、acrylic、butadiene（SBR/NBR/natural rubber）and specialty systems（PU、phenolic、epoxy）, with emulsion polymerisation, the Fox equation for copolymer Tg, MFFT, and coagulation/migration/crosslinking treated in a dedicated subsection（源：[[Chemical bonding]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **Regulatory frame（CN，wipes）**：a wipe SKU in the CN market sits in layers — general requirements GB/T 27728.1、baby & child GB/T 27728.2-2024（children ≤6）和消毒湿巾 GB/T 27728.3-2024（源：[[湿巾 Wet Wipes]]、[[GB-T 27728.2-2024 Wipes Part 2 Baby and Child Wipes（湿巾 第2部分 婴童湿巾专用要求）]]）.
- **Mandatory chemical-safety floor**：GB 43631-2023 covers wipes, diapers and tissue as 生活用纸品 with one shared table（heavy metals、FWAs、acrylamide ≤0.1 mg/kg、24 种致癌芳香胺 ≤20 mg/kg 等）（源：[[GB 43631-2023 General Safety Technical Specification for Infants and Children Paper Products（婴幼儿及儿童用纸品基本安全技术规范）]]）.
- **Labelling**：the wipes-lotion ingredient declaration（INCI descending order）is a modern extension of the cosmetic two-mode date marking + no-medical-claims skeleton（源：[[GB 5296.3-1995 Instructions for Use of Products of Consumer Interest General Labelling of Cosmetics（消费品使用说明 化妆品通用标签）]]）.
- **同族标准导航**：[[GB-T 27728-2011 Wipes（湿巾）]]、[[GB-T 27728-2011 Wipes（湿巾 · 副本1）]]（源：[[湿巾 Wet Wipes]]）.
