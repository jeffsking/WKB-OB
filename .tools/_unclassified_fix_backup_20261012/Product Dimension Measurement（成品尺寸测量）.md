## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

**Purposes-of-testing chain（11.1）**：the four purposes are **quality control 质量控制, process control 工艺控制, product development 产品开发, process optimisation 工艺优化**；the whole chapter follows one chain — "definition → quantifying index → standard method → commercial instrument"（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch11 Testing of Manufactured Fibres]]）。

**Section order（尺寸/形态类量）**：fineness 11.2, crimp 11.3, tensile properties 11.4, evenness 11.5, friction 11.6, shrinkage 11.7, entanglement 11.8（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch11 Testing of Manufactured Fibres]]）。

**Linear density 线密度**：fibre and yarn fineness is measured as mass per unit length；the preferred method is direct weighing, standard ASTM D1577-79；the Vibroscope is useful for staple bundles but carries a practical trap[原文残缺]（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch11 Testing of Manufactured Fibres]]，p.248–p.250）。

**Shrinkage 收缩**：defined as the decrease in length of a specimen，a negative value being stretch；instruments Toray FTA-500, Lawson-Hemphill CTT-DST, Textechno Dynafil, Rothschild DEFT/CCT；standard ASTM D2102-90；the key claim（p.265）is that "there must be a means of assessing and controlling shrinkage behaviour of the yarn"，with boiling-water / dry-heat shrinkage **and** shrinkage force measurable simultaneously on a running yarn（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch11 Testing of Manufactured Fibres]]）。

**Entanglement / opening length 缠结·开口长度**：opening length（distance between entanglement points）describes the temporary cohesion of an entangled yarn；methods include manual needle, automatic needle, electrostatic method（Obestat）, automatic thickness measurement（Itemat, Toray profilometer）；Stein's comparison [13] concludes Dynafil detects variation in polymerization, temperature and quench-air speed most effectively（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch11 Testing of Manufactured Fibres]]，p.267）。

**Polymer-structure characterisation（Ch10）**：a two-phase structure（ordered / crystalline + disordered / amorphous）；five widely used techniques are density measurement, optical birefringence, X-ray diffraction, small-angle X-ray scattering and infrared spectroscopy（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch10 Characterization of Polymers and Fibres]]，p.219）；the chapter says **nothing** about fluff pulp, SAP or absorbency measurement（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch10 Characterization of Polymers and Fibres]]）。

**Specification labelling（邻接维度）**：CN textile/apparel labelling is a mandatory standard with seven compulsory content items, including **size/spec**；size, fibre content and care method must live on a **durable label**（源：[[GB 5296.4-2012 Instructions for Use of Products of Consumer Interest Part 4 Textiles and Apparel（消费品使用说明 第4部分 纺织品和服装）]]）。⚠️ Disposable hygiene products are **explicitly out of scope** of GB 5296.4（Annex A）（源：[[GB 5296.4-2012 Instructions for Use of Products of Consumer Interest Part 4 Textiles and Apparel（消费品使用说明 第4部分 纺织品和服装）]]）。
