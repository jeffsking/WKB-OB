# WO 2009/071584 A1（Grindeks 咪唑类化合物制备工艺·文件名标注有误）

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

⚠️ **本页是一条"文件名与内容不符"的记录页，不是一份卫材资料。** 文件名写作 `WO2010071584A1_SCA_CNF-SAP.pdf.md`（暗示 SCA 的 SAP × 纤维素纳米纤丝主题），但**正文实际是拉脱维亚 Grindeks 公司的药物化学 PCT 申请 WO 2009/071584 A1**，主题为咪唑类化合物的制备工艺，**与本库卫材研发/采购主题无关**。

## Abstract 摘要

Title on the face: "PROCESS FOR THE PREPARATION OF 5-(2-ETHYL-DIHYDRO-1H-INDEN-2-YL)-1H-IMIDAZOLE AND SALTS THEREOF". Abstract: "The invention provides an improved, highly efficient method for preparing 5-(2-ethyl-2,3-dihydro-1H-inden-2-yl)-1H-imidazole, and its salts, in particular its pharmaceuticals acceptable salts." Claim 1 is a three-step route: **(a)** condensing 1-trityl-1H-imidazole-4-carboxaldehyde with phthalide to form 2-(1-trityl-1H-imidazol-4-yl)indan-1,3-dione; **(b)** alkylating that product with ethyl iodide to form 2-ethyl-2-(1-trityl-1H-imidazol-4-yl)indan-1,3-dione; **(c)** removing the trityl group by acid hydrolysis to form 2-ethyl-2-(1H-imidazol-4-yl)indan-1,3-dione — en route to **5-(2-ethyl-2,3-dihydro-1H-inden-2-yl)-1H-imidazole hydrochloride**.
