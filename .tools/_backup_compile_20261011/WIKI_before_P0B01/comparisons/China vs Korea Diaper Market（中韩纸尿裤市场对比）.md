## 结论 Conclusion

China wins on **scale + cost + speed** but carries **raw-material import dependency**; Korea wins on **brand equity + premium pricing** but faces **anti-China sourcing sentiment**. For sourcing strategy, China offers cost/agility upside while demanding fluff-pulp risk management; Korea offers a premium-playbook reference but limited volume.

（中国胜在规模+成本+速度但受原料进口依赖；韩国胜在品牌资产+高端溢价但面临中国供应链负面情绪。采购上中国提供降本/敏捷空间但需管控绒毛浆风险，韩国提供高端打法参考但体量有限。）
