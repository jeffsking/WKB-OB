# GB 5009.137-2025 Determination of Antimony in Foods（食品安全国家标准 食品中锑的测定）

## 一句话定位

China's **food-matrix total-Sb determination standard**（GB 5009.137-2025，HG-AFS 第一法 + ICP-MS 第二法引至 GB 5009.268）— although scoped to foods, it is the cleanest published CN reference for the **消解（total-content）Sb 口径** that the Sb-free PET fibre program benchmarks against.

## Summary 摘要

GB 5009.137-2025 replaces the 2016 edition. The 2025 revision adds ICP-MS as a second method (implemented by reference to GB 5009.268) and tightens the reductant chemistry of the first method. Method 1 (HG-AFS): digest the sample by wet, microwave (110 ℃→190 ℃ profile) or pressure-vessel routes, pre-reduce Sb(V) with thiourea-KI or thiourea-ascorbic acid in acidic medium, generate volatile stibine with 20 g/L KBH4, atomize and read fluorescence against a 0–20.0 µg/L curve. Performance: LOD 0.01 mg/kg, LOQ 0.04 mg/kg at 0.5 g/25 mL, with repeatability tiers of 10/15/20%. For the hygiene-materials library the significance is methodological rather than jurisdictional: this documents the digestion-total-Sb approach (and the glassware-acid-cleaning discipline) that differs from migratable-Sb extraction used in toy/paper standards — the same three-way split already captured in the Sb test-methods concept page.
