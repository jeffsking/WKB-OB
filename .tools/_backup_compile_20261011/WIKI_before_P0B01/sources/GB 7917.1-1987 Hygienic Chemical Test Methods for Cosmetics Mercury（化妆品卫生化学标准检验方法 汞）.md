# GB 7917.1-1987 Hygienic Chemical Test Methods for Cosmetics Mercury（化妆品卫生化学标准检验方法 汞）

## 一句话定位

Historical CN method standard for **total mercury in cosmetics**（化妆品总汞，冷原子吸收法）— the 1987-era method companion of GB 7916-1987; its Hg ≤1 ppm mindset is the ancestor of the mercury limits still seen in GB 15979-2024 / GB 43631-2023 tables.

## Summary 摘要

GB 7917.1-87 is one of the 1987 cosmetics hygienic test-method family (GB 7917.x chemical, GB 7918.x microbiological) issued alongside GB 7916-1987. It specifies cold-vapor AAS for total mercury: digest the 1 g cosmetic sample by one of three routes (reflux, V2O5-catalysed, or peroxide extraction for non-waxy matrices), reduce Hg²⁺ to elemental vapour with stannous chloride, and read at 253.7 nm against a 0–2 mL series of a 0.10 µg/mL standard; LOD 0.01 µg absolute / 0.01 ppm at 1 g. Sampling rules (≥6 packs, ≥3 opened, retained 3 months) prefigure modern chain-of-custody practice. The standard is documented here as a historical calibration point: current CN hygiene-product mercury limits (≤1 mg/kg in GB 15979-2024 and GB 43631-2023) are executed via the 2015 Cosmetics Specification ch.4 methods (microwave digestion), not this 1987 text.
