# 中国母婴市场未来3-5年趋势洞察 2027-2030（China Mother-Baby Market Trend Insight）

## Positioning 定位

Internal market-insight deck（中国区总经理市场洞察报告，2026-05，CONFIDENTIAL）covering the China mother-baby market to 2030: macro PEST, market size, consumption upgrade, channel revolution, competition, six trends, three growth tracks, and five strategic recommendations. Provides the demand-side frame for OI product-line prioritization.（需求侧全景：为 OI 项目优先级提供市场背景。）

## Summary 摘要

Market grows from 8+ trillion RMB (2025, +7.5% yoy) to ~9.35–9.5 trillion (2030E, CAGR 2.9%) despite births down to 7.92M (2025, −55.7% vs 2016); target age band extends 0–3 → 0–12. Z-gen parents (>60%) drive premiumization: 57% put ingredient safety first; high-end spreads from milk formula to diapers/care/apparel (>50% high-end share); lower-tier cities grow fastest. Channels: content e-commerce 12%→30% (Douyin ecosystem), integrated e-commerce 38%→28%, instant retail 5%→10%. Diapers (2025H1): online retail +54%, premium ≥2.5 RMB/piece >50% of market, pants +60%. Competition: domestic milk-formula brands >60% share (Feihe 19%, Yili 12.5%, Junlebao 10.8%). Six trends: consumption upgrade vs population decline; content-channel reshaping; AI+smart hardware (smart monitoring 8%→76% penetration by 2030E; feeding→69%, mobility→65%); childcare services law (2,323 亿 market 2030E, CAGR 7.1%); going-global (2025 exports 667.5 亿, OEM→brand); green mother-baby (degradable materials, zero-carbon, ESG as differentiation). Strategy: premium across categories; content-commerce depth; AI/IoT differentiation; product+service (childcare) extension; internationalization.（市场换挡：从量到质；高端化、内容渠道、智能化、绿色化为四主线。）
