# GB-T 28004-2011 Disposable Diapers（纸尿裤 片、垫）

## 一句话定位

The **2011 single-volume edition** of China's diaper product standard (baby + adult in one text) — now the historical baseline against which the 2021 split editions (28004.1 baby / 28004.2 adult) are compared; its run-off/rewet/leakage triple is the ancestor of today's CN diaper acceptance limits.

## Summary 摘要

GB/T 28004-2011 (SAC/TC 141; drafters include 中国制浆造纸研究院、广州宝洁、雀氏、金佰利（中国）、恒安、尤妮佳（中国）、SCA 贸易（上海）等) defines product classification (pants 纸尿裤 / slips 纸尿片 / underpads 护理垫), dimensional deviations (length ±6%, width ±8%, piece mass ±10%), and the penetration-performance trio: **run-off（滑渗量）** on a 30° incline, **rewet（回渗量）** under a 1.5 kPa block via pre-weighed filter paper, and **leakage（渗漏量）** through the backsheet, all with 0.9% saline. Limits are notably looser than the 2021 editions (e.g. baby-pants rewet ≤10.0 g vs the tighter 2021 tiers). pH window 4.0–8.0 and moisture ≤10.0% complete Table 1. Raw-material clauses already ban recycled fibres and bind fluff pulp to GB/T 21331 and SAP to GB/T 22905. Underpads are judged pass/fail with a 150 mL dose. The Annex A method (30° panel, standard funnels, size-dependent dosing) is the direct ancestor of the 2021 method family.
