# Ch52: Frying of Foods

> Source: Mujumdar (ed.), *Handbook of Industrial Drying*, 4th ed., CRC Press, 2014, Ch.52, pp.1222–1244. (Oreopoulou, Krokida & Marinos-Kouris)

Relevance to hygiene REL absorbent-core engineering: **very low** 极低相关。Frying is hot-oil cooking of food — no web, no fluff pulp, no SAP, no air/contact dryer. The single portable idea is the case-hardening mechanism; kept short deliberately. 译注：油炸与卫材干燥几乎无交集，仅"表面结壳堵塞内部干燥"机理可作概念借鉴，故本页从简。

---

## 1. Transport Picture 传热传质图景

> "When food is placed in hot oil, a complex heat-and-mass-transfer phenomenon takes place. Heat is transferred by convection from the surrounding oil to the surface of the food and by conduction within the solid food. Water is vaporized and escapes from the surface of the food to the oil, while oil penetrates into the food." (p.1225)

Four stages (Farkas, p.1225): initial heating → surface boiling → falling rate ("resembles the falling-rate period observed in food dehydration processes") → bubble end point. Crust/core interface "remained at a constant temperature of 102°C after the crust was formed" (p.1227).

---

## 2. The One Transferable Idea: Case-Hardening 唯一可迁移概念

> "An outer layer of a crust begins to form as a result of this dehydration and food begins to case harden." (p.1234)

> "crust thickness … depend greatly on frying temperature. Higher temperatures result in a thinner crust layer"; lower temperature/longer time → "firmer the crust and texture, and the higher the oil absorbed" (p.1239)

Mechanism 机理 (译注): a low-permeability surface skin sets while the interior is still wet and pressurised — surface-barrier choke of interior moisture egress. The same defect signature appears on over-aggressively dried coated webs and thick absorbent cores; crust thickness behaves as a rate variable, not a fixed outcome.

---

## 3. Limitations 局限

- All mass-transfer relations are correlative fried-food regressions, not transport laws; core PDEs are OCR-garbled in the source (Eq 52.2–52.4, 52.11–52.13) and unusable verbatim
- Filtration units at p.1238 ("1 mm / 5 mm") are contextually µm — an OCR slip [待验证]
- No hygiene-substrate content, no air/through-dryer correlations

## 4. Hygiene Implication 卫材关联

Very low 极低相关 (译注): use case-hardening (p.1234) as a drying-defect diagnostic — when a coated web or thick core shows a set surface over a moist interior, treat it as surface-barrier choke and relax the surface heat flux rather than extending time ([[Industrial Drying Ch40 Drying of Coated Webs]]).
