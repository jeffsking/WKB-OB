# GB-T 34405-2017 Determination of Acrylamide Migration in Household Papers（家用纸制品中丙烯酰胺迁移量的测定 LC-MS-MS法）

## 一句话定位

A **migration-caliber**（迁移量口径）method for acrylamide in household papers — artificial-sweat extraction at 37 ℃ followed by isotope-dilution LC-MS/MS; the counterpart (not substitute) of the GB/T 37859 content-caliber method cited by GB 43631-2023.

## Summary 摘要

GB/T 34405-2017 (SAC/TC 374) measures acrylamide migrating out of household paper products under simulated skin contact: the sample soaks in freshly prepared artificial sweat (GB/T 3922-2013 recipe) with D3-acrylamide internal standard, at 37±2 ℃ for 10 min (tissue/wipes) or 4 h (diaper & sanitary-towel skin-contact cover layers only), then the extract is run on LC-MS/MS in ESI+ MRM mode (72>55 quantifier). Calibration spans 0.05–5.0 µg per 50 mL. Performance is strong — LOD 0.005 mg/kg, LOQ 0.025 mg/kg, repeatability ≤10% — comfortably below the 0.1 mg/kg limits set elsewhere. The key interpretive rule: this is a **migration** test; the mandatory acrylamide limit in GB 43631-2023 is a **content** test (GB/T 37859). The two calibers answer different questions ("what can transfer to skin" vs "what is in the product") and cannot be interconverted.
