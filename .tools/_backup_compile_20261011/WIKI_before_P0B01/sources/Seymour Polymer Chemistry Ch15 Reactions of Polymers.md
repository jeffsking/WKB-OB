# Seymour Polymer Chemistry Ch15 Reactions of Polymers

> Source: Seymour/Carraher, *Polymer Chemistry*, Ch.15 — Reactions of Polymers.
> 定位说明 / Citation convention: 原文为 .txt 抽取件，**无正式印张页码**，本报告以原文行号（line no.）与章节号（§15.x）定位；原书页码缺失处标注 [待验证]。

## Executive Summary 内容概要

Chapter 15 treats the **polymer chain itself as a chemical substrate** — everything that happens to a polymer *after* it is made. The chapter spans five reaction families: **pendant-group reactions**, **main-chain degradation**, **crosslinking**, **internal cyclization**, and **end-group reactions**, then closes with macromolecular (enzyme) catalysis and the mechanisms by which polymers absorb energy and fail.

本章的核心视角是"聚合物的化学寿命"：**合成完成不是终点**。对卫材最直接相关的两块是（a）§15.10 交联与凝胶点——SAP（交联聚丙烯酸网络）交联度调控的理论基础；（b）§15.7–15.8 降解与 §15.16 失效机制——PP/PE 无纺布与薄膜在热氧、紫外与表面活性剂环境下的性能衰减。

## Pendant-Group Reactions 取代基反应

### Polyolefins 聚烯烃 (§15.1)

> *"Polyolefins, like simple alkanes, may be chlorinated by chlorine at elevated temperatures or in the presence of ultraviolet light. This free radical reaction produces HCl and chlorinated polyolefins (Tyrin), which are used as plasticizers and flame retardants."*

- **叔氢活性 / tertiary-H reactivity**：*"Since a tertiary hydrogen atom is more readily replaced than a secondary hydrogen, polypropylene is more readily chlorinated than HDPE."* — 这与 Ch.07 中"PP 因叔碳原子而抗氧化性弱于 HDPE"是同一个结构根源（同一弱点，既是易氯化之利，也是易氧化之弊）。
- **交联 / crosslinking**：*"Polyolefins may be crosslinked by heating with peroxides such as di-tert-butyl peroxide or by irradiation. It is advantageous to crosslink these polymers after they have been fabricated, as in wire coatings. The crosslinked products are less soluble and more resistant to heat than are the linear polyolefins."* — 先成型后交联（post-fabrication crosslinking）是线缆护套的标准工艺逻辑。

### Aliphatic Pendant Groups 脂肪族侧基 (§15.3)

- **PVAc → PVA 水解**：*"Esters such as poly(vinyl acetate) (PVAc) may be hydrolyzed to produce alcohols such as poly(vinyl alcohol) (PVA), which should have the same DP as the ester."*
- 乙烯醇单体本身不可分离：*"While the enol tautomer of acetaldehyde (vinyl alcohol) cannot be isolated, its water-soluble polymer can be obtained by the hydrolysis of any poly(vinyl carboxylic acid ester)."*
- 水溶性可控：*"The solubility of the product in water is dependent on the extent of hydrolysis."* — 即 PVA 的水溶性由醇解度（水解程度）调节，这是 PVA 作为可水溶/可分散纤维与胶黏剂组分的关键设计变量。

### Polyamides 聚酰胺 (§15.5)

> *"Polyamides such as proteins or nylons may be reacted with ethylene oxide or formaldehyde. The latter serves as a crosslinking agent with proteins in embalming, leather production, and the stabilization of regenerated protein fibers."*

## Degradation 降解 (§15.7–15.8)

两种根本机制 —— **链端解聚** vs **主链随机断裂**：

| Mechanism 机理 | Source wording 原文定义 | Implication 含义 |
|---|---|---|
| Depolymerization (Unzipping) 解聚 | *"In depolymerization monomer is split off from an activated end group. This is the opposite of the addition polymerization and is often referred to as 'unzipping.'"* | 单体从活化端基逐个脱落，分子量初期下降慢、单体收率高 |
| Random Chain Scission 随机链断裂 | *"Chain scission is similar to the opposite of stepwise polycondensation where units are split apart in a random manner."* | 主链任意位置断裂，分子量急剧下降、力学性能快速劣化 |

降解触发因素包括热、紫外与氧化（thermal / UV / oxidative）。PP 属随机链断裂型（叔碳氢自由基链式氧化），与 PMMA 的 unzipping 型行为不同 —— 这是抗老化配方（主抗氧剂 + 副抗氧剂 + UV 吸收剂）选择的机理依据。

## Crosslinked Materials 交联与凝胶点 (§15.10)

> *"As the extent of crosslinking increases there is a steady increase in the viscosity of the melt. At some extent of crosslinking, there is a rapid increase in viscosity and the mixture becomes elastic and begins to feel like a rubber. At this point the mixture is said to be 'gelled.' Beyond this point the polymer is insoluble."*

**Flory 交联方程（原文 Eq. 15.37–15.44）**：

| Equation 方程 | Expression 表达式 | Meaning 含义 |
|---|---|---|
| Eq. 15.37 | Mw,p = Mw,0 (1 − p) / ((1 − p)(f − 1)) | 转化率 p 处的重均分子量 |
| Eq. 15.38 | pgel = 1/(f − 1) | 单一官能度类型体系的凝胶点转化率 |
| Eq. 15.39 | (pgel)² = 1 / [r(f′ − 1)(f″ − 1)] | 两种官能度类型体系的凝胶点 |
| Eq. 15.41 | DPw = DPw,0(1 − p) / ((1 − p)(DPw,0 − 1)) | 重均聚合度随转化率演化 |
| Eq. 15.42 | pgel = 1/(DPw,0 − 1) | 用初始重均聚合度表达凝胶点 |
| Eq. 15.44 | pgel = 1/DPn,0 | 用数均聚合度表达凝胶点 |

**解读**：官能度 f 越大，pgel 越小 → 凝胶点来得越早、网络越致密。这是 SAP 交联剂选型（三官能 vs 四官能交联剂）的定量依据。

## Internal Cyclization 内环化 — 碳纤维 (§15.11)

> *"Carbon fibers and associated composite materials are the result of internal cyclization. Polyacrylonitrile, when heated, undergoes internal addition forming a condensed polycyclic material called 'black orlon' (Sec. 9.4). Further heating to about 1000°C removes the hydrogen atoms and most of the nitrogens giving a polyaromatic structure containing about 95% carbon. Further heating to about 2800°C gives a product with almost 99% carbon."*

> *"When heated, polyacrylonitrile forms a complex ladder polymer containing a quina-zirine-like structure... This reaction is the first step in the formation of much of the carbon fibers."* — 梯型聚合物（ladder polymer）是内环化的中间结构。

## End-Group Reactivity 端基反应 (§15.12)

- **Macromer 大分子单体**：*"Polymers containing one reactive end group are often referred to as macromolecular monomers or macromers."* / *"Macromers are 'short' polymers that contain an active end group. This end group can be a site of unsaturation, heterocycle, or other group that can further react."*
- **Telechelic polymer 双官能端聚合物**：*"Polymers that contain two active end groups are referred to as telechelic polymers."* — 结构示意：`Reactive group—MMMMMMM—reactive group`

## Nature's Macromolecular Catalysts 酶催化 (§15.15)

- **Lock-and-key 锁钥模型**：*"calls for an exact or highly similar complementary fit between the enzyme and the reacting molecule(s). Geometry plays an essential part in permitting the electronic (polar and electrostatic) attractions to form the necessary complex."*
- **Induced-fit 诱导契合模型**：*"the enzyme originally does not fit the required shape. The required shape is achieved upon binding—the binding causing needed 'assisting factors' or proximity and orientation to effect a decrease in the energy of the transition state."*
- **Michaelis-Menten 米氏动力学**：*"The Michaelis-Menten model is commonly employed in describing nonallosteric enzyme reactions."*

| Relation 关系式 | Meaning 含义 |
|---|---|
| Ri = k₂[EM] | 由酶-底物复合物生成产物的速率 |
| Ratei = k₂[E]₀[M] / (K + [M]) | 米氏方程一般形式 |
| Rm = k₂[E]₀ | 酶饱和时的最大速率 |

*"Since the enzyme concentration is constant, the rate of product formation under these conditions is independent of [M] and is said to be zero order."*

## Mechanisms of Energy Absorption 能量吸收机制 (§15.16)

> *"There are three major mechanisms of energy absorption: shear yielding, crazing, and cracking. The latter two are often dealt with together and called normal stress yielding."*

| Mechanism 机理 | Source wording 原文 | Chinese 注解 |
|---|---|---|
| Shear yielding 剪切屈服 | *"In shear yielding, oriented regions are formed at 45° angles to the stress. No void space is produced in shear yielding."* | 无空穴，韧性材料的主要耗能方式 |
| Crazing 银纹化 | *"a craze is spanned top to bottom by fibrils that act to resist entrance of opportunistic molecules such as water"*；*"Crazing often occurs prior to and in front of a crack tip... the craze portion contains both fibrils and small voids"* | 含微空洞的纤维束结构，是裂纹前驱体 |
| Cracking 开裂 | *"A crack is an open fissure"* | 敞开裂隙，已失去承载能力 |
| ESC 环境应力开裂 | *"Aqueous solutions of surface-active materials, such as detergents, can produce brittle cracking, particularly in stressed materials. The term environmental stress cracking (ESC) was introduced to describe such situations."* | 表面活性剂促进的慢速脆性失效 |

**ESC 的关键认识（必须记住）**：*"In both ESC and environmental crazing, direct chemical attack on the polymer chain is minor or not involved. It appears that the organic liquid or other promoting substance is absorbed or locally dissolved at the defects so as to assist further failure, possibly by plasticization of the stressed material or modification of the surface energy."* — 即 **ESC 不是化学降解，而是塑化/表面能改性辅助的力学失效**。

## Hygiene Implication 卫材关联

本章对卫材有三处直接可落地的关联：

1. **SAP 交联度调控**（§15.10）：pgel = 1/(f − 1) 给出交联剂官能度与凝胶点的定量关系；交联剂添加量把凝胶点推到高转化率附近，使线性聚丙烯酸链充分增长后再凝胶，可最大化网络溶胀空间 —— 这是 AAP/CRC 与可萃取物水平的机理上游变量。
2. **PP/PE 无纺布货架期**（§15.7–15.8）：PP 属随机链断裂型降解，主链断裂导致分子量下降、脆化黄变；抗老化需主抗氧剂 + 副抗氧剂 + UV 吸收剂组合（原文未给具体配方与剂量，[待验证]）。
3. **薄膜/热封件的 ESC 失效**（§15.16）：湿巾盖、防水底膜、热封条长期接触表面活性剂，失效模式是 ESC 而非化学降解；选材与配方应针对"缺陷处塑化"而非"耐化学性"。

§15.11 碳纤维、§15.15 酶催化、§15.4 芳香侧基磺化制离子交换树脂与卫材业务**基本无关**，不强行关联。
