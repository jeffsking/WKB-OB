# Cabot DARCO KB-G Powdered Activated Carbon (TDS)

## Positioning 一句话定位

Manufacturer technical datasheet (TDS, dated 6 April 2016) for DARCO® KB-G powdered activated carbon by Cabot Norit Activated Carbon. In this library it anchors the **powdered activated carbon (PAC) option within the odour-control material family**（本页是气味管理材料族中「粉末活性炭」路线的厂商一手参数来源）.

## Summary 摘要

The document is a two-page datasheet. DARCO KB-G is produced by chemical activation of wood using the phosphoric acid process（木基磷酸法化学活化）and targets purification of highly colored and/or foaming process liquids in the food, chemical and pharmaceutical industries. It combines high adsorptive capacity with good filtration characteristics and is especially effective for high-molecular-weight organics such as large color bodies and proteins at low carbon dosage. The product is Kosher certified and meets the latest U.S. Food Chemicals Codex. Specifications are guaranteed values under lot-to-lot QC (ISO 9001); general characteristics are average values, not purchase specifications. Packaging: 15 kg multiply paper bags (48 bags/pallet, 720 kg net) or 2 × 250 kg bulk bags. Cabot lists regional contact points for North/South America, Europe (Cabot Norit Nederland B.V., Amersfoort), Asia Pacific (Shanghai) and Japan.
