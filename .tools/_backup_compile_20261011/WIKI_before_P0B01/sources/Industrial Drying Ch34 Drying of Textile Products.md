# Ch34: Drying of Textile Products

> **Source:** Carr, B., Lee, J. & Ok, J., in Mujumdar (ed.), *Handbook of Industrial Drying*, 4th ed., CRC Press, 2014. Ch.34, pp.777–790.

---

## 1. Scope Includes Nonwovens 范围包含非织造

> "Textile is defined as any product made from fibers. Thus textiles refer not only to woven fabrics but also to **nonwoven fabrics**, knitted fabrics, tufted fabrics... Approximately **25% of the energy** used in wet processing is consumed in drying." (p.779)

---

## 2. Energy Ladder 能耗阶梯

| Method | kJ/kg H₂O | Relative Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Squeeze** 挤压 | **58** | 1× (baseline) |
| **Vacuum** 真空 | **700** | 12× |
| **Steam can** 蒸汽烘缸 | **4,700** | 81× |
| **Convection** 对流 | **7,000** | 121× |

> "Mechanical dewatering is generally much less expensive than thermal drying. Thus, as much water as possible is usually removed mechanically." (p.779)

**Key rule:** Remove as much water mechanically as possible before thermal drying.

---

## 3. Key Design Rules 关键设计规则

### Predry to ~30% Regain, Then Final Drying 预干燥至~30%回潮率

> "Fabrics that are heat set in the drying process will be predried by some means to a moisture regain of approximately **30%**. They will be transported through a convection oven using a **tenter frame** that fixes the width of the fabric, giving it dimensional stability." (p.781)

### Hydrophilicity Dictates Dewatering Limit 亲水性决定脱水极限

> "For structures composed of hydrophilic fibers such as cotton, the moisture regain can typically be reduced from about **150% to 60–100%**. On the other hand, for structures composed of hydrophobic fibers such as polyester, **significantly lower moisture regain can be obtained**." (p.782)

**Hygiene implication:** Hydrophobic synthetic webs (PET/PP) shed water to a far lower regain than cellulosic ones — relevant for wipe substrate selection and SAP partitioning.

### Through-Air vs Impingement: Permeability Gate 穿流vs冲击：透气性门限

> "As the air permeability of the material increases, the airflow rate increases resulting in a higher drying rate. In this case, **through-air drying is a good option**; however, if air permeability of the material is too low... **impingement drying is often used instead**." (p.786)

### IR: Predry Only, Scorch Risk 红外：仅预干燥，烧焦风险

> "IR ovens are typically used to predry materials... IR drying is **not normally used to accomplish final drying** because IR energy is absorbed by dry textiles and there is a **danger of scorching or burning** the product." (p.788)

### RF/Microwave: Uniform, Low-Overdry 射频/微波：均匀、少过热

> RF/microwave "usually have the advantage of providing a more **uniform final drying** and reduced chance of **overdrying**" (p.788–789)
