# Wearable Electronics (NSR Editorial)

## One-line Position 一句话定位
A 2023 *National Science Review* guest editorial by Huisheng Peng outlining the evolution of wearable electronics from rigid → thin-film → fiber-shaped devices and introducing a 10-paper Special Topic.（彭慧胜的 NSR 客座社论，勾勒可穿戴电子从刚性到薄膜/纤维化的演进主线并导读特刊10篇论文。）

## Summary 摘要
- Wearable electronics spans chemistry, physics, materials science, electrical, and biomedical engineering; early devices were rigid, later flexibilized for safe/skin-contact comfort.（多学科领域，由刚性走向柔性。）
- Two mainstream routes: soft polymers as electrodes/active layers, or rigid materials patterned into stretchable (wavy) structures; thinning toward films is a key direction.（两大柔性化路线：软聚合物或刚性可拉伸结构；薄膜化是主流。）
- Fiber-shaped devices can be woven into highly flexible, breathable textiles looking like ordinary clothes and "washed for hundreds of cycles" — a major direction of the last decade.（纤维状器件可织入透气织物、可水洗数百次。）
- The Special Topic collects 10 papers: next-gen wearable power (J. Wang, P.G. Bruce), display futures (Z. Bao, Peng), artificial muscles (X. Zhou, Z. Liu), TENGs (Z.L. Wang), stretchable-interface strategies (X. Chen), thermal-drawn fibers (W. Yan), radiative cooling (C.-W. Qiu, G. Tao), epidermal electronics (interview with J.A. Rogers).（特刊10篇覆盖电源、显示、人工肌肉、摩擦发电、界面、热拉纤维、辐射制冷、表皮电子。）
- Outlook: academia + industry jointly push material synthesis, structure design, interface optimization, and real applications (e.g. textile displays already on market).（展望：产学研共推产业化。）

## Key Claims 关键论断
- Thin-film and fiber-shaped architectures are the two mainstream directions of modern wearable electronics.（薄膜化与纤维化是两大主流方向。）
- TENG-based self-powered sensing offers a battery-free power route for smart hygiene/wearable products.（摩擦纳米发电机自供能路线对无电池智能卫材有参考价值。）
