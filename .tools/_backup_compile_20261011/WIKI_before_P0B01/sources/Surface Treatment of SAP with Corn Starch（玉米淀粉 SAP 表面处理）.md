# Surface Treatment of SAP with Corn Starch（玉米淀粉 SAP 表面处理 / Melendres & Carrillo, AJChE 2019）

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

A 2019 peer-reviewed study (AJChE, Technological Institute of the Philippines) showing **corn starch as a renewable SAP surface modifier** that improves AUP and gel-layer permeability (GLP) — an academic prior-art anchor for the SAP surface-treatment theme.

## 摘要 / Abstract

Commercial SAP particles were mixed with a corn-starch suspension (0.002–0.20 g starch per g SAP) and dried at 150 °C. Free Swell Capacity (FSC) and Absorption Under Pressure (AUP) were measured at varying NaCl concentrations vs untreated SAP. At 0.04 g/g starch, AUP beat the untreated SAP; GLP improved significantly, explained by controlled particle swelling and a more porous gel layer that speeds liquid flow. Starch (a biodegradable carbohydrate polymer) is proposed as a renewable surface modifier for SAP used in baby diapers, incontinence products and feminine napkins.
