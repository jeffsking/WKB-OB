# CoPET/PET 与 PE/PET 成本结构拆解（Cost Teardown Workbook）

## Positioning 定位

Six-sheet engineering cost model（元/吨成品纤维；皮芯比 40/60；价格基准 2026-07；纯制造成本，不含利润与波动）decomposing CoPET/PET（TT 型）vs PE/PET（ET 型）bicomponent ES fibre economics from monomer prices through polymerization to sheath-core spinning. The quantitative backbone behind the CoPET sourcing blueprint（[[CoPET 采购蓝图：成本拆解与供应分层]]）。CoPET/PET vs PE/PET 成本拆解底表。）

## Summary 摘要

Sheet 1 fixes boundaries & inputs: PTA 5,775; MEG 4,350; IPA（华东进口）9,700; LDPE 9,250; ethylene ~7,300（一体化估算）; sheath:core 40/60; CoPET chip feed ratio PTA/IPA/MEG = 0.66/0.20/0.34; PET chip 0.86/0.34. Sheet 2 splits raw materials: in CoPET/PET fibre cost, PTA 47.3%, IPA 8.1%（CoPET 独有）, MEG 15.5%; in PE/PET, ethylene 29.9% + polymerization others 20.0%. Sheet 3 polymerization: CoPET copolymer window narrow（IPA）, PE high-pressure LDPE energy-heavy, PET routine — totals 918 vs 934 元/t fibre. Sheet 4 spinning: TT-type 1,850 元/t（same-polymer interface, high yield）vs ET-type 2,050（heterogeneous interface needs treatment）. Sheet 5 full cost: CoPET/PET 9,528 vs PE/PET 9,771 元/t（−2.5%）。Sheet 6 trade-off ledger: CoPET wins on manufacturing cost / compatibility yield / tunable 110–180 °C melt / chip ~22% cheaper than PE resin; PE/PET wins on no-modification, softer hand, mature supply, and terminal premium coverage.（六个工作表：边界→原料→聚合→纺丝→全成本→优劣清单。）
