# Trilobal PET for MD Distribution in 45 gsm Dual-Card ATB Liner

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

A full **re-engineering proposal for a 45 gsm two-card / four-dosing ATB liner**: substitute trilobal mono-PET in the lower card only, raise MD fluid spreading by ~30%, and hold converting at 600 m/min without losing skin softness or CD tensile. It is the batch's most complete **engineering-physics page** (groove capillary pressure, wicking-front kinetics, bond-density model, process window, converting failure modes, cost view).
本页是**45 gsm 双梳理 / 四给料 ATB 面层的完整重构方案**：仅在下层替换三叶形单组分 PET，机向铺展提升约 30%，并在 600 m/min 转化速度下不牺牲贴肤柔软与 CD 拉伸——是本批**工程物理最完整的一页**（沟槽毛细压、浸润前沿动力学、粘合密度模型、工艺窗口、转化失效模式、成本视角）。

## Summary 摘要

The ask is four-fold: maximise MD urine distribution length (replace part of the ES with trilobal-section mono-PET), protect skin-contact softness (no measurable regression in hand or surface feel), hold CD tensile at the level a 600 m/min converting line demands, and specify fibre composition per carding layer plus the process window.
需求有四条：最大化 MD 尿液铺展长度（以三叶形单组分 PET 替代部分 ES）、保护贴肤柔软（手感/表面触感无可测退化）、维持 600 m/min 转化线所需的 CD 拉伸、并逐梳理层给出纤维配比与工艺窗口。

The diagnosis is that an all-round-fibre web spreads badly in MD because fluid must repeatedly re-form a meniscus to hop from pore to pore — every hop costs pressure, and the front stalls once the large pores drain into the core. A trilobal axial groove, by contrast, is a capillary that never breaks along the fibre axis: where the fibre is MD-aligned the groove is an MD conduit, and it keeps drawing after the bulk pores have emptied. The deck cites ≈300% surface area of a round fibre at equal denier, 10–30× groove suction vs inter-fibre pore suction, and the published wicking ranking 4DG > trilobal > round.
诊断指出：全圆截面纤网 MD 铺展差，是因为液体必须反复重建弯月面才能在孔间跳跃，每次跳跃都要消耗压力，大孔排入芯体后前沿即停滞；而三叶形轴向沟槽是**沿纤维轴不断裂的毛细管**，纤维 MD 取向时沟槽即为 MD 导管，在大孔排空后仍能持续吸水。文中给出：同旦数下约为圆形纤维 **300% 表面积**、沟槽吸力为纤维间孔隙的 **10–30 倍**、已发表芯吸排序为 **4DG > 三叶形 > 圆形**。

The design principle is **decoupling functions across the two cards**: card 1 (top) stays 100% ES with low doffer draft (≈2:1 MD/CD) to carry CD tensile and cross-web integrity; card 2 (bottom) takes ES + up to 45% trilobal PET with high doffer draft (≈4:1 MD/CD), long cut length and low crimp to build straight MD grooves — and is allowed to lose CD tensile because the 25 gsm ES top half defends the web-level value.
设计原则是**把功能解耦到两张梳理**：第 1 梳理（上层）保持 100% ES、低道夫牵伸（MD/CD≈2:1），承担 CD 拉伸与横向完整性；第 2 梳理（下层）用 ES + 最高 45% 三叶形 PET、高道夫牵伸（≈4:1）、长切断、低卷曲以形成顺直 MD 沟槽——它被允许损失 CD 拉伸，因为网级 CD 值由未改动的 25 gsm ES 上半层守住。

### Converting failure modes at 600 m/min (risk-ranked) 600 m/min 转化失效模式

1. **HIGH — Ultrasonic seals**: PET does not soften at PP/PE ultrasonic settings, so in a cuff or side-seam lane it behaves as unbonded filler and seal strength drops without warning. Keep PET out of ultrasonic lanes or switch those lanes to thermal/adhesive bonding.
2. **MED — Edge tear & CD strain**: reduced CD tensile shows up as edge nicks propagating at spreader bars and bowed rolls. Keep unwind tension ≤2.0 N/cm, run tapered tension, inspect slit edges at every roll change.
3. **MED — Lint and fuzz**: higher mono-fibre fraction plus faster blade wear → more free fibre ends. Add vacuum lint extraction at the slitter and ahead of the glue station.
4. **MED — Static**: PET tribocharges far more than PE. Static bars before glue applicator and core placement; specify antistat in the spin finish.
5. **LOW — Adhesive bonding**: PET surface energy ≈43 mN/m vs PE ≈31 mN/m → hot-melt wet-out and green strength should improve slightly.

### Trial plan and gate 试验计划与门

Four phases: (1) fibre qualification (2–3 wks: lobe-profile drawings from 2–3 suppliers, contact angle after 1st/2nd/3rd wetting, dry-heat shrinkage at 140/150 °C); (2) carding trial (1 wk: card 2 draft at 1.25/1.45/1.60, MD/CD orientation measurement); (3) line trial (1 wk: Options A and B, nominal and compensated oven, full property set, hold 200 m for converting); (4) converting at 600 m/min, ≥30 min steady state — ultrasonic seal strength first, then lint/static/blade wear, then finished-product MD stain, rewet, run-off.
**Gate to phase 4**: MD stain length ≥ +20% vs incumbent **AND** CD tensile ≥9.0 N/50 mm **AND** hand index ≤108. Miss any one → drop back to Option A.
