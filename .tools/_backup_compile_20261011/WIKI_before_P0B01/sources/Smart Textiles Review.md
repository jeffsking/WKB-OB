# Smart Textiles Review

## One-line Position 一句话定位
A 2022 MDPI *Textiles* review giving the field's backbone taxonomy (passive/active/ultra-smart) and a full function map — chromic, thermoregulation, shape-memory, waterproof-breathable, wearable electronics, PCM — explicitly linking nonwoven and hygiene products.（MDPI 综述，提供智能纺织品分类骨架与完整功能版图，并明确点名非织造与卫生产品。）

## Summary 摘要
- Three-tier taxonomy is the skeleton: passive (sense only), active (sense + respond), ultra-smart (sense + respond + adapt); core components = sensors, actuators (active), control units (ultra-smart). External stimuli: mechanical, thermal, magnetic, chemical, electrical.（三级分类 + 三类组件。）
- Three implementation routes: inherent fabric conductivity, post-fabrication circuit attachment, or hybrid; main methods = conductive yarns (weave/knit/embroider/laminate/stitch), printing (screen/inkjet), and component interconnect.（三条实现路径与制造方法。）
- Function map spans chromic (7 mechanisms), temperature regulation, shape memory, waterproof-breathable, wearable electronics, and PCM — microencapsulated PCM can be incorporated into the nonwoven structure.（功能版图 + PCM可入非织造。）
- Strong market framing: global smart-textiles CAGR ~25% (2021–2031), reaching US$23.82 Bn by 2031; medical smart textiles USD 2.10 Bn by 2027 (CAGR 7.51%); hygiene products ≈ 50% of medical-textiles market.（市场数据。）
- Six core challenges: textile–electronics incompatibility, lack of regulations/standards, washability, recyclability, scale-up, biocompatibility & comfort.（六大挑战。）
- "The most important feature is comfort" for wearables — must also meet mechanical resistance and durability; requires cross-disciplinary integration.（舒适性是首要接受参数。）

## Key Claims 关键论断
- Hygiene products are ~50% of the medical-textiles market — the primary battleground for smart-function deployment in absorbent/hygiene goods.（卫生产品是医疗纺织品近半市场，智能功能落地主战场。）
- Microencapsulated PCM can be loaded into nonwoven structures — directly applicable to diaper/core comfort layers without changing line form.（微胶囊PCM可入非织造，可直接用于卫材舒适层。）
- Chromic materials (7 stimulus mechanisms) provide a ready knowledge map for wetness/temperature indicators on hygiene products.（七类变色机制为湿感/温度指示提供现成知识地图。）
