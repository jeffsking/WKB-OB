# Liquid Crystalline Polymeric Wires for Selective Proton Transport, Part 1

## One-line Position 一句话定位

This chapter establishes the *fabrication* of bio-inspired, water-free proton-conducting "polymeric wires" — dendronised PAZE self-assembled into columnar LCP channels inside anodised aluminium oxide (AAO) templates.
（中文：本章确立生物启发、无水质子导电"聚合物线"的制备路线——树枝化 PAZE 在阳极氧化铝（AAO）模板孔内自组装成柱列液晶通道。）

## Summary 摘要

- Motivated by the two drawbacks of Nafion® — water-dependent transport and methanol crossover — the work turns to bio-inspired, anhydrous supramolecular nanochannels.
  （中文：针对 Nafion® 两大缺陷——依赖水的传输与甲醇穿透——本研究转向生物启发、无水的超分子纳米通道。）
- Side-chain liquid crystalline poly[2-(aziridin-1-yl)ethanol] (PAZE), grafted with discotic dendrons at 40% and 72% modification degrees, is the wire backbone; the polymer main chain forms the channel and backbone nitrogen acts as the electron donor for hopping transport without water.
  （中文：以 40% 与 72% 改性度的侧链液晶 PAZE 为线材基体；聚合物主链构成通道，骨架氮原子作为电子供体实现无需水的跳跃式传输。）
- AAO provides mechanical strength and induces homeotropic (perpendicular-to-surface) column alignment via its well-defined cylindrical pores; it is electrically insulating and suited to nanodevices.
  （中文：AAO 提供机械强度并借规整圆柱孔诱导柱列垂直（homeotropic）取向；其为电绝缘体，适合纳米器件。）
- Baking (90 °C for 10 min, then cooling at 0.5 °C/min to 30/40/50 °C with 3/24/72 h isothermal holds) precisely controls column orientation and order; the process scales to 187 cm² and is reproducible.
  （中文：热退火（90 °C 10 min 后 0.5 °C/min 冷却至 30/40/50 °C 并等温 3/24/72 h）精确控制柱列取向与有序度；工艺可放大至 187 cm² 且可重复。）
- Low swelling and tunable hydrophobicity (contact angle 86° → 168°) lay the groundwork for selective proton/methanol transport, which is characterised in Part 2.
  （中文：低溶胀与可调疏水性（接触角 86° → 168°）为后续"选择性质子/甲醇传输"（Part 2）奠定基础。）

## Key Numbers & Conclusions 提炼数字与结论

| Item 项 | Value 数值 | Note 备注 |
|---|---|---|

| AAO support AAO 支撑 | pores 0.02 / 0.2 μm, disc Ø 13 mm | ~40% of surface is pore area 孔面积占比 |

| Columns per pore 单孔柱数 | ~225 | Avg column ~42.1 Å |

Conclusion 结论: nanoconfinement + dendronised PAZE + AAO yields oriented, low-swelling, water-free proton channels; the higher-modification PAZE-72 is recommended for transport applications.
（中文结论：纳米限域 + 树枝化 PAZE + AAO 形成取向、低溶胀、无水的质子通道；高改性度 PAZE-72 被推荐用于传输应用。）

## Key Claims 关键论断

- Supramolecular organisation is crucial for efficient proton transport — discotic LCPs self-assemble into continuous 1D wires via π–π stacking.
  （中文：超分子有序结构对高效质子传输至关重要。）
- Columnar orientation is required for ion-conducive channels; disordered columns give no pathway.
  （中文：柱列取向是形成离子传导通道的必要条件。）
- The channels are acid-free and transport via a hopping mechanism without needing water.
  （中文：通道不含酸，靠跳跃机制传输，无需水。）
- Low water/methanol uptake (~11%) is a major improvement over acid-based membranes (Nafion ~21%).
  （中文：低水/甲醇吸收（~11%）相对酸性基膜（Nafion ~21%）是显著改进。）
