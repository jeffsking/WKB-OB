# EP1146916B1 Oil Resistant Polybutylene-Based Hot Melt Adhesive (Bostik)

## 一句话定位

Granted European patent (Ato Findley / Bostik) on an **oil-resistant, polybutylene-based hot melt adhesive** — a specialty durability variant in Bostik's disposable hot-melt portfolio (this batch's third Bostik hot-melt patent).

## 摘要

The patent (title: "OIL RESISTANT POLYBUTYLENE-BASED HOT MELT ADHESIVE") grants an oil-resistant hot melt adhesive based on polybutylene, for disposable articles. The bibliographic header lists proprietor Ato Findley Inc (Wauwatosa, WI, US) — a Bostik predecessor entity — and multiple inventors including Strelow, Oteman, Gibes, Simons, Alper, Harris, Kanderski. It is the European phase of PCT/US2000/001416 (WO 2000/044412), priority US 238119 (1999-01-27). Detailed claim language is in the full text; this summary captures the bibliographic and classification scope.
