# IPC BCC FEI Update（IA2.0 · 2026-06-22）

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

IPC BCC 的**前端创新（FEI）汇报**（Jing Shi, 2026-06，基于 IA2.0 消费者测试与 IPC 组合计划）：9 个大注（big bets）+ 创新日历 2027–2029+ + GRD 支持管线。本库定位为 [[IPC BCC OI Portfolio（开放创新项目组合）]] 的**上游漏斗页**——FEI 筛出的高潜机会即 OI 组合的候选来源。

## Summary 摘要

Deck states 141 innovation concepts across tiers were developed to future-proof the business; FEI CATS captured **9 high-potential opportunities**. The winning-protection block centers on **LeakShield** (more coverage, 6x absorption, ready now; scopes CN/AU/BR/YK) and TRY (from proven CN solution to ANZ premium scale-up and BR affordable development — with dtx/spacing/USD costings and 0.4Mn CAPEX per machine). Skin health & breathability block: **pH balance** (first pH-balanced nappy liner infused with Zinc to neutralize pee/poo impact), **Ultra Slim Core / HUGGIES Layered Slim** (0.1 cm ultra-thin light core, one-way moisture-wicking zero-feel surface, 10% weight-reduced), **Breathable OC** and **Airy Window** (waistband with breathable "mini windows"; up to 12 h dry skin). Future innovation spaces needing technology development: **Thermal Regulation** (performed well in YK/CN/BR among affluent consumers), **Microbiome** (IA2.0 sentiment improving; needs differentiation + regulatory support), **Odor Control** (balance deodorization vs change-indication). A 2027–2029+ innovation calendar lists TRY/TRY LITE, ROTC Slim, Peach Vitamin, Peachy Play, Slim II, FITRA upgrades, COCO V6+Airism, Kirigami, Biocare pre/probiotics, LeakShield Superdam, 360 Pant Pocket, K-C Sensitive items etc., with "another 6 put into FEI pool". Two project cards are detailed: **Cohesive fastener** (replace hook&loop; cohesive A/B; high shear low peel; stable after aging; no glossy appearance; ~50% cost saving vs hook/loop; shear/peel strength to improve; TRL 1/1/2 → TRL4 2027) and **Odor Control** (multi-path: capture/alter/microbial/perception; platforms AqFresh liner-film, carbon-based, zeolite catalyst/particles; eNose screening results as in key claims). Final tables list GRD-supported ongoing projects with FEI owners and TRL4 years, and a launch-time pipeline (Healthy Skin: AIRism 2.0 2028, BioCare 2029, NanoBreeze 2.0 2030; Leakage: COCO V6 2028, Waffle 2029, 360 pocket 2030; Fit & Comfort: Affordable TRY 2027, Cohesive 2028, Thermal control 2029, Cooling Summer 2030; Specific Occasions: Odor Control 2029, Smart diaper 2030, Sustainability)。
