# Ebewole Polymer Science Ch15 Polymer Properties and Applications

> Source: Ebewole, *Polymer Science and Technology*, Ch.15 — Polymer Properties and Applications (pp. 414–451).
> 定位说明：本章源文件带**正式印张页码**（414–451），引文均标注页码；源提取件明确存疑处标注 [待验证]。

## Executive Summary 内容概要

Chapter 15 is the book's **"application dictionary"**: for each polymer family it gives the structural feature, key properties (Tg/Tm/crystallinity/strength), processing route and typical applications. It covers 20+ families — PE (LD/HD/LL/Ultra-HD), PP, PS/GP-PS/HIPS/SAN, PVC/CPVC, PMMA, polyacrylates, PAN, ABS, nylons (6 / 6,6 / 4,6 / 6,10 / 11 / 12), PC, PPO, PPS, PET/PBT, fluoropolymers (PTFE / PVDC / PCTFE), NBR, PU, TPE, and ionomers.

本章对卫材的价值在于**选型第一手依据**：原书在 Table 15.3（p. 426）明确列出 PP 的 *"Nonwovens … are important applications"* —— 非织造布是 PP 的重要应用；PE、PET、PAN、PU、PTFE 亦各有卫材映射。

## Polyethylene 聚乙烯 (p. 418)

> *"Low-density polyethylene is a partially crystalline solid with a degree of crystallinity in the 50 to 70% range, melting temperature of 100 to 120°C, and specific gravity of about 0.91 to 0.94."* (p. 418)

> *"High-density polyethylene, on the other hand, has few side chains, typically 1 per 200 carbon main chain atoms. Linear polyethylenes are highly crystalline, with a melting point over 127°C — usually about 135°C."* (p. 418)

| Grade 牌号 | Crystallinity 结晶度 | Tm 熔点 | Specific gravity 比重 | Chain architecture 链结构 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LDPE | 50–70% | 100–120 °C | 0.91–0.94 | 支化多 |
| HDPE | 高结晶 | > 127 °C，通常 ~135 °C | — | 侧链少（~1/200 主链碳） |

## Polypropylene 聚丙烯 (pp. 422–426)

> *"Isotactic polypropylene is essentially linear, with an ordered arrangement of propylene molecules in the polymer chain."* (p. 422)
> *"Isotactic polypropylene crystallizes with three methylene units per turn of the helix."* (p. 422)

- **与 PE 的结晶构象差异**：原文作 *"Unlike polyethylene, isotactic polyethylene does not crystallize in a planar zigzag conformation due to steric hindrance from the relatively bulky methyl group."* —— 源提取件已加注：**此处 "isotactic polyethylene" 应为 "isotactic polypropylene"**（甲基位阻使其无法取平面锯齿构象，只能取螺旋构象，每圈 3 个亚甲基单元）。引用本书时建议按"等规聚丙烯"理解，原书措辞 [待验证]。
- **卫材点名**：*"Nonwovens … are important applications."*（Table 15.3, p. 426）

## Poly(vinyl chloride) 聚氯乙烯 (p. 427)

> *"Unstabilized PVC undergoes dehydrochlorination when heated above its Tg (about 87°C) — for example, during melt processing."* (p. 427)

**加工警示**：未稳定化 PVC 在 Tg（~87 °C）以上即脱 HCl —— 熔体加工必须配热稳定剂，加工窗口比"Tm 以下都能加工"的直觉窄得多。

## Polyacrylonitrile 聚丙烯腈 (p. 431)

- *"Acrylic and modacrylic fibers … have more than 85% acrylonitrile content."*（腈纶/改性腈纶的定义门槛）
- *"PAN is the starting material for carbon fiber production."* —— PAN 是碳纤维前驱体。
- 结构特征：*"PAN … strong hydrogen bonding, stiff rod-like chains"*（源提取件注明见第 5 章引述）。

## ABS (p. 432)

> *"ABS consists of a continuous SAN matrix with dispersed rubber (polybutadiene) domains."* (p. 432)
> *"Toughness is achieved by the impact of rubber particles on the matrix — crack termination at the rubber particles."*

**增韧机理**：橡胶粒子作为裂纹终止点（crack termination）—— 与 Seymour Ch.15 §15.16 的 shear yielding / crazing 能量吸收框架是同一问题的两种表述。

## Nylons / Polyamides 聚酰胺 (p. 436)

> *"Nylon 6,6 has a higher melting point (265°C) than Nylon 6 (220°C) due to the stronger hydrogen bonding between chains."* (p. 436)
> *"Moisture absorption in nylons decreases with increasing hydrocarbon content between amide groups."* (p. 436)

| Nylon 品种 | Tm 熔点 (per source) | Note 注解 |
|---|---|---|
| Nylon 6,6 | 265 °C | 链间氢键更强 |
| Nylon 6 | 220 °C | — |

吸湿性随酰胺基之间烃链段增长而下降 —— 这是 PA 类材料在潮湿环境尺寸稳定性的结构根源。

## Polycarbonate 聚碳酸酯 (p. 437)

> *"PC is produced by interfacial polycondensation."* / *"PC is a glassy, amorphous polymer with excellent clarity, high impact strength, and good heat resistance."* (p. 437)

## Engineering Polyesters — PET / PBT (p. 443)

> *"PET is produced by direct esterification or transesterification followed by polycondensation."* (p. 443)
> *"PET is a semicrystalline polymer with excellent mechanical properties and chemical resistance."*
> *"PET is the primary fiber for textile and tire cord applications."*

## Fluoropolymers — PTFE (p. 444)

> *"PTFE has an extremely high melting point (327°C), is highly crystalline, and has exceptional chemical resistance."* (p. 444)

## Polyurethanes 聚氨酯 (p. 447)

> *"PU is a family of polymers formed by the reaction of diisocyanates with polyols."* (p. 447)

应用覆盖全线：foams、elastomers、fibers、coatings、adhesives。

## Thermoplastic Elastomers 热塑性弹性体 (pp. 448–450)

> *"TPE's consist of block copolymers with hard and soft segments."* (p. 448)
> *"Thermoplastic polyamides are a relatively new class of block-copolymer thermoplastic elastomers. They have amide linkages between the hard and soft segments. This class of thermoplastic elastomers has the highest performance and is the most expensive of the TPEs."* (pp. 448–450)

## Unsaturated Network Polyesters 不饱和网络聚酯 (p. 449)

> *"Unsaturated network polyesters may be produced by using an unsaturated dibasic acid or glycol or both."*
> *"This prepolymer is then dissolved in styrene monomer, which participates in cross-linking with the double bonds in the prepolymer."* (p. 449)

## Hygiene Implication 卫材关联

本章是卫材原料选型的**直接可用**章节，原书即点名了非织造布：

1. **PP（p. 426）**：*"Nonwovens … are important applications"* —— 卫材非织造布第一大原料；等规 PP 的螺旋结晶（每圈 3 个亚甲基单元）是其高熔点与可纺性的结构根源。
2. **PE（p. 418）**：LDPE 结晶度 50–70%、Tm 100–120 °C，HDPE Tm ~135 °C —— 直接对应纺粘 PE 低熔点共混层（低温热封/粘合）与 HDPE 硬挺层的选材分界。
3. **PET（p. 443）**：*"primary fiber for textile and tire cord applications"*，纺粘 PET 在湿巾与包装中大量使用。
4. **PTFE（p. 444）**：Tm 327 °C、高结晶、耐化学 —— 高性能过滤膜（疏水/化学稳定）方向的候选。
5. **PU / TPE（pp. 447–450）**：非织造布粘合胶、弹性组件、涂层；TPE 的硬段-软段嵌段结构解释其"热塑加工 + 橡胶弹性"。
6. **加工警示（p. 427）**：PVC 在 Tg ~87 °C 以上脱 HCl，涉及 PVC 的纺丝/加工必须控温并加热稳定剂。
7. **PA 吸湿（p. 436）**：非织造布在潮湿环境下的尺寸稳定性需按"酰胺基间烃链长度"评估。
