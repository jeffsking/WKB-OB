# US10905605 — Pull-On Wearable Article with Informational Image（带信息图案的套穿式可穿戴制品）

> Source: US patent 10,905,605 B2, **The Procter & Gamble Company（宝洁）, Cincinnati, OH (US)**. Filed Oct. 23, 2017 (Appl. No. 15/790,191); granted **Feb. 2, 2021**. Earlier publication US 2018/0055700 A1 (Mar. 1, 2018). Term extended/adjusted under 35 U.S.C. 154(b) by **382 days**, and subject to a terminal disclaimer. Int. Cl. A61F 13/15, A61F 13/84. **3 Claims, 15 Drawing Sheets.**
>
> 拆解说明（source's own note）：原始 txt 为专利公告文本 OCR，双栏交错；本页引用均按原文 verbatim。**本专利主题为拉拉裤/训练裤上的「信息图案」（informational image）认知引导设计，并非电子传感** —— 尽管源文件名含「传感」字样。

## Technical Field 技术领域

Pull-on pants-type wearable articles（套穿式裤型可穿戴制品）adapted for use by a **pre-literate child（学龄前不识字儿童）**. The invention sits in the field of *visual + tactile user guidance on the garment exterior*, not in electronics or wetness chemistry:

- Int. Cl. **A61F 13/15**（吸收制品）and **A61F 13/84**（吸收制品的部件/附件）; additional classifications listed: A61F 13/56, 13/496, 13/514, 13/49, 13/68, 13/72, 13/42.
- Inventors: Donald Carroll Roe; Melanie D Allen; Kathleen Quinlan Ames-Ooten; Mark John Ciesko; Barry Robert Feist; Eiro Fukkuda; George Bartol Glackin.
- Continuation chain（优先权链）: 11/083,606 (2005-03-18, now US 7,887,522) → 12/985,682 (2011-01-06, now US 8,657,802) → 14/159,962 (2014-01-21, now US 9,844,478) → 15/790,191.

## Problem Solved 解决的问题

The patent addresses a **donning-ergonomics（穿戴人机工学）** problem, not a fluid-handling problem.

| Problem 问题 | Source wording 原文表述 | Note 备注 |
|---|---|---|
| Pull-on articles must be dragged over hips/buttocks 需拉过臀胯 | "these articles are typically placed on a user in a closed waist configuration, and therefore are designed to be pulled up over the hips and buttocks of the user into position around the waist and between the legs." | 闭合腰围构型导致穿戴困难 |
| Children instinctively grab the FRONT waist region 儿童本能抓前腰 | "most children, particularly in the **12-30 month age range**, are naturally inclined to grab the most easily visible and accessible portion of the pull-on product, which is the front waist region." | 目标年龄段 12–30 个月 |
| Front pulling jams the product 前部拉扯会卡住 | "this action increases the circumferential tension in the back of the diaper, causing it to lodge tightly at the bottom of the buttocks." | 后腰周向张力增大，卡在臀底 |
| Prior art (Klemp, US 6,702,795) fails pre-literate users 现有技术在学龄前无效 | "Klemp generally discloses 'decorative or instructional printing' and specifically shows words printed on the article. A pre-literate child, however, would not understand such printing" | 文字印刷 + 低照度/浅视角难辨 |
| Stated need 需求 | "there is a need for an article to quickly and unerringly convey visual messages, instructions, or cues in a manner that is understandable to and responsive by a pre-literate child." | 无需识字的快速无误传达 |

## Core Mechanism 核心机理

The "mechanism" is **cognitive + haptic signalling（认知与触觉信号）**, not a chromic or electrical transition:

1. **Cognitively functional graphic（认知功能图形）** — a graphic *in the image of a hand* placed on the side region. The hand image is self-explanatory: it tells the child "grasp here" without words.
2. **Texture feature（纹理特征）** — a physical, feelable structure formed *in the laminate*: outer nonwoven layer + inner nonwoven layer + **elastomeric element（弹性体元件）disposed between** them, where **the outer layer comprises apertures（开孔）which expose portions of the elastomeric element**, thereby providing the texture feature on the exterior surface. The graphic is **proximate to** this texture feature.
3. **Composite image（复合图像）** — printed graphic and texture can combine into one image, e.g. a printed palm whose fingers/finger-pads are formed by the texture pattern.
4. **Legibility vs. laminate state（可读性与层合体状态）** — the graphics must be **legible when the laminate is in the relaxed state（松弛态下可读）**. Two process routes are described: print the true image on the relaxed laminate, or print a distorted image on the extended laminate so it resolves on retraction.
5. **Non-chromic（非变色）** — the source explicitly characterises this as a graphic/texture design; **no thermochromic, hydrochromic or conductive sensing element is claimed**.

## Claims Coverage 权利要求覆盖范围

Claim 1 is a **structure + visual + function tightly coupled（结构+视觉+功能强耦合）** claim. Elements:

| Element 要素 | Requirement 要求 |
|---|---|
| E1 preamble | "A pull-on pants type wearable article **adapted for use by a pre-literate child**" — a use/audience limitation |
| E2 main portion | outer cover; defines front waist region, rear waist region, crotch region extending between and connecting them |
| E3 side regions + laminate | first and second side regions adjacent the main portion; laminate overlapping at least part of the main portion forming first/second transverse regions adjacent to the respective side regions; **at least a portion of the laminate extendable between a relaxed state and an extended state** |
| E4 graphics | a **first and second cognitively functional graphic in the image of a hand**, one on each side region; at least a portion of each disposed on the laminate; at least a portion of each disposed on the respective transverse region; **both legible when the laminate is in the relaxed state** |
| E5 texture feature | each graphic **proximate to a texture feature** formed in the laminate: outer nonwoven + inner nonwoven + elastomeric element between them, **outer layer comprising apertures exposing portions of the elastomeric element** |
| E6 function | graphics + texture **"provide an informational image to the child that the article is to be grasped in the location of the image"** |

Claim 2: "wherein the graphic is printed directly on the laminate." Claim 3: "wherein the elastomeric element includes at least one of an elastic film and a strand."

All six E1–E6 limitations must be present together; the claim is narrow (hand shape, both sides, relaxed-state legibility, apertured-nonwoven texture).

## Hygiene Implication 卫材关联

Relevance to the owner's **chromic-ink（变色油墨）programme** and REL absorbent-core work:

1. **Not a chromic-ink patent — but a strong "where to print" reference.** This patent claims *where* and *how* a graphic sits on an extensible side panel (relaxed-state legibility, apertured-nonwoven texture proximity). A chromic wetness or comfort indicator placed on a stretch laminate must meet the same **relaxed-state legibility（松弛态可读）** and **print-durability（耐水/尿液不洇渗、不转移）** requirements spelled out here. See [[Hygiene Chromic Ink Supplier Landscape（卫生护理变色油墨供应商调研 · 2026-06-12）]].
2. **Explicit hook for wetness signalling.** The specification lists **"feel wet liner（湿感衬里）"** among the messages an informational image may convey — i.e. the same patent family contemplates a wetness-related cue at the same location, which is adjacent to — but distinct from — a chromic wetness indicator. Adjacent prior art: [[US 20070270773 A1（Thermochromic Wetness Indicator）]].
3. **Printing-process constraints transfer directly to chromic inks.** Printing on **zero-strain vs pre-stretch laminates**, choosing relaxed-state vs extended-state artwork, cross-seam registration tolerance via curved borders — these are the same process variables that govern applying thermochromic/hydrochromic inks on elastic ears or side panels.
4. **Texture + colour as composite signal.** Apertured-nonwoven-over-elastomer texture can be combined with a printed/chromic image to give **visual + tactile dual cueing（视觉+触觉双提示）**; relevant for baby training pants and for adult-incontinence or cognitively impaired users.
5. **Contrast with true sensing routes.** For the smart-sensing track, compare [[US8273939B2 Disposable Absorbent Articles Having a Windowed Removable Sensor (P&G)]]（windowed removable sensor）and [[US11013641B2 Garment for Detecting Absorbent Article Leakage (Kimberly-Clark)]]（leakage detection）; see also [[Smart Sensing Diaper Technology Research Report（智能感知纸尿裤技术研究报告 · 2026-04）]].
6. **Freedom-to-operate（自由实施）: NOT assessed here.** Term/jurisdiction status is **not resolved by this source**. The source records a 382-day PTA and a terminal disclaimer plus a four-member continuation chain, but **no expiry date or jurisdiction status can be asserted from it** — flagged as `[待验证]` / manual review. Any FTO question must go to IP counsel with the USPTO record and the corresponding CN/EP family members.
7. **Core/absorbency: no relevance.** The patent does not touch topsheet, ADL, REL or SAP structure; do not cite it for absorbent-core design claims.
