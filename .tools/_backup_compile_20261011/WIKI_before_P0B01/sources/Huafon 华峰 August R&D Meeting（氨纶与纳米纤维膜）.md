# Huafon 华峰 August R&D Meeting（氨纶与纳米纤维膜）

## 一句话定位

**华峰（Huafon）** 在 8 月研发交流会上的材料，主题为**卫品用氨纶（常规/细旦/生物基/无溶剂）**与**纳米纤维防水透湿膜（WBM）**。本批作为「弹性体与功能膜供应方」信息源。

## 摘要

The deck is Huafon's August R&D meeting material. **Group**: headquartered in Wenzhou, founded **1991**, Top-500 Chinese enterprise, **3 listed companies**, **18,000+** employees, ~**US$10bn** 2025 revenue, **#1 global capacity** in adipic acid, polyurethane and microfibre. **Huafon Chemical** (est. Dec 1999, listed 2006, ~67% private / 33% public) makes spandex, polyurethane and adipic acid from two Chinese bases (Ruian-Zhejiang, Fuling-Chongqing).

**Hygiene portfolio**:
1. **Common spandex** — **120–1100 dtex**; packages 1.0 / 2.0 / 3.0 / 4.5 kg; colours natural, purple, pink, light blue, dark blue; custom specs available.
2. **Fine-denier spandex** — **120 / 160 / 240 / 320 dtex** for the **waist of feminine-care pants and baby pull-ups**, all validated by customers; a **new formulation** is offered to **reduce breakage risk**, requiring a process change on the customer side.
3. **Nanofiber WBM** — **PU** made by **high-voltage electrospinning**: pore size between water vapour (~0.0004 μm) and liquid droplets (~100 μm); **hydrostatic pressure 8000 mmH₂O**, **air permeability 3 CFM**, **4 gsm**, **8 μm**, **WVTR >100,000 g/m²/24h**, versus PE dry/wet-process benchmarks (1620 / 625 / 1298 mmH₂O; 0.056–0.103 CFM; >25 gsm; WVTR 8,000–10,000).
4. **Bio-based spandex** — **Susterra® 1,3-propanediol** route (corn → bio-BDO/PDO → bio-PTMEG + MDI → bio-elastane), **>70% bio-based content**; LCA shows **2.18 kg CO₂-eq/kg** vs 5.0 (PO route) and 4.5 (BDO), non-renewable energy **63.9 MJ/kg** vs 111.0 / 94.6. Huafon will have its own **bio-PTMEG** capability. Challenge: **recovery rate** may not consistently match conventional spandex.
5. **Solvent-free spandex** — **DMAC-free**; DMAc **ND** (not detected) by **EN 17131:2019 / GC-MS** (limit 500 mg/kg); better creep performance and higher modulus. Challenges: **low heat-bonding temperature 130–145 °C**, lower elongation and recovery.

The deck closes with the spandex production flow (PTMG + MDI → polymerisation → DMAC dissolving → spinneret → false twister → godet rolls → finishing).
