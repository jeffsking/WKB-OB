# 功能性纤维产品简介（PTT/超仿棉/咖啡炭/纳米凉感）

## 一句话定位

某中国功能性纤维企业的产品手册，覆盖 PTT 弹性纤维、超仿棉、超柔丽、抗紫外涤纶、负离子涤纶、**咖啡炭纤维（消臭）**、**库思玛纳米凉感纤维**等。作为「差别化/功能纤维供应商」信息源。

## 摘要

The catalog introduces a range of differentiated functional fibers. **PTT fiber** has elasticity comparable to spandex-wrap yarn (~20% fabric stretch) at lower cost with good dimensional stability. **Super-cotton (超仿棉)** mimics cotton in look/handle/comfort yet exceeds it in wicking, anti-static and thermal stability, and can be upgraded with antibacterial, flame-retardant or UV functions. **Coffee-carbon fiber** uses honeycomb carbon pores from recycled coffee grounds to physically adsorb odor — 80–90% deodorization on a 40 PPM ammonia test — and adds warming/heat-retention. **Coolsmart nano-cooled fiber (库思玛)** embeds a nano cooling element to reduce moisture and give a cool-dry touch for summer lightweight fabrics.
