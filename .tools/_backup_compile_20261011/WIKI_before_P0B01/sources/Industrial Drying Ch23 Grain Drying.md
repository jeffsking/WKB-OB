# Chapter 23 — Grain Drying

> Source: Mujumdar (ed.), Handbook of Industrial Drying, 4th ed., CRC Press, 2014, Ch.23, pp.561–571.

(Raghavan & Sosle 执笔。页码由 PDF 页 − 21 推算，应与印刷版抽查核对。)

**相关度声明**：食品/农业章 — 对卫材**直接相关性低到中**（★★★）。全章不提绒毛浆/SAP/非织造布；可迁移的是三个框架：深床 vs 薄层、调质/多段干燥、双护栏规格逻辑 — 对绒毛浆打包/卷取调理与仓储湿度控制确有实用价值。不为谷物/农业场景编造卫材应用。

## Scope 范围与定位

Equipment-and-operations survey: low-intensity crop conditioning (aeration, natural-air, in-storage supplemental heat) → heated-air bin/portable dryers (batch/recirculating/continuous) → dryer selection → solar → nonconventional routes (heat-pump, desiccant, particle–particle conduction) → hay drying. Central logic: match dryer type to scale, crop, and facility while reaching safe-storage moisture without heat damage.

## Deep-Bed vs Thin-Layer 深床与薄层

> "Due to the fact that the layer of grain that is dried is thin (less than about 0.20 m), no significant moisture gradient develops through the grain [8]. This means that the drying temperature is limited only by the possibility of heat damage to the grain." (p.569)

Deep beds (silo up to 3.5 m, p.569) develop gradients — over-dry near the plenum, under-dry far side; continuous-flow dryers narrow the grain column (0.30 m vs 0.45 m) to lower the moisture differential (p.566–567). 解读：**绒毛浆包/卷就是一个深床**，不是薄层 — 应预期内湿外干的梯度，用干燥驻留+后调理去均衡，而不是单次强制到规格。

## Tempering / Dryeration 调质与多段干燥（最有价值的迁移点）

> "Dryeration is the term referring to the two-stage process by which grain is dried in a heated-air dryer to within about 2% of its 'dry' moisture content and then moved to and stored in an aerating bin for about 10 h [3]. This allows time for moisture within the kernels to move to the outside for easier removal." (p.563)

- Dryeration: capacity up to +60%, fuel −20% or more (p.563–564).
- Combination drying: "high-temperature drying in combination with aeration or natural-air drying" (p.563); high-temp to 19–23% then natural-air bin, energy −50% (p.563–564).

解读：最后几个水分点最难除 — 调质静置让内部水分重新分布，再用低能耗空气收尾。绒毛浆包出干燥器后干到近目标→静置均衡→低能耗收尾，形状与收益同谷物（省能、终水分均匀）。能耗总账框架见 [[Industrial Drying Ch46 Energy Aspects]]。

## Guardrail Logic 双护栏规格逻辑（Table 23.1, p.564）

| Guardrail | Grain value |
|---|---|
| Safe-storage moisture | 11–13% wb (most grains); long storage −1–2% |
| Max safe heated-air temp (by end-use) | 43 °C seed / 54 °C commercial / 82 °C feed |

解读：每个干燥规格都需要的两条护栏 — 目标残余水分 + 按最终用途定最高安全温度。卫材代换：绒毛浆/SAP 残余水分规格 + 粘合剂/纤维热损伤窗口。仓储通风按环境条件选窗：> "the operator should select days when the average relative humidity is less than or equal to 70% [4] and the air temperature is more than 1.1°C (30°F) above ambient." (p.562)

## Method & Limits 方法与局限

设备/操作综述章（品牌设备向、北美与发展中国家框架），无新干燥速率模型 — 动力学骨架（thin-layer/deep-bed 形式体系）移交后续章节。诚实边界：谷物风量数值不得移植到卫材产线；调质对非谷物未量化，bale 均衡类比是定性的；地域改变数值 (p.568)。OCR 粘词严重（"Dry eration"），引文/公式需对照印刷版 `[待验证]`。

## Hygiene Implication 卫材关联

- **打包/卷取调理**：绒毛浆包出干燥器后按 dryeration 逻辑调质静置，让内部水分外迁再低能耗收尾（p.563）— 产能/能耗收益与谷物同构（+60% / −20%）。
- **深床心态**：把 bale/roll 当深床管理，设计驻留+后处理均衡梯度，勿单次到规格 (p.569)。
- **双护栏规格**：为绒毛浆/SAP/粘合剂定"目标残余水分 + 按用途分级最高热暴露温度"两条护栏；长期仓储再收紧 1–2% (Table 23.1, p.564)。
- **仓储通风**：均衡作业选低 RH 窗口避免回湿（RH ≤ 70%、气温高于环境 1.1 °C，p.562）。
