# FZ-T 64112-2024 Nonwovens for Baby Diapers（婴幼儿纸尿裤用非织造布）

## 一句话定位

The **textile-industry product standard** specifically for baby-diaper nonwovens（面层/底层/防漏隔边/导流层/包裹层/腰贴六位分工）— the first CN standard to organize diaper nonwoven acceptance by in-diaper position rather than by process.

## Summary 摘要

FZ/T 64112-2024 (SAC/TC 209/SC 7) was drafted by essentially the whole CN diaper-nonwoven supply base (延江、南海南新、大源、优全、恒安卫生材料、俊富 etc.). Its organizing principle is **position**: one standard covers topsheet, backsheet, leak cuffs, ADL, core wrap, and waist/side-tape nonwovens, with composite/laminated products excluded. Clause 2 anchors every property to the GB/T 24218 nonwoven test-method series (basis weight, strip tensile, liquid strike-through with simulated urine, rewet per 24218.14, multi-penetration per 24218.13) plus textile-system chemical methods (formaldehyde, pH GB/T 7573, colourfastness, FWA per FZ/T 01137-2016, aromatic amines, Pb/Cd, phthalates) and the dated GB 18401-2010 / GB 15979-2002 citations. **Important limitation: the available RAW_FULL transcription stops at chapter 3 — the requirement tables and inspection rules of chapters 4+ are absent from the source file, so no numeric limits can be recorded here; all limits are [待验证] pending a complete source.**
