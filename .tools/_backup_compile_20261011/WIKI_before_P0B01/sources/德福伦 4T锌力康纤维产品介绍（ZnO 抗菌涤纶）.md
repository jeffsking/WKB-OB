# 德福伦 4T锌力康纤维产品介绍（ZnO 抗菌涤纶）

## Positioning 一句话定位

德福伦（Different®）**4T锌力康**氧化锌抗菌涤纶短纤的产品册（含 SGS/广微测/天纺标检测报告与专利证书）：**熔体添加型（in-melt）抗菌纤维**的完整证据链样本；在本库中支撑 [[ZnO Antibacterial Fibre（氧化锌抗菌纤维）]] 概念与卫品抗菌/除臭材料选型。

## Summary 摘要

Product brochure. Definition: polyester fibre with antibacterial zinc oxide added to the spinning melt, special 4T square cross-section; six-in-one function claim (antibacterial, bacteriostatic, light, lofty, odour-removing, UV-protective). Mechanism (dual path): ① contact reaction — Zn²⁺ adsorbs onto negatively charged cell membrane (Coulomb attraction), penetrates the wall, reacts with sulfhydryl (-SH) groups, denatures proteins and inactivates enzymes; ions re-release after cell death → durable effect; also disrupts electron transport/respiration/transport systems; ② photocatalysis — ZnO as N-type semiconductor generates ·OH radicals and ·O2⁻ from air/moisture under light (and even without light after nano-surface treatment), decomposing odorous small molecules (ammonia, acetic acid, isovaleric acid, formaldehyde) into water/CO₂ while retaining UV-shielding and skin-care effects. Product forms & specs: 纺纱型 1.56–6.67 dtex / 32–102 mm; 填充型 6–76 mm; 非织造型 32–65 mm; special specs customisable. Evidence: SGS Zn-content reports (masterbatch & white fibre, US EPA 3052 + ICP-OES, 2020-06-03); 广东省微生物分析检测中心（广微测）AAA-class antibacterial report 2019FM03274R0 (after 50 washes: E. coli / S. aureus / C. albicans pass AAA; zone-of-inhibition ≤5 mm → non-leaching safety); 天纺标 FZ/T 73023-2006 AAA report CKTC-VT20042454 (94.2% / 88.0% / 89.8% inhibition after 50 washes); 广微测 antiviral report 2020FM10146R01 (H1N1 antiviral activity rate 99.7%). IP & certs: invention patent ZL201010621316.4（无机纳米氧化锌多功能复合抗菌聚酯纤维的制造方法，2016-01-06 授权）; ISO 9001:2015 / ISO 14001:2015; OEKO-TEX STANDARD 100（附录 6 产品级别 I 婴幼儿级，REACH 附录 XVII、CPSIA、GB 18401 符合，证书 18.HCN.59065 有效期至 2020-12-31——**已过期，需复核现证**）。
