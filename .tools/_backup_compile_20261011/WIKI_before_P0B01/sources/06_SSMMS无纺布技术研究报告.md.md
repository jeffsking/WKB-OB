# 06_SSMMS无纺布技术研究报告（SSMMS Nonwoven Technology Report）

## Positioning 定位

Industry research compilation (2026-04, web-sourced: 企业官网/行业报告/展会信息) on SSMMS spunmelt composite nonwovens（五层纺粘-熔喷复合无纺布）, centered on 广鸿新材's industrialization. Supplies the structure-performance baseline and China supply landscape for hygiene top/backsheet material selection.（SSMMS 结构-性能基线与中国供应格局。）

## Summary 摘要

SSMMS = Spunbond-Spunbond-Meltblown-Meltblown-Spunbond: S layers (PP, 15–30 μm) give strength/abrasion; the double M layers (PP, 1–5 μm) give filtration/barrier/antibacterial. Vs SMS: hydrostatic head >50 cmH₂O (vs 30–40), filtration efficiency >95% (vs 85–90%), strength +20–30%, softer hand, comparable breathability. One-step process: 3 spunbond + 2 meltblown extruders → metering → forming → thermal calendering; window: extrusion 190–250 °C, meltblown 230–280 °C, spinning 3,000–5,000 m/min, calendering 130–160 °C at 50–150 N/mm; corona/electrostatic charging (30–100 kV, 0.5–2 s) adds 10–20% filtration; post-finishing includes hydrophilic（纸尿裤/卫生巾）/repellent/antibacterial/antistatic. Cost structure: PP chip 80–85% (7,500–9,000 RMB/t), masterbatch 10–15% (15,000–30,000). Prices: hygiene-grade 15–30 gsm at 12,000–16,000 RMB/t; medical 15,000–20,000; premium 20,000–28,000; hydrophilic +10–20%; SSMMS premium over SMS ≈ 1,200 RMB/t. Capacity: 广鸿新材 leading (Zhejiang, est. 2006, 60,000 t/y, 20+ lines; SMS/SMMS/SSMMS/SSS + spunlace + PET); 新鑫 (Hubei) 30 t/d high-end. Equipment: 江苏三羊 (8–13 t/y lines), 广东闪纺, 温州祁荣, Reifenhäuser (import). Applications: medical (surgical gowns 35–45 gsm EN 13795; coveralls GB 19082), hygiene (diaper topsheet/backsheet, adult incontinence, pet pads), industrial filtration/electronics/auto. Trends: functionalization, lighter weight, smartness, green, multi-composite.（SSMMS 定义、工艺、成本价格与产业格局。）
