# 诺尔集团供应商拜访会议纪要（Nuoer Supplier Visit Minutes · SAP Process & Grade Strategy）

## Positioning 定位

Integrated minutes of the 2026-07-18/19 Nuoer Group supplier visit（两份现场纪要整合：SAP 工艺现场交流 + 复合芯体/SAP 搭配/GDP 工艺探讨）， including a six-stage annotated process-flow diagram. Primary source for Nuoer's production process capability, composite-core grade matrix, and the KC high-GBP upper-layer SAP development track.（诺尔产能/工艺/牌号/新品研发四合一纪要。）

## Summary 摘要

Capacity & position: 6 lines（诺尔生物 3 + 蓝湾 3）, 56 万吨/年 total, ~80% utilization, 30+ 万吨 sales last year; China #1 SAP share since 2021Q4. Process: post-neutralization（先低温聚合 −4~−6 ℃/0–5 ℃、6 种引发剂、吹 N2、"小罐慢跑"10 罐循环连续聚合→造粒→滚筒烘干 ≤500 ℃ 至含水 ≈3%→磁选→研磨筛分 PSD 300–600 μm→色选→表面交联涂布 + 130 ℃ 二次干燥（固含 ≈96%）+ 抗黄变/抑菌剂混料→四重检验（色选/磁选/摇摆筛/精检仪）→包装）; open-loop PSD control（人工每小时抽样 + 30 目固定筛）; sealed dosing design enables solid additives（SiO₂/纳米级/吸味材料）. Process comparison: 后中和 = low extractables + high-GBP fit; 前中和（竞品）= higher retention but slow; 带式 = fast initial then poor. Grade strategy: multi-valent metal salt coating lifts GBP to a ceiling (~40) at retention cost; since 2015 promotes layered grade combos（511+610S 上下层）replacing Sumitomo/三大雅 structures; 2016 application lab（芯体机+尿片机）offers one-stop development. Grade matrix: 511 KC-exclusive（AUL 18–19、保水 31.5、27 s）; 760 economy; N8（611）CRC 39–40 under-layer-only; 951 new fast high-retention under-layer; 950/959 new（959 dual-use, benchmarked vs P&G's BASF 7059 dual-layer approach）. KC's ask: upper layer high GBP (25→40) + AUL ~20 + retention 28–29 + 60–70 s; Nuoer's counter: extreme GBP hurts speed/retention — use 导流槽 structure + speed-difference layering; agreed same-day trial（上层 511 + 下层 951+N8 五五/四六预混，带槽 13.5 g/片）and August lab samples of the high-GBP grade. Competitive read: domestic brands weak on material science; P&G deepest; our team must stay ahead.（产能工艺 × 牌号矩阵 × 上下层搭配路线 × 即时打样。）
