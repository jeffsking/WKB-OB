# US7195621B2 Disposable Garment Comprising Meltblown Nonwoven Backsheet (P&G)

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

P&G 的**已授权美国专利（US 7,195,621 B2，仅 1 项权利要求）**：用**熔喷无纺布（meltblown nonwoven）替代 PE 膜**作为一次性衣物的阻隔/透气组件，并给出一套**可执行的四参数窗口**（纤维直径、克重、透气度、透湿度）+ 三个通过 10 psi 不漏的实施例。是本库 [[Film-Free Backsheet（无膜背层）]] 路线最早、且参数最硬的一份一手出处。

## Abstract 摘要

Title: "DISPOSABLE GARMENT COMPRISING MELTBLOWN NONWOVEN BACKSHEET". The invention addresses the discomfort of polyethylene film backsheets: a plastic film has insufficient moisture breathability and almost no air permeability, causing skin irritation. Microporous polyfilm is the next attempt, but may deliver "sometimes less than 50% of the desired level" of breathability and still almost no air permeability. The inventor's diagnosis of the competing nonwoven prior art (unexamined Japanese publication kokai H03-240151, filed 16 Feb 1990, Asahi Kasei Kogyo) is that its **0.5–2.5 µm** average-diameter fibres retain liquid but **the pores are too small**, so the sheet lacks the desired air permeability and its tensile strength drops. The invention therefore moves *up* in diameter: a melt-blown web of fine fibres with an average fibre diameter of **about 3 µm to about 10 µm**, basis weight **about 3 g/m² to about 60 g/m²**, and an air permeability value of **at least about 15 cm³/cm²/sec**. A second disclosed aspect specifies **web modulus between 230 gf/mm and 650 gf/mm**. Three worked examples at 15 / 15 / 50 g/m² report no leakage under both 1 psi and 10 psi. Measurement follows Japanese Industrial Standards throughout (see table below). The granted single claim is directed to a disposable garment with topsheet, backsheet, core **and an elasticized leg cuff, wherein the elasticized leg cuff comprises the melt-blown web** meeting those four numbers plus moisture breathability **at least 7,000 g/m²/24 hours**. The file also carries a **Certificate of Correction** for Patent No. 7,195,621 B2.

## Key claims 关键论断

- **去膜背层（film-free backsheet）不是新想法，2000 年就有量化专利**——本案把"熔喷替代 PE 膜"落成了四个可直接验收的数字，而非概念性主张。→ [[Film-Free Backsheet（无膜背层）]]
- **本案的经验是"纤维不是越细越好"**——0.5–2.5 µm 的先行技术因孔径过小而失去透气并拉低强度；本案反向取 **3–10 µm**，与本库 [[Formation-Limited Barrier（阻隔的成形受限机理）]] 关于"成形/均匀性而非平均直径决定阻隔"的论述方向一致。→ [[Meltblown Nonwoven（熔喷非织造布）]]
- **同时限定模量（230–650 gf/mm）是工艺性要求**：既要够软能悬垂，又要够强能在加工线上跑起来——这解释了为什么单纯追求超细纤维没有产线价值。
- **权利要求与说明书的差异极大，读范围时必须注意**：摘要/SUMMARY 讲的是 **backsheet**，而**唯一授权权利要求把熔喷网放在弹性腿围（elasticized leg cuff）**上。→ 见下方 manual_review。
