## Background 背景

Diaper waist / leg / cuff elastics use spandex. Both candidate brands **share the same dry-spinning process route** (dry spinning accounts for >95% of global spandex capacity), so the comparison is not about process family but about **hard-segment chemistry, surface finish and compliance documentation**.（纸尿裤腰围/腿围/侧边弹性体用氨纶；两家候选品牌**同走干法纺丝路线**（干法占全球氨纶产能 95% 以上），因此差异不在工艺族，而在**硬段配方、表面处理与合规文件**。）
