# China Diaper Tech Roadmap 2026（China_Diaper_Tech_Roadmap_2026）

## 一句话定位

English-version strategy deck (parallel to the Chinese deck above) for global R&D leadership — same conclusions, with explicit observations on China's innovation model and strategic recommendations.

## 摘要

The English deck restates the Chinese deck's findings and adds three observations on **China's innovation model**: (1) **consumer-driven innovation** — sensitivity to skincare/seamless/eco and willingness to pay drives fast tech landing (concept-to-shelf far shorter than in the West; YiYing probiotic launched in months); (2) **ODM deep participation** — HaoYue, Reliable are not just manufacturers but patent-holding R&D partners (HaoYue 30+ invention patents covering fluff-free core & ultrasonic welding); (3) **speed & iteration advantage** — a "scroll-style" competition that accelerates the whole industry (seamless-waist tech trickled from premium to mass in one year). Strategic recommendations: establish a China tech-scouting unit, build agile co-development, run a dual-track tech roadmap, and **re-evaluate supply-chain positioning** — treat leading Chinese ODMs and material suppliers as strategic technology partners, not cost centers.
