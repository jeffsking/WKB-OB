# Natural Superabsorbent Plastic Materials Based on Functionalized Soy Protein

## 一句话定位

一篇关于**大豆蛋白基天然高吸水材料（SAP）**的研究论文，论证用琥珀酸酐（SA）酰化大豆分离蛋白（SPI）+ 甘油增塑制备可吸水塑料，作为石油基 SAP 的可降解替代路线。

## 摘要

The paper develops natural superabsorbent plastics from acylated soy protein. Soy protein isolate (SPI, trade name SUPRO 500E IP, from PROANDA, Spain) is functionalized with succinic anhydride (SA) to raise hydrophilicity, then blended at 50 wt% protein (unmodified or acylated SPI) with 50 wt% glycerol (GL) plasticizer and processed via a two-stage thermomechanical procedure. Materials are characterized by FTIR, TGA, DMTA, tensile tests, water imbibing capacity (WIC, Baumann apparatus) and water uptake per UNE-EN ISO 62:2008. The work targets the drawbacks of conventional acrylic-acid SAP — poor biodegradability and high cost — by using an abundant, low-cost co-product of the soybean-oil industry.
