# EP2413866B1 Disposable Wearing Article (Unicharm)

## 一句话定位

Granted European patent (Unicharm) on a **disposable wearing article** with an elastic/fit feature — a pant-type article in the "elastic component" cluster of this IP block.

## 摘要

The patent (title: "DISPOSABLE WEARING ARTICLE") grants a disposable wearing article. The bibliographic header lists proprietor Unicharm Corporation (Ehime, JP), multiple inventors (Yamashita, Otsubo, Hashimoto, Ishikawa, Okuda), and classification A61F 13/15, A61F 13/49. It is the European phase of PCT/JP2010/002334 (WO 2010/113488), priority JP 2009080821 and JP 2010068328. The filename tags it as an elastic-component (弹性部件) patent. Detailed claim language on the elastic/fit feature is in the full text; this summary captures the bibliographic and classification scope.
