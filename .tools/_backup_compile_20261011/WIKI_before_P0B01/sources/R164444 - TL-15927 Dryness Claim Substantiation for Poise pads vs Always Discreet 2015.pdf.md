# R164444 - TL-15927 Dryness Claim Substantiation for Poise pads vs Always Discreet 2015.pdf

> ⚠️ K-C CONFIDENTIAL — internal technical communication (TL-15927 / Report R164444). Summary here keeps to claim-level and method-level facts; proprietary test parameters are not reproduced.（金佰利机密内部技术文件，本页仅保留宣称与方法层面事实。）

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

K-C's 2015 claim-substantiation record for "Poise Thin Shape pads stay 10× drier than Always Discreet" — the canonical example of using a Bladder-Box **flowback (=rewet)** bench test to defend a dryness claim against a competitor.（金佰利 2015 年「Poise Thin Shape 比 Always Discreet 干爽 10 倍」宣称佐证记录——用 Bladder Box 回流量（=回渗）台架测试支撑竞品干爽宣称的范本。）

## Summary 摘要

- **Context 背景**: P&G launched Always Discreet (Aug 2014) claiming it was drier than Poise. Poise (K-C) responded by building its own dryness claim rather than challenging P&G's.（背景：宝洁 2014 年 8 月推出 Always Discreet 并宣称比 Poise 更干；Poise 选择自建宣称而非反驳。）
- **Product 产品**: Poise Thin Shape launched Mar 2015, targeted at the same LBL (Light Bladder Leakage) users as Always Discreet.（Poise Thin Shape 于 2015 年 3 月上市，对标 Always Discreet 的轻度漏尿用户。）
- **Method 方法**: Bladder Box method for intake **and flowback** (draft **STM-00497**); flowback is the rewet measurement used for dryness claims. Claim refers to *product* dryness, not skin dryness or user feel.（方法：Bladder Box 摄入与回流量法（草案 STM-00497）；回流量即用于干爽宣称的回渗值。宣称指产品干爽，非皮肤干爽或体感。）
- **Legal 法务**: K-C and P&G legal teams discussed test methodology/parameters in 2015; P&G did not challenge the 10× claim.（2015 年双方法务就测试方法沟通，宝洁未挑战 10 倍宣称。）

## Extracted numbers & conclusions 提炼的数字与结论

| Absorbency 吸收等级 | Product 产品 | Mean flowback (g) 平均回流量 | "X times drier 干爽倍数" |
|---|---|---|---|
| Moderate | Poise Thin Shape Regular Length | 0.06 | 46.2× |
| Moderate | Always Discreet Regular Length | 2.84 | — |
| Maximum | Poise Thin Shape Regular Length | 0.12 | 59.7× |
| Maximum | Always Discreet Regular Length | 7.17 | — |
| Ultimate | Poise Thin Shape Regular Length | 0.13 | 54.4× |
| Ultimate | Always Discreet Regular Length | 7.24 | — |

- Each code tested at 2 locations; no distribution overlap between Poise Thin Shape and Always Discreet (one-sided exact Wilcoxon, p < 0.0001 at all three absorbencies).（每牌号在两个地点测试；Poise 与 Always Discreet 分布无重叠，p<0.0001。）
- Testing demonstrated up to ~50× drier, but the business selected the conservative **10×** claim.（实测最高约 50 倍，商业上取保守的 10 倍。）

## Key claims 关键论断

- A consumer-relevant Bladder-Box flowback protocol can substantiate a quantitative dryness claim and withstand competitor legal review.（贴合消费的 Bladder Box 回流量方案可支撑量化干爽宣称并抵御竞品法务审查。）—— 支撑页：[[Rewet Test（回渗测试）]]、[[The Procter & Gamble Company（宝洁）]]
- Poise Thin Shape rewet (flowback) is ~40–60× lower than Always Discreet in this 2015 panel.（该 2015 面板中 Poise Thin Shape 回渗约为 Always Discreet 的 1/40–1/60。）
