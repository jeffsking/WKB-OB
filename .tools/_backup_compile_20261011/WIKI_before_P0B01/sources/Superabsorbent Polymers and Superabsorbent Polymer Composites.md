# Superabsorbent Polymers and Superabsorbent Polymer Composites

## One-line Position 一句话定位
A 2007 overview of SAP crosslinking polymerization, composites with layered silicates, and their emerging biomedical / nanotechnology applications.（概述 SAP 交联聚合、与层状硅酸盐的复合材料及其前沿应用。）

## Summary 摘要
- SAPs (hydrogels) are loosely crosslinked 3D polyelectrolyte networks carrying dissociated ionic groups; they absorb fluids >15× dry weight and retain them under load via hydrogen-bond solvation and osmotic pressure.（SAP 为含离子基团的弱交联三维网络，吸水倍率 >15× 干重，在负载下持液。）
- Historical anchor: USDA Northern Regional Laboratory created the first SAP (starch–polyacrylonitrile graft copolymer "super slurper") in 1961; polyacrylate family later became dominant in personal care.
- Synthesis techniques: bulk, solution/crosslinking, suspension / inverse suspension, and radiation-initiated crosslinking (pure, initiator-free SAPs).
- Crosslinking: bulk/core vs surface; surface crosslinking raises gel strength under pressure and prevents gel-blocking / "fish eyes". Particle size and PSD directly affect free-swell rate and inter-particle voids.
- SAPCs: organic–inorganic nanocomposites using phyllosilicates (kaolinite, montmorillonite, etc., layer thickness ~1 nm); enable silver/zinc nanoparticle fabrication, catalysis, optics, electronics, biomedical and water-treatment uses.
