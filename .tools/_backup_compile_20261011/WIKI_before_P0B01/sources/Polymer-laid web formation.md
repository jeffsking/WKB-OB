# Polymer-laid web formation

## One-line Position 一句话定位

Chapter 4 is the technical core of the web-formation triptych: it treats spunbond and meltblown as integrated melt-extrusion processes where resin molecular structure sets the processing window, which sets fibre architecture and end-use performance in hygiene/medical disposables.（中文：第 4 章是"成网三章"的技术核心，将纺粘/熔喷视为一体化熔融挤出工艺，树脂分子结构决定工艺窗口，进而决定卫材/医用终端性能。）

## Summary 摘要

- §4.2 opens with polymer science: polyolefin architecture (isotactic/syndiotactic/atactic PP; HDPE/LDPE/LLDPE; PET; PA; TPU), resin properties governing spinnability, and the metallocene single-site catalyst revolution.（先讲聚合物科学：聚烯烃立构、可纺性树脂属性、茂金属单活性中心催化剂革命。）
- Spunbond process engineering (§4.3–4.7) and meltblown engineering with extensive parametric/modelling literature (§4.9–4.13) follow.（随后是纺粘工艺工程与含大量参数/建模文献的熔喷工程。）
- Composites and future trends (§4.14–4.15) close the chapter, tying polymer-laid technology back to the hygiene market.（复合与趋势收尾，呼应第 1 章卫生用品市场。）
- The argument is cumulative: resin structure → processing window → fibre diameter/web architecture → end-use performance.（论证链条：树脂结构→工艺窗口→纤维形貌→终端性能。）

## Key Claims 关键论断

- Isotactic PP and the MFR band are the resin-selection hard constraints behind hygiene nonwovens.（等规 PP 与 MFR 区间是卫材无纺布的树脂选型硬约束。）
- The ~25–45 °C PP/PE melting gap is the physical basis for sheath-core and through-air thermal bonding.（PP/PE 熔点差是通过-空气/皮芯热粘的物理基础。）
- Broad MWD raises melt elasticity → draw resonance → fibre breaks (a defect-rate driver).（宽分子量分布引发拉伸共振断丝。）
- Metallocene catalysts widen the design space: finer denier, lower bond temp, broader MFR.（茂金属拓宽设计空间：更细旦、更低粘合温度、更宽 MFR。）
