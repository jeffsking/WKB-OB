# R168596_TL-16794 Adult Care Wetting Pattern Fundamentals 2015-2016: New Methodologies and Target Zones

## Positioning 定位

K-C confidential technical report (Nettekoven & Berceau, 2018) documenting eight new single-insult wetting pattern (单次排尿润湿图谱) studies for DEPEND® and POISE® in 2015–2016. It is the库内基准 for adult-care Target Zone design criteria, X-ray-based wetting pattern measurement methodology, and the evidence that outdated design inputs create cost and brand-equity risk.（成人失禁产品目标区设计与润湿图谱测量方法学的权威内部依据。）

## Summary 摘要

After 13–15 years without funded research, nearly all DEPEND/POISE wetting pattern inputs were outdated or assumed — e.g., only 2 of 9 POISE pad codes had ever been tested (Extra & Ultra Plus, 2000). The AFC Materials Team placed eight new studies (Apr 2015–May 2016, projects SAFARI and SHOPAD) using consumer study agencies instead of local clinical recruiting. Methodology development covered protocol questions (can consumers detect a single insult — subjects reported insult number for 71–88% of products across 11 HUTs), shipment stability (X-ray before/after showed no noticeable pattern change over 4-day shipping), mounting-board issues (Plexiglas boards interfered with X-ray densitometry due to non-uniform basis weight; POISE pads adhered via garment adhesive, DEPEND garments mounted with Velcro + adhesive patch), and analysis tooling (a modified imaging-analysis program measuring length/width/area/centroid, reliable to 5–6 g for POISE). Results: POISE wetting pattern lengths grew 8–48 mm since 2000; several R&E assumptions were disproven (not heavily front-loaded; patterns vary over time and across codes); surge coverage gaps and over-designed pads were identified. DEPEND results moved female surge phasing forward from 60 mm to 30 mm from the front absorbent edge, informed PASSAT/FIORANO designs, and supported a target single-insult reduction from 80 g to 50 g for Moderate products. Over $3.6MM of cost savings was proposed; a 5-year fundamentals update cadence was recommended.（八项新研究刷新了成人失禁线的目标区数据、方法学与多项设计假设。）
