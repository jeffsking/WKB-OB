# Scope Contract — 2025 Fundamental Rewet Improvement（2025 回渗根基性改进项目章程）

## Positioning 定位

K-C internal scope contract（requestor Kerry Clemmer，2025-06-13）establishing the ground-up rewet fundamentals program across NA adult care (Depend/Poise) and baby (Huggies). The program's competitive frame, benchmark numbers, method assignments and timing make it the charter document for the库's rewet workstream.（回渗改进项目章程：目标、基准、方法、时间表。）

## Summary 摘要

Rationale: rewet is an active claims space where competitors can show superiority; P&G's recent gains make incremental fixes insufficient — a fundamental, ground-up approach is needed to approach zero rewet. Evidence: Depend Fresh Protect metanalysis（post-ATACAMA, Female Large）shows Assurance 0.36 g vs Depend Fresh Protect 0.19 g vs Always Discreet 0.19 g (A/B statistical grouping); privileged data shows Always Discreet recently improved. Huggies: BUFF BABY T4 glidepath（HUT 40744, codes S & G on U03 line; ActivTech hybrid core + Nourer SAM + 80gsm MOJAVE 2.1 surge + 16gsm Platinum liner; covers BCW vs SB）benchmarked against Pampers Swaddlers S4. Program table: (1) Competitive analysis — how P&G achieves low rewet across Depend Fresh Protect/Always Discreet, Poise Original/Always Discreet, Huggies Little Movers/Swaddlers（Aug 2025, Priority A）; (2) Determine approach — diagnose and recommend technology path to best-in-class, stretch = beat P&G（Nov 2025, A）; (3) Test method — propose consumer-relevant, claims-robust rewet measurement（Mar 2026, B）. Scope: Depend Fresh Protect（Female Large, GRIFFIN; TTM-00672 closed cradle）and Poise Originals 6-drop long（ARTEMIS; TTM-00643 FIUP）1st priority; Huggies Little Movers Step 4（BUFF BABY T5; TTM-00623 low/high）2nd; Huggies targets 0.15 g low / 0.30 g high pressure or better. Roles: Central GR&D（learning plan, analytical testing, knowledge gaps）+ NA ICT（products, next steps, D&C/Discovery interfaces）.（立项背景 × 竞品基准 × 三步走 × 方法分工。）
