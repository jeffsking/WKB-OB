# CN 113423778 B（吸水性组合物及其制造方法·花王 / Water-absorbing composition with CNF & cellulose derivative — Kao）

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

A granted Chinese invention patent (assignee: **花王株式会社 / Kao Corporation**) on a **water-absorbing composition of cellulose nanofiber (CNF) + a high-viscosity, low-etherification cellulose derivative (e.g. CMC-Na)**. A nanocellulose–SAP route from a global hygiene major — FTO-relevant.

## 摘要 / Abstract

The invention is a water-absorbing composition containing cellulose nanofiber and a cellulose derivative, and its production method. The cellulose derivative has a 1 wt% aqueous-solution viscosity at 25 °C of ≥1000 mPa·s and an etherification degree < 0.9; it is preferably selected from carboxymethyl cellulose, carboxyethyl cellulose, and their salts, more preferably sodium carboxymethyl cellulose (CMC-Na).
