## One-line positioning 一句话定位

SCA Hygiene Products AB's foundational patent family on **embedding cellulosic nanofibrils (NFC) inside the SAP polymer network** — the source page for the "nanocellulose × SAP" route, and the earliest document in the library that ties nanofibrillated cellulose to diaper-core SAP performance.

## Abstract 摘要

The family discloses a superabsorbent polymer composite in which **cellulosic nanofibrils of diameter ≤100 nm** are incorporated **within the three-dimensional network of polymer chains of a superabsorbent polymer** (i.e. not merely blended as a fibrous matrix). The stated problem is twofold: SAP particles are hard to fix in place inside an absorbent structure, and **gel blocking**（凝胶堵塞）occurs when swollen SAP gel blocks liquid transport. A further problem is that raising crosslink density to gain mechanical strength restricts swelling and makes highly-crosslinked SAP brittle. The claimed composite therefore targets **improved gel strength at retained absorbency**. Claim 1 is the broad composition claim; claims 2–14 add the "no fibres >100 µm", SAP chemistry (CMC or acrylic/methacrylic acid repeating units), cross-linker and nanofibril dose windows; claims 15–19 cover particles and foams (nanofibrils in the pore walls; pore-size gradient; plasticizers/surfactants/blowing agents); claims 20–21 cover absorbent articles (diaper, pant diaper, incontinence guard, sanitary napkin); claims 22+ cover the manufacturing method (nanofibrils suspended in solvent → optional microfibers → monomers → neutralizer → crosslinker → initiator → polymerise; step order is free). A fifth aspect claims the **use of cellulosic nanofibrils for increasing the gel strength of a SAP**.

## Key claims 关键论断

- **Nanofibrils must be inside the SAP network, not beside it** — claim 1's "incorporated within a three-dimensional network" is the structural discriminator versus conventional fibre-blended SAP composites. → [[SAP-Cellulosic Nanofibril Composite（SAP-纤维素纳米纤丝复合体）]]
- **Gel strength is the target property** — the fifth aspect explicitly claims using NFC to increase SAP gel strength. → [[SAP-Cellulosic Nanofibril Composite（SAP-纤维素纳米纤丝复合体）]]
- **Gel blocking is the named failure mode being solved.** → [[SAP（高吸水树脂）]]
- **Low nanofibril loading (0.5–5 wt%) is the most-preferred window** — i.e. the benefit is claimed at very low dosage. → [[纳米纤维素（CNC、CNF、BNC）]]
- **The fibre diameter ceiling (>100 µm exclusion) keeps the composite in the nano/micro regime.** → [[纳米纤维素（CNC、CNF、BNC）]]
