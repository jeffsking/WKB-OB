# Polypropylene Fibres 聚丙烯纤维

> Source: Manufactured Fibre Technology, Ch.16, pp.457–478.
> 综述型章节，文末 REFERENCES 1–25，主要引用 Ahmed (1982)、Wishman & Hagler (1985) 等专著与期刊。

## Definition 定义

- **Stereoregular polymer 立构规整聚合物**：[p.457] "the polymer was stereoregular... The stereospecific polymer, with all the methyl groups on one side, was called isotactic polypropylene." 聚丙烯是首个具有工业重要性的立构规整聚合物，1970 年代进入纺织领域，产量居聚酯、尼龙、腈纶之后第四位，但多用于工业而非服装。
- **MFI 熔融指数**：[p.468] "the weight in grams of melt flowing out in 10 min... under a load of 2.16 kg at 230°C." 用于划分纤维级与工业级树脂。
- **Controlled rheology (CR) resins 控制流变树脂**：[p.468] "polymers with narrow molecular weight distribution has increased significantly to give what are known as the controlled rheology (CR) resins." 窄分子量分布、可高速低温纺丝的树脂牌号。

## Polymerisation and Catalyst 聚合与催化剂

- 丙烯来源与规格：Table 16.1（不同原料产率）、Table 16.2（聚合级丙烯，纯度 ≥99.5%）。
- **Ziegler-Natta catalyst 齐格勒-纳塔催化剂**：[p.458] "The key to their success lay in the discovery of a coordination catalyst system, now known as the Ziegler-Natta catalyst." 催化剂与工艺进步 [p.458] "have increased the polymer yield ten-fold, narrowed the molecular weight distribution, and reduced the non-crystallizable resin"；以每 kg Ti 计可产出 8000–250000 kg 聚合物 [p.461]。

## Stabilisation against Degradation 抗降解稳定化

**根因**：[p.465] "More than any other textile-grade polymer, polypropylene owes its existence to the host of stabilizers that are compounded to restrict degradation. This susceptibility is mainly because of the presence of a tertiary carbon in the chain." 即链中叔碳原子（tertiary carbon）是热氧与光氧降解的薄弱点。

**机理**：以自由基链式反应（Initiation / Propagation / Termination）描述热氧降解 [p.465–466] 与光氧降解。

**稳定剂六分类** [p.467]：自由基清除剂（radical scavengers）、过氧化物分解剂（peroxide decomposers）、UV 吸收剂、能量猝灭剂（energy quenchers）、金属钝化剂（metal deactivators）、HCl 清除剂；另补 **HALS（受阻胺光稳定剂）**：[p.467] "compound commonly known as HALS (hindered amine light stabilizers)... contain a 2,2,6,6-tetramethyl piperidyl group."

## Dyeability Limitation 染色限制

[p.458] "Another major problem these fibres faced... is the lack of dyeability; they do not have dye sites as other commercially useful fibres have. Mass-coloration using suitable pigments has led to the introduction of a wide range of coloured fibres." 缺少染座 → 只能纺前母粒着色（mass-coloration）。这与 REL 侧的功能母粒（functional masterbatch）做法是同一思路。

## Fibre Production — Six Melt-Spinning Routes 六种熔纺工艺

按 MFI 划分纤维级与工业级树脂；六种工艺为：短程空气淬冷（short air-quench）、长程空气淬冷、纺-拉一步法（spin-draw）、纺-拉-变形 BCF（spin-draw-texturing）、水淬（water quench）、裂膜扁丝（split-film / slit tape）。工艺参数量化于 Table 16.4–16.6。

## Structure Development 凝固过程的结构演化

- **Spinline stress 纺丝线应力**：[p.474] "The spinline stress may increase from 10⁵ dyn cm⁻² at a take-up value of 50 m min⁻¹ to greater than 10⁷ dyn cm⁻² for a take-up value of 600 m min⁻¹; the corresponding increase in birefringence is five-fold." 提高卷绕速度显著提升取向与双折射 → 强度上升、伸长下降，是调控产品性能的核心变量。
- **Skin-core effect 皮芯效应**：[p.473] "The spun fibres exhibit a skin-core effect with the skin having higher orientation than the core."
- **Smectic phase 近晶相（亚稳有序态）**：[p.474] "a phase possessing order intermediate between the amorphous (atactic) and crystalline states... is referred to as the 'smectic' phase." 因部分近晶结构存在，[p.475] "the draw texturing of POY polypropylene at relatively high speeds has been made possible despite high crystallinity of feeder yarn because of the presence of part smectic structure."
- 晶型：3₁ 螺旋构象，α / β / γ 晶型与 smectic 相的转变（Fig. 16.4–16.8）。

## Fibre Properties 纤维性能

Table 16.7 [p.476–477]：密度 0.91、熔点 165°C、吸湿 0–0.05%（moisture absorption）。轻质、几乎不吸湿是 PP 在卫生材料中作为面层/芯体纤维相的根本优势。

## Hygiene Implication 卫生材料启示（REL 相关性）

**本章是全书与 REL 工作相关度最高的章节之一**，PP 是卫生非织造面层、纺粘/熔喷层与 ES 双组分纤维（PE-PP）的直接原料：

- **纤维成形与纺粘/熔喷的直接联系**：本章的 MFI—分子量分布—可纺性、纺丝线应力—双折射—取向、皮芯效应三条因果链，是理解纺粘（spunbond）与熔喷（meltblown）工艺窗口的基础；本章明确指出其为第 19 章非织造（spun-bonding、melt-blowing）埋下伏笔。见 [[Meltblown Nonwoven（熔喷非织造布）]] 与 [[Nonwoven Process Classification（非织造布工艺分类）]]。
- **双组分纤维（ES / PE-PP）**：皮芯取向差异与热定形行为，是 sheath-core 双组分纤维热风粘合（through-air bonding）时皮层熔融、芯层保持纤维形貌的结构基础，见 [[Bicomponent Fibre Production & Performance Review — Spinneret, Crimp & Functional Masterbatch（复合纤维生产及性能探讨）]]。
- **稳定剂体系**：PP  tertiary carbon 导致的热氧降解，直接决定高速纺粘纺丝时的热稳定性、黄变与气味，以及多次挤出回收料的性能衰减；HALS 与酚类抗氧剂的复配是产线实际配方问题。
- **结晶与软化点**：熔点 165°C、密度 0.91（Table 16.7）解释了为何 PP 皮层可低温热粘合而 PET 芯层保持尺寸稳定，也是 ultra-thin core 热压复合的工艺温度窗口依据。
- **疏水/不吸湿**：吸湿 0–0.05% 意味着 PP 面层必须靠亲水整理（surfactant finish）获得润湿性，与面层多次透水（run-off / rewet）表现直接相关。
- **母粒着色路线**：与 PP 聚烯烃体系相容的功能母粒（亲水、抗菌、弹性）是 [[Polyolefin Hot Melt Adhesive（聚烯烃热熔胶）]] 与功能非织造的共同技术底座。
- 纤维截面形状（如三叶形）对蓬松与遮盖的影响见 [[三叶形截面纤维（Trilobal Fibre）]]。
