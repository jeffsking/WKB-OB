# Ebewole Polymer Science Ch01 Introduction Basic Concepts and Classification

> Source: Ebewole, *Polymer Science and Technology*, Ch.1 (Introduction). 章节主题：历史发展、基本概念与定义、聚合物分类。

Chapter 1 is the **entry hub 入门枢纽** of the book and carries three tasks: (1) building the disciplinary history of polymer science from celluloid (1868) to the engineering-plastics era; (2) fixing the strict definitions of *polymer / monomer / repeating unit / structural unit / degree of polymerization / molecular weight distribution / functionality*; (3) laying the taxonomic foundation used in all later chapters.

本章是全书的术语与分类学起点；后续各章的定量论证（聚合动力学、分子量、热转变、结晶、力学性能）都依赖此处确立的定义。

## Historical Development 历史发展

The source frames polymer development as three stages: **empirical activity → structural understanding → controlled synthesis 经验 → 结构理解 → 可控合成**.

> "The first generation of man-made polymers was the result of empirical activities; the main focus was on chemical composition with virtually no attention paid to structure. However, during the first half of the 20th century, extensive organic and physical developments led to the first understanding of the structural concept of polymers — long chains or a network of covalently bonded molecules." (Ch.1, p.1)

> "Staudinger first proposed the theory that polymers were composed of giant molecules, and he coined the word macromolecule to describe them." (Ch.1, p.1)

> "The Nobel-prize-winning development of stereo-specific catalysts by Professors Karl Ziegler of Germany and Giulio Natta of Italy led to the ability of polymer chemists to 'order' the molecular structure of polymers." (Ch.1, p.2)

Landmarks cited in the chapter (年代—材料—最初应用):

| Period 年代 | Material 材料 / event | Note 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| 1868 | Celluloid (Hyatt / Parkes) | first man-made polymer 首代人造高分子 |
| 1909 | Bakelite (phenol-formaldehyde) | thermoset 热固性 |
| 1920s | PVC / nylon / cellulose acetate | Carothers' condensation work 缩聚 |
| 1930s | PS / acrylic / melamine | |

| 1960–70s | High-temperature plastics (PPS, PES) | |

## Basic Concepts and Definitions 基本概念与定义

> "The word polymer is derived from classical Greek *poly* meaning 'many' and *meres* meaning 'parts.' Thus a polymer is a large molecule (macromolecule) built up by the repetition of small chemical units." (Ch.1, p.3)

> "styrene (monomer) is defined as any molecule that can be converted to a polymer by combining with other molecules of the same or different type." (Ch.1, p.3)

The chapter anchors these definitions on two worked examples — **polystyrene (addition, Eq. 1.1)** and **nylon 6,6 (condensation, Eq. 1.2)** — to expose the difference between *repeating unit* and *structural unit*.

| Concept 概念 | Source definition 原文定义 | Note 中文注解 |
|---|---|---|
| Polymer | "a large molecule (macromolecule) built up by the repetition of small chemical units" (p.3) | 由单体聚合而成；分子量是统计量 |
| Monomer | "any molecule that can be converted to a polymer by combining with other molecules of the same or different type" (p.3) | 聚合前的反应单元；按官能度分类 |
| Repeating unit | the unit "enclosed in square brackets" — the smallest repeating structure in the chain (p.3) | "The structure of the repeating unit is not exactly the same as that of the monomer even though both possess identical atoms occupying similar relative positions." (p.3) |
| Structural unit | "The residue from the monomer employed in the preparation of a polymer" (p.4) | In nylon 6,6 the repeating unit consists of **two** structural units: –HN–(CH₂)₆–HN– (from hexamethylenediamine) and –OC–(CH₂)₄–OC– (from adipic acid) (p.4) |
| Degree of polymerization (DP, *n*) | "The subscript designation, n, in Equations 1.1 and 1.2 indicates the number of repeating units strung together in the polymer chain (molecule)" (p.4) | DP 与分子量成正比；缩聚机理的关键变量 |

### Molecular weight averages and polydispersity 分子量平均值与多分散性

- **Number-average molecular weight Mn** — "derived from measurements that, in effect, count the number of molecules in the given sample" (Ch.1, p.6); source form: `Mn = Σ ni Mr / Σ ni`.
- **Weight-average molecular weight Mw** — "based on methods in which the contribution of each molecule to the observed effect depends on its size" (Ch.1, p.6); source form: `Mw = Σ ni Mr² / Σ ni Mr`.

> "The quantity Mw/Mn is an indication of just how broad the differences in the chain lengths of the constituent polymer molecules in a given sample are. That is, this ratio is a measure of polydispersity, and consequently it is often referred to as the heterogeneity index." (Ch.1, p.6)

> "In an ideal polymer such as a protein, all the polymer molecules are of the same size (Mw = Mn or Mw/Mn = 1). This is not true for synthetic polymers – the numerical value of Mw is always greater than that of Mn." (Ch.1, p.6)

统计本质：合成高分子只能用**分子量分布**描述，不能用单一分子量；Mw/Mn（heterogeneity index 多分散性指数）是分布的宽度表征，与现代统计物理中的"矩"概念对应。

## Classification of Polymers 聚合物分类

The chapter's taxonomic first principle is **functionality 官能度**, not chemical composition or application.

> "The functionality of a molecule is simply its interlinking capacity, or the number of sites it has available for bonding with other molecules under the specific polymerization conditions." (Ch.1, p.6)

> "A molecule may be classified as monofunctional, bifunctional, or polyfunctional depending on whether it has one, two, or greater than two sites available for linking with other molecules." (Ch.1, p.6)

> "Styrene is therefore bifunctional." (Ch.1, p.6)

Four classification axes are crossed:

| Axis 分类轴 | Categories 类别 |
|---|---|
| Origin 来源 | natural vs. synthetic 天然 / 合成 |
| Structure 结构 | linear / branched / cross-linked / ladder × functionality 线型 / 支化 / 交联 / 梯形 |
| Mechanism 机理 | addition vs. condensation 加聚 / 缩聚 |
| Thermal behavior 热行为 | thermoplastic vs. thermoset 热塑性 / 热固性 |

| Homopolymer 均聚物 | Copolymer 共聚物 |
|---|---|
| single monomer 单一单体 | ≥2 monomers 两种及以上单体共聚 |
| Repeating unit = structural unit | repeating unit composed of several structural units |
| PE, PS, PVC | ABS, SBR, EVA |

| Thermoplastic 热塑性 | Thermoset 热固性 |
|---|---|
| Linear / branched 线型或支化 | Cross-linked network 交联网络 |
| Melts reversibly 受热可反复熔融 | Decomposes (infusible) 受热分解、不熔融 |
| PE, PP, PS, PVC | Phenolics, urea-formaldehyde |

Functionality rule of thumb from the source: f = 1 → no polymerisation; f = 2 → linear chain; f > 2 → branching / cross-linked network. 官能度 ≥2 才能聚合；>2 导致支化或交联网络。

## Academic Context and Critique 学术对话与批判

- **Staudinger's macromolecular hypothesis (1920s)** overturned the colloid-chemistry view of polymers as aggregates of small molecules; the source credits Staudinger with coining *macromolecule*.
- **Terminology tension polymer vs. macromolecule**: the source (written 1997) uses both interchangeably; IUPAC later favoured *polymer* because *macromolecule* blurs with low-molar-mass oligomers. 属成书年代的过渡期写法。
- **Polydispersity as defect or resource**: the book states polydispersity as the statistical essence of synthetic polymers; controlled/living polymerisation (RAFT / ATRP) that pushes Mw/Mn toward 1.0 was not mature at the time of writing.
- **Semantic shift of functionality**: in classical organic chemistry it denotes functional-group type; here it is redefined as *interlinking capacity* (a Carothers, polymer-specific definition). 与有机化学存在语义摩擦。

## Hygiene Implication 卫材关联

Relevant in three respects（与卫材研发直接相关）：

1. **Molar mass / MWD → melt rheology for spunbond & meltblown.** 本书确立的 Mn / Mw / Mw/Mn 是聚烯烃树脂规格的核心描述符；纺粘与熔喷树脂的分子量及其分布直接决定熔体粘度、可纺性与纤网强度，故树脂选型时必须同时看 Mw 与 Mw/Mn，而非单一 melt index。参见 [[Polypropylene Handbook Spunbond and Meltblown Nonwoven Resins]]、[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch19 Spunbonding and Melt-Blowing Processes]]。
2. **Functionality → SAP network design.** 官能度（f > 2 → 交联网络）是超吸水性树脂（SAP，聚丙烯酸钠类）交联密度设计的概念源头：共价交联剂或二价阳离子交联点的官能度决定网络结构与凝胶强度，进而决定吸收量与 GBP（加压吸收量）。参见 [[A review on superabsorbent polymers]]、[[GB-T 22875-2018 SAP for Diapers and Sanitary Towels（纸尿裤和卫生巾用高吸收性树脂）]]。
3. **Thermoplastic vs. thermoset → bonding route selection.** 热塑/热固二分法是面层、背层与结构胶选材的第一道筛选：热轧/热风/超声波 bonding 依赖热塑性聚烯烃的反复熔融行为，而交联体系（热固）不可熔融，需改用胶粘或机械加固路径。

Not covered in this chapter（本章未涉及）: Tg/Tm 数值、结晶动力学、粘弹性本构——这些分别属于本书后续章节（见相关来源）。
