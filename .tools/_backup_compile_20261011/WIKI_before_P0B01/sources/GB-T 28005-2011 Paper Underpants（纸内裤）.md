# GB-T 28005-2011 Paper Underpants（纸内裤）

## 一句话定位

Product standard for **disposable paper underpants**（纸内裤，薄型非织造布面料）— hotel/medical/travel one-time underwear; a low-stakes but instructive example of how nonwoven-based disposable apparel is specced (tensile + air permeability + pH + dye safety + GB 15979 hygiene).

## Summary 摘要

GB/T 28005-2011 (SAC/TC 141) applies to disposable underpants constructed from thin nonwovens. The technical table is compact: MD/TD mean tensile ≥0.60 kN/m, air permeability ≥3.00 m/s via the Annex A method (5 strips × 2 sides, 20 cm² test area, 100 Pa pressure drop), pH 4.0–7.5 by cold extraction, and a pass/fail colour-fastness soak test in 0.9% saline. Red lines: no recycled raw materials, aromatic amine dyes banned (GB/T 17592, LOD 20 mg/kg). Hygiene follows GB 15979's underpants tier with batch veto, and each piece must be individually packaged. Nothing here is novel for diaper work, but the standard is a same-family (SAC/TC 141, 2011-09-29 same publish date as GB/T 28004-2011) nonwoven-apparel reference.
