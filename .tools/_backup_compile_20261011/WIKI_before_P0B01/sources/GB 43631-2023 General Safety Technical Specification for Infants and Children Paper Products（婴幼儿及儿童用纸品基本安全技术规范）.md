# GB 43631-2023 General Safety Technical Specification for Infants and Children Paper Products（婴幼儿及儿童用纸品基本安全技术规范）

## 一句话定位

China's **mandatory chemical-safety floor**（强制国标）for all paper products for children ≤6 — the single table that wipes, diapers and tissue SKUs must clear on heavy metals, FWAs, acrylamide, formaldehyde, solvents, preservatives, phthalates and aromatic amines; it is the standard that GB/T 27728.2-2024 delegates its "safety indicators" clause to.

## Summary 摘要

GB 43631-2023 (ICS 85.010 / CCS Y30, 工信部) is a mandatory national standard applying to infant/children paper products manufactured or sold in China. It classifies products into 生活用纸品 (tissue, wipes, diapers) and 文化用纸品 (paper/board/books) and sets one shared chemical baseline plus category-specific rows. The shared floor: four heavy metals, migratable fluorescent substances "无", acrylamide ≤0.1 mg/kg, and a 24-species carcinogenic aromatic amine list (Annex A) at ≤20 mg/kg for printed/dyed items. Notable per-category spreads: formaldehyde is 20 mg/kg for tissue but 6 mg/kg for wipes and diapers; wipes carry extra solvent limits (methanol/benzene/toluene/xylene ≤20 mg/kg) and a hard MIT/MCI "不应检出" preservative clause; phthalate limits differ by an order of magnitude between wipes (DBP+BBP+DEHP ≤0.0012%) and diapers (≤0.1%). Methods are anchored to existing standards: heavy metals via the 2015 Cosmetics Specification ch.4 §1.6 with microwave digestion, FWA via GB/T 27741-2018 ch.5 (tissue/纸巾) or GB/T 28004.1-2021 Annex D (diapers) or GB/T 27728-2011 Annex D (wipes), formaldehyde via GB/T 34448-2017 HPLC, acrylamide GB/T 37859, phthalates GB/T 37860, VOC GB/T 36985 headspace, migratable elements GB 6675.4. Transition: goods produced/imported before 2025-01-01 may sell through shelf life; pre-implementation printed books get a 13-month grace.
