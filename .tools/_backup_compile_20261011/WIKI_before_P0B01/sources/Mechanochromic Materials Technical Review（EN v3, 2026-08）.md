# Mechanochromic Materials Technical Review（EN v3, 2026-08）

## One-line Positioning 一句话定位

The **market, competitor and supplier-landscape review** of the Mechanochromic block — it overturns the "buy a commercial pressure material" assumption, maps four technology routes (M1–M4), screens four material candidates, and lands on the sourcing verdict **"build, not buy"** (custom threshold + custom printing process).

（中文：本批 Mechanochromic 的**市场 / 竞品 / 供应商格局综述**——推翻「买一款商用压力材料」的初始假设，铺开 M1–M4 四条技术路线，筛查四类候选材料，最终给出「自建而非采购」（自定义阈值 + 自定义印刷工艺）的寻源结论。）

## Summary 摘要

The review starts by overturning a Day-1 assumption: the plan was to adopt an existing commercial pressure-activated material (CTI HPP ink, Fujifilm Prescale), but all commercial materials are irreversible metrology types with fixed thresholds at or above 40,000 psi. No skin-close, soft, low-threshold, reversible off-the-shelf spec exists — an innovation white space. The named trap is using **industrial metrology materials as consumer indicator materials**, a triple mismatch of threshold, reversibility and safety.

Market framing: US$1.2 B (2024) → US$4.1 B (2033) at 14.6 % global CAGR; APAC leads at 17.2 %; polymers > 42 % of material forms; North America holds 38 % share but slows; zero diaper-specific SKUs. The review is explicit that a market tailwind is not technical accessibility — head IP sits in the US/EU (Merck, GIVIDI, Avient, Sun Chemical).

Competitive landscape: mid-high concentration with four leaders locking high-value applications (Merck — polymer platform; GIVIDI — proprietary security pressure materials; Avient — elastomers/composites; Sun Chemical — printable mechanochromic inks) plus two printable-ink differentiators at the hygiene gate (CTI, LCR Hallcrest). The entry segment with no giant guarding it is **printable pressure-activated inks**.

Technology routes: M1 mechanophore activation, M2 photonic-crystal structural colour, M3 CLCE mesogen orientation, M4 surface wrinkling. The selection implication is that continuous fit monitoring needs a reversible route (M2/M3), while one-shot over-tightness alerts can use semi-reversible routes (M1).

Material candidates: HPC (regulatory gold standard, power-law strain–colour relation), naphthopyrans (covalent scission, modular colour), spiropyrans (vivid but polar-sensitive and thermally transient), Cu-based HCP (coordination-polymer emission). Engineering hurdles are T1 threshold down-shift, T2 the 25–27 % strain regime (Barty-King et al. 2025: HPC stays viscoelastic below 25 % strain and collapses into a flowing/sheared state beyond it) and T3 rheology/signal stability. The honest limit: a relaxed signal under sustained load is a false-negative safety reading — therefore **signal-stability validation is the pilot's life-or-death gate**.

Sourcing: eight international suppliers/institutes ranked by hygiene fit (CTI primary; Fujifilm Prescale and Sensor Products as metrology tools; Sun Chemical and LCR Hallcrest as ink alternatives; Merck and Avient as platform partners; Chungnam/Hanyang as research partners), and a domestic list where only **Henan Jiayida** and **Shenzhen Xingcheng** are confirmed with real mechanochromic capability. Three application scenarios are ranked: A over-tightness indicator (nearest term), B comfort monitoring (research-grade), C infant anti-strangulation (highest priority). Every conclusion carries an evidence grade (F fact / I inference / A assumption / E estimate).

（中文摘要：综述先推翻初始假设——原计划采购现成商用压力材料（CTI HPP 油墨、Fujifilm Prescale），但商业材料均为不可逆计量型、阈值固定在 ≥40,000 psi，不存在「贴近皮肤、柔软、低阈值、可逆」的现货规格，构成创新空白；被点名的陷阱是把工业计量材料当消费指示材料用。市场面：2024 年 12 亿 → 2033 年 41 亿美元，全球 CAGR 14.6%、亚太 17.2%，聚合物占材料形态 >42%，北美占 38% 但放缓，纸尿裤专用 SKU 为零；同时明确「市场顺风 ≠ 技术可得」，头部 IP 在美欧（Merck、GIVIDI、Avient、Sun Chemical）。竞争格局为中高集中度：四家龙头锁定高值应用，另有两家「可印刷油墨」差异化厂商守在卫品入口（CTI、LCR Hallcrest）；无人把守的入口段正是可印刷压敏油墨。技术路线分 M1 力致变色分子激活、M2 光子晶体结构色、M3 CLCE 介晶取向、M4 表面起皱；结论是连续合身监测须走可逆路线（M2/M3），一次性过紧报警可用半可逆路线（M1）。材料候选四类：HPC（法规金标准、幂律应变—色关系）、萘并吡喃、螺吡喃、铜基 HCP；工程门槛为 T1 阈值下移、T2 25–27% 应变区间（25% 以下 HPC 保持黏弹，超过则进入流动/剪切态）、T3 流变与信号稳定性；诚实边界是「信号弛豫＝假阴性安全读数」，故信号稳定性验证是试产的生死门。寻源面：8 家国际供应商/机构按卫品适配度排序，国内仅嘉亿达与星辰确认具备真实力致变色能力；三类应用场景排序为 A 过紧指示（最近期）、B 舒适监测（科研级）、C 婴儿防勒（最高优先）。全篇结论均标注证据等级 F/I/A/E。）

### B. Four technology routes 四条技术路线

| Route 路线 | Trigger 触发 | Colour output 变色输出 | Form 形态 | Read 解读 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 Mechanophore 力致变色分子 | Stretch to critical force threshold | Bond scission/rearrangement reveals chromophore | Elastomer/polymer networks | Tunable threshold; covalent route |
| M2 Photonic-crystal structural colour 光子晶体结构色 | Stretch changes lattice spacing | Reflectance wavelength shift (blue → red) | Nanostructured films/fibers | Power-law relation; reversible & quantifiable |
| M3 CLCE mesogen orientation | Strain reorients mesogens | Birefringence/reflectance change | CLCE fibers/films | Passive, highly sensitive; research-grade only |
| M4 Surface wrinkling 表面起皱 | Stretch-induced micro-wrinkles | Brightness/hue/view-angle dependence | Stretchable displays, soft robotics | Limited resolution — unfit for safety indication |

### C. Material candidates 材料候选

| Candidate 候选 | Mechanism 机理 | Visual output 视觉输出 | Strategic advantage 优势 |
|---|---|---|---|
| HPC (hydroxypropyl cellulose) | Viscoelastic lattice spacing change | Compressive blue-shift (red → blue) | **Edible / non-toxic / biodegradable; pharma supply chain（法规金标准）** |
| Naphthopyrans 萘并吡喃 | Covalent bond scission (ring-opening) | Modular colour change | Exceptional synthetic accessibility; permanent or transient |
| Spiropyrans 螺吡喃 | 6π electrocyclic ring-opening | Intense vivid colour | Well documented; polar-sensitive & thermally transient |
| Cu-based HCP 铜基配位聚合物 | Coordination-polymer emission shift | Luminescence shift | Emission-based; robust to environmental contaminants |

### D. Engineering hurdles 工程门槛

| Gate 门槛 | Content 内容 | Note |
|---|---|---|
| T1 Threshold down-shift | ≥ 40,000 psi → 20–150 kPa | 两个数量级的定制下移 |
| T2 The 25–27 % strain regime | Below 25 % strain HPC stays in stable viscoelastic pitch compression; beyond 25–27 % it flows/shears and sensor integrity collapses（Barty-King et al. 2025） | 25–27% 是完整性崩塌点 |
| T3 Rheology & signal stability | Shear-thinning + inelastic lateral flow can relax the colour away even while stress persists; gelling agents (e.g. gelatin) must tune rheology to lock the signal | 须凝胶剂锁信号 |
| Honest limit | Relaxed signal under sustained load = false-negative safety reading, worse than no indicator | 试产生死门 |

### G. Application scenarios and verdict 应用场景与结论

| Scenario 场景 | Trigger 触发 | Needs 需求 | Maturity 成熟度 |
|---|---|---|---|
| A Over-tightness indicator 过紧指示 | Waistband/tab tension beyond safe threshold | Low threshold, reversible/semi-reversible, printability, skin/oral safety | **Nearest 最近期**；building blocks available, customization needed |
| B Comfort monitoring 舒适监测 | Repeated strain across core/topsheet | High elongation, reversible, soft conformability | Research-grade only（CLCE/应变织物）；须联合预研 |
| C Infant safety · anti-strangulation 防勒 | Same as A with an over-tight force threshold | A + strong compliance (food-contact grade FDA 21 CFR / EU 10-2011) | Highest priority 最高优先 |

- Core strategy verdict: **build, not buy** — custom threshold + custom printing process.
- Pathway: Path 1 waistband/tab indicators（最短可行）→ Path 2 full-field pressure distribution（智能涂层 + 手机辅助读数）→ Path 3 structural-colour strain feedback（HPC 膜连续跟踪）。
- Note also recorded: this deck is **not legal advice**; regulatory conformity for infant skin-contact products must be certified by professional consultants; powder-form materials must not contact skin layers until validated.
