# GB-T 27728.2-2024 Wipes Part 2 Baby and Child Wipes（湿巾 第2部分 婴童湿巾专用要求）

## 一句话定位

China's voluntary national standard (GB/T) setting **particular requirements for baby & child wet wipes**（婴童湿巾专用要求）— the compliance baseline for any wipes SKU targeting children ≤6 in the CN market.

## Summary 摘要

Part 2 of the GB/T 27728 series (ICS 85.080 / CCS Y39), drafted under SAC/TC 141 (全国造纸工业标准化技术委员会), proposed by 中国轻工业联合会. Drafting units include 中国制浆造纸研究院、好孩子、维达、恒安、金佰利（中国）、广州宝洁、花王（中国）研发中心、露乐健康、赛得利（九江）等 — i.e. essentially the whole CN wipes/hygiene value chain. The standard layers baby-wipe requirements on top of the general requirements of GB/T 27728.1: raw-material minimization and long-safe-use-history principle, fragrance per GB/T 22731, production lotion per the 2015 Cosmetics Safety Technical Specification with an explicit MIT/MCI ban, and a total recycled-fibre ban. Intrinsic quality is compact (liquid content ≥1.7×, pH 4.0–7.5); safety is delegated to GB 43631 and hygiene to GB 15979. Inspection rules: batch release inspection + biennial type inspection + optional agreed (raw-material) inspection; AQL 4.0 on safety, 6.5 on the rest. Labeling goes beyond GB 38598 with full lotion ingredient disclosure (≥0.01% w/w, descending order, INCI Chinese names).
