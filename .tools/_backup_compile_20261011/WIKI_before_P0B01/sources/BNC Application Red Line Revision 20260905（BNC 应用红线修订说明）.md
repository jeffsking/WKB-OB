# BNC Application Red Line Revision 20260905（BNC 应用红线修订说明）

## One-Line Positioning 一句话定位

This is the **governing decision record** that resets which BNC projects may be filed at this library's parent organisation. It is the source of the role-based wording used by every BNC-facing page after 2026-09-05, and it is the reason intermediate-role routes (PEG dry granule, masterbatch carrier, synergist) are admissible at all.
（本页是**治理性决策记录**：重置了哪些 BNC 项目可以立项。它是 2026-09-05 之后所有 BNC 相关页面所引用的「角色化红线」表述的来源，也是中间体类路线（PEG 干颗粒、母粒载体、增效剂）得以进入候选集的依据。）

> Status 状态：已确认生效 | Confirmer 确认人：王总 | Executor 执行：IMA | Related 关联：[[Pain-Point Backward Derivation Template v2（材料合作分析模板 v2）]]

## Summary 摘要

1. The document records a single, dated change: the old wording **"do not start projects that replace fluff pulp / SAP"** becomes **"do not start projects that replace fluff pulp / SAP *as an end material*; intermediate, carrier and synergist roles are not subject to this restriction"**, effective **2026-09-05**.（修订：旧表述「不启动替代绒毛浆/SAP 项目」→ 新表述「不启动以终端材料身份替代绒毛浆/SAP 的项目；中间体/载体/增效角色不受此限」，生效日 2026-09-05。）
2. The stated reason is a case, not a principle. The Baikerui PEG displacement intermediate（方向四）replaces neither fluff pulp nor SAP, but the old red line classified by *substitution object* and so blocked the route from the candidate set.（修订理由是案例实证：方向四既不替代绒毛浆也不替代 SAP，旧红线按「替代对象」一刀切，该路径一度进不了候选集。）
3. The retrospective identifies the root cause as a **missing dimension**: the old frame classified by "what does BNC replace" (topsheet / ADL / SAP / fluff) and judged "does BNC work as a final material" — it had no *delivery form* axis at all (wet film, suspension, dry powder, granule, masterbatch, re-wettable intermediate).（复盘确认根因是**维度缺失**：旧框架按「BNC 替代谁」分类、判据是「作为最终材料行不行」，完全缺「交付形态」维度。）
4. Under the new wording, the other two Baikerui directions stop being collateral damage: hydrophobic dry-powder reinforcement（方向一）is a *reinforcer* role and SAP enhancement（方向三）is a *synergist* role — neither triggers the red line.（新表述下方向一属增强剂、方向三属增效剂，均不再触发误伤。）
5. Section III fixes the boundary of the ruling: bulk end-material substitution remains prohibited; intermediate（re-wettable dry granule / masterbatch）, carrier（grafted anti-microbial / sensing / heating / colour-change）, reinforcer（PP melt / foaming）and SAP synergist or spacer roles are **not** restricted and go through normal project review per Step 2 of the template.（仍禁止：以 BNC/纳米纤维素替代绒毛浆或 SAP 作为大宗终端材料立项；不受限：中间体、载体、增强剂、SAP 增效剂/间隔剂等角色，按《05 模板》Step 2 角色库正常立项评审。）
6. Section III also rules on **document history**: the "strictly forbidden to start fluff or SAP replacement projects" wording in the earlier feasibility documents is retained verbatim as decision history, and its conclusions *at the end-material-substitution level* remain valid; for intermediate / carrier / synergist roles this notice governs.（历史文档效力：早期可行性文档中「严禁启动替换绒毛浆或 SAP 项目」等表述保留原文作为决策历史，其「终端材料替代」层面结论仍有效；涉及中间体/载体/增效角色的判断以本说明为准。）
7. Section IV lists the follow-up: review templates and vendor-visit flows adopt the new wording; BNC review meetings cite this notice as the red-line basis; and a future tightening is pre-declared if an "intermediate by name, substitute in substance" case appears.
8. Credibility: **high** for library purposes — it is a dated, named, confirmed internal decision record with an explicit effective date and a stated reversal condition. It carries no test data and is not a materials source.（可信度判断依据：具名、具日期、已确认生效的内部决策记录，且有明确的反向触发条件；不含实测数据，不作为材料参数来源，但对「能否立项」具最高效力。）

## Key Claims（key_claims）

- **The red line is about role, not about material.** A statement of the form "BNC may not replace X" is now incomplete; the admissible form is "BNC may not replace X *as an end material*". —— 支撑页：[[BNC Application Red Line（BNC 应用红线）]]
- **A red line can be defeated by a missing axis, not only by bad judgement.** The failure mode recorded here is a classification axis that could not represent the winning route. —— 支撑页：[[Pain-Point Backward Derivation（痛点倒推法）]]
- **The revision does not soften the end-material conclusion.** Fermentation economics, absorption mechanism and scale economy still rule out bulk substitution; only the intermediate / carrier / synergist door opened. —— 支撑页：[[BNC 卫生用品技术可行性评估（访前尽调详细版）]]
- **The rule is self-limiting.** A pre-declared reversal condition ("intermediate by name, substitute in substance") is written into the notice. —— 支撑页：[[Pain-Point Backward Derivation Template v2（材料合作分析模板 v2）]]
