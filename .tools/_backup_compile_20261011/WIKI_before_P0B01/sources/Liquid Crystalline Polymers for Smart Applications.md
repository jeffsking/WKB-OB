## Scope and Structure 范围与结构

The thesis is built on one premise: a liquid crystalline polymer (LCP 液晶聚合物) is a *programmable* smart material because mesophase order couples an external stimulus to macroscopic function. Part 1 uses **calamitic** (rod-like) LCPs for light-triggered release; Part 2 uses **discotic/columnar** LCPs mimicking biological ion channels for proton transport.

- **General Introduction and Objectives** (p.1) — "Smart" materials; liquid crystals; nematic/cholesteric, smectic and columnar mesophases; calamitic vs. discotic molecules; main-chain (MC) vs. side-chain (SC) LCPs; strategy and four stated objectives (p.11).

## Key Findings 关键发现

1. **Design lever = mesogen placement and shape.** Both the position of the mesogenic group in the polymer architecture (MC-LCP vs. SC-LCP) and its shape (calamitic vs. discotic) determine the liquid crystalline properties and therefore which application is reachable.
2. **Photoresponsive polyesters.** Three nematic LCPs with α-methylstilbene in the main chain and aliphatic or aromatic spacers were synthesised; two synthetic routes were compared for yield and molecular weight.
3. **First-order photoisomerisation.** Solution kinetics measured by ¹H NMR under timed UV exposure were first order, k ≈ 10⁻⁴ s⁻¹ for all members of the family.
4. **UV-induced wettability flip.** Static water contact angle of a P2/P4 membrane *decreased* after irradiation at 365 nm — the surface becomes more hydrophilic; AFM showed the accompanying roughness change. Relevant as a mechanism for any light-triggered surface property switch.
5. **Z→E isomerisation observed in the mesophase.** At 180 °C the back-isomerisation under 254 nm was monitored for the first time as a liquid-crystalline texture change under POM — i.e. photochemistry is addressable *inside* the LC state, not just in solution.
6. **Microcapsules are dark-stable and light-releasing.** Single-shell capsules of varying core loading were formed with a semicontinuous nozzle device; vanillin release under 365 nm plateaued at ~20 min, and the mechanism was shown both theoretically and experimentally to be main-chain contraction on E→Z isomerisation.
7. **Homeotropic columnar alignment.** A baking process induced homeotropic orientation of modified poly(epichlorohydrin) and poly(epichlorohydrin-co-ethylene oxide), confirmed by azimuthal XRD; this gave 10⁻²–10⁻³ S/cm proton conductivity that did **not** collapse at low relative humidity.
8. **Bio-inspired wires in AAO.** Columnar mesophase growth of dendronized poly[2-(aziridin-1-yl)ethanol] inside anodic alumina required strict thermal control; ~225 columns per support were imaged; hybrid systems transported protons selectively over other monovalent cations with methanol crossover ~100× below Nafion 117.
9. **Method warning.** Electrochemical impedance spectroscopy gave unreliable proton conductivity for polyamine-based hybrids; current–voltage curves were required instead.

## Relevance to Hygiene R&D 与卫材研发的关联

**Moderate 中等关联** — this is not an absorbent-core reference, and nothing in it addresses acquisition, SAP, or acquisition-layer permeability. Its value is as the **mechanism layer behind the chromic / stimuli-responsive programme**, not as a process reference.

- Directly useful for a **light- or stimulus-triggered release or wettability** concept: finding 4 (UV → contact angle drop) and finding 6 (stable capsule, triggered release with a ~20 min plateau) are the proof-of-mechanism for any "core releases an active on stimulus" claim, including odour-control or skin-care additive delivery in a topsheet or core.
- Finding 7 is the strongest transferable idea: **orientation (processing history) buys property**, here humidity-independent transport. The same logic underpins why thermal-bonding/sheath-core orientation dictates nonwoven performance. See also [[Mechanochromic Elastomer from PDLC 5CB PDMS]] for the parallel LC-in-elastomer strain-chromic route.
- Limits honest stated: crude knock-against-distance is 365 nm UV, whose cost and line integration make it a longer-shot enabler than thermochromy for a disposable hygiene product; the proton-conduction half of the thesis (Part 2, fuel cells) has essentially **no** hygiene pull.
- Touchpoint to core materials is indirect: this is the intellectual background to the wetness-indicator work in [[US20070270773A1 Thermochromic Wetness Indicator]] rather than to PP/SAP per se.
