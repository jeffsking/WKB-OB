# GB-T 13519-2016 Polyethylene Heat-Shrinkable Film for Packaging（包装用聚乙烯热收缩薄膜）

## 一句话定位

Product standard for **PE heat-shrink film**（包装用聚乙烯热收缩薄膜）— the transport/outer-packaging film class used to bundle diaper bales and case packs; a packaging-acceptance reference rather than a product-safety standard.

## Summary 摘要

GB/T 13519-2016 (SAC/TC 48) replaces the 1992 edition and reorganizes the product into transverse-shrink and longitudinal-shrink classes keyed to shrink-ratio profiles at 140 ℃ in an oil bath. Mechanical floors scale with gauge: MD tensile ≥16→18 MPa and TD ≥14→16 MPa crossing 0.060 mm, TD elongation ≥300→400%, right-angle tear ≥50→60 kN/m. Dimensional control is by width bands (±5 to ±35 mm) and thickness bands (limit ±0.010–0.025 mm, mean ±8–12%). Rolls are limited to 3 segments of ≥50 m each with marked splices — a converting-line-relevant clause since splice frequency drives wrap-line downtime. Five "other" properties (COF, transmittance, wetting tension, heat-seal strength, impact) are deliberately left to buyer-seller agreement, so procurement specs must fix them explicitly. Storage ≤50 ℃ and shelf life ≤1 year cap warehouse practice.
