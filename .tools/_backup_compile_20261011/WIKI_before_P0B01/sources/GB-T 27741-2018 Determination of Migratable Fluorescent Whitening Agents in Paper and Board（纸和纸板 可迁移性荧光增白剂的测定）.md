# GB-T 27741-2018 Determination of Migratable Fluorescent Whitening Agents in Paper and Board（纸和纸板 可迁移性荧光增白剂的测定）

## 一句话定位

The **test-method standard** behind every "可迁移性荧光增白剂不得检出" clause in CN hygiene-product standards (GB 15979-2024, GB/T 21331-2021, GB 43631) — it defines *how* migratable FWA is judged, not the limit itself.

## Summary 摘要

GB/T 27741-2018 (SAC/TC 141) replaces the 2011 edition and provides one qualitative and two quantitative routes for migratable fluorescent whitening agents（可迁移性荧光增白剂）in paper, board and pulp. The qualitative method is the de-facto gate for hygiene papers: extract with pH 7.5–9.0 ammonia-buffered water, acidify the filtrate to pH 3.0–5.0, let a 5×5 cm gauze adsorb the FWAs for 30 min at 40 ℃, then inspect under 254/365 nm UV against a blank — fluorescence brighter than the blank in both parallels means "positive". Quantification is either UV-Vis at 348 nm (reported as VBL equivalent, LOD 20.0 mg/kg, but lignin/dye interference) or HPLC-FLD (Ex 350 nm / Em 430 nm, C18 column, TBA ion-pair mobile phase) targeting the 7 FWAs listed in Annex A, LOD 1.0 mg/kg. 2018 changes vs 2011 include revised scope/definition, added balances and thermostatic shaker baths, added LOD/recovery clauses, and the new Annex A FWA identity list.
