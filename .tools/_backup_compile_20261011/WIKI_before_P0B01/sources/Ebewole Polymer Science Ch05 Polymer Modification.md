# Ebewole Polymer Science Ch05 Polymer Modification

> Source: Ebewole, *Polymer Science and Technology*, Ch.5 (Polymer Modification). 章节主题：共聚改性、分子构筑控制、聚合后反应（含纤维素衍生物）。

Chapter 5 addresses the central question of polymer engineering: **how to obtain target properties from existing polymers rather than synthesising new ones from scratch 不从头合成新聚合物，而是在已有聚合物基础上获得目标性能**.

本章明确限定在**化学改性**，与 Ch.9 的物理共混/合金化（physical blending / alloying）分工。

## Three Routes to Polymer Modification 三大改性路径

> "Development of tailor-made materials by polymer modification is usually less costly and can be achieved more readily than through synthesis of new polymers." (Ch.5, p.118)

> "The performance of a polymer is determined primarily by the composition and structure of the polymer molecule." (Ch.5, p.118)

> "Thus polymer modification can be accomplished by employing one or more of the following techniques: • Copolymerization of more than one monomer; • Control of molecular architecture; • Postpolymerization polymer reactions involving functional/reactive groups introduced deliberately into the polymer main chain or side groups." (Ch.5, p.118)

| Route 路径 | Stage 发生阶段 | Structural outcome 结构结果 | Examples 代表 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Copolymerization 共聚 | during polymerisation 聚合中 | random / block / graft 无规/嵌段/接枝 | SBR, ABS, EVA |
| Molecular architecture control 分子构筑控制 | during polymerisation 聚合中 | branching / cross-linking / block 支化/交联/嵌段 | LDPE, LDPE-g-MA |
| Postpolymerization reactions 聚合后反应 | after polymerisation 聚合后 | cross-linking / grafting / derivatisation 交联/接枝/衍生化 | cellulose derivatives, modified UF |
| Physical blending / alloying 物理共混 | during processing 加工中 | blend / alloy / composite 共混/合金 | PP/EPDM, PC/ABS (Ch.9) |

## Copolymerisation 共聚改性

### A. Styrene–butadiene copolymers 丁苯共聚物

> "Styrene–butadiene random copolymer exhibits a homogeneous single phase and has properties intermediate to those of the parent homopolymer. On the other hand, block or graft copolymers of styrene and butadiene form heterogeneous multiphase systems whose properties are not simply characteristic of each homopolymer, but are dictated by the multiphase character of the copolymer." (Ch.5, p.119)

> "Most of the defects of polybutadiene homopolymer can be overcome by the incorporation of 28% styrene into the copolymer." (Ch.5, p.119)

Composition ladder given in the source: **28% styrene → SBR** (footwear, hose, adhesives); **60–74% styrene → hot-melt adhesives / latex paints**; high styrene → thermoplastic. 组成连续变化对应从弹性体到热塑性塑料的性能谱。

### B. Ethylene copolymers 乙烯共聚物

> "EEA, for example, also has the flexibility of plasticized vinyl without the thermal instability and plasticizer migration problems associated with PVC." (Ch.5, p.120)

> "the range of properties of the copolymer can be varied by varying the proportion and molecular weight of the comonomer." (Ch.5, p.120)

EVA / EMA / EEA are compared against LDPE (Tables 5.2, 5.3): copolymerisation raises flexural modulus behaviour, environmental stress-crack resistance (ESCR) and impact strength — 即"无增塑剂获得柔韧性 plasticizer-free flexibility"。

### C. ABS — three monomers, two phases ABS 三单体双相模型

> "ABS is the generic name of a family of engineering thermoplastics produced by a combination of three monomers: acrylonitrile, butadiene, and styrene." (Ch.5, p.121)

> "Polymer chemical resistance and heat and aging stability depend on acrylonitrile, while its toughness, impact resistance, and property retention at low temperature are developed through butadiene. Copolymer rigidity, glossy surface appearance, and ease of processability are contributions from styrene." (Ch.5, p.121)

> "ABS resins consist essentially of two phases: a rubbery phase dispersed in a continuous glassy matrix of styrene–acrylonitrile copolymer (SAN) through a boundary layer of SAN graft." (Ch.5, p.121)

Three-level morphology 三层次形态: dispersed rubbery phase 分散橡胶相 + continuous glassy SAN matrix 连续 SAN 玻璃态基体 + SAN graft boundary layer SAN 接枝边界层（起相容剂作用）。调控参数为橡胶浓度、粒径与分布。

### D. Condensation polymers as copolymers 缩聚物共聚

> "Addition polymers consist of a chain of carbon atoms on their backbones. Modification of these polymers is limited essentially to the manipulation of the pendant group. On the other hand, for condensation polymers, in addition to the modification of the pendant structure, there is the benefit of the additional possible structural alteration of the backbone." (Ch.5, p.122)

> "by a suitable choice of monomers, the spacing between the polar ester groups and hence the cohesive energy density can be controlled." (Ch.5, p.122)

缩聚物的改性自由度高于加聚物：除侧基外还可改造主链（引入芳香/脂环/不饱和二酸或二醇），从而调控刚性与 CED（与 Ch.3 次键、Ch.4 的 CED–Tg 关系直接呼应）。Copolyester 应用：涂料、聚氨酯热塑弹性体、层压树脂、热固模塑料。

## Postpolymerization Reactions 聚合后反应

> "As indicated earlier, another powerful tool for upgrading polymer properties is the postpolymerization reaction of preformed polymers. These reactions may occur on reactive sites dispersed in the polymer main chain. Such reactions include chain extensions, cross-linking, and graft and block copolymer formation." (Ch.5, p.125)

**Polysaccharides 多糖（纤维素）改性**: cellulose is infusible and insoluble; derivatisation with a controlled degree of substitution restores processability.

> "In the preparation of cellulose derivatives a controlled degree of substitution of the three hydroxyls is necessary." (Ch.5, p.125) — complete reaction of the three hydroxyls is generally not attempted.

**Condensation polymer modification**: UF resin modified with a flexible triamine to reduce internal stress (Example 5.3).

## Academic Context and Critique 学术对话与批判

- **Hildebrand–Scott solubility-parameter theory** — the CED concept from Ch.3 is applied directly to copolyester modification.
- **Pauling hydrogen-bond theory** — basis for cellulose –OH substitution chemistry.
- **Rubber-toughening theory (1950s)** — the chapter's ABS description follows the classic two-phase toughening framework.
- **Engineering criterion "modification vs. synthesis"** — the source states modification is "less costly … more readily", but at large industrial scale complex copolymer synthesis can be cheaper than postpolymerisation modification; 该判据偏实验室/中试规模。
- **Morphology determinism** — "properties … dictated by the multiphase character" is a core axiom of polymer morphology, yet the continuum between homogeneous and multiphase (e.g. semi-IPN) resists binary classification.
- **Degree of substitution control** — the three cellulose hydroxyls (C2/C3/C6) differ in reactivity, a long-standing issue in cellulose chemistry.

## Hygiene Implication 卫材关联

1. **SAP as a postpolymerization cross-linked network 超吸水性树脂的聚合后交联.** 聚丙烯酸钠类 SAP 的典型路线是"先合成聚丙烯酸（或其盐），再经交联形成三维网络"——包括共价交联剂与多价阳离子交联。这一路径正是 Ch.5 §III 的 **postpolymerization cross-linking**（p.125），而非从头设计新单体。因而 SAP 的凝胶强度与吸收量可在聚合后独立调节，这是工业上控制 GBP 与 CRC 的关键自由度。参见 [[A review on superabsorbent polymers]]。
2. **PP toughening PP 增韧.** 等规 PP 的低温脆性（Ch.4, Tg 示例约 −10 °C）通过接枝共聚（如 PP-g-MA）或与 EPDM 共混改善；PP/EPDM 属于"橡胶增韧塑料"，其形态逻辑与本章 ABS 的"分散橡胶相 + 连续玻璃态基体 + 接枝边界层"模型同源。参见 [[Ebewole Polymer Science Ch04 Thermal Transitions in Polymers]]、[[Ebewole Polymer Science Ch02 Polymerization Mechanisms]]。
3. **Ethylene copolymers in hot-melt adhesives 乙烯共聚物与热熔胶.** EVA / EMA / EEA 通过共聚单体比例与分子量获得"无增塑剂柔韧性"（p.120），避免了增塑剂迁移问题；这正是卫材结构胶选用烯烃共聚物体系的原因之一。参见 [[EP4428211A1 Hot Melt Adhesive Composition (Bostik)]]。
4. **Cellulose derivatisation 纤维素衍生化.** 绒毛浆与纤维素基材料的化学改性（醚化/酯化，控制取代度）是提升吸液与稳定性的手段；取代度控制原则见 p.125。
