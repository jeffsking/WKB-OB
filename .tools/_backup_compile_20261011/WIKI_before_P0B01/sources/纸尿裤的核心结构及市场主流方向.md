## Positioning 一句话定位

An industry review of diaper core structure and market direction: layer-by-layer function, brand-level structural differentiation, and the four innovation axes of the absorbent core (structure / material / process / commercialisation). （中文：纸尿裤核心结构与市场主流方向的产业综述——逐层功能、品牌结构差异，以及芯体的结构/材料/工艺/商业化四条创新轴。）

## Summary 摘要

The document is a synthesised market review rather than primary research. Its reusable content is (a) the canonical four-layer architecture and auxiliary systems, (b) the structural-engineering taxonomy used by leading brands (channels,分区, rear-pocket, 3D topsheet, breathable backsheet), (c) the materials shift toward **SAF 超吸水纤维**, **bioSAP** and **PLA/PHA 生物基包裹层**, and (d) process shifts toward **ultrasonic bonding instead of hot-melt** and **online one-piece forming**.

Most performance figures attributed to brands (e.g. 98% leak protection, 99% bacterial barrier, 89% reduction in redness) are **vendor claims from public materials**, not independently measured; they are recorded here as claims with that caveat.

## Extracted Data and Conclusions 提炼的数字与结论

### 基础结构 Basic architecture

| Layer 层 | Material 材质 | Function 功能 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 表面包覆层 Topsheet | 亲肤透气无纺布（高端用天然棉柔/打孔膜） | 快速下渗、阻止回渗、排湿热气 |
| 导流层 ADL | 无纺布 | 均匀纵向扩散、扩大吸收面积 |
| 吸收芯层 Core | 混合芯体（绒毛浆 + SAP）或复合芯体（无尘纸 + 蓬松无纺布 + SAP） | 俘获/吸收/分散/储存 |
| 底膜 Backsheet | PE 膜 → 透气 PE 膜 → 无纺布替代（高端） | 防漏屏障 |
| 辅助系统 | 闭合系统（前腰贴/魔术扣/弹性腰围）、立体防漏护围、尿显条 | 固定、防侧漏、更换提示 |

- 一片纸尿裤约由 **近 20 种原材料**构成。

### 芯体结构创新 Core structure innovation

| Direction 方向 | Mechanism 机理 | Reported effect 报告效果 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 立体承载空腔 | 模具定型规则下凹空腔，SAP 独立封闭，空腔间留贯通流体通道 | 精准定位防跑动、避免凝胶堵塞、提升湿气穿透率 |
| 按需分布（干法纸 EAM） | 定制孔隙结构 + 分区铺设 SAP + 多通道 | 芯体与导流层材料合二为一，更薄且吸收/扩散/储存更优 |
| 超吸收通道 | 三条吸收通道均匀分散湿气 | 防止吸液后下坠（厂商宣称） |
| 蜂巢芯体（大王） | 六边形蜂巢 + 差异化超声波粘接（点线轻度粘接预留膨胀空间、大三角边缘高强度粘接固定） | 取代热熔胶、形成通风通道、抗断层起坨 |
| 在线一体成型 | 模具定型 + 胶粘/热压/超声波封边 | 缩减工序、保证出厂 SAP 分布 |

## Key Claims 关键论断

1. **Four-layer architecture is stable; differentiation lives in the core 四层结构稳定，差异在芯体** — topsheet/ADL/core/backsheet plus ~20 raw materials; brand competition moved from "absorbency volume" to scenario-specific structural engineering, see [[Fluff-Free 4th-Gen Absorbent Core（无木浆第四代芯体）]].
2. **Channelisation is the main structural lever 通道化是主要结构杠杆** — 立体空腔 + 按需分布 + 多通道 simultaneously attack SAP migration, gel blocking and 起坨/断层; see [[Core Channel Pattern（芯体通道花型设计）]].
3. **SAF converts absorbency from particles to a shaped network SAF 把吸水从颗粒变成定型网络** — 60% SAF blend gives 28.62 g/g saline absorbency with thickness-only swelling; the layer-count optimum (3 layers, 22.58 g/g) shows stack design is a tuning knob.
4. **bioSAP is a carbon lever, not (yet) a cost lever bioSAP 是碳杠杆而非成本杠杆** — 15–25% footprint reduction matters because raw material purchasing drives ~80% of indirect emissions; see [[Biodegradable & Bio-based Diaper Materials（可降解与生物基卫材材料）]].
5. **Backsheet is part of the microclimate system 底膜属微气候系统** — quantified micro-hole counts and moisture transmission rates (150 holes/cm²; 8,000 g/m²/24h) and the 30–35 °C / 25–70% RH target band; see [[Diaper Microclimate]].
6. **Vendor claims need tagging 厂商宣称须标注** — 98% leak protection, 99% bacterial barrier and 89% redness reduction are marketing figures without cited test protocols; treat as claims, not verified data.
