# Market Pulp Monthly March 2026（TTOBMA 木浆月报 · 2026-03-15）

> **数据月份口径 Data vintage**：正文各价格均为 **TTO 2026 年 2 月净价指数**（DP 为 1 月）；市场事件与预测截至 **2026-03-15**。本页所有数字按此口径引用，后须月报更新时以「追加」方式登记，不覆盖本页。

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

TTOBMA 木浆月报（2026-03-15，微信转存全文）：BHK 涨价落地、BSK 供应过剩、**绒毛浆一枝独秀**的三线分化月；对采购的含义是**针叶系（含绒毛浆原料端）夏季承压、Q4 补库窗口**。本页是本库 `Resources/Pulp World` 批次**首份**落库的浆价月报页，首次给出**绒毛浆价格指数**数据。

## Summary 摘要

Executive summary lists nine headlines: BHK China +$10 in Feb with March increase being executed; NBSK China +$3; BHK market activity post-Chinese New Year better than expected; BSK weaker than BHK, flat-to-down in March; **Indonesia harvesting permits revoked** (hits BHK/BCTMP/DP); **Iran war** with oil at $90–100/bbl (peaked near $120); **Phoenix Resources BCTMP (850 kt/yr) turbine failure outage** in early March. BHK detail: +$20 announced in early March but only +$10 executed against Chinese buyer resistance; April +$20 planned, hinge on Shanghai paper week; European restocking (pulp stocks in days-of-consumption declining) supports prices; NA–China spread narrowed (NA was anomalously below China in Feb given $70–80/t extra logistics for South American suppliers). BSK detail: China spot ~$670/t (Feb $655–660); **China domestic BSK output +1.5–2.0 Mt in 12–18 months** immediately removing 20–25% of market pulp demand; softwood chip imports diversified (Vietnam 140k BDMT from ~zero in 2023 due to pine-wilt salvage; Fiji 85k BDMT redirected from Japan); global outages include Domtar Crofton closed and **Metsä Joutseno 3-month shutdown from Mar 15/31** (all staff laid off in 90-day process; 6 months down in 2025 already); Russian logging declining with 90% of imported forestry equipment possibly unusable by 2028. UKP: Feb index $592/ADMT (-$7), China discount ~$100 vs NBSK; Fibre Excellence Tarascon (250 kt/yr) in distress — French state support €150M, wood cost $450/t pulp. **Fluff pulp: Feb index $828/ADMT (+$14), second consecutive rise; China resale +~$20 in 30 days; sentiment up; buyers securing surcharge insurance on Iran-war freight risk; outages = US South winter storm, ENCE Navia strike, unplanned maintenance, market-related downtime; outlook slightly better than other BSK markets; low SBSK price makes conversion attractive**; downstream fiber-cement (James Hardie) Q4 volumes down, two plants closed, product +5–11% Jan 1, expects no pulp cost increase before H2 2026. BCTMP: third consecutive monthly rise but HYP stubbornly weak on oversupply; APP Indonesia's new 1.4 Mt/yr FBB line at Kerawang starting within weeks (integrated HYP) — India import surge expected 2026–27. DP: European producers running low rates selling Europe-only; high oil lifts VSF/Lyocell prices (war effect) but high cotton stocks in China cap DP upside; BHK→DP conversions announced (Altri Biotek 260 kt BHK → 180–200 kt DP by end-2026; an Indonesian producer partial conversion mid-2027). War macro: Strait of Hormuz (20% of global oil) effectively closed since early March; container rates up (Drewry +8% first week; Maersk emergency surcharges $1,800/20′ dry, $3,800/40′ reefer); Asia–Europe keeps routing via Cape of Good Hope; USD index up >2% since war start (offsetting prior weakness); **TTOBMA cuts: end-product growth 2%→1%, market pulp demand to -0.5% (from +1%), D/S balance below 97% threshold for Q2/Q3 (weakening), BSK $600s through summer, recovery starting Q4 (conflict assumed resolved in 3–6 months), 2027 peak lower than earlier forecast**. Strategy notes: summer likely the price trough — buyers should watch Q4 restocking; high-cost BSK producers face prolonged negative margins and may exit.
