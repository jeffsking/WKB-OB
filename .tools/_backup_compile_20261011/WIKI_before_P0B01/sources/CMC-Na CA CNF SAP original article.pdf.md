# CMC-Na CA CNF SAP original article.pdf

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

Peer-reviewed 2025 study of a fully bio-based **cellulose aerogel** superabsorbent (CMC-Na + citric-acid crosslinker + CNF scaffold) reaching 153 g/g absorption — a low-microplastic alternative to petro-based acrylic SAP for hygiene.（2025 年同行评议研究：全生物基纤维素气凝胶高吸水材料（CMC-Na + 柠檬酸交联剂 + CNF 骨架）吸水达 153 g/g，是卫品中石油基丙烯酸酯 SAP 的低微塑料替代。）

## Summary 摘要

- **Material 材料**: Sodium carboxymethyl cellulose (**CMC-Na**) as the absorbent body; **cellulose nanofibers (CNF)** as the structural scaffold; **citric acid (CA)** as the crosslinker adding carboxylate sites.（材料：羧甲基纤维素钠（CMC-Na）为吸水主体；纤维素纳米纤丝（CNF）为结构骨架；柠檬酸（CA）为交联剂并引入羧酸位点。）
- **Process 工艺**: homogeneous mixing → freeze-drying (lyophilization, 48 h) → **160 °C thermal cross-linking** to form a robust network.（工艺：均匀混合 → 冷冻干燥（48h） → 160°C 热交联成网。）
- **Motivation 动机**: conventional SAPs are petroleum-based (acrylate/acrylic acid) with poor sustainability and microplastic risk; bio-based SAMs are a strategic direction for hygiene.（动机：传统 SAP 为石油基、可持续性差且有微塑料风险；生物基 SAM 是卫品战略方向。）
- **Authors / venue 出处**: Luying Chen, Hainan Zhang, Xiaofeng Sui; *Int. J. Biol. Macromol.* 327 (2025) 147551 (Elsevier). CNF (8 wt%) sourced from SAPPI Maastricht BV (Netherlands).（作者/出处：陈、张、隋；IJBM 327(2025)147551；CNF 购自 SAPPI Maastricht。）

## Extracted numbers & conclusions 提炼的数字与结论

| Parameter 参数 | Value 数值 | Note 备注 |
|---|---|---|
| Water absorption 吸水 | 153 ± 3 g/g | optimal formulation |
| Water retention 保水 | 123 ± 4 g/g | optimal formulation |
| CMC-Na dose | 7 wt% | absorbent body |
| CA dose | 0.005 wt% | crosslinker |
| CNF dose | 0.1 wt% | scaffold (SAPPI 8 wt% suspension) |
| Freezing temp | −80 °C | ice-gel formation |
| Cross-linking | 160 °C, 35 min | thermal |
| pH stability | pH 1–13 | absorption maintained |
| Saline stability | NaCl/KCl/CaCl₂/MgCl₂/AlCl₃/FeCl₃ | retention maintained |

> Optimal composition from the dose-sweep: CMC-Na 7%, CA 0.5% (0.005 wt in the mix), CNF 0.1%.（剂量扫描最优组成如上。）

## Key claims 关键论断

- A cellulose aerogel can reach >150 g/g absorption while being fully bio-based and microplastic-free — directly relevant to the bio-SAP roadmap.（纤维素气凝胶可在全生物基、无微塑料前提下达到 >150 g/g 吸水，与生物基 SAP 路线图直接相关。）—— 支撑页：[[Biodegradable SAP（可降解高吸水树脂）]]、[[纳米纤维素（CNC、CNF、BNC）]]
