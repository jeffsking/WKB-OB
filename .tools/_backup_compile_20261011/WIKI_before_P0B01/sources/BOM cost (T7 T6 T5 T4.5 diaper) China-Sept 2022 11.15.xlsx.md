# BOM cost (T7 T6 T5 T4.5 diaper) China-Sept 2022 11.15.xlsx

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

A 2022-Q3 China domestic diaper & pants bill-of-materials (BOM) workbook that breaks every tier (T7→T3.5) and size down into material category, grade, supplier and unit price — a procurement baseline for the Open Innovation cost work.（一份 2022 Q3 中国区纸尿裤/拉拉裤分档 BOM，逐档逐码拆解物料、牌号、供应商与单价，是开放创新成本工作的底表。）

## Summary 摘要

- The workbook is a line-item BOM covering Domestic Diaper (OD) and Domestic Pants (PDP/MDP) across tiers T7, T6, T5.8, T5.5, T5.4, T5.2, T5, T4.5, T4, T3.5 and sizes NB/S/M/L/XL/XXL/XXXL.（工作簿为逐行 BOM，覆盖 T7 至 T3.5 各档与 NB–XXXL 各码。）
- Chassis materials include Out cover (外层无纺布), Liner (面层), Liner/Poly Side Wrapsheet (上/下包裹), Waist band NW (弹性腰围无纺布), Flap (折页), Inner/Outer Panel (内/外腰围), plus SAP (吸水粉) and adhesives (EA elastic ad / CA cons ad).（底材含外覆、面层、上/下包裹、弹性腰围、折页、内/外腰围，以及 SAP 与胶黏剂。）
- Vendors observed: 延江/Yanjiang (topsheet & cover), 王金/Wangjin (spunlace wrapsheet), 诺邦/Nuobang & 欧丽/Auline (LSN/PSN wrapsheet), 南六/Nanliu (poly side wrapsheet), 俊富/Jofo (waist band & flap SMS/SS), 聚胶 & 汉高/Henkel (adhesives), 三大雅/San Daya & LG & 诺尔/Nuoer (SAP).（可见供应商：延江、王金、诺邦、欧丽、南六、俊富、聚胶、汉高、三大雅、LG、诺尔。）
- Front matter flags the price column was updated ([Price已更新]) at extraction, so unit prices reflect a refreshed 2022-Q3 snapshot.（提取期标注了「Price 已更新」，单价反映 2022 Q3 刷新口径。）
