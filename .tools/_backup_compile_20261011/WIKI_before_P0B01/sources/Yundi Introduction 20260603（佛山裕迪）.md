# Yundi Introduction 20260603（佛山裕迪）

## 一句话定位

**Yundi（佛山裕迪，中文名待核）** 2026-06-03 公司介绍，本批作为「卫品薄膜（PE 透气膜/印刷尿显膜）供应商」信息源。

## 摘要

Yundi (referenced as Foshan Yundi Film) is a film supplier with ~30 years of product R&D, six product categories and 50+/40+ industry-leading production equipments. Its **PE breathable film series** is specialized for hygienic film materials (diapers, sanitary napkins). Its printing machine can print 6 colors on the front and 2 on the back plus a **urine-discolor feature**, max 10 colors, and products can be laminated with adhesive-bonded fabric and air-through thermal-bonded nonwovens. This positions Yundi as a printed/breathable hygiene-film supplier (including urine-indicator film).
