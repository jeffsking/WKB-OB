# 01_PLA_PPC 可降解材料研究报告（PLA + PPC Degradable Materials Report · Levima Full-Chain Analysis）

## Positioning 定位

Industry research compilation (2026-04; sources: 联泓新科年报/行业报告/专利; prices 2024–2025 market data) on PLA（聚乳酸）+ PPC（聚碳酸亚丙酯）degradable materials, framed around 联泓新科's full-chain layout. Supplies the cost/price baseline and capacity landscape for bio-based material sourcing decisions in the库's degradable-materials track.（PLA/PPC 原理、成本、产能与应用全景。）

## Summary 摘要

PLA：from plant starch → lactic acid（光学纯度 ≥99%）→ lactide（收率 ≥90%、纯度 ≥99.5%）→ ring-opening polymerization（主流两步法，Mw 70–100 万）; Levima's self-developed chain gives high optical purity lactide and controllable Mw. PPC：CO₂ + PO alternating copolymer（CAS catalyst, supercritical polymerization 80–120 °C / 5–10 MPa, selectivity ≥95%, residual monomer <100 ppm）; fully degradable, rigid-tough balance, barrier, transparent; listed in national key R&D program. PLA/PPC blends: PPC toughens PLA（impact ↑ with PPC content）; MDI compatibilizer lifts both impact and tensile. Capacity: Levima PLA 4 万吨/年 + lactic acid 10 万吨（江西科院, 2024）; PPC 5 万吨/年（联泓格润, end-2025; ~19.7 亿 capex）。Global PLA: NatureWorks 15 万吨、TotalEnergies Corbion 7.5 万吨、丰原 10 万吨、海正 4 万吨、联泓 4 万吨、金发 3 万吨。Costs: PLA 15,000–25,000 元/吨（2–3× PP）、PPC 18,000–22,000、PP 基准 7,000–9,000、PBAT 12,000–18,000; PLA 原料成本中玉米淀粉占 40–45%; PPC 中 PO 占 50–55%、CO₂ 仅 5–8%。Applications: packaging, fibre/nonwoven（可纺性）, 3D printing, medical, agriculture（地膜 3–6 个月降解）; PPC in films/foams/medical carriers. Trends: lactide cost-down, high-heat/high-toughness grades, chemical recycling, standards.（原理 × 产能 × 成本价格 × 应用。）
