# Chapter 8 Drawing of Melt-Spun Fibres 熔纺纤维的牵伸

> Source: Manufactured Fibre Technology, Ch.08, pp.170–186.
> Author of chapter: A.K. Sengupta. 本章作者：A.K. Sengupta。

## Summary 摘要

This chapter treats **drawing 牵伸（orientation stretching 取向拉伸）** as the decisive post-spinning step that converts an as-spun filament of low strength and low orientation into a usable fibre. It moves from the mechanics of the drawing unit (draw twister 牵伸加捻机), through the necking/cold-drawing mechanism, to the structure–property chain "draw ratio → crystallinity / orientation / birefringence / density → tenacity and modulus", and closes with industrial routes including high-tenacity two-stage drawing, high-speed spinning, spin-draw, POY drawing and draw-warping. 本章把「牵伸比 → 结晶／取向／双折射／密度 → 强度模量」确立为一条完整的因果链。

## Key Points 核心观点

1. **Drawing is orientation strengthening, not elastic deformation 牵伸的本质是取向强化而非弹性形变** (p.170). Source wording: "Through the drawing operation the fibre is orientationally strengthened due to alignment of the molecular chains along the fibre axis and shows enhanced recovery." 强度、模量与尺寸稳定性（dimensional stability）随之提升。
2. **Spinning speed sets the residual draw ratio 纺速决定残留牵伸比** (p.171). Source wording: "an increase in spinning speed increases the orientation of the molecular chains along the fibre axis as a consequence of which the residual draw ratio of the fibre reduces with the increase in spinning speed." This underpins the **LOY / POY / HOY / FOY** yarn classes; **POY (partially oriented yarn 预取向丝)** is produced at intermediate spinning speeds of **1500–4000 m/min** and requires subsequent drawing.
3. **Necking is initiated by strain softening and stabilized by strain hardening 细颈由应变软化引发、由应变硬化稳定** (p.174). Source wording: "neck formation is caused by mechanical instability occasioned by strain softening, and cold drawing … is accomplished by stabilization of the neck through strain hardening resulting from molecular orientation." If strain hardening is insufficient — e.g. adiabatic heating at high drawing speed, or low molecular weight — the neck cannot stabilize and drawing fails.
4. **PET shows a critical stress for stress-induced crystallization PET 的应力诱导结晶存在临界应力** (p.175). Above Tg the deformation map splits into a flow-deformation region and a stress-induced crystallization region. Source wording: "Stress-induced crystallization occurs only when the strain rate is sufficient to generate a critical stress value; the effect of temperature is secondary." 应力主导结晶，温度只通过分子流动性间接起作用。
5. **Draw termination is structural, not random 牵伸终止源于结构变化而非随机缺陷** (p.182). Source wording: "The termination of drawing is thus not due to random defects, but is probably associated with certain changes in supermolecular structure which finally leads to local overstresses." Hence the limiting draw ratio is reproducible.
6. **High-tenacity routes use hot drawing and two-stage drawing 高强丝依赖热牵伸与两段牵伸** (p.184). Source wording: "Since the drawing tension increases with both crystallinity and draw ratio, hot drawing is used; the higher molecular mobility present in hot drawing facilitates the drawing process by reducing drawing tension."

## Drawing Mechanics 牵伸力学

- Three-region load–elongation model on an undrawn filament (Fig. 8.3): region I elastic, region II post-yield plastic, region III strain hardening before break. Key parameters: **yield strain 屈服应变**, **yield stress 屈服应力**, **neck angle 细颈角**.
- Neck angle is controlled by hot-plate temperature, hot-pin temperature, draw ratio and feed speed; a **snubbing pin 阻滞销** is used to stabilize the neck position. Necking 细颈: "the sample first yields with the appearance of a neck and thereafter the neck propagates through the sample" (p.173).
- **Natural draw ratio 自然牵伸比** (p.182): "the draw ratio up to which the fibre undergoes plastic deformation at constant load".
- Nylon 6 (polyamide 6 聚酰胺6) necking differs from PET: nylon 6 is already crystalline and its cold-drawing behaviour is influenced by moisture (pp.175–178); Table 8.1 reports that with increasing draw ratio nylon 6 tenacity rises and elongation falls (p.178).

## Effect of Draw Ratio on Structure 牵伸比对结构的影响

Structure is tracked by **X-ray crystallinity X射线结晶度**, **birefringence 双折射**, **density 密度**, and **dichroic ratio 二向色比** measured with a dichroic dye for *amorphous* orientation, complemented by **IR spectroscopy at 1700–1800 cm⁻¹** (C=O end-group concentration) and intrinsic viscosity for molecular weight distribution (p.180, pp.178–183).

- Birefringence rises with draw ratio (Fig. 8.10, p.180).
- The divergence between density-derived and X-ray crystallinity evidence supports the **Provorsek microfibril-slip model 微纤间滑移模型** (p.181) — inferred rather than directly proven (the source itself rates this evidence as self-consistent but indirect).
- Drawing temperatures quoted in the source: nylon drawn at room temperature, PET at about **90 °C**, high-tenacity PET in two stages with an **80–100 °C** differential, and POY annealing at **120–250 °C**.

## Limiting Draw Ratio and Chain Scission 极限牵伸比与断链

Drawing terminates where orientation strengthening is overtaken by two competing processes: increasing structural heterogeneity along the orientation axis, and **molecular chain scission 分子断链**. The **Slutsker & Utevskii local overstress model** plus a critical IR end-group concentration are cited as evidence (pp.182–183, Fig. 8.14). 取向强化与结构异质性的竞争决定了可重复出现的极限牵伸比。

## Industrial Routes 工业工艺路线

- **Draw twister 牵伸加捻机**: two zones (drawing + twisting); **machine draw ratio** is set by feed roll / draw roll speeds, distinct from the actual draw ratio.
- **High-tenacity drawing 高强化牵伸**: hot drawing and two-stage drawing to lower tension and limit chain scission (p.184).
- **High-speed spinning and spin-draw 高速纺与纺牵一步法**: treated only briefly here because Chapter 4 already covers high-speed spinning in detail (p.185). The source itself flags this as a completeness gap — 在本章内论证较弱。
- **Draw-warping 整经牵伸** (p.186): "more than a thousand ends are drawn together to form a warp beam" — 上千根丝并成经轴一起牵伸。

## Hygiene implication 卫生材料启示

Relevant, but **indirectly**. 本章讲连续长丝的纺后牵伸，而吸收芯体与非织造布主要使用短纤维与一步法纺丝成网，专门的 substantive drawing 工序并不存在。可用之处在三处：

1. **Spunbond filament orientation maps onto this chapter 纺粘长丝的取向可直接套用本章框架**：spunbond 的拉伸/引伸区就是"受限版"的 drawing —— 纺速越高、分子预取向越强、残留可拉伸空间越小。这解释了为什么提高纺速会同时提高强力和降低伸长——与面的拉伸强度和柔软手感的矛盾同一根源。参见 [[Pentagram PET vs Rounded Trilobal PET (Topsheet Shaped Fibre Selection)]]。
2. **热粘合收缩风险可由本章的"取向强化 vs 回复"解释**：高取向丝在 Tg 以上受热会回缩，这正是 ES/PE-PP 皮芯热风粘合时需要控制热风温度与张力的结构原因。参见 [[Pressure vs Thermal vs Ultrasonic Bonding（压力·热·超声波粘合对比）]] 与 [[PP基皮芯与PET基皮芯对比]]。
3. **PET 的临界应力判据**对 topsheet 用 PET 纺粘的在线拉伸窗口有参考价值：能否形成稳定取向取决于应力水平而非仅仅温度。

对 REL 超薄芯体本身（绒毛浆 + SAP 体系），本章无直接适用内容 — Chapter 8 has no bearing on fluff pulp, SAP or core absorbency.
