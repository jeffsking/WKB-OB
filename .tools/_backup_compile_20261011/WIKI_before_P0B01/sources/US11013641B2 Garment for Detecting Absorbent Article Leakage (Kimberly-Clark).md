# US11013641B2 Garment for Detecting Absorbent Article Leakage (Kimberly-Clark)

## 一句话定位

Granted US patent (Kimberly-Clark) on a **garment for detecting absorbent article leakage** — a leakage/monitoring accessory for diapers, paired in this batch with P&G's windowed-sensor article (US8273939B2) as a "sensor-equipped article" theme.

## 摘要

The patent discloses a garment for detecting absorbent article leakage and methods of detecting absorbent article leakage utilizing the same. The bibliographic header lists assignee Kimberly-Clark Worldwide, Inc., inventors Sullivan, Nhan, Strong, Long, and classifications A61F 13/42, 13/74, 13/84. It is the US national phase of PCT/US2017/026034 (WO2018/186842). Detailed claim language on the detection garment is in the full text; this summary captures the bibliographic and classification scope.
