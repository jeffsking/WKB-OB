# LMP-PP Resin Development Brief

## Positioning 一句话定位

This project brief establishes a three-tier target architecture and acceptance criteria for domestic fiber-grade low-melting-point polypropylene used in hygiene nonwovens.  
（中文：该任务书建立国产卫材用纤维级低熔点 PP 的三档目标架构与验收标准。）

## Summary 摘要

The technical principle is to introduce ethylene or C4–C10 α-olefin comonomers that interrupt isotactic PP sequences and reduce lamellar perfection. Plan A targets a conventional 150–158°C grade compatible with early line trials; Plan B targets a 135–150°C binder resin with higher comonomer loading and a controlled low-isotacticity fraction; Plan C is an experimental ≤135°C resin for the sheath of an all-PP bicomponent fiber. The brief identifies xylene-solubles growth, slow crystallization, surface tack, roll sticking, and thermal-setting collapse as central process risks. It therefore requires stronger quenching and re-optimization of drawing and heat-setting conditions. Acceptance combines melting point with fiber tenacity, elongation, and loaded thermal shrinkage rather than relying on resin data alone. It also warns that low-melt heat-seal film grades cannot be transferred directly to fiber applications because their MFR is generally too low.
