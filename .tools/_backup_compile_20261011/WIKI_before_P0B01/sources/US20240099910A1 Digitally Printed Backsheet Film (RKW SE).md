# US20240099910A1 Digitally Printed Backsheet Film (RKW SE)

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

**RKW SE 的美国申请公开**（US 2024/0099910 A1，2024-03-28）：一张**可透气、聚烯烃、填充并拉伸（filled & stretched）**的背层膜，把**胶黏剂整合进膜的某一层**，从而使**印刷层（优选数字印刷）可以直接落在该层上**。它同时给出两个可验收的门槛值：**WVTR ≥500 g/m²·24h（ASTM D6701-01）**与**耐水柱 >300 mm（EDANA WSP 80.6）**——是本库"背层既透气又耐水压"这一对矛盾的量化出处。

## Abstract 摘要

The application (title: "DIGITALLY PRINTED BACKSHEET FILM") relates to a breathable, polyolefinic, filled, stretched film comprising an imprint which is bonded to the film via an adhesive, to a method of producing it and to its use. **Breathability is achieved by adding hard fillers to the polymer matrix prior to extrusion and then stretching the extruded film**, giving a film that is impermeable to liquid yet permeable to water vapour; the specification links this to reduced skin irritation and counteraction of diaper dermatitis (self-drying effect). The acknowledged prior-reference film with these properties is **WO 2020/225165 A1**. The background also sizes up the incumbent printing route: **flexographic printing** is economical only for large print runs, because the cost and lead time of artistic development and tool setup are high and the repeat length is limited — hence the interest in a film amenable to flexible/digital printing of many designs. The stated object is a film with the **lowest possible specific weight per unit area**, that can be flexibly printed, is breathable, liquid-tight, mechanically suited as a backsheet even in the printed state, is **haptically pleasant, low-rustle, soft and supple**, and is suitable for lamination with a nonwoven. Structurally, the invention puts **the adhesive into at least one film layer**, so the imprint can be applied **directly** onto that layer. Claim 1 fixes the two acceptance numbers (WVTR ≥500 g/m²/24 h per ASTM D6701-01; water column >300 mm per EDANA WSP 80.6). Claim 10 is the independent method claim: prepare composition → extrude → stretch → **apply a print directly to a layer of the film**. Claim 11 covers use as a backsheet in disposable hygiene articles. Example 1 uses **80% Granic WAC-GCR Group** (an inorganic filler and adhesive: calcium oxide mixed with a PE copolymer) plus **20% EVA, Greenflex FL 65 (Versalis)** — an EVA with **VA content 28 wt%**, density 0.952 g/cm³, MFR 2.5 g/10 min (190 °C / 2.16 kg, ISO 1133); processing melts polymer and mineral filler in a compounding extruder well above the polymer melting temperature (e.g. above 200 °C).

## Key claims 关键论断

- **把"胶"做进膜里，印刷才有落点**——Claim 1 的落脚点不是"加了图案"，而是"膜有至少一层把胶黏剂整合在其中"，从而印刷可以直接压在这一层上。→ [[RKW SE]]
- **透气与耐水在这里是同时验收的两个数**：WVTR ≥500 g/m²·24h 与耐水柱 >300 mm **并列出现在同一权利要求**，说明两者都要满足而非二选一。→ [[WVTR 测试口径（失重法 vs 增重法）]]
- **两个胶黏剂方案可选**：EVA（5–50 wt%，VA 10–60 wt%）或金属氧化物/氧化钙（20–80 wt%）——后者本身就是填充剂与胶黏剂的二合一（Granic WAC 就是氧化钙 + PE 共聚物）。
- **出发点是把现有印刷方式的痛点（只对大批量经济）换掉**：朝向可灵活换版的数字/直接印刷，并且不许牺牲手感（低沙沙声、柔软）与与非织造布复合的能力。→ [[SMMMS Film-Free vs Microporous Film Backsheet（无膜背层 vs 微孔膜背层）]]
