# GB 5296.4-2012 Instructions for Use Part 4 Textiles and Apparel（消费品使用说明 第4部分 纺织品和服装）

## 一句话定位

China's **mandatory labeling standard** for textiles and apparel（GB 5296 系列第 4 部分）—— defines the seven mandatory label items and the durable-label rule; for this library it matters as the labeling regime for textile components (e.g. woven labels on elastic waistbands) while disposable hygiene products are explicitly out of scope.

## Summary 摘要

GB 5296.4-2012 is fully mandatory and governs instructions-for-use of textiles and apparel sold in China. Seven content items are compulsory: maker identity, product name, size/spec, fibre content (per FZ/T 01053), care symbols (per GB/T 8685), executed product standard, and GB 18401 safety class. The durable-label clause mandates that size, fibre content and care method live on a permanent label (with carve-outs for goods where such labels impair use, and for group-customized non-personal-care products). Appendix B turns labeling errors into countable defects — each missing mandatory item is one severe defect, as is inconsistency across label forms — which is how apparel QC auditors actually score labels. Appendix A excludes disposable products, footwear, toys, umbrellas, bags, cleaning cloths etc., so diaper/wipe packaging is governed by GB 38598 / product standards instead, and GB 31701's labeling requirements (安全类别 + 婴幼儿用品 wording) sit on top of this framework for children's textiles.
