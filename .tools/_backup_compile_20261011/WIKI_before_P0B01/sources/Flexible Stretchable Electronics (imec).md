# Flexible Stretchable Electronics (imec)

## One-line Position 一句话定位
An imec lecture page by Jan Vanfleteren presenting two industrializable flexible/stretchable-electronics technologies (UTCP ultra-thin packaging and meander-interconnect elastic circuits) for 2.5D/3D wearable and implantable needs.（imec 讲座，介绍两类可工业化柔性/可拉伸电子技术。）

## Summary 摘要
- Traditional electronics = packaged components on flat rigid PCB; growing demand now for 2.5D (free-form surfaces) and full 3D electronics.（从刚性PCB走向2.5D/3D电子。）
- Application drivers: comfort/ergonomics of wearable & implantable circuits, design aesthetics (2.5D/3D light sources), and eco factors (automotive material efficiency, lower CO₂).（驱动力：舒适、美学、生态。）
- UTCP (ultra-thin chip package): bare Si (or other inorganic semiconductor) chips thinned to 20–30 µm, embedded in a stack of spin-on polyimide layers with fan-out metallization → miniaturized, lightweight flexible package <100 µm total.（UTCP：芯片减薄至20–30 µm，埋入聚酰亚胺，总厚<100 µm。）
- Dynamically stretchable circuits: individual components or component islands interconnected by meander-shaped thin-film/PCB metal wirings, embedded in elastic polymers (PDMS silicone rubber or PU).（可拉伸电路：蛇形走线 + PDMS/PU弹性体封装。）
- The lecture covers process flow, reliability data, and applications of both technologies.（含工艺流、可靠性数据与应用。）

## Key Numbers & Conclusions 提炼数字与结论
| 项目 | 数值/结论 | 备注 |
|---|---|---|
| UTCP chip thinning | 20–30 µm bare Si | embedded in polyimide |
| UTCP total thickness | < 100 µm | fan-out metallization |
| Stretchable embedding | PDMS (silicone) or PU (polyurethane) | meander interconnects |
| Demand trend | 2.5D free-form → full 3D electronics | wearables/implants |

## Key Claims 关键论断
- UTCP ultra-thin packaging (<100 µm) and elastic embedding offer a viable route to integrate ultra-thin sensing chips onto nonwoven/film hygiene substrates.（UTCP超薄封装可为卫材贴肤传感芯片提供集成路线。）
- "Component-island + meander wiring + elastic encapsulation" prevents fracture under stretch — applicable to sensors on elastic waistbands.（元件岛+蛇形走线结构可避免拉伸断裂，适用于弹性腰围传感器。）
