# Chapter 44 — Dryer Feeding Systems

> Source: Jumah & Mujumdar, in Mujumdar (ed.), *Handbook of Industrial Drying*, 4th ed., CRC Press, 2014, Ch.44, pp.1017–1038 [页码范围待验证；正文引句页码 p.1017–1038 均已核对].

## 1. Overview 概述

Chapter 44 argues that the feeder and its storage bin are "an integral unit of the dryer system" and that feed consistency, dispersion, and rate control determine dryer performance. It gives selection guidelines anchored on bulk-solids flow behaviour (Carr's property table, material classes), classifies storage bins (funnel / mass / expanded flow) and feeder classes (volumetric vs gravimetric), details five feeder types (screw, vibrating, belt, rotary-table, rotary airlock), and closes with dryer-specific feeding: flash, fluid-bed, rotary, conveyor, spray, and drum. 给料一致性、分散性与计量精度决定干燥机表现——SAP 粉体/浆料计量输送的通用方法论。

Relevance to hygiene materials: ★★★☆☆ moderate — SAP 计量/给料是卫生用品产线的真实课题：料仓流型、失重式计量、旋转气锁、雾化器选型全部直接可迁移。

## 2. Key Claims 关键论点（原句摘录）

- "the bin or the storage tank and feeder should be regarded as an integral unit of the dryer system, and selected and designed as such. Without a properly designed bin to handle the wet solid, the feeder is useless." (p.1017)
- "Most dryers cannot accept feeds directly unless they are free flowing. A liquid needs the right range of viscosity, surface tension, and discrete size of particles that allow it to be pumped and, if necessary, sprayed." (p.1018)
- "physical testing of the feeder for the specific feedstock is essential; small changes in feed consistencies may, in some cases, change feeder characteristics significantly." (p.1018)
- Hygroscopic hazard (p.1020, Table 44.1): "Hygroscopic Indicates potential hazards to flowability, need for sealing and inert gas environment"; "Throat and gate dimensions should be at least two and one-half times the diameter of the largest particle." (p.1020)
- Mass-flow bin (p.1021): "The materials leave in a first-in, first-out manner, thus minimizing the problem of caking, degrading, or segregation during storage … Constant solids density at discharge, making it possible to get good solids control."
- Accuracy gap (p.1023, Table 44.4): volumetric "Feed-rate repeatable accuracy is +2% at best and is affected by variation in the bulk density of the material"; gravimetric "normally two to five times better than volumetric one irrespective of changes in bulk density or the occurrence of flow problems."
- Screw feeder for sticky feed (p.1023–1025): "Screw flights near the discharge are typically notched … double-bladed knife sections that cut up lumps"; jacketed screws possible; single-span ≤6 m.
- Vibrating feeder (p.1026): "Gentle handling of materials … abrasive or friable solids."
- Rotary airlock (p.1030): "Serves as gas pressure seal to pass solids from one pressure environment to another"; up to 150 kPa; "Suitable for feeding fine powders."
- Flash drying (p.1031–1032): feed mechanism must "supply nonlumpy wet solid … with a very controlled flow rate. Otherwise, the adjusted residence time in the dryer will be changed, resulting in off-specification of the product quality"; backmixing capital + operating cost "can be a substantial fraction of the overall flash-drying costs."
- Spray feed train (p.1034): "The feed strainer or filter is important because all matter likely to jeopardize the performance of the dryer through partial or total blockage of the atomizer must be removed. Demineralized water or clean condensate is usually required for use in cases of feed blockage. … The atomizer is the most important element in the spray-drying plant. Four types of atomizers are used … Rotary, Pressure nozzle, Pneumatic nozzle, Sonic nozzle."
- Conclusion (p.1038): "the full range of moisture content is tested as moisture-induced stickiness can cause severe operational problems."

## 3. Concept Deep-Dive 概念深挖

### 3.1 Mass-flow bin enables feeder accuracy 整体流料仓是计量精度的前提
Only a mass-flow bin (first-in-first-out, no dead zones, constant discharge density, p.1021) delivers the uniform bulk density a volumetric feeder needs. The three volumetric rules (p.1022) — uniform bulk density, uniform prefeed, precise element velocity — are exactly what a hygiene powder line must enforce for SAP/超吸水混配计量. Without them, "flow stoppages or the uncontrolled flooding of materials … compromise feeder accuracy" (p.1020).

### 3.2 Feeder selection for sticky/fine hygiene feeds 黏性与细粉给料选型
Notched/disintegrating screw flights and cut-and-folded/ribbon configurations handle sticky materials (p.1024–1025); rotary airlock is the gas-seal interface (≤150 kPa) for fine powders into pressurized dryers (p.1030) — 即 airlaid 成型器或闪干区的正确进料接口。

### 3.3 Dispersion/atomisation as the real process step 分散与雾化才是真正的工艺步骤
Flash dryers demand "very rapid, even dispersion of the wet material into the drying airstream" (p.1031); in spray plants the atomiser governs drop size and thus dried-particle size (p.1034) — 与 SAP 浆料/涂料液进入喷雾塔前的准备完全同构。

## 4. Hygiene Implication 卫材关联

- **H44-1 SAP/添加剂计量**: require a mass-flow silo + gravimetric feeder (2–5× more accurate than volumetric, p.1023) for SAP or superabsorbent-blend metering — 计量精度直接决定芯体吸收性能并避免批间漂移。
- **H44-2 浆料/涂料液流变窗口**: define and QC the viscosity/solids window of SAP slurry, coating liquor, and fragrance emulsion at the supplier interface; trend lot-to-lot — 决定产线能否运行的是流变，不是干燥机。
- **H44-3 雾化器前必过滤**: demand a micron-rated strainer and demineralised flush path on any spray or slot-die coating line — 雾化器堵塞是喷涂/喷雾线第一大故障。
- **H44-4 旋转气锁进料**: where SAP/fluff fines enter a pressurized airlaid former or flash zone, specify a rotary valve airlock (≤150 kPa gas seal) rather than an open chute.
- **H44-5 全水分域试车**: in supplier qualification, require feeder trials across the worst-case MC/viscosity envelope — 黏性失控出现在规格边缘。
- Caveat: accuracy figures are vendor-derived; no rheological treatment of non-Newtonian SAP slurry/latex `[待验证：+2%容积式精度为供应商声称值，未经独立验证]`. 无纤维互锁喂料（绒毛浆）讨论。
