# CN 115368497 B（一种纸尿裤生产用高吸水性树脂及其制备方法 / Diaper-grade SAP — Yixing Danson）

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

A granted Chinese invention patent (assignee: **宜兴丹森科技有限公司**, a SAP maker) on a **diaper-production SAP** with controlled extractables & surface-crosslink ratio to avoid oily/slick touch after long use. SAP composition/process → FTO-relevant.

## 摘要 / Abstract

The invention relates to a diaper-production SAP and its preparation: SAP-A 80–120 phr, organic solvent 100–200 phr, crosslinker aqueous solution 19–42 phr, reducing-agent aqueous solution 3–6 phr, with controlled temperature and added water, yielding a SAP with 4-h static extractables 0.7–2.5 wt% and excellent hand-feel; the ratio of 4-h static extractables to the 16-h stirred extractables of the pre-surface-crosslink SAP-A is 2–9. It solves crosslink-layer cracking / insufficient restriction of linear-polymer migration during swelling, thereby eliminating the oily/slick feel on touch after long-term diaper use.
