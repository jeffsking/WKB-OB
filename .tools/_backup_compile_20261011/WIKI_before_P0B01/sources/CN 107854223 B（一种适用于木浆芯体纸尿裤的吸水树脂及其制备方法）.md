# CN 107854223 B（一种适用于木浆芯体纸尿裤的吸水树脂及其制备方法 / SAP for fluff-pulp-core diapers — Lolle）

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

A granted Chinese invention patent (assignee: **露乐健康科技(广州)有限公司 / Lolle**) on a **SAP specifically for fluff-pulp-core diapers**, with in-situ nano-SiO2 to raise permeability/diffusion and low rewet. SAP composition/process → FTO-relevant.

## 摘要 / Abstract

The invention discloses an SAP for fluff-pulp cores and its preparation: acrylic acid is mixed with deionised water and neutralised with NaOH; sodium silicate, acrylamide, crosslinker and initiator are added to give a milky solution; under N2, UV polymerisation yields a transparent gel; the gel is granulated, dried, milled to powder; the powder is stirred with sodium aluminate and surface-treated with a treatment liquid. Nano-SiO2 generated during neutralisation disperses uniformly, raising liquid permeability & diffusion; the SAP has low residual monomer, small rewet, large water retention, and high/loose particle strength after swelling — especially suited to fluff-pulp-core diapers.
