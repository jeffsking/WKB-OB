# Spouted Bed Drying (Ch.14)

> Source: Mujumdar (ed.), Handbook of Industrial Drying, 4th ed., CRC Press, 2014, Ch.14, pp.361–382 (Pallai, Szentmarjay, Mujumdar).

## Chapter Overview 章节概览

Chapter 14 treats the spouted bed dryer (SBD) as a particle-processing technology for materials too coarse to fluidize and, more importantly, for heat-sensitive pastes, slurries, suspensions, and synthetic products dried on a bed of inert particles. Arc: principle/device → residence-time distribution (RTD) → agricultural drying results → pulp/paste drying on inert particles → development (microwave-coupled MSB) → assessment. Its engineering center is the mechanically spouted bed (MSB) with inert packing: wet feed coats large inert particles, dries in a thin film at low wet-bulb temperature, then wears off as powder.

> Note: page numbers derived from PDF-page − 21; spot-check against print edition.

## Key Viewpoints 关键观点

### 1. Short dwell + inert particles = heat-sensitive drying 短驻留+惰性粒子

> "Because of the short dwell time in the spout, SBDs can be used to dry heat-sensitive solids, and, using inert solids as the bed material, SBDs have been used successfully to dry pastes, slurries, and heat-sensitive materials." (p.361)

### 2. Draft tube removes the scale-up ceiling 气升管解除放大上限

> "The classic or conventional spouted bed (CSB) is a cylindrical vessel with a conical bottom fitted with an inlet nozzle for the introduction of the spouting air … This device suffers from limited capacity due to the maximum spoutable bed height and inability to scale up the apparatus beyond 1-m diameter. The introduction of a hollow, tall vertical tube (draft tube) … eliminates the former restriction by acting as a pneumatic conveyor." (p.362)

### 3. Thin-film inert-particle mechanism 薄层干燥机理

> "The wet solid distributed on the large surface of the inert particles forms a thin layer in which a very short drying process occurs. Since the inert particles provide a large contact surface, the heat and mass transfer processes of drying are short even at relatively low wet bulb temperature. Therefore, moisture diffusion resistance in solid can be considered negligible …" (p.374)

The product sees low wet-bulb T, not the 120–190 °C inlet gas. CTD (⁶⁰Co tracer) shows an inert particle spends ~70% of its cycle in the sliding layer, 15–20% in the intensive zone, ~2–10 s in the drying zone (p.375).

### 4. Explicit polymer data point: PVC 聚合物数据点

> "Initial moisture content 60% m/m; Temperature of drying air Inlet 120°C, Outlet 60°C; Final moisture content 0.5% m/m; Specific energy consumption 9000 kJ/kg; Specific rate of evaporation 6.4 kg/m²h." (Table 14.21, p.377)

The high specific energy is justified because the product "saved its primary grain size" — grinding avoided; a quality/energy trade-off directly informative for SAP powder production.

### 5. Suited to synthetic products with bound moisture 适用于合成产品

> "SBDs are especially suited for drying of heat-sensitive materials with surface moisture, as well as those with bound moisture, such as seeds, foodstuffs, pharmaceuticals, and synthetic products, in one or two stages." (p.380)

For bound moisture (plastics), tangential air inlet + inner transport screw let volumetric gas velocity be adjusted "regardless of the gas velocity requirement for particle motion" (p.381).

### 6. Controllable residence time prevents overheating 可控驻留防过热

> "Intense particle motion: Good particle mixing prevents localized overheating and ensures the product's uniform moisture content … The residence time of particles may be changed and regulated within very wide limits …" (p.380)

## Key Terms & Process Details 关键术语

- Mechanically spouted bed (MSB): central conveyor screw ensures circulation, controls RTD (p.365).
- Inert-particle drying partial processes: coating → drying → wearing (p.375).
- Performance anchors (Tables 14.8–14.24, kJ/kg water): wheat 3360, corn 2884–3868, potato pulp 3025, yeast 3000–3500, paprika 4091–6158, PVC 9000, CaCO₃ 9715; specific evaporation 100–400 kg water/m²h achievable while keeping solid below damage temperature.
- Inert particles can themselves be polymers: polypropylene beads (ρ 820 kg/m³, d 3.9×10⁻³ m) and Hostaform (p.377).
- Temperature ceiling example (seeds for sowing): "the temperature must not exceed 40–45 °C … In the temperature range between 46 and 50 °C, the aleuron cells form an almost closed, elastic skin in which the diffusion of the moisture is very slow." (p.367)

## Strengths & Weaknesses 优劣评估

- Strengths: explicit heat-sensitive + synthetic-product framing (p.380–381); concrete specific-energy data across many materials; clear coating→drying→wearing mechanism with CTD evidence; motion/airflow decoupling principle (p.381).
- Weaknesses: "there are few large-scale industrial applications" (p.382) — mostly lab/pilot data; scale-up relations "cannot be applied to MSB dryers with inert packing" (p.365); no fluff/SAP/nonwoven data — relevance by analogy; 9000–9715 kJ/kg for PVC/CaCO₃ reflects the inert-bed premium; OCR noise in equations/tables [待验证 against print].

## Hygiene Implication 卫材关联

Relevance: ★★★☆☆ — moderate: genuine polymer/heat-sensitive-powder content (PVC, PP, Hostaform, "synthetic products") directly transferable to SAP-slurry and binder-emulsion drying, with the honest gap that no fluff/SAP data exist.

- Gentle powder route: when drying SAP gel or binder emulsion to powder, mimic the thin-film-on-inert-particle principle (p.374) — keep product at wet-bulb T, not inlet-gas T — to avoid crosslink scission/case-hardening; benchmark energy at ~3000–9000 kJ/kg water.
- Quality ceiling temperature: adopt the seed rule logic (p.367) — define a max-particle-temperature window (not just inlet-air T) for heat-sensitive nonwoven binders and lotion/fragrance coatings.
- Grinding-avoidance premium: PVC's 9000 kJ/kg buys retained grain size (p.377); for SAP grades where attrition/PSD must be controlled, quantify higher specific energy against PSD spec.
- Scale-up honesty: no textbook scale-up factor for MSB with inert packing (p.365, p.382) — a hygiene production line would need pilot validation [待验证].
