# General Conclusions and Appendixes

> Capstone chapter of the thesis *Liquid Crystalline Polymers for Smart Applications*. Consolidates the two research threads (photoresponsive polyesters / oriented ion-transport membranes) under the "smart applications" objective and records the methodological caveat on EIS.

## One-line Position 一句话定位

The thesis capstone: it consolidates both platforms (photoresponsive LCP polyesters for controlled release; oriented columnar membranes for selective, humidity-independent proton transport) and states the EIS-vs-I–V measurement caveat.
（论文收官章：收束两大平台（光响应液晶聚酯用于控释；取向柱状膜用于选择性、湿度无关质子传输），并给出 EIS 与 I–V 表征的方法学警示。）

## Summary 摘要(3-6句忠实原文)

- Two technical platforms close six chapters under the unified "smart applications" goal: photoresponsive main-chain LCP polyesters and columnar/ordered ion-transport hybrid membranes.（两大平台在"智能应用"总目标下收束六章：光响应主链液晶聚酯与柱状/有序离子传输杂化膜。）
- Photoisomerization is the controlled-release engine: α-methylstilbene main-chain polyesters undergo UV-triggered Z↔E isomerization causing backbone contraction, releasing vanillin from microcapsules that are dark-stable.（光异构是控释引擎：含 α-甲基芪的主链聚酯经 UV 触发 Z↔E 异构致主链收缩，从而在暗态稳定的微胶囊中释放香兰素。）
- First report of high-temperature membrane isomerization: Z→E photoisomerization of α-methylstilbene derivatives in a polymeric membrane at 180 °C was first reported, monitored in situ via POM texture changes.（首次报道膜态高温异构：180 °C 下聚合物膜中 α-甲基芪衍生物的 Z→E 光异构首次被报道，并以 POM 织构变化原位监控。）
- Ordered orientation yields excellent ion transport: baked homeotropic modified poly(epichlorohydrine) gives columnar proton channels of 10⁻²–10⁻³ S/cm, independent of relative humidity.（有序取向带来卓越离子传输：焙烧垂直取向的改性聚环氧氯丙烷提供 10⁻²–10⁻³ S/cm、与相对湿度无关的质子电导。）
- Ordered nanowire arrays enable selective transport: columnar mesophase + polymeric nanowires (~225 columns/pore) in AAO give selective proton transport, methanol crossover 2 orders below Nafion 117.（有序纳米线阵列实现选择性传输：AAO 内柱状介相与聚合物纳米线（约 225 根/孔）实现选择性质子传输，甲醇渗透比 Nafion 117 低两个数量级。）
- Methodological caveat: for polyamine hybrids, EIS proton conductivity is unreliable; use current–voltage curves.（方法学警示：对聚胺杂化材料，EIS 质子电导不可靠，须用电流–电压曲线。）

## Key Claims 关键论断

- Both LCP platforms realize the thesis "smart applications" goal (controlled release + selective humidity-independent transport) — support page: [[Liquid Crystalline Polymers Smart Applications]].
- EIS is unreliable for polyamine hybrids; I–V is the correct method — see [[Liquid Crystalline Polymeric Wires for Selective Proton Transport, Part 2]] and the polyester platform in [[Synthesis and Characterization of a New Family of Photoactive Liquid Crystalline Polyesters based on α-methylstilbene]] / [[Applying Nature's Genius to Discotic Liquid-crystal Polymers for Proton Conducting Membrane]].
