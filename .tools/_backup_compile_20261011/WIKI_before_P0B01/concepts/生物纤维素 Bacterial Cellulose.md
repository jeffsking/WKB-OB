# 生物纤维素 Bacterial Cellulose
> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 2 citing sources (+ adjacent vault pages). All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义
Bacterial cellulose (BC, 细菌纤维素) is cellulose produced by bacteria, forming a **high-purity, highly crystalline, hydrophilic nanoscale fibre network** — obtained without the lignin-removal burden of plant cellulose.（生物纤维素（BC/BNC）是由细菌合成的纤维素，具有高纯度、高结晶度、亲水性的纳米纤维网络，且**无需脱木质素**。）
Drying a BC hydrogel is defined as the **controlled removal of liquid while managing capillary stress, ice templating, fibre bonding and pore preservation** to reach a target thermal–mechanical property set.（BC 气凝胶干燥 = 在液体脱除过程中控制毛细应力、冰模板、纤维结合与孔结构保持，以获得目标热—力性能组合。）（源：[[Bacterial Cellulose Aerogel Drying（细菌纤维素气凝胶干燥）]]）
> ⚠️ **Naming note 口径说明**: the cited pages treat BC as one member of the nanocellulose family (CNC / CNF / BNC / bagasse nanocellulose). Figures below that come from **plant-CNF or CMC systems** are labelled *adjacent*, not BC.（源：[[纳米纤维素（CNC、CNF、BNC）]]）

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理
- **Three drying routes（三条干燥路线）**: (1) **capillary-stress** — ambient/hot-air/oven drying passes through a liquid–gas interface, and capillary pressure can pull nanofibres together, raising density and reducing swelling/gas permeability; (2) **sublimation** — freeze-drying first immobilises water as ice then sublimes it, with ice-crystal size and orientation templating the final pore architecture; (3) **critical-point** — supercritical CO₂ drying avoids the interface. Freezing rate and direction matter.（源：[[Bacterial Cellulose Aerogel Drying（细菌纤维素气凝胶干燥）]]）
- **Freeze-drying preserves pores but not necessarily the highest mechanical strength（冻干保孔，但不必然带来最高机械强度）**; pre-freezing rate and architecture are part of the drying process window.（源：[[Effect of Drying Methods on Bacterial Cellulose Aerogel]]）
- **Chemical route to a cellulose superabsorbent（纤维素超吸水材料的化学路线）**: DS fixes the solubility ceiling, cross-link density fixes the swelling ceiling — DS ≈ 0.4 is the binary switch; because a real core is compressed, the cross-link optimum shifts higher than free-swell testing implies; **burst-fibre is a structural fix for gel blocking**.（源：[[Cross-linked Cellulose and Its Derivatives]]）

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联
- **Cellulose *is* the core absorbent chemistry（纤维素就是芯体吸收化学）**: fluff pulp（绒毛浆）is cellulose; regenerated cellulose fibres (viscose, acetate, Lyocell) are topsheet / distribution-layer inputs; CMC and cellulose derivatives are SAP co-absorbents.（源：[[Seymour Polymer Chemistry Ch10 Naturally Occurring Polymers]]）
- **Fibre surfaces are the pore walls（纤维表面即孔壁）**: in capillary sorption, absorbency is a surface-and-geometry problem — the pore network, not the polymer, is the absorber.（源：[[Fibers and Fibrous Materials]]）
- **Nanocellulose–SAP adjacency（纳米纤维素—SAP 邻接）**: cellulose nanofibre / CMC-Na composite absorbent compositions are patented by hygiene majors (e.g. Kao, CN 113423778 B), and bagasse-pith nanocellulose composites grafted onto cationic cellulose ether form an adjacent bio-based route.（源：[[CN 113423778 B（吸水性组合物及其制造方法·花王）]]）；（源：[[CN 104004521 B（一种蔗髓纳米纤维素基复合保水剂的制备方法）]]）
