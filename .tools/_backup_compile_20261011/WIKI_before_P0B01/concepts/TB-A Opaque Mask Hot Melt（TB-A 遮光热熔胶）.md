# TB-A Opaque Mask Hot Melt（TB-A 遮光热熔胶）

> One-line definition 一句话定义：A stiff, highly filled white hot-melt ink whose job is to be a **rigid shutter** — it hides a printed under-colour when relaxed and cannot follow the panel as the laminate stretches, so the colour appears by area. Every component is already qualified somewhere in a diaper.
> （中文：一种刚性、高填充的白色热熔墨，职责是充当**刚性快门**——松弛时遮住底印颜色，拉伸时无法跟随面板，使颜色按面积显露。其全部组分均已在纸尿裤某处通过验证。）

## Mechanism / Structure 机理 / 结构拆解

1. **The whole formulation exists to hit one number（整个配方只为一件事）**：2,500–3,500 mPa·s at the die (design 2,900 ± 400 mPa·s @ 160 °C), so it runs on a standard diaper hot-melt system.
2. **Stiffness is the mechanism, not a property（刚性即机理）**：the α-methylstyrene resin raises the film modulus to ≈ 85 MPa so the mask does not stretch with the panel. **The α-methylstyrene level, not the pigment level, is the release-critical parameter** — if the mask softens toward the panel modulus it stretches with the substrate and the bars stop appearing.
3. **Hiding power must be complete（遮盖力须彻底）**：rutile TiO₂ (d50 0.28 µm, alumina/silica surface-treated, supplied as a **pre-dispersed masterbatch**) carries the whole mask function; precipitated CaCO₃ (d50 0.8 µm, stearate-coated) is extender and matting agent — it kills gloss so the mask reads flat white.
4. **Deliberately absent（刻意排除）**：optical brighteners (migration risk), azo pigments (aromatic amine cleavage), phthalate plasticisers, and any heavy-metal chromophore. The mask is white on purpose — all colour lives in the supplier's flexo under-print, where it is already tox-cleared.
5. **Backing chemistry（底层化学）**：APAO (propene-rich, Mw ≈ 40 kDa, 8,000 mPa·s @ 190 °C) anchors to PP spunbond facing without a primer; a hydrogenated C5 tackifier wets out onto the fibre crowns within the 0.4 s open time; FT wax (mp 105 °C, narrow MWD, < 0.5 % oil) cuts die viscosity, sets in < 0.5 s and prevents block on the roll.
6. **Tox package is "nothing new"（毒理包＝「无新增」）**：placement is garment-facing outer surface only, outside the absorbent core, no component within 15 mm of the leg cuff or waist edge, and the pigment is fully bound in the binder (the primary low-migration lever).

（中文：①配方目标只有一个数——模头处 2,500–3,500 mPa·s（设计 2,900±400 @160 ℃），以兼容标准纸尿裤热熔系统；②刚性即机理——α-甲基苯乙烯树脂把膜模量抬到约 85 MPa，**α-甲基苯乙烯含量（而非颜料含量）才是放行关键参数**；③遮盖力须彻底——金红石 TiO₂（d50 0.28 µm、铝硅表面处理、以预分散母粒形态供应）承担全部遮盖功能，沉淀 CaCO₃（d50 0.8 µm、硬脂酸包覆）作填充与消光剂，使掩膜呈平光白；④刻意排除——荧光增白剂（迁移风险）、偶氮颜料（芳香胺裂解）、邻苯二甲酸酯增塑剂、任何重金属发色团；白色是刻意的，颜色全部放在已被毒理放行的供应商柔印底印中；⑤底层化学——APAO（富丙烯、Mw≈40 kDa）无需底涂即可锚定 PP 纺粘面层，氢化 C5 增粘树脂在 0.4 s 开放时间内润湿纤维冠，FT 蜡（熔点 105 ℃）降黏、<0.5 s 固化并防卷绕粘连；⑥毒理包「无新增」——仅用于贴身外侧、位于吸收芯之外、距腿围与腰缘 15 mm 以上，颜料完全包裹在黏结剂中。）

## Lessons 经验教训

> ✅ Verification（2026-09-29，来源 [[Stretch-Triggered Colour Indicator for Diaper Pant Side Panels]]、[[Mechanochromic Waistband Tension Indicator Proposal（2026-08-19）]]）：两份文档对 TB-A 给出**同一配方（7 组分、同 wt %）与同一关键指标**（膜模量 85 MPa、模量比 ≥35:1、对比度 ≥0.86），互证一致；R5 风险条目亦指向同一控管（TiO₂-free 变体预合格 + 只买母粒）。

- **教训一（认知）**：**"安全"的论据是"没有新东西"**——APAO、氢化树脂、FT 蜡、金红石 TiO₂、包覆 CaCO₃ 今天都已存在于纸尿裤结构胶与印刷底膜中；这不是"低毒"，是"已在使用清单内"。
- **教训二（规格）**：控制刚度要控 **α-甲基苯乙烯树脂含量**，不是控颜料含量。
- **教训三（采购）**：TiO₂ 的监管状态近年多次变动，且吸入危害针对**自由粉体**而非膜内结合颜料；两道控制——只买预分散母粒 + 保持无 TiO₂ 配方并行合格；上市决策前须复核当时的分类结论。
- **待补 `[待验证]`**：TB-A 在 155 °C 下超过 48 h 的黏度漂移；在 40 °C/48 h/5 kPa 之外更严苛仓储条件下的抗粘连数据。
