# ES 纤维 ES Fiber

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 3 citing sources. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义
**ES fibre = PP/PE sheath-core bicomponent staple fibre**（PP/PE 皮芯复合短纤维）used to build air-through (hot-air bonded) nonwovens（源：[[ES 纤维热风非织造布驻极性能初探]]）. The PE sheath gives a low-melting bonding surface while the PP core retains stiffness/structure.

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理
- **Hydrophilic finish anchoring 亲水油剂锚定**：the PE sheath is non-polar, so the surfactant adsorbs only by **dispersion forces**; larger hydrophobic groups / longer chains give stronger anchoring and better rinse (multi-insult) durability（源：[[ES Fibre Hydrophilic Function and Applications]] §1）.
- **Two finish classes 油剂两类**：single-hydrophilic (扩散型, weak adhesion, excellent 1st strike, fast decay, spreading) vs multi-hydrophilic (strong adhesion, good multi-strike, point-penetration wetting)（源：同上）.
- **Electret mechanism 驻极机理**：space charge is trapped at the crystalline-phase interface / ordered crystalline regions (high-density PE = high charge stability); polarization charge arises from the **Maxwell-Wagner effect enhanced at the PP/PE interface**（源：[[ES 纤维热风非织造布驻极性能初探]] §1）. Finer linear density and higher areal density improve electret performance（源：同上）.
- **Electret limitation 局限**：ES air-through charge-storage stability is lower than PP meltblown; residual antistatic / water-absorbing finish releases charge（源：同上）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联
- ES air-through is the mainstream fibre behind **topsheet / ADL** constructions; the multi-hydrophilic route is the "mainstream material and development direction for premium disposable hygiene products"（源：[[ES Fibre Hydrophilic Function and Applications]] §4）.
- Finished-diaper validation: single-hydrophilic topsheet rewet 3.89–6.17 g vs multi-hydrophilic 0.84–1.32 g; imported premium benchmark 1.06 g (embossed) / 1.36 g (perforated)（源：同上 §3）.
- Referenced national limits: first absorption ≤50 s, second ≤100 s, rewet ≤15 g（源：同上 Table 1）.
- Elastomer-modified ES route gives ultra-soft, low-modulus air-through webs while keeping the heat-bonding window（源：[[ES 双组分弹性纤维热风布（弹性体改性）]]）.
