## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理
- **Emission source（排放来源）**: formaldehyde emission is associated with **UF (urea-formaldehyde) resin** adhesives; the F/U molar ratio is the most critical formulation variable.（源：[[Adhesive Handbook Part 4 Applications]]）
- **Durability caveat（耐久性警示）**: the amino-methylene bond hydrolyses, making UF bonds **unstable under high humidity / high temperature** — the condition set most relevant to emission risk.（源：[[Adhesive Handbook Part 4 Applications]]）

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联
- **Cross-sector compliance mapping（跨行业合规映射）**: the E1 logic (indoor exposure, infant risk preference) is flagged as a direct analogue for **the VOC and odour compliance requirements of diapers and baby wipes**, and as a key input for compliance-driven strategic purchasing.（源：[[Adhesive Handbook Part 4 Applications]]）；（源：[[Adhesive Handbook]]）
- **UF/湿强剂 chemistry adjacency（UF/湿强剂化学邻接）**: UF / melamine wet-strength-resin crosslinking chemistry and cure kinetics are treated in the same chapter, so the E1 regulatory frame is adjacent to wet-strength-resin R&D.（源：[[Adhesive Handbook Part 4 Applications]]）
