# 莱赛尔 Lyocell

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 10 citing sources. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

**Lyocell / Tencel is a regenerated cellulose fibre spun from a direct solvent, NMMO (N-methylmorpholine-N-oxide)** — a route that **omits xanthation and ripening**, such that *viscose's* fibre strength and wet modulus are exceeded, while it **retains a propensity for fibrillation**, verbatim *"propensity for some degree of fibrillation"*（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch17 Rayon Fibres]]）。Property comparisons are tabulated there as Table 17.12 (Camilon) and Table 17.14 (Tencel)（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch17 Rayon Fibres]]）。

中文：莱赛尔/天丝是用 NMMO 直接溶剂纺丝的再生纤维素纤维；省去黄化与熟成，强度与湿模量超越传统粘胶，但存在原纤化倾向（原文 p.512）。

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

**Solvent route.** Lyocell sits under **direct solvent spinning (MMNO/NMMO)**, which needs no derivatisation — unlike the viscose process — and that is why its strength and wet modulus exceed viscose's; the same property set brings the fibrillation liability（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch17 Rayon Fibres]]）。

**Fibrillation mechanism and its two fixes.** The cause is intrinsic: the fibre has **no skin layer** and high crystallinity/orientation, so **water plus friction peels the surface open into fine fibrils**; it is most visible **wet** — invisible dry — so validation must be a **post-wash fabric comparison**（源：[[原纤化]]）。Two routes exist: (a) **intrinsic** cross-linking to lock the structure (Lenzing **A100** is the cross-linked type); (b) **process-side management** — tension/friction control in spinning-weaving plus enzyme treatment in finishing. Route (b) is cheaper and flexible but **batch-variable** — changing dye house or vessel can change the result（源：[[原纤化]]）。

**Hydroentanglement behaviour.** Viscose/Lyocell have **low wet modulus and entangle cheaply**, whereas PP/PET need hydrophilic finishes; and **high jet impact force unlocks the Lyocell-fibrillation microfibre route**（源：[[Mechanical bonding]]）。

中文：莱赛尔属直接溶剂纺丝（NMMO），无需衍生化，故强度与湿模量高于粘胶，同时带来原纤化责任；原纤化成因是「无皮层 + 高结晶/取向 + 水与摩擦」，湿态最明显，验证须水洗后比对；根治靠本体交联（A100），管理靠工艺端张力/摩擦控制与酶处理（便宜但批次波动）。水刺中粘胶/莱赛尔低湿模量易缠结，高冲击力可解锁莱赛尔微纤化路线。

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **Trilobal Lyocell for LSN spunlace（芯包/水刺用）.** Trilobal Lyocell is a named carrier for the fibre cross-section that creates axial grooves → a quasi-continuous low-resistance distribution path along MD, for the LSN hydroentangled nonwoven; it maps to project **OI-08「Trilobal Lyocell for LSN」**, targeting wet strength and hydrophilic channels（源：[[三叶形截面纤维（Trilobal Fibre）]]）。Concrete batch evidence: the COCO V6 Summary adds **LSN spunlace containing trilobal Lyocell** as a core-wrap material (6 km, arriving Tianjin 9/20)（源：[[COCO V6 试产原料准备会（Summary 版）]]）。See [[芯包材料对比（LSN MPM vs PSN MPM）]]、[[水刺 Hydroentanglement]]。
- **C1 liner DOE "best surface area + feel", highest cost.** Lenzing trilobal Lyocell was selected as the **C1 "best solution"** upper-layer shaped fibre (2D 10% Lyocell + 90% ES), rated *best surface area and feel* but *highest cost*; it is the **only cellulosic route** among C1–C4 (the other three are polyester/polyolefin)（源：[[兰精集团]]、[[Diffusion-Enhanced Topsheet C1-C4 (Cost-Performance-Supply Chain)]]）。See [[KC Diffusion-Enhanced Topsheet v3 DOE Plan (C1-C4 Liner DOE)]]。
- **Entanglement economics.** Lyocell/viscose are the cheap-to-entangle fibres in a spunlace line（源：[[Mechanical bonding]]）。
- **Supply-chain risk is real.** The vault documents a Lenzing A100 shortage (model changeover: LF 2.0 in 2023, HV100 launched Shanghai 2026-03, still in brand sampling), with domestic替代 led by Sateri; procurement must name the exact model/spec, not just "Tencel"（源：[[天丝为什么突然这么难买]]）。See [[兰精莱赛尔与国产莱赛尔对比]]、[[赛得利]]。
- **Contract-with-fluff-pulp.** Regenerated-cellulose wet-strength behaviour reads across to fluff pulp, the core basis for why SAP + bicomponent thermal-bond reinforcement are needed（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch17 Rayon Fibres]]）。See [[绒毛浆（Fluff Pulp）]]。
