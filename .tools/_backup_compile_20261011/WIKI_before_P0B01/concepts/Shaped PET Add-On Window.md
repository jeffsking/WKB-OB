# Shaped PET Add-On Window 异形 PET 添加窗口

> **Definition 定义**：The dosage range in which one-component profiled (shaped) PET improves wicking and MD distribution **without** destroying bonding, softness or converting stability — for a 25 gsm air-through-bonded topsheet the practical window is narrow, roughly **5–15 wt%**, with **10–12 wt%** as the working start.（单组分异形 PET 在提升芯吸与 MD 铺展、同时不破坏粘合、柔软度与转化稳定性的**剂量区间**；对 25 gsm 穿透热风粘合面层，实用窗口较窄，约 **5–15 wt%**，**10–12 wt%** 为常用起点。）

## Why the window exists 窗口为何存在

1. **Shaped PET carries no low-melt sheath（异形 PET 无低熔点皮层）**：every bond point in an air-through-bonded web must come from the ES fraction, and PE-to-PET adhesion is only about **half** of PE-to-PE (`k ≈ 0.55`). Bond count therefore falls **faster** than the ES weight fraction does.（ATB 网中所有粘合点须由 ES 组分提供，PE-to-PET 粘合效率仅约 PE-to-PE 的一半（k≈0.55）。粘合点下降速度**快于** ES 重量占比的下降。）
2. **Benefit saturates, risk accelerates（收益饱和、风险加速）**：MD-distribution benefit saturates after ~15%, while bond/softness risk keeps rising. Hence "20% is an upper stress-test, not a launch candidate".（MD 铺展收益在约 15% 后饱和，而粘合/手感风险持续上升——「20% 是上限压力测试，不是量产候选」。）
3. **Fibre stiffness is quadratically punishing（刚度惩罚呈四次方）**：bending stiffness ∝ E·d⁴; PET modulus (≈4 GPa) is roughly a decade above the PE sheath (≈0.3 GPa), and trilobal lobes sit far from the neutral axis, adding a further **1.3–1.6×** at equal denier. Skin-side exposure is felt, not just measured.（弯曲刚度 ∝ E·d⁴；PET 模量约 4 GPa 比 PE 皮层约 0.3 GPa 高一个数量级，三叶叶瓣远离中性轴，同旦数下再增 1.3–1.6 倍。贴肤侧暴露是"感受得到"的，不只是"测得出"的。）
4. **Placement beats dosage（层位优于剂量）**：the recommended build puts **0% at the skin** and concentrates the profiled fraction in the core-side strata — softness is protected by construction rather than by blend compromise.（推荐构造在**贴肤层放 0%**，把异形纤维集中在芯体侧各层——柔软度靠结构而不是靠配比折衷来保护。）

## Lessons learned 经验教训

> ✅ **Validation（2026-09-29）**：本批四份来源的添加量建议落在**同一量级**（10% / 10–20% / 15% / 26%），且四份都把上限理由指向同一个机理——**粘合点与手感**（[[Pentagram PET Fiber Add-On for 25 gsm ATB Diaper Liner (Deep Dive)]]、[[Pentagram-Free Shaped PET Alternatives for 25 gsm Diaper Topsheet]]、[[ATB Topsheet with Bico Spunbond for 600 mpm Ultrasonic Lamination]]、[[Trilobal PET for MD Distribution in 45 gsm Dual-Card ATB Liner]]）。与既有 [[三叶形截面纤维（Trilobal Fibre）]] 页"配比的两难"表述一致。

- **要点**：窗口的**上限由粘合与手感决定，下限由收益决定**；提高克重或改用多梳理分层可以抬高上限（45 gsm 方案可到 26%），但同一条上限逻辑不变。
- **待补 `[待验证]`**：25 gsm 与 45 gsm 之外的克重/线速组合下的窗口；异形纤维价格带对"成本上限 ≤20%"的约束力。
