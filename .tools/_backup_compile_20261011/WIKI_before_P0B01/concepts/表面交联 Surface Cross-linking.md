# 表面交联 Surface Cross-linking
> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 5 citing sources. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义
`定义待补（引用来源未给出明确定义）` — the vault does not carry a standalone definition sentence. Surface cross-linking is evidenced **through its process pages** as a **post-treatment that builds a highly cross-linked shell on the SAP particle surface**, distinct from the internal (bulk) cross-linking of the network（源：[[SAP（高吸水树脂）]]、[[SAP 表面包覆（SAP Particle Surface Coating）]]）.

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理
- Two-stage logic: the aggregate is made by aqueous/suspension polymerisation, then **surface cross-linking** is applied as post-treatment in the sequence 后处理（表面交联）→ 包装（源：[[SAP（高吸水树脂）]]）.
- Coating variants share the same goal — deposit the cross-linker (or inorganic powder) **below the product-bed surface** in a mixer, by liquid spray (CN 101641398 B) or gas-dispersed particulate solid (CN 101679760 B)（源：[[SAP 表面包覆（SAP Particle Surface Coating）]]）.
- The central design tension is **Vortex vs AUL**: fast uptake needs small particle / shallow surface cross-linking, high AUL needs deep cross-linking. The stated resolution is a **surface cross-linking gradient** — low internal cross-link (fast uptake) + high outer cross-link (retention) — which is the core advantage of the kettle process（源：[[SAP Four Core Metrics Breakdown（SAP 四大核心指标拆解 CRC_AUL_GBP_Vortex）]]）.
- Post-treatment coupling: adding liquid lowers solids content, so a **second drying step is unavoidable**; the temperature–time window here is where surface cross-linking is (or is not) completed（源：[[SAP 表面包覆（SAP Particle Surface Coating）]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联
- Surface cross-linking is the **main process lever for GBP** — the "hidden boss" metric that governs whether later voids leak after the SAP has gelled ("前几次不漏，后面必漏")（源：[[SAP Four Core Metrics Breakdown（SAP 四大核心指标拆解 CRC_AUL_GBP_Vortex）]]、[[Gel Blocking（凝胶阻塞）]]）.
- It also sets dust and flowability in the core-forming line（源：[[SAP 表面包覆（SAP Particle Surface Coating）]]）.
- Supplier capability note: 诺尔 has three post-stage surface cross-linking routes and can run ICP surface cross-linking, adding powders and metal cations — a stated sourcing differentiator（源：[[诺尔（Nuoer）]]）.
