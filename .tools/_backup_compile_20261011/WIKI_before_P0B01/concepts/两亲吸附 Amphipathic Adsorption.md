# 两亲吸附 Amphipathic Adsorption

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 2 citing sources. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

Adsorption at an interface driven by the **amphipathic (dual hydrophilic–hydrophobic) nature of the molecule**. The source defines surfactant function by outcome, not structure: "High interfacial adsorption at low bulk concentration is what makes a molecule a surfactant; not every amphiphile qualifies"（源：[[The Role of Surfactants]]）.

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

- **Interfacial partitioning.** An amphiphile partitions to the interface and lowers the interfacial free energy; below the CMC the surface is populated by monomers, and near the CMC interfacial adsorption saturates（源：[[The Role of Surfactants]]）.
- **Substrate dependence.** On a non-polar polyolefin surface (the PE sheath of an ES fibre) there is no strong charge or polar adsorption — anchoring is by dispersion forces that scale with the size of the hydrophobic group and chain length（源：[[ES Fibre Hydrophilic Function and Applications]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **Fluff-pulp treatment and core-layer chemistry.** The surfactant chapter builds the chain surfactant → interfacial adsorption → free-energy change → wetting/penetration kinetics → macroscopic absorbency, directly actionable for fluff-pulp and core chemistry（源：[[The Role of Surfactants]]）.
- **Durable topsheet hydrophilicity.** Design logic for ES-fibre finishes: point penetration with a small wetted area on the skin-contact side yields a drier topsheet; durable multi-insult hydrophilicity needs larger, longer-chain amphiphiles（源：[[ES Fibre Hydrophilic Function and Applications]]）.
