## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

**Where it sits in the core-test family.** The vault groups core tests into channels — 容量 / capacity, **可达性 / accessibility**, 受压持液 / retention under load, 及时锁液 / prompt lock-up — and places the **Bromocresol SAM 分布（JA-22229）** method in the 可达性 channel, alongside GBP (STM-00229), Composite permeability (TTM-00006) / X-ray imaging (TTM-00631) and Mannequin Forced Failure（源：[[Rewet Test（回渗测试）]]）。That grouping implies the method characterises how liquid reaches and is held within the swollen SAM mass, but the vault does not state the bromocresol mechanism explicitly `[待补]`。

**Related SAM metrics that do carry numbers.** SAM characterisation by the five indicators (PSD / GBP / CRC / AUL / Vortex) is the **formal KC口径 for SAP incoming-material judging** and has complete STM numbering（源：[[SAM Testing Methods（超级吸水材料测试方法）]]）; the same five-indicator set is confirmed in the raw-material method index（源：[[Raw Material Testing Method Summary（原材料测试方法汇总表）]]）。**PSD** here is particle-size distribution — a material-level distribution distinct from the spatial, in-core SAM distribution this page names `[待补]`。

**Gap flagged by the vault itself.** Two deswelling tests are explicitly **not yet available**: "SAM deswelling at rewet pressure" is *under development* and "AUL Core deswelling test" is *needed*（源：[[Rewet Test（回渗测试）]]）。

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **REL core relevance is direct but under-specified.** SAM spatial distribution governs the target-zone capacity and the rewet/slide-through behaviour of an ultra-thin REL core; the method is therefore a structural-quality check on SAP placement, not a raw-material grade check `[待补]`（源：[[STM-00305 SAM分布.docx]]）。
- **Fits the core-test acceptance framework** — any distribution result must be read back against [[芯体测试方法与验收基准（STM）]] and the finished-product method index（源：[[Finished Product Testing Method Summary（成品测试方法汇总表）]]）。
- **Material-level vs core-level split.** The SAM five-indicator kit judges incoming SAP（源：[[SAM Testing Methods（超级吸水材料测试方法）]]）, while this distribution test sits at the core/diaper level — the two must not be conflated.
- **Complex-core caveat.** The nine complex-core judgement items (including 回渗、吸收性、滑渗) all resolve to the **internal quality standard, with no KC STM code** — so any SAM-distribution acceptance for a composite ("COCO"-type) core still lacks a global STM home（源：[[Raw Material Testing Method Summary（原材料测试方法汇总表）]]）。
