#   TTM 467: Mannequin Intake (Link to Method in EtQ)

![M8\n\nMucous\nSinds\n\n1\n2\n3\nLead the world in\nessentials for a better life.\n](1/parseimages/figure9eb2ac6b2bbd40b5a87df3eeaf808b33_0_2.png)
#   Development Status

- Standard method used for several years

- Work in progress to minimize variability from test conditions

#   Status for Claims

- Used for claims of 'absorbs runny mess' in KCNA

- Investigating for 'absorbs BM in seconds' claim for EMEA

- Investigating possibility to allow claims in LAO

#   Outputs

1. Grams of Simulant remaining on Mannequin ('pooled on skin')

2. Grams of Simulant that come off of liner surface in a rewet test ('pooled on liner')

3. Grams of Simulant absorbed into the diaper (not coming out with rewet or remaining on mannequin)

#   Other Notes

- Requires BM Gun and mannequin to run standard method, but may be possible to use alternate simulant delivery method to approximate the method

- Standard method requires Sat Cap box for rewet, but could use a weight instead to approximate the method

#   TTM 656: BM Flat Plate Absorption (Link to Method in EtQ)

![](1/parseimages/figure727f3389e02f47a48c2f5b6bc5d7c75b_0_2.png)
$\bullet$ Development Status

- Standard method used for several years

$\bullet$ Status for Claims

- Not used for claims to date, used for research purposes only

- Investigating for 'absorbs BM in seconds' claim for EMEA

- Investigating possibility to allow claims in LAO

$\bullet$ Outputs

1. Grams of Simulant remaining on upper plate and that come off of liner surface with blotter paper and weight ('pooled')

2. Grams of Simulant that reached each layer of the diaper

$\bullet$ Other Notes

- Requires BM Gun and mannequin to run standard method, but may be possible to use alternate simulant delivery method to approximate the method

- Recommended to use only A* simulant, as this is the only simulant which has been validated to partition through each layer the same as real BM
