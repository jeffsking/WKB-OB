## 模块1：核心论点（Core Arguments）

### CORE ARGUMENT 1 — Consumer need is a ranked 7-item list, and "Cleaning Efficacy" is structurally the first-listed need; the entire deck is organized to answer it.



"What are consumers looking for when they purchase baby wipes?"
— SLIDE 3

支撑依据：
- SLIDE 3 以问句立论，并给出需求清单（原文顺序）：Cleaning Efficacy / Safe & Gentle Skin Care / Good Quality / Convenience / Functional Packaging / Environmentally Friendly / Pleasant Scent（子项：Unscented for many）。
- 该清单带明确外部研究溯源：GL-90472 - Final Vendor Report KC BCC Wipes Benchmarking C&U（SLIDE 3），即这是 C&U（Consumer & Usage）Benchmarking 的_VENDOR 报告结论，不是内部臆测。
- 该论点在 SLIDE 7 被收束并升级：#1 Benefit Desired in a Wipe is CLEAN—these three parts work together to deliver it. —— 即 "Cleaning Efficacy"（SLIDE 3 首位需求）与 "CLEAN"（SLIDE 7 的 #1 Benefit）是同一条主线的首尾呼应。
- 可验证性：SLIDE 2 的 Agenda（Consumer → Basesheet → Formulation → Converting/Packaging → Product）证明后续 4 章正是为 SLIDE 7 的 CLEAN 三元（Basesheet / Formulation / Packaging）逐一展开。

### CORE ARGUMENT 2 — Wipes escape the diapering occasion: convenience extends usage to 7 non-diapering scenario families, which is what keeps the category alive long after diapering ends.



"The Convenience of Baby Wipes makes them Useful Beyond Diapering."
— SLIDE 4

支撑依据：
- SLIDE 4 将场景二元切分：Diapering-Related（4 项） vs Beyond Diapering（7 个一级项），来源 GL-87997 – Baby Wipes Usage Diary（SLIDE 4）。
- Beyond Diapering 的 7 个一级项：Baby/Kids Hands & Face（含 Mealtime、Out of Home 两个二级子项）/ Wiping Nose / Parent's Hands / Make-Up Removal / Adult/Child Toileting / Large and Small Surfaces / Pets & Pet Messes。
- 关键量化推论：SLIDE 5 称 "Many stay in category through child age 11"，而 SLIDE 5 的第三个决策节点仅为 "3-4 Years"。即换尿布期（约 0–4 岁）结束后，仍有约 7 年的在品类时间靠 Beyond-Diapering 场景支撑 [推导，非原文]。这使 SLIDE 4 的场景扩张不是"锦上添花"，而是品类生命周期的延续机制。

### CORE ARGUMENT 3 — Needs are non-stationary: three discrete decision-change points drive a predictable trade-down trajectory, and retention inside the brand is conditional on needs being met.



"…and keeps them in many homes for years after diapering is done. Needs also change over time."
— SLIDE 5

支撑依据：
- 原始图轴（Project JOURNEY Data Synthesis，SLIDE 5）三节点：1st Baby = Category Entry / 6 mos / 3-4 Years。
- 节点 1（Category Entry）的消费者心智与皮肤状态："Only the Best for Baby" + "Baby has very delicate/sensitive skin"（SLIDE 5 图）。
- 节点 2（6 mos）的生理与皮肤变化："BM consistency changes with solid food diet" + "Baby skin more developed/less delicate"（SLIDE 5 图）。
- 节点 3（3-4 Years）："More hands & face; surface usage" + "Lower volume consumed"（SLIDE 5 图）。
- 演讲者备注给出机制句：Consumers often start with a higher tier and trade down at the subsequent points.（SLIDE 5），以及留存的条件句：as long as their needs are being met（SLIDE 5）。
- 可验证性：该 trade-down 方向与 SLIDE 25 (Ch4) 的 Tier 阶梯（Economy Tier 3 → Mainstream Tier 4 → Premium Tier 5 → Super-Premium Tier 6）可逐档对应，使"trade-down"从定性描述升级为可落档的模型（详见模块 3 表 D、模块 5）。

### CORE ARGUMENT 4 — Segmentation is 2-dimensional: WILLINGNESS TO PAY (Y) × SKIN BENEFITS (X); these two attitudes "emerged as the key dimensions", and Huggies self-positions at the high-WTP end.



"Attitudes around willingness to pay and desired skin benefits emerged as the key dimensions that separated the segments."
— SLIDE 6 (Speaker Notes)

支撑依据：
- 坐标轴原文（SLIDE 6 图）：Y 轴 WILLINGNESS TO PAY（下端 VALUE → 上端 PREMIUM）；X 轴 SKIN BENEFITS（左端 NO ADDED INGREDIENTS → 右端 MANY SKINCARE INGREDIENTS）。
- 分群规模编码：*Size of circle represents segment size（SLIDE 6）—— 这是一个明确的三变量可视化（2 定位轴 + 1 规模变量）。
- 品牌定位句：As a premium brand, Huggies is targeted to those with greater willingness to pay.（SLIDE 6）
- 次级维度：Convenience and cleaning effectiveness also come into play and further develop segment identity（SLIDE 6）—— 说明 Convenience / Cleaning 是修饰性维度，不是切分轴。
- 边界声明：源文件仅明确点名一个分群 Bargain Buyers（左下象限）；其余 3 个象限的分群名称在源文件中 [未找到原句]，本报告不做补名。

### CORE ARGUMENT 5 — CLEAN is not a single attribute but a three-part coupled system: Basesheet + Formulation + Packaging, with a feedback loop between Formulation and Packaging.



"#1 Benefit Desired in a Wipe is CLEAN—these three parts work together to deliver it."
— SLIDE 7

支撑依据：
- SLIDE 7 图结构：中心蓝圈 CLEAN；三个红框 Basesheet / Formulation / Packaging 以箭头指向 CLEAN；Formulation ↔ Packaging 之间存在双向箭头。
- "work together" 是原文措辞，说明三元是协同而非加和；双向箭头进一步说明 Formulation 与 Packaging 之间存在相互约束/耦合（对应 SLIDE 20 (Ch4) 的 Packaging 功能之一 Retains moisture，即包装保水能力与配方液相量互为条件）。
- 可验证性：三元与后续章节一一对应 —— Basesheet → SLIDE 8–10 (Ch2)；Formulation → SLIDE 11–16 (Ch3)；Packaging → SLIDE 17–22 (Ch4)。
