# Executive Summary

This training session equips participants with the knowledge and tools to effectively request and interpret patent clearance assessments – also known as freedom-to-operate (FTO) or right-to-practice (RTP) analyses. Patent clearance is a critical step in mitigating business risk, ensuring that new products, technologies, and processes can be commercialized without infringing third-party intellectual property rights.

# PCA Process – Capital Appropriations

$\bullet$ Expenditure of funds to purchase new equipment requires an appropriation request

* The Finance Team typically leads the preparation and routing of the request (See CFI 51-20)

$\bullet$ The goal of the appropriation risk is to ensure that decision makers fully understand the projected benefits and perceived risks

* Appropriation Request should include a summary of any material legal risks (if any)

$\bullet$ Beware of advanced ordering

* Occasionally equipment will be ordered well in advance of launch/BFC due to long lead times
