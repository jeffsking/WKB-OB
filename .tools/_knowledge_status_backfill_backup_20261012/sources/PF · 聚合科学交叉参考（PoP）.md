## 一、全书地图（Book Map — 各章聚合科学交叉参考）

### Ch1 — Technology of Polyolefin Film Production

> 交叉概要：- concept: Polyolefin film grades (LDPE/LLDPE/HDPE/PP) and their catalytic origin
- related PoP (Principles of Polymerization, Odian 2004) topic: free-radical polymerisation (Ch3, high-pressure LDPE radical process) and stereoregular polymerisation via Ziegler–Natta / metallocene (Ch8, isotactic PP tacticity)
- weave note: Ch1 states LDPE is "polymerised by the high-pressure radical process" (p.7) and PP catalysts give ">99% isotactic structure" (p.13). PoP Ch3 notes PE blowing/melt strength hinges on radical-chain branching; PoP Ch8 notes ZN gives >95% α-PP and metallocene single-site raises isotacticity/PDI control. Ch1's qualitative "long branches → melt strength" is exactly the topological consequence PoP explains via chain-transfer/branching in radical PE; Ch1's ">99% isotactic" maps to PoP's stereoregular PP (iPP Tm 165–175 °C, here 162 °C). No page numbers invented; PoP facts are from the distilled module.
- concept: Antioxidant classes (hindered phenol + phosphite; HALS)
- related PoP topic: polymer degradation & stabilisation (Ch9, oxidative degradation via hydroperoxides)
- weave note: Ch1 p.24–25 describes hindered-phenol hydrogen donation and phosphite peroxy-group removal, and HALS radical scavenging (p.25). PoP Ch9 §成分 identifies "氧化降解(氢过氧化物)" and the HALS/nitroxide cycle; the synergist pair (primary radical scavenger + secondary hydroperoxide decomposer) is the same two-class scheme PoP calls out for PVC/PP ageing control. Ch1 gives the application-level mechanism; PoP supplies the peroxy/alkoxy radical chemistry.
- concept: Surface energy / polar-group introduction by corona and maleic-anhydride grafting
- related PoP topic: polymer reactions / grafting (Ch9, graft Onto/From/Through)
- weave note: Ch1 p.21 notes PP "can be grafted with maleic anhydride to increase the adhesion." PoP Ch9 lists grafting Onto/From/Through as the three modification paradigms; MAH-grafted PP tie layers (Ch1 p.37) are a textbook "Onto" graft used in hygiene-film lamination.。
