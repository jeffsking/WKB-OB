## 模块1：核心论点（Core Arguments）

### Argument 1 — Coform is KC's proprietary, patented basesheet platform and the structural root of HUGGIES® product differentiation.

KC positions coform not as one of several equivalent nonwoven routes, but as patented, in-house, and exclusive technology. The chapter opens by attaching three markers to coform simultaneously: legal protection ("patented process"), sensory outcome ("uniform clothlike basesheet"), and ownership ("KC Proprietary Technology"). This is a platform claim, not a product claim — it asserts that the competitive advantage sits in an asset/process KC owns and competitors cannot buy off the shelf.

- 支撑依据 (SLIDE 8)："Huggies wipe are made using a patented process which creates a uniform clothlike basesheet." + 页面标注 *"KC Proprietary Technology"*。
- 支撑依据 (SLIDE 9, 图像)：徽章文字 "THE ONLY Wipe With TRIPLE CLEAN LAYERS" —— 独占性（"THE ONLY"）被做成品牌资产。
- 支撑依据 (SLIDE 10)："10 global coform assets – 20 banks of capacity" —— 专有权被物理资产固化，构成资本壁垒。
### Argument 2 — Coform's architecture is a deliberate functional division of labor: polymer = wet strength, pulp = absorbency, at a nominal 2/3 : 1/3 mass split.

This is the single most important engineering sentence in the chapter. Coform is not a blend chosen for cost; it is a two-material system where each component owns one performance axis. Polymer (meltblown polypropylene) supplies the mechanical integrity the sheet needs in the wet state; cellulosic pulp supplies the liquid-handling capacity. The two axes are largely non-substitutable, which is precisely why the ratio is described as "typically" rather than "optimally" — it is an engineering constraint, not a formulation choice.

- 支撑依据 (SLIDE 8)：*"Polymer fibers provide wet strength, pulp fibers provide absorbency"；"Typically 2/3rds pulp (biodegradable) and 1/3rd polymer (not biodegradable)"*。
- 量化交叉验证：SLIDE 10 给出单片 1.34 g pulp + 0.5 g PP，实测浆占比 = 1.34 / 1.84 = 72.8%，高于 SLIDE 8 表述的 "2/3"（66.7%）。【本报告推导】 → 说明 "2/3" 是口径性近似值，真实配方偏浆侧约 6.1 个百分点，详见模块3表 T5。
### Argument 3 — TripleClean™ is the legal and structural codification of coform: a 3-input sandwich (meltblown / cellulosic / meltblown) whose functional payoff is hand protection via a "torturous path".

SLIDE 9 does two distinct jobs. Structurally, it resolves the "2-input" description of SLIDE 8 into a 3-input geometry: two meltblown layers sandwiching one cellulosic core, consolidated on a forming wire and then embossed. Commercially, it converts that geometry into a trademark with a pre-written legal substantiation string (Claim-07188) — i.e. the physical structure and the marketing claim were engineered together. The consumer-facing translation of the sandwich is hand protection: contamination must traverse a non-linear, "torturous" route through the thickness before it can reach the user's hand.

- 支撑依据 (SLIDE 9)："TRIPLE CLEAN* layers, the trademark for HUGGIES® Wipes proprietary base sheet, refers our unique 3-input process of constructing the base sheet."
- 支撑依据 (SLIDE 9, Claim-07188)："...by creating a composite structure of meltblown microfibers, cellulosic fibers and a sandwiching layer of meltblown microfibers."
- 支撑依据 (SLIDE 9)："Triple Layers help with hand protection because of the 'torturous' path the mess must take."
- 下游验证 (Ch4, SLIDE 21)：*"one of the benefits of coform is the ability to deliver a soft, thick, cushiony wipe"；"In our Consumer Use Tests, parents regularly tell us that Huggies® protects hands better."*
### Argument 4 — The competitive landscape maps cleanly onto basesheet technology choice: Coform = HUGGIES®, Spunlace = Pampers + most Private Label, Airlaid = some Private Label.

The chapter implicitly argues that basesheet route is the primary axis of market segmentation — more decisive than formulation or packaging. The technology each player uses is presented as a known, distinguishing fact. Notably, KC is the only branded player on coform; P&G (Pampers) sits on spunlace. This makes coform a technology-based oligopoly differentiator rather than a cost-parity commodity choice.

- 支撑依据 (SLIDE 8, 图像)：对照表使用者栏 —— Coform：*HUGGIES®；Spunlace："Pampers and most Private Label"；Airlaid："Some Private Label"*。
- 支撑依据 (SLIDE 8, 图像)：配图标题 "250X Microscope Images of Wipes" —— 用显微结构做可视化差异化证据。
### Argument 5 — Coform's defensibility is asset-scale, quantified: 10 assets / 20 banks / ~40 billion wipes per year, embedded in an 8-site global converting footprint plus contract manufacturers.

The chapter closes not with a technical claim but with a capacity and footprint claim. The message: even if the process were understood, replicating it requires roughly 20 production banks distributed across continents, co-located with converting. Scale is presented as the moat that converts a patent into a durable cost-advantaged platform.

- 支撑依据 (SLIDE 10)：*"10 global coform assets – 20 banks of capacity"；"Capable of ~40 billion baby wipes per year"*。
- 支撑依据 (SLIDE 10)：KC 在 8 个地点设有 Baby Wipes 转换（converting）资产；其中 7 个地点同时设有 coform 资产；另有 *"several contract manufacturers including Merisco and Tufco in the USA and Nice Pack in the U.K."*。
