# REL Variant C vs Option B Dual-Layer（成本·性能·供应链）

Comparison of the two REL（增强层）architectures for **OI-02 PVA for TABCW**：a single-layer homogeneous blend（单层均质混合，Variant C）versus a dual-layer architecture that decouples transport from support（解耦输运与支撑的双层结构，Option B）。

## Comparison Table 对比维度表

> Must include "Cost 成本" and "Supply Chain Risk 供应链风险" columns（必含「成本」与「供应链风险」两列）.

| Dimension 维度 | Variant C（single-layer homogeneous 单层均质） | Option B（dual-layer 双层） | Basis 依据 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Architecture 架构 | Single layer, four components homogeneously blended（PTT 28% / Hollow 14% / PVA 8%） | Layer A Intake 20–22 gsm（PTT 35% / Hollow 20% / ES 38% / PVA 7%，porosity ~92%）+ Layer B Scaffold 16–18 gsm（PVA 12% / ES 60% / PTT 20% / Hollow 8%，porosity ~88%） | [[IPC BCC OI Materials Development Team 2026 Annual Review Outline（v7）]] |
| Performance 性能 | Void Volume 95–105 cm³/g；compression recovery 85–88%；1st STT <2.5 s；MD permeability 6,000–9,000 D | Void Volume 108–117 cm³/g；compression recovery 91–94%；1st STT <1.8 s；MD permeability 9,000–13,000 D | 同上 |
| Limitation 局限 | Fast fibres and strong fibres constrain each other in one layer → cannot be tuned independently（快纤维与强纤维互相牵制，无法独立调参） | Requires a second carding / air-lay stream and precise parameter control（需第二成型流与精细参数控制） | 同上 |

## Applicable Scenarios 各自适用场景

- **Variant C（单层均质）**：成本敏感、无需解耦输运与支撑的既有产线；但已接近各项的混纺极限。
- **Option B（双层 REL）**：以 **Void Volume 108–117 cm³/g** 为目标的性能升级项目（OI-02 推荐路线），接受 +RMB 1,400/t 与改造 capex。

## Conclusion 结论

Option B dual-layer is the recommended route to hit the 108–117 cm³/g void-volume target, at a cost of **+RMB 1,400/ton** and a **single-source PVA hard constraint（Kuraray KURALON K-II）**——性能换供应链集中度（一句话结论）。
