## Summary 摘要

Company: Shandong Nameixin Biotechnology（品牌 纳美欣）， est. 2018-07-30, Jinan high-tech zone, 3M RMB registered capital; national high-tech + Shandong gazelle + specialized-SME; core = nanocellulose（细菌纤维素 BC、CNF/CNC）bio-extraction; partnerships with CAS Institute of Microbiology, Fudan, Qilu University of Technology; BC wound dressing with 3D-printed sandwich porous structure exceeded 10M RMB sales in 2024Q1. Dressing→diaper transfer: shared materials（CMC/alginate fibres, PE breathable film, SAP）; active care（tannic-acid ointment topsheet, jaundice shading liner）; smart（humidity sensor + Bluetooth）; ultrasonic welding（no VOC）; closed-loop recycling（ozone-regenerated SAP/fluff）. Diaper SAF exemplars: Dongsu 2025 antibacterial electrospun SAF（PVA + illite + aerogel）; 2023 chitosan-gel sprayed on PET（fast intake, anti-reflux, antibacterial, biodegradable）; 2016 in-situ polymerized SAP on ES web（≤3 mm, rewet ≤1.0 g）; 2022 5-layer spunlace（导流/柔滑/抑菌/吸水/稳定）. Process routes table: dry-wet spinning（PVA/PAA; acetone coagulation; stretch 100–150 °C; crosslink 120–180 °C）; one-pot dry spinning; hyperbranched natural-fibre modification（ion exchange 60–80%; ≥360 g/g）; alkalization-etherification degradable fibre（DS 0.6–0.95; 60–100 g/g）. Frontiers: Janus three-layer asymmetric structure（one-way "in-only" migration）; freeze-thaw PVA fibre（200× absorbency）; β-cyclodextrin + oregano oil; shaped cross-sections（Y/cross/W）and hydrophobic-hydrophilic cross-laid webs.（公司×迁移清单×实例×工艺×前沿。）
