# GB-T 36420-2018 Management System for Safety Evaluation of Chemicals and Raw Materials（生活用纸和纸制品 化学品及原料安全评价管理体系）

## 一句话定位

A **management-system standard**（体系标准，非限值标准）：要求生活用纸企业建立化学品与原料的供应商评价 → 安全评价 → 采购验收 → 贮存领用 → 可追溯闭环；其资料性附录 A/B 提供了 GB 43631-2023 之前最系统的卫品化学品建议清单（中文：化学品及原料安全评价体系）。

## Summary 摘要

GB/T 36420-2018 (SAC/TC 141) defines how tissue/hygiene-paper manufacturers shall organize chemical and raw-material safety management: suppliers must deliver quality certificates plus MSDS (or a CAS-anchored ingredient list), an internal assessment team evaluates every chemical and raw material, approved items enter controlled lists with process documents, acceptance includes COA verification (Annex C lists suggested upstream product standards, e.g. GB/T 21331 绒毛浆、GB/T 22875/22905 SAP、QB/T 4508 吸水衬纸) and sampling tests, and full traceability plus annual audit closes the loop. Its raw-material clause already contains the recycled-fibre split that later appeared in GB 43631. The informative annexes are the data-rich part: banned classes (CMR, T+/T, aromatic amine dyes, APEO in process chemicals; MIT/MCI in wipes) and advisory concentration limits per product family — wipes get preservative limits (phenoxyethanol ≤1.0%, IPBC ≤0.01% banned for baby wipes, chlorhexidine ≤0.3%…) plus 1,4-dioxane ≤30 mg/kg; diapers get heavy metals, phthalates ≤0.1%, dioctyltin ≤0.1% (as Sn), PCP ≤0.5 mg/kg, AOX ≤5.0 mg/kg. **These advisory numbers are 2018-era and partially superseded by the mandatory GB 43631-2023 (e.g. wipe/diaper formaldehyde 6 mg/kg, wipes phthalates 0.0012%) — the two documents must be read as "management system vs legal floor", not merged.**

## 关键论断（key_claims）支撑页

- **「体系标准 vs 限值标准」双轨**：GB/T 36420 管流程与台账，GB 43631-2023 管法定限值；附录数字是**建议基线**，不能当作合规终裁依据 —— 支撑页：[[GB-T 36420-2018（生活用纸化学品安全评价体系）]]、[[GB 43631-2023（婴幼儿及儿童纸品化学安全总门槛）]]
- **苯氧乙醇 ≤1.0% 的湿巾限值与化妆品规范一致**（2015 版《化妆品安全技术规范》防腐剂表同口径）—— 支撑页：[[Phenoxyethanol（苯氧乙醇）]]
- **附录 C 是上游原料标准清单**（绒毛浆、SAP、面层、吸水衬纸等 11 项），可直接作为供应商 COA 核验 checklist —— 支撑页：[[Material Safety Control（材料安全双轨管控）]]
