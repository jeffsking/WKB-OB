# Seymour Polymer Chemistry Ch02 Polymer Structure and Morphology

> Source: Seymour/Carraher, *Polymer Chemistry*, Ch.2 — Polymer Structure (Morphology).
> 定位说明 / Citation convention: 原文为 .txt 抽取件，**无正式印张页码**，本报告以文件行号（line no.）定位；原书页码缺失处标注 [待验证]。

## Executive Summary 内容概要

Chapter 2 is the structural core of the book: it answers how a polymer goes **from atomic arrangement to macroscopic morphology**. Seymour introduces a **four-level structure scheme**（primary → secondary → tertiary → quaternary）and builds on it the stereochemistry, intermolecular-force hierarchy, crystallinity, Tg/Tm transitions and chain entanglement. 本章的学术贡献是把"分子间作用力 × 链构象 × 形貌层次"三者耦合成一个可操作的结构-性能分析框架——这是 PP 等规度决定结晶度与 Tm、SAP 依赖非晶区溶胀的微观依据。

## Four Levels of Structure 聚合物四级结构

> *"We generally describe the structure of both synthetic and natural polymers in terms of four levels of structure. The primary structure describes the precise sequence of the individual atoms that compose the polymer chain."* (line 8-15)

> *"The secondary structure describes the molecular shape or conformation of the polymer chain. For most linear polymers this shape approaches a helical or 'pleated skirt' (or sheet) arrangement..."* (line 36-37)

> *"The tertiary structure describes the shaping or folding of the polymer."* / *"Finally, the quaternary structure represents the overall shape of groups of the tertiary structures where the tertiary structures may be similar or different."* (line 40-43)

| 层级 Level | 英文定义 Definition | 中文释义 |
|---|---|---|
| Primary 一级 | precise sequence of the individual atoms | 主链原子序列（可用单一重复单元 ×100/500/1000 次表示） |
| Secondary 二级 | molecular shape / conformation | 链构象：helical 螺旋或 pleated sheet 折叠裙状 |
| Tertiary 三级 | shaping or folding of the polymer | 链折叠方式 |
| Quaternary 四级 | overall shape of groups of tertiary structures | 超分子组装（可同质或异质） |

注：该四级分类借自蛋白质化学，Seymour 将其系统引入合成聚合物分析。

## Stereochemistry and Polymer Memory 立体化学与"记忆"

- **记忆 / Memory**：*"The ability of polymers to remember and have a memory are a direct consequence of their size."* (line 67)
  - 第一义（可逆 reversible）：交联网络中链段被化学/物理交联锚定，应力-应变去除后回到原始排列 → 橡胶弹性。
  - 第二义（不可逆 nonreversible）：合成与加工历史造成的链取向被"冻结"，构成材料的全部历史 → 加工-性能耦合。
- **尺寸-相态演变 / Size → phase**：Table 2.1 展示 C1–C4（气态烃）→ C5–C10（汽油）→ C1000–C5000（PE 固体）的相态跃迁，说明"大"本身就是一种质变的来源。
- **Tacticity 立体规整度**：由手性碳的几何构型决定（isotactic 全同 / syndiotactic 间同 / atactic 无规）；规整度 → 结晶能力 → Tm 与力学性能。

## Intermolecular Forces 分子间作用力层级

> *"While the London forces are typically weaker than the dipole–dipole forces, the former are also present in polar compounds, such as ethyl chloride and PVC."* (line 400-402)

> *"Strong polar molecules such as ethanol, poly(vinyl alcohol), and cellulose are attracted to each other by a special type of dipole–dipole interaction called hydrogen bonding... These are the strongest of the intermolecular forces and may have energies as high as 10 kcal/mol (40 kJ/mol) repeat unit."* (line 405-408)

> *"Intermolecular hydrogen bonds are usually present in fibers, such as cotton, wool, silk, nylon, polyacrylonitrile, polyesters, and polyurethanes."* (line 409-411)

**作用力叠加决定 Tm**：*"the high melting point of nylon-66 (265°C, Fig. 2.11) is the result of a combination of London, dipole–dipole, and hydrogen bonding forces between the polyamide chains."* (line 424-426)

| 作用力 Force | 相对强度 Relative strength | 典型聚合物 Typical polymers |
|---|---|---|
| London dispersion 伦敦色散力 | 最弱 weakest | PE、PP（聚烯烃） |
| Dipole–dipole 偶极-偶极 | 中等 intermediate | PVC、ethyl chloride |
| Hydrogen bonding 氢键 | 最强 strongest，可达 10 kcal/mol (40 kJ/mol) per repeat unit | cellulose、PVA、nylon、PAN、聚酯、聚氨酯 |

## Chain Entanglement and Melt Viscosity 链缠结与熔体粘度

> **Eq. 2.1**：*"log η = 3.4 log z + log K"*，*"The constant K is temperature-dependent."* (line 439-441)

链长（以 z 表示的临界缠结链长）以约 3.4 次幂放大熔体粘度——这是高分子加工（熔融纺丝、挤出）中"分子量稍增、粘度陡增"的定量根源，为 Ch.3 分子量与 Ch.5 流变提供桥梁。

## Tg and Tm 玻璃化转变与熔点

> *"The flexibility of amorphous polymers is reduced drastically when they are cooled below a characteristic transition temperature called the glass transition temperature (Tg). At temperatures below Tg, there is no segmental motion and any dimensional changes in the polymer chain are the result of temporary distortions of the primary valence bonds."* (line 462-465)

> *"Amorphous plastics perform best below Tg, but elastomers must be used above the brittle point, or Tg."* (line 466-467)

> *"The melting point (Tm) is called a first-order transition temperature, and Tg is sometimes called a second-order transition temperature. The values for Tm are usually 33–100% greater than Tg, and symmetrical polymers like HDPE exhibit the greatest difference between Tm and Tg."* (line 468-474)

**PP 关键数据（line 475-478）**：isotactic polypropylene 的 Tg = **373 K (100°C)**，但因高结晶度，在熔点 **438 K (165°C)** 以下基本不流动；反之高度非晶的 polyisobutylene，Tg = **203 K (−70°C)**，室温即流动。

| 聚合物 Polymer | Tg | Tm | 说明 Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Isotactic PP 等规聚丙烯 | 373 K / 100°C | 438 K / 165°C | 高结晶度 → 低于 Tm 仍保持尺寸稳定 |
| Polyisobutylene 聚异丁烯 | 203 K / −70°C | — | 高度非晶 → 室温流动 |
| Nylon-66 尼龙66 | — | 265°C | 三类分子间力叠加所致 |

## Morphological Structures 结晶形貌

- **单晶 / Single crystal**：*"highly linear polyethylene (7.6) can form diamond-shaped single crystals with a thickness on the order of 11 to 14 nm when crystallized from dilute solution. The surface consists of 'hairpin turned' methylene units..."* (line 762-765)
- **球晶 / Spherulite**：*"When polymers are produced from their melt, the most common structures are these spherulites. These spherulites can be seen by the naked eye and viewed as Maltese cross-like structures with polarized light and crossed Nicol prisms in a microscope."* (line 775-777)
- **系带分子 / Tie molecules**：*"The individual spherulite lamellae are bound together by 'tie' molecules that are present in both spherulites. Sometimes these tie segments form intercrystalline links which are threadlike structures that are important in developing the characteristic good toughness found in semicrystalline polymers."* (line 778-782)
- **非晶区 / Amorphous regions**：*"the structures that exist between these lamellar structures are generally occupied by amorphous structures including atactic chain segments, low molecular material, and impurities."* (line 802-803)
- **串晶 / Shish-kebab**：*"Under externally applied stress, including simple melt flow, the tertiary structure can approach a 'shish kebab' arrangement, where there are planes of platelets separated by areas where there exist both crystalline and amorphous regions."* (line 823-827)

## Methodology Critique 方法论批判

- **链折叠 vs 伸展链**：单晶究竟是 folded 还是 extended 仍有争议；Seymour 倾向折叠链模型（"hairpin turned" methylene units）。
- **Tg 的热力学地位**：原文称 Tg 为 "second-order transition temperature"，但现代观点多认为 Tg 是动力学冻结而非真正热力学相变——引用时需注意作者时代语境。
- **Memory 的两义性**：可逆（elastic recovery）vs 不可逆（mechanical history effect）对应黏弹性建模中的核心张力，本章仅给出定性描述。

## Hygiene Implication 卫材关联

- **PP 无纺布热封窗口**：i-PP 的 Tm = 165°C 意味着热风穿透、热轧、超声波点焊必须在 Tm 附近局部熔融、冷却后重结晶锁定。报告卡片给出的工程温度区间 140–160°C 属抽取值转述，**原书未给出该区间**，标注 [待验证]；可确定的是原文"below its melting point of 438 K (165°C) 基本不流动"。
- **SAP 吸水依赖非晶区**：晶区不可溶胀，只有 amorphous regions 允许水分子渗透与链段舒展。SAP 的高吸水本质是高度非晶的交联聚电解质网络在非晶相中的溶胀——设计上要最大化非晶含量（低规整度、低结晶倾向），同时用交联防止溶解。
- **Tg 决定弹性体服役温度**：弹性体必须在 Tg 以上使用，否则链段冻结变脆。卫生用品弹性腰围/腿围所用弹性体的 Tg 需显著低于室温，这是材料选型的第一道门槛。
- **结晶度 ↔ 强韧平衡**：tie molecule 与 intercrystalline links 是半晶聚合物韧性的来源——PP 纤维纺丝的牵伸与热处理就是在调控片晶/系带分子比例，直接决定无纺布的强度-伸长平衡。
