# EP1347788B1 Antimicrobial Hot Melt Adhesive (Bostik)

## 一句话定位

Granted European patent (Bostik Findley) on an **antimicrobial hot melt adhesive** for disposable nonwoven articles — an early (2005) hygiene-grade hot-melt variant adding a bacteriostat to the standard construction-adhesive formulation.

## 摘要

The invention provides a hot melt adhesive having antimicrobial properties for the manufacture of disposable nonwoven articles (diapers, sanitary napkins, surgical drapes, adult incontinence products). Suitable polymers listed include polybutene-1, SIS/SBS/SEBS block copolymers, EVA and APAO. The claimed composition (by weight) is: about 10–80% polymer; about 20–70% tackifying resin; about 0–50% plasticizer; about 0–50% wax; about 0.1–5% antioxidant; about 0.01–5% bacteriostat; totalling 100%. The bacteriostat inhibits or prevents bacterial growth that could change adhesion, colour and odour. Proprietor Bostik Findley, Inc. (US); inventors Svenningsen, Strelow, Alper, Hoppa-Willbrandt.
