# GB-T 33280-2016 Diaper Specifications and Dimensions（纸尿裤规格与尺寸）

## 一句话定位

The CN **sizing nomenclature standard**（规格与尺寸锚点）for baby and adult diapers — fixes what NB/S/M/L/XL/XXL must mean in weight (baby) and hip circumference (adult), plus the load-based method for measuring "max applicable waist".

## Summary 摘要

GB/T 33280-2016 (SAC/TC 141) applies to taped and pant diapers for babies and adults (not slips/underpads). It converts the market's informal S/M/L naming into weight windows for babies (e.g. M = 6–11 kg) and hip-circumference windows for adults (M = 80–105 cm), each paired with a minimum "max applicable waist" that the product must physically achieve. Annex A defines how max waist is measured: for taped diapers the front fastening tape length plus the rear end stretched under a 750 g load; for pants the waistband stretched by 1,500 g (baby) or 2,500 g (adult), doubled to give circumference. Compliance is checked against pack labeling — the pack must state size plus weight (baby) or hip (adult). Drafters span the whole industry (P&G, Unicharm, K-C China, Hengan-chain suppliers), making this the de-facto shared language for CN diaper size claims; it feeds directly into product-standard testing (GB/T 28004.1/28004.2-2021) which reference performance rather than sizing.
