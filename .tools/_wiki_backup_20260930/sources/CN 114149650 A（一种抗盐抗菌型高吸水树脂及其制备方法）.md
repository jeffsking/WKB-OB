# CN 114149650 A（一种抗盐抗菌型高吸水树脂及其制备方法 / Salt-tolerant antibacterial SAP — Anhui Furuixue）

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

A Chinese published patent application (applicant: **安徽富瑞雪化工科技股份有限公司**) on a **salt-tolerant, antibacterial SAP** loaded with nano-TiO2/SiO2 and a composite antibacterial agent. SAP composition → FTO-relevant.

## 摘要 / Abstract

The invention discloses a salt-tolerant antibacterial SAP made from (wt parts): unsaturated sulfonic-acid small monomer 25–38, nano-TiO2 11–17, nano-SiO2 9–13, composite fibre 11–19, composite nano-antibacterial agent 3–8, composite crosslinker 7–11, N,N'-methylenebisacrylamide 9–17, high-substitution crosslinked carboxymethyl cellulose 3–7, antioxidant 1–5. The composite fibre is made of carboxymethyl chitosan, saponin, montmorillonite, sepiolite and diatomite. It overcomes prior-art shortcomings, is safe, non-toxic, pollution-free, has good salt/antibacterial resistance and high absorbency, with social value and application prospects.
