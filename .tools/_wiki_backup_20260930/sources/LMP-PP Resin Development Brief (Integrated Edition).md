# LMP-PP Resin Development Brief (Integrated Edition)

## Positioning 一句话定位

This integrated project brief defines the technical decision gates, target windows, failure modes, and staged validation route for domestic low-melting-point polypropylene (LMP-PP，低熔点聚丙烯) used in hygiene nonwovens.  
（中文：该综合任务书界定了国产卫材用低熔点 PP 的技术决策门、目标窗口、失效模式与分阶段验证路线。）

## Summary 摘要

The brief treats low melting point as a polymerization and crystallization-design problem rather than a compounding-only problem. Ethylene and C4–C10 α-olefin comonomers disrupt isotactic sequences, while metallocene single-site catalysis offers more uniform insertion and a narrower molecular-weight distribution than Ziegler–Natta catalysis. The program is organized into three tiers: a lower-risk 150–158°C grade, a 135–150°C binder grade, and an ultra-low-melt sheath-resin target for all-PP bicomponent fibers. For the deepest melting-point reduction, the document combines high comonomer loading, a controlled low-isotacticity fraction, and the deliberate exclusion of nucleating agents. A sheath–core fiber assigns bonding to the low-melt sheath and mechanical support to the conventional PP core, avoiding the single-component conflict between crystallinity-derived strength and melting-point suppression. The recommended sequence is air-through staple-fiber validation before bicomponent spunbond scale-up, with DSC（差示扫描量热法）used to distinguish true melting point from softening or bonding-onset claims.
