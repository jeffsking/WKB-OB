# 非织造芯体材料技术讨论会（Bryan 优先清单 2026-06-09）

## One-line Positioning 一句话定位

A whiteboard brainstorm minute (2026-06-09) on **fibre systems, cross-sections and core stacking for the nonwoven REL core** — the earliest in this batch where the PE/PTT baseline, the 4D→1D section roadmap and the 8% PVA route are recorded. Cited as the origin of several open items that later reappear as OI-02 / OI-04 / OI-20 actions.
（白板头脑风暴纪要，记录 REL 芯体的纤维体系/截面/叠层方案，是本批中 PE/PTT 基准、4D→1D 截面路线与 8% PVA 配方的最早出处。）

## Summary 摘要

The meeting was structured as four whiteboard blocks: fibre recipes, cross-section/dtex evolution, supplier mapping, and a SAP double-layer core sketch. Four fibre systems were tabled — **A: PE/PTT 30/70 s/s at 155 °C** (baseline), **B: ES bicomponent** (PE/PET or PE/PP, 8000 series), **C: CoPET/PET 160/240 dtex bonding at 115 °C** ("hairs", for airlaid), **D: PE/PTT 6D** from a Jiangnan supplier direction. Section evolution ran **hollow → 4D (14%) → 1D V-shaped → 3D s/s → 7D V (high loft)**, with an ES path of 6D → 12D gradient dtex to lift void volume; the 40% slot takes 3D ES and the 8% slot takes hydrophilic PVA. Suppliers named: **Anhui Wanwei (PVA)**, **Jiangnan (PE/PTT 6D candidate)**, **NCC as an inter-layer reinforcement for SAP**. The core sketch pairs **SAP¹ + NCC (upper)** with **SAP² + spunlace (lower)**, hydro-entangled as the whole-layer bonding step. Confirmed directions were the PE/PTT 30/70 baseline, 4D as current optimum, and 8% Wanwei PVA; open items were the PE/PET vs PE/PP choice, CoPET/PET "hairs" processability in airlaid, NCC–SAP compatibility, and the effect of spunlace on SAP fixation.
