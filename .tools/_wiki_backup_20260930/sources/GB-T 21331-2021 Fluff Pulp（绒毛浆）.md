# GB-T 21331-2021 Fluff Pulp（绒毛浆）

## 一句话定位

China's product standard for **fluff pulp**（绒毛浆）used as raw material for disposable hygiene products — the CN-market acceptance baseline for fluff pulp lots (quality, chemical safety, microbial).

## Summary 摘要

GB/T 21331-2021 (ICS 85.040 / CCS Y31, SAC/TC 141) is the first revision of the 2008 fluff pulp standard. It drops the old product-grade split and instead classifies pulp by chemical treatment: **treated**（处理浆，耐破度显著降低，利于散纤）vs **untreated**（未处理浆）. The 2021 revision's main technical changes: migratable fluorescent substances, AOX, and four heavy metals (Pb/As/Cd/Hg) are now specified with test methods; 二氯甲烷抽出物 is replaced by 丙酮抽出物; dry bulk, absorption time/capacity methods are reworked in normative Annex B (specimen former with 50 mm tube, 80-mesh screen, 500 g platen, 0.9% saline at 23 °C). Microbial indicators are delegated to GB 15979, and microbial/raw-material failure rejects the batch. Drafters include 中轻（晋江）卫生用品研究、国家纸张质量监督检验中心、中国制浆造纸研究院、Intellego（英特尔奈）、可靠护理、恒安、Georgia-Pacific 吉平（上海）.
