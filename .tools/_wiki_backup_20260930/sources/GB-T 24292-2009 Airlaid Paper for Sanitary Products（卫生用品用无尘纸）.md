# GB-T 24292-2009 Airlaid Paper for Sanitary Products（卫生用品用无尘纸）

## 一句话定位

Product standard for **airlaid paper**（无尘纸）— both plain and SAP-loaded grades — as used in wipes, diaper acquisition/absorption layers and absorbent cores; the upstream raw-material standard named in GB/T 28004.1-2021's material chain.

## Summary 摘要

GB/T 24292-2009 (SAC/TC 141) covers airlaid paper supplied as jumbo rolls or sheets for disposable hygiene products, splitting the product family into 普通无尘纸 (cellulose-only) and 合成无尘纸 (with superabsorbent polymer). Requirement structure is paper-physics-heavy: basis-weight deviation ±10%, width ±3 mm, thickness ±0.4 mm, brightness 75.0–90.0%, pH 4.0–9.0, moisture ≤10%, plus an absorption ratio ≥2.0× for the plain grade (exempted for SAP grades, which is logically consistent since SAP dominates capacity). The hygiene clause cites the dated GB 15979-2002 at the 妇女经期卫生用品 tier — a dated reference that in practice now resolves to the current GB 15979 version. Notably, clause 4.9 already bans using discarded hygiene products as raw material — an early recycled-content red line now echoed by GB 43631-2023. Warning: the source OCR lost the numeric value of the MD tensile index limit; treat as [待验证].
