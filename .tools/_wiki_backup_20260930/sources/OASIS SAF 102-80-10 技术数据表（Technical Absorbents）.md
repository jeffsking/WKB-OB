# OASIS SAF 102/80/10 技术数据表（Technical Absorbents）

## Positioning 一句话定位

Technical Absorbents Ltd（TAL）**OASIS SAF™ 102/80/10** 牌号的技术数据表：超吸水纤维（SAF）本库唯一在库厂商的一手参数；直接支撑 [[Super Absorbent Fibre（超吸水纤维 SAF）]] 与 [[SAF vs SAP（超吸水纤维 vs 高吸水树脂）]]。

## Summary 摘要

Two-page datasheet for OASIS SAF™ Product Type **102/80/10**（型号三段数字疑为「纤度/切断长度/载荷档」的厂商编码，官方未解释 `[待验证]`）。Absorbency (quoted on **dry fibre**, per TAL Standard Test Methods): 15-minute free swell capacity **47 g/g in 0.9% saline** and **>120 g/g in DM（去离子水）**; absorption under load at 0.3 psi **22 g/g (0.9% saline)**, **>45 g/g (DM)**. Physical: colour white; staple length **78 mm**; moisture content **14% typical**; count **9 dtex mean**; pH (saline extract) **5.5 mean**. Packaging: bales wrapped and sealed in polypropylene, each labelled with fibre type, unique bale ID and bale weight.
