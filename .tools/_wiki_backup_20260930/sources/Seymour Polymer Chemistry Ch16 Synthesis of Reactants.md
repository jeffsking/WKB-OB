# Seymour Polymer Chemistry Ch16 Synthesis of Reactants

> Source: Seymour/Carraher, *Polymer Chemistry*, Ch.16 — Synthesis of Reactants.
> 定位说明 / Citation convention: 原文为 .txt 抽取件，**无正式印张页码**；本报告以原文行号（line no.）与章节号（§16.x）定位。

## Executive Summary 内容概要

Chapter 16 is the **"crude oil → monomer" route manual** of the whole book. Starting from the fossil-fuel feedstock streams (C1 methane, C2 ethylene, C3 propylene, C4 butadiene/isobutylene, aromatics, acetylene), it traces the industrial synthesis of every major polymer reactant and quantifies the industry at the **billion-lb/yr** level.

本章的阅读价值在于"**工艺链映射**"：每条单体路线都对应 **基础原料流 → 关键中间物 → 目标单体** 三步。对卫材的意义是把 PE/PP/SAP/PVC 四类主料追溯到各自的原料流（C2/C3/C2+Cl），从而理解成本与供应链波动的上游来源。

## Basic Feedstock Streams 基础原料流 (§16.1)

> *"Ethylene represents the two-carbon precursor (Fig. 16.2). Here, depending on the reaction conditions, a wide variety of intermediates and products can be formed."*

- **乙烯流**：*"Of course, polyethylene itself is part of the ethylene stream. Its copolymerization yields a large number of industrially important copolymers, terpolymers, graft polymers, and block polymers."*
- **裂解产量（原文数据）**：*"Ethylene, propylene, and butylene are produced at annual rates of 52 billion, 29 billion, and 0.4 billion lb., respectively, by the vapor phase cracking of light oil fractions of petroleum feedstocks or petroleum gases."*
- **C1 甲烷流**：HCN、methanol、formaldehyde、syn-gas — 构成甲醛系树脂（酚醛/脲醛/三聚氰胺）与 AIBN 的原料起点。
- **C3 丙烯流**：cumene → phenol + acetone → BPA；亦是 MMA、PP 的源头。
- **C4 流**：butadiene、isobutylene → SBR/NBR/ABS/BR 与丁基橡胶。
- **乙炔流（煤基）**：作为 C2 的替代路线，传统上用于 VCM 与若干单体。

### Isoprene 异戊二烯的多路线合成

> *"Isoprene (2-methyl-1,3-butadiene), shown as derived from acetylene feedstock (Fig. 16.3), is commercially formed from a number of additional routes."*

*"One industrially important route starts with acetylene and acetone, while another begins with isobutylene and formaldehyde."* / *"Dimerization of propylene to 2-methyl-1-pentene, isomerization to 2-methyl-2-pentene, and finally pyrolysis to isoprene and methane is also a route to isoprene."*

| Maleic anhydride 马来酸酐 | 邻苯二甲酸酐副产；丁烯/巴豆醛氧化 | 不饱和聚酯 |

> *"Both the cyclohexanol and cyclohexanone produced by the cobalt(II) acetate–catalyzed air oxidation of cyclohexane are oxidized to adipic acid by heating with 50% nitric acid; 1.8 billion lb of adipic acid is produced annually in the United States."*

### BPA 安全性预警（本书原文观点）

> *"Bisphenol A [(bis-4-hydroxyphenyl)dimethylmethane], used for the production of epoxy resins and polycarbonates, is obtained by the acidic condensation of phenol and acetone."*
> *"Recently, it has been found that bisphenol A is an endocrine system disrupter. The consequences of this are still being determined."*

## Synthesis of Vinyl Monomers 乙烯基单体合成 (§16.3)

- **Styrene 苯乙烯**：*"Styrene, which is one of the most important aromatic compounds, is produced at an annual rate of about 11 billion lb by the catalytic vapor phase dehydrogenation of ethylbenzene. The latter is obtained by the Friedel-Crafts condensation of ethylene and benzene."* — 另有一条联产路线同时产出苯乙烯单体与环氧丙烷（PO）。
- **VCM 氯乙烯**：*"Vinyl chloride (chloroethene, VCM), which was formerly obtained from acetylene, is now produced at an annual rate of about 15 billion lb. by the transcatalytic process in which chlorination of ethylene, oxychlorination of byproduct hydrogen chloride, and dehydrochlorination of ethylene dichloride take place in a single reactor."* — **单反应器内完成氯化 + 氧氯化 + 脱 HCl**，是氯平衡利用的工业典范。
- **其他路线**：VDC（1,1,2-三氯乙烷热解）、VAc（乙烯+醋酸+O₂）、**Acrylonitrile（丙烯氨氧化 ammoxidation，原文标注致癌）**、TFE（氯二氟甲烷热分解）、VDF/VF（脱 HCl）、Butadiene（丁烷催化热裂）、Chloroprene（二氯丁烯脱 HCl，neoprene）、**Acrylic acid（乙烯氧化羰基化）**、**MMA（丙烯+甲醇催化氧化羰基化）**。

## Free Radical Initiators 自由基引发剂 (§16.4)

| Initiator 引发剂 | Synthesis 合成路线 (per source) |
|---|---|
| Benzoyl peroxide | benzoyl chloride + Na₂O₂（水相） |
| t-Butyl hydroperoxide | H₂O₂ + isobutylene（酸催化加成） |
| t-Butyl peroxide | t-BOOH + isobutylene |
| Dicumyl peroxide | cumene 空气氧化 |
| AIBN | acetone + KCN + hydrazine hydrochloride 多步合成 |

> *"2,2′-Azobisisobutyronitrile (AIBN, 2,2-dicyano2,2-azopropane) is obtained from the reaction of acetone with potassium cyanide and hydrozine hydrochloride."*
> *"As shown in Eq. (16.51), the reaction of potassium cyanide with hydrazine hydrochloride produces hydrogen cyanide and hydrazine. The latter reacts with acetone to form acetone dihydrazone, which reacts with the former (HCN) to produce a substituted hydrazone, which is then oxidized to AIBN by sodium hypochlorite."*

— AIBN 合成涉及 **HCN（氰化氢）**，这是其工业生产的安全要点。

## Hygiene Implication 卫材关联

本章是**成本/供应链**视角的章节，与卫材有三处实质关联：

1. **主料溯源**：PE（膜、PE 纤维）← 乙烯流；PP（纺粘/熔喷无纺布主料）← 丙烯流；SAP 主单体 acrylic acid ← 乙烯氧化羰基化；PVC 涂层 ← VCM ← 乙烯 + 氯。四条链的上游景气度直接决定卫材成本结构。
2. **BPA 合规**：卫材中的 PC/环氧组件（婴儿用品外壳、湿巾盖）应评估 BPA 迁移风险，本章给出 BPA 为内分泌干扰物的定性预警；具体法规限值（GB 4806、(EU) No 10/2011 等）不在本章范围，[待验证]。
3. **引发剂安全**：丙烯酸溶液聚合常用 AIBN/偶氮类引发剂，其工业合成路径涉及 HCN——供应商工艺安全与残留控制是 SAP 原料审核项。
