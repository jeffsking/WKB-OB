# Korean Diaper Market Analysis & Competitive Landscape（韩国尿布市场分析及竞争格局）

## 一句话定位

A recorded business review of the **Korean diaper category（韩国纸尿裤品类）** — market trajectory, competitive landscape, and the premiumization / occasion-based-marketing playbook that sustained category leadership. Source is a single-brand internal view (Kimberly-Clark Korea), so treat as **competitor-intelligence, medium credibility**.

> 说明：原文为语音转录（presentation transcript），发布日期未标注，按内容引用 2026 YTD 数据估算为 2026-08，待核实。

## 摘要

The Korean diaper market has endured a **decade-long birth-rate decline (~8%/yr)**, but the decline is moderating and a small rebound is expected in 2025-26 (newborns +5% this year). Against this, the presenting brand (Huggies/Kaon) sustains **~65% value share** through two engines: (1) **premiumization（高端化）** — trading consumers up tiers via functional proof and skin-health credibility; and (2) **occasion-based marketing（场景营销）** — sub-brands for summer, overnight, swim, premie, etc., where summer SKUs carry a +5~10% price premium. Competitors (Pampers, Kindle) trail; **Kindle** started as a German premium brand but switched to a China-based OEM model (100% from China) and holds only ~5% share. A notable demand-side risk: Korean parents show **negative sentiment toward China-sourced baby products**, yet many Korean brands quietly use Chinese OEM — a brand-equity vulnerability. The strategic conclusion: in a shrinking market, **premiumization + brand equity is the only durable growth path**.
