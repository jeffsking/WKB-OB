# Research Advances in Superabsorbent Polymers

## One-line Position 一句话定位
A 2024 MDPI review of SAP synthesis/modification, particle/fiber/gel forms, and industrial–agricultural–biomedical applications.（综述 SAP 合成改性、颗粒/纤维/凝胶形态与多领域应用。）

## Summary 摘要
- SAPs are lightly crosslinked 3D networks swelling >1000× dry weight; osmotic pressure + hydrophilic groups drive absorption (Flory network theory), and absorption falls with ionic strength / multivalent cations.
- Synthetic SAP: polyacrylic acid (PAA, market leader), polyacrylonitrile (hydrolyzed to absorbent fiber), polyvinyl alcohol (biodegradable but lower uptake). Natural SAP: cellulose, chitosan, starch, protein, amino acid, alginate.
- Modification by grafting acrylic acid / acrylamide onto natural backbones lifts absorption while keeping biodegradability; composite addition (bentonite, calcium alginate, kaolin) tunes swelling and cost.
- Forms: particles (most commercial), fibers (spun/electrospun, biomedical), gels/aerogels (drug delivery, water purification).
- Applications span concrete self-healing, wastewater/dye adsorption, agriculture water/fertilizer control, and biomedical drug delivery & wound dressing.
