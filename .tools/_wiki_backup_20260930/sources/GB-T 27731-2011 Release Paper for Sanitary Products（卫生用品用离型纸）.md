# GB-T 27731-2011 Release Paper for Sanitary Products（卫生用品用离型纸）

## 一句话定位

Product standard for **silicone-coated release paper**（卫生用品用离型纸）used in sanitary napkins / diaper back-strip applications — the acceptance spec for the anti-adhesion liner around SAP-containing articles and for packaging separators.

## Summary 摘要

GB/T 27731-2011 (SAC/TC 141; drafters 中国制浆造纸研究院、佛山新飞卫生材料、小护士（天津）、广州宝洁、国家纸张质检中心) covers release paper supplied in jumbo rolls for one-time hygiene products. Technical requirements combine paper physics (basis weight series 29.0–44.0 g/m², density, MD tensile, tear index) with silicone-release performance: peel force against a standardized tape of 0.05–0.20 N/25 mm (Annex A) and residual adhesion rate ≥80.0% after 70 ℃ / 20 h aging (Annex B) — the two parameters that actually decide whether the release liner behaves on the converting line. Appearance requirements ban holes, cracks, folds, stains; silicone side must be wound inward; splice counts must be marked. Microbial indicators follow GB 15979 with a sole-veto rule. Note: the source OCR degraded some inequality directions in Table 1; values below are transcribed as-is with direction `[待验证]` where ambiguous.

## 关键论断（key_claims）支撑页
