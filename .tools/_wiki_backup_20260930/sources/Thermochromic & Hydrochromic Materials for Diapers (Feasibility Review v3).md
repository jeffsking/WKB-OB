# Thermochromic & Hydrochromic Materials for Diapers (Feasibility Review v3)

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

The **route/MVP/sourcing/safety review for temperature- and wetness-indicating materials in diapers** — it takes a confirmed microclimate pain point through three implementation routes, a draft bench spec, an eight-plus-thirteen supplier map, safety hard gates and a 12-month phased roadmap with gates. It is the batch's "**what can actually ship, and what must be proven first**" page.
本页是**尿裤用温敏/湿敏指示材料的路线—MVP—寻源—安全评审**：从已确认的微气候痛点出发，给出三条实现路线、台架规格草案、国际与国内供应商地图、安全硬门与 12 个月分阶段带门路线图，回答「**什么能真正落地、什么必须先被证明**」。

## Summary 摘要

The problem framing is explicit: diaper microclimate (heat and humidity) drives skin irritation, caregivers lack a visible cue for "when to act"; a consumer-visible reason-to-believe (RTB) for thermal comfort / wetness is differentiation beyond basic absorbency; existing wetness indicators show "wet" only and do **not** signal thermal comfort or humidity accumulation. Success is bounded — any solution must show measured benefit in **both** dry and wet states with no unacceptable trade-off on absorbency, fit, cost, skin health or manufacturability, and the indicator must stay on the non-skin-contact face.
问题定位明确：尿裤微气候（热与湿）引发皮肤刺激，看护者缺乏「何时该处理」的可见提示；面向消费者的热舒适/湿态可感知理由（RTB）是超越基础吸收的差异化；现有尿显只显示「湿」，**不**显示热舒适或湿度积累。成功边界明确——方案必须在**干态与湿态**都有可测收益，且不得在吸收、贴合、成本、皮肤健康或可制造性上有不可接受折衷，指示层必须位于非皮肤接触面。

Three routes are defined, all sharing microencapsulation as the enabler: **R1 thermochromic ink/pigment** (printed stripe or laminated label patch at the outer-surface core-centreline / crotch panel; reversible for comfort cues); **R2 hot-melt wetness glue** (glue line on the backsheet inner face; urine triggers an irreversible yellow/orange→blue change; 84% of the market; lowest risk, fits existing converting lines); **R3 water/solvent hydrochromic ink** (printed on breathable film or nonwoven with no extra step, custom graphics, eco-friendly).
定义三条路线，共享微胶囊化为使能技术：**R1 温致变色油墨/颜料**（外表面芯体中线/裆部印刷条或复合标签片；可逆，用于舒适提示）；**R2 热熔尿显胶**（底膜内面胶线；尿液触发不可逆的黄/橙→蓝；占市场 84%；风险最低、适配现有转化线）；**R3 水/溶剂型湿致变色油墨**（直接印于透气膜或非织造布，无需额外工序，图形可定制、环保）。

Mechanisms: thermochromic systems are **reversible leuco-dye microcapsules** (leuco dye + developer + solvent, stable below threshold, electron transfer / phase change at or above threshold, reversible on cooling) with a customisable 30–37 °C activation band and representative display points 32/34/36 °C; hydrochromic systems trigger on **liquid contact** (far more realistic than a true RH sensor). For MVP the review prefers the hot-melt route and treats water-based ink as the phase-2 differentiation / eco option.
机理：温致变色为**可逆 leuco 染料微胶囊**（leuco 染料 + 显色剂 + 溶剂，阈值以下稳定，达到/超过阈值发生电子转移或相变，降温可逆），激活带可定制于 30–37 ℃，代表性显示点 32/34/36 ℃；湿致变色由**液体接触**触发（远较真正 RH 传感器现实）。MVP 阶段建议优选热熔路线，水基油墨作为第二阶段差异化/环保选项。

### Safety and compliance hard gates 安全与合规硬门

- ISO 10993 (cytotoxicity / skin irritation / sensitisation), EU REACH (SVHC restriction + full substance declaration for the chromic layer), FDA guidance, China GB/T 35613-2017 & GB/T 36420-2018 hygiene-product safety baselines.
- **Matsui Shikiso (松井) caution**: some legacy products contain trace formaldehyde and official guidance excludes baby direct contact → resolve baby-safety before any skin-adjacent use.
- The indicator MUST sit on the non-skin-contact face (outer cover / backsheet window); powder forms are banned from skin layers until validated.

### MVP plan 三条候选 MVP

| MVP | Scope 范围 | Difficulty 难度 | Priority 优先级 | Position 位置 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A · Thermochromic indicator 温敏指示 | Outer-surface printed / labelled reversible thermo ink; RTB for thermal-comfort / cooling claim; no structural function | Medium | P1 | Non-skin-contact outer face (front-lower / crotch), core-centreline |
| B · Hydrochromic indicator 湿敏指示 | Hydrochromic window / patch adjacent to the existing wetness indicator; validates "visible humidity / wetness cue" comprehension | Low–medium | P1 | Adjacent to existing WI |
| C · Combo thermal + hydro 复合 | Dual-zone display (blue = humidity, red/green = temperature); tests whether caregivers decode two signals | Med–high | P2 | Both |

- **Sequencing rule**: do NOT replace the existing wetness indicator — use it as differentiation learning; the combo (C) only after single-function validation, and fall back to single-function if the misread rate is high.
- **MVP A acceptance**: stable repeatable colour in band; ≥70% correctly read "too hot / too cold / just right"; no curl / blocking / set-off / smudge; readable after 40 °C / 75% RH for 1 week.
- **MVP B acceptance**: stable colour after a set insult; caregivers distinguish it from the traditional WI; no roller / plate / seal contamination; no abnormal colour after 4 weeks in pack.
- **Exit gate**: the bench protocol must test dry AND wet states and quantify the **false-trigger rate** — the single most important exit gate.
