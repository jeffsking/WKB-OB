# CN 110776598 B（一种高吸水性树脂及其在尿不湿中的应用 / SAP and its use in diapers）

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

A granted Chinese invention patent on a **bamboo-charcoal / polyacrylamide composite superabsorbent polymer (SAP)** formulation with an antibacterial claim, positioned for diaper (尿不湿) use. It is a candidate prior-art reference for SAP composition freedom-to-operate (FTO) screening.

## 摘要 / Abstract

The invention relates to the field of polymeric superabsorbent resins, specifically a high-absorbency resin and its application in diapers. The composition comprises, by weight parts: modified bamboo-charcoal fibre (改性竹炭纤维) 6–10, polyacrylamide (聚丙烯酰胺) 20–40, an absorption aid (吸水辅助剂) 0.1–3, acrylic acid (丙烯酸) 10–20, crosslinker (交联剂) 0.1–2, initiator (引发剂) 0.1–0.5, reinforcement agent (增强剂) 5–10, and antibacterial agent (抗菌剂) 0.1–1. The disclosed method is claimed to be simple, well-controlled, efficient and low-cost. The resulting SAP is claimed to have fast water absorption, large liquid uptake and small rewet, with durable antibacterial/anti-inflammatory and deodorising effects and reduced environmental pollution. Diapers made therefrom are claimed to show fast uptake, high absorbency, low rewet, good antibacterial effect, breathability, toughness and skin-friendliness.
