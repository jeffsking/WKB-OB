# EP4428211A1 Hot Melt Adhesive Composition (Bostik)

## 一句话定位

European patent application (Bostik SA, 2024) on a **hot melt adhesive composition** built on a styrene block copolymer + acid-modified rubber — a recent (2024) Bostik hot-melt entry in this batch's polyolefin/block-copolymer adhesive cluster.

## 摘要

The application (publication EP 4 428 211 A1) relates to a hot melt adhesive composition comprising at least one styrene block copolymer and at least one acid modified rubber. The bibliographic header lists applicant Bostik SA (Colombes, FR) and inventors FU Shangyi, LI Danfeng, LUO Yi (Shanghai, CN). It claims priority to EP 23305315 (2023-03-09). Detailed claim language is in the full text; this summary captures the bibliographic and compositional scope stated in the abstract.
