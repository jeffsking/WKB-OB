# Daehyun Microcapsule Catalogue（DH-Catalogue EN-CN）

## 一句话定位

Daehyun（(株)大炆 / 大炫）微胶囊产品目录的**另一份副本**（无「(1)」后缀），内容与副本一一致；作为「变色微胶囊供应方」信息源的重复登记件。

## 摘要

Companion copy of the Daehyun EN-CN catalogue. It repeats the same structure: company vision "The World of Microcapsule", five product families (**TCC** thermochromic, **PCC** photochromic, **Aroma**, **M-Care**, **I-Care**), and the same capability claims — in-house synthesis, oil/water applicability, adjustable capsule size, customized temperature and colour, customized fragrance and concentration, optional **HLR** (high light resistance). Slurry and powder forms are offered. No hygiene-specific grade, capacity, pricing or commercial term appears in the read portion → `[待验证]`.
