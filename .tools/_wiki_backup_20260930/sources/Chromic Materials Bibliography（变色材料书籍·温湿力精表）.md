# Chromic Materials Bibliography（变色材料书籍·温湿力精表）

## One-line Positioning 一句话定位

A curated 22-title bibliography on chromic materials, indexed by stimulus class (thermo- / hydro- / mechanochromic) — it is the library's **reading map for the chromism family**, and the anchor source for the taxonomy concept page.

（中文：一份按刺激类别（温致 / 湿致 / 力致变色）索引的 22 册变色材料书目——是本库**变色材料族的阅读地图**，也是「变色现象分类」概念页的锚定来源。）

## Summary 摘要

The workbook catalogues 22 books with author, year, subject area and a stimulus tag, then repeats the list under three thematic sheets (温致变色 / 湿致变色 / 力致变色) with a "role in this theme" column. Together with the library's existing 无纺布 — 五大工艺论剑 and related sources it is the entry-level reference shelf.

The general overview sheet scores each title on a 温/湿/力 axis; the thematic sheets re-rank them for the specific job. Thermo- and hydrochromism have the deepest shelves (11 and 8 titles respectively), while mechanochromism has 8 — and those 8 are heterogeneous: four are chromic-molecule books (Xu & Chi's *Mechanochromic Fluorescent Materials*, Dürr's *Photochromism*, Bamfield's *Chromic Phenomena*, Zwicker's *Wearable Sensors* context) and four are wearable/stretchable electronics or smart-textile titles (*Stretchable Electronics*, *Smart Textiles*, *Smart Fibres Fabrics and Clothing*, *Wearable Electronics and Photonics*, *Intelligent Textiles and Clothing*).

This split is the substantive finding: **mechanochromism in hygiene is a molecule problem in one half of the literature and a device problem in the other**. The bibliography also quietly places two absorbent-hygiene classics — *Absorbent Technology* (Chatterjee & Gupta, 2002) and *Modern Superabsorbent Polymer Technology* (Buchholz & Graham, 1998) — on the hydrochromic shelf, which is where the wetness-indicator work can borrow directly.

Limitations are recorded honestly: none of the 22 titles carries a public access link, several are multi-edition and the sheet mixes edition years (e.g. *Introduction to Liquid Crystals* listed as 2019/2020, *Intelligent Textiles and Clothing* as 2006/2010). It is a reading map, not a literature review — no findings are extracted or graded.

（中文摘要：该表按作者、年份、学科领域与刺激标签编目 22 本书，并分「温致 / 湿致 / 力致」三张主题表重复列出并加「在本主题中的角色」列。总览表在温/湿/力三轴上打分；温致与湿致书架最深（各 11 与 8 本），力致 8 本且高度异质——一半是变色分子类书（徐/迟《力致荧光变色材料》、Dürr《光致变色》、Bamfield《变色现象》、可穿戴传感相关），一半是可拉伸电子与智能纺织类书。这一分裂本身即结论：**卫品场景下的力致变色，一半文献把它当分子问题、另一半当器件问题**。书目另把两本吸收性卫生经典（《Absorbent Technology》2002、《Modern Superabsorbent Polymer Technology》1998）归入湿致变色书架，正是尿湿指示工作可直接借用的位置。局限如实记录：22 本均无公开获取链接，存在多版本与年份混列。它是阅读地图，不是文献综述——不提取结论、不做证据分级。）
