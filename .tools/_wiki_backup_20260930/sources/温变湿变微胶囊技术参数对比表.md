# 温变湿变微胶囊技术参数对比表

## 一句话定位

**温变 vs 湿变油墨微胶囊** 核心技术参数对比表（约 15 个维度），本批作为「变色微胶囊技术」信息源，并直接派生对比页。

## 摘要

The spreadsheet compares **thermochromic (温变) vs hydrochromic (湿变) ink microcapsules** across ~15 parameter dimensions: mechanism, microcapsule particle size, printing process suitability, three core barriers, and global benchmark suppliers. Key technical points: particle size screen/offset grade 1–10 μm, mainstream 2–15 μm; size uniformity (CV) international ≤15% vs domestic ~30%; hydrochromic demands far tighter wall density (water molecule ~0.28 nm). CTI (US) dominates both thermochromic and hydrochromic; domestic players (景艺姿, 侨盛新材料, 广州乐迪新材料) are at early stage.
