# Absorbent Hygiene Products (AHPs)

## One-line Position 一句话定位
A 2022 EDANA dossier compiling AHP market/composition/safety facts and the CODEX™ trace-chemical risk-assessment methodology.（EDANA 2022 卷宗：AHP 市场、组成、安全与 CODEX 痕量化学物质风险评估。）

## Summary 摘要
- EDANA (founded 1971, >300 member companies) is the voice of the nonwovens / AHP industry across Europe, Middle East and Africa; this Version 2.0 dossier (01.04.2022) addresses AHP safety and trace substances.（EDANA 代表欧洲/中东/非洲的非织造与吸收性卫生用品产业。）
- AHP categories: baby diapers, feminine hygiene products (napkins, pantyliners, tampons), adult incontinence products.
- AHPs are assembled from fluff pulp, cellulosic fibres, superabsorbent polymers (SAP), nonwovens (PP), polymer films (PE), adhesives and elastics; SAP is the key enabler of thinner + more absorbent products over ~6 decades.
- Manufacturing is high-speed, dry, GMP-based: modern baby-diaper lines >1000 units/min, pantyliners >2000 units/min; hot-melt glue zones reach 90–170 °C.
- Safety rests on feedstock qualification, toxicological EBRA (Exposure-Based Risk Assessment), REACH/GPSD/medical-device compliance, and the voluntary 2018 Stewardship Program + CODEX™ (list of trace chemicals with guidance values).
