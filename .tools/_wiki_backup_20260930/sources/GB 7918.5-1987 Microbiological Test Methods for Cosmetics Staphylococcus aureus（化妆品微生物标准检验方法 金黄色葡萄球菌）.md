# GB 7918.5-1987 Microbiological Test Methods for Cosmetics Staphylococcus aureus（化妆品微生物标准检验方法 金黄色葡萄球菌）

## 一句话定位

Historical CN method standard for **Staphylococcus aureus in cosmetics**（化妆品金黄色葡萄球菌检验）— the enrichment–isolation–confirm chain that the current "金黄色葡萄球菌不得检出" gates in GB 15979 descend from.

## Summary 摘要

GB 7918.5-87 prescribes the classical S. aureus workflow: enrich a 1:10 sample dilution in SCDLP broth (7.5% NaCl broth as fallback, with lecithin/Tween added for preserved products) for 24 h at 37 ℃; streak onto Baird Parker agar — where lithium chloride suppresses Gram-negative competitors and sodium pyruvate enhances S. aureus recovery — for 24–48 h; purify suspect colonies on blood agar; then confirm by three traits: Gram-positive grape-like cocci morphology, mannitol fermentation with acid production, and a positive plasma coagulase test (slide agglutination within 5 min, tube clotting within 24 h against known +/− controls). A positive is reported only when all legs agree. This qualitative confirm-chain is the direct ancestor of the "specific pathogens not detectable" clauses (金黄色葡萄球菌、铜绿假单胞菌、溶血性链球菌 etc.) that GB 15979-2024 applies to wipes and diapers.
