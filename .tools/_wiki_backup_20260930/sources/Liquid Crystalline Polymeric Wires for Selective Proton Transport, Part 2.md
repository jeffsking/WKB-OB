# Liquid Crystalline Polymeric Wires for Selective Proton Transport, Part 2

## One-line Position 一句话定位

This chapter *characterises* the solid-state ion transport of the Part 1 wires, proving selective, water-free proton transport and exposing the EIS limitation for ion-free channels.
（中文：本章表征 Part 1 聚合物线的固相离子传输，证明选择性、无水的质子传输，并揭示 EIS 对无离子通道的局限性。）

## Summary 摘要

- The hybrid membrane replaces ion-containing membranes (e.g. Nafion) with ion-free PAZE columns; selectivity arises from dipole / weakly polarising bonds, not from fixed ionic groups.
  （中文：杂化膜以无离子 PAZE 柱替代含离子膜（如 Nafion）；选择性来自偶极/弱极化键，而非固定离子基团。）
- EIS underestimates true ionic conductivity for ion-free channels (the AC signal reads polarisation of weakly polar bonds, not ions); correct characterisation requires solution-phase I–V / C–V exploiting the charge mismatch.
  （中文：对无离子通道，EIS 低估真实离子电导（交流信号读到的是弱极化键的极化而非离子）；正确表征需用溶液相 I–V/C–V 利用电荷失配。）
- Thermal annealing (50 °C, 72 h) gives near-homeotropic column alignment, lowers proton resistance, and makes selectivity superior to Nafion 117 for all alkali ions except a Na⁺ anomaly in HYB40-02.
  （中文：50 °C 72 h 热退火获得近垂直柱取向，降低质子电阻；除 HYB40-02 的 Na⁺ 反常外，对所有碱金属离子的选择性均优于 Nafion 117。）
- Environmental robustness: conductivity is nearly independent of RH/temperature; low uptake (PAZE 6%, hybrid 9%) gives wet-state and dimensional stability.
  （中文：环境鲁棒性：电导几乎不随相对湿度/温度变化；低吸收（PAZE 6%、杂化 9%）带来湿态与尺寸稳定性。）

## Key Numbers & Conclusions 提炼数字与结论

| Item 项 | Value 数值 | Note 备注 |
|---|---|---|
| Hybrid conductivity 杂化膜电导 | ~2–3 × 10⁻⁵ S/cm | Nearly RH-independent 几乎与 RH 无关 |
| Nafion 117 conductivity Nafion 电导 | dry 0.145 / RH98% 0.481 / 70 °C 2.02 mS/cm | Strongly RH/temp dependent 高度依赖 RH/温度 |
| Methanol permeability 甲醇渗透率 | 3.49×10⁻⁸ (HYB40-02T50) vs 2.56×10⁻⁶ cm²/s (Nafion) | ≈ 2 orders of magnitude lower 约低两个数量级 |
| Uptake 吸收 | PAZE 6% / AAO 30% / hybrid 9% | Low swelling 低溶胀 |
| Proton selectivity Y_ion/Y_H⁺ (Na/K) | Nafion 47.3/14.2/22.8%; HYB72-02T50 1.7/4.8/9.3%; HYB40-02T50 18.2/6.1/2.8% | Hybrid better than Nafion (except HYB40-02 Na⁺ 68%) |

> 🔴 manual_review: The chapter introduction states methanol permeability is "three orders of magnitude lower" than Nafion 117 (L6876–6878), but the measured data and conclusions report ~2 orders of magnitude (ratio ≈ 73×; 2.56×10⁻⁶ / 3.49×10⁻⁸). The two figures are inconsistent and must be reconciled before citation.
> （中文：本章引言称甲醇渗透率"低三个数量级"，但实测数据与结论为约两个数量级（比值≈73×），口径不一致，引用前须澄清。）

## Key Claims 关键论断

- Hybrid membranes display a high proton-selectivity unreported for any other membrane (to the authors' knowledge).
  （中文：据作者所知，杂化膜展现出其他膜未报道过的高质子选择性。）
- Ion-free dendritic columns self-assemble without cationic templates; the channels are formed by a backbone with weakly polarising bonds.
  （中文：无离子树枝状柱无需阳离子模板自组装；通道由具弱极化键的骨架构成。）
- Homeotropic-like alignment lowers proton resistance and reduces methanol crossover (~2 orders below Nafion 117).
  （中文：近垂直取向降低质子电阻并减少甲醇交叉（约低于 Nafion 117 两个数量级）。）
- The material is promising for selective-ion-transport-priority applications such as artificial photosynthesis.
  （中文：该材料在人工光合作用等以选择性离子传输为首要需求的应用中具潜力。）
