# Daehyun Microcapsule Catalogue（DH-Catalogue EN-CN 副本一）

## 一句话定位

韩国微胶囊厂商 **Daehyun（(株)大炆 / 大炫）** 的中英双语产品目录，覆盖**温变（TCC）、感光（PCC）、香味（Aroma）、防虫（M-Care）、抗菌抗虫（I-Care）**五大胶囊系列。本批作为「变色微胶囊供应方」信息源。

## 摘要

The catalogue presents Daehyun as a microcapsule specialist pursuing "The World of Microcapsule". Stated capabilities: **in-house synthesis** of ultrafine capsules, **oil- and water-based** applicability, **adjustable capsule size**, **customized activation temperature and colour**, **customized fragrance and concentration**, and optional **high light fastness (HLR)**. The **TCC (thermochromic)** line is offered as slurry or powder, in Normal and HLR grades, with a colour chart spanning roughly **15–70 °C** (common points 25/31/35/55/65 °C and 15/20/33/40/45/50/60/70 °C), and is marketed as **eco-friendly using BPAF (BPA-free)**. The **PCC (photochromic)** line changes with sunlight/UV, offers high solvent resistance, and is positioned for sunglasses, fashion and anti-counterfeit inspection (UV-exposure check is **irreversible**). **Aroma** encapsulates fat-soluble perfume oils (rose, mint, jasmine, lemon, lavender, eucalyptus, coffee, chocolate, etc.); **M-Care** uses natural repellents (citronella, eucalyptus, IR-3535); **I-Care** uses wasabi oil, phytoncide, pyrethrins and cinnamon. Company locations: Goyang-si, Gyeonggi-do (Korea) and Jiaxing, Zhejiang (China).
