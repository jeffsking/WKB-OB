# US11071659B2 Underpants-Type Disposable Diaper with Graphic Sheet (Daio Paper)

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

Granted US patent on the **manufacturing method for the non-stretchable region of a pants-type diaper containing a printed graphic sheet** — the reference source for how elastic strands are cut (snicked) without cutting through the graphic and without leaving uncut strands.

## Abstract 摘要

A method of producing an underpants-type disposable diaper forms an outer member having a **non-stretchable region**（非弹性区）at an intermediate position in the width direction and **stretchable regions**（弹性区）on both sides. Elastic members are fixed to inner and outer sheet layers only in at least two edge portions of the stretchable regions, and are NOT fixed in the portion to be the non-stretchable region. A graphic sheet is disposed between the inner and outer sheet layers in the non-stretchable region and is visible through the outer face. The key novelty is a **no-extra-sheet region**（无夹层区）disposed at the side portion of the non-stretchable region, extending outward from one side of the graphic sheet, in which no other sheet lies between the inner and outer sheet layer. The elastic members are then cut only over that no-extra-sheet region by clamping and applying pressure + heat, leaving **cut marks and idle elastic members**（切断痕与失效橡筋）over that region while the elastic members in the region overlapping the graphic sheet stay **uncut**. This equalises the pressure-receiving thickness in the front-back direction, so incomplete cutting barely occurs even with the graphic sheet present, and the graphic stays visually clean. The patent notes the prior-art failure mode: where some elastic members to be cut extend over a region not overlapping the graphic sheet, the reduced thickness means they are not cut; raising the pressure is disfavoured because it partially cuts the sheet layers and forms apparent cut marks.

## Key claims 关键论断

- **Cut marks must sit beside the graphic, never over it** — the geometric separation of the cut position from the graphic sheet is the whole invention. → [[Graphic Sheet in Non-Stretch Zone（非弹性区图形片）]]
- **Idle elastic members are a designed feature, not a defect** — they are the cut fragments/residual portions that no longer contract. → [[Elastic Strand Snicking（弹性橡筋断丝失效化）]]
- **A crotch-side weld is the retention device** when front and back outer members are separate — without it, idle elastics protrude or fall out of the opening. → [[Elastic Strand Snicking（弹性橡筋断丝失效化）]]
- **Pressure + heat clamping is used instead of perforating** — the elastic is cut through the sandwiched layers without forming holes in the inner/outer sheets. → [[Elastic Strand Snicking（弹性橡筋断丝失效化）]]
