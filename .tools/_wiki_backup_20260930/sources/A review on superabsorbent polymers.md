# A review on superabsorbent polymers

## One-line Position 一句话定位
A 2025 review mapping SAP absorption mechanisms, cross-sector applications, and the techno-economic / life-cycle trade-off between synthetic and bio-based SAP.（综述 SAP 机理、跨领域应用，以及合成与生物基 SAP 的技术经济/生命周期权衡。）

## Summary 摘要
- SAPs are hydrophilic crosslinked networks absorbing 10–1000% of their dry weight without dissolving; absorption is driven by osmotic pressure plus electrostatic repulsion of ionic groups, and is suppressed by ionic strength, particle size and pH.（吸水由渗透压与离子基团静电排斥驱动，受离子强度、粒径、pH 抑制。）
- The review covers biomedical, hygiene, agricultural, cement/concrete, slurry-dewatering and miscellaneous applications, then overlays Techno-Economic Analysis (TEA) and Life Cycle Assessment (LCA).
- Synthetic SAP (polyacrylate / polyacrylamide) dominates on cheap feedstock and performance; bio-SAP (cellulose, chitosan, starch, alginate, PGA) is renewable but often lower-swelling and sometimes higher in production-stage GWP.
- For a 28 w/w% SAP diaper, SAP contributed 25% of GWP, 22% of non-renewable cumulative energy demand, 16% abiotic depletion, 15% acidification; raw materials drove ~87% of total impact.
- Reusability is application-dependent: sodium polyacrylate achieved 4 recycle cycles in some slurry dewatering; after 5 cycles its capacity fell from 95→50%.
