## Positioning 一句话定位

A technology review of super absorbent fibre (SAF): four production routes with process windows, plus recent research fronts (green biomass, Janus capillary engineering, antibacterial integration, porosity engineering). （中文：高吸水纤维（SAF）技术综述——四条生产工艺路线与工艺窗口，以及绿色生物质、Janus 毛细工程、抗菌复合、孔隙率调控等研究前沿。）

## Summary 摘要

SAF differs from granular SAP by **form**: fibre geometry delivers large surface area, fast uptake, soft hand and — critically for hygiene cores — the ability to **mechanically immobilise SAP particles** and create inter-particle flow channels. The document maps four routes, from petrochemical copolymer spinning to cellulose-based biodegradable modification, and documents how far absorbency can be pushed (≥360 g/g hyperbranched; 200× PVA freeze-thaw) versus what is commercially realistic (60–100 g/g for the biodegradable staple route).

The application section shows SAF is dominated by hygiene (≈95% of absorbent resin consumption), with industrial (cable water-blocking, anti-condensation, oil-water separation), food, agriculture and textile uses as secondary markets.

### 四条工艺路线 Four production routes

| Route 路线 | Key steps 关键步骤 | Post-treatment 后处理 | Absorbency 吸水性能 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 干-湿法纺丝（PVA/PAA） | 溶液 A(PVA) + B(丙烯酸/丙烯酰胺中和液) + C(过硫酸钾) + D(二醛交联剂)；N₂ 保护 65–80 °C 反应 3–8 h；70–90 °C 喷丝，经 10–50 mm 空气层入丙酮凝固浴 | 热拉伸 100–150 °C ×1.5–2.5 倍 ×2–10 min；热交联 120–180 °C ×30 s–30 min | 较高 |
| 干法纺丝（一步聚合） | 单体 + PVA + 潜交联剂 + 引发剂；N₂ 保护 55–85 °C 水相聚合 6–8 h；分段控温 60–65 °C → 80–90 °C；真空脱泡 1–3 h | 热交联 120–180 °C ×3–30 min | 较高 |
| 超支化结构（天然纤维基材） | 丙烯酸 + 天然纤维在有机钛酯化催化剂（钛酸四正丁酯）下酯化 → 纤维丙烯酸酯；与接枝单体 + 三功能支化单体（TMPTA）共聚 | Na⁺/K⁺ 离子交换度 60–80%；热处理 120–135 °C；气流干燥与梳理 | **≥360 g/g** |
| 碱化醚化法（纤维素短纤） | 麻/棉/绒毛/竹或粘胶/莱赛尔 → 加湿打饼；碱化至 60–90% 得碱纤维素；醚化至取代度 0.6–0.95 得 CMC 纤维 | 酸浴中和、溶剂洗涤、上油、离心甩干；湿喂给/湿开纤/干燥 | **60–100 g/g**，纤度 1.29–10.0 dtex |

### 应用结构 Application mix

- 医疗卫生与保健：约占吸水树脂产品年消耗的 **95%**（婴儿尿布、成人失禁垫、卫生巾、医用敷料、止血材料）；
- 纤维形态优势：更薄更软、**无粒状物泄出**、加工性好，并可减少热熔胶用量（见 [[Super Absorbent Fibre（超吸水纤维 SAF）]]）；
- 其他：电缆阻水纱/带、防结露非织造布、油水分离、重金属离子吸附滤布、防火凝胶、食品保鲜、农林保水（TAL「美植袋」苗木成活率 +12.5%、缓解土壤水分损失 9–12 天）、运动服装蒸发冷却。

## Key Claims 关键论断

1. **Form is the differentiator 形态即差异** — SAF's advantage over granular SAP is structural: fibre interstices form flow channels, particles are held in place, hot-melt usage drops, and no particle leakage occurs; see [[CN 101464548 A（光缆-电缆阻水材料及其制备方法·含超吸水纤维 SAF）]] for the cable-blocking analogue.
2. **Absorbency and process complexity trade off 吸水倍率与工艺复杂度权衡** — ≥360 g/g (hyperbranched) and 200× (PVA freeze-thaw) are lab-level highs; the biodegradable staple route settles at 60–100 g/g, i.e. **green route costs absorbency today**.
3. **Cellulose routes are the biodegradable path 纤维素路线是可降解路径** — 碱化 60–90% / 醚化取代度 0.6–0.95 → CMC 纤维，60–100 g/g、1.29–10.0 dtex，可直接进入梳理-针刺非织造工艺。
4. **Directional transport beats passive absorption 定向输运优于被动吸水** — Janus 三层以毛细压差实现「只进不出」，与芯体通道化目标同源；见 [[Fluff-Free 4th-Gen Absorbent Core（无木浆第四代芯体）]]。
5. **Antibacterial integration is the commercial hook 抗菌集成是商业化抓手** — 改性伊利石/气凝胶、壳聚糖-海藻酸微球、β-环糊精精油缓释三条路线均已进入专利阶段。
6. **Hygiene remains the volume market 卫品仍是体量市场** — ≈95% of absorbent resin demand; industrial/food/agri are diversification options rather than volume replacements.
