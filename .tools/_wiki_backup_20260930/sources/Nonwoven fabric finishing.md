# Nonwoven fabric finishing

## One-line Position 一句话定位

Chapter 8 (A. Ahmed) addresses the finishing stage where nonwoven substrates are transformed into value-added, fitness-for-purpose products via wet/dry finishing, chemical finishes and lamination — the chapter where hygiene function (softening, rewetting, barrier, lotion delivery) is actually engineered.（中文：第 8 章讲后整理——通过湿/干整理、化学整理与复合层压，把无纺布基材转化为功能化成品，是卫生用品功能（柔软、回渗、阻隔、加液）真正被设计的环节。）

## Summary 摘要

- It frames finishing as either wet (washing, impregnation, dyeing, coating) or dry (calendering, embossing, emerising, microcreping), stressing end-use-driven process selection.（将整理分为湿法/干法，强调终端驱动选型。）
- Wet operations (scouring, coloration, printing), chemical finishes (antistatic, antimicrobial, lubricant, flameproof, waterproof, softener, UV) and their application methods are detailed.（详述湿整理、化学整理（抗静电/抗菌/润滑/阻燃/防水/柔软/抗UV）及施加方式。）
- Mechanical finishing (splitting, perforating, drying/relaxation, Micrex® microcreeping, calendering, surface treatments) follows.（机械整理：分切、打孔、松弛干燥、微皱、轧光、表面处理。）
- "Developing technologies" (plasma, microencapsulation, laser etching, biomimetic lotus-leaf, electrochemical) and on-line inspection close the chapter, with hygiene/medical end-uses repeatedly foregrounded.（收尾于新兴技术（等离子、微胶囊、仿生荷叶、电化学）与在线检测，卫生/医用终端反复浮现。）

## Key Claims 关键论断

- No standard routine — finishing is a design space governed by end-use.（无标准流程，整理是终端驱动的设计空间。）
- Lotion impregnation makes nonwovens active-delivery vehicles (lotion/medicated wipes).（加液浸渍使无纺布成为活性物递送载体。）
- Hydrophilic softeners = rewetters that spread liquid laterally to keep skin dry.（亲水柔软剂=回渗剂，横向铺展液体保持干爽。）
- Plasma fixes PP's poor wettability dryly; lamination engineers backsheet barrier/breathability.（等离子干法改善 PP 润湿；层压设计底膜阻隔/透气。）
