# EP0820747A1 Absorbent Article with Elastic Feature (P&G)

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

A 1996-priority P&G European application that replaces the **nonwoven + elastomer laminate** approach to diaper elastics with a **co-extruded elastomeric / non-elastomeric film**, using mechanical activation to switch elasticity on — the earliest "activation" document in the library's elastic-feature cluster.

## Abstract 摘要

The application discloses an absorbent article having an improved **composite elastic feature**（复合弹性部件）that is easier to handle during manufacture and subsequent processing. The feature comprises at least one **extruded elastomeric layer** and at least one **extruded, substantially non-elastomeric layer** which together form a film. Before the film is elasticised by **activation**（激活）, the non-elastomeric layer inhibits the elastic properties of the elastomeric layer; after activation, that inhibiting effect is lost. The two layers are preferably co-extruded from the same die head to form the film in one process step. The background section explains the problem being solved: conventional elastic features are laminate webs of an elongatable non-elastic nonwoven plus an elastic layer, which must be mechanically stretched (incrementally, or by "cut and slip" before activation) to become elastic. The drawbacks named are (i) the nonwoven's relatively weak tensile strength, which makes the elastomeric properties hard to control during cut-and-slip, assembly and mechanical stretching; and (ii) contamination of machinery by both fibres and glue. The composite elastic feature may form a segment of the topsheet, of the backsheet, or both, and may extend outward from the absorbent-core edges as an elasticised leg cuff. Nine claims cover the article and its variants (rupture at pre-determined points, non-elongatable non-elastomeric material, co-extrusion, mechanical-stretching activation, topsheet/backsheet segments, leg cuff).

## Key claims 关键论断

- **"Activation" is the invention's switch** — the film ships/stores in a non-elastic, easy-to-handle state and becomes elastic only after mechanical stretching. → [[Co-extruded Elastic Film Feature（共挤弹性膜弹性部件）]]
- **Removing the nonwoven removes two manufacturing pains** — weak nonwoven tensile strength and fibre/glue contamination. → [[Co-extruded Elastic Film Feature（共挤弹性膜弹性部件）]]
- **Co-extrusion is preferred over lamination** — one process step from the same die head. → [[Co-extruded Elastic Film Feature（共挤弹性膜弹性部件）]]
- **The elastic feature can be the topsheet, the backsheet, or both** — i.e. the elastic element is not confined to a waistband/cuff. → [[并列复合自卷曲纤维]]
