## Technical Field 技术领域

**Thermochromic wetness indication on absorbent articles/garments（吸收制品与服装上的热致变色湿态指示）**.

> "This invention relates to absorbent articles and/or garments such as **diapers, incontinent briefs, feminine pads, bandages** and alike absorbent articles and/or garments with thermochromic wetness indicator(s)."

Category coverage（品类覆盖）is the key delta vs. the earlier Howell patents: **diapers, incontinent briefs（失禁裤）, feminine pads（卫生巾）, bandages（绷带）**. Int. Cl. A61F 13/15.

## Core Mechanism 核心机理

**Thermal activation by the temperature of the excreted liquid（以排泄物温度热激活）** — a *thermochromic*, not hydrochromic, route:

> "Upon contact of urination and/or other bodily wastes ... **the temperature of absorbent articles and/or garments and/or the thermochromic wetness indicator(s) increases and the increased temperature change activates a visual color change**."

Mechanism chain（机理链）: urine/body waste contacts the article → **local temperature rises** → the thermochromic compound crosses its transition → **visual colour change**. Claim 8 requires the activation to occur "upon exposure to **temperature change generated by urine, and/or bodily wastes and/or bodily excretions**, either with **direct or indirect contact** of the absorbent article and/or garment and/or the thermochromic wetness indicator(s)."

Two chromic material classes are claimed（Claim 2）:

- **Thermochromic leuco dye(s)（热致变色隐色染料）** — conventional, ink-form, screen-printable;
- **Thermochromic liquid crystal(s)（热致变色液晶）**.

The specification also contains a broad substitution statement: covered indicators include "any suitable wetness indicator(s), including any suitable wetness indicator(s) that change color based, at least in part, on **temperature, moisture-content, and/or other suitable parameters**" — i.e. the disclosure reaches beyond strictly thermal triggers.

**No formulation, no fatty-acid/solvent table, no microcapsule process and no experimental data are disclosed**（源文明确指出：本申请为概念性/方法学公开，无配方、无实验数据）— in sharp contrast to the Howell patents.

Structural note from the source: claims 3–9 use a **"As per claim X ... may include"** quasi-independent format that stacks features incrementally, and claims 3 and 9 both anchor to the **backsheet exterior side**. ## Hygiene Implication 卫材关联

Directly relevant to the owner's **chromic-ink programme（变色油墨项目）** — this is the closest of the six sources to the active project.

1. **Category extension is the transferable insight.** Thermochromic wetness indication is pushed beyond diapers into **incontinent briefs, feminine pads and bandages**. For a K-C portfolio spanning baby, feminine and adult care, this frames chromic indication as a **platform feature, not a baby-diaper-only feature**.
2. **Thermochromic vs hydrochromic route（热变 vs 湿变路线）.** This document is firmly **thermochromic**: the trigger is the *temperature* of the excreted liquid, with a stated **35–40 °C (95–104 °F)** window. That window sits on top of skin/ambient temperature, so **false triggering from body heat, insulation, and ambient RH is the intrinsic weakness** — the source itself flags insulation and RH factors as unresolved. Compare with the hydrochromic/water-activated route in [[Water-Activated Ink Wetness Indicator & Matsui Shikiso Technical Exchange（水变油墨尿显暨松井技术交流纪要 2026-08-14）]] and the route trade-off in [[Thermochromic & Hydrochromic Materials for Diapers (Feasibility Review v3)]].
3. **Backsheet-exterior printing is the low-disruption integration path（底膜外侧面印刷 = 低扰动集成路径）.** Claims 3/5/9 place the indicator on the **exterior of the liquid-impermeable backsheet** applied by printing/painting/gluing/staining/dyeing/melting. This matches the source's own stated pain point that competing solutions "require extensive changes to the manufacturing process ... which in turn drives up the manufacturing costs". **Implication for the project: prefer an outer-backsheet printed chromic graphic that leaves the topsheet/ADL/REL/SAP stack untouched.** See [[Hygiene Chromic Ink Supplier Landscape（卫生护理变色油墨供应商调研 · 2026-06-12）]] for supplier capability against that constraint.
4. **Design freedom for branding and multi-zone indication.** Claims 4/6/7 allow arbitrary graphics, multiple positions, and arbitrary pre/post-activation colour combinations — usable for brand elements and multi-zone wetness cues without additional structural change.
5. **Visual design logic worth reusing: light ground + high-contrast activated colour** — exemplified as **tan → blue**. This is a "dry = pale/neutral, wet = saturated contrast" pattern.
6. **Reversibility and safety constraints are NOT addressed here.** The document discloses **no formulation, no microcapsule process, no migration/skin-contact data and no toxicology** — so it cannot support any safety/regulatory or reversibility decision. Those must come from supplier data and internal testing. Adjacent structural/visual prior art: [[EP 0 776 645 A1 Disposable Diaper with Visual Wetness Indicator (Unicharm)]] and [[US5197958A & US5389093A Wetness Indicating Diaper (Howell)]].
7. **Freedom-to-operate（自由实施）: NOT assessed — no conclusion is drawn here.** Facts recorded from the source only: this is an **A1 published application**, which by its nature does not itself confer exclusive rights; **grant status, maintenance and expiry are not recorded in the document** and are flagged `[待验证]` / manual review. The source's own §8 contains legal-status speculation and design-around commentary; **that analysis is deliberately not reproduced or adopted on this page**, and nothing here should be read as an FTO or invalidity opinion. Route any such question to IP counsel with the USPTO record for Appl. No. 11/434,592.
