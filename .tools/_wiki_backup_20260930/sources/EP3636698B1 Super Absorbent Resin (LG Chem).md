# EP3636698B1 Super Absorbent Resin (LG Chem)

## 一句话定位

Granted European patent (LG Chem) on a **super absorbent resin (SAP)** — the European member of LG Chem's SAP patent family, complementing US9950308B2 and US12145129B2 in this batch.

## 摘要

The patent (title: "SUPER ABSORBENT RESIN") grants a super absorbent resin. The bibliographic header lists proprietor LG Chem, Ltd. (Seoul, KR), inventors KIM Kyu Pal, KIM Gi Cheul et al., and a broad IPC set covering C08J (polymer preparation/foaming), A61F 13/53, A61L 15/60, B01J 20/26. It is the European phase of PCT/KR2018/009352 (WO2019/050183), priority KR 20170113133 (2017-09-05). Detailed claim language is in the full text; this summary captures the bibliographic and classification scope.
