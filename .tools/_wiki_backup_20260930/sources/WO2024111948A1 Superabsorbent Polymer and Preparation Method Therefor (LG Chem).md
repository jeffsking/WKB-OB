# WO2024111948A1 Superabsorbent Polymer and Preparation Method Therefor (LG Chem)

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

**LG 化学的 PCT 国际申请公开**（WO 2024/111948 A1，2024-05-30 公开，韩文本）。它的价值在于把 SAP 目标**写成五个同时成立的量化门槛**（CRC / EFFC / Vortex / 1 分钟自来水吸收量 / 加压盐水 2 小时再润湿），并给出达成这些指标的两个工艺旋钮：**表面交联液中有机溶剂（沸点 <90 ℃）的比例**与**表面交联剂密度**。是本库少见的、把"吸收速度 ↑ 与 CRC/AUP ↓ 相克"这一矛盾写进背景并试图同时破局的 SAP 专利。

## Abstract 摘要

Korean title: **고흡수성 수지 및 이의 제조방법**（English face-title: SUPERABSORBENT POLYMER AND PREPARATION METHOD THEREFOR). The invention targets a SAP having **high absorption speed together with excellent centrifuge retention capacity (CRC) and absorption under pressure (AUP), plus suppressed rewet and leakage**. The background states the trade-off plainly: absorption speed and CRC/AUP are **mutually opposed properties** — driving polymerization to high foaming to raise speed weakens the polymer core and degrades CRC and AUP. Claim 1 therefore defines the resin by the simultaneous satisfaction of five metrics: (i) CRC **≥30 g/g**; (ii) **EFFC (0.3 psi) > 29.5 g/g** where **EFFC = (CRC + 0.3 psi AUP)/2**; (iii) vortex absorption speed **<37 s**; (iv) **1-minute tap-water absorbency >115 g** (1 g of SAP swollen for 1 min in 2 L of tap water); (v) **pressurized-saline 2-hour rewet <3.7 g** (4 g of SAP swollen 120 min in 100 g of 0.9 wt% NaCl aqueous solution, filter paper placed on the resin, pressed at 0.75 psi for 5 min, measured as the water exuded back into the filter paper). Claim 2 narrows to CRC **32–40 g/g**, EFFC **30–40 g/g**, vortex **20–30 s**; claim 3 tightens rewet to **≤3.4 g**. Claim 4 is the independent method claim: prepare a monomer composition (acrylic acid-based monomer with at least partly neutralized acidic groups, internal cross-linker, polymerization initiator) → cross-link polymerize into a hydrogel polymer → dry, pulverize and classify into a base resin → form a surface cross-linked layer in the presence of a **surface cross-linking solution containing a surface cross-linker, water, and an organic solvent whose boiling point is below 90 °C**, wherein the **water/organic solvent weight ratio is ≥1.0 and <3.0** and the **organic solvent content of the solution is ≥24 wt%** (claim 5: 25–30 wt%). Claim 6 adds a normalized dose measure: **surface cross-linker density = (weight of surface cross-linker / weight of water) × 1,000**, being **≥1.0 and <17.0**. Claim 7 lists the solvents (methanol, ethanol, isopropanol, acetone, ethyl acetate). Claim 8 sets a downstream quality gate: after surface cross-linking, agglomerates above **850 µm** shall be **<4.0 wt%**. Claims 9–10 add and dose an inorganic additive (silica, clay, alumina, silica-alumina composite, titania) at **0.01–5 parts per 100 parts by weight of the SAP**.

## Key claims 关键论断

- **这是一份"用五个数同时约束"的 SAP 权利要求**——不用结构限定，而用 CRC + EFFC + Vortex + 1 min 自来水 + 加压盐水 2 h 再润湿 五项并列门槛来圈住目标树脂。→ [[SAP性能指标（CRC AUL GBP Vortex）]]
- **"速度 vs CRC/AUP 相克"被写进了背景**：高发泡聚合提速会削弱聚合物芯强度。这解释了为什么本案把工艺旋钮放在**表面交联液的溶剂/水比例**上，而不是继续加发泡。→ [[SAP（高吸水树脂）]]
- **两个可执行旋钮**：溶剂 ≥24 wt%（优选 25–30 wt%）且沸点 <90 ℃；交联剂密度 1.0–17.0（按每 1,000 份水计）。→ [[SAP性能指标（CRC AUL GBP Vortex）]]
- **下游质量也被写成权利要求**：850 µm 以上团聚物 <4.0 wt%；无机物 0.01–5 phr。这是把"URA SURFACE 交联后结块"这个常见良率问题升格为技术特征。
- **检索报告给出了诚实的风险信号**：US 5,597,873 A 被列为 **Y**（结合后显而易见），且 LG 自己更早的 **KR 10-2018-0073334 A** 也在引证之列；另有 claims 7–10 的形式缺陷。→ 见下方 manual_review。
