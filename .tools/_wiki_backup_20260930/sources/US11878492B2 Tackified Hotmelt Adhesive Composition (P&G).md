# US11878492B2 Tackified Hotmelt Adhesive Composition (P&G)

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

P&G's granted patent on a **four-component polyolefin hotmelt built for nonwoven–film bonding in diapers**, whose distinguishing feature is that the **base polymer is itself a two-copolymer metallocene blend split by crystallinity**, and whose performance is expressed as **rheology at body temperature** rather than as a bond-strength number.

## Abstract 摘要

The patent claims a hotmelt composition comprising a **low molecular weight metallocene-catalyzed polymer** with peak molecular weight below 130,000 g/mol, a **high molecular weight polyolefin** with peak molecular weight from about 130,000 to about 700,000 g/mol, an **amorphous polyolefin** with enthalpy of crystallization less than 10 J/g, and a **tackifier**. Claim 1 additionally requires that the low-MW metallocene polymer itself comprise **two** low-MW metallocene-catalyzed propylene-ethylene copolymers — a first with enthalpy of crystallization below 20 J/g and a second above 20 J/g. The composition is described as particularly useful in absorbent articles such as diapers, giving especially good **nonwoven–film bonds** and also usable for nonwoven–nonwoven bonds. Claim 10 defines an alternative route in which the amorphous polyolefin is a metallocene-catalyzed propylene-based polymer with Mw <10,000 g/mol, viscosity <500 mPa·s at 170 ℃ and **no DSC-detectable melting point**. Claim 13 caps mineral oil at **less than 10 wt%** — an explicit low-plasticizer stance. Claim 15 fixes the rheological target set at 37 ℃ (body temperature), which is unusual framing: the composition is defined by how it behaves on the skin, not only by how it bonds. Claims 16–18 cover the bonded absorbent article and the bonding process (contact or spray applicator, line speed >2 m/s).

## Key claims 关键论断

- **The polymer architecture is the invention, not the additives** — a three-way polyolefin molecular-weight/crystallinity split plus tackifier, with no reliance on styrenic block copolymer or large oil load. → [[Polyolefin Hot Melt Adhesive（聚烯烃热熔胶）]]
- **Crystallinity is the control variable** — enthalpy of crystallization thresholds (10 / 20 / 25–45 J/g) divide each component's role. → [[APAO（非晶态聚α-烯烃）]]
- **Low oil content is a deliberate formulation stance** (<10 wt% mineral oil) — relevant for odour, migration and core wet-strength concerns. → [[Polyolefin Hot Melt Adhesive（聚烯烃热熔胶）]]
- **37 ℃ rheology is used as a proxy for wear comfort** — G' <9.5 MPa and cross-over <70 ℃ govern whether the adhesive reads as stiff at body temperature. → [[Polyolefin Hot Melt Adhesive（聚烯烃热熔胶）]]
- **High line speed (>2 m/s) is part of the claim** — bonding must survive modern diaper line speeds. → [[Polyolefin Hot Melt Adhesive（聚烯烃热熔胶）]]
