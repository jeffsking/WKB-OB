# Zihua 上海紫华薄膜科技公司介绍（KC 2026-06-02）

## 一句话定位

**上海紫华薄膜科技有限公司（Shanghai Zihua Film Technology，紫江控股全资子公司）** 面向 KC 的 2026-06-02 公司介绍，主营**纸尿裤背层 PE 膜（吹塑 + 流延）与印刷**。本批作为「卫材背层膜供应商」信息源。

## 摘要

Zihua is presented as a wholly-owned subsidiary of **Shanghai Zijiang Holdings**（紫江控股，1981 创立、1999 上市，中国最大包装集团之一，员工 7,000+、子公司 80+），with ~**30 years** of PE film experience and **ISO 9001 / ISO 14001** certification. It claims to serve high-end clients and to have been **KC's first local PE film supplier in China**, working with **KCCN since 1999**; first shipments: **KC Softex 2024-05**, **KC Costa Rica 2024-12**, **KC Peru 2025-10**; ongoing projects: KC Softex BCC sweety, KC ECM OEM (Winsun) BCC 15 gsm, KC Vietnam AFC purple. Capability-wise, film capacity is split across **blown** and **casting** lines, with printing via **gravure** and **flexo**; a dedicated **"available capacity for KC" (MT/month)** is quoted per process. Quality kit includes **ISRA** detectors and **Thermo Fisher** thickness gauges on all filming/printing machines. Innovation items: **low-GSM blown film at 12–13 gsm with hydrostatic pressure ≥65 mmH₂O**; **conductive-ink printed smart sensor** for real-time wetness/temperature monitoring; **fully automated film packaging** (claimed industry-first end-to-end supply-chain connectivity). The Asia backsheet benchmark slide lists market WVTR around **1000–3000** with blowing/casting processes and **Henkel/Focus** adhesives.
