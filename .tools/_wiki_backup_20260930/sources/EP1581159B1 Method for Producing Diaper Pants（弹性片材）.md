# EP1581159B1 Method for Producing Diaper Pants（弹性片材）

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

**SCA Hygiene Products AB 的已授权欧洲专利（B1）**，讲的是**裤型纸尿裤怎么被"折边 + 侧缝焊接"造出来**：把一侧（前或后）的侧边缘向内折到置于两侧部的**支撑件（supports）**上，沿横向折叠线折合后，折边与另一侧的展开侧边缘**单层搭接**，在支撑件区域内超声或热焊接成缝。本页还给出量化到毫米与 N/25mm 的**可撕开侧缝（tearable side seam）**工艺窗口，是本库对标 CD 裤式加工工艺与「一撕脱裤」换码方式的关键参数来源。

## Abstract 摘要

Title: "METHOD FOR PRODUCING DIAPER PANTS OF THE DISPOSABLE TYPE" (DE: VERFAHREN ZUR HERSTELLUNG VON WINDELHÖSCHEN VOM EINWEGTYP; FR: PROCEDE DE FABRICATION DE COUCHES-CULOTTES JETABLES). The blank comprises an absorbent body (1) enclosed between an inner liquid-permeable cover sheet (2) and an outer cover sheet (3), at least one cover sheet being thermally weldable, and further comprising front portion (4), rear portion (5) and a crotch portion (6) delimited by leg openings (10), with side parts extending laterally outside the absorbent body from the waist band to the leg openings. The **characterizing steps**: side-edge portions (5A, 5B) of either the rear or the front portion are **folded in towards the inner cover sheet over supports (15; 17)** that extend along the full length of that portion; the blank is then folded about a transverse line in the crotch portion so that the waist-forming end edges come to lie edge-to-edge and the folded-in side edges **overlap** the side edges of the other portion; finally the overlapping side-edge portions are **welded within the support area by ultrasonic or thermal welding**. The description gives the advantages: because the side edges are connected **overlapping one another**, an aesthetically pleasing weld seam is obtained **without turning the blank inside out**; because the seam is exposed **only to shear forces** it can be made **tearable**; and because they overlap only **singly**, the joint is **not bulky**. Claim 6 additionally requires the innermost overlapping side edge to end in a **grip part situated outside the weld seam (12)** — the physical pull-tab for tearing the pants open.

## Key claims 关键论断

- **这是一条"免内翻"的工序路线**——不用把空白片材里外翻面，靠单层搭接就能得到外观好的焊缝，并因此**不臃肿**。→ [[CD Pants Converting Process（CD裤式产品加工工艺）]]
- **焊缝只承受剪切力，所以能被做成"既够牢又能手撕"**——这正是可脱卸/ Standing-change 功能的结构基础。→ [[Pant-Type Disposable Absorbent Article（裤型一次性吸收物品）]]
- **三组数值是本案最有价值的转移know-how**：搭接 < 4 mm、焊缝宽 8 mm、强度 10–45 N/25mm，且明确焊缝**可撕开**。→ [[CD Pants Converting Process（CD裤式产品加工工艺）]]
- **折边由支撑件（turning the supports）完成，且待焊接区保持无张力**——说明弹性侧片在这个过程中由夹具托住，不让弹性参与定尺寸。→ [[Pant-Type Disposable Absorbent Article（裤型一次性吸收物品）]]
