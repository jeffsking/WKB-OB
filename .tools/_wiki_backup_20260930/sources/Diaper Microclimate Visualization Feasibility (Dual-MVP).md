# Diaper Microclimate Visualization Feasibility (Dual-MVP)

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

The **decision-frame page for microclimate visualization**: a conditional GO on a dual-MVP (temperature + humidity) indicator strategy, with hard safety boundaries, a supplier scoring frame, an RFQ strategy and a 12-month phased roadmap. It is the batch's "**how the programme is allowed to proceed**" page.
本页是**微气候可视化决策框架页**：对「温度 + 湿度」双 MVP 指示方案给出有条件 GO，并明确安全硬边界、供应商评分框架、RFQ 策略与 12 个月分阶段路线图，回答「**该项目被允许以什么条件推进**」。

## Summary 摘要

The recommendation is a **conditional GO for a dual-MVP strategy**, implemented as a low-regret approach focusing on two separate visual signals (MVP A temperature, MVP B humidity) rather than a complex combined sensor. The philosophy is "**simple visual signals, not precision instrumentation**" — providing a reason to believe (RTB) that helps caregivers act, not a clinical thermometer. The priority is explicit: **do not replace the existing wetness indicator (WI)**, focus on differentiation learning first. The hard boundary is zero compromise on absorbency, fit, cost, skin health and manufacturability.
结论为**有条件 GO 的双 MVP 策略**：以低后悔（low-regret）方式并行推进温度（MVP A）与湿度（MVP B）两个独立视觉信号，而非复杂的复合传感器。理念是「**做简单视觉信号，不做精密仪器**」——提供帮助看护者行动的可信理由（RTB），而非临床温度计。优先级明确：**不替代现有尿显（WI）**，先做差异化学习。硬边界为吸收、贴合、成本、皮肤健康与可制造性零让步。

Two technology routes are restated. **Reversible thermochromic (temperature)** — reversible phase change via microcapsules, applied as printed stripes or laminated label patches on the outer cover; activation window 30–37 °C with design targets 32/34/36 °C, response time ≤120 s to a recognisable state, readable after ≥10 thermal cycles; the constraint is that **dry-state thermal signalling is technically demanding** and any claim path requires stringent internal bench data. **Hydrochromic (humidity & exposure)** — liquid/moisture triggers resin dissolution or complexation (invisible → visible blue/cyan); mainstream path is hot-melt wetness glue (84% of the market, lowest technical risk, adapted to current converting lines), with water/solvent-based ink printed directly on PE breathable film as the eco/graphic alternative; bench metrics are response ≤60 s and 5/10/20 mL liquid-exposure bench tests.
两条技术路线被重申。**可逆温致变色（温度）**——经微胶囊实现可逆相变，以外层印刷条或复合标签片形式施加；激活窗口 30–37 ℃、设计目标点 32/34/36 ℃，响应时间 ≤120 s 达可辨识态，≥10 次热循环后仍可读；约束是**干态热信号技术难度高**，任何宣称路径都需严格的内部台架数据支撑。**湿致变色（湿度与暴露）**——液体/湿气触发树脂溶解或络合（不可见 → 可见蓝/青色）；主流路径为热熔尿显胶（占市场 84%，技术风险最低、适配现有转化线），水/溶剂型油墨直接印于 PE 透气膜为环保/图形替代路径；台架指标为响应 ≤60 s、以 5/10/20 mL 液体暴露试验验证。

Hard boundaries are then stated: chemical limits (100% BPA-free, zero formaldehyde, zero heavy metals) and a regulatory framework (ISO 10993 for cytotoxicity/irritation, REACH, FDA, GB/T 35613-2017); a caution that **some legacy technology contains trace formaldehyde** and requires strict auditing; and a mandatory rule of absolute zero back-transfer, visible colour bleeding or direct skin contact — the indicator lives **on the outer cover / backsheet window**, never in the skin-contact layer (topsheet & core).
随后给出硬边界：化学限值（100% 无 BPA、零甲醛、零重金属）与法规框架（ISO 10993 细胞毒性/刺激性、REACH、FDA、GB/T 35613-2017）；提醒**部分传统技术含微量甲醛**须严格审核；并确立强制规则——绝对禁止回渗、可见渗色与直接皮肤接触，指示层位于**外层/底膜窗口**，绝不进入皮肤接触层（面层与芯体）。

## Extracted numbers and conclusions 提炼的数字与结论

### MVP framework MVP 框架

| MVP | Priority 优先级 | Function 功能 | Verification gates 验证门 | Core risks 核心风险 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A · Temperature indicator** 温度指示 | P1 | Visual RTB for thermal comfort / cooling; carries no structural function. Colour script: blue = cool, green = comfort, red = warm | Stable colour shifts in the targeted 30–37 °C zones; **>70%** consumer comprehension of too hot / cold / comfort | The trust gap (exterior temperature vs perceived skin feel); UV stability and long-term drift; premature triggering in shelf storage or transport |
| **B · Humidity / wet-exposure indicator** 湿度指示 | P1 | Visual cue for wet exposure, moisture accumulation and wet-zone expansion; adjacent to the existing WI | Stable activation post-insult (**≤60 s**); distinct consumer differentiation from the traditional WI | Visual/semantic overlap with the primary WI; interference from urine pH, topsheet lotions or SAP dust; reaction speed too slow (unnoticed) or too fast (redundant) |
| **C · Combined indicator** 复合指示 | P2 | Determine whether caregivers can decode two simultaneous, distinct signals without semantic fighting; strict colour separation (blue/cyan = humidity, red/green = temperature) | Learning prototype only — if comprehension fails or error rates are high in Phase 4, halt and revert to single-function MVPs | Semantic conflict; added complexity |

### Safety and compliance 安全与合规

- Chemical limits: 100% BPA-free, zero formaldehyde, zero heavy metals.
- Regulatory: ISO 10993 (cytotoxicity / irritation), REACH, FDA, GB/T 35613-2017.
- Mandatory rule: absolute zero back-transfer, visible colour bleeding, or direct skin contact; powder forms banned from skin layers until validated.
- Caution: some legacy technology contains trace formaldehyde — strict auditing required.

### Supplier landscape and scoring 供应商格局与评分

- **Thermochromic specialists**: CTI (US, full spectrum, requires diaper customisation); Matsui (Japan, legacy leader, safety checks required); OliKrom (France, custom temperature bands).
- **Hydrochromic (hot-melt) leaders**: Bostik, Henkel, H.B. Fuller — the mature 84% hot-melt market; lowest risk for MVP B.
- **Dual-capability & regional partners**: DaeHyun (Korea, full material pool); Qianshebian / Guangzhou Ledi (China, cost-effective regional speed).
- Scoring frame uses five columns — technical maturity, safety/compliance, diaper experience, capacity, cost — with Matsui, CTI, DaeHyun, Bostik and Henkel scored across all five (⭐ ratings only; no numeric weights given).
- Strategic takeaway: for MVP A engage CTI & Matsui for advanced microencapsulation; for MVP B engage Bostik & Henkel to leverage existing hygiene hot-melt infrastructure.

### RFQ strategy (demand exact constraints) RFQ 策略

Stop asking "do you have a product?" and demand exact constraints: **performance** — can you hit a visible 30–37 °C reversible transition specifically on PE film or nonwoven substrates? **bleeding** — do you guarantee zero colour bleeding for liquid-exposure hydrochromics? **process** — what are your exact converting constraints (drying temperatures, lamination pressure, coating methods)? **safety** — do you have proven, documented non-skin-contact hygiene migration data? **logistics** — what are the exact MOQs and lead times for a diaper feasibility prototype?

### 12-month roadmap 12 个月路线图

| Phase 期 | Timing 时点 | Scope 内容 | Gate 门 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phase 0 | Month 0–1 | Card / coupon sampling (film / nonwoven patches off-diaper) | Commitments secured for **5+ supplier samples** |
| Phase 1 | Month 2–3 | Outer-layer dummy build (integrated into outer cover, no human testing) | **≥2 stable colour systems identified**; legibility verified and converting-machine compatibility confirmed (no curling / smudging) |
| Phase 3 | Month 5–7 | (per deck sequence) response-time and contrast validation | Response times (≤120 s temp, ≤60 s hydro) and contrast metrics met |
| Phase 4 | Month 7–9 | Consumer comprehension testing (dry/wet state comparisons) | **≥70% correct signal decoding** (too hot/cold or wet exposure) without confusing the signals |
| Launch 量产 | Month 11–12 | Mass-production feasibility | Final Go/No-Go based on BOM, final cost and compliance audit |

### Critical success factors 关键成功要素

- **Data vs emotion**: the product is a visual cue to help caregivers know when to act, not a precision clinical thermometer — overpromising will kill the claim.
- **The trust gap**: the biggest risk is consumer perception (the diaper looks hot but feels cool to the touch); Phase 4 comprehension testing is non-negotiable.
- **The final gate**: if at any point the technology compromises absorbency, fit, cost or skin safety, the MVP is immediately halted — **core function always wins**.
