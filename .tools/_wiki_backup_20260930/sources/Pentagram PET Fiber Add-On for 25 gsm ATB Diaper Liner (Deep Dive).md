# Pentagram PET Fiber Add-On for 25 gsm ATB Diaper Liner (Deep Dive)

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

A simulation-led **formulation and validation plan for adding pentagram (five-lobed) PET to a 25 gsm two-card air-through-bonded PE/PET ES liner** — it defines the recommended add-on level, denier, placement, supplier spec and pilot DOE. It is the batch's "**how much shaped PET is enough, and what breaks if you add more**" page.
本页是**「在 25 gsm 双梳理 ATB PE/PET ES 面层中掺入五角星形 PET」的配方与验证方案**：给出推荐添加量、旦数、放置层位、供应商规格与中试 DOE，回答「**加多少才够、加多了会坏在哪**」。

## Summary 摘要

The deck frames the 25 gsm liner as an ATB-bonded PE/PET ES bicomponent staple web built from two carding feeds — fine 2D ES for softness and coverage, coarser 4D ES for loft and permeability — where hot-air bonding relies on **PE sheath softening while the PET core stays structural**. The target is to raise capillary absorbency and machine-direction (MD) urine distribution *before* liquid reaches the SAP-rich core, **without** turning the topsheet into a liquid-storing ADL.
本资料把 25 gsm 面层定位为 ATB 粘合的 PE/PET ES 双组分短纤网：两次梳理喂入——细旦 2D ES 负责柔软与覆盖、粗旦 4D ES 负责蓬松与透气；热风粘合依赖 **PE 皮层软化，PET 芯层保持结构**。目标是液体到达富 SAP 芯体**之前**提高毛细吸收与机向（MD）尿液铺展，**同时不把面层变成储液型 ADL**。

Three mechanisms are proposed for the star cross-section: (1) **capillary rails** — open axial grooves act as low-volume liquid pathways once a hydrophilic finish lowers the contact angle, favouring spreading over local pooling; (2) **MD bias** — adding profiled PET to the second card can bias lateral urine distribution along MD; (3) **loft support** — PET is stiffer than PE/PET ES at bonding temperature, so a moderate addition helps preserve void volume under light pressure.
星形截面给出三条机理：①**毛细导轨**——亲水整理降低接触角后，轴向开口沟槽成为低容量液路，利于铺展而非局部驻液；②**MD 偏置**——在第二梳理加入异形 PET 可把横向尿液分配偏向机向；③**蓬松支撑**——粘合温度下 PET 比 PE/PET ES 更硬，适度添加可保持受压下的空隙体积。

The main constraint is stated bluntly: **one-component PET is not an ES binder**. The deck therefore recommends pre-blending star PET into the **lower-card** 4D ES stream only, keeping the skin-side card untouched until hand feel and wetback are proven.
核心约束写得很直接：**单组分 PET 不是 ES 粘结剂**。因此建议只把星形 PET 预混进**下层梳理**的 4D ES 流，贴肤侧梳理保持不动，直到手感与回渗被验证。
