# Consumer Relevancy of FIUP Method（FIUP 方法的消费者相关性论证）

## Positioning 定位

Method-justification memo（2025-02）tabulating FIUP（TTM-00643）against P&G's AFC method parameter-by-parameter, citing wetting-pattern studies and pressure research as the consumer-relevancy evidence chain. Companion to [[FIUP Test Method TTM-00643（女性摄入与回渗测试法）]] and the dryness claim substantiation work（TL-15927/R164444）。方法参数的"为什么这么设"证据页。）

## Summary 摘要

Parameters compared: insult position — P&G mid-pad vs FIUP Total Length/12 forward & backward of center, grounded in wetting-pattern studies showing WP centroid sits 2.5–11 mm from pad center (R168596 appendix, studies 37110/37470); fluid — saline both（ionic strength representative）; amount — per-HUT, FIUP uses 75th-percentile total loading ÷ 3 across three insults（majority ≤2 voids, ≥11% have ≥3）; flow rate 8 mL/s（peak flow for adults with bladder/urinary dysfunction, McSorley 1991）; temperature 37 °C vs room temp（body temperature more relevant）; insult pressure 0.25 vs 0.30 psi（measured average on-pad 0.26–0.93 psi, max 1.8 psi）. Blotter: P&G 31gsm collagen（skin-like）vs KC 100lb verigood 88×300 mm（collects any returning fluid — more rigorous lock-up test, larger coverage）; rewet weight 0.5 vs 1.0 psi; rewet timing — 2 min after insult and 2 min rewet time vs immediate/15 s（2-min waits more rigorous for collecting available fluid）; rewet footprint 61 mm dia or 25×50 mm（localized weight acceptable since pressure uneven）. Key references: TTM-00643, R168596, ULS 34724, McSorley X-3893, AFC pressure study 37932, Emily Morin rewet methodology, TL-15927/R164444 appendix C（blotter vs collagen）.（逐参数对照 + 参考文献链。）

## Key Numbers & Conclusions 提炼的数字与结论

| 项目 Item | FIUP (KC) | P&G (AFC) | 消费者相关性依据 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Insult position | Total Length/12（中心前后） | Mid-pad | WP 质心偏离中心 ±2.5–11 mm（R168596） |
| Fluid / Temp | Saline / 37 ℃ | Saline / 室温 | 尿液离子强度；体温更相关 |
| Flow rate | 8 mL/s | ? | 膀胱/尿道功能障碍成人峰值流速 |
| Insult pressure | 0.25 psi | 0.30 psi | 实测贴身压力 0.26–0.93 psi（max 1.8） |
| Single insult size | 75 分位总载荷 ÷ 3 ×3 次 | per HUT | 多数 ≤2 次，≥11% 用户 ≥3 次 |
| Blotter | 100lb verigood 88×300 mm | 31gsm collagen 70Ø | blotter 捕获全部回液=更严苛；面积覆盖更大 |
| Rewet | 0.5 psi / 2 min 后 / 2 min 时长 | 1.0 psi / 即时 / 15 s | 压力不均匀→局部加权可接受；2 min 更严苛 |
