# GB 7918.1-1987 Microbiological Test Methods for Cosmetics General（化妆品微生物标准检验方法 总则）

## 一句话定位

The **general-provisions part**（总则）of the 1987 cosmetic microbiology method series — sample-preparation rules per product matrix (liquid/oily/cream/solid) plus the SCDLP medium definition; the operational foundation beneath GB 7918.2 (plate count) and GB 7918.5 (S. aureus).

## Summary 摘要

GB 7918.1-87 was issued with parts 2–5 on 1987-05-28 (effective 1987-10-01). The surviving transcription documents its core contribution: matrix-specific 1:10 sample preparations — water-soluble liquids straight into saline; oily liquids emulsified with sterile liquid paraffin and Tween-80 at 40–44 ℃; hydrophilic creams settled in saline; hydrophobic creams/lipsticks ground with paraffin + Tween-80 or homogenized in SCDLP medium; solids dispersed at 30–32 ℃. The lecithin/Tween-80 (and SCDLP) neutralization logic established here is what lets plate counts and pathogen screens work on preserved cosmetics — the same trick GB 7918.2 and 7918.5 inherit. Note the transcription is partial (starts mid-clause 2.1); earlier general clauses are not available in the source.
