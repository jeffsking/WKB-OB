## Summary 摘要

The FBI team（成立一年余，6 人）organizes its portfolio into demand spaces: leakage protection（absorbent core platform = COCO V6; functional liner platform = online 3D liner）, healthy skin（skincare/Aion 2.0 filmless, breathable 10× filmless diaper, waistband air-window holes）, and three new spaces from IPC marketing — thermal regulation, microbiome, odor control — with FBI focusing on 2029+ launches, some in 2028. COCO V6：pilot line set up in April (Tianjin pilot is low-speed ~100 m/min; high-speed engineering solution underway); success benchmark = 5.6 Super X; channel-core trials showed channel position near the insult point improves MD distribution, a straight center channel works regardless of insult location, and shift-core（crotch density retained）improved 3rd cradle intake and U-shape results; two-step plan (channel design → capillary materials); China launch pulled from 2029 to 2028. Functional liner：online ultrasonic bonding 3D liner (Project Max) with a Guangdong Foshan equipment vendor whose duplicable ultrasonic horns are interchangeable with existing Herman units — >30% raw-material saving vs offline material; Brazil offline-first next year, online 2028; China (Vista, T4M) 2027. Origami coil with vendor Zheng Yu is PENDING (IP concerns + COCO V6 resource priority). Other platforms：try-light face-2 (trilobal top fibre, 12 gsm low-melting modified PP spunbond bottom), hummingbird (no progress; supplier has only one pilot line), SMS/EAA filmless leak barrier ($2+ cost increase; single-layer risky for AB sizes — side PE film strips + narrow SMS center solution), air window (waistband holes; 2028 T5 target), temperature indicator (water-soluble A+B reversible/irreversible chemistry, textile-proven, trigger point 35–37 °C to be set with vendor), botanic ingredient platform (zinc oxide not claimable in China; screen 10 → 2 botanic ingredients on 4 dimensions incl. gypsum/urine inhibition; microencapsulation for long-lasting efficacy), microbiome (probiotics/postbiotics), odor control (pre-use powder in package; post-use BM/urine), smart diaper (first stage temperature/humidity/pH/urine volume/BM detection; health indicators deferred over accuracy risk), and a humidity-activated shape-transforming fibre shown as inspiration material.（平台全景：芯体、功能面层、护肤、温控、微生物组、气味控制、智能指示。）
