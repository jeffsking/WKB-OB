## Summary 摘要

The snapshot reports 10 additions and 194 cumulative entries. Industry items include paired supplier-visit plans, a regional industry report, a research recording, and a mechanochromic waistband-tension proposal. Technical items include a bicomponent-fiber supplier profile, a spunbond process article, and a low-melt copolyester bicomponent-fiber breakthrough report. Research items include a nonwoven laboratory article and a consolidated national-standard collection covering diapers, SAP（高吸水树脂）, fluff pulp（绒毛浆）, topsheet, wipes, disinfection products, infant textiles, degradation evaluation, microbiology, and chemical migration. The index thus marks the point where a regulatory compliance base and a supply-base profile layer were established alongside early REL-related material.
