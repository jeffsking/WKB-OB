# Korea Visa Grant Notice APEV03R08CH（APEV03R08CH-new passport）

## 一句话定位

A personal Korean visa grant notice (C-3-9, multiple-entry) for the knowledge-base owner. Indexed for traceability; **no derived page** per the Personal-block rule.

## 摘要

The document is a Republic of Korea Ministry of Justice **Visa Grant Notice（사증발급확인서）**. Key fields: Status of Stay **C-3-9**, Period of Stay **90 Days**, **Multiple** entries, Date of Issue **2025-08-28**, Visa Validity **2030-08-28**, Issuing Authority **Korean Consulate General in Shanghai**, Visa No. **SH2500303509**.
