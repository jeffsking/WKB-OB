# Pentagram-Free Shaped PET Alternatives for 25 gsm Diaper Topsheet

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

The **counterweight deck to the pentagram deep dive**: a literature deep dive + processability screening that recommends *rounded* profiled PET cross-sections instead of sharp star shapes, and gives add-on/dpf windows plus a screening matrix. It is the batch's "**shape must be manufacturable, not just sharp**" page.
本页是**五角星形深度评估的对照页**：以文献综述 + 可纺性筛选替代尖角星形截面，给出添加量/旦数窗口与筛选矩阵，回答「**截面必须可制造，而不仅是够尖**」。

## Summary 摘要

The deck opens from the current 25 gsm topsheet platform — air-through bonded PE/PET ES with 2D + 4D fibres in a two-carding build — and notes that pentagram PET has high capillary promise but real process risk in extrusion and drawing: sharp star-like cross-sections raise spinneret pressure drop, tip break risk and draw instability.
资料从当前 25 gsm 面层平台（2D + 4D PE/PET ES、双梳理、穿透热风粘合）出发，指出五角星形 PET 毛细潜力高，但在挤出与拉伸段存在实质工艺风险：尖锐星形截面会抬高喷丝板压降、尖端断裂风险与拉伸不稳定性。

It then screens alternative cross-sections that keep the capillary-channel wicking logic with better manufacturability: **rounded trilobal** (best balance, easiest industrial scale-up), **rounded 4-groove / capillary-channel** (most similar concept, best capillary boost, moderate tooling complexity), **Y-shaped** (useful middle ground), **hollow trilobal** (specialty bulk option) and **plus / cross-shaped** (not first choice). The recommendation is explicitly to use rounded corners and moderate groove depth, and to avoid overly sharp lobes when extrusion robustness is the priority.
随后筛选出保留毛细通道逻辑、但可制造性更好的截面：**圆角三叶形**（综合平衡最佳、最易工业化放大）、**圆角四沟槽 / 毛细通道型**（与五角星形概念最接近、毛细提升最强，工装复杂度中等）、**Y 形**（有益的中间方案）、**中空三叶形**（特种蓬松选项）、**十字形**（非首选）。建议明确：用圆角与中等沟深，在可纺性优先时避免过尖叶瓣。

The design window is then set: 10–20 wt% rounded trilobal (start 15%) at 1.5–2.0 dpf; 8–15 wt% rounded 4-groove (start 12%) at 1.3–1.8 dpf; 10–18 wt% Y-shaped at 1.5–2.2 dpf; 10–15 wt% hollow trilobal at 2.0–3.0 dpf — all with PE/PET ES held at 80–90 wt%, the existing hydrophilic finish package maintained, and staple length matched to the current carding line (typically 38–44 mm).
设计窗口为：圆角三叶形 10–20 wt%（起 15%）@1.5–2.0 dpf；圆角四沟槽 8–15 wt%（起 12%）@1.3–1.8 dpf；Y 形 10–18 wt% @1.5–2.2 dpf；中空三叶形 10–15 wt% @2.0–3.0 dpf——同时 PE/PET ES 保持 80–90 wt%、沿用现有亲水整理包、切断长度匹配现有梳理线（通常 38–44 mm）。

### Recommended design window 推荐设计窗口

| Shape 截面 | Add-on 添加量 (wt% of total web) | Suggested dpf 建议旦数 | Development comment 开发意见 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rounded trilobal 圆角三叶形 | 10–20 wt% (start 15%) | 1.5–2.0 dpf | Best balance of wicking, softness, processability 综合最佳 |
| Rounded 4-groove / capillary-channel | 8–15 wt% (start 12%) | 1.3–1.8 dpf | Stronger capillary guidance; moderate tooling complexity |
| Y-shaped | 10–18 wt% (start 15%) | 1.5–2.2 dpf | Useful backup shape 备用方案 |
| Hollow trilobal 中空三叶形 | 10–15 wt% | 2.0–3.0 dpf | Use when loft and dry feel are also priorities |

### Platform rules 平台规则

1. Keep PE/PET ES bondable fibre at **80–90 wt%** for first trials.
2. For one-component shaped PET, **do not start above 20 wt%** in a 25 gsm ATB topsheet.
3. Maintain the current hydrophilic finish package or equivalent.
4. Match staple length to the current carding line (typically 38–44 mm).

### Suggested first DOE 建议首个 DOE

| Trial | Shape 截面 | Level 添加 | dpf |
|---|---|---|---|
| T0 | Baseline 基准 | 0 wt% | — |
| T1 | Rounded trilobal | 10 wt% | 1.5 |
| T2 | Rounded trilobal | 15 wt% | 1.5 |
| T3 | Rounded trilobal | 20 wt% | 2.0 |
| T4 | Rounded 4-groove | 12 wt% | 1.5 |
| T5 | Y-shaped | 15 wt% | 1.8 |

### What to measure 需测指标（liquid distribution dashboard）

Strike-through time 穿透时间 · Rewet 回渗 · MD spread length MD 铺展长度 · CD spread width CD 铺展宽度 · Acquisition under load 载荷下摄入 · Softness / handfeel 柔软手感 · Bond integrity / lint 粘合完整性与起毛 · Tensile & elongation 拉伸与伸长。

Expected: top candidates may improve MD wetting spread by **about 10–25%**; CD spread may improve modestly if web orientation is also optimised; too much mono-PET can reduce bond integrity and softness. "The best formulation is the one with the best **total diaper performance**, not the highest single wicking number."
预期：优选方案 MD 润湿铺展可提升约 **10–25%**；若同时优化网面取向，CD 铺展亦可小幅改善；单组分 PET 过量会降低粘合完整性与柔软度。「最优配方是**整裤综合表现最好**的那一个，而不是单项芯吸数据最高的那一个。」
