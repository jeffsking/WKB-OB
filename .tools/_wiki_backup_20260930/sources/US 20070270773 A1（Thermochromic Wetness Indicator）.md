# US 2007/0270773 A1（Thermochromic Wetness Indicator / 热变色尿湿指示 — Mackey）

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

A US published patent application (inventor: Suzan Delain Mackey, individual) on **absorbent articles with a thermochromic wetness indicator** — an early prior-art reference for colour-change wetness signalling, adjacent to the KB's thermochromic/hydrochromic wetness-indicator theme.

## 摘要 / Abstract

The present invention includes absorbent articles and/or garments with thermochromic wetness indicator(s), providing a method of wetness detection using products including diapers, incontinent briefs, feminine pads, bandages and other like absorbent products.
