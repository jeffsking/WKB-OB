# CN 107057251 B（一种聚丙烯酸钠/纳米纤维素晶体复合高吸水性树脂及其制备方法 / NaPAA–NCC composite SAP — Nanjing Forestry Univ.）

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

A granted Chinese invention patent (assignee: **南京林业大学 / Nanjing Forestry University**) on a **sodium-polyacrylate / nanocellulose-crystal (NCC) composite SAP** made by photopolymerisation, with high absorbency (1200–3000 g/g). A nanocellulose–SAP route from academia — FTO-relevant.

## 摘要 / Abstract

The SAP is sodium polyacrylate / nanocellulose-crystal composite, prepared by photopolymerisation from acrylic acid, nanocellulose-crystal-g-polyacrylamide, crosslinker N,N'-methylenebisacrylamide and photoinitiator 2-hydroxy-4'-(2-hydroxyethoxy)-2-methylpropiophenone at mass ratio 1 : 0.005–0.04 : 0.0001–0.0005 : 0.02–0.04. The method has mild conditions and is easy to implement. Water absorbency reaches 1200–3000 g/g and saline absorbency 71–139 g/g; the composite has a rich pore structure that raises water/saline uptake rate.
