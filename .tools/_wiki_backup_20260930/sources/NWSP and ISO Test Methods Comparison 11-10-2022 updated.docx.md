# NWSP and ISO Test Methods Comparison 11-10-2022 updated.docx

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

A K-C internal statistical study (Nov 2022) benchmarking the company's in-house absorbency test methods against industry standards — establishing that capacity/intake/rewet numbers are method-dependent and cannot be compared 1:1 across methods.（金佰利 2022 年 11 月内部统计研究，将公司自研吸收性能测试方法对标行业标准，结论是容量/摄入/回渗数值依赖方法，不可跨方法 1:1 比较。）

## Summary 摘要

- **Business objective 业务目标**: Compare K-C methods to other methods used in industry for capacity, intake, and rewet.（对比 K-C 方法与行业其他方法在容量、摄入、回渗上的差异。）
- **Methods 方法**: Capacity (g) — STM-00033 vs **ISO 11948**; Intake (s) & Rewet (g) — TTM-00672 vs **NWSP 70.9**.（容量：STM-00033 对比 ISO 11948；摄入与回渗：TTM-00672 对比 NWSP 70.9。）
- **Design 设计**: n = 32 per product and per test, 4 lots per code.（每个产品/方法 n=32，每牌号 4 个批号。）
- **Products 产品 (6)**: Depend Fit Flex (DFF), Depend Protection Plus (DPP), Kirkland Signature (KS), Member's Mark (MM), Always Discreet (AD), Assurance (ASSR).（受测 6 品牌。）
- **Core finding 核心结论**: Significant product×test-number interactions at 95% confidence for all three attributes → the average result for a product is dependent on which test was run; cross-method translation is non-trivial.（三类属性均存在显著的产品×测试交互 → 产品均值依赖所用方法，跨方法换算不可直接平移。）

## Extracted numbers & conclusions 提炼的数字与结论

| Attribute 属性 | Method A 方法A | Method B 方法B | Overall mean 整体均值 | Note 备注 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Capacity 容量 (g) | ISO 11948 | STM-00033 | 1164.48 vs 740.13 | ISO ~1.57× higher |
| Rewet 回渗 (g) | NWSP 70.9 | TTM-00672 | 0.11 vs 0.23 | TTM ~2× higher |
| Intake time 摄入时间 (s) | NWSP 70.9 | TTM-00672 | method-dependent | 1st/2nd/3rd intake from TTM vs single from NWSP |

Per-product highlights (rewet, TTM-00672):
- Depend Fit Flex (DFF) ≈ 0.44 g; Depend Protection Plus (DPP) ≈ 0.46 g — highest rewet.（回渗最高。）
- Always Discreet (AD) and Kirkland (KS) ≈ 0.11–0.12 g — lowest rewet.
- Capacity (ISO 11948): Assurance highest (≈1317 g), Always Discreet lowest (≈871 g).

> Variance models show significant effects for test, product, and lot-within-product in rewet, and for product & lot in capacity — i.e. both the method and the manufacturing lot drive spread.（回渗方差受方法、产品、批号显著影响；容量方差受产品与批号显著影响。）

## Key claims 关键论断

- K-C internal STM/TTM numbers are **not** directly interchangeable with ISO/NWSP external numbers; any competitive benchmark must state the method.（K-C 内部 STM/TTM 数值与外部 ISO/NWSP 不可直接互换，竞品基准必须注明方法。）—— 支撑页：[[Rewet Test（回渗测试）]]、[[芯体测试方法与验收基准（STM）]]
- Always Discreet is consistently the lowest-capacity product in this panel; Depend family runs higher rewet under the TTM-00672 protocol.（Always Discreet 在该面板中容量始终最低；Depend 系列在 TTM-00672 下回渗偏高。）
