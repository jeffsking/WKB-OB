# SNF FloSorb & DryTech Superabsorbent（企业介绍）

## 一句话定位

## 摘要

The document is SNF Group's corporate Code of Conduct / "Water Science" profile. SNF describes itself as a global specialty-chemical player whose activities are driven by scarcity of key resources — fresh water, oil and minerals — with more than 80% of applications designed to treat, preserve or optimize water use. SNF operates in 50+ countries. **FloSorb / DryTech** appears as its superabsorbent product family.
