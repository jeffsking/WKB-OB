# CN 112778606 A（一种分区透气底膜及其制备方法、纸尿裤 / Zoned breathable backsheet — Hengan）

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

A Chinese published patent application (applicant: **福建恒安集团有限公司 / Hengan**) on a **zoned breathable backsheet** with multiple independent feed ports for differentiated breathability — for adult diapers (air permeability + leak-proof). Backsheet manufacturing patent → FTO-relevant.

## 摘要 / Abstract

The invention discloses a zoned breathable backsheet and its preparation / a diaper. By increasing a conventional cast film's single feed port to multiple ports, each independently controls the additive recipe, extruded through corresponding screws into corresponding flow channels, merged and secondarily drawn into an integrally formed zoned breathable film. Versus conventional backsheet, the zoned film is usable as an adult-diaper backsheet: by designing zone-specific breathability it both improves poor air-permeability risk of adult diapers and provides leak-proofing against urine.
