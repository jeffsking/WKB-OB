# Dry-laid web formation

## One-line Position 一句话定位

Chapter 2 is the Handbook's deepest treatment of drylaid web formation — the textile-derived branch — culminating in airlaying and the raw-material science (fluff pulp, SAP, bicomponent blends) that defines modern hygiene cores and ADLs.（中文：第 2 章系统阐述干法成网（源自纺织），重点在气流成网与绒毛浆/SAP/双组分配比，直接定义现代卫生芯体与导流层。）

## Summary 摘要

- The chapter progresses from carding physics (worker/stripper/licker-in/doffer, metallic wire geometry, aerodynamic control) through feed/weight-control systems to cross-lapping and perpendicular-laid structures.（从梳理机理、喂入与称重控制，到交叉铺网与垂直铺网。）
- It culminates in airlaying (aerodynamic web formation) and its raw-material science: wood pulp, man-made fibres, superabsorbents (SAP) and bicomponent blends.（高潮在气流成网及其原料科学：木浆、人造纤维、SAP 与双组分混合。）
- For hygiene R&D the chapter explicitly names the "liquid acquisition layer for nappies" and gives concrete rules: airlaid MD:CD≈1, PP/PE BICO 35/65, M&J 3 mm vs 4 mm fibre-length rule.（对卫材研发，本章明确点名"纸尿裤导流层"，并给出气流成网各向同性、PP/PE 双组分 35/65、M&J 3 mm/4 mm 纤维长度等量化规则。）
- Carding and cross-lapping govern uniformity and the "bath-tub" weight profile that later needlepunching/hydroentanglement must correct.（梳理与交叉铺网决定均匀性，并带来"浴缸状"克重剖面，需后续加固工艺修正。）

## Key Claims 关键论断

- Airlaid webs are isotropic; carded webs are not — isotropy is a design variable for omnidirectional acquisition.（气流成网各向同性，是各向同性导流的设计变量。）
- Hydrophobic synthetics preserve wet bulk; blends with pulp + SAP form the nappy acquisition layer.（疏水合成纤维保湿态蓬松，与浆/SAP 混合成导流层。）
- SAP immobilises fluid under compression — core must not rewet under buttock pressure.（SAP 受压 immobilises 液体，是芯体抗回渗的设计依据。）
- Cross-lapping bath-tub profile propagates into bonding and must be co-designed.（交叉铺网缺陷会传递至加固，须协同设计。）
