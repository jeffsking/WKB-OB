# Introduction 导论

> Source: Manufactured Fibre Technology, Ch.01, pp.1–13.

## Scope 本章范围

本章为全书导论（introduction 导论）：从纤维的定义与分类出发，勾勒人造纤维（manufactured fibres 人造纤维）的技术全景——"是什么 → 从何来 → 怎么做 → 用在哪 → 书怎么写"。全章含七个子节：定义与分类（1.1）、人造纤维演进（1.2）、成纤工艺（1.3）、纤维产品与性能（1.4）、生产趋势（1.5）、应用领域（1.6）、本书范围（1.7）。

## Definition 纤维的定义

- 判别标准在**几何形态与基本使用性能**，而非化学来源（p.1）：>"A textile fibre is a long thin object with a high ratio of length to thickness. It is characterized by a high degree of fineness and outstanding flexibility. In addition, it should have dimensional and thermal stability and minimum levels of strength and extensibility consistent with the end use."
- 天然纤维多为短纤维（staple fibres 短纤维），长度约 10–500 mm；蚕丝为长丝（filament 长丝）。人造纤维先制成连续长丝，需要时再切断成短纤（p.1）。
- 人造纤维分三子类：**改性天然聚合物**（modified natural polymers，如再生纤维素）、**全合成**（wholly synthetic）、**无机**（inorganic）（p.2）。

>"The origins of wholly synthetic fibres can be traced to the pioneering work of W.H. Carothers in the USA. ... the first successful wholly synthetic fibre was produced commercially in 1938 from adipic acid and hexamethylene diamine. This fibre was called nylon 66." (p.2)

## Two Principal Operations 两大主操作

>"Fibre manufacture involves the following two principal operations: (1) production of the polymer, and (2) conversion of the polymer into the fibre. For fibres based on raw materials derived from nature, step (1) is not involved." (p.3)

这一二分法（two-step split 制造二分法）构成全书技术章节主轴：第 3–7 章讲转化（挤出/纺丝），第 8–9 章讲拉伸与热定形，第 10–11 章讲表征与测试，第 12–18 章分述各纤维族，第 19–20 章讲非织造与回收（p.12–13）。

### Polymerisation 聚合机理

- **缩聚 / 逐步聚合（condensation / step-growth polymerization）**：>"The synthesis of condensation polymers proceeds by the step-wise reactions of functional groups, each reaction proceeding with the elimination of a small molecule, usually water." (p.3)
- **加聚 / 链式聚合（addition / chain-growth polymerization）**：>"Addition polymers are formed by the addition of unsaturated monomers to the growing chain without the elimination of water or other small molecules." (p.3)（自由基 / 离子机理）

### Conversion to Fibre 纤维化转化

| 工艺 Process | 定义 Definition | 页码 p. |
|---|---|---|
| 熔纺 Melt-spinning | 聚合物熔体经毛细孔挤出成纤 | 4 |
| 溶液纺 Solution-spinning | 挤出溶液成纤后去除溶剂（干法 / 湿法） | 4 |
| 拉伸 Drawing | 使分子取向（orientation 取向）并结晶（crystallinity 结晶度） | 4 |
| 热定形 Heat-setting | 经后结晶（post-crystallization）与应力松弛（stress relaxation）稳定尺寸 | 4 |

## Property Spectrum 性能谱系与产业格局

- >"It is noteworthy that compared with the natural fibres, the manufactured fibres have a wider property spectrum. This is an important and useful feature of these fibres." (p.6) —— 以 Table 1.2 的密度、回潮（moisture regain）、强度数据支撑，说明性能可通过分子设计定制。
- >"In 1995, 23.19 million tonnes of manufactured fibres were produced, accounting for almost 53% of the total (natural and manufactured) world fibre production of 43.651 million tonnes; nearly 87% of the total manufactured fibre production was from synthetic polymers." (p.8)
- 聚酯（polyester）在合成纤维中占主导（约 52%，p.9）；应用分布为服装 / 家用 / 产业用三大块（p.10–11）。

## Hygiene Implication 卫材关联

- **纺丝-拉伸-热定形三步链是卫材用纤维的性能开关**：纺粘（spunbond 纺粘）与熔喷（meltblown 熔喷）本质都是 melt-spinning 熔纺的变体，拉伸阶段的取向度与热定形的应力松弛直接决定 ES 双组分纤维（bicomponent fibre 复合纤维）的**潜在卷曲（latent crimp）**与热风布的回弹性 —— 见 [[Meltblown Nonwoven（熔喷非织造布）]] 与 [[Nonwoven Process Classification（非织造布工艺分类）]]。
- **"性能谱系更宽"是可设计性的直接依据**：PP/PE 聚烯烃纤维的低密度、低回潮（near-zero moisture regain）特性正是卫材面层疏水、芯体导流层亲水改性（ hydrophilic finish 亲水整理）的前提，见 [[Coform Composite Nonwoven（熔喷-木浆复合非织造）]]。
- **加聚路线 = 聚烯烃纤维的工业基础**：PP、PE 均由加成聚合（addition polymerization）制得，这解释了为什么卫材主力纤维（PP 纺粘、PE/PET 双组分、PE 膜）成本受烯烃单体价格驱动，见 [[Handbook of Nonwovens]]。
- **纤维几何定义可直接迁移**：长径比（length-to-thickness ratio）与细度（fineness）是绒毛浆纤维与合成纤维共用的比较口径；截面形状（cross-section 截面）设计如三叶形，见 [[三叶形截面纤维（Trilobal Fibre）]]。
