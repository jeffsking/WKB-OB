# Stimuli-Responsive Polymer Materials (Nature Materials Review)

## One-line Position 一句话定位
A foundational 2010 Nature Materials review mapping how polymers that switch properties under external stimuli are architected (2D films, 3D particles) and applied — a design toolbox directly transferable to smart textiles, wearable sensing, and hygiene-material surfaces.（一份奠基性综述，系统梳理"刺激响应聚合物"的架构与应用，可直接迁移到智能纺织品、可穿戴感知与卫材表面工程。）

## Summary 摘要
- Defines responsive polymer materials as systems that adapt to surroundings and reversibly convert chemical/biochemical signals into optical, electrical, thermal, and mechanical signals (and vice versa).（操作性总定义：材料可双向转换信号。）
- Organizes the field into 2D architectures (polymer brushes, gel thin films, layer-by-layer multilayers, membranes) and 3D particles (micelles, vesicles, nanogels, capsules, core–shell particles).（两大空间维度：2D薄膜与3D粒子。）
- Surface reconstruction is the core mechanism of 2D systems; film confinement accelerates response from minutes–hours (bulk) to seconds–hours, with sub-10 µm gel films responding in < 1 s.（薄膜化大幅加速响应动力学。）
- Drug delivery is the largest application driver; the ideal device is framed as a 25–100 nm capsule with a PEO brush, targeting ligand, and stimuli-responsive pores.（药物递送是最大应用引擎。）
- Identified open challenges: long-term stability/durability, multi-stimulus "biocomputing", and insufficient response-kinetics modelling.（三大挑战：稳定性、多刺激、建模。）
- Explicitly lists **textiles** among application domains, alongside biosensors, MEMS, and smart coatings.（原文明确把textiles列为应用领域。）

## Key Claims 关键论断
- Responsive polymers reversibly convert signals (temperature, pH, light, force, field) into material-property changes — the universal operational definition.（可逆信号转换是核心。）
- Nanometre-thick coatings deliver high added value at low cost — a direct argument for hygiene/smart-textile adoption.（纳米薄涂层低成本高增值。）
- Textiles are an explicitly named application domain, supporting温控透气/变色/指示织物 directions.（textiles为原文点名应用域。）
