# Ch46: Energy Aspects in Drying

> **Source:** Strumiłło, W., Jones, R. & Zyłła, Z., in Mujumdar (ed.), *Handbook of Industrial Drying*, 4th ed., CRC Press, 2014. Ch.46, pp.1074–1096.

---

## 1. Energy Scale 能耗规模

> "Industrial dryers consume a significant part of the total energy used in manufacturing processes, **12% on the average**." (p.1074)

- **Paper:** ~**33%** of sector energy (Table 46.2)
- Drying removes the least water in absolute terms but is the most costly step

---

## 2. Thermodynamic Minimum vs Practice 热力学最低vs实际

| Metric | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Theoretical minimum** 理论最低 | **2200–2700 kJ/kg** | Latent heat + bound-water penalty (p.1079) |
| **Actual practice** 实际 | **2700–8000 kJ/kg** | Gap = savings headroom |

> "Theoretically, the amount of heat required for the evaporation of 1 kg of moisture under standard conditions is **2200 to 2700 kJ/kg**. The upper limit of this value refers to the removal of **bound moisture**." (p.1079)

**Procurement rule:** Benchmark any supplier offer against this floor.

---

## 3. Energy Efficiency Definitions 能效定义

### Energy Efficiency 能效

$$\eta = \frac{E_{ev}}{E_t}$$

where $E_{ev}$ = energy for moisture evaporation at feed temperature, $E_t$ = total energy supplied

> "The energy efficiency η is most frequently quoted in technical specifications. It commonly relates the energy used for moisture evaporation at the solids feed temperature (E_ev) to the total energy supplied to the dryer (E_t)" (p.1076)

**Warning:** Do NOT confuse "energy efficiency" with "drying efficiency" (Eq 46.6, accounting for exhaust enthalpy) — vendors may quote the flatterer.

---

## 4. Heat Loss Breakdown 热损失分解

| Loss Category | Share |
|---|---|
| **Moisture evaporation** 水分蒸发 | 20–60% |
| **Material heating** 物料加热 | 5–25% |
| **Exhaust air loss** 排气损失 | **15–40%** |
| **Wall loss** 壁面损失 | 3–10% |
| **Other** 其他 | 5–20% |

**Key insight:** Exhaust-air loss (15–40%) **dwarfs** wall loss (3–10%). Target: exhaust recovery + airflow reduction, NOT better insulation alone.

---

## 5. Savings Ladder 节能阶梯

| Step | Method | Cumulative Saving | Investment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Fix leaks, draft control | **13%** | Low |
| **2** | Better insulation | **26%** | Medium |
| **3** | Exhaust air recycle | **64%** | High |
| **4** | Waste heat exchange with condensation | **71%** | Highest |

> "Major capital expenditure... Scheme 5 waste heat exchange with condensation... 71%" (p.1080)

### National Potential by Option 国家级潜力

| Option | National Potential | Penetration |
|---|---|---|
| **Improved dewatering of feedstock** | **4%** | High |
| Leak/draft control | 13–25% | High |
| Insulation | 13–27% | Medium |
| Exhaust recycle | 40–60% | Low |
| Heat recovery | 64–71% | Low |

## 6. Heat Recovery Technologies 热回收技术

### Heat Pump 热泵

| Technology | SMER (kg water/kWh) |
|---|---|
| **Conventional convective** | 0.5–1 |
| **Theoretical max (thermal)** | 1.55 |
| **Heat pump** | **3** |
| **MVR** | **3.7** (134 kJ/kg) |

> "A typical value of SMER achieved by a heat pump is **3 kg/kWh**, which compares very favorably with conventional convective drying, for which values ranging from **0.5 to 1 kg/kWh** are the norm." (p.1085)

**MVR:** 134 kJ/kg water — "20 times better than the typical adiabatic spray drying" (p.1086)

---

## 7. Process Control 过程控制

- Finish at **max acceptable moisture** (not over-dry)
- Microcomputer control cuts **6.5–20%** energy (p.1090–1092)
- Process intensification: raising textile-dryer T from 140→180°C cuts **25%** (p.1076)
