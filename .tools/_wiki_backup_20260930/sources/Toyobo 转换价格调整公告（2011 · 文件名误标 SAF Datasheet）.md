# Toyobo 转换价格调整公告（2011 · 文件名误标 SAF Datasheet）

## Positioning 一句话定位

**文件名误标件**：文件名为「Toyobo Acrylic SAF Datasheet」，正文实为东洋纺 2011-03-15 的 **IR 公告**（2 万亿分日元的 2012 年到期零息可转债转股价调整）。与 SAF/超吸水纤维无关；本页登记误标以免后续误引。

## Summary 摘要

Toyobo Co., Ltd. (Code 3101) notice dated 15 March 2011, signed by President Ryuzo Sakamoto, IR contact Ikuo Takeuchi. Content: adjustment of the conversion price of the outstanding ¥20,000,000,000 zero-coupon convertible bonds due 2012, from ¥458.0 to **¥445.1**, effective 16 March 2011 (Japan time), pursuant to Condition 5.2.6, triggered by the issue of 140,000,000 new shares to non-shareholders (resolved 23 February 2011) at a consideration below the then-current market price. **无任何 SAF、丙烯酸纤维或产品技术数据。**
