# Ch40: Drying of Coated Webs

> **Source:** Hung, H.-C., Wimberger, J. & Mujumdar, A.S., in Mujumdar (ed.), *Handbook of Industrial Drying*, 4th ed., CRC Press, 2014. Ch.40, pp.929–948.

---

## 1. The Drying Curve Controls Morphology 干燥曲线控制形态

> "Because of the product quality problems associated with the drying process, the **proper quantity of heat and mass transfer has to be applied to the coating at the proper time** in the drying curve." (p.931)

### Three Stages 三个阶段

| Stage | Mechanism | Defect Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm-up** 预热 | Heating substrate + coating | — |
| **Constant-rate** 恒速期 | Surface evaporation dominates | Safe window; fast evaporation |
| **Falling-rate** 降速期 | Internal diffusion limits | Mottle, blistering, curl, brittleness |

### Latex vs Solvent vs Paper 乳液vs溶剂vs纸张

> "The drying curve of **water-based latex coatings has a longer constant drying rate** than solvent-based coatings. Paper has a drying curve with a short warm-up period and a constant drying rate period **even longer than latex coatings**." (p.931)

**Hygiene implication:** Emulsion/lotion coatings on wipes behave like latex — a longer benign constant-rate before the defect-prone falling-rate zone.

---

## 2. Defect Catalog 缺陷目录

| Defect | Cause | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Mottle** 不均匀 | Binder migration during drying | Control preheat rate and constant-rate time |
| **Blistering** 起泡 | Rapid over-drying → surface seals | Reduce heat flux in falling-rate |
| **Curl** 卷曲 | Nonuniform moisture profile | Uniform drying schedule |
| **Wrinkles** 褶皱 | Uneven shrinkage | Gradual drying |
| **Railroading** 条纹 | High-shear air flow disturbs wet coating | Limit jet temp to wet-bulb of constant-rate |
| **Skinning** 结皮 | Surface dries before interior | Reduce surface heat flux |

> "Over-drying or rapid drying can cause **brittleness, blistering, curl, and wrinkles**. Brightness, ink receptivity, and varnishability can be reduced." (p.931)

> "The high-velocity air impinging directly on the coating surface tends to **dry the surface coating** and **accelerates the binder migration**... 'Rail road tracking' will take place when the dryer temperature **exceeds the wet bulb temperature** of the constant drying rate period." (p.933)

---

## 3. Dryer Hardware Ladder 干燥设备阶梯

| Technology | Application | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Steam cylinders** 蒸汽烘缸 | Paper coating | Conduction; convection assist |
| **Air cap** 风唇 | Paper coating | Gentle surface drying |
| **Impinging jet tunnel** 喷射风洞 | Coated paper | High flux; surface-first |
| **Air flotation** 气浮干燥 | Film lamination | Cushion pressure; web stability |
| **Air turns** 风拐 | Coated webs | Gentle |
| **IR** 红外 | Predry only | Wavelength matching; scorch risk |
| **UV** 紫外 | 100% solids cure | No solvent; clear-cut reaction |
| **EB** 电子束 | 100% solids cure | High speed; deep cure |

---

## 4. UV/EB Cure for Hygiene 卫材UV/EB固化

> "UV-curable coatings are **100% solids** and **solvent removal equipment is not required**... In the case of curing or cross-linking, it generally provides a **clear-cut reaction with no residual promoters or cross-linking agents** that might cause degradation of the product or problems with **governmental agency regulations on toxic residues in products for food and drug applications**." (p.942)

**Hygiene implication:** For skin-contact hygiene films and wipes, 100% solids UV/EB is the regulatory-friendly route — no solvent VOC, no extractable cross-linker residuals.

---

## 5. Safety 安全

- **LEL** (Lower Explosive Limit): Critical for solvent-borne coatings
- **Inert-gas drying:** N₂ for high-VOC systems
- **VOC recovery/incineration:** EPA compliance required
- **NFPA standards:** Binding legal requirements
