# SAPPYER® SLI/SLIM/SLIE + Bio-balanced SAP（LG Chem）

## 一句话定位

LG Chem **SAPPYER®** SAP 牌号资料（SLI / SLIM / SLIE + 生物循环平衡 SAP），本批 SAP 供应商信息源的牌号级补充。

## 摘要

The document details LG Chem's **SAPPYER®** SAP grade family — **SLI / SLIM / SLIE** — and a **bio-circular balanced (BCB) SAP** line. It sits within LG Chem's broader sustainable-material portfolio (SAP, NPG, IPA, Acrylates, ABS, PE, PP, PC, PVC, etc.). The doc also describes a circular pyrolysis route recovering ~8 tons of "Circular Pyrolysis Oil" from 10 tons of plastic waste. Grade-level performance specs (absorbency, AUL, etc.) were not extracted in the read portion → marked `[待验证]`.
