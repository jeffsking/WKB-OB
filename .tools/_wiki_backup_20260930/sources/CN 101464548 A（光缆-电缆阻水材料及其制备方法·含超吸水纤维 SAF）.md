# CN 101464548 A（光缆/电缆阻水材料及其制备方法·含超吸水纤维 SAF / Water-blocking material with SAF — Donghua Univ.）

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

A Chinese published patent application (applicant: **东华大学 / Donghua University**) on a **water-blocking material using superabsorbent fibre (SAF)** for optical/cables — SAF is the acrylic-acid-based superabsorbent fibre (OASIS SAF context). Cable application, but SAF is SAP-fibre technology adjacent to hygiene SAP.

## 摘要 / Abstract

The invention relates to a water-blocking material for optical/cable coating and joints, and its preparation. The material is made by blending superabsorbent fibre (SAF) with a low-melting fibre and a base fibre, or SAF with a bicomponent fibre, in a certain ratio, then web-forming, consolidation and slitting into rolls. The SAF is a terpolymer of acrylic acid (AA), methyl acrylate (MA) and a small amount of SAMM (special acrylate salt/MA); the low-melting fibre is PE/PP/copolyamide/PVC; the base fibre is PP/PET/nylon/viscose. Swell value 400–9900. The material can be used in medical, hygiene care, horticulture and technical textiles; the method uses existing equipment, is simple, low-cost, pollution-free and degradable.
