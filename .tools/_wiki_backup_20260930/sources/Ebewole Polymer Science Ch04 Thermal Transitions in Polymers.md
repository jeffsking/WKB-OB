# Ebewole Polymer Science Ch04 Thermal Transitions in Polymers

> Source: Ebewole, *Polymer Science and Technology*, Ch.4 (Thermal Transitions in Polymers). 章节主题：Tg 与 Tm 的定义与测量、玻璃化转变的结构影响因素、加工与应用温度窗口。

Chapter 4 is the **thermal–structure–property bridge 热-结构-性能桥梁**: it takes the glass transition temperature **Tg** and the crystalline melting point **Tm** as its two axes and explains why polymer thermal behaviour differs fundamentally from that of simple small molecules.

本章直接决定 polymer 材料的**选材、加工与应用温度窗口**；对卫材热轧/热风粘合与热熔胶配方尤为关键。

## How Polymer Thermal Behaviour Differs 聚合物热行为的三个特殊性

> "In the first place, those molecules large enough to be appropriately termed polymers do not exist in the gaseous state. At high temperatures, they decompose rather than boil since what we would consider conventionally as their 'boiling points' are generally higher than their decomposition temperatures." (Ch.4, p.96)

> "the transition between the solid and liquid forms of a polymer is rather diffuse and occurs over a temperature range whose magnitude (of the order of 2 to 10°C) depends on the polydispersity of the polymer." (Ch.4, p.96)

> "two important temperatures at which certain physical properties of polymers undergo drastic changes have been identified: • The glass transition temperature, Tg; • The crystalline melting point, Tm." (Ch.4, p.96)

固-液转变的弥散宽度（约 2–10 °C）由**多分散性 polydispersity** 决定——Ch.1 定义的 Mw/Mn 在此获得宏观热行为的直接体现。

## The Glass Transition 玻璃化转变

> "At a characteristic temperature, Tg, the rate of volume expansion increases suddenly to a higher constant level, i.e., there is a change in the slope of the volume–temperature curve from a lower to a higher volume coefficient of expansion." (Ch.4, p.97)

> "there is an abrupt change in physical behavior from a hard, brittle, glassy solid below Tg (region D) to a soft, rubbery material above Tg (region C)." (Ch.4, p.97)

> "For a crystalline polymer, the changes at Tg are less drastic. This is because these changes are restricted mainly to the amorphous domains while the crystalline zones remain relatively unaffected." (Ch.4, p.97)

Three-state model for an amorphous polymer: **hard glassy solid → soft rubbery material → viscous liquid 硬玻璃态 → 软橡胶态 → 粘性液体**. For a semicrystalline polymer, between Tg and Tm the material "is composed of rigid crystals and a rubbery amorphous phase" — 半结晶聚合物在 Tg 与 Tm 之间是"刚性晶粒 + 橡胶态无定形相"的复合体。

> "The equilibrium crystalline melting point, Tm, for polymers corresponds to the temperature at which the last crystallite starts melting." (Ch.4, p.96) — and "the value of Tm depends on the degree of crystallinity and size distribution of crystallites." (Ch.4, p.96)

> "The glass transition temperature of rubber determines the lower limit of the use of rubber and the upper limit of the use of an amorphous thermoplastic." (Ch.4, p.96) — Tg 是橡胶的使用温度下限，也是非晶热塑性塑料的使用温度上限。

## Structural Factors Controlling Tg Tg 的结构影响因素

> "Chain flexibility is determined by the ease with which rotation occurs about primary valence bonds. Polymers with low hindrance to internal rotation have low Tg values." (Ch.4, p.100)

> "Bulky side groups that are stiff and close to the backbone cause steric hindrance, decrease chain mobility, and hence raise Tg." (Ch.4, p.100)

> "The increased free volume results in a lower Tg." (Ch.4, p.100)

> "Double bonds in the cis form reduce the energy barrier for rotation of adjacent bonds, 'soften' the chain, and hence reduce Tg." (Ch.4, p.100)

> "it is to be expected that the presence of strong intermolecular bonds in a polymer chain, i.e., a high value of cohesive energy density, will significantly increase Tg." (Ch.4, p.100)

The source supports each factor with a data table (Tables 4.1–4.7). Tg is presented as **designable 可设计** over roughly −120 °C to 220 °C:

| Structural factor 结构因素 | Change 变化 | Effect on Tg 对 Tg 的影响 | Example values cited 书中示例 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chain flexibility ↑ 链柔性 | PE / PDMS | ↓ | −120 °C / −123 °C (Table 4.1) |
| Backbone rigidity ↑ 主链刚性 | PC / polysulfone / PPO | ↑ | 150 / 190 / 220 °C |
| Side-group bulk ↑ 侧基位阻 | PE → PP → PS → poly(α-methylstyrene) | ↑ | −120 → −10 → 100 → 192 °C (Table 4.2) |
| Side-chain flexibility ↑ 侧链柔性 | methacrylate series R = Me → C₁₂H₂₅ | ↓ | 105 → −65 °C (Table 4.3) |
| Symmetry ↑ 对称性 | PP vs. PIB; PVC vs. PVDC | ↓ | Table 4.4 |
| cis vs. trans | diene polymers | cis lower cis 更低 | Table 4.5 |
| Polarity / intermolecular force ↑ 极性 | poly(methyl acrylate) → poly(acrylic acid) → poly(zinc acrylate) | ↑ | Table 4.6 / 4.7 |
| Cross-linking ↑ 交联 | — | ↑ | [未找到原句] |
| Molecular weight ↑ 分子量 | — | ↑, converges to Tg∞ 收敛于 Tg∞ | Fox–Flory relation |

> "the same effect of increased Tg with increasing CED can be observed when one considers going from the intermolecular forces in poly(methyl acrylate) … through the strong hydrogen bonds in poly(acrylic acid) to primary ionic bonds in poly(zinc acrylate)" (Ch.4, p.101)

CED 桥接：本章的 cohesive energy density（内聚能密度）把 Ch.3 的次键理论（偶极 / 氢键 / 离子）与 Tg 定量关联起来——CED 越高，Tg 越高。

Other factors enumerated: **branching 支化**, **crystallinity 结晶度**, **plasticization 塑化**, plus external factors (pressure, test rate 压力与测试速率) and **copolymerization 共聚** as an industrial Tg-tuning tool (Ch.8).

## Academic Context and Critique 学术对话与批判

- **Gibbs–DiMarzio theory (1958)** — configurational-entropy interpretation of Tg; **Fox–Flory relation** — Tg = Tg∞ − K/Mn.
- **V–T slope method** was the standard Tg definition before the 1960s; by the 1990s DSC (heat-capacity step) was more common, but this 1997 text retains the V–T exposition.
- **Is Tg a true phase transition?** The source adopts the V–T slope-break description, i.e. a kinetic-transition stance; the thermodynamic school argues Tg is not a true transition. 本书立场偏工程实用。
- **Tm/Tg interrelation**: industrial rules of thumb (e.g. Turnbull–Cohen, Tm/Tg ≈ 1.5) are not discussed in the chapter.
- **CED linearity caveat**: the CED–Tg correlation holds mainly within a polymer family; cross-family comparison needs correction — the chapter does not state this limitation.

## Hygiene Implication 卫材关联

本章与卫材研发高度相关：

1. **Thermal bonding windows 热轧/热风粘合窗口.** 聚烯烃纤网的粘合必须跨越 Tg（非晶区链段获得运动能力）并接近 Tm（晶粒熔融、界面扩散缠结）；半结晶 PP/PE 在 Tg 与 Tm 之间呈"刚性晶粒 + 橡胶态无定形相"（Ch.4, p.97），这正是热轧粘合既能成结又能保持纤网尺寸稳定的结构原因。粘合温度窗口的下限由 Tg 约束、上限由 Tm 与热收缩约束。参见 [[Polypropylene Handbook Spunbond and Meltblown Nonwoven Resins]]、[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch19 Spunbonding and Melt-Blowing Processes]]。
2. **Bicomponent fibre design 双组分纤维.** 皮芯结构（PE 皮 / PP 芯）的粘合窗口来自两种聚合物 Tm 的差值；Ch.4 的 Tm 定义（"the temperature at which the last crystallite starts melting"，p.96）提示熔点并非锐点，而是受结晶度与晶粒尺寸分布影响的范围——因此粘合温度需按最慢熔融的晶粒设定。
3. **Hot-melt adhesive formulation 热熔胶配方.** 热熔胶的 Tg 决定其低温脆性与开放时间，通过共聚、增粘树脂（塑化）与分子量调节 Tg 是配方设计的核心杠杆；烯烃嵌段共聚物体系即典型应用。参见 [[EP2456819B1 Olefin Block Copolymer Hot Melt Adhesive (Bostik)]]。
4. **PP 低温脆性 与 SAP 干态玻璃态.** 等规 PP 的 Tg 书中示例约 −10 °C（Table 4.2），故其在 −10 °C 以下进入玻璃态而变脆；聚丙烯酸盐类 SAP 因 –COO⁻ 间强氢键/离子作用 CED 高、Tg 高，干态呈玻璃/硬橡胶态，吸水后形成水凝胶。书中示例给出的等规 PP Tm ≈ 165 °C 在本摘录中未标注页码，标为 [待验证]。
