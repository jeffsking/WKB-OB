## Positioning 一句话定位

A CTO-facing supplier visit programme covering 16 suppliers across six frontier material domains (PLA/PPC 可降解、生物基 SAP、CNF/BC 纳米纤维素、BC 微凝胶、SSMMS 五层复合、智能感知 IoT), compiled from seven IMA technical reports plus public registry and news retrieval. （中文：面向 CTO 的供应商拜访计划，覆盖 6 大前沿材料领域、16 家供应商，数据来自 IMA 知识库 7 篇技术报告全文 + 公开工商/新闻检索。）

## Summary 摘要

The document is an engagement plan, not a technical study: each supplier entry carries a company profile, the technical talking points drawn from the corresponding report, and a question清单 split across technical / commercial / strategic dimensions. Its durable value lies in the **process-parameter tables** it consolidates — nanocellulose preparation energy, BC fermentation windows, SSMMS structure and process settings, SAP reverse-phase suspension conditions, bio-based SAP route comparison, and IoT sensor BOM structure.

The plan also contains a global benchmarking layer (NatureWorks / TotalEnergies Corbion / 丰原 for PLA; Nippon Shokubai / BASF / Evonik / Sumitomo for SAP; CelluForce / Borregaard / Nippon Paper for nanocellulose; Ontex / P&G for smart diapers) and a three-tier strategic recommendation (Tier 1 立即行动, Tier 2 积极跟踪, Tier 3 技术储备).

## Key Claims 关键论断

1. **Nanocellulose is a reinforcement lever, not yet a cost lever 纳米纤维素当前是增强杠杆而非成本杠杆** — 1–10 wt% CNF delivers 20–100% tensile and 30–200% modulus gains, but the energy route (TEMPO 1–5 vs 30–50 kWh/kg) is what decides whether it becomes affordable; see [[纳米纤维素（CNC、CNF、BNC）]] and [[三类纳米纤维素对比（CNC、CNF、BNC）]].
2. **BC is high-purity but long-cycle 细菌纤维素高纯度但周期长** — >99% purity, 84–89% crystallinity and 200–1000× water uptake at 28–30 °C, yet 7–14 天静置周期 dominates cost; the 10:90 BC/壳聚糖 microgel route shows how BC moves into high-value niches; see [[细菌纳米纤维素]].
3. **SSMMS buys barrier performance at a known premium SSMMS 以溢价换阻隔** — >50 cmH₂O hydrostatic head and >95% filtration for roughly +1,200 元/吨 over SMS, plus 10–20% for hydrophilic finishing; see [[Meltblown Nonwoven（熔喷非织造布）]].
4. **Reverse-phase suspension defines the high-end SAP window 反相悬浮界定高端 SAP 工艺窗口** —油水比 3–5:1、中和度 70–80%、70–80 °C / 2–4 h、粒度 150–850 μm, with surface crosslinking as the performance moat; see [[SAP（高吸水树脂）]].
5. **Bio-based routes are differentiated by degradation speed and premium 生物基路线以降解速度与溢价区分** — PASP degrades in 2–4 months at +50–100% cost; bio-based monomer routes approach parity at +10–20%; see [[Biodegradable & Bio-based Diaper Materials（可降解与生物基卫材材料）]] and [[Degradable SAP Routes（可降解 SAP 四路线对比）]].
6. **Smart sensing is cost-bound 智能感知受成本约束** — module BOM must fall to roughly the 10–30 元 band before mass adoption; BLE is the dominant link; see [[智能尿裤功能层]].
7. **Strategic tiering 分层结论** — 可降解 PLA/PPC 已量产、SSMMS 成熟 → 供应链动作；生物基 SAP 早期商用、纳米纤维素仍在中试 → 跟踪与储备；智能 IoT → 前瞻布局。
