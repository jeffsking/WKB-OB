# 2026-05-05 - QUINN.pptx

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

K. Clemmer's "Depend War on Wetness" deck (2026-05-05) framing rewet as a competitive battle vs Always Discreet and positioning **capillarity** (Young–Laplace) as the engineering lever to close the gap.（Clemmel 的「Depend 抗湿战役」PPT（2026-05-05），将回渗定位为对 Always Discreet 的竞品攻坚战，并以毛细作用（Young–Laplace）作为缩小差距的工程杠杆。）

## Summary 摘要

- **Competitive pressure 竞争压力**: K-C lags Always Discreet (AD) on rewet; AD runs a "40% DRIER" competitive claim (160 mL loading, 50 s wait).（金佰利回渗落后 AD；AD 打出「干爽 40%」竞品宣称。）
- **History 历史**: Prior programs cut rewet via **surge** and **embossing**; current biggest opportunities are in *other materials*.（历史方案靠 surge 与压花降回渗；当前最大机会在其它材料。）
- **Current approach 当前策略**: Goal = best-in-class rewet (DPU2). Method = systematically identify and eliminate every drop of residual fluid from every component via a layer-by-layer teardown with CR&D; primary analysis tool = **capillarity**.（目标 DPU2 回渗最佳；做法为与 CR&D 逐层拆解，用毛细作用分析定位并消除各组分残留液体。）
- **Liner trade-off 面层权衡**: AD's liner is drier via lower wettability (θ), but this raises leakage for DPU2 — a key design tension.（AD 面层靠更低润湿性更干，却使 DPU2 渗漏升高——核心设计张力。）

## Key claims 关键论断

- Rewet is now a materials/capillarity problem, not just a surge+embossing problem; lowering wettability dries the liner but risks leakage.（回渗已是材料/毛细问题，非仅 surge+压花；降润湿性使面层更干却增渗漏风险。）—— 支撑页：[[Rewet Test（回渗测试）]]
- A quantifiable capillarity test (with microCT pore-size mapping) is positioned as the central rewet-design tool.（可量化的毛细测试（配 microCT 孔径成像）被定位为回渗设计核心工具。）
