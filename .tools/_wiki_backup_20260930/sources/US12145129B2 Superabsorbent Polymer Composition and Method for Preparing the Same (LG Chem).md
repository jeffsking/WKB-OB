# US12145129B2 Superabsorbent Polymer Composition and Method for Preparing the Same (LG Chem)

## 一句话定位

Granted US patent (LG Chem, 2024) on a **superabsorbent polymer composition and its preparation method** — the third LG Chem SAP patent in this batch, marking LG Chem's sustained SAP IP activity through 2024.

## 摘要

The patent (title: "SUPERABSORBENT POLYMER COMPOSITION AND METHOD FOR PREPARING THE SAME") grants an SAP composition and a preparation method. The bibliographic header lists assignee LG Chem, Ltd., inventors Park, Kim (Jun Kyu), Kim (Dong Hyun), and a broad IPC set (B01J 20/26, A61L 15/60, B01J 20/28–32, B29B 9/02–16, B29B 13/06–10). It is the US national phase of PCT/KR2019/013302 (WO2020/122390), priority KR 10-2018-0160287 and KR 10-2019-0124302. Detailed claim language is in the full text; this summary captures the bibliographic and classification scope.
