# Elite Color (ECER) Company Profile（优彩环保英文版公司介绍）

## Positioning 一句话定位

优彩环保（Elite Color / ECER，002998）的**英文版公司介绍**，补齐其低熔点纤维产能与业务结构的官方口径；在本库中是 [[优彩环保]] 实体页与 CoPET/低熔点纤维供应分层讨论（对照 [[CoPET（低熔点共聚酯）]]、[[CoPET 采购蓝图：成本拆解与供应分层]]）的更新来源。

## Summary 摘要

English corporate deck. History: founded 2003 as Jiangyin Jianghe Chemical Fiber（江阴江河化纤）, restructured 2015 as Elite Color, IPO 2020 (Shenzhen A share, 002998), convertible bonds 2023. Scale: ~USD 700 M yearly fibre sales revenue, USD 350 M low-melt-fibre fixed-asset investment, 1,200 employees. Identity: recycled polyester specialist — "Top 3 producer of recycled colored polyester fibers worldwide", customers incl. Tesla, Mercedes-Benz, P&G, IKEA, Adidas, Li-Ning etc. Capacity: up to 1 Mt/year over 28 lines — low melt fibre 480 kt, recycled solid fibre 360 kt, hollow conjugate 120 kt, low-melting yarn 30 kt (claims world #1 in low-melting yarn). Products: low melting fibre (2D–20D, 32–102 mm, multiple colors; applications include upholstery, flooring, **hygiene**, automotive, carpet), low melting yarn (FDY 12–192f), automotive/carpet/geotextile solid PSF (1.5D–130D), hollow conjugate bico/mono (3D–40D). Awards/certs: National Sci-Tech Progress Award 2nd class (2018), 绿色纤维 certification, GRS, OEKO-TEX STANDARD 100 (valid to 2026-02), ISO 9001, ISO 50001. Outlook: focus on hollow conjugate and **ES fibres**, sustainability, overseas expansion.
