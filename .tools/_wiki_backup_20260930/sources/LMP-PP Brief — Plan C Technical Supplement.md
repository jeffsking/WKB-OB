# LMP-PP Brief — Plan C Technical Supplement

## Positioning 一句话定位

This supplement defines Plan C as an ultra-low-melting-point PP sheath resin for an all-PP sheath–core bicomponent fiber and converts that architecture into resin, spinning, and acceptance constraints.  
（中文：本补充页把方案 C 定义为全 PP 皮芯双组分纤维的超低熔点 PP 皮层树脂，并将结构方案转化为树脂、纺丝与验收约束。）

## Summary 摘要

The low-melt sheath functions as the bonding phase while the conventional PP core preserves fiber shape and mechanical load. Compared with a PE/PP system, PP/PP bonds at a higher temperature but offers chemical compatibility and an all-polypropylene material pathway. The resin-side strategy combines approximately 10 wt% ethylene-plus-butene comonomer, a 5–30 mass% low-isotacticity fraction, and the exclusion of nucleating agents to suppress lamellar perfection. The principal processing risks are excessive xylene solubles, insufficient quenching, a narrow heat-setting window, and the need to recalibrate drawing temperatures and ratios. The sheath–core structure acts as a design buffer: reducing sheath share reduces low-melt resin consumption and can make a 145–150°C sheath workable when a sufficient differential from the approximately 165°C core is preserved. Validation is based on air-through bonding behavior, fiber mechanical properties, shrinkage, denier, cut length, and finish.
