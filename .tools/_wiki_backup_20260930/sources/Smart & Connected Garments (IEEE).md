# Smart & Connected Garments (IEEE)

## One-line Position 一句话定位
A 2016 IEEE Consumer Electronics Magazine review presenting "clothing as a sensor network" — CNT-fiber antennas/actuators, self-powered harvesting, and the data/privacy challenges of garments permanently in the IoT.（以CNT纤维天线为核心的联网服装综述，呈现"服装即传感器网络"愿景。）

## Summary 摘要
- Beyond social function, smart garments act as transmitters, sensors, or energy-harvesting entities with processing capability, coordinating with cloud servers.（智能服装兼具发射/传感/能量采集与处理能力，协同云端。）
- Typical use: real-time tracking of wearer location and vital signs for firefighters, police, military, miners, industrial workers — garment-based tracking is harder to lose/fail than carried devices.（一线人员实时生命体征追踪。）
- Technical主线: carbon nanotube (CNT) fibers — seamless hollow cylindrical graphite fibers woven into clothing as embedded antennas or actuators with excellent mechanical/electrical properties.（CNT基纤维是技术主线。）
- Antenna integration: ~50×5 mm 20-element patch at 60 GHz for low-power high-speed comms; CNT patch antennas show good gain at 4–9 GHz; multi-antenna beam-forming/switching improves performance.（天线集成：60 GHz 20单元贴片、4–9 GHz增益。）
- Power: batteries are the only viable source today but add weight/range issues; research shifts to piezo/thermoelectric self-powering, e.g. heel dielectric-elastomer stack ~1 J per step.（供电转向压电/热电自供能，每步约1 J。）
- Data: networked clothing generates "small data" (sparse, near-instantaneous); challenge is transfer-learning models across users.（数据：小数据+迁移学习。）
- Society/ethics: privacy erosion, body monitoring, IoT-normalized biosurveillance.（社会伦理：隐私与生物监控风险。）

## Key Claims 关键论断
- CNT fibers enable woven antennas/actuators — the material backbone for conductive, flexible, washable smart textiles.（CNT纤维支撑导电柔性可洗智能织物。）
- Garment-embedded sensing + wireless tracking is directly transferable to incontinence/pediatric remote-care monitoring.（嵌入式传感+无线追踪可迁移至失禁/婴幼远程照护监测。）
