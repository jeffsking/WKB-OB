# PPS 薄膜压力传感应用场景（触感压力分布技术）

## 一句话定位

**PPS（中国）** 的薄膜压力分布传感技术资料，本批作为「触感/压力传感技术（可用于纸尿裤贴合与舒适度量测）」信息源。

## 摘要

PPS (China) offers a **flexible pressure-sensing film/sensor** technology delivering soft, stretchable, fine-grained distributed pressure mapping. Documented application scenarios: (1) medical & rehab tactile measurement; (2) wearable pulse-waveform sensor (blood pressure, high-frequency pulse-shape capture, no chest strap, OEM-friendly); (3) robotic grip-force / tactile control; (4) touchscreen 3D control combining force + capacitive sensing, only 1–2 mm thick, multi-touch. Relevant to hygiene: potential use in fit/comfort pressure mapping of diapers/pants.
