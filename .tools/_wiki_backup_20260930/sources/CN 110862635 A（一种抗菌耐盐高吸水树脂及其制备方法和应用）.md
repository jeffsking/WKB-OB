# CN 110862635 A（一种抗菌耐盐高吸水树脂及其制备方法和应用 / Antibacterial salt-tolerant SAP — Shenzhen Lemin）

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

A Chinese published patent application (applicant: **深圳市乐闵科技有限公司**) on an **antibacterial, salt-tolerant SAP** with a hot-surface-treatment step, positioned for disposable diapers. SAP composition/process → FTO-relevant.

## 摘要 / Abstract

The invention discloses an antibacterial salt-tolerant SAP and its preparation/use. Steps: neutralise acrylic acid with alkali; add primary crosslinker, an Ag-SiO2 composite / intercalated-graphene antibacterial composition, initiator and composite fibre to form a gel; granulate and dry; then melt-extrude aromatic polyamide with nano-silver ions, add ion-exchange resin and a composite crosslinker, and hot-surface-treat all granular polymer by pressure spraying. The SAP is intended for disposable diapers, claimed to separate/remove urine inorganic salts, vent moisture & heat, stay dry/comfortable, inhibit bacteria and prevent infant nappy rash.
