# Physics of Liquid Crystals (Book Review)

## One-line Position 一句话定位
A 1994 book review (Lavrentovich, *Liquid Crystals Today*) of de Gennes & Prost's *The Physics of Liquid Crystals* (2nd ed., OUP 1993) — the foundational soft-matter monograph on liquid-crystal physics.（对液晶物理奠基性专著的书评，属软物质基础文献。）

## Summary 摘要
- The 1974 first edition filled the gap of a systematic liquid-crystal physics text and elevated the field to a cornerstone of "soft matter" science.（第一版奠定液晶为软物质基石。）
- The 2nd edition keeps the classic two-tier narrative: mathematical theory paired with qualitative physical picture.（两层级叙述：数学理论 + 定性图像。）
- Ch.1 classifies liquid crystals (thermotropic / lyotropic; nematic / smectic / columnar).（第一章：液晶分类。）
- Ch.2 covers nematic long-range orientational order, scalar & tensor order parameters, Landau–de Gennes phenomenology, Monte Carlo, and biaxial nematics.（第二章：向列相序参量与唯象理论。）
- Ch.3 (core) presents Frank–Oseen elastic theory, equilibrium director distributions, surface anchoring, dielectric/diamagnetic/flexoelectric effects, and director-fluctuation theory.（第三章：Frank-Oseen弹性理论。）
- Ch.4–6 largely unchanged except blue phases (two complementary models); Ch.7–10 detail smectic/columnar phases — twist-grain-boundary, hexatic, freely suspended films, ferroelectric smectics.（第四至十章：近晶/柱状相新进展。）
- Critique: the book's handling of divergent elastic free-energy terms (k13 mixed splay-bend, k24 saddle-splay) — dismissed as surface-energy-reducible — is argued to be insufficiently justified.（批评：弹性自由能发散项处理欠严谨。）
- Verdict: an "excellent guide" to soft-matter systems; "if soft matter science has a bright future, this book surely brings that day closer."（评价：excellent guide。）

## Key Claims 关键论断
- Liquid crystals are a foundational soft-matter system with well-developed order-parameter and elasticity theory.（液晶是软物质基石，具成熟序参量与弹性理论。）
- Relevance to this collection is peripheral — liquid crystals may inform far-future chromic/display functions on textiles but are not a near-term hygiene material.（与本合集关联弱，仅作为远期显色/显示材料设想。）
