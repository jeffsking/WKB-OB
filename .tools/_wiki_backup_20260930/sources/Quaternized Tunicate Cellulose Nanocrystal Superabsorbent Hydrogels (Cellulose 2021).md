# Quaternized Tunicate Cellulose Nanocrystal Superabsorbent Hydrogels (Cellulose 2021)

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

一篇**开放获取的期刊论文**（*Cellulose*, 2021, 28:3723–3732，ORIGINAL RESEARCH），不是专利。它给了本库一条**全生物基可降解 SAP 路线的完整数据组**：纤维素 + CMC 用 EPI 交联，并用**季铵化海鞘纤维素纳米晶体（Q-TCNCs）**作纳米填料。最有价值的一点是它**同时测了去离子水与合成尿**，并直接拿市售尿裤吸收材料做对照——两条曲线的**排序是反转的**。

## Abstract 摘要

Full title: "Superabsorbent hydrogels enhanced by quaternized tunicate cellulose nanocrystals with adjustable strength and swelling ratio" (Peng Wei, Weiwei Chen, Qinghua Song, Yibing Wu, Youjia Xu; received 8 Jan 2021, accepted 12 Feb 2021, published online 2 Mar 2021; open access under CC BY 4.0). The matrix is **cross-linked cellulose and carboxymethyl cellulose (CMC)**; the reinforcement is **quaternized tunicate cellulose nanocrystals (Q-TCNCs)**; the cross-linker is **epichlorohydrin (EPI)**. Because cellulose, CMC and Q-TCNCs all carry large amounts of hydrophilic groups, the hydrogels absorb strongly: the abstract states equilibrium swelling **exceeds 500 g/g in deionized water** and **exceeds 120 g/g even in synthetic urine**, "higher than those of commercial absorbent materials composed of copolymers of acrylic acid and acrylamide". Both water uptake and mechanical properties are **adjustable via Q-TCNC content**. The authors frame the goal as replacing polyolefin-based materials to reduce environmental pollution. Four formulations are compared (Table 1): Q-0 (no nanocrystals), Q-4 and Q-10 (0.12 and 0.30 wt% Q-TCNCs, with CMC reduced correspondingly from 3 to 2.88 and 2.70 wt%), and H-10 (same 0.30 wt% loading but using **unmodified H-TCNCs**, to isolate the effect of quaternization); all at cellulose 3 wt%, EPI 0.1 mL/g, 12 h, 60 °C.

## Key claims 关键论断

- **"比市售材料好"这句话必须带上介质**——去离子水里市售材料更高（约 245 vs 125–185 g/g），**合成尿里才反转**（53 vs 79–123 g/g）。这是本页最容易被误引的地方。→ [[Sodium Polyacrylate SAP vs Cellulose-Based SAH（成本·性能·供应链）]]
- **可降解路线的真实优势是"耐盐"而不是"吸得多"**：盐浓度超过约 0.024 g/g 后曲线走平，说明静电屏蔽饱和；而市售聚丙烯酸体系在盐溶液中塌陷更明显。→ [[Biodegradable SAP（可降解高吸水树脂）]]
- **纳米填料是双向旋钮**：Q-TCNC 从 0 → 0.3 wt%，有效交联密度 0.122 → 0.443 mol/L，于是强度 1.9 → 5.4 kPa、溶胀 756 → 516 g/g。**加填料等于用吸收量换强度**，与本库 [[SAP-Cellulosic Nanofibril Composite（SAP-纤维素纳米纤丝复合体）]] 里"低添加量换凝胶强度"的思路同构，但用量口径不同（本源为 wt% of total formulation；SCA 专利族为 wt% of SAP）。
- **季铵化是第二级旋钮**：同为 0.3 wt%，季铵化的 Q-10 比未改性的 H-10 更致密（ESR 516 vs 605；强度 5.4 vs 2.7 kPa）——**表面电荷改性本身就是独立变量**。→ [[纳米纤维素（CNC、CNF、BNC）]]
- **高价位阳离子是这套羧基体系的硬伤**：Fe³⁺ 在 0.01 g/g 就把溶胀压到约 20 g/g，作者解释为与 CMC 羧基配位交联。对含矿物填料/金属离子的配方体系是直接警示。→ [[Seawater-Degradable 3D Hydrogel System（海水可降解三维水凝胶体系）]]
