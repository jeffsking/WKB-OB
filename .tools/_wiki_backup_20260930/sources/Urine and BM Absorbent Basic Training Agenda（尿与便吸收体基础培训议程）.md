# Urine and BM Absorbent Basic Training Agenda（尿与便吸收体基础培训议程）

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

Planning spreadsheet (two identical sheets) for K-C's "Urine and BM Absorbent Basic Training" — the agenda that maps **who covers which absorbent topic in how many minutes**, and the source page for the training-series structure itself. Used in this KB as the index to the 2021 training deck family（本页是 2021 培训系列的议程索引页）.

## Summary 摘要

The workbook states the training goal for Amber's team: explain **why we design the way we do** — what happens to the fluid (intake, run-off, rewet) and why, and the guiding principles (e.g. how removing SAM impacts fluid handling and product considerations). Delivery: **4 virtual sessions, recorded for future onboarding**, audiences BCC teams, MAPS/ACT, GNW+GRE, Poise Depend. A "maybe add a separate session about absorbent pad forming and zoning" flag and extra attendee names (Alejandro Aguilar, Uyen Lam) are recorded in the sheet. The main table lists each Training Element with owner, time and deck-modification comments（主表逐条列出讲题、讲者、时长与讲义修改意见）.

## Extracted numbers and conclusions 提炼的数字与结论

| Item 项目 | Value / Conclusion 数值/结论 | Note 备注 |
|---|---|---|
| Sessions 场次 | 4 场：Foundations 2 hr · Material basics 1 hr · BM 1 hr · Bench & modeling 1.5 hr | 虚拟 session + 录制存档 |
| Pad forming / zoning 垫体成型与分区 | DeGrave 两讲各 20 min（forming technology；zoning flat vs pocketed） | 议程备注"是否单设成型与分区一场" |
| SAM / Pulp / Nonwovens 材料三讲 | 15 / 15 / 25 min（Lee / Bowen / Keck） | 各讲均有删减指令 |
| Bench & modeling 台架与建模 | Brubaker 30+30 min；Baker 30 min（保留 method-vs-ULDP 对照表） | 建模聚焦相关 calculators + virtual portal |
