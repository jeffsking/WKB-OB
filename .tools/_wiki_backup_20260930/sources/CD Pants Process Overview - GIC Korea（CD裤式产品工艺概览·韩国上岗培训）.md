## 第一部分：安全规程（Three Safety Obligations + 通用规则）

### 三项安全义务（原文要点）

1. **有义务拒绝**任何你认为不安全的行动、可能造成财产损坏的任务，或**你未受适当培训 / 无适当工具**的任务，并向 Team Leader 或 Kimberly-Clark sponsor 报告。
2. **有义务当面制止**任何正在或即将实施不安全行为 / 从事未经培训任务的人。
3. **有义务在被人指出不安全行为或状况时立即停止**手中工作并解决该问题，即便需要联系他人协助。

### 通用安全规则

| 类别 | 内容 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| **禁戴饰品** | 含婚戒、手表、项链、耳环；**例外：耳钉与医疗警示手环/项链** | — |
| **强制 PPE**（除 front horseshoe、办公室、会议中心外的**所有区域**）| ① **高可视安全背心或上衣**；② **ASTM 认证安全鞋**；③ **防撞帽或安全帽**；④ **ANSI Z87 认证带侧护的透明安全眼镜**；⑤ **听力防护** | 五项 PPE |

## 第三部分：CD Pants 工艺（四段 16 步）

> 工艺示意图五大块：**SURGE/LINER PROCESS → PRODUCT CUT OFF PROCESS → INSERT PROCESS → INSIDE/OUTSIDE PANEL PROCESS → FLAP/OUT COVER PANEL PROCESS**

### ① Fluff forming process 绒毛浆成型

| 步骤 | 内容 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Pulp fiberization** | 浆板纤维化 |
| 2 | **SAM system（SAM ratio）** | SAM 系统（配比）|
| 3 | **SAM/Fluff mixing @ forming chamber** | 成型室内 SAM 与绒毛浆混合 |
| 4 | **Pad formation（w/wo empty channel）** | 芯体成型（带/不带空通道）|

**Key learning（源文要点）**

- **Pad formation**：*"Using a forming drum with **5 different vacuum zones** for pad formation and pad release, **contour segments & forming screen** is also required based on pad shape design."*（成型鼓 **5 个真空区**；按芯形配置**轮廓段与成型网**）
- **Weight control**：*"Having a **scarfing system** for product weight variation control."*
- **Pulp fiberization**：*"Would need to check **fiberization%** based on quality requirement in order to determine fluff quality."*（组件：LUFF FEED ROLLS / ANVIL / HAMMER GRINDER PLATES / SEAL BAR / ROTOR DISC / BREAKER BAR / PULP INFEED / SEAL / HAMMERMILL SCREEN）
- **SAM system**：*"Using a **positive displacement blower** for transferring SAM particle from SAM hopper to forming chamber."*（正压排量风机输送 SAM 颗粒）

### ② Insert combining process 插入体组合

| 步骤 | 内容 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Surge/Liner lamination w/wo embossing with pad** | 面层/导流层与芯体层合（带/不带压花）|
| 2 | **Flap/Out cover lamination with pad** | 护围/外覆层与芯体层合 |
| 3 | **Flap folding while flap/leg elastic lamination with flap** | 护围折边，同时腿围弹性体与护围层合 |
| 4 | **Insert cut off and insert applicator** | 插入体切断 + 插入体施加 |

**Key learning**

- **LSE module / JOA *"Surge Cut and Place Module"***，位于 **2 楼夹层（2nd floor mezzanine）**；图注含 **Slip vacuum / Transfer vacuum**。
- **Flap folding**：*"Normally, we use **3 different folding boards** with dimension difference. Meanwhile, the flap/leg elastics will be laminated with flap material. … Based on product spec, the **flap width and folding boards can be changed**."*
- **Insert cut off**：*"This is a **critical process** before combining with panel material."*
- ⚠️ **Insert applicator**：*"The main purpose is to **change CD pants product direction from MD to CD**."*（**本件最关键的一句** —— "CD Pants"之名的工艺根源）

### ③ Waist panel combining process 腰围片组合

| 步骤 | 内容 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Inside/Outside panel material + panel elastic lamination | 内外腰围片材料与腰围弹性体层合 |
| 2 | Panel elastic chopping | 弹性体切断（chopping）|
| 3 | Panel slitting | 分条 |
| 4 | Insert to Panel lamination | 插入体与腰围片层合 |
| 5 | Panel folding | 对折（bi-folding）|
| 6 | Panel sealing | 封合 |
| 7 | Final cut off | 最终切断 |

**Key learning**

- **Step 2（转向）**：*"Panel elastics web path is changed from the **vertical position to horizontal position with a twisted angle which is 90 degree**."*（弹性体走向由垂直转水平，**扭转角 90°**）
- **Chopping**：*"While rotating the chopper module manually or automatically, the panel elastic will be **severing by chopper teeth** after panel material go through."*
- **Insert to panel**：*"Insert to panel quality really depends on **panel adhesive pattern width** as well as **intermittent adhesive control** which is **solenoid valve control**."*（质量取决于**胶型宽度**与**间歇胶控制 = 电磁阀控制**）
- **Panel sealing**：*"This is a **critical process** for getting enough panel bonding strength."*；*"When using different panel material such as **SMS, TABCW** material, the **process setup needs to be adjusted**."*
- **Panel folding（bi-folding）**：*"The main purpose … is to get an **even alignment for front side waist panel and back side waist panel**."*；*"A **wheel screw position** can be adjusted based on front and back side waist panel **gap difference (mm)**."*

## 教训 Lessons

1. **"CD Pants"的"CD"来自工艺** —— **insert applicator 把产品方向从 MD 转为 CD** 是整线的枢纽步骤，不是营销词。
2. **插入体先做好、再与腰围片组合** —— 四段工艺的切分点是 **insert cut off 被明写为"critical process before combining with panel material"**。
3. **成型鼓用 5 个真空区**（成型 + 释放），并按**芯形**配**轮廓段与成型网** —— 与 [[80 高速pattern芯体成型工程总纲（导航·速查·口径·分歧·验收）]] 的 **6 区固定角度**是**不同机台/不同代次**口径，**不合并**。
4. **克重变异靠 scarfing 系统控制**，而非靠成型鼓本身。
5. **腰围弹性体要转 90°** —— 从垂直走向扭转为水平走向，是裤式产品特有的机构动作。
6. **间歇胶由电磁阀控制** —— insert-to-panel 质量**取决于胶型宽度 + 间歇胶控制**。
7. **封合是关键工序** —— 换材料（SMS / TABCW）**必须调整工艺设定**。
8. **对折（bi-folding）可调** —— 靠 **wheel screw** 按前后腰围片**间隙差（mm）**调整。
