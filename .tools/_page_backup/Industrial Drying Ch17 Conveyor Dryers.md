# Ch17: Conveyor Dryers

> **Source:** Poirier, D., in Mujumdar (ed.), *Handbook of Industrial Drying*, 4th ed., CRC Press, 2014. Ch.17, pp.409–420.

---

## 1. Core Mechanism 核心机理

> "Product is carried through the dryer on conveyors and hot air is forced **through the bed of product**... the conveyor dryer is best suited for drying particulate material in the 1- to 50-mm diameter range. Drying in the conveyor dryer involves forcing air through the bed of product; therefore **impermeable sheeted materials or slurries are not well suited** to conveyor drying." (p.409–410)

**Key boundary:** Through-circulation = air forced THROUGH a permeable bed. Impermeable sheets → impingement (Ch15) or Yankee (Ch14) instead.

### SAP Named Verbatim

> "Some examples of products that are often dried on single pass/single-stage dryers include expanded, non-coated snacks, baked snacks... nuts, charcoal, some synthetic rubbers, **super absorbent polymers**, etc." (p.411)

**Hygiene relevance:** SAP powder is explicitly named — direct, unmistakable relevance.

## 2. Configuration Options 配置选择

| Configuration | Description | Hygiene Application |
|---|---|---|
| **Single-pass / single-stage** | One bed, air through once | SAP powder drying |
| **Single-pass / multiple-stage** | One bed, multiple air stages (most versatile, p.411) | Nonwoven through-air oven |
| **Multiple-pass** | Product passes through multiple beds (most popular, p.412) | Airlaid TAD bonding |

## 3. Quality Control 质量控制

### Bed Uniformity Is Critical 铺料均匀是核心

> "If the feeding or spreading is not right, the dryer will not be able to dry the product uniformly. The end result will be poor product quality, wasted energy, and reduced capacity... air will flow preferentially through the shallow areas... these shallow areas will be over dried... clumps... air will tend to flow around clumps rather than through them. This results in **wet product inside the clumps**." (p.415)

**Hygiene implication:** Cross-bed moisture uniformity is set at the feeder. For a nonwoven through-air oven this predicts exactly the streaks/shading defects in webs.

### Volumetric Heat-Transfer Coefficient 体积传热系数

> "It is much easier to define the heat transfer in the form Q = h_v · V · ΔT where h_v is the volumetric heat transfer coefficient (kW/m³·°C) and V is the volume of the product bed (m³)." (p.416)

**Engineering trick:** Bypass the unknowable surface area — size from bed volume + h_v measured on a lab tray/belt sim.

### Evaporative Cooling Safety 蒸发冷却安全

> "The product in the dryer is typically at a much lower temperature than the air temperature due to evaporative cooling. If the product is dried to a very low moisture content, near or approaching bone dry, the evaporation rate is reduced and the product may approach the air temperature." (p.418)

**Safety lever:** Latent heat sink protects heat-sensitive web until bound moisture is gone. Past that, surface temperature climbs — degradation/casehardening window opens.

### Glass Transition as Set Point 玻璃化转变作为设定温度

> "If cooling to a temperature below ambient is required (for example to set a coating by getting it below its **glass transition temperature**), chilled air is used as the cooling medium." (p.410)

**Hygiene implication:** For nonwoven thermobonding — low-Tg bicomponent sheath is cooled below Tg to lock fibre bonds.

## 4. Energy Recovery 能量回收

- Exhaust air losses: ~**10%** of total (p.419)
- Preheated make-up air recovers >10% energy (p.419)
- Casehardening occurs at low humidity — manage relative humidity in the dryer

---

## 5. Design Rules for Hygiene Lines 卫材设计规则

| Rule | Application |
|---|---|
| Run first bed shallow, deepen after surface sets (p.420) | Airlaid through-air oven |
| Air temperature is primary control variable | All conveyor dryers |
| Retention time and bed depth are secondary | Nonwoven TAD |
| Bed permeability may drop suddenly as particles compress (p.416) | Wet-laid/airlaid web consolidation |
| Theoretical modeling will never replace testing or previous experience (p.415) | Pilot data mandatory |
