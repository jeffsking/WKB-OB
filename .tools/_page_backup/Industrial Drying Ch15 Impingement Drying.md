# Ch15: Impingement Drying

> **Source:** Mujumdar, A.S., *Handbook of Industrial Drying*, 4th ed., CRC Press, 2014. Ch.15, pp.383–394.

---

## 1. Application Scope 适用范围

> "Impinging jets of various configurations are commonly used in numerous industrial drying operations involving rapid drying of materials in the form of continuous sheets (e.g., **tissue paper**, photographic film, **coated paper**, **nonwovens**, and textiles)" (p.383)

**Hygiene relevance:** Tissue Yankee hoods, through-air nonwoven bonding ovens, and lotion/coating cure on wipes are all impingement applications.

### Key Design Rule 核心设计规则

> "Impinging jet drying is recommended **only if a major fraction of the moisture to be removed is unbound**. If the drying rate is internal diffusion controlled, the high heat transfer rates of the impingement system can often result in **product degradation** if the product is heat-sensitive." (p.383–384)

**Hygiene implication:** Hygiene webs (fluff, nonwoven, SAP) carry bound moisture in the falling-rate period. Pushing impingement heat flux there risks fibre damage / curl / casehardening.

---

## 2. Operating Parameters 操作参数

| Parameter | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Jet velocity 喷嘴速度 | 10–100 m/s | Re > 2000 for fully turbulent |
| Air temperature 空气温度 | 100–350°C | Product-dependent |
| Thermal efficiency 热效率 | **75–90%** | Well-designed dryer (p.388) |
| Specific energy 单位能耗 | **3100 kJ/kg water** | Air-floater dryers (p.388) |

### Evaporative Cooling Safety Lever 蒸发冷却安全机制

> "Because of the extremely high evaporative cooling in the constant rate period, the product surface temperature can be held well below its degradation or ignition temperature." (p.385)

**Key insight:** A 350°C jet can keep a tissue surface far below ignition because the latent heat sink dominates. Once bone-dry, surface temperature climbs toward air temperature — degradation window opens.

---

## 3. Web Defect Catalog 幅材缺陷目录

| Defect | Cause | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Case hardening** 结壳 | High heat flux + low humidity → surface dries first, seals interior | Use through-air for bound moisture; reduce jet temperature |
| **Cracking** 开裂 | Rapid shrinkage from fast drying | Limit heat flux in falling-rate period |
| **Coating streaks (railroading)** 涂布条纹 | High-shear air flow disturbs wet coating | Limit jet temperature to wet-bulb of constant-rate period |
| **Fire** 火灾 | Combustible dust + high temp + O₂ | Inerting; temperature control; dust control |

> "Extremely high drying rates obtained by the use of high-velocity jets at elevated temperatures may cause such problems as case hardening or cracking due to rapid shrinkage." (p.384)

> "Very high shear stresses generated by jet impingement can cause the high-velocity ink or coating to flow, causing undesirable streaks (railroading) on the product." (p.384)

---

## 4. Design Procedure 设计流程

1. Choose nozzle configuration (round or slot array)
2. Determine geometry (nozzle size, spacing, distance)
3. Set velocity/temperature/spacing (interrelated; Re > 2000)
4. Compute constant-rate drying rate via empirical correlations with corrections
5. Check surface temperature against degradation/ignition limits
6. Determine recycle ratio via enthalpy balance

### Martin Nu/Sh Correlations 马丁关联式

- **Array of Round Nozzles (ARN):** Valid over stated Re/f/H/d ranges, ±15% accuracy
- **Array of Slot Nozzles (ASN):** Similar validity
- Corrections needed for: crossflow, semiconfinement, mass flux, large ΔT, suction, surface motion, oblique impingement, swirl

## 5. Hygiene-Materials Action Cards 卫材应用卡

| Card | Insight | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **H15-1** | Tissue is fully impingement-dried; heavier nonwoven/airlaid mats only lose surface moisture via impingement | Through-air or conveyor does the core; impingement only for surface |
| **H15-2** | 3100 kJ/kg water, 75–90% efficiency = procurement benchmark | Require suppliers to report against this floor |
| **H15-3** | Case hardening = curl + web defects + over-dry | Cap jet temp at wet-bulb; switch to through-air for bound moisture |
| **H15-4** | Fire hazard from combustible tissue/nonwoven dust | Inerting, temperature control, dust housekeeping |
