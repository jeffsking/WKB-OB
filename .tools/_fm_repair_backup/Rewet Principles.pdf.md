## Desorption

Intake materials do not trap fluids but encourage drainage to the core.

Materials have large pores to reduce capillarity.

### Material and process variability and capability are considered in design.

For each design principle, the variability and capability of the materials and process should be considered during development to ensure that a reliable commercial execution is delivered.
