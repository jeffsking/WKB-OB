# Background

$\bullet$ Rewet is the mass of fluid that transfers into a blotter media from a product over a given time under a specified applied pressure.

$\bullet$ These rewet design principles can be leveraged for:

* Communicating about rewet and its causes.

* Developing absorbent structures.

* Troubleshooting rewet issues.

$\bullet$ We split the sources of rewet into two categories:

# Desorption

### Intake materials do not trap fluid.

Intake materials have lower capillarity and wettability compared to where fluid is locked up.

![](2/Oy0YFe4o874ND9Udefofxe/ppt_3867e2b60070d7dc3f413086d0b0036e_3b36588fe67dd21aa5d5462ecbf372f6001a693ee6c0644e/tmp_zip_img/40f0e139-abeb-4f48-b02d-005e5a9ce383.png)

Materials resist compression to ensure pores stay fully open, even in use.

Pore networks are well connected, and no fluid is trapped at interfaces.
