# Background

$\bullet$ As individual projects change intake material characteristics, they may find variables that influence rewet

$\bullet$ Instead of passively collecting project data, it is desired to actively build data around intake material design for rewet

# Draft Approach

1. Focus on areas that are not as well studied in order to reduce scope: bodyside liner and topical surge

$\bullet$ Traditional (under liner) surge has been studied extensively

2. List what variables might be important to study

3. Consider if we have studied that variable before or not

4. (in meeting) Identify most important areas to study to inform discussions with vendors

5. (after meeting) Discuss material availability with vendors and choose final codes for study
