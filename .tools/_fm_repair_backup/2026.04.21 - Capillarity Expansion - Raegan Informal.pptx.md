## Why it matters

$\bullet$ Capillarity of the intake system dictates rewet

$\bullet$ Quantifies residual fluid, how much remains and where it sits

$\bullet$ Reveals material levers as wettability and pore size via la Young-LaPlace equation

$\bullet$ Underpins our cross-brand low rewet strategy
