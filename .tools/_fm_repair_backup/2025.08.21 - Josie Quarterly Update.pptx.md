## Desorption

### Intake materials do not trap fluids but encourage drainage to the core.

Materials have large pores to reduce capillarity.

![](2/Oy0YFe4o874ND9Udefofxe/ppt_3867e2b60070d7dc3f413086d0b0036e_e89b9dbe573bd5523fd3dbd5ed734499001a693ee6c0644e/tmp_zip_img/0e49badb-80e6-41da-b353-1de026428d73.png)
Materials resist compression to ensure pores stay fully open, even in use.

![](2/Oy0YFe4o874ND9Udefofxe/ppt_3867e2b60070d7dc3f413086d0b0036e_e89b9dbe573bd5523fd3dbd5ed734499001a693ee6c0644e/tmp_zip_img/80a421e0-7412-4fca-8762-5f5743fc1124.png)
Materials are minimally wettable to reduce capillarity.

![](2/Oy0YFe4o874ND9Udefofxe/ppt_3867e2b60070d7dc3f413086d0b0036e_e89b9dbe573bd5523fd3dbd5ed734499001a693ee6c0644e/tmp_zip_img/ac203cd6-84fb-4ba7-a994-c728248d330a.png)
The number of pores is minimized to provide less places for fluid to be trapped.

![](2/Oy0YFe4o874ND9Udefofxe/ppt_3867e2b60070d7dc3f413086d0b0036e_e89b9dbe573bd5523fd3dbd5ed734499001a693ee6c0644e/tmp_zip_img/30f181a3-0e54-48cc-a41e-e468a49117e9.png)
### Multiple components work as a system to deliver fluid to the core.

Pore networks are well connected, and no fluid is trapped at interfaces.

![](2/Oy0YFe4o874ND9Udefofxe/ppt_3867e2b60070d7dc3f413086d0b0036e_e89b9dbe573bd5523fd3dbd5ed734499001a693ee6c0644e/tmp_zip_img/b142f1e0-7c52-41a3-adda-39f96387a081.png)
A capillary gradient draws fluid away from skin and towards where it can be locked up.

![](2/Oy0YFe4o874ND9Udefofxe/ppt_3867e2b60070d7dc3f413086d0b0036e_e89b9dbe573bd5523fd3dbd5ed734499001a693ee6c0644e/tmp_zip_img/cf5cf8b2-44c6-426e-9c1e-2547f1bf5084.png)

# Bringing it to life for T5 BUFF BABY

$\bullet$ Low pressure rewet target of 0.15g

* Liner-surge embossing can reduce to 0.15g target

* Lesson learned from Depend

$\bullet$ High pressure rewet target of 0.3g

* Biggest driver is optimized intake system, which T4 & T5 have used to drive down to ~0.4g

* Resilient surge (i.e.: MOJAVE) + low basis weight liner (i.e.: spunbond)

* Latest T5 learning demonstrated <0.3g with various core changes

* Need to confirm the driving mechanisms in order to consistently deliver target
