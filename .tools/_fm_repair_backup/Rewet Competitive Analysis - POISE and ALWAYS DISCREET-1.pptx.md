# Summary

$\bullet$ Capillary pressure – ALWAYS DISCREET intake system has a much higher capillary pressure than the POISE intake system. The expected outcome would be higher rewet from the desorption mechanism

$\bullet$ Capillary pressure – ALWAYS DISCREET liner and surge both have higher capillary pressure than the POISE liner and surge.

$\bullet$ Pore Size and Wettability – ALWAYS DISCREET liner and surge are more wettable and have a smaller pore size than POISE liner and surge.

$\bullet$ ALWAYS DISCREET surge is coextensive with the liner.

$\bullet$ The coating and spherical nodules contained in two layers of the ALWAYS DISCREET pad have not yet been identified.
