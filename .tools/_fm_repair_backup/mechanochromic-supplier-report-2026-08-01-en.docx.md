Mechanochromic ≠ hydrochromic. Mechanisms covered in this report (only four types) `[F, review DOI:10.1177/00405175251352163]`:

|Mechanism|Trigger|Color-change source|Typical form|
|---|---|---|---|
|Mechanophore activation|stretching to critical force threshold|bond breaking / rearrangement, chromophore develops color|elastomer / polymer network|
|Photonic crystal (structural color)|stretching changes lattice spacing|shift in reflection wavelength (blue → red)|nanostructured film / fiber|
|Liquid crystal elastomer (CLCE) reorientation|strain changes mesogen orientation|change in birefringence / reflected wavelength|CLCE fiber, film|
|Surface wrinkling|stretching induces micro-wrinkles|change in brightness / hue / view-dependent color|stretchable displays, soft robotics|


Note: pressure-indicating film (e.g., Fujifilm Prescale) is irreversible mechanochromic, used for metrology; the rest are mostly reversible, suitable for repeated monitoring.

Day-1 main hypothesis: "Diaper tensile / fit indication can directly adopt existing commercial pressure-activated materials and be commercialized in the short term."

Flip testing (evidence-graded):

1. Commercial pressure-activated materials (CTI HPP ink, Prescale film) are all irreversible metrology-type, with thresholds fixed at ultra-high pressure (≥40,000 PSI) or industrial contact pressure; no off-the-shelf soft, low-threshold, reversible specification for skin contact [F].

2. The "smart diaper" force / liquid research found (Donghua Univ. BC/CNT fiber) is a liquid / water sensor (moisture-responsive), outside this mechanochromic scope, and is electronic, non-chromic [F, ACS AMI 2022].

3. Mechanochromic textiles (CLCE fiber, magnetoplasmonic strain sensor) are all research-stage, with no skin-contact hygiene-product commercial precedent [I].

Conclusion (hypothesis flipped): Diaper force indication cannot directly reuse existing commercial products — it is an innovation white space. However, printable pressure-activated inks (CTI, LCR Hallcrest) and domestic pressure-sensitive dyes / powders (Jiayida, Xingcheng) provide viable "build" technology building blocks; pursue a build (self-define threshold + printing process) path rather than buy (off-the-shelf SKU).
