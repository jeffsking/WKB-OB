# Historical Info: BCC

$\bullet$ First formal rewet methods use 0.5psi

* Both Horizontal FIFE (STP 590-W / STM-2417) and FIFE Flowback/Rewet (STP 552-W / STM-2416)

* Appear ~1991

* FIFE Flowback/Rewet is replaced with Surface Moisture

* Horizontal FIFE is fully replaced with Cradle Intake by around 2015

* Pressure is applied via the vacuum sat cap box

$\bullet$ When Lo/High Rewet (TTM-00623) is developed in 2015 it intends to use 0.46psi for rewet, but it actually uses 0.24psi (TL-15701)

* It's not clear where the error is – perhaps a calculation error?

* Low pressure likely comes from surface moisture, which was invented around PICASSO embossed liner/surge system

# Historical Info: AFC

$\bullet$ The first methods were shared with BCC, so used 0.5psi for flowback

$\bullet$ When fem and adult care methods were aligned in 2004, the pressure was increased to 1.0psi (TR-3006, TL-11230)

* The only justification given was that the higher pressure reflected pressures exerted by adult users instead of babies (more may be in notebook P-8821)

* Pads used rewet tester, garments used sat cap box due to pad size

$\bullet$ When FIUP was introduced, it used 0.7psi (TL-15297)

* Early bladder box testing used 0.7psi during insult which was similar to the pressure loose pants put on the pad in TR-896 (TL-15706)

* This was likely also the same for Closed Cradle as it was developed

$\bullet$ In 2020, the pressure was reduced to 0.5psi (TTM-00643v4 GR&R)

* Only justification given was the larger diameter weight was "for heavy end products that better simulates real consumer experience."

# Historical Info: AFC

$\bullet$ The first methods were shared with BCC, so used 0.1psi for insult

$\bullet$ When fem and adult care methods were aligned in 2004, the pressure was increased to 1.2psi (TR-3006, TL-11230)

* Higher pressure reflected pressures exerted by adult users instead of babies from TR-896

# Timing

# History: AFC/BCC

* Before TTM-0001/2

* Typically 1 minute wait time between when one insult is absorbed and the next one delivered

* Typically 2-3 min rewet times (both are referenced for the same test methods in different places)

* TTM-0001/0002

* Moved the flowback from after the 2nd to after the 3rd insult due to variability concerns of replacing the product for the final insult

* Allowed 1 or 15 min wait times (1 to see how quickly it can be locked up, 15 to allow SAM to swell)

# History: BCC

* TTM-00623 Lo/Hi Rewet:

* 2 minute rewet duration was chosen by vertical wicking test of blotter

* 2 minute 1st insult to 1st rewet/15 minute 2nd insult to 2nd rewet duration was chosen by looking at the change in rewet after insult over time – rewet amount stopped changing as rapidly and was less variable

$\bullet$ Wait time between insults was chosen in a similar way

# History: Adult Care

$\bullet$ `<1998 - STP 590-W Horizontal FIFE intake/flowback` was the primary method used for all products
  * STP 552-W could also be used for the flowback only

$\bullet$ 1999 – STM-2414 (FIFE Intake/Flowback – for garments) and STM-2456 (Timed Intake Dryness Evaluation – for pads) are introduced

$\bullet$ 2001 – VICE (Visual Intake Cradle Evaluation) introduced as a cradle intake/flowback method for garments

$\bullet$ 2004 – TTM-0001 replaces STM-2456 for pads with more consumer-relevant parameters

$\bullet$ 2005 – TTM-0002 replaces STM-2414 and VICE for garments for similar reasons

* 2014 – a bladder box was used alongside TTM-00148 to assess intake/flowback (TL-15705, TL-15706) as more consumer relevant due to pressure applied during insult

$\bullet$ 2015 – bladder box was formalized in TTM-00463 FIUP

$\bullet$ 2016 – TTM-00672 Closed Cradle was introduced for garments

$\bullet$ 2020 – a wider rewet weight with lower pressure was introduced to FIUP & CC to better simulate consumer experience

# History: Baby Care

$\bullet$ `<1998 - STP 590-W/STM-2417 FIFE intake/flowback was the primary method used for all products, though it was used more as a screener and TEWL was used for claims`

$\bullet$ 1998 – STPs became STMs (590 $\rightarrow$ STM-2417, 516 $\rightarrow$ STM-2416)

$\bullet$ 2004 – Test method parameters were standardized (a number of parameters were used by deviation by individual researchers)

* By this time though, there was little use of the methods as TEWL was used for dryness claims and cradle intake was starting to be used for intake

$\bullet$ 2012 – TTM-00302 Surface Dryness developed to quantify perception of skin dryness around global project PICASSO

$\bullet$ 2013/4 – P&G makes dryness claims that puts emphasis back on rewet

$\bullet$ 2016 – TTM-00623 Lo/Hi introduced as a “global unified” method
