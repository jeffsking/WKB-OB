# Conclusion

$\bullet$ Rewet can impact skin dryness throughout wear because of the interaction via microclimate

* Other factors influence skin dryness, so this information is useful only for one aspect of skin dryness

* Consider other significant factors like outer cover breathability when designing for dryness

$\bullet$ It takes a more extreme rewet than our normal range to see a resulting change in skin condition

* Still a good technical check of product design so worth doing

# Future

$\bullet$ Establishing a connection between rewet and other dryness measures increases its usefulness in the claims space

* Currently can make “pad stays drier” or “locks away moisture” based on rewet, but now might help support other dryness claims too

* Rewet is cheaper and easier to run than an EWL or panel test

$\bullet$ We are strengthening our reasoning through planned studies

* Robust data to support claims in case of challenge through purpose-driven EWL and sensory panel studies on inco pads

* Discussions with Legal ongoing about claims language and substantiation methods
