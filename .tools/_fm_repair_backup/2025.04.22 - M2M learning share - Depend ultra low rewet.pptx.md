# Goal

Systematically identify and eliminate every drop of water from every layer to demonstrate ≤0.15g of rewet on Depend Fresh Protection (as measured by closed cradle).

* Surge and liner-surge interfaces are in good shape after ATACAMA

* Liner is a known area for improvement
