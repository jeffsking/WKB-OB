# ADT POV 2025 - Complex Core Strategy

Recommendation:

K-C Internal Asset Manufactured Product: Leverage ActivTech Blizzard Technology for proprietary cost advantaged performance of a complex core

External Contract Manufactured (ECM) Product: Do NOT release ActivTech technology to external supplier. Assist ECM partners to develop external complex core using available commercial technology to meet K-C performance metrics, recommend focusing on few strategic partners to limit teaching.

Background:

Kimberly – Clark has launched ActivTech Technology in North America in Poise UT pads and Huggies T4 in 2024. This is a proprietary complex-core style technology that leverages the Blizzard process of creating a SAM/adhesive network that is sprayed onto substrate – delivers good core consistency, better SAM immobilization, at higher speeds. External complex cores produced via OEM's like Zuiko and others deploy adhesive sprayed onto substrate, then SAM dropped onto the substrate, therefore typically requiring more adhesives to capture the SAM and non-wovens to enable the process. With the Personal Care North America Onslaught and Power programs, several brands are leveraging new ECMs such as Winsun, Thrive, and Haoyue to enable innovation or additional capacity for current variants that would rely on external to K-C complex core designs that are not ActivTech. With this, Depend, Poise, and GoodNites have all inquired if ActivTech technology can be made at an ECM. This PoV aims to clarify the rationale to the recommendation above.
