# PURPOSE

The purpose of this test method is to measure the fluid intake time of products under consumer relevant pressures. This test method also measures the surface dryness of the product after it is insulted using the rewet test methodology.

# 3.0TEST SPECIMEN PREPARATION

# Poise Products

Figure 1

# 3.1.1Remove outside wrapper from product (if applicable).

# 3.1.2Weigh the product to the nearest 0.01 gram and record.

# 3.1.3Measure the target thickness to the nearest 0.1 mm and record.

3.1.4Mark target locations. Lay product flat using two (2) pieces of double sided tape to hold product taut.

A.Using a ruler with centimeter (cm) markings, measure the length (L) of the absorbent area only. (See Figure 1.)

B.Take the length of the product and divide (/) by six (6). Now you have the measurement of one-sixth (1/6) of the product.

C.Take one sixth of the product length and divide (/) by two (2). Now you have the measurement of one-half (1/2) of one-sixth (1/6).

D.Find the Middle-Front and Middle-Back locations of the product by taking 1/6 multiplied by two (2) and adding ½ of 1/6. These target locations are described in the equation based on the absorbent length (L), below. Note that each calculation inside parenthesis is rounded to the nearest tenth (0.1) before proceeding to the next step in the calculation. Once the calculation is complete, measure the calculated distance from the front of the absorbent area, and mark the target location with a marker. (See Figure 1)

# Depend (DPU) product

3.1.5Place whole product onto a flat surface. Fully stretch the product before securing to surface with double-sided tape

3.1.6Mark product location based on the information provided in the table in the Appendix.

3.1.7Remove the waist band elastic using a scissors. Stay at least a ¼” away from the edge of the absorbent pad.

# 3.1.8Weigh the trimmed product and record to the nearest 0.01 grams.

# 3.1.9Measure thickness at the target location and record to the nearest 0.1mm.

# 4.0PROCEDURE

# Product Placement in Bladder Box

# 4.1.10Open bladder box (make sure box is not in use.)

4.1.11Align product at insult target location against the intake port at the top of the box. Use double-sided tape or ½” velcro to secure product to the top of the bladder box.

# 4.1.12Close the bladder box and use clips to secure it shut.

4.1.13Set bladder box to the desired pressure of 0.25 psi. To set the box pressure, use the PV up and down arrow buttons on the far left of the box power source.

4.1.14Once the pressure is set, press the “start” button. The box will begin to fill with air.

# 4.1.15Once the SV reads 0.25 psi, the box is ready for the insulting product process.

# Insulting Product

4.1.16Ensure the pump is calibrated to the appropriate flow rate and dispense time. (See Tables 2-6 in Appendix 12.1.)

# 4.1.17Place stop watch (cleared to zero [0]) in one hand.

# 4.1.18Place nozzle in other hand.

4.1.19Make sure to press the “run” button on the pump and the “Start” button on the stop watch simultaneously. Observe the fluid going into the product.

4.1.20Press “stop” on the stop watch as soon as fluid is absorbed (this is the ‘Intake Time’.)

# 4.1.21Record the Intake Time start timer set for 15 minutes.

# 4.1.22After the 15 minute wait, repeat steps 5.2.1-5.2.7

4.1.23After recording the 3rd insult time, press the “abort” button on the bladder box to release the pressure in the box.

# 4.1.24Once SV readout on the bladder box reads zero (0), unclip and open bladder box.

4.1.25Carefully remove the insulted product from the box and weigh the product to the nearest 0.01 gram.

4.1.26Place insulted side up, on a flat, solid surface. Measure the bulk at the insult point to the nearest 0.1 millimeter.

# Re-wet

4.1.27Place two (2) pieces of pre-weighed, pre-cut, blotter paper atop the insulted product.

# 4.1.28Place 25 mm (1 inch), 0.7 psi weight on blotter paper at insult target location.

# 4.1.29Set timer for two (2) minutes.

4.1.30When time is up, remove weight from blotter paper (wiping weight with dry paper towel) and carefully move the 2 pieces of blotter paper to a balance to weigh them; ensure that the balance is zeroed (0) prior to weighing the blotting paper.

# 4.1.31Record weight of the blotter paper.

# Run-Off Failure

4.1.32If fluid flows beyond the nonabsorbent leg elastic material onto the bladder, this is considered a failure. Do not record the intake time. Record FAIL in the datasheet for that insult. Abort test; do not continue with remainder of test.

# 5.0RESULTS

# Record the Dry and Wet weights of the product to the nearest 0.01 gram.

# Record the Dry and Wet Bulk of the product to the nearest 0.1 mm.

# Record all 3 intake times to the nearest 0.01 second.

# Record Dry and Wet weights of the blotter paper to the nearest 0.001 gram.
