#   Consumer Insight & Benefit

The ActivTech Hybrid core is engineered to deliver best-in-class leakage performance while unlocking premium benefit spaces in flexibility for softness, comfortable fit and movement, and skin dryness. By eliminating pad debulking, the core provides a noticeably softer hand feel along with increased flexibility and improved body conformity. Across global regions, the ActivTech Fluffless core is leveraged to highlight breathability and skin health benefits, with a strong focus on reduced reset. These benefits are achieved in a core that is up to 2x thinner while maintaining the same level of protection.

#   China Cost Reality Key Takeaways:

$\bullet$ China transitions to externally sourced complex core in 2018 (+$13/SU)

$\bullet$ Within three years $11/SU of on cost was taken out by moving the core online

$\bullet$ Raw materials accounted for ~88% of the total reduction in cost of sales from 2018-2023

$\bullet$ For raw material cost ~75% of the reduction was driven by vendor price decreases

Over the same timeframe:

$\bullet$ The total diaper profit pool in China contracted

$\bullet$ K-C China ceded some of that profit pool to retailers

This suggests the raw material cost deflation was market wide rather than unique to K-C China

#   Global context: Complex cores are leading innovation sectors & premium tiers. Hybrid cores (like BB) have been in NA since 2015.

Complex cores come in many forms:

$\bullet$ 100% SAM

$\bullet$ Laminated SAM structures

$\bullet$ Offline complex cores

$\bullet$ SAM-heavy hybrids (like Buff baby)

Pampers launched Complex Core in 2012 and was the first "complex core" to be sold in North America.

The design is now present in Tiers 3–6 across all major markets: US, China & Broader Asia, Eastern & Western Europe, Australia, and Brazil

Traditional SAM/fluff cores are still the majority (by total global volume – all tiers & brands), but growth and innovation is concentrated in complex core platforms- both inside and outside of KC

Competitive benchmarking shows multiple Chinese brands using complex cores, especially in tiers 5-7.

In premium and upper-mid diaper tiers, Complex cores & SAM-heavy hybrid cores are now very common for global majors across Europe, China, Japan, Korea, and parts of Latin America.
