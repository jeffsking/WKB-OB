# Capillary Pressure Method

$\bullet$ How it works:

* Specimen is positioned on a stage, which has constant fluid communication to a reservoir

* Stage can move vertically to change hydrostatic head (pressure)

* Amount of fluid in the sample is measured (saturation, normalized to sample weight)
