#   Chemical Mechanochromism via Naphthopyran

Unlike traditional spiroheterocyclic mechanophores, naphthopyrans maintain partial aromaticity in their ring-opened state. This structural feature provides exceptional fatigue resistance and limits spontaneous, undesired coloration in polar environments.

#   Fatigue Resistance

High stability in the charge-neutral quinoidal ground state.

#   Modular Synthesis and Scale-Up

The commercial viability of Naphthopyrans stems from highly modular synthetic routes coupling naphthols with diaryl propargyl alcohols.

#   Acid-Catalyzed Liquid Chemistry

Inputs: Naphthols + Diaryl Propargyl Alcohols

Process: PPTS + trimethyl orthoformate in 1,2-dichloroethane

Highlights: High yields, 1-3 hour reaction times, prevents unwanted Meyer-Schuster side reactions.

#   Solid-State Mechanochemistry

Inputs: Naphthols + Diaryl Propargyl Alcohols

Process: Solvent-free ball-milling with catalytic InCl3

Highlights: Eliminates solvents, perfectly preserves acid-sensitive functional groups.

#   Structural Mechanochromism via Hydroxypropyl Cellulose

Shifting from molecular bonds to physical architectures, HPC offers an entirely different route to force-responsive optics.

HPC naturally self-assembles into a cholesteric liquid crystal phase, acting as an intrinsically mechanochromic material that is simultaneously biodegradable, edible, and scalable via continuous roll-to-roll production.

Optical Property Note: HPC selectively reflects right-handed circularly polarized (RCP) light, requiring no polarizers for direct viewing by the naked eye or digital cameras.

![NotebookLM\n](1/parseimages/figure86c10ec78c0848f99db342e98cb5f6ff_0_4.png)
#   Compressing the Photonic Bandgap

Structural color in HPC is not driven by dyes, but by physical architecture. When mechanical strain compresses the cholesteric pitch of the HPC film, the structural gaps shrink. This creates a predictable, reversible blueshift through the visible spectrum—translating physical compression directly into chromatic variation.

At >25-27% applied strain, HPC transitions from a stable viscoelastic regime into an inelastic lateral flow regime, causing color relaxation.
