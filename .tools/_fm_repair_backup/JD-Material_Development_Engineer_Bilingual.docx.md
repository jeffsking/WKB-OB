## Key Role

This position serves as the technical execution arm of the Material Open Innovation Leader, focusing on material-level technology development, testing validation, and process optimization. Through collaboration with internal and external technical resources, the role translates innovative concepts into scalable material solutions that support BCC IPC product line innovation and cost-reduction objectives.

|---|---|---|

|2|Material Testing &amp; Data Analysis: Establish and execute material physical, functional, and safety testing plans (e.g., tensile, air permeability, absorption rate, skin irritation); compile test data into technical reports|Testing data accuracy 100%; technical report delivery on-time rate ≥95%; maintain and update testing SOPs|
