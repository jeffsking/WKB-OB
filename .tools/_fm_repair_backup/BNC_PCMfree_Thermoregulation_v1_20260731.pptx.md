FUNCTION DECOMPOSITION

#   What Thermoregulation Actually Requires

Thermoregulation is not one trick — it is four separable physical functions.

![](1/parseimages/figure7825e6f7eb214a6799fbf21ec536d72a_0_3.png)
#   Insulation

Resist conductive heat flow (high R-value) — keep heat out / in.

![](1/parseimages/figure13cababa08ca48f59c27d896bd004597_0_6.png)
#   Buffering

Raise thermal mass so temperature changes slowly (low dT/dt).

![](1/parseimages/figure143bed4dd92846199bce32b66a3475ba_0_9.png)
#   Dissipation

Move heat out — evaporative and radiative pathways.

![](1/parseimages/figured900476bf7734a779c9b251a9b1c3456_0_12.png)
#   Spike absorption

Absorb a discrete thermal event at a setpoint via latent heat.

#   WHAT THE MICROCLIMATE NEEDS

Multi-hour wear comfort is a steady-state problem: insulation + buffering + dissipation.

Spike absorption only matters for the first ~60 s after a warm void.

3 of 4 functions are structural, not chemical.

HEAT-TRANSFER MECHANISM

#   Four Heat Pathways, One Network

The nanofiber mat suppresses every conductive/radiative route — and adds two dissipative loops.

|1. Solid conduction|Point-contact skeleton minimizes solid path + phonon boundary scattering|
|---|---|
|2. Gas conduction|Nano-pores (≈20-200 nm) suppress gas-phase conduction via Knudsen effect|
|3. Radiation|Nanofiber mat scatters IR; low-emittance tortuous path|
|4. Convection|Sealed, sub-mm pores block air movement within the layer|


|Loop A · Bound-water sensible buffering|Hydroxyl-bound water raises thermal mass → slows dT/dt without latent heat.|
|---|---|
|Loop B · Evaporative dissipation|Moisture-vapor transmission through the porous mat sheds heat when wet.|

HONEST LIMITS

#   What We Give Up by Eliminating PCM

![](1/parseimages/figure4891140ad7ab4781a98640dea65bcc70_0_2.png)
#   BNC-only is not a perfect substitute

Three residual gaps are real — and quantified. Honest framing: PCM-free is the default, not the universal, answer.

#   1 · Transient spike absorption

No latent plateau — BNC-only lags PCM by ≈1-2 min in first-window $\Delta$T (model, indicative).

#   2. Setpoint precision

No sharp isothermal hold at Tm — regulation is rate-based, not temperature-locked.

#   3. Above-window performance

Above the setpoint, PCM contributes nothing extra either — so the gap is bounded, not open-ended.
