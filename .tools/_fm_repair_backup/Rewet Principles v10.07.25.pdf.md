## Desorption

### Intake materials do not trap fluid but encourage drainage to the core.

Materials have large pores to reduce capillary pulling/trapping of fluid.

![✓\n](2/Oy0YFe4o874ND9Udefofxe/pdf_3867e2b60070d7dc3f413086d0b0036e_1085ca24cb1062e75106c589d930bc6d001a693ee6c0644e/tmp_zip_img/00e51865-5ef1-4fc1-bd41-076904d1212d.png)

Material resists compression of pores to keep pores as large as possible in-use.

For each design principle, the variability and capability of the materials and process should be considered during development to ensure that a reliable commercial execution is delivered.
