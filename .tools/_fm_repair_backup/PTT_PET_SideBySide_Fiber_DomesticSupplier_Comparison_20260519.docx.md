## Part 1: PTT/PET S-S Fiber - Material Overview

PTT/PET Side-by-Side (S-S) bicomponent fiber is a high-performance elastic material produced by co-spinning Polytrimethylene terephthalate (PTT) and PET side-by-side. The differential thermal shrinkage between the two polymers generates permanent helical crimp, providing natural elasticity WITHOUT spandex. Key advantages over spandex include: better dyeability, chlorine resistance, heat stability, and no aging degradation.
