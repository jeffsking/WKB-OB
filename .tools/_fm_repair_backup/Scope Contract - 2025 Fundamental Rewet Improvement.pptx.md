## Project Description

Rewet is an active claims space where competitive products can show superiority to KCNA products, and recent improvements by P&G show this space will require further development. Efforts to improve rewet have made progress, but not enough to meet or beat competition. A fundamental, ground-up approach will be needed to achieve rewet levels approaching zero for Depend, Poise, and Huggies.
