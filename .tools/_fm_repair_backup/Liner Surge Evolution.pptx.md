# New Innovation Needs

* Near Term: Drier 3D liner & topical surge for urine applications

* Recommendation: Create a new tech program focused on achieving low levels of rewet (say 0-0.3g) with 3D liners and topical surges. Skin Health??

* Potential Applications: Active Baby Diapers and POISE Ultra Thins

* Spin-off Potential: DEPEND DSU and UBK

* Long Term: Printing solutions for intake systems containing 3D liners & topical surges

* Recommendation:

* Create design/needs brief for printing team to initiate printing technology development.

* Consider modifications to material basis weight, material composition, system location, and color to help enhance appearance.

* Potential applications: POISE, UBK

* Barriers to overcome:

* High speed print quality

* Ink build-up

* Ability to see (challenging if continuing to print on the non-body-contact side)

$\bullet$ Consider: Do we need liner & topical surge innovation to also achieve superior protection beyond perception?
