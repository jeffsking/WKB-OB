ABSTRACT

An understanding of consumer loading data is critical for successful design of

incontinence pad and liner absorbents.  This report summarizes single insult,

leakage, and product loading information from the most recent Poise® studies as  of April 2009.

Key words:  POISE, single insult, incontinence, leakage, loading

INTRODUCTION

Loading and leakage data from Home Use Tests (HUT) have historically been successfully used to  design incontinence products.  Single insult data is used to determine insult sizes for modeling

and bench tests.  Single insult data also can provide guidance for designing products with

sufficient for void volume.  Product loading data may be used as guidance for designing products  with sufficient capacity.

Several HUTs and a single insult study were completed since the last summary (TL-11541) was

issued in 2005.  This report is intended to be an updated version of TL-11541 for Poise®

consumer information collected as of April 2009.

OBJECTIVES

The objective of this work was to summarize the most recent single insult, leakage, and urine  loading information from POISE Home Use Tests (HUTs), updating TL-11541 from 2005, to

provide quantitative background information for incontinence projects requiring absorbent  development.

SINGLE INSULT INFORMATION

Single insult studies were developed to measure the actual size of discrete voids into

incontinence products.  They are specialized Home Use Test (HUTs) in which the users are asked  to remove their product as soon as it is insulted.  This information is commonly used for the

absorbent modeling of incontinence products, bench test development, and other product design applications.

Table 1 and Figure 1 summarize the size of single insults for Poise® pantiliners and pads by percentile. An exception is Ultimate Long, which has not been tested to date.
