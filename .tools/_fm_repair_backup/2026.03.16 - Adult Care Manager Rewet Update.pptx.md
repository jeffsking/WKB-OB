Young-LaPlace Equation

$$\Delta P=\frac{-2\gamma\cos\theta}{R}$$

P = pressure
γ = surface tension
θ = contact angle
R = pore radius

$R$ = pore radius

The major factors that affect capillary pressure are wettability ($\theta$) and pore size ($R$)

Intake materials have lower capillarity and wettability compared to where fluid is locked up.

# Conclusion

$\bullet$ P&G rewet (instant transfer to skin) is only a small part of what's happening to skin throughout product wear

$\bullet$ K-C rewet can impact skin dryness throughout wear because of the interaction via microclimate

* It takes a more extreme rewet than our normal range to see a resulting change in skin condition, but is still a good technical check

$\bullet$ Establishing a connection between rewet and other dryness measures increases its usefulness for claims

* Currently can make "pad stays drier" or "locks away moisture" based on rewet, but now might help support other dryness claims too

* Rewet is cheaper and easier to run than an EWL or panel test
