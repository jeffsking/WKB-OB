## Scope and Structure 范围与结构

**EN**: An integrated undergraduate-to-Master's level textbook covering the two principal stages of fibre manufacture — production of the fibre-forming polymer and its conversion to fibre. Emphasis is on fundamental principles and recent developments, deliberately without advanced mathematics. Fifteen authors contribute; the treatment is intensive rather than extensive.

**中文**：一本面向本科至硕士的整合型纤维制造教科书，沿'成纤聚合物生产 → 聚合物转化为纤维'两大阶段展开，强调基本原理与新进展，刻意回避高等数学；由 15 位作者分章撰写，取'精而深'而非'广而浅'。

## Relevance to Hygiene R&D 与卫材研发的关联

这是卫材无纺布的**成纤-纺丝主线骨架**：卫材一次/acquisition 层、背层、弹性腰围、纺粘/熔喷阻隔层所依赖的聚合物纺丝与成网原理，均可在本书找到体系化基础。

- **纺粘与熔喷 = 卫材核心成网工艺**：Ch19 [[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch19 Spunbonding and Melt-Blowing Processes]] 直接对应 SSMMS/ATB 等无纺布产线；与 [[Polypropylene Handbook Spunbond and Meltblown Nonwoven Resins]] 互为树脂-工艺两面。
- **丙纶纤维**：Ch16 [[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch16 Polypropylene Fibres]] 是卫材主要无纺布基材（PP）的聚合物学与纺丝学基础。
- **纺丝流变与结构**：Ch02 [[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch02 Structural Principles of Polymeric Fibres]]、Ch03 [[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch03 Fluid Flow in Fibre Spinning]]、Ch04 [[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch04 Melt-Spinning Processes]] 提供取向/结晶/Tg、剪切-拉伸流变、喷丝与可纺性等机理，直接支撑纤维细旦化与复合纤维开发。
- **复合/异形纤维**：Ch14 [[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch14 Speciality Polyamide and Polyester Yarns]]（共轭双组分、异形与中空纤维）与 [[Bicomponent Fibre Technology Development Trends]] 支撑 BiCo 复合纤维（皮芯/并列、亲水与柔感）路线。
- **质量与测试**：Ch11 [[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch11 Testing of Manufactured Fibres]] 面向纤维质控与研发测试口径。
- **可持续**：Ch20 [[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch20 Reuse of Polymer and Fibre Waste]] 对应树脂/纤维废料回用与循环经济。
- **状态说明**：该书**已在库内逐章完整编译**（Ch01–Ch20 均有独立章页），本页作为书目索引页统一收口。
