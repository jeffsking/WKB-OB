## Scope and Structure 范围与结构

A compact four-part experimental paper (结构为四段式实验论文):

1. **§1 亲水性概述 — Hydrophilicity basics**: definition of hydrophilicity; adsorption mechanism of surfactant finish on the polyethylene (PE) sheath of ES fibre; classification into single-hydrophilic (单亲) vs multi-hydrophilic (多亲) finishes; the "point penetration, small wetted area → drier topsheet" design logic.
2. **§2 结果与讨论 — Results and discussion**:技术指标 (absorption speed, rewet) with the referenced national limits; strike-through time test (10 layers of standard filter paper, saline); rewet test at 20 g/m² basis weight.
3. **§3 实例测试 — Case tests**: finished-diaper validation of single-hydrophilic vs multi-hydrophilic topsheets; a commercial multi-hydrophilic finish trialled on whitened hydrophilic ES fibre blends (1.65 dtex × 38 mm and 2.22 dtex × 38 mm, 50:50 by mass, 25 g/m² fabric) in embossed and perforated topsheet variants; benchmarked against an imported premium baby diaper.
4. **§4 结论 — Conclusions**: three numbered conclusions plus references [1]–[5] (Zhejiang Sci-Tech / Soochow University theses and earlier Chinese nonwoven literature).

## Key Findings 关键发现

**Mechanism 机理**

- ES 纤维皮层 PE（聚乙烯）为非极性聚合物，不带电荷；亲水油剂表面活性剂与 PE 之间**不存在强电荷作用或极性吸附，仅存在色散力吸附**（dispersion-force adsorption）。
- Dispersion force scales with molecular size: the larger the hydrophobic group and the longer the molecular chain, the stronger the anchoring to PE → better rinse resistance → durable multi-insult hydrophilicity. Small surfactant molecules desorb easily and are flushed away [1].
- Design rule stated explicitly: smaller wetted area on the skin-contact side during urine strike-through ⇒ higher perceived dryness. The route to that is **single-point-penetration high hydrophilicity that suppresses lateral spreading** and reduces rewet [2].

**Finish classification 油剂分类**

| Finish type 类型 | Adhesion to fibre 附着力 | First-strike performance 首次 | Multi-strike durability 多次 | Wetting morphology 润湿形态 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Single-hydrophilic 单亲（扩散型） | Weak 小, easily rinsed off 易被尿液冲洗 | Excellent 极佳 | Poor 衰减快 | Spreading 扩散状, wetted dia. > 1 cm |
| Multi-hydrophilic 多亲 | Strong 大, rinse-resistant 不易冲洗 | Good 良好 | Good 良好 | Point penetration 点滴穿透, wetted dia. min 0.5 cm |

**Strike-through time 穿透时间（Table 2, successive insults）**

| Type 类型 | 1st | 2nd | 3rd | 4th |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Single-hydrophilic 单亲无纺布 / s | 0.73 | 2.48 | 4.52 | 6.85 |
| Multi-hydrophilic 多亲无纺布 / s | 1.26 | 1.35 | 1.51 | 1.62 |

**Rewet 回渗量（Table 3, 20 g/m², five specimens）**

| Type 类型 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | Average 平均 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Single-hydrophilic 单亲 / g | 3.24 | 2.85 | 4.19 | 3.62 | 4.71 | **3.722** |
| Multi-hydrophilic 多亲 / g | 0.12 | 0.14 | 0.21 | 0.18 | 0.16 | **0.162** |

**Finished-diaper validation 成裤验证**

- **Single-hydrophilic topsheet (Table 4)**: first absorption ≈ 0.86–1.24 s, fifth absorption 7.39–8.62 s; rewet 3.89–6.17 g. Classic single-burst behaviour with rapid decay 单次强亲水、衰减快.
- **Multi-hydrophilic topsheet (Table 5)**: first absorption 1.39–1.86 s, fifth 1.96–2.41 s; rewet 0.84–1.32 g.
- **Commercial multi-hydrophilic finish trial (Tables 6 & 7)** — 25 g/m² air-through fabric from whitened hydrophilic ES fibre: embossed topsheet T1–T5 = 1.25 / 1.44 / 1.56 / 1.71 / 1.82 s with rewet 1.06 g; perforated topsheet = 1.39 / 1.45 / 1.57 / 1.65 / 1.77 s with rewet 1.36 g. The imported premium-brand benchmark returned the same figures (differences small), i.e. the domestic multi-hydrophilic route **matches imported premium performance**.

**Referenced national specification 国标引用（Table 1, 部分）**

| Indicator 指标 | Requirement 要求 |
|---|---|
| First absorption speed 第一次吸收速度 | ≤ 50 s |
| Second absorption speed 第二次吸收速度 | ≤ 100 s |
| Rewet 回渗量 | ≤ 15 g |

**Conclusions 结论**

1. ES fibre hydrophilic finishes divide into single-hydrophilic and multi-hydrophilic types by chemistry 按油剂化学特性分单亲型、多亲型.
2. Single-hydrophilic finish gives high rewet and poor in-use experience — 质量档次不高 (low quality tier).
3. Multi-hydrophilic air-through nonwoven delivers fast multi-insult absorption, short strike-through time and low rewet — the mainstream material and development direction for disposable hygiene products 高档纸尿裤主流原料与发展方向.

## Relevance to Hygiene R&D 与卫材研发的关联

**Rating 评级: HIGH 高 — directly applicable to the REL / topsheet / rewet programme.**

- **Mechanism maps straight onto our durables vs non-durables question 机理直接对应耐久亲水议题.** The dispersion-force anchoring model explains why hydrophilic finish durability is a *surfactant molecular-design* variable (hydrophobe size, chain length), not merely an add-on level. This is the lever behind "hydrophilisation durability" in topsheet supplier discussions.
- **Quantifies the rewet/speed trade-off and refutes the usual framing.** The common assumption is that faster first strike-through ⇒ better dryness. This source shows the opposite at multi-insult level: the single-hydrophilic fabric wins insult #1 (0.73 s vs 1.26 s) yet loses decisively on rewet (3.722 g vs 0.162 g) and on insults 2–4. **First-strike time alone is not a valid dryness proxy. 单次穿透时间不能单独作为干爽性代理指标。**
- **Wetting morphology is a design variable, not a side effect.** Point-penetration vs spreading (0.5 cm vs > 1 cm wetted spot) is used here as the causal bridge between finish type and topsheet dryness — consistent with the "small diffusion area ⇒ dry skin-contact surface" logic in our rewet design principles.
- **Numbers usable as screening targets.** The 20 g/m² multi-hydrophilic rewet of ~0.16 g vs ~3.7 g provides a bench-scale acceptance discriminator for candidate finish packages; finished-diaper rewet of 0.8–1.4 g is a realistic premium target.
- **Caveats 保留意见 (do not over-extrapolate)**: single Chinese trade-journal source n = 5 with self-built lab rigs 自制装置 (not stated to be NWSP/ISO/GB-T standard methods); the oil剂 was not identified beyond "某进口品牌"; basis weight, calender pattern (压纹 vs 打孔) and the 50:50 dtex blend are confounded with finish type in Table 6–7. Treat values as directional, not as validated tolerances. Fabrics tested at 20–25 g/m² only.
