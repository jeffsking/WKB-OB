# Chemical bonding

## One-line Position 一句话定位

Chapter 7 (R.A. Chapman) systematically treats chemical (latex/binder) bonding — the dominant alternative to thermal consolidation and the route of choice when a broad property palette must be engineered, with explicit flushable-wipe and incontinence binder chemistry.（中文：第 7 章系统讲化学（胶乳/粘合剂）粘合——热粘的主要替代路线，需在柔软/防水/透气/阻燃/可回收间做多目标设计，并明确给出可冲散拭布与失禁用品粘合剂化学。）

## Summary 摘要

- It defines the binder and its multiple roles, then surveys binder polymer chemistry: vinyl, acrylic, butadiene (SBR/NBR/natural rubber), and specialty systems (PU, phenolic, epoxy).（先定义粘合剂及其多重角色，再梳理乙烯基、丙烯酸、丁苯等聚合物化学。）
- A rigorous subsection explains emulsion polymerisation, the Fox equation for copolymer Tg, MFFT, and coagulation/migration and crosslinking.（严谨小节：乳液聚合、Fox 共聚物 Tg 方程、最低成膜温度、凝聚/迁移与交联。）
- The mechanism of chemical bonding is analysed: wetting, binder cohesion, adhesion, distribution, binder:fibre ratio — including the FRP transition at high add-on.（分析粘合机理：润湿、内聚、粘附、分布、粘合剂/纤维比，及高添量下的纤维增强塑料转变。）
- Application methods (saturation, foam, spray, print, coating, solution) and dryers follow, closing with explicit hygiene/medical and wipes applications.（施加工艺与干燥，收尾于明确的卫生/医用与拭布应用。）

## Key Claims 关键论断

- Binder selection is a multi-objective design problem, not merely "hold fibres together".（粘合剂选型是多目标设计问题。）
- Latices are the workhorse — water-borne for penetration and energy-efficient drying.（胶乳是主力，水基利于渗透与节能干燥。）
- Copolymer Tg (Fox) sets handle and wet stability — softer monomers → softer fabric.（共聚物 Tg 决定手感与湿稳定性。）
- Binder migration during drying creates binder-rich skin / starved core → delamination risk.（干燥中粘合剂迁移形成表富里贫，致分层风险。）
