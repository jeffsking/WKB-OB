# Polypropylene Handbook Spunbond and Meltblown Nonwoven Resins 聚丙烯手册：纺粘/熔喷非织造布树脂

> Source: *Handbook of Polypropylene and Polypropylene Composites*, 2nd Edition, Revised and Expanded; editor Harutun G. Karian (RheTech, Inc.); Marcel Dekker, Inc., 2003; ISBN 0-8247-4064-5; Plastics Engineering Vol.51; 20 chapters + Preface + Index, ~729 pages. This page is compiled from the full-book 深度拆解报告 `PP Handbook_report.md.md`, which works from a preview text (~136,000 characters).
> Caveat 说明：Ch.4、Ch.6–16、Ch.18 的正文本预览未收录，相关论点以 Preface 摘要与交叉引用为准（报告中标注 [未找到原句]）。

## Executive summary 执行摘要

The book is the authoritative PP reference of the early 2000s. Its value to hygiene nonwovens concentrates in **Ch.2 §2.9.1 Fibers**, which for the first time systematically gives the process parameters of the two PP nonwoven routes:

- Spun-bonded 纺粘：CR homopolymer, **MFR ≈ 35 g/10min**, air quench + high-pressure air draw, vacuum deposition on a moving porous belt, bonding by **heated calender rolls**.
- Melt-blown 熔喷：**MFR ≥ 500 g/10min**, **narrow MWD**, special melt-blowing die with high-temperature high-velocity air; fine fibres give **water-vapour permeable / liquid-water impermeable** behaviour.

This pair is the technical basis of the SMS/SMMS multilayer structures used in baby diapers, adult incontinence products and feminine care.

## Nonwoven processes 非织造布工艺（Ch.2 §2.9.1, p.28）

### Classification 三分法

> "Nonwoven fabrics account for more PP usage than any other single fiber application. There are three types of nonwoven fabrics: thermobonded, from staple fibers; spun-bonded; and melt-blown."

### Spun-bonded 纺粘

> "In this process, molten filaments are air quenched and then drawn by air at high pressure. To form a fabric, the filaments are then deposited on a moving porous belt to which a vacuum is applied underneath. Bonding of the fibers is accomplished by passing the fabric through heated calender rolls. The type of resin usually used is a CR homopolymer of about 35g/10min MFR."

### Melt-blown 熔喷

> "In the melt-blowing process, polymer is extruded through a special melt-blowing die. The die feeds high-temperature air to the exiting filaments at a high velocity, and, in addition, the exiting filaments are quenched with cool air."
> "The melt-blowing process requires resins that have very high melt flow rates, on the order of 500g/10min or higher. A resin of narrow MWD is also desirable."
> "Fabrics from fine melt-blown fibers can be used in medical applications because they allow the passage of water vapor but prevent the penetration of liquid water and aqueous solutions."

### Property contrast 性能对比

> "Spun-bonded fabrics are strong, whereas melt-blown fabrics are soft and have high bulk."

The source also notes "often combinations of two types are used together" — the SMS/SMMS logic — but gives **no optimal M-layer ratio**, which the report flags as an unresolved trade-off between liquid barrier and strength.

## Critical assessment 批判性评估

Strengths 优势：20 章全链条覆盖；作者多来自 Basell、BP Amoco、Ametek、Owens Corning、Suzorite 等产业一线；2003 版反映 1999–2003 的产业整合与 TPO 增长。

Limitations 局限（报告中明确列出）：
1. 非织造布内容偏薄：Ch.2 §2.9.1 仅约 4 页，缺少现代 SMS/SMMS 多层工艺、亲水处理、超细纤维等 2005 年后进展。
2. 缺少定量成本-性能优化模型。
3. 环境维度薄弱：碳足迹、LCA 未纳入。
4. 中国/新兴市场视角缺失。
5. 回收章节未覆盖化学回收、upcycling。

## Hygiene implication 卫材关联

Highly relevant — this is the single most directly applicable PP reference in the batch. 可落地结论：
1. **S 层采购规格**：锁定 CR 级 HPP、MFR 约 35 g/10min（报告建议窗口 30–40 g/10min，高克重可下调至 25–30）。
2. **M 层采购规格**：窄 MWD、MFR 500–1200 g/10min 的专用熔喷级均聚 PP；MFR 不足会断丝，MWD 过宽导致纤维粗细不均、过滤效率差。
3. **M 层功能定义**：透湿阻液——阻隔尿液/经血回渗同时允许水蒸气透出，是干爽性的机理依据。
4. **合规论据**：PP 单体无健康疑虑，可写入欧盟/北美合规文档（对比 PS 苯乙烯、PVC 氯乙烯）。
5. **克重与成本**：PP 密度 0.9 g/mL 是热塑性塑料中最低之一，同克重下更厚更蓬松，可参数化进运输成本模型。
6. **功能填料**：若引入 TiO2/抗菌剂等功能填料，可用 1–3% PPgMAH 作偶联剂。
