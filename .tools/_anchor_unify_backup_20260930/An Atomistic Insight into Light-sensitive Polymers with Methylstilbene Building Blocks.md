# An Atomistic Insight into Light-sensitive Polymers with Methylstilbene Building Blocks

> Chapter 3 (reprint of Bandeira N.A.G., Tylkowski B., Bogdanowicz K.A., Giamberini M., Bo C., *Polymer International* 2015, 64, 935–941). Computational (quantum-chemical) validation of the structure–photoresponse causal chain established in Ch.1–2.

## One-line Position 一句话定位

Provides the atomistic, quantum-chemical proof that trans→cis isomerization of the 4-methylstilbene unit is the sole driver of P4 shell collapse and vanillin release — bridging molecular design to macro device performance.
（以原子级量子化学计算证明 4-甲基芪单元的 trans→cis 异构是 P4 壳层塌缩与香兰素释放的唯一驱动，桥接分子设计与宏观器件性能。）

## Summary 摘要(3-6句忠实原文)

- The structure–photoresponse causal chain of photoactive main-chain LCPs is validated at the atomic level: UV-induced trans→cis isomerization of the 4-methylstilbene unit causes large-scale backbone contraction, driving shell morphologic collapse and vanillin release.（光敏主链液晶聚合物的"结构–光响应"因果链在原子级被验证：UV 诱导的 trans→cis 异构引发大尺度主链收缩，驱动壳层塌缩与释放。）
- cis destroys liquid crystallinity: trans-stilbene is extended/rod-like and LC-capable; cis loses the linear form required for LC, hence isothermal isotropization under UV.（cis 态破坏液晶性：trans 呈伸展棒状具液晶性，cis 失去线性几何而丧失液晶性，故 UV 下等温各向同性化。）
- 4-Methylstilbene main-chain polymers are irreversibly photochromic (thermal back-isomerization does not readily occur), suiting one-shot/latched photo-triggered smart materials.（4-甲基芪主链聚合物呈不可逆光致变色，适合一次性/锁存式光触发智能材料。）
- Periodic-boundary DFT/TDDFT (HSE06) reproduces the experimental spectral blue-shift + intensity loss on isomerization and confirms trans→cis; reverse cis→trans is ~3× slower kinetically (lower absorption probability).（周期性边界 DFT/TDDFT 复现了实验光谱蓝移与强度减弱，确认 trans→cis；反向 cis→trans 动力学慢约 3 倍。）
- The isophthaloyl unit is the key design knob: it adds two localized virtual orbitals (pushing π(C=C)* to LUMO+2) and lowers Tg to 45 °C for amorphous, easy processability.（间苯二甲酰单元是关键设计旋钮：新增两个局域虚轨道并将 Tg 降至 45 °C，获得非晶易加工性。）
- Chain models (C2/C8) confirm per-monomer shortening of 4.7 Å / 5.4 Å, forming more "grooves" consistent with the experimental perforated/concaved capsule shell.（链模型证实每单体缩短 4.7/5.4 Å，形成更多"沟槽"，与实验穿孔/凹陷壳层一致。）
