# Seymour Polymer Chemistry Ch14 Plasticizers and Additives

> Source: Seymour/Carraher, *Polymer Chemistry*, Ch.14 — Plasticizers and Additives（增塑剂与添加剂）.
> Citation convention 引用约定: 源文件为 .txt 抽取件，**无正式印张页码**；本页以源文件行号（line no.）定位。源文件标注多数引用为"约 line"（approximate），本页保留该不确定性，原书页码缺失处标注 [待验证]。

## Executive Summary 内容概要

Chapter 14 is the book's **formulation handbook（配方学手册）**: 14 classes of additives, each mapped to a specific processing or service-life problem — plasticization、oxidation、thermal degradation、UV、flame、microbial attack、lubrication、curing、static、foaming、compatibilization、impact、processing aid. 对卫材的意义最直接：**PP/PE 纺粘-熔喷的抗氧化与加工稳定体系、抗静电剂对生产良率的影响、PE 透气膜与复合结构的相容剂/抗冲改性**，全部落在本章；**增塑剂迁移毒性**是婴幼儿用品合规的核心议题。

## Plasticizers 增塑剂（§14.1）

> *"According to the ASTM D-883 definition, a plasticizer is a material incorporated in a plastic to increase its workability and flexibility or distensibility. The addition of a plasticizer may lower the melt viscosity, elastic modulus, and glass transition temperature (Tg) of a plastic."* (line 51)

| 类型 Type | 机理 Mechanism | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| **Internal plasticization 内部增塑** | *"through copolymerization giving a more flexible polymer backbone or by grafting another polymer onto a given polymer backbone"*；如 **poly(vinyl chloride-co-vinyl acetate)** (line 54) | 共聚/接枝引入柔性链 |
| **External plasticization 外部增塑** | *"achieved through incorporation of a plasticizing agent into a polymer through mixing and/or heating"* (line 59) | 物理混入，存在迁移风险 |

> *"The presence of bulky groups on the polymer chain increases segmental motion and placement of such groups through grafting acts as an internal plasticizer."* (line 56)

**DOP 的历史与地位**（line 62）：*"Waldo Semon patented the use of tricresyl phosphate as a plasticizer for poly(vinyl chloride) (PVC) in 1933. This was later replaced by the less toxic di-2-ethylhexyl phthalate (DOP), which is now the most widely used plasticizer."* — DOP 由 2-乙基己醇与邻苯二甲酸酐反应制得（line 64）。

**溶度参数匹配 Solubility-parameter matching**（line 67）：

> *"Plasticizers with solubility parameters and type of bonding similar to those of the polymer are more apt to be compatible than when the solubility parameters are different. The solubility parameter for PVC (9.66) is near that of good plasticizers for PVC such as DOP (8.85)."*

| 参数 Parameter | 数值 Value | 备注 Note |
|---|---|---|
| PVC 溶度参数 δ | 9.66 | 与增塑剂匹配判据 |
| DOP 溶度参数 δ | 8.85 | 与 PVC 相近，故相容 |

**毒性与迁移 Toxicity and migration**（line 70, 114-119）：

> *"The development of plasticizers has been plagued with toxicity problems. Thus, the use of highly toxic polychlorinated biphenyls (PCBs) has been discontinued. Phthalic acid esters, such as DOP, may be extracted from blood stored in plasticized PVC blood bags and tubing. These aromatic esters are also distilled from PVC upholstery in closed automobiles in hot weather. These problems have been solved by using oligomeric polyesters as nonmigrating plasticizers, instead of DOP."*

> *"Experimental investigations and assessments show that the average person takes in about 2 g a year of external plasticizers. Most of this is from traces of DOA migrating from food packaging."* (line 73)

**替代路径**：**oligomeric polyesters（寡聚聚酯）作为非迁移型增塑剂**（line 70）。

## Antioxidants 抗氧化剂（§14.2）

> *"Antioxidants retard oxidative degradation. Heat, mechanical shear, ultraviolet (UV) radiation, and heat can be responsible for the formation of free radicals, which in turn can act to shorten polymer chains and increase crosslinking, both leading to a deterioration in material properties. Free radical production often begins a chain reaction."* (line 78)

| 类别 Class | 机理 Mechanism | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary antioxidant 主抗氧剂** | *"donate active hydrogen atoms to free radical sites, thereby quenching or stopping the chain reaction"* (line 81) | 供氢终止链反应（受阻酚类） |
| **Secondary antioxidant / synergist 副抗氧剂/协同剂** | *"act to decompose free radicals to more stable products"* (line 84) | 分解氢过氧化物（亚磷酸酯类） |

> *"the antioxidant acts as a chain transfer agent to produce a dead polymer and a stable free radical that does not initiate chain radical degradation. However, the phenoxy free radical may react with other free radicals to produce a quinone derivative."* (line 87) —— **醌类副产物正是酚类抗氧剂导致制品黄变（phenol yellowing / gas fading）的化学来源**。

## Heat Stabilizers 热稳定剂（§14.3）

> *"In addition to the free radical chain degradation described for polyolefins, another type of degradation (dehydrohalogenation) also occurs with chlorine-containing polymers such as PVC. As shown by the following equation, when heated PVC may lose hydrogen chloride and form a chromophoric conjugated polyene structure. Since the allylic chlorides produced are very unstable, the degradation continues as an unzipping type of chain reaction."* (line 92)

| 要素 Element | 内容 Detail |
|---|---|
| 降解类型 | **dehydrohalogenation 脱卤化氢**，生成**发色共轭多烯**→ 黄变 + 脆化 |
| 自催化特征 | **拉链式 unzipping** 链式反应；*"This type of degradation is accelerated in the presence of iron salts, oxygen, and hydrogen chloride."* (line 94) |
| 传统稳定剂 | *"Toxic lead and barium and cadmium salts act as scavengers for hydrogen chloride"*（仅限导线涂层等，line 97） |
| 低毒替代 | **有机亚磷酸酯**（混合芳基/烷基亚磷酸酯、三苯基亚磷酸酯）络合游离金属离子；**环氧化不饱和油（如环氧大豆油）作 HCl 清除剂**（line 100） |

## UV Stabilizers 光稳定剂（§14.4）

> *"Since the energy of this radiation is 100 to 72 kcal, it is sufficiently strong to cleave covalent bonds and cause yellowing and embrittlement of organic polymers."* (line 105) （单位口径 [待验证]，通常为 kcal/mol 或 kcal/einstein）

> *"The metal complexes function as energy transfer agents, free radical scavengers, and decomposers of hydroperoxides. The pigments absorb UV radiation and act as screening agents. More than 100,000 tons of UV stabilizers is used annually by the U.S. polymer industry."* (line 108)

| 机制 Mechanism | 中文 | 代表（章节地图 line 301） |
|---|---|---|
| Energy transfer 能量转移 | 能量转移剂 | 镍配合物 |
| Free radical scavenging 自由基清除 | 自由基清除剂 | 2-hydroxybenzophenone、benzotriazole |
| Hydroperoxide decomposition | 氢过氧化物分解 | 金属配合物 |
| Screening 屏蔽 | 屏蔽剂 | 颜料、**炭黑 carbon black** |

## Flame Retardants 阻燃剂（§14.5）

> *"Fuel, oxygen, and high temperature are essential for the combustion process."* (line 113)

三种作用机制：

| 机制 Mechanism | 原文依据 Evidence | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| **自由基终止** | *"Since halogen and phosphorus radicals couple with free radicals produced in the combustion process and terminate the reaction, many flame retardants are halogen or phosphorus compounds."* (line 116) | 卤/磷自由基捕获 |
| **稀释降温** | *"Fillers such as alumina trihydrate (ATH) release water when heated and hence reduce the temperature of the combustion reaction."* (line 119) | ATH 受热放水 |
| **成炭屏蔽** | *"Char, formed in some combustion processes, also shields the reactants from oxygen and retards the outward diffusion of volatile combustible products. Aromatic polymers tend to char, and some phosphorus and boron compounds catalyze char formation."* (line 125) | 炭层阻隔氧与挥发分 |

**协同效应 Synergy**（line 122）：

> *"Synergistic flame retardants such as a mixture of antimony trioxide and an organic bromo compound are much more effective than single flame retardants. Thus, while a polyester containing 11.5% tetrabromophthalic anhydride burned without charring at high temperatures, charring but no burning was noted when 5% antimony oxide was added."*

| 配方 Formulation | 燃烧行为 Burning behaviour |
|---|---|
| 聚酯 + 11.5% 四溴苯二甲酸酐（TBPA） | 高温下燃烧，**不成炭** |
| 上述 + 5% Sb₂O₃ | **成炭但不燃烧** |

## Application Additives 应用添加剂（§14.6–14.14）

| 添加剂 Additive | 原文定义/机理 Definition | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| **Biocide 抗菌剂** | 抑制微生物侵蚀（PVC 的 "pink staining" 粉红斑，line 176, 308） | 抗菌/防霉 |
| **Lubricant 润滑剂** | *"improve the flow characteristics of a material during its processing... by reducing the melt viscosity or by decreasing adhesion between the metallic surfaces of the processing equipment and the material"* (line 154) | 内润滑降分子摩擦/外润滑降设备黏附（line 156-158） |
| **Curing agent 固化剂** | accelerator（CBS/DMBT）、peroxide、drier（line 310） | 硫化/固化 |
| **Antistatic agent 抗静电剂** | *"dissipate static electrical charges... Most antistats are hygroscopic and attract a thin film of water to the polymer surface"* (line 130-134)；*"Internal antistats are admixed with the polymer, while external antistats are usually sprayed on the polymer surface."* (line 221) | 内加型/外喷型；干冷气候静电最明显（line 132） |
| **Chemical blowing agent CBA** | *"employed to create lighter weight material through formation of a foam"*；*"Cellular polymers not only provide insulation and resiliency but are usually stronger on a weight basis than solid polymers."* (line 137-139) | 化学发泡剂 |
| **Compatibilizer 相容剂** | *"provide miscibility or compatibility to materials that are otherwise immiscible... Typically compatibilizers act to reduce the interfacial tension and are concentrated at phase boundaries"* (line 142-144)；**reactive（化学键合）vs nonreactive（物理增容）**(line 146) | 相容剂 |
| **Impact modifier 抗冲改性剂** | *"improve the resistance of materials to stress. Most impact modifiers are elastomers such as ABS, BS, methacrylate–butadiene–styrene, acrylic, ethylene–vinyl acetate, and chlorinated polyethylene."* (line 149-151) | 抗冲改性剂 |
| **Processing aid 加工助剂** | acrylate copolymers（line 315） | 加工助剂 |

## Methodology Critique 方法论批判

- **行号为约值**：源文件自述多数引用为"约 line（approximate）"（如 line 63-65、114-119），并非精确行号；本页保留"约"的语义，回原书核对时不可当作精确定位。
- **UV 能量单位缺失**："100 to 72 kcal"（line 105）未标 mol 或 einstein；按 200–400 nm 波段换算应为约 143–72 kcal/mol，原文上限 100 与之不符，**标注 [待验证]**。
- **"2 g/年"的暴露数据年代不明**：原文未给出该评估的年份、地域与方法（line 73），且邻苯二甲酸酯法规在 2000 年后大幅收紧，该数字**不宜直接用于当前合规论证** [待验证]。
- **抗氧剂"链转移剂"表述需限定**：line 87 说抗氧剂作 chain transfer agent 生成"稳定自由基"，同时又说 phenoxy 自由基会生成醌类衍生物——后者恰恰是黄变源，说明"稳定"是相对的，主抗氧剂本身会被消耗并产生有色副产物。
- **阻燃协同案例是单一体系**：11.5% TBPA + 5% Sb₂O₃ 的数据来自聚酯体系（line 122），**不可外推到聚烯烃**；聚烯烃的溴-锑协同效率与基体成炭倾向不同。

## Hygiene Implication 卫材关联

本章是全书对卫材配方最直接的章节，以下均为实质关联：

- **PP/PE 纺粘-熔喷的稳定化体系就是 §14.2**：聚烯烃在挤出纺丝中经受 200 °C 以上高温与强剪切，原文明确热、机械剪切、UV 都会产生自由基，进而**断链（chain shortening）与交联（crosslinking）同时发生**（line 78）——这正对应 PP 纺丝中的**MFR 漂移（降解导致熔体流动速率上升）与凝胶/晶点（交联导致）**这一对矛盾。主抗氧剂（受阻酚供氢）+ 副抗氧剂（亚磷酸酯分解氢过氧化物）复配是工业标准解法；但需注意**酚类抗氧剂的醌类副产物是黄变源**（line 87），卫材白色面层对色相敏感，配方需选低色污体系。
- **抗静电剂直接决定无纺布生产良率**：PP/PE 是绝缘材料，纺丝与卷取中的静电积累导致飞丝、粘连、成网不匀；原文指出抗静电剂靠**吸湿薄膜**导电（line 134），因此**干冷环境下失效**——这与车间湿度控制直接相关；内加型（粒料共混）与外喷型（表面喷涂）的分工也决定了其耐久性与迁移性权衡。
- **润滑剂与加工助剂决定高速产线的稳定性**：内润滑剂降熔体黏度、外润滑剂降设备金属面黏附（line 156-158），对应螺杆扭矩、口模积料（die drool）与热辊粘辊问题——这是 600 mpm 级高速产线良率的常见瓶颈。
- **相容剂是多层共挤与复合结构的界面工程**：PP/PE/粘合层、PE + 填料的复合体系需要**反应型相容剂（如马来酸酐接枝 PP）**在相界面形成化学桥（line 146）；与 Ch.13 的偶联剂（硅烷/钛酸酯）构成"无机-有机界面"与"有机-有机界面"两套工具。
- **增塑剂迁移是婴幼儿用品的合规红线**：原文给出 DOP 可从 PVC 血袋与 tubing 中被萃取、从汽车内饰中挥发（line 70），替代方案是**寡聚聚酯非迁移型增塑剂**。对卫材中任何含 PVC 的涂层/膜材（床单、防护垫），应优先考虑非迁移体系，并核对目标市场的邻苯二甲酸酯限令 [待验证]。
- **抗冲改性剂与弹性件**：EVA、CPE、MBS、丙烯酸类等弹性体抗冲改性剂（line 151）与 Ch.09 的 TPE 谱系互补，用于低温韧性与抗撕裂。
- **阻燃对卫材需求有限**：纸尿裤/卫生巾主体为纤维素与聚烯烃薄壁材料，通常无强制阻燃要求；仅婴幼儿睡袋、防溢乳垫等特定品类需评估，首选 ATH（同时兼具 Ch.13 的填料功能）。
