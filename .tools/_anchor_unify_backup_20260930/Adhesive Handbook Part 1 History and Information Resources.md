# Handbook of Adhesive Technology — Part 1: History and Information Resources

## One-line Position 一句话定位
The meta-foundations of the whole handbook: a 2400-year adhesive timeline that maps directly onto which adhesive families a hygiene-product line should buy, plus a three-level information model for locating adhesion knowledge.

## Evidence 证据

- **胶-密界限模糊** — "Only in modern times have we attempted to differentiate between adhesives and sealants. For the most part it has been a vain attempt, as many so-called adhesives also serve as sealants, and all sealants have adhesive properties." [Line 98-103]
- **天然胶仍主导** — "Today, even in the most developed countries, natural adhesives dominate the market because they are less expensive than synthetic-based materials, and they perform the intended function." [Line 463-471]
- **历史即路线规划工具** — "As new materials are developed, a review of the history of uses can lead one to see where they might be applied to improve old applications, and sometimes to satisfy requirements of entirely new applications." [Line 15-17]
- **"结构性"术语滥用** — "Adhesive manufacturers and their advertising departments now miss no opportunity to use, or abuse, the word [structural]. Companies which formerly sold urethane, acrylic, or anaerobic adhesives now call their products 'structural urethane,' 'structural acrylic,' or 'structural anaerobic' adhesives." [Line 402-409]
- **轻量化驱动航空胶** — "From the earliest flights to the most modern aerospace equipment, light weight has been one of the most vital considerations." [Line 499-504]
- **原料多样性** — "It would be a virtual impossibility for any single volume to list all the ingredients which might conceivably be employed in an adhesive compound." [Line 380-384]
- **相容剂角色** — "large quantities of adhesive resins called compatibilizers are used between layers of noncompatible extruded films, primarily in packaging applications." [Line 444-450]
- **CTBN 改性** — "This development led to the carboxy-terminated nitrile (CTBN) rubber modifiers used to flexibilize epoxy and other brittle resin adhesives in the late 1960s." [Line 424-426]
- **信息过载** — "The average person can easily become swamped with too much information, to the point where it can become difficult to decipher the good from the bad, the old from the new, the important from the trivial, or the temporary from the permanent." [Line 584-597]
- **小行业困境** — "the fact remains that the Adhesives Industry is a small industry… information about adhesives becomes submerged in the detail of other processes and products." [Line 611-626]
- **三级信息模型** — "The first is the academic level… The second level of information is developmental… Finally, the third level of information is anecdotal." [Line 647-675]

## Business Connection Points 业务连接点

- **卫材胶黏剂体系的"成熟窗口"时间轴** — PVAc 1939 → VAE 1950s（>55 % 醋酸乙烯）→ 全面商用 1960s：芯体面材贴合、湿巾无纺布贴合最常用最经济的水基体系，成本优势与性能经 70+ 年验证。
- **腰贴/封条/防漏边的 PSA 依赖** — 天然橡胶基 PSA（1845 Henry Day → 1925 3M 胶带产业）至今仍是 PSA 最大基料。采购策略：天然橡胶价随农产品周期波动，需在 3M / Bostik / Nitto 等供应源间对冲。
- **热熔胶 = 产线速度与能耗的决定项** — 1965 Kraton 101（SBS）商业化 → 现代无纺布复合、腰围贴合、防漏边涂布的主流基础树脂。SBS/SIS 直接决定产线速度、能耗与单耗成本；1960s 后期的 CTBN 改性提示"成本不显著上升但性能跃升"的配方路径。
- **采购谈判的核心原则** — 历史数据支持"性能相当时优先天然"：淀粉/改性淀粉用于包装纸贴合与纸芯定型仍是最优性价比。
- **胶-密一体不应割裂议价** — 防漏边贴合 + 密封液体本质是"胶-密一体"；多层复合膜（7 层/3 胶层）直接对应面层-导流层-SAP 锁-背膜结构，Compatibilizer 可作为"用粘合剂解决分层/相容性"的替代思路。
- **规格谈判看数据不看术语** — 须明确剥离强度、持粘性、耐温性等力学测试数据，避免供应商用"结构级"溢价推销非必要性能。
- **信息三层模型可迁移到 R&D 流程** — 学术层（SAP-pulp 界面机理、纤维润湿理论）→ 研发层（供应商技术包）→ 应用层（产线实操与异常案例），配合 Green Seal 环保胶标准做合规结构化。
