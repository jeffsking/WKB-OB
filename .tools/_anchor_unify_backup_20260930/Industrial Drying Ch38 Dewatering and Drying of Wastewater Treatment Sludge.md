# Ch38 — Dewatering and Drying of Wastewater Treatment Sludge

> Source: Mujumdar (ed.), *Handbook of Industrial Drying*, 4th ed., CRC Press, 2014, Ch.38, pp.885–901 [页码范围待验证；正文引句页码 p.887–900 均已核对].

## 1. Overview 概述

Chapter 38 reviews the complete water-removal chain for wastewater-treatment sludge: (i) moisture classification, (ii) mechanical dewatering (filtration, pressing, centrifugation — water removed without evaporation), and (iii) thermal drying (direct, indirect, combined, and hybrid dryers). Its organizing thesis is economic: because sludge arrives at >95% moisture, the engineering logic is to push as much dewatering as possible into the cheap, non-evaporative mechanical stage before thermal drying is justified. 机械脱水优先于蒸发干燥 —— 这是本章的中心经济论点，对卫材浆料/纤维悬浮液的除水路线同样适用。

Relevance to hygiene materials: ★☆☆☆☆ — the substrate (municipal/industrial sludge) is far from absorbent hygiene products, but two substrate-independent lessons transfer: the dewatering-before-evaporation energy hierarchy, and the sticky/plastic-phase handling problem for wet hydrophilic suspensions.

## 2. Key Claims 关键论点（原句摘录）

- "Wet sludge from wastewater treatment plants has greater than 95% moisture content." (p.900, Conclusion); "The moisture in sludge can be as high as 99%." (p.887, §38.3)
- "Sludge drying is an energy intensive process. Consequently dewatering, i.e., removing water without evaporation, becomes an important process before thermal drying is utilized." (p.888, §38.4)
- "The bound water content is the theoretical limit of mechanical dewatering." (p.887, §38.3)
- Four moisture classes: "(a) free moisture … removed by gravitational settling; (b) interstitial moisture … removed by strong mechanical forces; (c) surface moisture … held on the surface … by adsorption and adhesion; and (d) intracellular and chemically bound moisture." (p.887, §38.3)
- Mechanical dewatering ceiling: belt presses discharge cake "at about 15 to 25% solid content when the feeding solid concentration varies from 2 to 5%" (p.889); "The current generation of centrifuges can achieve solid contents of 25 to 35%." (p.890)
- Sticky zone: "Once the sticky zone (solid content between 55 and 70%) is reached, the sludge turns into a paste and strong shearing is necessary in order to have a high degree of mixing." (p.891, §38.5.2)
- Direct vs indirect: "Direct dryers are simple in design but the vapors released from the sludge have to be separated from the drying medium" (p.892); "The indirect drying system has the advantage of producing minimal amounts of vapors and is therefore easy to manage"; "The drying rate of indirect dryers may be lower than that of direct dryers because the latter can operate at much higher temperatures." (p.892)
- Safety: "In direct drying with high solid contents at high temperatures, prevention of the explosion of the fine powders must be seriously considered." (p.899, §38.5.6) — 单句带过，细节需参照 Ch43 的粉尘爆炸处理。
- Sterilization: "The US EPA requirement of 70°C for 2 h is equivalent to 90°C for 10 to 15 min, which can be easily satisfied by indirect dryers." (p.899)
- Economics: "The cost of sludge dewatering ranges from about US$34 to 57/t of dry solids … In the case of belt filter presses … US$40 and US$64" (p.899); "The capital investment for sludge drying is roughly US$1.8 million/t/h evaporation rate." (p.899); "flue gas drying preceding incineration can save as much as 60% on the cost compared with direct incineration." (p.899)

## 3. The Dewatering-Before-Evaporation Hierarchy 先脱水后蒸发的能量层级

Energy ladder of water removal 水分脱除能量阶梯:

| Stage 阶段 | Energy character | Cost (chapter's own figures) |
|---|---|---|
| Gravity / thickening 重力浓缩 | zero added energy | — |
| Mechanical dewatering 机械脱水 | removes water as liquid; bond-strength order 1–70 kJ/kg (p.888: vacuum filtration ≈1 kJ/kg, drying test 20–40 kJ/kg, expression ≈70 kJ/kg) | US$34–64/t dry solids |
| Thermal drying 热干燥 | removes water as vapor; latent heat ~2260 kJ/kg at 100°C | US$65–80/t dry solids + ~US$1.8 M/t/h capex |

That is 1–2 orders of magnitude of energy difference between mechanical and thermal removal — 对卫材工程师的直接映射：SAP/纤维浆料或成网前的湿幅，先用真空/压榨/穿透式预脱水把液态水挤掉，再进热风干燥机。

"Bound water" is operational, not absolute: the measured bound-water content depends on the determination method (dilatometric freeze at −20°C, vacuum filtration, expression, drying, thermal analysis; p.887–888) — 给任何浆料规定"脱水极限"时必须注明测定方法。`[待验证：此方法论点为分析文件解读，原文细节未逐字核对]`

## 4. Sticky-Phase Handling 粘性相操控

Any hydrophilic fine-solids suspension transits a paste region on drying. Design responses from the chapter:
1. Back-mix recycle 回混：mix dried sludge with raw sludge to ~70% solids to stay above the sticky zone (p.891).
2. Strong mechanical shear 强剪切：cage mill, kneading disc, porcupine agitator (p.891).
3. Indirect heated surface with self-cleaning scrapers (p.892).

同样的手段适用于黏性卫材辅料的干燥（如高吸水母粒、涂布纤维浆料）。

## 5. Hygiene Implication 卫材关联

- **Dewater-first rule（H38-1）**: before committing a web or SAP-slurry to a thermal dryer, maximize mechanical dewatering (vacuum, press, screw press) — 每机械脱除 1 kg 液态水约省一个数量级的热能。
- **Sticky-zone design（H38-2）**: expect a paste zone (~55–70% solids analog) for any binder-laden furnish; use back-mix or shear.
- **Vapour capture（H38-4）**: indirect/closed-loop drying shrinks the gas stream to treat — 与排放控制章节的逻辑一致。
- **Waste-heat reuse（H38-5）**: "save as much as 60% on the cost" (p.899) — 新建卫生用品干燥线应内置余热回收。
- Overall substrate relevance is low (★☆☆☆☆); the transfer is process-economics logic and sticky-feed handling, not equipment specifics. 本章节价值在于普适的除水经济学，而非具体设备选型。
