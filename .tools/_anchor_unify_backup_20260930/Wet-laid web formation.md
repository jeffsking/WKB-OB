# Wet-laid web formation

## One-line Position 一句话定位

Chapter 3 (C. White) covers wetlaid nonwovens — the papermaking-derived branch — developing the fluid mechanics of slurry deposition, the fibre palette (cellulose, Tencel/Lyocell, SWP, deep-grooved PET) and the bonding toolbox, with hygiene/wipes as named end-uses.（中文：第 3 章讲湿法成网（源自造纸），发展浆料沉积的流体力学、纤维谱系与粘合体系，卫生用品/拭布为明确终端。）

## Summary 摘要

- It opens with the standards definition (EN 29092 / ISO / EDANA) separating wetlaid nonwovens from wetlaid papers, then derives the Bernoulli balance for slice velocity.（先以标准定义区分湿法无纺布与湿法纸，再推导堰口速度的伯努利方程。）
- Two deposition regimes are established: filtration (individual fibres → uniform) vs thickening (pre-flocculated → non-uniform).（两种沉积机制：过滤式均匀、增稠式不均。）
- The fibre palette is surveyed: cellulose, Tencel/Lyocell (wet strength + fibrillation), SWP synthetic wood pulp, deep-grooved PET, polyolefins.（纤维谱系：纤维素、天丝/莱赛尔、合成木浆、深沟槽 PET、聚烯烃。）
- Bonding options: cellulose H-bonding, latex, thermal bicomponent (PP/PE or PET/CoPET), PVA, hydroentanglement — the latter hands directly to Ch.5.（粘合：氢键、胶乳、热风双组分、PVA、水刺——后者直接衔接第 5 章。）

## Key Claims 关键论断

- The L/D > 300 test is the operational separator from paper at the nonwoven/paper boundary.（L/D>300 是湿法无纺布与纸的操作性分界。）
- Slice velocity follows a clean square-root law linking machine speed to pond height/pressure.（堰口速度遵循平方根律，联动机速与液位/压力。）
- Filtration vs thickening deposition is the central uniformity control.（过滤 vs 增稠沉积是均匀性的核心控制。）
- Deep-grooved PET moves fluid transport from a web-design to a fibre-design problem.（深沟槽 PET 将流体输运从网设计下放到纤维截面设计。）
