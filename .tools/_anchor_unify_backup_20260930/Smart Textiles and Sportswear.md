# Smart Textiles and Sportswear

## One-line Position 一句话定位
A 2022 Serbian-language review (English abstract) surveying the generational evolution of special-purpose fibers and the sensor–activator–control architecture of intelligent textiles, with sportswear case studies (PCM, SMP, biomimetics).（运动/防护智能纺织品综述，梳理特种纤维代际与智能纺织感知-响应架构及运动服应用。）

## Summary 摘要
- Special-purpose fibers emerged mid-20th century for fire/heat, ballistic, radiation, and NBC protection; 2nd-gen = modified synthetics (aramid, carbon, glass, ceramic; Nomex, Kevlar, Dyneema…), 3rd-gen = intelligent fibers turning static into dynamic function.（特种纤维代际：二代改性合成纤维，三代智能纤维。）
- Intelligent textile composition: sensor + activator + control unit, must perform stimulus capture, data processing, response triggering, data/energy storage, and communication; classified passive / active / ultra-smart.（智能纺织品组成与三类划分。）
- Sportswear materials: PCM knits with microcapsules for thermal regulation; shape-memory polymers (e.g. Diaplex) changing elasticity/insulation near Tg.（PCM微胶囊与形状记忆聚合物用于热调节。）
- Biomimetic materials: lotus-effect self-cleaning, butterfly structural colour (Teijin Morphotex, 61 alternating PA/PES nanolayers), shark-skin swimsuits (Speedo), Nike Macro React scale-like ventilation.（仿生：自清洁、结构色、鲨鱼皮、仿鳞散热。）
- Embedded electronic sportswear monitors heart/lung/muscle vitals and transmits wirelessly (e.g. CSIRO basketball glove, Bluetooth LED cycling jacket).（嵌入式电子运动服监测生命体征并无线传输。）

## Key Claims 关键论断
- Intelligent textiles perceive and respond to stimuli — a shift from passive protection to active function.（智能纺织品从静态保护转向动态响应。）
- PCM microcapsule thermal management is transferable to comfort layers in hygiene products (encapsulation tech already used for fragrance/skincare microcapsules).（PCM微胶囊热管理可迁移至卫材舒适层。）
