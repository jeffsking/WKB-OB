# Drum Dryers (Ch.9)

> Source: Mujumdar (ed.), Handbook of Industrial Drying, 4th ed., CRC Press, 2014, Ch.9, pp.199–227 (Wan Ramli Wan Daud).

## Chapter Overview 章节概览

The drum dryer is a steam-heated rotating cylinder (or pair) that spreads a viscous liquid, slurry or paste into a thin adherent sheet and evaporates moisture by the intense heat flux of condensing steam inside the drum. The chapter classifies by drum count/config (atmospheric double/single/twin, enclosed, vacuum double), states operating variables (steam 2–7 bar, speed 2–30 rpm, nip 0.05–0.5 mm), describes feeding/spreading and the rheology of the boiling pool, analyses four drying zones with heat-flux data, and covers product handling and control.

> Note: page numbers derived from PDF-page − 21; spot-check against print edition.

## Key Viewpoints 关键观点

### 1. Conductive thin-film regime for viscous feeds 传导薄层机制

> "The drum dryer is commonly used to dry viscous, concentrated solutions, slurries or pastes on steam-heated drums. It can also be used to dry the concentrated solutions or slurries that become more viscous or pasty because of flashing or boiling off of moisture or of irreversible thermochemical transformations of their content that occur on their first contact with the hot drum surface." (p.201)

Ideal for polymer solutions/gels too viscous for spray drying or too sticky for fluid beds.

### 2. Vapor-bubble porosity → instant rehydration 蒸汽泡多孔结构

> "Exposure of the thin sheet to the high heat flux and high temperature for a short period of time can also impart a porous structure to the dried sheet because of the rapid formation of vapor bubbles within the sheet during 'boiling-like' drying. Porous products are excellent in instant food formulations because they are more readily wetted and can be easily rehydrated." (p.202)

### 3. Pilot testing is mandatory 必须中试验证

> "the performance of a drum dryer drying a specific product cannot be adequately predicted by theoretical or semitheoretical models but must be based on drying performance test of the product on a pilot plant drum dryer." (p.205)

Also: "it is still a long way before the drum dryer can be modeled completely" (p.205); setpoints are "trial and error" (p.209).

### 4. Starch gelatinization: a swelling, shear-thinning gel 淀粉糊化的聚合物凝胶行为

> "The gelatinization of starch slurries into a shear-thinning power law paste also changes the spreading and the mixing regime in the pool … It also swells up several times its original volume. In this case, the boiling pool is transformed into a rotating, asymmetric cylinder of paste at the nip." (p.206–207)

"The sheet is thinner for more shear-thinning liquid (n < 1.0)" (p.206); sheet thickness for shear-thinning liquid is up to 1.26× the nip (p.226). A canonical aqueous-polymer-gel regime.

### 5. Zone-wise heat transfer 分区传热

> "The sudden exposure of the liquid feed to the high temperature and intense heat flux on the drum surface at the application zone causes it to immediately heat up and boil … Most of the free moisture is evaporated during this initial boiling of the feed." (p.207); "The heat flux can be as high as 85 kW/m²" (p.208); overall U "between 2000 and 7000 W/m² K" (p.207), falling to "600 and 1250 W/m² K" in the initial-sheet zone (p.208).

The dried layer insulates — fast surface set followed by internal diffusion control.

### 6. Polyacrylamides are a named drum-dried polymer 聚丙烯酰胺

> "Chemicals that have been successfully dried on a drum dryer are polyacrylamides, and various salts such as silicate, benzoate, propionate, and acetate salts." (p.209)

Polyacrylamide — a water-soluble acrylate-family polymer, structural cousin of crosslinked sodium-polyacrylate SAP — is explicitly validated.

### 7. Vacuum drum spares heat-sensitive structure 真空转鼓

> "A heat-sensitive material dried on a vacuum drum dryer suffers less damage and retains most of its structure and functionality than that dried in ordinary atmospheric drum dryer." (p.208); "Heat-sensitive products such as pharmaceuticals and vitamin-containing products can be dried in a vacuum drum dryer." (p.209)

### 8. Glass-transition stick failure 玻璃化转变粘刀

> "if the sugar content is high, some of the sugar does not crystallize properly as drying proceeds but becomes molten instead at well above the glass transition temperature. The uneven scrapping of the doctor's blade at the rubbery and glassy parts of the sheet forms wrinkles … become 'sticks' in the final product." (p.210)

Generalises to any amorphous polymer web/film crossing Tg mid-drying.

## Strengths & Weaknesses 优劣评估

- Strengths: explicit acrylate-family polymer feed (polyacrylamides, p.209); quantified zone heat fluxes usable for sizing; honest Tg-stick failure discussion (p.210); vacuum variant for heat-sensitive materials (p.208–209).
- Weaknesses: no transferable property model for polymer gels — pilot test mandated (p.205); spreading models assume Newtonian or constant-power-law fluids, violated by a swelling crosslinked hydrogel (p.206); the critical-boiling-flux equation (Eq 9.2) is garbled in the source transcription [待验证 — verify against print]; Tg-stick described but not quantified.

## Hygiene Implication 卫材关联

Relevance: ★★★★☆ — moderate: drum drying of aqueous polymer gels, with transferable lessons:

- Pilot-test any polymer gel before scaling: run the mandated pilot-drum performance test (p.205) with steam 2–7 bar, speed 2–30 rpm, nip 0.05–0.5 mm; verify a sheet forms (p.204).
- Exploit vapor-bubble porosity for instant-rehydration flakes: high-flux short-time boiling (≤85 kW/m², p.202) yields a porous, fast-wetting sheet — potentially valuable for absorbent composites.
- Use the vacuum double drum for labile hydrogels (enzyme-SAP, bioactive coatings) — retains structure/functionality (p.208–209).
- Stay below the glass-transition stick point when drying polymer sheets/films (SAP binder, nonwoven coating) on hot surfaces (p.210).
- Polyacrylamide's validated drum-dryability (p.209) is direct evidence the technology suits acrylate-family gels; for pulp/board products see [[Industrial Drying Ch35 Drying of Pulp and Paper]].
