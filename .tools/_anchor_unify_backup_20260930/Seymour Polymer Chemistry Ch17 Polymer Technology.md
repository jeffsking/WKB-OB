# Seymour Polymer Chemistry Ch17 Polymer Technology

> Source: Seymour/Carraher, *Polymer Chemistry*, Ch.17 — Polymer Technology.
> 定位说明 / Citation convention: 原文为 .txt 抽取件，**无正式印张页码**；本报告以原文行号（line no.）与章节号（§17.x）定位。

## Executive Summary 内容概要

Chapter 17 is the **engineering handbook that turns polymer molecules into polymer products**: fibers (§17.1), elastomers (§17.2), films and sheets (§17.3), foams (§17.4), reinforced plastics and laminates (§17.5), molding (§17.6), casting (§17.7), extrusion (§17.8), coatings (§17.9) and adhesives (§17.10).

本章的主论题是 **"加工即分子排列的工程化"**：产品性能不只取决于分子结构，更取决于加工过程中的取向、冷却、交联与复合。对卫材而言，本章几乎是工艺全景图 —— spunbond/meltblown 无纺布落在 §17.1 熔体纺丝，底膜落在 §17.3/§17.8，纸尿裤多层结构落在 §17.5 + §17.9 + §17.10。

## Fibers 纤维 (§17.1)

### Four spinning routes 四种纺丝路线

| Route 路线 | Source wording 原文定义 | Chinese 注解 |
|---|---|---|
| Melt spinning 熔体纺丝 | *"Melt spinning was developed in the 1930s. In melt spinning, the polymer is melted or extruded, clarified by filtration, and pumped through a die having one or more small holes. The die is called a spinneret."* | PP/PE/PET/PA 主流路线 |
| Dry spinning 干纺 | *"In the dry spinning process, the solution is filtered and then forced through a spinneret into a spinning cabinet through which heated air is passed to dry the filament."* | 溶剂由热空气带走 |
| Wet spinning 湿纺 | *"Wet spinning is similar to dry spinning except that fiber formation results from the coagulation of the polymer solution as it is introduced into a nonsolvent bath."* | 凝固浴成纤（viscose/aramid） |
| Gel spinning 凝胶纺丝 | *"Gel spinning is used to produce high strength and modulus fibers. High-molecular-weight (such as 10⁶ Daltons for polyethylene) polymer is dissolved in a high-temperature solvent at low concentration (ca 1%). The hot solution is extruded into a cooling zone..."* | 超高分子量 + 极低浓度 → 高取向高强高模 |

### Fiber fineness 纤维细度

> *"One tex is 1 g per 1000 m or 10⁻⁶ kg/m. The tex has replaced denier as a measure of the density of the fiber."*
> *"One denier is 1 g per 9000 m, so 1 denier = 0.1111 tex."*

### Reaction spinning 反应纺丝 (PU 弹性纤维)

> *"The most important example is the production of selected segmented polyurethane elastomeric fibers. Here, the prepolymer is the soft segment generally a low-molecular-weight polyether or polyester. Reaction with an aromatic diisocyanate converts the end groups, generally hydroxyls, into isocyanate groups. The bath contains a diamine such as 1,2-diaminoethane. The reaction between the amine and isocyanate forms the hard urea linkages."*

— **纺丝与化学反应同时发生**：软段（聚醚/聚酯）给弹性，胺-异氰酸酯生成的脲键硬段给物理交联点。这是氨纶（spandex）类弹性纤维的结构基础。

### Fiber production scale 纤维产量（原文汇总）

Polyester ~400 亿磅/年（熔纺）；Nylon-6/66 > 100 亿磅/年（熔纺）；Acrylic > 70 亿磅/年（干纺）。（数值为原文数据汇总，非逐句引用，引用前建议复核 [待验证]）

## Elastomers 弹性体 (§17.2)

> *"The most widely used elastomer, SBR, is obtained by coagulation of an emulsion of a copolymer of butadiene and styrene. In addition to being produced by the free radical emulsion polymerization technique, elastomers are also produced by cationic copolymerization (butyl rubber) and by anionic polymerization (polybutadiene)."*

- **Mastication 塑炼**：*"Mastication is intended to bring the material to the necessary consistency to accept the compounding ingredients. Mastication results in a lowering of chain size to an average chain length on the order of 10⁴ carbons."*
- **Banbury 密炼机**：*"The Banbury has rotors rotating at different speeds creating a 'kneading' action such as that employed in handling bread dough. A shearing action between the rotors and the walls of the mixer is also achieved."*
- **Vulcanization 硫化（关键定量）**：*"Between 1–5% of sulfur (by weight) is added in typical black rubber mixes giving a vulcanized material with an average of about 500 carbon atoms between crosslinks. Larger amounts of sulfur will give a tougher material eventually giving a somewhat brittle, but quite strong, ebonite as the amount of sulfur is increased to about 40%."*

**解读**：硫用量是交联密度的直接旋钮 —— 1–5% S ≈ 每 500 个碳原子一个交联点；升到 ~40% S 得到硬而强的 ebonite。这个"交联点间距 ↔ 宏观软硬"的定量关系，与 Ch.15 §15.10 凝胶点方程是同一套语言。

## Films and Sheets 薄膜与片材 (§17.3)

- **Cast films 流延膜**：regenerated cellulose via slit die；cellulose acetate from solution。
- **Extrusion films 挤出膜**：flat slit die（平挤）或 air-blowing tubular（吹膜）。
- **Calendering 压延**：counter-rotating rolls，用于 mixing、lamination、coating、embossing。
- **Drawing / orientation 拉伸取向**：PVC 在 **160–180 °C** 可定向，**> 190 °C 无法定向** —— 取向窗口窄，温度过高链松弛快于取向。
- **PVC 板材配方**：plasticizer + pigment + filler + lubricant + stabilizer。

## Polymeric Foams 泡沫 (§17.4)

- **Unicellular（闭孔）**：insulation、buoyancy；**Multicellular（开孔）**：upholstery、carpet backing。
- **EPS**：K 值 ~0.24 Btu；**PU foam**：polyol + diisocyanate 反应发泡。
- 年产量 > 150 万吨，增速 ~10%/年（原文汇总数据，[待验证]）。

## Reinforced Plastics & Laminates 复合材料与层压 (§17.5)

Filament winding（纤维浸预聚体缠绕 mandrel 后固化）、Prepreg（预浸料带）、Pultrusion（连续纤维 + 热固性树脂拉挤）、Laminating（层压板：phenolic / epoxy / polyester，*stacking impregnated sheets between polished metal plates under high pressure and heat*）。

## Molding 模塑 (§17.6)

| Process 工艺 | Source wording 原文 | Note 注解 |
|---|---|---|
| Injection molding 注塑 | *"Injection molding involves forcing (injecting) a molten polymer into a mold where it cools, becoming solid; the mold separates, allowing the molded material to be released."* | 1872 年即已有 camphor-plasticized cellulose nitrate（celluloid）注塑机专利 |
| Compression molding 压缩模塑 | *"The most widely employed molding process is compression molding, where the material is placed in the bottom half of an open, heated mold. The second half of the mold is closed and brings heat and pressure against the material, softening the material further and eventually allowing it to crosslink, if it is a thermoset."* | 热固性主导工艺 |
| Thermoforming 热成型 | *"Thermoforming involves heating a sheet or thick film just above its Tg or Tm, stretching it against a rigid mold, cooling, and trimming the formed part. Inexpensive aluminum, wood, epoxy, and steel molds are often employed."* | 加工温度窗口 = 略高于 Tg 或 Tm |

材料选择（原文 Table 17.9 语境）：PS/HIPS 包装、ABS 耐用件、HDPE 车箱衬里、PET 可微波托盘、PC/PMMA 采光顶与展示件。

## Casting and Extrusion 浇铸与挤出 (§17.7–17.8)

> *"Extruders can be described in terms of their length, l, and screw diameter, d, by the ratio l/d."*
> *"Melt-fed extruders have a l/d ratio of about 8; solid-fed extruders often have l/d's in the range of 20–40. Large extruders can produce 20 tons per h of extruded material."*
> *"Reactive extrusion is the term used to describe the use of an extruder as a continuous reactor such as polymerization or polymer modification."*

**反应挤出**是把挤出机当连续反应器用——聚合或聚合物改性（如接枝）在螺杆内完成，直接呼应 Ch.15 的聚合物反应化学。

## Coatings and Adhesives 涂料与粘合剂 (§17.9–17.10)

| Adhesive class 粘合剂类别 | Source wording 原文 | Chinese 注解 |
|---|---|---|
| Pressure-sensitive 压敏胶 | *"These are actually viscous polymer melts at room temperature. The polymers must be applied at temperatures above their Tg to permit rapid flow."* | 室温下即处于黏流态，Tg 必须低于使用温度 |
| Hot-melt 热熔胶 | *"Thermoplastics often form good adhesives simply by melting, followed by subsequent cooling after the plastic has filled surface voids. Nylons are frequently employed in this manner."* | 熔融—浸润空隙—冷却固化 |
| Reactive 反应型 | *"These additives are either low molecular weight polymers or monomers that solidify by polymerization and/or crosslinking reactions after application."* | 施工后才聚合/交联固化 |

涂料（§17.9）按干燥机理分两类：chemical curing（alkyds、epoxies、PU）与 solvent evaporation；施工方式含 roll、blade、curtain coating；遮盖力（hiding power）取决于颜料与基料的折射率差。

## Hygiene Implication 卫材关联

本章与卫材加工链高度对应，几乎逐节可落地：

1. **无纺布（§17.1）**：spunbond/meltblown 是 melt spinning 的工程化延伸——PP 熔体经过 spinneret 后被高速气流牵伸成细纤维。本章给出熔纺的物理框架（熔融 → 过滤 → 喷丝板 → 牵伸取向），但**未给出 meltblown 纤维直径、气流速度、驻极工艺**等参数，这些需另查 [待验证]。
2. **弹性腰围（§17.2 / §17.1 反应纺丝）**：PU 弹性纤维的软段-硬段结构是氨纶弹性的来源；传统橡胶件则靠硫磺用量调交联密度（1–5% S ≈ 500 C/交联点）。
3. **纸尿裤复合结构（§17.5 + §17.9 + §17.10）**：无纺布+透气膜层压（laminating）、PE 涂布底膜（coating）、热熔胶粘合（hot-melt，"熔融—浸润—冷却"）——三种工艺叠加构成完整芯体结构。
4. **注塑硬制品（§17.6）**：湿巾翻盖、卡扣等采用注塑；热成型窗口须略高于 Tg/Tm。
5. **挤出（§17.8）**：PE 底膜与 PP 异型材用 solid-fed 挤出（l/d 20–40）；PP 接枝改性属反应挤出范畴。
