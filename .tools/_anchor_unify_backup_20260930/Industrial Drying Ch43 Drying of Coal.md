# Chapter 43 — Drying of Coal

> Source: Pikoń & Mujumdar, in Mujumdar (ed.), *Handbook of Industrial Drying*, 4th ed., CRC Press, 2014, Ch.43, pp.991–1014 [页码范围待验证；正文引句页码 p.992–1009 均已核对].

## 1. Overview 概述

Chapter 43 is a large equipment catalogue of coal-drying technology: rotary, rotary-tube, chamber-with-stirrers, pneumatic, fluid-bed, spouted-bed, vibratory, mill-type, shaft, moving-bed, and superheated-steam dryers, plus low-rank-coal, hot-oil, combined grinding-drying, Fleissner and mechanical-thermal-expression variants. Two themes carry cross-industry value: (1) **safety** — explosion panels, cocurrent operation to avoid ignition, low-oxygen/inert (superheated-steam) drying against fire/explosion and spontaneous combustion; (2) **bulk-solids drying economics** — heat consumption per kg of water evaporated. 主题可迁移：防爆安全逻辑 + 能耗统一基准；基质本身（煤）不可迁移。

Relevance to hygiene materials: ★★☆☆☆ low — 煤与 fluff/SAP 无基质对应，但任何可燃细粉（绒毛浆、SAP 细粉、 airlaid 浆料）干燥/气力输送线都适用其安全与能耗框架。

## 2. Key Claims 关键论点（原句摘录）

- "The evaporation of surface moisture takes place in the first drying period at a constant drying rate. … Hygroscopic moisture depends on the rank of coal … The evaporation of hygroscopic moisture takes place in the second drying period at a falling drying rate." (p.992)
- "Generally these dryers operate in the cocurrent mode to avoid the possibility of ignition. The drying medium is hot air or combustion gases." (p.992)
- "The system is equipped with explosion flaps (17)." (p.999)
- "It is important not to have high oxygen content in the drying gas to avoid explosion and fire hazard. This is a key issue in coal drying. Low-rank coals can be highly reactive and hence they are more susceptible to fire and explosion hazard due to spontaneous combustion. Hence indirect dryers have some advantages in this regard." (p.1009)
- Superheated steam: "Among those of major interest in coal drying are no fire or explosion hazard, ability to dry at elevated temperatures, faster drying rates leading to small equipment size … Low-temperature (150 to 200 °C) … greatly reduces the danger of ignition." (p.1007)
- Energy metric: "The heat consumption in such dryers amounts to about 3700 kJ/kg H₂O." (p.993, rotary); "The heat consumption is 2800 to 3500 kJ/kg H₂O." (p.1007, superheated steam)
- Drying rate per unit volume (p.993/1007): rotary ≈35–120 kg/m³h; pneumatic 400–900; superheated steam ≈0.2 kg/m³·s. Industrial fluid-bed porosity 0.55–0.75 (p.1000).

## 3. Concept Deep-Dive 概念深挖

### 3.1 Inert / low-O₂ drying as explosion defence 惰性低氧干燥作为防爆手段
Two distinct safety arguments: (a) avoid ignition by design — cocurrent rotary dryers (p.992), explosion flaps on pneumatics (p.999); (b) remove the hazard by removing oxygen — low-O₂/inert gas and, most decisively, superheated steam whose medium is non-oxidising (p.1007). Universal combustible-dust rule: remove the oxidant, not just the ignition source. 通用防爆法则：除氧化剂，而非仅除点火源。

### 3.2 Energy benchmark on kJ/kg H₂O 能耗统一基准
Every dryer type is benchmarked by heat consumption per kg water evaporated. For hygiene, the lesson is to compare drying routes on energy per kg water removed, not per kg product — same denominator as the pulp chapter. `[待验证：kJ/kg-H₂O 数字为煤系工厂经验值，移植到纤维素/丙烯酸体系需重新推导]`

### 3.3 Where transfer stops 迁移边界
Coal-rank physics, spontaneous-combustion kinetics, coking/briquetting MC targets have no cellulose/SAP analogue; fluidization correlations are coal-calibrated. 煤阶相关物理量不可迁移，通用相关性公式常数不可直接套用。

## 4. Hygiene Implication 卫材关联

- **H43-1 防爆泄压片+惰性化**: for fluff-defibration, SAP-fines, or airlaid pneumatic conveyors, require explosion-relief panels and a documented oxygen-control/inerting policy on the supplier's line — 绒毛/SAP 细粉可燃，防爆是真实的合规与安全需求。
- **H43-2 自热可能时选间接/低氧干燥**: where a hygiene powder is finely divided and heat-sensitive, ask whether an indirect or inerted dryer was chosen over a direct-fired one, and why.
- **H43-3 能耗以 kJ/kg-H₂O 报价**: when a supplier proposes a new dryer, require the energy quoted per kg water removed for apples-to-apples comparison.
- **H43-4 直燃并流的点火风险核查**: in audits of direct-fired hygiene ovens, check the firing/direction arrangement and ignition-risk mitigation for the combustible web.
- Honest assessment: substrate relevance is genuinely low; value is the generic safety and energy-economics frames only. 基质相关性确实低，价值仅在通用安全与能耗框架。
