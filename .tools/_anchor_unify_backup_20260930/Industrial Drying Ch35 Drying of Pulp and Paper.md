# Ch35: Drying of Pulp and Paper

> **Source:** Polat, G. & Mujumdar, A.S., in Mujumdar (ed.), *Handbook of Industrial Drying*, 4th ed., CRC Press, 2014. Ch.35, pp.791–817.

---

## 1. THE Procurement Keystone 采购关键句

### Flash Drying & Hornification 闪蒸干燥与角质化

> "The **lower moisture bound** is critical, as **overheating below it can cause thermal cross-linking that makes reconstitution of the original fiber difficult**. Pulp temperature is maintained **below 70°C** in both stages, with air inlet temperatures of **400 and 170°C**, respectively." (p.815)

**This is THE most actionable sentence in the Handbook for fluff-pulp qualification.** Over-drying market pulp in the final stage welds fibres via thermal cross-linking (hornification), raising defibration energy and hurting pad integrity. Suppliers control pulp temperature °C precisely to avoid this.

### Micro-Reading 微解读

1. Claims the lower moisture bound is critical and names the failure mode — thermal cross-linking → poor reconstitution
2. Operational rule: pulp temperature held below 70°C across both stages despite 400/170°C inlet air
3. Energy integration — second-stage exhaust preheats first-stage inlet
4. Staging — bulk water off first (~30% MC), delicate finish at low T
5. Finishing — cool before baling to lock in quality

## 2. Paper Drying Physics 纸张干燥物理

### Cylinder Drying Is Conduction-Limited 烘缸干燥是导热限制

> "The multicylinder dryer section of a paper machine consists of a number (up to 70) of large, hollow, cast iron or steel cylinders... The major mode of heat transfer is the **conduction through the steam-heated cylinder shells**." (p.794)

- **85–90%** of paper dryers are cylinders (Table 35.1)
- Heat must cross: steam film → cast-iron shell → paper → air film
- **H-values:** Newsprint 285–315 W/m²K; Fine paper 255–285; Kraft sack 230–255; Linerboard 170–230 (Table 35.3)
- Machine speed ceiling: "very difficult to operate... at speeds much higher than **1000 m/min**" (p.800)

### Pocket Ventilation 气隙通风

> "Pocket ventilation systems can increase the drying efficiency up to **20%** depending upon the previous conditions." (p.798)

- 5.5°C steam-temperature drop on dryer-limited linerboard costs ~8% production (p.796)
- Condensate rimming and noncondensable gases strangle capacity

### Drying Strategy Sets Strength–Bulk–Swellability 干燥策略决定强度-蓬松-溶胀

| Drying Mode | Result |
|---|---|
| **Restrained** (felt/draw) | High tensile, low stretch, compact |
| **Tension-free** (floater/through-air) | Higher bulk, stretch, TEA, lower modulus |
| **Through-air tissue** | Soft, high-bulk products; exceeds Yankee cylinder rates |

## 3. Thermomechanical Consolidation 热机械固化

> "Press, impulse, and Condebelt drying techniques... uses mechanical, thermal, and interactive effects... **sometimes called thermomechanical web consolidation processes**." (p.810)

| Technology | Energy (kJ/kg H₂O) | Rate | Conditions |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Conventional press** | 2,500–3,500 | — | — |
| **Press drying** | 550–1,400 | ~10× at 177°C, ~20× at 288°C | — |
| **Impulse** | 550–1,400 | **500–8,000 kg/m²h** | 150–500°C, 1–5 MPa |
| **Condebelt** | 550–1,400 | — | — |

**Fluff implication:** High-temperature drying history means collapsed pores and lost swellability.

### Lignin Tg Bonding Threshold 木质素Tg粘结阈值

> At 150°C surface temperature, "both the tensile index and the modulus of elasticity remain at high levels in the range of 39 to 70% initial solids content, but **decreases sharply at higher solids content**" (p.810)

## 4. Pulp Drying 木浆干燥

### Conventional 常规
- Cylinder/air-floater drying
- Market pulp: dried to ~10% moisture (90% fiber, 10% water) (p.814)

### Flash Drying 闪蒸干燥
- Two-stage: bulk water off at 400°C air, delicate finish at 170°C air
- Pulp temperature maintained below 70°C (the hornification clause)
- Exhaust from stage 2 preheats stage 1 inlet

### Steam Drying 蒸汽干燥
- **30% cost advantage** vs conventional (p.815)
- Higher throughput at lower energy
