## Key Findings 关键发现

**Fibre generations 纤维三代演进**

- **First generation**: synthesised fibres (polyamide) — historic cost multiple over cotton and wool, offset by tunable performance.
- **Second generation**: modified conventional synthetic fibres with greatly increased performance — aramid, carbon, glass, ceramic. Tradenames named: Nomex, Kevlar, Twaron, Technora (aromatic polyamides); Spectra and Dyneema (high-performance polyamides/polyethylene); Vectran (aromatic polyester); Teflon (PTFE), Dacron and Mylar (polyester).
- **Third generation**: smart and intelligent fibres, which **replace static function with dynamic function**.
- End use dictates not only fibre type but also twisting method, textile structure selection and finishing stage; materials may be **woven, knitted, nonwoven (netkane) or composite structures**.

**Definition and classification 定义与分类**

- No settled definition of smart textiles exists. Some sources treat it as a single grouping; others split it into **smart** vs **intelligent**, where both sense stimuli but *intelligent* additionally responds with a **pre-programmed reaction**.
- Core architecture: **sensor + activator (starter) + control unit**, executing five functions — receiving the environmental stimulus, capturing parameters, data processing, initiating response, storing data/energy, and communication/response output. In its simplest form smart textile senses and reacts automatically without a control unit; in more complex form it acts through a processing unit.
- **Three response levels**: passive (can sense stimuli) → active (can sense and react) → ultra-smart (can sense, react **and adapt**).
- **Nine-category protective-clothing taxonomy**: ballistic/mechanical; extreme heat and/or fire; extreme cold; radiation; UV radiation; harmful chemicals; harmful particles; bacterial/viral; clean-room protection of sensitive production items.
- Classification by application area covers (1) sensors/activators — photo-sensitive materials, optical fibres, conductive polymers, heat-sensitive materials, shape-memory materials, chemically responsive polymers, intelligent coatings/membranes, mechanically responsive materials, **microcapsules**, micro- and nano-materials; (2) signal transfer, processing and control — neural networks and control systems, cognition theory and systems; (3) integrated processes and products — wearable electronics and photonics, adaptive/fast-response structures, biomimetic structures, bioprocesses, tissue engineering, chemical/drug release.

**Thermal and mechanical actuation 热/力学响应**

- **PCM (Phase Change Materials) knits**: microcapsules accumulate and retain heat at higher temperature and subsequently release it at lower temperature, giving thermo-regulatory performance.
- **Shape memory polymer (SMP)**: characterised by a large reversible elasticity change at the glass transition temperature. The flagship example is **Diaplex** (Japan), which maintains body temperature by redistributing its particles: at low temperature the particles arrange into a solid plate forming an insulating layer; rising heat increases kinetic energy, particles regroup and open channels to release excess heat and sweat. Claimed advantages over polyamide: **water resistance, breathability, anti-condensation**, and — because there are no pores as such — avoidance of pore clogging by salts from sweat or ice, plus UV stability and machine washability.

**Biomimetics 仿生**

- **Lotus effect**: micro- and nano-scale combination (tiny papillae covered with hair-like wax crystals) greatly reduces contact between water molecules and the surface. Self-cleaning technology was named by Time magazine (2006) as one of the best investments. Butterfly- and dragonfly-wing-inspired surfaces are described as naturally hydrophobic and rough, effectively shedding dirt during rain.
- **Velcro**: modelled on the burdock plant — many tiny hooks forming a hook-and-loop construction; a velvet/crochet combination used as an alternative to zips.
- **Structural colour (most transferable idea)**: butterfly wings carry only yellow, orange, red, brown and black pigments; blue, green and violet come from **nanoparticle layers separated by air layers** — mosaically fitted platelets with different characteristics and slits for light reflection, colour depending on **layer thickness**. Teijin's **Morphotex** applies this as a monofilament fibre, optically coloured by interference to produce colour **without pigment or dye**, built from **61 alternating PA and PES layers at 70 nm each**, laminated; four base colours (red, green, blue, violet) developed by precisely controlled layer thickness matched to visible light.
- **Shark-skin mimicry**: Speedo "Racer" scale structures reduce friction and increase swim speed; Nike "Macro React" (fish-scale inspired) opens to reduce moisture and heat.
- **Camouflage fabric** identified as probably the first biomimetic material — English troops in India, 1850.

**Electronics integration and processing 电子集成与工艺**

- Embedded electronics monitor heart, lungs and muscles; data stored locally or transferred wirelessly. Cases cited: Australian **CSIRO** basketball shooting sleeve using rhythmic sound waves; cycling garment with Bluetooth-driven grouped LEDs on the back showing direction, powered by batteries or small solar panels.
- Two insertion points in the manufacturing chain matter: **cutting** (separating plies destined for printing of smart components) and **sewing** (adding electronic components; trend toward seamless construction achieved by printing). **Finishing** fixes thermoplastic elements by heat press or ironing.

## Relevance to Hygiene R&D 与卫材研发的关联

**Rating 评级: LOW–MEDIUM 偏低至中. Four isolated mechanisms overlap; nothing about the product class does.**

Where it actually connects 真正有连接的地方:

- **Microcapsule PCM thermoregulation is the same mechanism we are trying to replace.** The described heat-storage/release cycle of PCM microcapsules is precisely the functionality targeted by our **PCM-free** BNC thermoregulation route. Useful as the incumbent baseline description, not as a solution.
- **Structural colour without pigment (Morphotex) is directly adjacent to our chromic programme.** Colour from periodic layer thickness rather than from dye molecules is the same physics behind our structurally coloured films; the concrete numbers here (61 alternating PA/PES layers, 70 nm each) are the only quantitative colour-structure data in the file and are worth keeping as a benchmark for what an interference-based colour stack looks like in fibre form.
- **Lotus-effect wetting control** — micro/nano roughness plus low surface energy reducing water contact — is the mirror image of the hydrophilisation problem we work on at the topsheet. Reading it as the water-repellent end of the same wetting spectrum is legitimate.
- **Nonwoven listed alongside woven, knitted and composite structures** as a legitimate substrate for special-purpose clothing, which is consistent with (though not informative about) our own material class.
- **Stage-aware insertion of functional components** (print at cutting, add electronics at sewing, heat-fix at finishing) is a useful process-design analogue for where a printed chromic indicator could sit in a converting line.
- **Bacterial/viral protection appears in the taxonomy** but is named only as a category — no mechanism or data.

Where it does not connect 不连接的地方:

- **No absorbency content at all** — nothing on fluff pulp, SAP, core structure, acquisition, strike-through or rewet. The paper's performance axes are protection, thermoregulation, motion and electronics.
- **No polyolefin, bicomponent or ES fibre content**; the named fibres (aramid, UHMWPE, polyester, PA) are outside our material set, and their function is entirely permanent-garment rather than disposable.
- **Evidence quality is weak**: it is a Serbian-language professional review whose citations [5]–[21] are largely web URLs accessed in June 2022, including presentation slideshows and image sites. Performance claims about Diaplex, PCM knits and shark-skin swimwear are asserted without test data. **Do not cite this source for quantitative performance figures.**
- **Recommendation 建议**: use as background only. If structural-colour or PCM-alternative work advances, cite primary sources; this page records where those ideas sit in the textile literature.
