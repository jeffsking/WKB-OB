# Molecular and Supramolecular Systems in Photochromism: Dihydroindolizines

## One-line Position 一句话定位
A 1995 review establishing dihydroindolizines as an easily synthesised, chemically tunable photochromic platform — and, by citing commercial spiroxazine lenses, the clearest template in this vault for how a stimuli-responsive dye reaches market inside a stable polymer matrix.

## Core Mechanism 核心机理

- **商业范式已存在** — "an important impetus was brought to the field … by the optimization of known systems such as spiroxazines … being commercially used in ophthalmic lenses" (lines 30-31)
- **DHI 的卖点** — "a new class of photochromic molecules, e.g. dihydroindolizines (DHI) … can be prepared easily" (lines 59-60)
- **完整光化学回路** — "The photochemically induced ring opening leads to a betaine, which reacts via the E- to the Z-isomer. Ring closure or 1,5-electrocyclization occurs thermally in a concerted fashion." (lines 197-200)
- **结构-反应性调控** — 分子分为 A/B/C 三区；region B 上的电负性取代基"stabilize the betaine-form (large t₁/₂)" (lines 170-177)
- **光控开关** — "These molecules can be used as photochromic switches that can be geared by light." (lines 415-416)
- **商业化工艺条件** — "the spiroxazines … have assumed commercial significance in ophthalmic lenses. This is mainly due to their high photostability, especially in a transparent plastic matrix." (lines 643-645)
- **对立项质疑的正面回应** — "Photochromic systems have long been criticized as being of no commercial value. Several applications and potential applications have been demonstrated nevertheless." (lines 617-618)

## Business Connection Points 业务连接点

- **UV 显影防伪包装（最直接可迁移）** — 螺噁嗪已在透明塑料基体中商业化，是纸尿裤/卫生巾纸箱与柔性包装上做 UV 显影图形、隐藏信息、品牌防伪的直接、已验证模板。
- **光驱动可复位湿敏/饱和度指示** — DHI 是可由光调谐的可逆开关；湿敏指示目前依赖 pH/染料一次性体系，光致变色叠层可作可复位的视觉线索，与一次性 pH 化学互补。
- **储运光暴露计时标签** — 彩态寿命可调（2b 达 213 min），可为光敏原料（SAP、胶黏剂）料桶设计"时编码"UV 暴露标签。
- **体液离子选择性光学指示** — 冠醚 DHI 提供"结合可调的反应性"与色离子载体分子识别，是嵌入面层或试纸条的离子选择性（如尿液 Na⁺/NH₄⁺）光学指示路线。
- **生物相容的工艺结论** — 关键制造准则是"把光致变色体分散在稳定聚合物膜中（ionomer/TPU 与无纺布层压），而非裸露在 SAP 芯层中"，因为湿化学环境会主导其失效。
- **放置约束** — DHI/螺噁嗪为有机光致变色体，需 UV 激发且有有限疲劳寿命；适合包装、标签与非接触外层指示，不适合置于吸水 SAP 芯层内。
