# Chapter 9 Heat-setting of Thermoplastic Fibres 热塑性纤维的热定型

> Source: Manufactured Fibre Technology, Ch.09, pp.188–202.
> Author of chapter: A.K. Sengupta. 本章作者：A.K. Sengupta。

## Summary 摘要

Drawn but unset filaments do not possess **dimensional stability 尺寸稳定性**: they tend to shrink, crease and wrinkle. This chapter explains how hot dry air, steam or hot water — "by the action of heat in the absence or presence of swelling agents, with or without tension" (p.188) — give the fibre its **set 定形**. It classifies set into three classes, identifies **partial melting and recrystallization 部分熔融与重结晶** as the dominant mechanism of permanent set, analyses the two-stage nature of PET shrinkage and the role of tension, and closes with two measurable definitions of degree of set: **critical dissolution time (CDT 临界溶解时间)** and the **bending-recovery method 弯曲回复法**.

## Set Types and Mechanism 定形的类型与机理

| Set type 定形类型 | Basis 机理基础 | Reversibility 可逆性 |
|---|---|---|
| Temporary 临时 | Weak van der Waals forces / hydrogen bonds | 低能量即可逆转 |
| Semipermanent 半永久 | Higher energy barrier | 需更高能量逆转 |
| Permanent 永久 | Structural change via partial melting and recrystallization | 不破坏结构则不可完全逆转 |

Source wording (p.190): "Set is categorized into three types, namely temporary, semipermanent and permanent [3]."

Mechanism of permanent set (p.190–191): "The most important mechanism of permanent setting in crystalline polymers is through partial melting and recrystallization, whereby partial melting of small, imperfect crystals takes place below the melting point to allow the growth of larger more perfect and regular crystalline units."

Why it cannot be undone (p.191): "the mechanism of heat-setting is to lead the structure to a position of lower minimum energy from the state prior to heat-setting." Hearle's crystal-size vs. melting-frequency diagram is cited with the plain statement that "small or imperfect crystals will melt and larger crystals will grow. This is a typical process of annealing" (p.192).

## Shrinkage is a Two-Stage Process 收缩是两步过程

Source wording (p.196): "shrinkage in PET is a two-stage process involving a rapid initial stage in which shrinkage is associated with disorientation in the amorphous zones, followed by a crystalline stage during which chain folding... occurs." Chain folding is evidenced by a specific IR absorption peak after high-temperature annealing (Koenig & Hannon; Wilson) (pp.195–196). 因此收缩与伸长之间没有唯一的一一对应关系 — no unique shrinkage/elongation correspondence exists.

Evidence base: Dismore & Statton annealed **nylon 66 in the relaxed state at 140–260 °C** and measured tenacity, shrinkage and breaking elongation (pp.193–195).

## Tension versus Free Annealing 张力退火 vs 自由退火

Source wording (p.196): "while free annealing reduces molecular orientation and tenacity, it increases diameter and denier of the fibre. With tension annealing, there is generally an increase in tenacity but no significant change in diameter or denier." The explanation offered is the **crystal dispersion model 结晶分散模型** (Valk et al., pp.196–197): two limiting configurations — "low temperature / high tension" versus "high temperature / low tension" — shift the distribution of crystal size and of physical crosslink density, thereby moving **Tg** and tenacity.

- **Thermal healing 热愈合** (p.198): "thermal healing or sealing of fissures, surface cracks... caused by the stressing operations" — 加工应力造成的裂隙与表面裂纹在热作用下愈合。
- **Dry versus wet heat 干热与湿热**: Warwicker's density comparison supports stronger set development in nylon under wet heat (pp.198–199).
- **Cellulose triacetate 三醋酸纤维素** is treated as a separate case (Hindeleh; Sprague; **tautness distribution 松紧分布** at p.199) (pp.198–202).

## Measuring Degree of Set 定形度的测量

- **CDT 临界溶解时间** (p.200): "estimates the time needed to dissolve a filament in a solvent of known concentration at a given temperature." Galil reports a correlation coefficient of **0.98** between density and log CDT, allowing crystallinity to be assessed indirectly (pp.200–201).
- **Bending-recovery method 弯曲回复法** (Prevorsek et al., p.201): filament wound on a mandrel, set under tension for **10 min**, then quantified as **S = (N2/N1)[1 - (L2/L1)^(1/2)] × 100**.
- Settability ranking (p.201): "polyester demonstrates better settability than nylon... Whereas the set in PET is more or less permanent with time, nylon has a tendency to gradually lose its set over the time span of the experiment." The 2000 min tracking by Prevorsek is the cited evidence. 由低结晶态重新结晶获得的定形，比"部分熔融再结晶"更持久。

Strength assessment from the source itself: the melting–recrystallization mechanism is strongly supported by multiple independent experiments; the tension effect on Tg/tenacity relies mostly on one group (Valk) and is self-consistent but single-sourced; the triacetate section rests on only two early studies and is rated medium-to-weak.

## Limitations 局限（源自原书内容）

- 偏重尼龙与 PET；聚丙烯、腈纶等仅一笔带过（p.190 提及但无系统数据）— polypropylene and acrylic are only mentioned in passing.
- "定形持久性"主要由 Prevorsek 单一团队数据支撑，跨纤维普适性偏弱。

## Hygiene implication 卫生材料启示

**本章相关性高，是这批章节里对卫生材料最直接有用的一章。** 热风粘合（through-air bonding）本质上就是在受限条件下对 ES/PE-PP 皮芯纤维做一次 set：

1. **Free vs tension annealing 直接对应产线张力窗口**：自由 annealing 降取向降强力、增直径增旦数；张力 annealing 提强力而旦数不变。热风烘箱里网带是约束还是松弛状态，决定了成品布的 CD/MD 强力比与手感 — this is the same variable discussed in [[Air-Through Nonwoven Supplier Audit（热风无纺布供应商考察纪要·修订版 2026-08-21）]] and [[Pressure vs Thermal vs Ultrasonic Bonding（压力·热·超声波粘合对比）]]。
2. **永久定形机理解释了为什么热粘合后的布不再回缩**：皮层 PP/PE 的小不完善晶体在低于熔点处部分熔融并重结晶为更完善晶体，结构落到更低能量极小态。这是热风穿透温度必须略高于皮层熔点、又不能破坏芯层 PET/PP 取向的原因。参见 [[ES 双组分弹性纤维热风布（弹性体改性）]]。
3. **两步收缩模型对尺寸公差有用**：非晶区解取向是快速的第一步，如果把商品布存放/再受热控制在第一步之后的结晶阶段之前，尺寸漂移是可预测的。
4. **Set 随时间流失的结论要谨慎外推**：本书仅针对 nylon vs PET 长丝（nylon 会随时间逐渐失去定形），对 PP/PE 体系无数据 — [待验证] whether the polyolefin sheath shows an analogous slow set loss.
5. 与上一章连读：牵伸建立取向（第8章），热定型决定这份取向能被保留到什么程度。见 [[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch08 Drawing of Melt-Spun Fibres]]。
6. 弹性材料（氨纶/弹性体）受热后的张力衰减与 creep，可比照本章"松弛态退火"理解：参见 [[Elastic Tension & Creep（橡筋张力与回缩率）]]。

对绒毛浆、SAP 与吸收芯体本身，本章无直接适用内容 — no bearing on fluff pulp or SAP absorbency.
