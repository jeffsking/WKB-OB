# Ebewole Polymer Science Ch02 Polymerization Mechanisms

> Source: Ebewole, *Polymer Science and Technology*, Ch.2 (Polymerization Mechanisms). 章节主题：按机理的聚合分类、链式聚合四步基元过程、共轭二烯立体化学。

Chapter 2 is the **mechanism hub 机理枢纽** of the book. It replaces classification by *product structure* with classification by *polymerisation mechanism*, and then dissects chain-reaction polymerisation into four elementary steps.

本章论证：聚合物的宏观性质根植于聚合机理；链式聚合的四步动力学图像（initiation–propagation–termination–chain transfer）是理解分子量与分子量分布控制的入口。

## Classification by Mechanism 按机理分类

> "A more complete but still oversimplified scheme that is still based on the different polymerization processes places polymers into three classes: condensation, addition, and ring-opening polymers." (Ch.2, p.26)

> "Probably the most general classification scheme is based on the polymerization mechanism involved in polymer synthesis. Under this scheme, polymerization processes are classified as step-reaction (condensation) or chain-reaction (addition) polymerization." (Ch.2, p.26)

> "Chain-reaction polymerization … involves the addition of unsaturated molecules to a rapidly growing chain." (Ch.2, p.26)

The source states the rationale explicitly: the Carothers condensation/addition dichotomy is judged incomplete because some addition polymers also eliminate small molecules, hence **mechanism-based 机理优先** classification is adopted as "the most general".

| Item 对比项 | Chain-reaction 链式（加聚） | Step-reaction 逐步（缩聚） |
|---|---|---|
| Representative mechanism 代表机理 | free-radical, ionic, coordination 自由基/离子/配位 | condensation 缩聚 |
| Active centre 活性中心 | chain-end radical / ion 链末端自由基或离子 | functional group on any chain segment 任意链段官能团 |
| Molecular weight vs. conversion 分子量随转化率 | high molar mass reached early; weak dependence on conversion 初期即得高分子量 | grows progressively with conversion (DP = 1/(1−p)) 随转化率逐步增大 |
| Termination 终止 | requires a termination step 需终止步骤 | no termination step 无终止步骤 |
| Functionality requirement 官能度要求 | monomer generally bifunctional 单体一般双官能 | functionality ≥ 2 官能度 ≥2 |

## Chain-Reaction Polymerisation: Four Steps 链式聚合四步

> "Like all chain reactions, it involves three fundamental steps: initiation, propagation, and termination. In addition, a fourth step called chain transfer may be involved." (Ch.2, p.26)

### A. Initiation 引发

> "Initiation involves the acquisition of an active site by the monomer." (Ch.2, p.26)

> "An initiator is usually a weak organic compound that can be decomposed thermally or by irradiation to produce free radicals, which are molecules containing atoms with unpaired electrons." (Ch.2, p.26)

> "while initiators trigger initiation of the chain and exert an accelerating influence on polymerization rate, they are not exactly catalysts since they are changed chemically in the course of polymerization." (Ch.2, p.26)

> "Benzoyl peroxide, azobisisobutyronitrile, and di-t-butylperoxide are commonly used free-radical initiators, as illustrated in Equations 2.2–2.4." (Ch.2, p.26)

Initiation proceeds in **two successive steps 引发分两步** (Ch.2, p.27): (1) radical generation from the initiator; (2) addition of the radical to the monomer. Routes to radical generation include thermal decomposition, photochemical (ultraviolet) decomposition, high-energy irradiation, and **redox initiation 氧化还原引发**:

> "the decomposition of peroxide or persulfate is greatly accelerated by the presence of a reducing system. This method of free-radical initiation is referred to as redox initiation." (Ch.2, p.26)

> "Persulfate ion initiator (e.g., from K₂S₂O₈) reacts with a reducing agent such as a bisulfite ion (e.g., from NaHSO₃) to produce radicals for redox initiation." (Ch.2, p.26)

> "The enhanced rate of free-radical formation in redox reactions permits polymerization at relatively lower temperatures." (Ch.2, p.26)

Note also: "Free radicals are inactivated by reaction with oxygen to form peroxides or hydroperoxides." (Ch.2, p.27) — 氧对自由基的失活作用是自由基聚合需除氧的原因。

| Initiator type 引发剂类型 | Example 代表 | Typical monomers 代表单体 | Stereo control 立体控制 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Peroxide / azo | benzoyl peroxide, AIBN | styrene, MMA, VAc, VC | low 低 |
| Anionic (organolithium / alkali metal) | n-butyllithium | styrene, butadiene, isoprene | medium 中 |
| Lewis acid | [未找到原句] | isobutene | low 低 |
| Coordination | Ziegler–Natta | propylene, butene-1 | high 高 |

### B. Propagation 增长

> "During propagation, the initiated monomer … adds other monomers — usually thousands of monomer molecules — in rapid succession. This involves the addition of a free radical to the double bond of a monomer, with regeneration of another radical. The active center is thus continuously relocated at the end of the growing polymer chain." (Ch.2, p.27)

Regiochemistry 区域化学: "The substituted carbon atom is regarded as the head and the unsubstituted carbon atom the tail of the vinyl monomer." (Ch.2, p.27)

> "head-to-tail linkages in which the substituents occur on alternate carbon atoms predominate; only occasional interruptions of this arrangement by head-to-head and tail-to-tail linkages occur." (Ch.2, p.28)

### C. Termination 终止

> "In termination, the growth activity of a polymer chain radical is destroyed by reaction with another free radical in the system to produce polymer molecule(s)." (Ch.2, p.29)

Two modes: **combination (coupling) 偶合终止** — "two growing polymer chains react with the mutual destruction of growth activity" and "Coupling reactions produce a single polymer" (Ch.2, p.29); **disproportionation 歧化终止** — "a labile atom (usually hydrogen) is transferred from one polymer radical to another", producing "two polymers from the two reacting polymer chain radicals" (Ch.2, p.29).

> "The predominant termination reaction depends on the nature of the reacting monomer and the temperature. Since disproportionation requires energy for breaking of chemical bonds, it should become more pronounced at high reaction temperatures; combination of growing polymer radicals predominates at low temperatures." (Ch.2, p.29)

Termination by reaction with initiator radicals is described as **"unproductive" 无效终止** (Ch.2, p.29).

### D. Chain Transfer 链转移

> "Transfer reactions do not result in the creation or destruction of radicals; at any instant, the overall number of growing radicals remains unchanged. However, the occurrence of transfer reactions results in the reduction of the average polymer chain length." (Ch.2, p.29)

> "a growing polymer chain is deactivated or terminated by transferring its growth activity to a previously inactive species" (Ch.2, p.29)

Transfer targets 转移对象: monomer / polymer / solvent / initiator / impurity. If the new radical A· is unreactive toward monomer, "the entire reaction could be inhibited" (Ch.2, p.29) — 即链转移剂与阻聚剂的界限取决于新自由基活性。

### E. Diene Polymerisation 二烯聚合

> "The 1,4 unit may occur in the cis or trans configuration. A diene polymer contains more than one of these structural units." (Ch.2, p.30)

> "Anionic diene polymerization with lithium or organolithium initiators like n-butyllithium in nonpolar solvents such as pentane or hexane yields polymers with high cis-1,4 content." (Ch.2, p.30)

> "Stereoregularity can also be controlled by the use of coordination catalysts like Ziegler–Natta catalysts." (Ch.2, p.30)

Microstructural outcomes reported in the source: free-radical at low temperature → predominantly **trans-1,4**, with 1,2 content around 20% (1,2 ~20%); raising temperature increases the **cis-1,4** fraction; anionic/nonpolar solvent → high cis-1,4; Ziegler–Natta → stereoregularity controllable; Alfin catalyst → high trans-1,4. 微观结构由引发体系 × 温度 × 溶剂三维决定。

Cross-chapter note: "In 1,4 addition, however, the unsaturation is part of the backbone. Two configurations are possible — trans or cis." (Ch.7, p.184); "Initiators are consumed in the reaction while catalysts are regenerated after the reaction." (Ch.7, p.182).

## Academic Context and Critique 学术对话与批判

- **Carothers dichotomy (1920s)** distinguished condensation vs. addition by whether a small molecule is eliminated; the source judges this scheme as not permitting complete differentiation.
- **IUPAC mechanism classification (from the 1950s)** formally established step vs. chain; Ebewole adopts it.
- **Free-radical kinetics (Bamford, Stewart, Mayo, 1940s–50s)** — the chain-transfer concept (Mayo equation) comes from this lineage.
- **Anionic diene polymerisation (Szwarc, 1956)** — "living" polymerisation made high cis-1,4 routine; by the 1997 date of writing the source treats it as mature technology.
- **Terminological tension initiator ≠ catalyst**: repeatedly clarified in Ch.2 and Ch.7 ("Initiators are consumed in the reaction while catalysts are regenerated after the reaction.", Ch.7 p.182). 工业催化文献中催化剂亦可能部分消耗，语义边界非绝对。
- **Unexpanded simplifications 未展开之处**: the source notes occasional head-to-head / tail-to-tail interruptions but does not discuss how initiator or monomer structure actively induces them; the combination-vs-disproportionation temperature rule is stated qualitatively without activation-energy numbers.

## Hygiene Implication 卫材关联

1. **SAP synthesis route 超吸水性树脂合成路径.** 聚丙烯酸钠类 SAP 的水相/反相悬浮与乳液聚合普遍采用 **redox initiation**（过硫酸盐 + 亚硫酸氢盐），因为 redox "permits polymerization at relatively lower temperatures" (Ch.2, p.26)；低温引发有利于控制交联网络形成与残余单体。参见 [[A review on superabsorbent polymers]]、[[Hydrogel-based Superabsorbent Polymers]]。
2. **Chain transfer as the Mw/Mn control knob 链长调控手段.** "Chain-transfer reactions reduce the average chain length of the polymer." (Ch.2, p.31, Example 2.1b) — 加入链转移剂可降低分子量、改变分布宽度；对聚烯烃树脂而言这直接影响熔体流动与可纺性。参见 [[Polypropylene Handbook Spunbond and Meltblown Nonwoven Resins]]。
3. **Why PP cannot be made by bulk free-radical polymerisation.** 等规 PP 的立体规整度必须由 **coordination catalysts (Ziegler–Natta)** 控制（Ch.2, p.30）；自由基路线无法控制立体规整，这解释了聚烯烃树脂的工业合成路线选择。
