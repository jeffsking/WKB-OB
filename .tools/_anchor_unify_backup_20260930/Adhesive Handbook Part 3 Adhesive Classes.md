# Handbook of Adhesive Technology — Part 3: Adhesive Classes (Ch. 20-45)

## One-line Position 一句话定位
The catalogue that names the hygiene-product adhesive stack explicitly — acrylic PSA dispersions and hot melts as the dominant technologies for disposable-diaper self-adhesive strips, with skin irritation as the governing constraint.

## Evidence 证据

- **⚠️ 卫材行业直接命中** — "Personal hygiene products such as disposable diapers make use of self-adhesive strips, covered with a release tape that is removed at the point of use. Again unmodified acrylic dispersions as well as hot melts dominate this application. The same considerations apply here as in first-aid dressings, and in particular the adhesive must not cause skin irritation." [Line 16549-16553]
- **初粘力的第一设计规则** — "A fundamental requirement for tack is a glass transition temperature substantially below the application temperature to permit the necessary degree of flow." [Line 16067-16068]
- **初粘力机理** — "Tack is also believed to stem from the viscoelasticity of many polymers, allowing them to conform to the substrate to be adhered and 'wet' it even in the dry state." [Line 16064-16066]
- **PSA 三分类** — "Pressure-sensitive adhesives fall into three broad product categories: water based, solvent based, and hot melt." [Line 16069-16070]
- **水性化的经济性驱动** — "Because waterborne polyurethane adhesives have no VOC (volatile organic content) emissions and are nonflammable, they are more environmentally friendly." [Line 11060-11061]；趋势由 "as the price of solvents soared" 推动 [Line 16095-16096]
- **水性化的代价** — "solvent-based polychloroprene adhesives have been losing favor in some applications, even though processing and end use performance are usually superior to that of most water-based polychloroprene systems" [Line 3007-3009]
- **天然胶的战略韧性** — "protein adhesives held onto their 'interior durable' product applications, due in large part to their unique combination of low cost and cold-curing capability or, alternatively, very fast hot press curing (about twice as fast as phenolics)" [Line 49-56]
- **酚醛的核心价值** — "their capability to deliver water, weather, and high-temperature resistance to the cured glue line... at relatively low cost" [Line 4022-4025]
- **甲醛的两难** — "Higher-molar-ratio resins tend to exhibit an overall better initial bond quality, but present an exponentially increased formaldehyde emission problem, most often disqualifying them from many, or most, modern uses." [Line 8011-8012]
- **pMDI 的优势与代价** — "While pMDI wood binders are derived from formaldehyde, they are distinguished from other wood binders by a total lack of formaldehyde emission." [Line 10016-10018]
- **碳水化合物胶的未来** — "future applications of these adhesives will increasingly depend on modifying the natural polymer to give components that can undergo further cross-linking to form water-insensitive bonds." [Line 2551-2554]
- **绿色强度工艺** — "drying to a tacky-to-the-touch condition, and mating the surfaces with moderate pressure provides excellent contact bond strength." [Line 3015-3019]

## Business Connection Points 业务连接点

- **腰贴 / 侧贴 / 顶贴的 PSA 选型基准** — 书中直接确认"非改性丙烯酸乳液 + 热熔胶"为主导，且**皮肤不刺激是第一约束**（与急救敷料同一标准）。Tg 必须显著低于使用温度。
- **热熔胶仍是第一大支出项** — EVA/PO 属聚烯烃类（$1,290 M 市场），关注 Tg、熔点、开放时间；PO（乙烯-辛烯共聚物）比 EVA 具有更好的耐水与再熔加工性。
- **水性化转型的取舍** — 环保合规是硬约束，但干燥能耗高，且加工/终端性能通常不如溶剂型；应只在水性化不可逆的环节（贴身面材、封条）强制推进。
- **淀粉/糊精与木浆协同采购** — $730 M 市场 + 纸品行业基础辅料，可并入木浆/纸品供应商谈判，同时提供干强剂/表面施胶剂的配套选项。
- **天然橡胶过敏风险** — 乳胶在卫材基本淘汰，但天然橡胶系胶乳在某些一次性用品边缘场景仍在使用；过敏评估是核心考量。
- **甲醛问题对工厂环境的影响** — 包装纸板用 PF/UF 的甲醛释放影响车间空气质量，可作为向供应商索取无醛（pMDI）替代的技术依据。
- **导电胶的老化数据可作警示参考** — 65 °C/85 % RH 下电阻上升 160 %-35,000 %，说明湿热环境下有机胶的电学稳定性极差——对任何"智能尿裤"导电层设计是直接的可靠性风险基线。
