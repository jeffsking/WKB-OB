# Chapter 39 — Drying of Biotechnological Products

> Source: Adamiec, Kamiński, Markowski & Strumiłło, in Mujumdar (ed.), *Handbook of Industrial Drying*, 4th ed., CRC Press, 2014, Ch.39, pp.903–924 [页码范围待验证；正文引句页码 p.905–924 均已核对].

## 1. Overview 概述

Chapter 39 treats drying as **preservation of biologically active material** — microorganism cultures, enzymes, antibiotics, vitamins, proteins — rather than bulk water removal. In these colloidal-capillary-porous substances, water is both solvent and a structural element of the particle, so drying must preserve activity/storage life, not merely hit a moisture target. 干燥目标从"含水率"转向"活性保留"。

Relevance to hygiene materials: ★★★ moderate — 这是益生菌/酶/活性成分湿巾与配方干燥的"母章"：低温柔护活性生物质的原理可迁移到卫材生物基添加剂的干燥与储存。

## 2. Key Claims 关键论点（原句摘录）

- "attention cannot be paid only to the removal of water, which is the medium and solvent of a substance, but also to the removal of water, which is a constructional element of the product particle. The presence or absence of such water is of fundamental importance for biotechnological product storage life, its activity, and applicability." (p.905)
- Lability classification (p.908): "1. Materials nonresistant thermally… bacterial cultures, enzymes, viruses, yeast, and fungi are included. 2. Biotechnological products of high thermal resistance… amino acids, antibiotics, and selected bacterial strains." Group 1 critical T ~60°C at MC 35–40% wb; Group 2 ~150°C at 5–7% wb.
- Kinetics (p.910): "dA/dt = −k_d A… kd = k{d,0} exp(−E_a/(R_u T))"; experimental vs calculated discrepancy 20–30% (p.910).
- Activation energies (p.911, Table 39.3): *Lactobacillus plantarum* 428 kJ/mol, *Lactobacillus acidophilus* 142, *Bacillus thuringiensis* (spore) 33, bacitracin 68, lysine 107.
- Spray at wet-bulb (p.914): "spray drying can also be applied to thermolabile substances provided the sprayed material is highly dispersed and the drying agent has a high temperature (the drying process takes place immediately at a wet bulb temperature)."
- Freeze/vacuum (p.917): "Moisture is removed at low temperature, which in fact excludes thermal inactivation of the product. The material structure is maintained (particles and cells are not damaged)."
- Vitrification (p.917): ultra-swift cooling (10,000°/min) → "amorphous ice is formed (transformation into a vitreous form, termed vitrification), which does not cause mechanical damage of cells."
- Protective additives (p.918): "protective substances… such as glycerin and sugars (fructose, saccharose, lactose…)… protect microorganisms against too high salt concentration and provide more favorable conditions for ice crystallization during freezing."
- Carrier/sorbent (p.920): carrier "protects cells or biopolymers against mechanical or thermal destruction" and "takes over part of moisture from biomass"; relative absorptivity K ranks zeolite/silica-gel/activated-carbon (high K, humidity-dependent) vs maize meal/potato flour/peat (K≈1–1.5).
- Encapsulation (p.922): dried microspheres ~10 µm via spray/freeze dryers for stable storage and prolonged action.

## 3. Concept Deep-Dive 概念深挖

### 3.1 Activity-loss kinetics as a design budget 活性损失动力学作为设计预算
First-order + Arrhenius gives a quantitative time–temperature budget: activity decay is exponential in 1/T with activation energy E_a. A higher inlet temperature is tolerable only if the particle sees it briefly (spray at wet-bulb) or if E_a is low (thermostable antibiotic). 对含酶/益生菌湿巾，该方程约束转化线的温度-驻留时间包络。`[待验证：E_a 数值来自分析文件转录，用于工艺设计前应核对原书 Table 39.3]`

### 3.2 Vitrification 玻璃化保护
Ultra-rapid freezing yields a glassy (non-crystalline) matrix that immobilizes reactants and spares cell membranes; glycerin acts as a plasticizer/stabilizer (dissolves salts, restores osmotic balance). 这是典型的聚合物玻璃化转变现象，与保湿剂/稳定剂的配方逻辑同源。

### 3.3 Carrier as moisture sink 载体兼水分汇
A food-grade carrier (starch, skim milk, maltodextrin) doubles as protectant and lowers the water to be evaporated — 保护与节能双重收益。

## 4. Hygiene Implication 卫材关联

- **H39-1 活性保留即验收标准**: set the drying spec from an activity-retention target (% viable cells / % enzyme activity), not just final MC — 把不可见的活性损失变成真正的验收指标。
- **H39-2 温度×时间预算**: compute the allowable product-temperature × residence-time envelope from the active's E_a before auditing an active-coating/impregnation dryer.
- **H39-3 湿球温度喷雾或冷冻干燥**: for live-culture or high-value enzyme wipes, qualify spray-at-wet-bulb (high dispersion) or a freeze-dry step; avoid sustained high-T hovering.
- **H39-4 配方侧保护剂**: require glycerin/trehalose/sucrose/skim-milk/maltodextrin in freeze- or spray-dried active systems; verify skin-contact compliance.
- **H39-5 食品级载体**: a starch/skim-milk carrier buffers moisture and lowers drying energy in powdered active premixes.
- **H39-6 微胶囊化**: spray-dried ~10 µm microcapsules (starch/chitosan matrix) enable stabilized, delayed-release actives in wipes `[待验证：封装段主要为化妆品/医药实验室规模，工业卫产线转化未经证实]`.
- Caveat: the chapter has no direct diaper/wipe examples; relevance is by analogy. 本章无卫材直例，相关性为类比迁移。
