# Characterisation, testing and modelling of nonwoven fabrics

## One-line Position 一句话定位

Chapter 9 (N. Mao & S.J. Russell, §9.22 B. Pourdeyhimi) is the measurement-and-modelling capstone: it compiles test standards and structure→property models that let the bonding/finishing chapters be verified and optimised — the quantitative backbone for every hygiene metric.（中文：第 9 章是测量与建模总章，汇总测试标准与"结构→性能"模型，使前序粘合/整理章可被验证与优化，是所有卫生指标的量化工具有。）

## Summary 摘要

- It asserts performance = composition × structure, then organises the field into bond-structure characterisation, dimensional parameters (weight, thickness, density, FOD, porosity, pore size), a compendium of test standards, measurement techniques and a modelling section.（主张性能=组成×结构，再分粘合结构表征、尺寸参数、测试标准汇编、测量技术、建模。）
- Fluid-handling properties are explicitly enumerated (permeability, absorbency, penetration time, wicking rate, re-wet, barrier, strike time) — effectively the test menu for absorbent hygiene products.（流体性能被明确枚举，实为吸收性卫生用品的测试清单。）
- Two liquid-transport modes are distinguished: wicking (in-plane) vs demand absorbency (through-plane).（区分两种液体传输：面内芯吸 vs 贯穿式需求吸收。）
- Modelling translates structure into properties via D'Arcy, Lucas–Washburn, the directional Mao–Russell model, Kozeny–Carman, Fourier and acoustic impedance.（建模以 D'Arcy、Lucas–Washburn、方向性 Mao–Russell、Kozeny–Carman 等将结构转为性能。）

## Key Claims 关键论断

- Performance = composition × structure — every test/model traces back to both.（性能=组成×结构，所有测试与模型均源于二者。）
- Wicking (in-plane) vs demand absorbency (through-plane) underpins ADL vs core uptake design.（面内芯吸 vs 贯穿吸收是导流层 vs 芯体吸收的设计基础。）
- Wettability is a measurable surface-energy balance, linkable to Ch.7/Ch.8 finishes.（润湿性是可测的表面能平衡，与第 7/8 章整理关联。）
- Standardised hygiene-specific test methods already exist for compliance QC.（针对卫生用品的标准化测试已存在，可用于合规 QC。）
