# Chapter 11 Testing of Manufactured Fibres 人造纤维的测试

> Source: Manufactured Fibre Technology, Ch.11, pp.248–267.
> Author of chapter: V.K. Kothari. 本章作者：V.K. Kothari。

## Summary 摘要

Purposes of testing are stated in 11.1: **quality control 质量控制**, **process control 工艺控制**, **product development 产品开发** and **process optimisation 工艺优化**. The chapter then works through, section by section: **fineness 细度** (11.2), **crimp 卷曲** (11.3), **tensile properties 拉伸性能** (11.4), **evenness 均匀度** (11.5), **friction 摩擦** (11.6), **shrinkage 收缩** (11.7) and **entanglement 缠结** (11.8) — each following the chain "definition → quantifying index → standard method → commercial instrument".

## Fineness 细度 (11.2)

- **Linear density 线密度** (p.248): "Fibre and yarn fineness is normally measured in terms of mass per unit length" — denier or tex.
- **Direct weighing method 直接称重法** preferred (p.248): "The preferred method for measuring linear density is the direct weighing method." Reason: cross-sections are non-circular and diameter measurement is difficult and time-consuming. Standard cited: **ASTM D1577-79**.
- **Vibroscope 振动计法**: useful for staple bundles, but the practical trap flagged at p.250 is that crimped staple gives a **falsely high** linear density unless extra tension is applied to remove crimp. Note also that the dtex expression at p.250 contains OCR errors in the source — [待验证] against the original text.
- Commercial moisture regain values are tabulated in Table 11.1, including **polyester 0.4%**, **nylon 4.5%**, **viscose 11.0%**.

## Crimp 卷曲 (11.3)

- **Crimp frequency 卷曲频率** (p.250): "the number of crimps or waves per unit length of extended or straightened fibre".
- Methods: wave-counting per **ASTM D3937-90**, the percentage-crimp method, and automatic instruments such as the Lenzing **Vibrotex**.

## Tensile Properties 拉伸性能 (11.4)

- Three straining principles exist — **CRE (constant rate of extension 恒速拉伸)**, **CRT (constant rate of loading)** and **CRL (constant rate of traverse)** — per **ASTM D76-90**. Reason for CRE dominance (p.252): "Most fibre and filament yarn testers employ constant rate of extension (CRE) as the straining principle"; CRL suffers creep and CRT has low accuracy, whereas CRE gives multiple parameters from one load–elongation curve.
- Parameters: breaking load, breaking elongation, **tenacity 强度** (p.253: "the breaking load per unit linear density of the unstrained specimen"), initial modulus, Young's modulus, work of rupture, **toughness 韧性**.
- Test settings: gauge length, crosshead speed, full-scale load, chart speed (**ASTM D2101 / D3822 / D76**).

## Evenness 均匀度 (11.5)

- Cutting-and-weighing versus capacitive methods (p.257): "Capacitive methods of determining yarn evenness are the most popular and widely used methods." The yarn passes between parallel capacitor plates; the dielectric mass change is proportional to mass per unit length.
- Outputs: evenness diagram, **CV(%)**, **spectrogram 波谱图**, variance–length curve. Uster spectrograms can localize periodic faults such as eccentric spinning cylinders or eccentric drafting rollers (p.261).

## Friction 摩擦 (11.6)

- **Static friction is always greater than kinetic friction** (p.263). Staple friction governs yarn properties; filament friction governs torque transmission and filament migration in false-twist and air-jet texturing.
- Methods: block-on-fibre-mat, yarn-to-metal and yarn-to-yarn tension methods based on **Amonton's law** (p.264): **T2/T1 = e^(μθ)**, obtaining the friction coefficient from the tension ratio around a friction pin. Instruments cited: Rothschild F-Meter, Zweigle.

## Shrinkage 收缩 (11.7)

- **Shrinkage 收缩** (p.264): "the decrease in length of a specimen"; a negative value is called stretch.
- Discontinuous and continuous methods; commercial instruments **Toray FTA-500**, **Lawson-Hemphill CTT-DST**, **Textechno Dynafil**, **Rothschild DEFT/CCT**; standard **ASTM D2102-90**.
- Key claim (p.265): "there must be a means of assessing and controlling shrinkage behaviour of the yarn." Simultaneous measurement of boiling-water / dry-heat shrinkage **and** shrinkage force on a running yarn is presented as better suited to quality control than shrinkage percentage alone.

## Entanglement 缠结 (11.8)

- **Opening length 开口长度** — the distance between two entanglement points, describing the temporary cohesion of an entangled yarn (p.267).
- Methods: manual needle, automatic needle, electrostatic method (Obestat), automatic thickness measurement (Itemat, Toray profilometer).
- Stein's comparison [13] concludes **Dynafil** detects variation in polymerization, temperature and quench-air speed most effectively (p.267).

## Limitations 局限（源自原书内容）

- 成书于 1997 年，仪器型号（Uster 3C/IIC、Dynafil 等）已过时，未涉及近年光学/在线 AI 检测。
- 部分公式 OCR 排版错乱（如 p.250 的 dtex 表达式），需对照原书。
- 各方法的精度与适用范围对比不足。

## Hygiene implication 卫生材料启示

Moderately relevant — this is the chapter most transferable to **incoming staple-fibre and nonwoven qualification**.

1. **Crimp testing is directly usable for hot-air bonded staple**: crimp frequency (ASTM D3937) and percentage crimp are the standard descriptors of ES staple; both affect carding openness, web loft and the final bulky hand of the through-air bonded layer. There is no substitute for them in this chapter's framework, and the source's warning about the vibroscope needing extra tension to remove crimp is a real trap when measuring crimped ES staple.
2. **CRE principle** underpins essentially every nonwoven tensile test used in the vault — see [[3D面层CD拉伸强度测试.pdf]] and [[面层粘合强度测试方法.pdf]]. The panel setting logic at p.252 (gauge length, crosshead speed, full-scale load) reads across unchanged.
3. **Friction**: staple-fibre friction governs carding and web formation behaviour, and for polyolefin staple — low surface energy, hence low inter-fibre friction — this is one mechanism behind poor carding cohesion. Amonton's law gives the standard way to measure it (p.264). [待验证] whether modern finish packages on commercial ES staple change the ranking reported here.
4. **Shrinkage force vs shrinkage percentage** is the most useful idea here for hot-air bonded webs: the chapter's point that force is more informative than percentage carries over directly to webs restrained on a conveyor through the oven — compare with [[FZ-T 64112-2024（婴幼儿纸尿裤用非织造布行业标准）]], which specifies nonwoven acceptance criteria in the same terms.
5. Links back to Chapter 10: structure-level measurements (crystallinity, orientation) versus property-level measurements (this chapter) are complementary — see [[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch10 Characterization of Polymers and Fibres]].

Nothing here applies to fluff pulp, SAP or core absorbency.
