## One-line Summary 一句话综述

This thesis presents novel liquid crystalline polymers as smart materials for two stimulus-responsive applications: light-triggered controlled-release microcapsules and bio-inspired, humidity-independent proton-conducting membranes.
（中文：本书以新型液晶聚合物为智能材料，面向两类刺激响应应用——光触发可控释放微胶囊，以及仿生、湿度无关的质子导电膜。）

## Table of Contents 章节目录

- [[General Introduction and Objectives]] — Conceptual foundation: defines smart materials via LCPs and frames the two application threads (photoresponsive microcapsules + proton membranes).
- [[Synthesis and Characterization of a New Family of Photoactive Liquid Crystalline Polyesters based on α-methylstilbene]] — Synthesizes UV-sensitive main-chain nematic polyesters P1–P4; interfacial polyesterification is the key high-Mw route; α-methylstilbene E–Z isomerization is the order/wettability switch.
- [[Preparation and Characterization of Light-Sensitive Microcapsules based on a Liquid Crystalline Polyester]] — P4 microcapsules release vanillin under 365 nm UV via E→Z backbone contraction; dark-stable, quasi-one-way switch. (Note: actual chapter is microcapsules, not a pervaporation membrane.)
- [[An Atomistic Insight into Light-sensitive Polymers with Methylstilbene Building Blocks]] — Periodic-boundary DFT/TDDFT confirms trans→cis isomerization solely drives P4 shell collapse/release; isophthaloyl is the low-Tg design knob.
- [[Applying Nature's Genius to Discotic Liquid-crystal Polymers for Proton Conducting Membrane]] — Bio-inspired discotic LCPs self-assemble into homeotropic columnar proton channels; water-independent 10⁻²–10⁻³ S/cm, size-selective cation transport.
- [[Liquid Crystalline Polymeric Wires for Selective Proton Transport, Part 1]] — Dendronized PAZE in AAO forms oriented polymeric wires via nanoconfinement; baking controls orientation, low swelling (~11%).
- [[Liquid Crystalline Polymeric Wires for Selective Proton Transport, Part 2]] — Ion-free columns give selective proton transport; EIS unreliable (use I–V); methanol crossover ~2× below Nafion. (manual_review: intro vs results disagree on 2 vs 3 orders of magnitude.)
- [[General Conclusions and Appendixes]] — Consolidates both platforms; states EIS-vs-I–V caveat; first report of 180 °C membrane Z→E isomerization.

## Key Themes 核心主题

- Photoresponsive controlled release (α-methylstilbene E–Z isomerization, microcapsules) — see [[Synthesis and Characterization of a New Family of Photoactive Liquid Crystalline Polyesters based on α-methylstilbene]] and [[Preparation and Characterization of Light-Sensitive Microcapsules based on a Liquid Crystalline Polyester]].
- Oriented columnar / ionic-transport membranes (discotic LCP, homeotropic baking, RH-independent conductivity) — see [[Applying Nature's Genius to Discotic Liquid-crystal Polymers for Proton Conducting Membrane]] and [[Liquid Crystalline Polymeric Wires for Selective Proton Transport, Part 2]].
- Atomistic mechanism & design knobs (DFT/TDDFT, isophthaloyl low-Tg lever) — see [[An Atomistic Insight into Light-sensitive Polymers with Methylstilbene Building Blocks]].
