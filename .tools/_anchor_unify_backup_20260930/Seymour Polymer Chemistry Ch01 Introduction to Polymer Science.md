# Seymour Polymer Chemistry Ch01 Introduction to Polymer Science

> Source: Seymour/Carraher, *Polymer Chemistry*, Ch.1 — Introduction to Polymer Science.
> 定位说明 / Citation convention: 原文为 .txt 抽取件，**无正式印张页码**，本报告以文件行号（line no.）作为定位引用；原书页码缺失处统一标注 [待验证]。

## Executive Summary 内容概要

Chapter 1 is the programmatic introduction: it answers **what a polymer is** (definition and nomenclature), **how polymers entered human civilisation** (from biopolymers to synthetic macromolecules), and **what economic weight the polymer industry carries**. Seymour argues on three levels — etymology/classification, history, and industrial economics — and closes with the axiom that governs the whole book: **the differences between all polymers are determined primarily by intermolecular and intramolecular forces and by the functional groups present**. 换言之：**结构决定性能**，这是后七章（分子结构、分子量、表征、流变、聚合机理、降解）的公理化起点。

## History of Polymers 聚合物史

- **词源 / Etymology**：*"The word polymer is derived from the Greek poly and meros, meaning many and parts, respectively. Some scientists prefer to use the word macromolecule, or large molecule, instead of polymer."* (line 11-12)
- **时代定位**：*"Since most chemists and chemical engineers are now involved in some phase of polymer science or technology, some have called this the polymer age. Actually, we have always lived in a polymer age."* (line 5-6)
- **同一原理 / Unified principle**：*"However, the same principles apply to all polymers. If one discounts the end uses, the differences between all polymers, including plastics, fibers, and elastomers or rubbers, are determined primarily by the intermolecular and intramolecular forces between the molecules and within the individual molecule, respectively, and by the functional groups present."* (line 14-18)
- **里程碑 1 — 硫化 / Vulcanization (Goodyear, 1839)**：*"Charles Goodyear and his brother Nelson transformed natural rubber, hevea rubber, from a heat-'softenable' thermoplastic to a less heat-sensitive product through the creation of crosslinks between the individual polyisoprene chain-like molecules using sulfur as the crosslinking agent."* (line 67-69). 随硫用量增加，橡胶变硬，成为 ebonite（硬橡胶），(line 72-73)。
- **里程碑 2 — 大分子概念 / Staudinger (1920)**：*"Staudinger believed that large molecules were based on the joining, through covalent bonding, of large numbers of atoms."* (line 415-416)
- **里程碑 3 — 合成工程 / Carothers & DuPont (1930s)**：*"His intention was to prepare molecules of known structure through the use of known organic chemistry and to 'investigate how the properties of these substances depended on constitution.'"* (line 420-421)

## Thermoplastics and Thermosets 热塑与热固

> *"Thermoplastics are two-dimensional molecules that may be softened by heating. Thermosets are materials that are three-dimensional networks that cannot be reshaped by heating. Rather than melting, thermosets degrade."* (line 70-72)

> *"Polymers are often divided according to whether they can be melted and reshaped through application of heat and pressure, called thermoplastics, or whether they decompose before they can be melted or reshaped, called thermosets (Table 1.3). While both thermoset polymers and thermoplastics can be recycled, because thermoplastics can be reshaped simply through the application of heat and pressure, recycling of thermoplastics is easier."* (line 769-773)

| 维度 分类维度 | Thermoplastic 热塑性 | Thermoset 热固性 |
|---|---|---|
| 结构 Structure | two-dimensional molecules，线型/支化 | three-dimensional network，交联网络 |
| 受热行为 Heat response | softened / melted by heating，可重塑 | cannot be reshaped；degrade rather than melt |
| 回收性 Recyclability | 加热加压即可重塑，回收更容易 回收较易 | 可回收但需其他途径，不可重熔 |
| 典型品种 Examples | LDPE、HDPE、PP、PS、ABS、PVC、PET | 酚醛、环氧、硫化橡胶/ebonite |

## Economic and Industrial Context 产业与经济背景

- 热塑塑料主要品种 / Major thermoplastics：LDPE、HDPE、PP、PS、ABS、PVC、PET (Tables 1.3-1.10)。
- 合成橡胶 / Synthetic rubbers：styrene-butadiene、polybutadiene、nitrile、ethylene-propylene。

- **就业 / Employment**：synthetic polymers 占化学工业就业 **1150 thousand (1994)** → **1206 thousand (2000)** (line 857-863)。

## Methodology Critique 方法论批判

- **术语之争 / Nomenclature dispute**：polymer vs macromolecule — Seymour 呈现两派但不裁决，并指出 "the same principles apply to all polymers"，暗示术语之争是表面的。
- **天然 vs 合成二元论的消解**：传统教学主张 natural polymers 与 synthetic polymers 分课讲授；Seymour 以还原论立场反驳——同一结构-性能原理适用。
- **基础研究与应用研究的断裂 / The "unnatural gap"**：*"Up to this point, it was considered that universities were where discoveries were made and industry was where they were put to some practical use. This separation between basic and applied work was quite prominent at this juncture..."* (line 404-406) — 在聚合物领域，大部分基础研究是在工业界完成的。
- **经验先于理论**：*"polymer scientists displayed a talent for making empirical discoveries before the science was developed."* (line 38-40) — Goodyear 在不知交联机理的情况下发现硫化，既是本学科的历史特征，也是方法论警示。

## Hygiene Implication 卫材关联

- **SAP 属交联热固型**：SAP（高吸水性树脂）是交联形成的三维网络，按本章定义归 thermoset——吸水膨胀后不溶解、不熔融，仅在极端条件下降解。交联度直接决定保水量与凝胶强度：交联度过高保水量下降，过低则凝胶崩解。
- **PP 无纺布依赖热塑性**：PP 是典型热塑聚合物，热熔融纺丝、热风穿透粘合、热轧复合、超声波点焊，全部是 "reshaped through the application of heat and pressure" 的工程体现，且加工废料可回收再熔融。
- **交联 = 回弹性的来源**：交联把线型链的永久滑移变为有限弹性形变；任何需要回弹的应用（弹性腰围、腿部罗口、弹性膜）都必须引入交联或物理网络；交联密度越高回弹越强、延展性越差。
- **烯烃纤维的增长背景**：1975→2000 年烯烃纤维产量 500→3180 百万磅的增长，正是卫材（尿裤/卫生巾）大量采用 PP/PE 无纺布的产业前提。
