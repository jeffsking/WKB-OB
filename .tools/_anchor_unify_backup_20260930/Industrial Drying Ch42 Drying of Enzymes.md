# Chapter 42 — Drying of Enzymes

> Source: Pilosof & Sánchez, in Mujumdar (ed.), *Handbook of Industrial Drying*, 4th ed., CRC Press, 2014, Ch.42, pp.979–989 [页码范围待验证；正文引句页码 p.979–986 均已核对].

## 1. Overview 概述

Chapter 42 treats enzyme drying as a **damage-minimisation problem**: enzymes are heat-labile protein catalysts that "are deactivated due to heat-induced structural changes," yet solid (powder/granule) form is required for storage stability and dust safety. Spray drying is the industrial workhorse; the chapter covers chamber layout (cocurrent preferred), activity-loss limits (outlet temperature; moisture below 15–20%), and glass-state stabilisation science (sugars, polyols, salts with Tg 60–200°C). The second half covers detergent enzyme granulation aimed at eliminating active dust. 酶干燥 = 工艺窗口 + 玻璃态配方 + 包覆颗粒三件套。

Relevance to hygiene materials: ★★★☆☆ moderate — 对含酶/活性成分的湿巾与清洁基材真实可用；对芯体 fluff/SAP 无直接作用。活性生物质低温柔护原理可迁移到卫材生物基添加剂。

## 2. Key Claims 关键论点（原句摘录）

- "Enzymes are protein catalysts of high molecular weight… deactivated due to heat-induced structural changes or, in the case of proteolytic enzymes, due to decomposition by themselves." (p.979)
- "Thus, cocurrent drying chambers are preferred to minimize heat deactivation of enzymes during the process." (p.980)
- "in the case of enzymes that are particularly sensitive to oxidation, it is preferable to use inert gas, such as nitrogen, during spraying and drying." (p.980)
- "Enzyme activity decreases with increasing outlet air temperatures. … Significant enzyme inactivation occurs when moisture decreases below 15 to 20%." (p.981); Table 42.4: fungal α-amylase at 78–80°C outlet loses 26–38% activity vs 4–10% at 63–66°C.
- Two-stage remedy (p.981–982): loss "can be reduced and even almost completely avoided when a controlled combination of spray drying and fluidized-bed drying is used"; low-T fluid bed at 40–50°C (p.982).
- Vitrification mechanism (p.983): "proteins are mechanically immobilized in the glassy, solid matrix during dehydration. The restriction of translational and relaxation processes is thought to prevent protein unfolding… sugars can continue to protect the dried protein by hydrogen bonding to the protein… serving as water substitutes when the hydration shell of the enzyme is removed."
- Carrier spec (p.984): "must exist in a glassy amorphous state with a glass-transition temperature (Tg) above 20 °C (a desirable range is therefore between 60 and 200 °C) and should be sufficiently chemically inert"; trehalose/maltose-type disaccharides preferred (p.985).
- Dose-response (p.984, Table 42.5): lactose 0% → 83% residual activity; 0.5% → 90% (95% after 1 h at 90°C); 1% → 96%. MgSO₄ (5–90% salt) gave "a recovery of more than 100% active enzyme after drying and after long-term storage" `[待验证：>100%回收数据来自专利源，机理未明，未经独立复现]`.
- Dust/coating (p.986): "the enzyme particles have to be coated in such a way that no active enzyme dust is present or released during handling"; coating must protect against "water, hydrogen peroxide, and peracetic acid (the bleach that migrates in detergent formulations)" yet "dissolve readily in the washing liquor, even at low temperatures."

## 3. Concept Deep-Dive 概念深挖

### 3.1 Activity-loss operating window 活性损失操作窗口
Two hard limits: cap outlet air temperature; never dry below 15–20% moisture in the first stage — finish in a 40–50°C fluid bed to avoid the hydration-shell-removal shock (p.983: final air-drying stress "can result in irreversible inactivation upon rehydration"). The two-stage spray→fluid-bed layout is the engineering realisation. 出口温度封顶 + 残余水分下限 = 工艺窗口。

### 3.2 Vitrification as stabilisation principle 玻璃化稳定机理
A carbohydrate glass immobilises the enzyme and replaces its hydration shell via hydrogen bonding. Quantitative lever is dose: 0.5% lactose lifts protease residual from 83% → 90% after drying (p.984). Caveat: "inactivation occurs also in formulations kept well below their glass-transition temperatures" (p.983) — Tg theory alone is incomplete. Tg 不是唯一判据。

### 3.3 Encapsulation for dust/chemical safety 包覆防尘与抗氧化剂
Coated granules replaced 1960s 100%-dust powders; the coating must block dust, resist abrasion, survive bleach/oxidants, yet dissolve readily in use liquor (p.986). For hygiene: template for enclosed, low-dust, chemically-protected active granules dosed into wipes.

## 4. Hygiene Implication 卫材关联

- **H42-1 玻璃态基质包埋活性物**: specify a trehalose/maltose/salt glassy carrier (Tg 60–200°C) for enzyme or probiotic wipe actives rather than free dried protein.
- **H42-2 出口温度封顶+防过干**: on any spray- or air-drying step for an active-coated substrate, require a two-stage (spray → low-T fluid bed 40–50°C) profile and a residual-MC floor.
- **H42-3 氧敏活性物用氮气**: nitrogen blanketing for oxidant-sensitive actives (certain enzymes, vitamins) in drying/coating.
- **H42-4 活性颗粒包覆规格**: low-dust, oxidant-resistant (H₂O₂/过氧乙酸), readily-dissolving coating — 兼顾工人/使用者安全与湿巾氧化剂环境的活性保护。
- Caveat: data are heavily patent-derived; relevance is for enzyme/active-containing wipes and cleaning substrates, not core absorbent-core materials. 数据多源自专利，相关面限于含酶/活性配方。
