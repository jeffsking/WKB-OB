# Thermal bonding

## One-line Position 一句话定位

Chapter 6 (A. Pourmohammadi) presents thermal bonding as web consolidation via heating a thermoplastic component to its softening/melting point then cooling — eliminating externally applied chemical adhesive in many cases, and the dominant route for diaper topsheet/ADL and many wipes.（中文：第 6 章讲热粘合——加热热塑组分至软化/熔融后冷却成布，多数情况免去外添化学粘合剂，是纸尿裤面层/导流层与多数拭布的主导工艺。）

## Summary 摘要

- It opens with the historical origin (Reed, 1942) and the energy/environment shift away from latex bonding, then surveys raw materials: binder fibres, bicomponent (conjugate) fibres, polymeric powder binders, with Tg/Tm tables.（先讲起源（1942 Reed）与节能/环保驱动，再列原料与玻璃化/熔融温度表。）
- The core systematically treats four heat-transfer routes: calender (conduction, area/point bonding), through-air/impingement (convection), and thermal-radiation/IR plus ultrasonic bonding.（核心梳理四种传热路径：轧光（传导）、热风/冲击（对流）、热辐射/红外与超声波。）
- Calender bonding develops process-parameter relations (temperature, nip pressure, contact time, engraving) and heat-transfer physics (conduction, DIH, Clapeyron).（轧光粘合展开工艺参数关系与传热物理：传导、形变生热、Clapeyron 效应。）
- It closes with fabric-structure consequences (bond-point failure, fibre morphology) and a broad application survey foregrounding hygiene disposables.（收尾于织物结构后果与以卫生用品为主的应用。）

## Key Claims 关键论断

- The bond is intrinsic to the fibre/powder polymer, not an added adhesive — central to topsheet/ADL design.（粘合点内生于纤维/粉末聚合物而非外加粘合剂。）
- Thermal bonding is a sustainability- and cost-led substitution for latex bonding.（热粘是替代胶乳粘合的节能/环保路线。）
- Sheath-core PP/PE and CoPET/PET bicomponents dominate hygiene webs (low-melt sheath fuses, core preserves strength).（皮芯双组分主导卫材网。）
- Target surface sintering; over-bonding embrittles and causes point-bond "popping".（目标为表面烧结；过粘致"爆点"失效。）
