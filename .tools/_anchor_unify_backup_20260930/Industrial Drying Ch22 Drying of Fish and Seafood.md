# Chapter 22 — Drying of Fish and Seafood

> Source: Mujumdar (ed.), Handbook of Industrial Drying, 4th ed., CRC Press, 2014, Ch.22, pp.545–557.

(M. Shafiur Rahman 执笔。页码由 PDF 页 − 21 推算，应与印刷版抽查核对。)

**相关度声明**：食品章 — 对卫材**直接相关性低**（★★）。基材是动物肌肉而非绒毛浆/SAP，作者全章未提卫材产品；不为其编造纸尿裤应用。可迁移的只有三条机理：case-hardening 规则、Tg′ 塌陷框架、分段温度逻辑。

## Scope 范围与定位

Survey of fish/seafood drying as preservation: muscle structure → pretreatments (salting, cooking, smoking) → dryer selection (sun, convective, vacuum, freeze) → low-a_w packaging → quality-change catalogue (microflora, browning, lipid oxidation, protein denaturation, shrinkage/pore formation, rehydration, texture, nutrition). Governing logic: fish is a high-moisture, high-protein, often high-fat matrix whose drying is dominated by case-hardening, surface-crust barriers, and Tg-governed shrinkage.

## Case-Hardening 表面结壳规则（最可迁移）

> "Heating the surface too fast can produce a hard crust (mostly dried soluble protein), which retards movement of moisture. This phenomenon (case-hardening) can severely reduce the rate of drying and must be avoided." (p.550)

> "The formation of a superficial pellicle (case-hardening), which may considerably retard drying, is avoided by precooking." (p.548)

解读：把"dried soluble protein"换成"dried binder / fused pulp skin"，就是热风非织造产线上封死内部水分通路的同款失效 — 与 [[Industrial Drying Ch15 Impingement Drying]] 的 casehardening/cracking 网材缺陷清单互为镜像。操作原则：控制表面通量，让内部水分持续供给一个可渗透（未玻璃化）的表面。

## Glass-Transition Collapse 玻璃化转变与塌陷

> "The glass transition theory is one of the proposed concepts to explain the process of shrinkage and collapse during drying... there is negligible collapse (more pores) in material if processed below glass transition and higher the difference between the process temperature and the glass transition temperature, the higher the collapse." (p.554)

Tg′ for fish/meat = 11–15 °C (p.554); freeze-drying stays glassy/porous, hot-air stays rubbery/dense. 解读：这是全章唯一的真聚合物物理桥梁 — 含粘合剂/纤维网材或 SAP 颗粒同样有 Tg，高于 Tg′ 干燥塌孔并熔结结构、低于则保孔隙率 — 与保持 airlaid 芯体蓬松度是同一杠杆。作者自认该理论"does not hold true for all products"（p.554，另有表面张力/毛细力等竞争机制）。

## Quality-vs-Rate Trade-off 品质-速率权衡与分段温度

> "The highest possible drying temperatures should be used to maximize thermal death even though low drying temperatures are best for maintaining organoleptic characteristics [49]. Other alternative is to use high drying temperature initially at high moisture content and then drying at low temperature." (p.552)

解读：高水分段高热通量（恒速期）→ 低水分段低通量（降速期）的分段轮廓，正是卫材产线避免热损伤的同一形状。热泵干燥作为低温节能选项 (p.551–552)，能耗总账见 [[Industrial Drying Ch46 Energy Aspects]]。

## Monolayer & Osmotic Equilibrium 单分子层与渗透平衡

- > "At water activities around the monolayer (aw ≈ 0.3), resistance to oxidation is greatest." (p.553) — 湿敏芯体仓储 RH 目标的定性锚点。
- Salting = osmotic dehydration, "Equilibrium is the end point of osmotic process, i.e., the net rate of mass transport is zero" (p.547) — 任何趋向环境 RH 决定的残余水分稳态的解吸的概念模板。

## Method & Limits 方法与局限

纯综述章（≈95 篇文献）+ 定性机理描述 + 借用的鱿鱼/乌贼孔隙率经验式（p.556，**数值不得移植**）。诚实边界：无定量干燥动力学（thin-layer/deep-bed 形式体系在 Ch.23–24）；D 值模型在变水分干燥下不适用 (p.552–553)；OCR 严重粘词，公式与引文需对照印刷版 `[待验证]`。

## Hygiene Implication 卫材关联

- **表面通量控制**：热风绒毛浆/粘合剂网材产线限制初始表面热通量，防止玻璃化/干皮层封住内部水分 (p.550)。
- **蓬松度杠杆**：airlaid/SAP 网材干燥区温度对齐相关 Tg 窗口以保孔隙、防橡胶态塌陷 (p.554–555)。
- **分段温度**：高水分段高通量、低水分段低通量的两段式轮廓 (p.552)。
- **仓储窗口**：a_w ≈ 0.3 单分子层氧化最小值作定性参照 (p.553)。
- 其余（盐渍、烟熏、水产微生物学）与卫材无关，不做延伸。
