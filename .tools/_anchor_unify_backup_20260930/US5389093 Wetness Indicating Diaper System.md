## Technical Field 技术领域

**Thermal-mediation wetness indication claimed at the SYSTEM level（系统级的热媒介湿态指示）** — the continuation reframes the parent's diaper as a **"diaper system"** and, critically, changes the subject from **"a baby"** to **"a wearer"**, extending the disclosure to adult wearers.

## Problem Solved 解决的问题

The background（lines 52-113）is stated by the source to be **substantially identical to the parent US 5,197,958**:

| Problem 问题 | Source wording 原文表述 |
|---|---|
| Single-point indicator + insufficient wicking 单点指示、导流不足 | "The prior art diapers are particularly unadvantageous when the original wetting location is significantly far from the indicator location and the amount of wetness is not substantial." |
| Hard to see / unaesthetic 难以查看、不美观 | "the baby has to be virtually undressed to find the indicator mark ... these single location indicators are not aesthetically pleasing." |
| Electronic route complex + safety fear 电子方案复杂且有安全隐患 | on US 4,356,818 (Macias): "These electronic wetness detectors are elaborate designs which require the addition of multiple components"; "mothers may fear possible electrocution of the child" |
| Not reusable / not wash-durable 不可复用、不耐洗涤 | "the indicating systems of the prior art are not able to withstand these harsh environments"; reusable cloth diaper must "after drying will reindicate a dry condition and then reindicate a wet condition" |
| Environmental driver 环保动因 | "the disposable diapers of the prior art are not reusable and therefore not recyclable." |

The invention's answer is unchanged in kind from the parent: **insulation + wicking + thermal threshold + thermochromic ink on the outer surface**, now claimed as a **system**.

## Core Mechanism 核心机理

Same physical principle as the parent, with the insulation element made more specific:

1. **Insulation layer（隔热层，E3）** — "an **insulation layer positioned between a wearer and said outer shell** which, **when dry is insufficiently thermally conductive** for a normal body temperature to raise said outer surface above a given threshold, but **being sufficiently thermally conductive when wet** to raise said outer surface temperature above a given threshold, **at least partially thermally insulates said outer surface from said inner surface**".
2. **Absorption condition（吸收限定，E5）** — new vs. the parent: the threshold is chosen so the indication is generally present "when said wearer secretes waste material **which is absorbed by said insulation layer**". The **insulation layer must itself absorb the exudate** — insulation and absorption are unified in one element.
3. **Wicking（导流，Claim 2 / Claim 8）** — "said material wicks moisture secreted from said wearer toward said outer layer"; in method claim 8, wicking brings the excreted moisture "**into thermal contact with said thermal indicator**".
4. **Thermal indicator + threshold（热指示器 + 阈值）** — Claim 6: the indicator **comprises thermochromic ink**; Claim 7: a mixture of thermochromic inks, one becoming **colourless above** the threshold; Claim 5: indicator **no longer visible below** the threshold（**reversible 可逆**）.
5. **Method chain（方法链，Claim 8）** — provide the three-portion outer shell carrying the thermal indicator → position it on the wearer with the connecting portion between the legs → at least partially thermally insulate → wick moisture toward the outer surface into thermal contact → provide a visual indication above the threshold. Claim 9: the visual indication is "provided by means of a **change in color**".

**Route: thermochromic（热致变色）, not hydrochromic** — the trigger remains temperature.

## Hygiene Implication 卫材关联

1. **"System" framing → modular indicator concept.** Claiming a **"diaper system"** rather than a diaper supports treating the indication function as a **separable module**（指示贴、指示条独立于主体）. For the chromic-ink programme this is an argument for a **detachable/replaceable chromic indicator element** instead of rebuilding the whole converting line — the same "don't modify the core" logic that appears in [[US20070270773A1 Thermochromic Wetness Indicator]].
2. **"Wearer" wording extends to adult care.** The change from "baby" to **"wearer"** means the disclosure covers **adult incontinence** as well as baby. K-C's adult-care portfolio is in scope — worth noting against [[BM Urine Indicator Concept（尿+便双指示概念）]], which targets urine+faeces dual indication.
3. **Insulation layer must also absorb — a stack design constraint.** E5 requires the exudate to be "**absorbed by said insulation layer**". Translated to a modern disposable: the element that thermally insulates must also be the element that takes the liquid. That is a real design tension for a **thin, high-SAP, fluff-free core** — a thin core insulates poorly, so body heat may leak to the backsheet and false-trigger a thermochromic patch. Evaluate against [[Diaper Structure & ADL-REL Compendium（纸尿裤结构·ADL与REL辨析）]]: if the ADL/REL is used as the wicking-into-thermal-contact bridge, the core thickness/bulk must simultaneously deliver insulation.
4. **Threshold tier gap is a design variable.** Only **>80 °F (≈26.7 °C)** and **>87 °F (≈30.6 °C)** are claimed as dependents here. A thermochromic system set near **ambient-to-skin temperature (≈30–35 °C)** versus one set at **urine temperature (≈35–40 °C)** is therefore a meaningful parameter choice, not a detail — and it is exactly the choice to test with suppliers. See [[Thermochromic & Hydrochromic Materials for Diapers (Feasibility Review v3)]] and, for the fatty-acid tuning table, [[US5197958 Wetness Indicating Diaper]].
5. **Reversibility and wipe-out behaviour.** Claim 5 requires the indication to disappear below the threshold. For a disposable that means the patch can **fade back** as the article cools — a consumer-perception risk (a wet diaper that "looks dry again"). Any chromic candidate must be checked for hysteresis and for whether a one-way (irreversible) hydrochromic signal is preferable.
6. **Safety/regulatory: NOT addressed by this source.** No migration, skin-contact, sensitisation or toxicology data is disclosed. Microencapsulated urea-formalin-wall slurries raise their own residual-formaldehyde question, which **this document does not discuss** → must be settled from supplier dossiers and internal testing, flagged `[待验证]`.
7. **Freedom-to-operate（自由实施）: NOT assessed — no conclusion is drawn.** Recorded from the source only: this is a **continuation of 07/861,475 (now US 5,197,958)**, and the patent face carries the verbatim notice that "the portion of the term of this patent subsequent to **Mar. 30, 2010** has been **disclaimed**". What that disclaimer means for enforceable term, and the status of any foreign family members, **is not determined here** → flagged `[待验证]` / manual review. The source's own §8 contains further推演 expiry estimates and design-around commentary; **that analysis is deliberately not reproduced or adopted**, and nothing on this page should be read as an FTO, validity or expiry opinion.
