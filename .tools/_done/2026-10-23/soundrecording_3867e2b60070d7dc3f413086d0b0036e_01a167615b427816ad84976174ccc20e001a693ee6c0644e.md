# 柔性纤维传感器技术介绍（会议转写）

## Positioning 一句话定位 / 定位

A technical due-diligence record of a **piezoresistive textile pressure-sensor** vendor, held specifically to answer one question: **can this technology measure the low-pressure regime (0–5 kPa) that governs red-mark / fit complaints in baby diapers?** The answer recorded here is "no commercial precedent, but physically plausible".

本页是一次**压阻式织物压力传感器**供方的技术摸底记录，唯一目的是回答一个问题：**它能不能测纸尿裤勒痕/贴合所对应的低压区（0–5 kPa）**。会议给出的答案是——**没有商业先例，但物理上可行**。

## Summary 摘要

The vendor builds a pressure-sensing **fabric**, not a film. Two yarns do the work: a silver-containing conductive yarn and a black piezoresistive yarn, with a white yarn embroidering the conductive path across the piezoresistive cloth so that applied force closes a conductive path and changes resistance. The claimed differentiator is process, not chemistry: instead of coating a drawn filament, they **deconstruct the conductive layer / piezoresistive layer / substrate layer into the fibre itself** and compound the functional fillers **at the melt-spinning stage**, so the filament exits already sensing. Substrate is high-elasticity nylon, which supplies the rebound; the structure is a **two-component compound, not a sheath-core**.

Commercially the company is young (founded end-2023), with a Weihai (Shandong) plant at roughly **200,000 m²/year**, and it owns fibre → fabric → algorithm. Its established markets are humanoid-robot e-skin, ergonomic chairs, AI mattresses, elderly-care mattresses (with a Class II medical device certificate) and automotive seats — **all at body-weight-level pressures**, i.e. one to two orders of magnitude above the diaper regime.

That gap is the crux of the meeting. The buyer side specified that **0–1 kPa covers 70–80% of the relevant data and 0–5 kPa essentially all of it** (0.01 N over 1 cm² ≈ 100 Pa), and that past vendors claiming small-force sensitivity failed on **drift and noise floor** rather than on triggering. The vendor conceded **no commercial case exists in the low-pressure regime**, but argued the physics favours it (high-pressure saturation is their weak zone, low pressure their strong zone) and that range can be re-tuned by changing the gain resistor. Two engineering unknowns remain open: a **soft, non-planar silicone dummy substrate** that deforms under load, and **motion-induced stretch signals that alias onto the elastic-band force signal**.

供方做的是压力**感应织物**而非薄膜。两根纱线分工：含银导电纱 + 黑色压阻纱，白纱把导电路径绣在压阻布上，受压闭合导电通路、电阻变化。差异化在工艺而非化学——不是拉丝后涂覆，而是**把导电层/压阻层/基材层解构进纤维本身**，**熔融成丝阶段**并入功能填料，出丝即带传感性；基材为高弹尼龙提供回弹，结构是**双组分混料、非皮芯**。

公司年轻（2023 年底成立），威海工厂约 **20 万 m²/年**，自持纤维→面料→算法。既有市场全部落在**人体自重级压力**：人形机器人电子皮肤、人体工学椅、AI 床垫、养老床垫（已获二类医疗器械证）、汽车座椅——比纸尿裤场景高 1–2 个数量级。

这个差距就是会议焦点。需方明确：**0–1 kPa 覆盖 70–80% 数据、0–5 kPa 基本全覆盖**（0.01 N@1 cm² ≈ 100 Pa），且历史供应商的失败点不在「触发」而在**漂移与噪底**。供方承认**低压区无商业案例**，但主张物理上占优（高压饱和是其弱项、低压是其强项），且量程可通过增益电阻重调。两个工程未知仍开放：**软质异形硅胶假体基底会随受力微变形**；**假体运动引起的拉伸信号与皮筋力信号混叠**。

## Applications verified in the field 已落地场景（供方自述）

1. **Humanoid robot / embodied AI** — e-skin fabric, tactile data gloves (claimed high shipment volume among data-collection firms), footbed insoles.
2. **Ergonomic chairs** — cooperating with domestic brands Xinxian / Heibaidião / Xihao; two objective comfort metrics: **contact area** and **peak pressure**; detects leg-crossing via centre-of-pressure shift.
3. **AI mattresses** — Muse **T12** high-end series carries the sensor; logic is sense → decide → actuate (air-cell adaptive support). Sleep staging uses **BCG** (mattress) rather than PPG (watch).
4. **Smart temperature-regulating bed linen** — benchmarking an imported product (~RMB 20k); a domestic newcomer founded by Wang Teng is launching a product this month with the sensor in mass production; fuses NTC + PVDF for multi-modal temperature/heart-rate.
5. **Elderly care / medical** — with Shanghai home-care platform Fusoukang; predicts **bed-exit** from pressure-distribution change to prompt caregiver assistance (fall prevention); logs whether the 2-hour repositioning was actually performed.
6. **Automotive seats** — XPeng, Luxeed, Zeekr; dynamic lumbar/back support.
7. **Others** — pet hardware (heart/respiration), sportswear (Descente / Anta Champion ski wear), compression garments (single-lead ECG shirt from the founder's Mijia-era work). Vendor also believes P&G is already a customer on a diaper project (unconfirmed).

## The diaper problem, stated precisely 纸尿裤场景的三个硬约束

| # | Constraint 约束 | Why it matters 影响 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Low-pressure regime** 0–1 / 0–5 kPa | 供方零商业案例；历史供应商在漂移与噪底上翻车，而非触发失败 |
| 2 | **Soft non-planar substrate** 软质异形硅胶假体 | 基底本身受力微变形，压力传导路径与常规泡沫/硬基底不同 |
| 3 | **Motion aliasing** 运动拉伸信号混叠 | 假体运动拉伸传感器本身的信号，会叠加到「皮筋勒紧力」上 |

Vendor's proposed mitigations 供方给出的缓解路径：

- **Baseline-then-load** 先空跑采基线：不穿纸尿裤让假体运动，采集拉伸基线后再做标定；
- **Zero the preload** 装配后预压清零：类比汽车座椅装配拉紧后把预压力归零；
- **Shear/friction decomposition** 剪切力/摩擦方向靠算法分离（传感机构不变）——这一点反而是需方此前未考虑的维度，对勒痕/压疮（剪切力为主因）评估价值高。
