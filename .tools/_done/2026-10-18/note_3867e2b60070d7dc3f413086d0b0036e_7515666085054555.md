# Polyolefin Hot Melt Adhesive — Application & Process Review with Henkel（聚烯烃胶应用及工艺探讨·汉高技术交流）

## Summary 摘要

Technical exchange between the hygiene R&D team and Henkel's adhesive team on whether polyolefin hot melt can replace Construction Glue today and core glue tomorrow. The certification route is already running at Caishan/Softex (add-on substitution for Construction Glue, promising data, consumer study still pending), while the bigger prize is the core: 2–3 g polyolefin instead of 4–5 g petroleum-based glue, motivated mainly by softness and odour rather than price. The blocking issues are all process-side: perforated Kevlar belts are expensive and get contaminated by dripping, intermittent spray strings, and atomization is not a free lunch — finer atomization buys coverage at the cost of peel strength. 一句话：材料路线已跑通认证，工艺路线（滴胶、拖尾、雾化-力值权衡）才是真正的关口。

## Core glue economics 芯体胶经济性

- Current petroleum-based core glue: **4–5 g**; target polyolefin **2–3 g**.
- Density effect: polyolefin ≈0.85, i.e. **4–8% (avg ~5%) lighter** than rubber-based → same volume, less mass.
- Benefits named by the customer, in priority order: **softness 手感 > odour 气味 > cost 成本**.
- Mechanism: core bonding is **adhesion to nonwoven** rather than cohesion, which is why glue mass can be cut.
- Constraint: a dedicated polyolefin glue tank adds SKU complexity at the plant — judged worth trying.

## Process concerns 工艺关切

### 1. Belt contamination 负压箱皮带污染

- Perforated belt required for vacuum; Kevlar reinforcement for life → hole-pitching accuracy, burrs, strict alignment; **very few qualified suppliers**.
- Cleaning demands silicone coating on top of that → `silicon coating + Kevlar + hole pitching` triple constraint drives price up.
- Reference: ~75 cm circumference Conwell belt ≈ **RMB 15,000–18,000**; NA replaces every **2–3 weeks** instead of cleaning.
- Consequence of dripping: plugged holes → vacuum loss → product may fly off at speed; also contaminates the outer cover.

### 2. Dripping / stringing 滴胶与拖尾

- **Continuous spray**: essentially clean, including start/stop.
- **Intermittent spray**: strings — gun cycling ~**3000+/min**, tail behaviour tied to nozzle/tooth design; at line stop the **gear pump loses pressure and speed collapses**, so polyolefin drips onto the belt.
- Root cause per Henkel: polyolefin is viscoelastically more elastic → cleaner break-off is hard.

### 3. Atomization vs bond strength 雾化与力值

- Why: core is ~100% SAP underneath; 0.25–0.4 mm irregular particles; the current two-step glue application (glue on nonwoven → SAP drop → glue on top) leaves microscopically uncoated zones.
- Goal: envelop SAP as it drops, forming a **3D network structure**.
- Henkel response: pressurised atomization works for polyolefin and heating + pressure widens the spray, **but** atomization is non-linear with bond strength — over-sprayed glue means less mass per filament and lower peel at some points; **no SAP-core validation yet**.
- Rubber vs polyolefin: no fundamental difference in atomizability; polyolefin's edge is density.

### 4. Equipment 设备

- High-pressure guns (Europe): **0.018 in → 0.012 in** orifice, better olefin pattern; China avoids small orifices due to **clogging**.
- Poor-pattern culprits seen in China: ITW Omega guns, wide-cavity equipment, badly maintained lines; Europe runs mostly **Nordson**.
