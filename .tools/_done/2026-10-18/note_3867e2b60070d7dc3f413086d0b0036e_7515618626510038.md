# Probiotic/Prebiotic Program — Compounding & Screen-Printing Review（益生菌-益生元项目·复配体系与丝网印刷工艺讨论）

## Summary 摘要

A working session on how the Skincare platform's actives (Nutrino/Nutro series and Biocare) actually land on a diaper. Two program lines are kept separate — Nutro as an aqueous platform, Biocare with both water- and oil-soluble targets — and both are blocked at the same place: not the actives, but (a) compounding the actives into a system that can run on a machine, and (b) getting that system onto an air-through topsheet with a visible, durable pattern. Screen printing works in the lab and fails at scale; the immediate plan is to inspect a rotary-screen candidate near Guangzhou/Foshan. 核心判断：卡点不在活性物，而在「复配成可上机的体系」与「上布后能可视化且能量产」这两段。

## Program structure 项目结构

| Line | System | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Nutro | Aqueous / water-soluble platform | Active screening essentially complete; now in raw-material validation; not yet landed on diaper; superiority vs Nutro three unproven |
| Biocare | Water- and oil-soluble targets | Candidate bank built, some suppliers contacted, final selection open |

- Why separated: the two have different **supportive logics**, so they are managed as two projects.
- Scope correction: Nutro originally aimed at aqueous + oily + emulsion, judged "too broad" → narrowed to water-soluble first.
- Reasoning order (reverse logic 倒推): **active → system properties → process → if process infeasible, re-open the active**. Line 1 (active selection) is not yet closed.

## Compounding 复配

- **Definition**: mixing components uniformly so that both the functional requirement and the downstream process requirement are met; simple systems only need stirring, complex ones need high-shear homogenisation and reactors — hence dedicated compounders.
- **Industry reality**: specialist compounders mostly run screen printing, release paper or slurry as their main business; compounding is a side capability — they do not survive on compounding alone.
- **Make-to-use 现配现用**: with no preservative, a batch sediments and generates gas after about a month; plant scheduling must match the third party's batching window, and that capability is currently **unassessed** (flagged risk).
- **Raw-material supplier ≠ compounder**: raw suppliers recommend formulas from a cosmetics standpoint and often cannot compound; landing on a diaper needs separate validation.
- **Cabot case**: formula is out, supply and process fine, but the system **cannot be visualised well on fabric**. Water-soluble, plant extract can be added on site; business model is slurry/base formula by grade at **1–3%** add-on; **no safety certification yet** (screening only), to be submitted once the final formula is fixed.
- Other cases: 飞歇 supplied an oily formula for Nutro three (customer added ZnO) but is not a manufacturer; 连装's formula was never safety-certified and, being water-soluble, let the customer add plant extracts itself — it earns nothing beyond the formula.

## Screen printing — the current bottleneck 丝网印刷卡点

| Item | Finding |
|---|---|
| Lab result | Flat-screen printing visualises clearly after water loss (video demo), but the volume is far too small |
| YanJan failure | No screen-printing equipment — used transfer printing (transfer roll → anilox → fabric) carrying only **~1500 units**, far below screen |
| Direction | Looking for **rotary screen printing** (faster, production-compatible); a candidate plant near Guangzhou/Foshan to be visited the next day |
| Base fabric | Current samples on **SMS**; air-through is bulky → worse. 0.8D / 1D / 1.2D tried, **finer denier = better wetting/visualisation** |
| Mechanism | Paste (with functional/colorant components, e.g. blue-tube titania) is printed through the screen; the treated zone is hydrophilic, so wetting morphology changes when wet → visual effect |

## Supplier screening 供应商盘点

- Existing verified skincare-delivery pool is thin (NBC named); most others are self-sourced and their later qualification is doubtful.
- From 8–9 raw materials only **two** qualified: **BASF** (functional actives, cooperative, complete documentation, could support process validation with a full system — priority partner) and **Sappi** (trader; can procure, no compounding capability).
- A new sourcing round covering **4 additional suppliers** has been triggered; compounding-supplier development continues in parallel.
- Open question (raised by the customer): among all suppliers reviewed in the Pro/touch round, which can supply the target active **and** compound it?
