#   Composition of Urine

Product of filtration of blood through
glomeruli in the kidney

Volume and composition are highly variable

Normal volume:

* 20mL / kg body wt / day

Composition:

* Water

* Salts and bile salts

* Amino acids and protein catabolites

* Other surfactants

#   Composition of Fecal Fluids (BM)

#   Composition:

* Highly particulate (bacteria and bacterial debris)

* Fluid component contains salts, proteins, lipids and carbohydrates.

* Mucin is usually present at moderate to high concentrations

#   Key chemistry – POME Infants*

* Carbohydrates: 7.2 (1.6-12.4)%

* Protein: 3.52 (0.99-6.02)%

* Fat: 1.45 (0.64-4.9)%

* Ash/Salt: 0.76 (0.43-1.48)%

* Moisture 87.11 (79.70-93.54)%

#   Composition of Menses

![Oligosaccharides\n\nProtein core\n\nDisulfide Bonds\n](1/parseimages/figure3797c8abfd0141b9915daa3bdc69755a_0_1.png)
Mucin : Large multimeric glycoprotein -- up to ~20,000,000 MW

Highly variable fluid composed of red blood cells, plasma, tissue fragments and mucus

Red blood cells range from 17% to 45%

White blood cells from 7.2 to 44 thousand/uL

Protein concentration from 1.7% to 15%

Mucus – highly variable

* [mucin] ranges from 0 to ~2%

* Mucin isoforms: Muc5b, Muc5ac, Muc16

Viscosity and elasticity – highly variable

pH range: 5.0 to 8.6 (mostly 6.4 to 7.8)

#   Composition of Sweat

Maximum rate of production:
~30mL/min

Composition:

* 99% to 99.5% water

* Salts: highly variable -- <0.1% to 0.9%

* ~0.3% lactic acid

* Trace amino acids and protein metabolites, trace lipids and sugars

pH range: 3.8 – 6.5
