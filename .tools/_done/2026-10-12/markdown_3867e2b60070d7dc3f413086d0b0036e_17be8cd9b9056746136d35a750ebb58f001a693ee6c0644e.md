# 《Absorbent Technology》第10章深读报告

## 章名: Nonwovens in Absorbent Materials（无纺布在吸液材料中的应用）

原著: P.K. Chatterjee & B.S. Gupta 编, Absorbent Technology, Elsevier Science B.V., 2002
章作者: Bhupender S. Gupta (NCSU College of Textiles) 与 D.K. Smith (Smith, Johnson & Associates)
页码: pp. 366–405(书内页 350–387,约40页)
读者: 王总（上海,吸液产品R&D与战略采购）

## 1. Executive Summary（执行摘要）

- 无纺布(nonwoven)之所以成为吸液产品的主导材料,源于两项独特属性——"high bulk for imbibing and holding large amount of fluid per unit mass" 以及"low cost of converting raw material into final product",并兼具一次性(disposability)、舒适(comfort)与易加工(ease of fabrication)等优势,是Ch3(毛细管机制)和Ch6(纤维)工程化的"落地载体"。
- 全章的核心骨架是四大结构要素(four major structural elements):纤维(Fiber)、纤网排列(Web Assemblage)、纤网粘合(Bonding)、成品整理(Finish)。作者强调这四项因素"Each of these factors can have an effect on the absorbency of the resulting structure",对采购而言意味着必须按要素分层评价供应商,而不是单一价格比较。
- 工艺层面,章中系统梳理了12种无纺布成形工艺(Uni-Directional Dry Form、Bonding of Dry Form Web、Airlaid、Airlaid Pulp、Wetlaid、Spunbond、Needlepunch、Spunlace、Meltblown、Laminate、Polymer Web、Advanced Composites),并将每种工艺与吸液性能建立因果链:空气铺设(airlaid)→高蓬松高吸液量;水刺(spunlace)→柔软亲肤面材;SMS/SMMS复合→强韧面材与阻隔。
- 产品层面,作者对五类吸液制品(婴儿纸尿裤、女性卫生巾、护理垫 Underpad、成人失禁垫、伤口敷料、擦拭布、油污吸收剂)逐一拆解,给出"premium diaper"典型结构(Fig.12)——Nonwoven Top Sheet / ADL / Fluff Pulp + SAP / Barrier Sheet / Stand-up Leg Cuff——为Ch11 性能测试和Ch12 产品整合提供了结构参照。
- 数据层面,章内三张核心表格给出了1975–2000年无纺布与吸液产品全球销售额、北美合成纤维发货量、以及SMS/SS复合材料性能对照,为战略采购的"量-价-性能"谈判提供了历史坐标。
## 2. Core Arguments（核心论点）

Two-Property Thesis(双属性论): 作者开篇即定义无纺布适合作为吸液材料的两个根本原因——"Two characteristics of nonwovens make them particularly suitable for use in an absorbent structure: high bulk for imbibing and holding large amount of fluid per unit mass of material and low cost of converting raw material into final product." 高蓬松(high bulk,Ch3对应毛细管储液)与低成本低成本低加工成本(low cost of converting)构成了后续一切工艺-产品选择的底层约束。

Four-Element Architecture(四要素架构): "In discussing nonwovens, consideration must be given to the interrelationships of the four major structural elements that influence absorbency: Constituent fiber; Assembly structure; Type of bonding; Finish." 四要素(纤维 → 排列 → 粘合 → 整理)构成因果链,而非平行关系——采购评审必须按此顺序追问而非只看价格。

Fiber Absorbency Mechanism Duality(纤维吸液机制二元性): 亲水纤维与疏水纤维遵循两条不同吸液路径——"A hydrophilic, swellable fiber gives a structure that is able to absorb liquid via fiber imbibition, giving rise to fiber swelling... A hydrophobic fiber, on the other hand, has only the latter mechanism available to it normally." 前者通过纤维溶胀(fiber swelling)+毛细管储液;后者仅靠毛细管(capillary and structure voids)。这一论点直接决定面材(topsheet)与芯体(core body)选材策略的不同。

Bonding Trade-off Thesis(粘合-吸液权衡论): "Because most binder materials are hydrophobic, the net effect usually is some reduction in absorbency." 化学粘合牺牲吸液,机械粘合(needle felting)保持纤维本征吸液但改变结构弹性——"the entanglement may restrict the inherent ability of structure to swell" 且 "the fabric may become more resilient and resist collapse when subjected to external pressure"。这是面材/芯体粘合方式选择的核心权衡。

Airform Bulk Advantage(空气铺设蓬松优势): "The random orientation of the fibers in the structure with some tendency of fibers to be lifted out of the X-Y plane and become partially oriented in the Z direction results in a web with higher bulk and larger void volume." airlaid工艺因Z向排列获得更高蓬松和更大空隙体积,直接对应core body的高吸液量需求。

SMS Composite Thesis(SMS复合论): "Such a composite leads to a unique structure, claimed to provide desirable performance at lower basis weight and cost." 熔喷(meltblown)提供阻隔与均匀性,纺粘(spunbond)提供强韧,组合(SMS/SMMS)在10–25 g/m²的低克重下获得"high strike through with effective retention of superabsorbent particles (SAP) smaller than 100 microns"。这是Ch6纤维工程化在Ch10的结构化表达。

ADL Density-Gradient Thesis(ADL密度梯度论): ADL通过沿厚度方向的密度梯度实现"acquisition + distribution"双功能——"The top one-third... has low density... especially effective in the acquisition of the presented liquid... The higher density portions... develop greater capillary pressures"——这是Ch3毛细管压力原理的工程化产品落地。

Paradigm Shift(范式转移论): 作者反复强调工艺革新带来产品结构换代——"In a dynamic field of product development such as the absorbent sanitary materials, new technical innovations can result in a paradigm shift, giving rise to the use of a different, advanced technology." 从woven cotton到spunbond PP、从fluff pulp现场粉碎到preformed airlaid pulp fabric,采购需识别这些转折点。

Synergistic Integration Thesis(协同整合论): "the challenge in the designing of an efficient absorbent product is to organize the structural elements into an integrated whole that benefits from the synergistic interaction between the parts." 单一材料最优无意义,必须作为整体系统优化——这是Ch12(产品整合)的预告。

Cross-Industry Genesis(跨行业起源论): "nonwoven technology has had its genesis in three originating technologies/industries: textile, paper and polymer processing." 这一认知意味着吸液产品非织造供应链同时来自纺织、造纸、聚合物三大行业,采购需跨行业比价与整合。
