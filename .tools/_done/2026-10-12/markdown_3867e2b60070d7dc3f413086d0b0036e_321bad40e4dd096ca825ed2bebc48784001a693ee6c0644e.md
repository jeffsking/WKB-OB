# SAP Research Advances — 核心概念词典

来源: Yang, Y. et al. Research Advances in Superabsorbent Polymers. Polymers 2024, 16, 501.
规则: 概念术语保留英文原文 + 中文释义；定义与使用场景的原文引用不翻译。

## 1. Superabsorbent polymer (SAP, 超吸水性聚合物)

操作性定义："Superabsorbent polymers are three-dimensional organic materials with moderately crosslinked structures that swell in aqueous solutions, absorb more than 1000 times their dry weight in liquids, and do not release absorbed liquid under pressure."（Page 2）

使用场景（原文）：
- Abstract (Page 1): "Superabsorbent polymers are new functional polymeric materials that can absorb and retain liquids thousands of times their masses."
- Introduction (Page 1): "In 1978, superabsorbent polymers were commercially produced in Japan for use in feminine products."
- Introduction (Page 1): "The resulting product was utilized in baby diapers, which increased their absorbency and minimized the quantity of superabsorbent polymers required."
- Section 2.1.3 (Page 7): "PVA is not as absorbent as PAA-based superabsorbent polymers but is biodegradable."
- Section 3 (Page 11): "Superabsorbent polymer particles are the most common and widely used form."
- Conclusions (Page 20): "Superabsorbent polymers have superabsorption abilities and specific retention ability for water or other body fluids."

## 2. Polyacrylic acid (PAA, 聚丙烯酸)

操作性定义：通过丙烯酸钾与丙烯酰胺在引发剂（K₂S₂O₈）和交联剂（MBA）作用下聚合交联成网络结构的高分子量 SAP 主链。"The molecular weight of the crosslinked PAA main chain can reach 10⁷ g/mol, and the content of water-absorbing groups is much higher than that of other polymer materials, such as polyvinyl alcohol and modified polyacrylonitrile, which provides a very high water-absorbing and long-lasting water-retaining capacity."（Page 5）

使用场景（原文）：
- Page 5: "PAA is the most commonly used superabsorbent."
- Page 5: "Acrylics dominate the superabsorbent market owing to their relatively inexpensive raw materials and ease of synthesis from high-molecular-weight products."
- Page 6: "The water absorption rate of the superabsorbent polymers tended to increase and then decrease with increasing crosslinker content."
- Page 7: "PAA-based superabsorbent polymers can agglomerate after absorbing water, resulting in hindered water absorption."
- Page 13: PAA/PVA 复合水凝胶、淀粉接枝 PAA 复合凝胶（772.4 g/g）。
- Page 20: "PAA polymers are the most common synthetic superabsorbent polymers."

关键性能参数：
- MW（单体）= 72；吸水 59 g/g；持水 >90%（Table 1）
- 亲水基团：–COOH（羧基）

## 3. Crosslinking density (交联密度)

操作性定义：由交联剂用量决定的网络交联程度；决定吸水率与机械性能的平衡。"When the content of crosslinking agent is too high, the crosslinking density is also too high, and although the molecular chain can expand smoothly, the intermolecular space is too small for water molecules to enter; thus, the water absorption of the superabsorbent polymers decreases."（Page 6）

使用场景（原文）：
- Page 6 (Figure 2B): 交联剂含量对吸水率影响——先升后降的典型抛物线。
- Page 6: "When the crosslinker content was low, the reaction system could not form a desirable three-dimensional crosslinked network, and some monomers did not form crosslinks."
- Page 17: "Electrostatically spun superabsorbent polymer nanofiber membranes... high porosities and small pore sizes"（间接相关）。

## 4. Crosslinker (交联剂)

操作性定义：在聚合反应中通过分子间桥连形成三维网络结构的试剂。文中典型代表：N,N-methylene bisacrylamide (MBA)、ethylene glycol diglycidyl ether、epichlorohydrin（环氧氯丙烷）。

使用场景（原文）：
- Page 5: "potassium persulfate (K₂S₂O₈) and the crosslinker N,N-methylene bisacrylamide (MBA)."
- Page 10: "epichlorohydrin as the crosslinking agent"（壳聚糖/羧甲基纤维素）。
- Page 13: "Polymers undergo crosslinking to directly form hydrogels, which involves chemical reactions or physical interactions (e.g., ionic interactions and hydrogen bonding)."

## 5. Initiator (引发剂)

操作性定义：产生自由基引发单体聚合的化合物。文中代表：potassium persulfate (K₂S₂O₈)、benzoyl peroxide、ammonium persulfate。

使用场景（原文）：
- Page 5: "Potassium acrylate and acrylamide can polymerize and crosslink... under the action of the initiator potassium persulfate (K₂S₂O₈)..."
- Page 5: "The type and amount of initiator used in the synthesis process have a strong influence on the water absorption properties of polyacrylate-type polymers."
- Page 5 (Figure 2A): 引发剂用量对聚合溶胀比的影响。

## 6. Hydrogel (水凝胶)

操作性定义："Polymers undergo crosslinking to directly form hydrogels, which involves chemical reactions or physical interactions (e.g., ionic interactions and hydrogen bonding)."（Page 13）

使用场景（原文）：
- Page 13: "a superabsorbent gel with the water uptake of 1914 g/g has been synthesized using monomers of 2-acrylamido-2-methyl-1-propanesulfonic acid and AA."
- Page 13: "Freeze-drying of hydrogels results in aerogels with ultrahigh porosity and ultralow thermal conductivity."
- Page 14: "Zhang et al. prepared superabsorbent hydrogel beads with sodium alginate and carboxymethyl chitosan with a maximum MB adsorption capacity of 2518 mg/g."
- Page 16: "Because hydrogels are sensitive to environmental conditions, the controlled release of drugs can be achieved by loading them onto superabsorbent polymer hydrogels."
- Page 18: "Varshney et al. used a physically crosslinked, highly absorbent poly(vinyl alcohol)/soybean isolate protein hydrogel as a dermal wound dressing."
- Page 20: "superabsorbent hydrogel should be a brilliant star due to its broad potential biomedical applications."
