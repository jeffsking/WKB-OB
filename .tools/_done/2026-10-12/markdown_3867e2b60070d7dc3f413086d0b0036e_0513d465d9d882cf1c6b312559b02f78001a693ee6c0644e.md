- 作者：Edward N. Brown (Rapra, 2002)
- 当前章节：Chapter 3 — Conditioning
- 输出位置：Ebook_Extract/Handbook_of_Polymer_Testing/reports/Handbook of Polymer Testing_ch3_report.md
- 行号引用说明：由于源文本为纯文本提取版、无正式页码，全文引用统一以 [Line XXX-XXX] 格式标注源文件行号范围（对应书中原页码约 p.39–p.55，其中 Appendix A 表格约占 p.49–p.55）。
## 1. Core Arguments（核心论点）

章节主题：测试前的"预处理 / 调湿"（Conditioning）——把试样带到可比较的参考状态。

"Plastics are affected by variations in ambient conditions such that their properties are dependent on the conditions at the time of testing and perhaps also on the conditions between production and test."
— 塑料性能受环境条件影响，测试时及生产到测试期间的条件都会改变测量结果。 [Line 9-11]

"The process of conditioning test pieces is aimed at providing a reference point for the measurement of properties."
— 预处理的本质是"建立一个可比对的参考点"。 [Line 11-13]

将预处理链条拆解为三段：Storage（生产到备测之间）→ Conditioning（把试样带到参考状态）→ Test conditions（测试时的条件）
"The conditioning process can be divided into: what happens between the process that formed the material and it being prepared for test; bringing test pieces to equilibrium with standard conditions; the conditions during test." [Line 19-26]

温度几乎总是被标准化和控制，湿度常常被控制，而机械预处理几乎总是被忽略；但在某些情况下，机械状态对结果的影响甚至比不控温还大。
"The attention paid to these agents is generally in the order given, with temperature virtually always standardised and controlled, humidity often controlled and mechanical conditioning more often than not ignored." [Line 28-33]

参考气氛标准是任意的：ISO 291 给出 23°C/50% RH 与 27°C/65% RH 两套标准气氛；23/50 为优选，但热带地区、涂层织物、校准实验室仍在用不同条件，连"标准温度"都未真正统一。 [Line 75-89]

湿度控制精度有物理极限：±2% RH 实际上几乎不可能达到，±5% RH 也有争议；最新 ISO 291 因此放宽了容差。
"The latest ISO 291 recognises the fact that ± 2% RH is effectively impossible to attain and ± 5% RH is often debatable." [Line 102-109] [Line 321-326]

温湿度均衡时间是材料/尺寸/介质相关的，标准给出的 4 h（温度）和 88 h（温湿度）只是下限；对于高吸湿材料（如聚酰胺），甚至需要加速程序或更高温度才能达到平衡。
"The plastics standard requires not less than 4 hours for temperature alone and not less than 88 hours for temperature and humidity." [Line 125-135] [Line 142-148]

在异常温度下测试时，存在"第二次预处理"：试样必须先在测试温度下达到热平衡，此时湿度因素往往被忽略；低温下还可能伴随结晶/形态等慢速结构弛豫。 [Line 209-228]

没有温度控制的测试设备，通常采取"柜中预处理 + 取出后立即快测"的做法；这在快速冲击测试中可接受，但对一般测试是"at best a dodgy process"（充其量是靠不住的做法）。 [Line 242-245]

实验室空调系统要求远超普通办公室：恒温必须"始终"满足而非"多数时候"满足，湿度控制的技术门槛和成本显著更高。 [Line 252-265]

## 2. Key Concepts with Operationalized Definitions（关键概念操作化定义）

### 2.1 Conditioning（预处理 / 调湿）

- 英文原文定义：引自 3.1 "The process of conditioning test pieces is aimed at providing a reference point for the measurement of properties." [Line 11-13]；并进一步被界定为 "bringing test pieces to equilibrium with standard conditions" [Line 22-26]。
- 出现位置：[Line 11-13]、[Line 73-170]
- 使用场景例句："The term conditioning is generally used for the second of these with the first being referred to as storage and the third as test conditions." [Line 25-26]
### 2.2 Storage（存储）

- 英文原文定义：生产成型到制备测试之间的时间窗。"The problem with storage from the tester's point of view is that he or she usually has no control over it." [Line 39-40]
- 出现位置：[Line 37-69]
- 使用场景例句："The changes in properties brought about by physical ageing or post moulding crystallisation can be exploited by choosing the time delay before testing … to maximise the property of interest." [Line 66-69]
### 2.3 Standard Atmosphere（标准大气）

- 英文原文定义：ISO 291 给出的两套"preferred"大气："23 °C and 50% RH (RH). Previously, 20 °C and 65% RH was generally used …"；最新 ISO 291 "has two standard atmospheres, 23/50 and 27/65, with 23/50 being preferred." [Line 75-84]
- 出现位置：[Line 75-89]
- 使用场景例句："ISO 291 also allows ambient atmospheres which may be control of temperature only (23 °C or 27 °C) or control of neither." [Line 87-89]
### 2.4 Equilibrium（平衡）

- 英文原文定义：指试样温湿度与环境达到稳态。"Conditioning has to be carried out for sufficient time for the material to reach equilibrium." [Line 125-126]
- 出现位置：[Line 64-68]、[Line 125-135]、[Line 209-219]
- 使用场景例句："… conditioning at an elevated temperature to remove moisture or conditioning until no weight change indicates equilibrium has been reached." [Line 133-135]
### 2.5 Glass Transition / Tg（玻璃化转变温度）

- 英文原文定义：书中未单独定义，但作为"环境温湿度对性能影响是否显著"的分界线使用："… the largest exception being if the material's glass transition (Tg) is around ambient." [Line 115-118]
- 出现位置：[Line 115-118]、[Line 174-175]
- 使用场景例句："… amorphous plastics materials can be 'refreshed' by heating to above Tg and re-cooled or annealed at just below Tg." [Line 174-175]
### 2.6 Mechanical Conditioning（机械预处理）

- 英文原文定义：通过重复形变让填料/橡胶体系达到稳定应力-应变曲线的预处理。"Repeated deformation under the same constraints leads to an equilibrium stress-strain curve being produced." [Line 183-184]
- 出现位置：[Line 179-194]
- 使用场景例句："Given sufficient time the filler-rubber structure can re-build and there is a return to the original stress-strain behaviour." [Line 184-190]
### 2.7 Accelerated Conditioning（加速预处理）

- 英文原文定义：针对强吸湿材料（聚酰胺）在高温高湿下加速平衡的程序。"An accelerated procedure is given in ISO 1110 involving exposure at 70 °C and 62% RH." [Line 142-144]
- 出现位置：[Line 142-148]
- 使用场景例句：用于评价聚酰胺等对水分敏感的塑料。
### 2.8 Heat Treatment / Refreshing（热处理 / "刷新"）

- 英文原文定义："… amorphous plastics materials can be 'refreshed' by heating to above Tg and re-cooled or annealed at just below Tg." [Line 174-175]
- 出现位置：[Line 166-175]
- 使用场景例句：用于通过重加热/退火来"修改"因冷却速率或时间带来的性能差异。
