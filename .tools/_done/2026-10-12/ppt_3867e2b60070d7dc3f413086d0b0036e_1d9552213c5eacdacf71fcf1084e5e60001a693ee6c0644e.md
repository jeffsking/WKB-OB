## Potty Training is a Challenging and Exhausting Process

![](2/Oy0YFe4o874ND9Udefofxe/ppt_3867e2b60070d7dc3f413086d0b0036e_1d9552213c5eacdacf71fcf1084e5e60001a693ee6c0644e/tmp_zip_img/f5133bfe-db39-4d94-9847-2df07c1bd19e.png)
$\bullet$ 54% of parents and children have taken at least one break from potty training

* It's proven that a lack of child engagement is the #1 reason why parents have stopped potty training their child and the #1 reason why parents take breaks

$\bullet$ There are a variety of reasons for why breaks happen:

* Child lacks interest

* Child is not ready

* Not enough time

* There is a break in the routine

* Causing stress or conflict with the child

## SUPERIOR MOTIVATION & LEARNING

Toddlers are so excited when they can show how they've learned to do something on their own. But with potty training, there's a lot to learn & frequent setbacks are common. Little wins & making the process fun along the way helps them stay motivated.

### Biggest Unmet Needs

$\bullet$ Able to feel wetness/learn potty cues

$\bullet$ Helps child to be more independent

$\bullet$ Encourages my child to potty train

![](2/Oy0YFe4o874ND9Udefofxe/ppt_3867e2b60070d7dc3f413086d0b0036e_1d9552213c5eacdacf71fcf1084e5e60001a693ee6c0644e/tmp_zip_img/05912942-d6e2-456c-abbc-6d84f9b9fd04.png)
## SUPERIOR SKIN HEALTH & COMFORT

Even though kids in potty training aren't tiny babies anymore, rash and skin irritation can still happen. It's important to keep their sensitive skin healthy and protected.

### Biggest Unmet Needs

$\bullet$ Do not feel wetness on skin

$\bullet$ Prevents skin irritation/rash

![](2/Oy0YFe4o874ND9Udefofxe/ppt_3867e2b60070d7dc3f413086d0b0036e_1d9552213c5eacdacf71fcf1084e5e60001a693ee6c0644e/tmp_zip_img/9b53f4f1-1b53-4d56-ab24-f43ec5aea286.png)
