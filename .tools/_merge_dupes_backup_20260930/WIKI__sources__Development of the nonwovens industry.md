# Development of the nonwovens industry

## One-line Position 一句话定位

Chapter 1 (by A. Wilson) is the orienting frame of the Handbook: it establishes the definition, taxonomy, raw-material dominance and market structure of the nonwovens industry, deliberately positioning nonwovens as a sophisticated, R&D-intensive sector with hygiene-disposables as its single largest end-use.（中文：第 1 章是全书框架章，确立无纺布定义、三族分类、原料格局与市场结构，并明确卫生用品是最大终端。）

## Summary 摘要

- The chapter opens by acknowledging that any definition of "nonwoven" carries exceptions, because the industry drew on textile, paper and polymer-processing practices.（开篇即指出"无纺布"定义总有例外，因其横跨纺织、造纸与高分子加工。）
- It fixes the EDANA and INDA definitions and classifies nonwovens into drylaid, wetlaid and polymer-laid (spunmelt) families.（锚定 EDANA/INDA 定义，将无纺布分为干法/湿法/聚合物成网三族。）
- Raw materials are surveyed with polypropylene dominant; man-made fibres exceed 90% of total output (PP 63%, PET 23%, viscose 8%).（原料以聚丙烯为主，人造纤维占产量 90% 以上。）
- Global market structure and growth are mapped, with hygiene-disposables the largest segment and wipes a further 14.8% of EU production.（全球市场结构中卫生用品占比最大，拭布另占 14.8%。）
- The narrative runs taxonomy → process families → materials → market → hygiene focus, locating every later chapter inside the diaper/wipe value chain.（逻辑主线为"分类→工艺族→材料→市场→卫生用品"，为后续各章定位。）

## Key Claims 关键论断

- Nonwovens resist a single clean definition — the field is convergent and boundary-crossing.（无纺布无单一洁净定义，属跨界聚合产业。）
- Three process families trace to three mother industries: drylaid→textiles, wetlaid→papermaking, polymer-laid→polymer extrusion.（三工艺族分别源自纺织、造纸、高分子挤出。）
- Hygiene is the dominant end-use, justifying the book's hygiene-centred lens.（卫生用品是主导终端。）
- PP's 63% share is the master variable for polymer/process choice in hygiene nonwovens.（聚丙烯 63% 占比决定卫材无纺布的聚合物与工艺选择。）
