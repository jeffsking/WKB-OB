# 热风无纺布

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 10 citing sources. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced in the vault is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

**热风无纺布（air-through / through-air bonded nonwoven，亦称 热风粘合无纺布）** is a **thermally bonded nonwoven** in which the web is bonded by **hot air passing through it**（热风穿透）. Bonding relies on the **sheath (e.g. PP/PE) partially melting below the sheath/core melting-point gap and re-crystallising into a more perfect crystal form**, which sets the structure permanently; the **through-air temperature must be slightly above the sheath melting point yet not high enough to destroy the core (PET/PP) orientation**（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch09 Heat-setting of Thermoplastic Fibres]]）.

The vault carries several overlapping headings for this material/process — [[热风粘合无纺布]]、[[热粘合热风无纺布 Airlaid]]、[[热风非织造 Hot-Air Bonded Nonwoven]] — see Open Questions（源：本页 cited-by 与全库同名标题）.

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

- **Permanent setting（永久定形）**: small imperfect crystals in the sheath（PP/PE）partially melt below the melting point and re-crystallise into more perfect crystals, dropping the structure to a lower-energy minimum — this is why a thermally bonded fabric no longer retracts（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch09 Heat-setting of Thermoplastic Fibres]]）.
- **Tension window is the process variable**: whether the oven belt constrains or relaxes the web decides the CD/MD strength ratio and hand（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch09 Heat-setting of Thermoplastic Fibres]]）.
- **Two-step shrinkage model 两步收缩模型**: non-crystalline disorientation is the fast first step; holding the fabric after that step controls dimensional tolerance（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch09 Heat-setting of Thermoplastic Fibres]]）.
- **SAP fixation on high-bulk air-through nonwoven**: SAP is deposited on a lower high-bulk air-through web → capped by an upper web → bonded land-to-land so SAP is confined inside pockets; inter-layer glue is minimal（示例 3 gsm/interface），主固定力来自 land 压合（源：[[Multilayer SAP Core（多层SAP芯体）]]）.
- **Functional additive carrier（功能助剂载体）**: an odour-control route applies **纳米除臭粉经油剂**（suspension in the oil finish）onto air-through nonwoven, sprayed on the **outcover side facing the film**; two delivery gates — nozzle clogging and powder drop-off/build-up on the KC line — must be solved before hand-off（源：[[中科依兰]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **Core wrap 芯体包裹**: air-through nonwoven is a named core-wrap material（要求蓬松柔软、导流性好、包裹 SAP），克重区间 **10–18 g/m²**（源：[[Composite Nonwoven Types Processes and Applications 2026（主流复合无纺布种类·工艺·应用）]]）.
- **Topsheet / backing layer development**: the EM 2026-06-02 programme targets **热风底布克重下探至 18–20 gsm**、打孔工艺力度收敛、3D 压花辊数路线（TR 三辊 vs ET 单辊）（源：[[Huayi Group（待核中文名）]]）.
- **SAP immobilisation carrier**: high-bulk air-through webs act as the SAP-carrying substrate in multilayer SAP cores（源：[[Multilayer SAP Core（多层SAP芯体）]]）.
- **Functional finishing**: the air-through web already carries a **hydrophilic oil finish** process step, which is repurposed as the vehicle for nano odour powder（源：[[中科依兰]]）.
- **Supplier landscape**: [[新维盛集团（Xinweisheng Group）]]（R2100 系列，2024 下半年起供 K-C 中国）、[[格菲林（Gefilin）]]（可比供应商梯队，与景其昌并列）、[[GFL General Presentation for KC Asia 20260603]]（13 条产线）、厦门延江（6–6.5 万吨/年）（源：[[新维盛集团（Xinweisheng Group）]]、[[格菲林（Gefilin）]]、[[GFL General Presentation for KC Asia 20260603]]、[[Composite Nonwoven Types Processes and Applications 2026（主流复合无纺布种类·工艺·应用）]]）.
