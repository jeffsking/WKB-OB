# 磷酸化纤维素 Phosphonoalkylcellulose

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 3 citing sources. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

A cellulose-derivative route in the cellulose superabsorbent family covered by Absorbent Technology Ch.7 (pp. 250–299). 定义待补（引用来源未给出明确定义）。中文：经磷烷基化改性的纤维素衍生物，属纤维素基超吸水材料路线之一。

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

Ch.7 framework: DS fixes the solubility ceiling, cross-link density fixes the swelling ceiling — both must be tuned jointly; the family (cross-linked CMC, HEC, phosphonoalkylcellulose, sulfonated/oxidised cellulose, MFC, lignin-bearing CTMP) is unified under Donnan swelling theory（源：[[Cross-linked Cellulose and Its Derivatives]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

The SAP lineage has two poles: cellulose-based (Ch.7, Kimberly-Clark phosphonoalkyl process) vs acrylic/hydrogel-based (Ch.8–9)（源：[[Cross-linked Cellulose and Its Derivatives]] 的父书框架）.
