# Donnan 溶胀

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 3 citing sources. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

Swelling of a **fixed-charge polyelectrolyte matrix** driven by **Donnan osmotic pressure**: mobile ions distributed across a charged, semipermeable matrix raise the osmotic pressure Π, and it is this mechanism that makes a superabsorbent polymer swell in water（源：[[Industrial Drying Ch28 Osmotic Dehydration and SAP Physics]]）. In the cellulose literature it serves as the **unifying swelling theory** for cross-linked cellulose superabsorbent derivatives（源：[[Cross-linked Cellulose and Derivatives]]）.

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

- **Donnan osmotic pressure.** A charged, semipermeable polymer matrix holds fixed ionic groups (e.g. –COO⁻) with their counterions; the resulting ion imbalance across the boundary generates an osmotic pressure Π that drives water in（源：[[Industrial Drying Ch28 Osmotic Dehydration and SAP Physics]]）.
- **Salt suppression.** Placing the gel in a saline/urine medium lets external salt screen the Donnan pressure, so the gel deswells — the physical reason standard saline-swell specs differ from pure-water swelling（源：[[Industrial Drying Ch28 Osmotic Dehydration and SAP Physics]]）.
- **Cellulose unification.** The same theory is used to tie together the family of cross-linked cellulose superabsorbent routes (control variables DS and cross-link density)（源：[[Cross-linked Cellulose and Derivatives]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **Why SAP deswells in urine.** Donnan swelling is the stated mechanism behind the drop in SAP absorbency in saline/urine, the basis for saline-swell specifications（源：[[Industrial Drying Ch28 Osmotic Dehydration and SAP Physics]]）.
- **Design caveat.** Because divalent Ca²⁺/Mg²⁺ in real fluids can act as ionic cross-link points and compete for the coordination shell, saline-fluid absorbency must be read as competitive complexation as well as Donnan screening — Ca²⁺ concentration is proposed as a control variable for CRC / AUL / SFC test fluids（源：[[离子强度 Ionic Strength]]、[[CRC 离心保持能力]]、[[AUL 加压吸水率]]）.
