# 表面活性剂 Surfactant

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 7 citing sources. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

A **surfactant**, defined **by function, not by structure**：*"a substance which, when present at a low overall concentration in a system, exhibits a relatively high concentration at one or more of the system interfaces (i.e., high adsorption) and thereby significantly alters the free energy of such interfaces"* — **not every amphiphile qualifies**（源：[[The Role of Surfactants]]）.

The source states the scope plainly：*"The use of surfactants provides one of the most powerful means available to the practitioner for altering absorbency, either to promote it or to suppress it"* — a **bidirectional tool**（源：[[The Role of Surfactants]]）.

（中文：表面活性剂按**功能**而非结构定义——低体相浓度下在界面高度吸附并显著改变界面自由能；库内强调它是**双向工具**：既能促进也能抑制吸液性。）

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

1. **CMC is the operating window.** Below CMC, linear monomer activity; near CMC, saturated interfacial adsorption（best free-energy reduction）; above CMC, micelles *"sequester monomer and effective diffusivity drops"*. Practical dose：*"略高于 CMC"*（源：[[The Role of Surfactants]]、[[CMC 临界胶束浓度]]、[[胶束 Micelle]]）.
2. **Dose near CMC, never above it.** *"Extra surfactant above CMC is spent on micelles that cannot wet — a direct, quantifiable overrun in formulation cost"*（源：[[The Role of Surfactants]]）.
3. **Wetting agents and rewetting agents have opposite optimum structures.** *"many good wetting agents are poor rewetters, and vice versa"* — wetting wants small head + high diffusivity; rewetting wants large hydrophobe + minimally hydrophilic head, nonionic, applied near cloud point（源：[[The Role of Surfactants]]、[[润湿剂 Wetting Agent]]、[[再润湿剂 Rewetting Agent]]）.
4. **Design the hydrophile position, not just chain length.** *"optimum wetting is achieved when the hydrophilic group is centrally located"* — a C7–C7 sulfate diffuses **>2×** faster than the straight C14 chain（源：[[The Role of Surfactants]]）.
5. **Cellulose is a non-customer for wetting agents.** Water's advancing contact angle on pure cellulose is **0°**, so a "wetting" gain on fluff is impurity removal, not cellulose wetting（源：[[The Role of Surfactants]]、[[接触角 Contact Angle]]）.
6. **Self-sizing is a real ageing failure mode**：*"the slow vaporization and subsequent deposition of the unremoved resin pockets to form patches of hydrophobic surface on the cellulose"*（源：[[The Role of Surfactants]]、[[自-sizing Self-sizing]]）.
7. **Debonding vs rewetting conflict has a known solution.** Cooperative (hemimicellar) adsorption of **ethoxylated quaternary ammonium + PEO alcohol** achieves both（源：[[The Role of Surfactants]]、[[解键剂 Debonding Agent]]、[[协同吸附 Cooperative Adsorption]]、[[半胶束 Hemimicelle]]）.
8. **Compatibility red line**：*"Mixing anionic and cationic surfactants produces a hydrophobic outer double layer and destroys rewetting"*（源：[[The Role of Surfactants]]、[[离子对吸附 Ion Pair Adsorption]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **Fluff-pulp treatment and core-layer chemistry** are the chapter's direct targets：surfactant → interfacial adsorption → free-energy change → wetting/penetration kinetics → macroscopic absorbency（源：[[The Role of Surfactants]]、[[绒毛浆（Fluff Pulp）]]、[[吸收芯体]]）.
- **Topsheet / ADL hydrophilic finish**：contact angle is the only surface parameter in the vault shown to track dryness directly — a less-wetting liner drains better at equal pore size; the sourcing spec should carry *"post-multiple-insult contact angle"*, not "hydrophilic"（源：[[接触角 Contact Angle]]、[[亲水整理耐久性]]、[[Topsheet（面层）]]、[[ADL（导流层）]]）.
- **ES-fibre finish durability is a surfactant molecular-design variable**：PE sheath adsorbs surfactant by **dispersion forces only**; larger hydrophobe / longer chain = stronger anchoring = durable multi-insult hydrophilicity（源：[[ES Fibre Hydrophilic Function and Applications]]）.
- **Topsheet PFOS/PFOA red line**：a topsheet must not use surfactants containing **PFOS / PFOA**（GB/T 30133 §5.1）— a mandatory surfactant-source line on the supplier questionnaire（源：[[Topsheet（面层）]]）.
- **Surfactant pile-up watch-out**：Stantex H215 / S6327 related build-up risk is logged as a watch-out in liner tier differentiation（源：[[BSL Tier Differentiation（面层分档与产品档位差异化）]]）.
- **Gradient design risk**：hydrophobic/hydrophilic gradient finishes involve surfactants and silicone repellents — same risk class as spunbond; assess penetration uniformity and total residual（源：[[亲水-疏水梯度设计]]）.
- **Surfactant-type finishes migrate on heating**（thermal migration）, so hydrophilicity decays over time — the motivation for a dry route like plasma（源：[[等离子处理 Plasma Treatment]]）.
