# Nonwoven 非织造布

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 10 citing sources in the vault. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced in the vault is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

A nonwoven resists a single clean definition — the field is **convergent and boundary-crossing**, because the industry drew on textile, paper and polymer-processing practices（开篇即指出"无纺布"定义总有例外，因其横跨纺织、造纸与高分子加工）（源：[[Development of the nonwovens industry]]）. The vault fixes the **EDANA and INDA definitions** and classifies nonwovens into **drylaid, wetlaid and polymer-laid (spunmelt)** families. These three process families trace to three mother industries: **drylaid → textiles, wetlaid → papermaking, polymer-laid → polymer extrusion**（源：[[Development of the nonwovens industry]]）. **Hygiene disposables are the single largest end-use**, which justifies the hygiene-centred lens used across this knowledge base.

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

**Position-based acceptance（按位置验收）：** the CN standard [[FZ-T 64112-2024 Nonwovens for Baby Diapers（婴幼儿纸尿裤用非织造布）]] organises diaper-nonwoven acceptance by **in-diaper position** — topsheet, backsheet, leak cuffs, ADL, core wrap, waist/side-tape — rather than by process; every property is anchored to the GB/T 24218 test-method series（源：[[FZ-T 64112-2024 Nonwovens for Baby Diapers（婴幼儿纸尿裤用非织造布）]]）. **Fibre fineness is not monotonic** — US7195621B2 records that ultra-fine 0.5–2.5 µm meltblown lost both breathability and strength, so the claimed window returns to 3–10 µm while also bounding modulus at 230–650 gf/mm so the web is soft enough to drape yet strong enough to run on the line（源：[[US7195621B2 Disposable Garment Comprising Meltblown Nonwoven Backsheet (P&G)]]）. **Fluff-free SAP immobilisation** is achieved by pore-size differential + hydroentanglement rather than by fluff pulp（源：[[EP3085346A1 Nonwoven Carrier for Absorbent Article (Ontex)]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

Nonwovens are the load-bearing substrate of every hygiene article layer in this vault: **topsheet（[[Topsheet（面层）]]）**, **ADL（[[ADL（导流层）]]）**, core wrap / SAP carrier（[[Hydroentangled Nonwoven Carrier for SAP Immobilization（水刺复合 SAP 固定载体）]]）, backsheet（[[Film-Free Backsheet（无膜背层）]]、[[Meltblown Nonwoven（熔喷非织造布）]]）and elastic/laminates. The dominant hygiene process families — **[[热风无纺布]]（air-through）**, **[[纺粘 Spunbond]]**, **[[熔喷 Meltblown]]**, **[[水刺 Hydroentanglement]]** and their composites（[[SMS 纺粘熔喷复合]]、[[SMS复合结构]]）— draw on **[[双组分纤维 Bicomponent]]** low-melting sheath fibres for thermal bonding. The PP resin → nonwoven process → delivered fabric property chain, with numbers, is the selection basis for line and resin (see [[Development of the nonwovens industry]]、[[Bicomponent Fibre Technology Development Trends]]).
