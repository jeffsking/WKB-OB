  <p id="#js_content &gt; h1:nth-child(2)">Hyosung Launches Bio-based Spandex for Hygiene Products</p>

  <p id="#js_content &gt; h1:nth-child(3)">晓星推出用于卫生用品的生物基氨纶</p>

  <p id="#js_content &gt; p:nth-child(4)">Manufacturer&#39;s $1 billion investment establishes the world&#39;s first end-to-end sugarcane-to-spandex production system within a single location</p>

  <p id="#js_content &gt; p:nth-child(5)">制造商投资10亿美元，在同一地点建立了全球首个从甘蔗到氨纶的全流程一体化生产系统。</p>

  <image id="#js_content &gt; section:nth-child(6)" src="https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_jpg/Be7DjJyR056Uu7uPdp7QVmicHvDLUqNVQkicMSNrpfM9bejibXHXQmiceVwSsjWVrrsLnTHu5GhhfttQbjXRwM8yvg2BKA3kibIu5cFEDcu4U2rs/640?wx_fmt=jpeg&amp;from=appmsg#imgIndex=1" alt="&#xA;"></image>

  <p id="#js_content &gt; p:nth-child(7)">Spandex manufacturer Hyosung TNC is advancing the development of sugarcane-based BIO spandex for baby diapers and personal hygiene products. This is being developed through its integrated bio-based production system in Phu My II Industrial Park, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. It is being produced at the new facility using Bio-BDO (1,4-Butanediol) and forms part of the company’s broader long-term bio-based materials platform.</p>

  <p id="#js_content &gt; p:nth-child(8)">氨纶制造商晓星Hyosung TNC正在推进用于婴儿纸尿裤和个人卫生用品的甘蔗基 BIO 氨纶的开发。该项目通过其位于越南胡志明市富美二期工业区（Phu My II Industrial Park）的一体化生物基生产系统进行开发。该产品在该新工厂内使用生物基 BDO（1,4-丁二醇）进行生产，是公司更广泛的长期生物基材料平台的一部分。</p>

  <p id="#js_content &gt; p:nth-child(9)">The development marks an important step in Hyosung’s strategy to move beyond fiber supply and build a more integrated bio-based value chain serving both the hygiene and textile industries.</p>

  <p id="#js_content &gt; p:nth-child(10)">这一发展标志着晓星战略迈出了重要一步，即超越单纯的纤维供应，构建一个服务于卫生和纺织行业的、更具一体化的生物基价值链。</p>

  <p id="#js_content &gt; p:nth-child(11)">The $1 billion investment establishes the world’s first end-to-end sugarcane-to-spandex production system within a single location. Hyosung owns and manages the entire value chain, from feedstock fermentation to finished fiber. This integrated approach ensures complete transparency of the supply chain, giving hygiene manufacturers total supply security, full traceability and certified bio-based content; all without compromising performance. The goal is to support the gradual scale-up of lower-impact alternatives over time. This initiative is built on a foundation of industry collaboration, as working across sectors is essential to drive the innovation required for a more sustainable future.</p>

  <p id="#js_content &gt; p:nth-child(12)">这笔 10 亿美元的投资在单一地点建立了全球首个从甘蔗到氨纶的全流程一体化生产系统。从原料发酵到成品纤维，晓星拥有并管理着整个价值链。这种一体化的方法确保了供应链的完全透明，为卫生用品制造商提供了绝对的供应安全、全流程可追溯性以及经认证的生物基含量；且这一切均未以牺牲产品性能为代价。其目标是支持低冲击（环保型）替代品随着时间的推移逐步扩大规模。这一举措建立在行业合作的基础之上，因为跨行业的协同对于推动更具可持续性未来所需的创新至关重要。</p>

  <p id="#js_content &gt; p:nth-child(13)">Spandex is a critical ingredient in everyday hygiene products, providing the elastic cuffs and waistbands in baby diapers, period care and incontinence products to help deliver fit, comfort and leak protection. Hyosung’s bio-based spandex has been developed to deliver the same stretch, recovery and softness as conventional alternatives, creating a potential pathway for brands seeking to reduce fossil-based inputs.</p>

  <p id="#js_content &gt; p:nth-child(14)">氨纶是日常卫生用品中的关键原料，为婴儿纸尿裤、经期护理和失禁用品提供弹性腿圕（橡筋）和腰围，以确保贴身、舒适和防漏。晓星的生物基氨纶在开发上旨在提供与传统替代品相同的拉伸、回复力和柔软度，为寻求减少化石原料投入的品牌商开辟了一条潜在路径。</p>

  <p id="#js_content &gt; p:nth-child(15)">Hyosung BIO spandex is chemically identical to conventional fossil-derived spandex. Hygiene manufacturers can run it on existing lines with no modifications to equipment, production processes or converting. This makes it easier for brands and OEMs to begin shifting elastics in key SKUs, such as diapers, without major re-engineering.</p>

  <p id="#js_content &gt; p:nth-child(16)">晓星 BIO 氨纶在化学结构上与传统的化石来源氨纶完全相同。卫生用品制造商可以在现有的生产线上运行该产品，无需对设备、生产工艺或加工进行任何调整。这使得品牌商和原始设备制造商（OEM）能够更轻松地在纸尿裤等核心 SKU 中开始更换弹性材料，而无需进行重大的技术改造。</p>

  <p id="#js_content &gt; p:nth-child(17)">The facility and its products hold International Sustainability and Carbon Certification Plus (ISCC PLUS), SGS EEPS certification for 70% bio-based content and OEKO-TEX Standard 100 safety approval, providing the documentation and chain of custody hygiene brands need to substantiate bio-based claims. Hyosung is also pursuing USDA Bio Preferred status for Bio-BDO, targeted for the third quarter of 2026.</p>

  <p id="#js_content &gt; p:nth-child(18)">该工厂及其产品已获得国际可持续发展与碳认证（ISCC PLUS）、针对 70% 生物基含量的 SGS EEPS 认证以及 OEKO-TEX Standard 100 安全认证，为卫生用品品牌商提供了证实其生物基声明所需的文件和产销监管链记录。晓星还在为生物基 BDO 申请美国农业部（USDA）的 Bio Preferred（生物基优先）认证，目标定于 2026 年第三季度。</p>

  <p id="#js_content &gt; p:nth-child(19)">The Vietnam facility has an initial annual capacity of 50,000 tons of Bio-BDO, with infrastructure in place to support further scaling-up to 200,000 tons per annum as the market for bio-based fiber develops. Unlike conventional BDO, which is derived from coal-based feedstocks, Hyosung’s Bio-BDO utilizes proven fermentation technology from U.S. partner Geno to convert sugars from sugarcane directly into 1,4-Butanediol.</p>

  <p id="#js_content &gt; p:nth-child(20)">该越南工厂的生物基 BDO 初始年产能为 5 万吨，并且基础设施已具备后续扩产至年产 20 万吨的能力，以配合生物基纤维市场的发展。传统的 BDO 来源于煤炭基原料，与之不同的是，晓星的生物基 BDO 采用了其美国合作伙伴 Geno 经过验证的发酵技术，直接将甘蔗中的糖分转化为 1,4-丁二醇。</p>

  <p id="#js_content &gt; p:nth-child(21)">All sugarcane is sourced from Brazil and verified under the VIVE Sustainable Supply Programme, which ensures rigorous sustainability and traceability standards are met from field to factory gate.</p>

  <p id="#js_content &gt; p:nth-child(22)">所有甘蔗均源自巴西，并经过 VIVE 可持续供应计划的验证，确保了从农田到工厂大门均符合严格的可持续发展和可追溯性标准。</p>

  <p id="#js_content &gt; p:nth-child(23)">“The bio business will become a core pillar of Hyosung’s strategy for the next 100 years,” says Hyun-Joon Cho, chairman of Hyosung. “We will strengthen our global market presence based on sustainable biomaterials.”</p>

  <p id="#js_content &gt; p:nth-child(24)">“生物业务将成为晓星未来 100 年战略的核心支柱，”晓星集团董事长赵显俊（Hyun-Joon Cho）表示，“我们将基于可持续生物材料，加强我们的全球市场地位。”</p>

  <p id="#js_content &gt; p:nth-child(25)">Simon Whitmarsh-Knight, Hyosung’s marketing and sustainability director, adds: “By establishing the world’s only fully integrated industrial-scale production system from bio-based BDO to bio-based spandex, we are ensuring reliable production and supply stability. By connecting Bio-BDO with downstream spandex production, we are creating a more transparent and resilient platform that can help customers explore lower-impact alternatives over time.”</p>

  <p id="#js_content &gt; p:nth-child(26)">晓星营销与可持续发展总监 Simon Whitmarsh-Knight 补充道：“通过建立全球唯一的、从生物基 BDO 到生物基氨纶的全一体化工业化规模生产系统，我们确保了可靠的生产和供应稳定性。通过将生物基 BDO 与下游氨纶生产相连接，我们正在打造一个更透明、更具韧性的平台，能够帮助客户随着时间的推移不断探索低冲击的替代方案。”</p>

  <p id="#js_content &gt; p:nth-child(27)">“We have consistently heard from customers about the need for a bio-based stretch solution. Now, we are able to offer not only the performance they expect, but also a story that brands can speak directly to their consumers,” says Scott Blackadar, Hyosung TNC Corporation personal hygiene global leader.</p>

  <p id="#js_content &gt; p:nth-child(28)">“我们不断从客户那里听到对生物基弹力解决方案的需求。现在，我们不仅能提供他们期望的性能，还能提供一个品牌可以直接向消费者讲述的故事，”晓星天禧公司个人卫生全球负责人 Scott Blackadar 表示。</p>

  <p id="#js_content &gt; p:nth-child(29)">The same integrated bio-based platform also underpins Hyosung’s work in fashion and activewear categories, including hosiery, leggings, underwear, swimwear and activewear. This is where the company is following a similar pathway of building industrial-scale infrastructure and preparing for wider commercial adoption over time.</p>

  <p id="#js_content &gt; p:nth-child(30)">相同的一体化生物基平台同样支撑着晓星在时尚和运动服饰领域的努力，包括袜类、紧身裤、内衣、泳衣和运动服。在这些领域，公司正遵循相似的路径，即建设工业化规模的基础设施，并为随着时间推移实现更广泛的商业化应用做准备。</p>

  <p id="#js_content &gt; p:nth-child(31)">Hyosung recently unveiled its bio-spandex developments at the Global Fashion Summit on May 5-6 in Copenhagen, where it highlighted how the same sugarcane-based technology can support future material transition across both hygiene and fashion categories. Bio-based materials are also emerging as a key focus across hygiene industry platforms such as CIDPEX and INDEX.</p>

  <p id="#js_content &gt; p:nth-child(32)">晓星近期在 5 月 5 日至 6 日于哥本哈根举行的全球时尚峰会（Global Fashion Summit）上展示了其生物氨纶的最新进展，会上重点强调了这种基于甘蔗的技术如何支持未来卫生和时尚两大领域的材料转型。生物基材料也正成为 CIDPEX和 INDEX等卫生行业平台的核心焦点。</p>

  <p id="#js_content &gt; section:nth-child(35) &gt; section &gt; section:nth-child(2) &gt; section &gt; p:nth-child(2)">本着：“资源整合、知识共享”的初心，致力于服务全球一次性卫生用品行业从业人员，汇集：行业新闻热点、市场趋势、最新技术和材料、必备知识、政策法规、线上线下论坛、产业链分析等资讯。</p>

  <p id="#js_content &gt; section:nth-child(35) &gt; section &gt; section:nth-child(2) &gt; section &gt; p:nth-child(4)">上海法迈商务咨询有限公司（简称：法迈咨询），于2020年成立于上海法迈咨询是全球领先的信息咨询服务公司，专注于一次性卫生用品行业，每年在全球范围内举办行业活动。我们立足中国，服务全球，通过整合优质资源，以专业的态度为客户提供卓越的服务体验。我们的客户覆盖全链产业，服务形态多样，包括会议组织、咨询服务、采购支持、商务活动和专业培训等，致力于为客户创造切实的价值商业。</p>

  <p id="#js_content &gt; section:nth-child(35) &gt; section &gt; section:nth-child(2) &gt; section &gt; p:nth-child(6)">我们以高水平、专业化的研发、采购、业务拓展和管理咨询服务，为企业打造可持续发展的环境，助力盈利增长。针对客户需求，我们提供定制化会议和精准推广解决方案，帮助企业在不同的发展阶段实现目标，从而拓展全球市场。</p>
