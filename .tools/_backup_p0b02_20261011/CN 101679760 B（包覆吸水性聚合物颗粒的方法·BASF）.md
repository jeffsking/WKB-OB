# CN 101679760 B（包覆吸水性聚合物颗粒的方法·BASF / Coating SAP particles with gas-dispersed solids — BASF）

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

A granted Chinese invention patent (assignee: **BASF SE / 巴斯夫欧洲公司**) on a **process for coating SAP particles with a particulate solid dispersed by a gas stream**, fed below the product-bed surface. Process sibling of CN 101641398 B (liquid-spray coating) — both are BASF SAP post-treatment/core patents.

> Note 说明：本专利四副本（`09_包覆吸水性聚合物颗粒的方法 (EDANA ERT 完整引用).pdf.md`、`10_Method for coating SAP particles (with EDANA ERT citations).pdf.md`、`CN101679760B.pdf.md`、`v3非传统资源_②OEM测试协议_9_Nippon_Shokubai_CN_101679760_B___EDANA_测试方法引用清单.pdf.md`）为同一授权公告的重复提取，已合并至本页。

## 摘要 / Abstract

The invention relates to a method for preparing superabsorbent polymer particles, wherein in a mixer the SAP particles are coated with a particulate solid that is dispersed by means of a gas stream, and the feed of the dispersed particulate solid into the mixer terminates below the surface of the product bed.
