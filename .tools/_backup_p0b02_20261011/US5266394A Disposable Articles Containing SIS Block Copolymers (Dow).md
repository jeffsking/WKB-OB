# US5266394A Disposable Articles Containing SIS Block Copolymers (Dow)

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

**Dow 的已授权美国专利（US 5,266,394，1993-11-30，29 项权利要求）**，用**窄窗口的 SIS（聚苯乙烯-聚异戊二烯-聚苯乙烯）嵌段共聚物**配一套**四参数同时命中**的热熔胶体系，专用于**纸尿裤/卫生巾等多线（multi-line）结构粘接**。它是本库 styrene 系热熔胶（SIS/SBS）一侧的**基准参数页**，也是 [[Polyolefin HM vs SIS-SBS HM Adhesives（性能·成本·供应链）]] 对比中"SIS/SBS 一侧"的直接出处。

## Abstract 摘要

Title on the face: "DISPOSABLE ARTICLES OF MANUFACTURE CONTAINING POLYSTYRENE-POLYISOPRENE-POLYSTYRENE BLOCK COPOLYMERS" (文件名所示的 "hot melt adhesive compositions (block co…)" 为文件命名，非封面标题). The polymers carry **high average molecular weight polystyrene blocks (12,000 to 20,000)** while keeping the **overall average molecular weight low (60,000 to 110,000)**; blended with a compatible tackifier resin, preferably also a secondary tackifying resin or plasticizing oil, and a stabilizer, they form hot-melt adhesives with **superior heat resistance, superior static time to failure at low viscosity, good peel adhesion, good tack, and a high ability to bond polyethylene or polypropylene substrates below the temperature that would damage the substrate**. The technical need is spelled out in the background: in multi-line diaper construction the adhesive must (a) bond non-woven/fluff to a PE or PP sheet, (b) survive mixing and application temperatures without thermal degradation, (c) perform at body temperature and under warehouse/shipping heat, and (d) have **low enough viscosity to be applied at low temperature so the polyolefin substrate is not distorted**. Claim 1 fixes the composition at **15–35 wt%** A-B-A block copolymer (B = polyisoprene 30,000–70,000 MW; A = polystyrene 12,000–20,000 MW; overall 60,000–110,000; A component **at least 27 to about 50 parts** per 100 parts of copolymer), **45–70 wt%** compatible primary tackifying resin, **0–30 wt%** plasticizing oil or secondary tackifying resin, and **0.1–2 wt%** stabilizer. The worked examples use 100 parts rubber + **220 parts ECR-149B** (aromatic-modified aliphatic hydrocarbon tackifier, Exxon Chemical) + **80 parts Tufflo 6056** (plasticizer oil, Lyondell Petroleum) + **3 parts Irganox 1010** (stabilizer, Ciba-Geigy).

## Key claims 关键论断

- **这是一份"低粘度 + 高耐热 + 长剪切保持三者同时成立"的配方专利**：常规的权衡被打破的关键在于高 PS 嵌段分子量（12k–20k）与低整体分子量（60k–110k）的组合。→ [[Hot-Melt Slot Coating（热熔狭缝涂布）]]
- **四个参数必须同时命中，不是取平均就行**——这一条是可迁移的工程教训：买现成牌号（Kraton D1107 等）而不控窗口，测得 SAFT 47 ℃、保持 80 min。→ [[Polyolefin HM vs SIS-SBS HM Adhesives（性能·成本·供应链）]]
- **面向的应用正是本库的 multi-line 结构粘接**：PE/PP 膜与无纺布/绒毛浆粘合，且**施胶温度必须低到不使聚烯烃基材变形**。→ [[Surge-Liner Adhesive Bonding（导流层-面层施胶粘合）]]
- **真假 SBS/SIS 之争在 1993 年就被量化过**：本案主张 SIS 优于当时标杆 SBS（Stereon 840 A），与更早 US 4,526,577 的结论相反。→ [[Polyolefin HM vs SIS-SBS HM Adhesives（性能·成本·供应链）]]
