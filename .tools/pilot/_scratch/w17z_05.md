#   Transporting Webs and Components

$\bullet$ Conveyors and Rolls transport materials and components from one process step to the next.

$\bullet$ A vacuum source is used to hold materials against their moving surfaces.

$\bullet$ Adjusting vacuum levels and the geometry of hole patterns is necessary to maximize control. (web width, material porosity, material tendency to wrinkle are factors to consider in design)
