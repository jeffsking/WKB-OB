#   Critical Processes and Equipment

* Dust filtration

* Filter contaminants from Process and Forming air systems

* Briquetter

* Turns dust into tightly compressed bricks for removal

* Balers

* Turn loose material into tightly compressed bales for removal

#   Spark Suppression & Explosion Protection

* Spark Suppression

$\bullet$ System detects and extinguishes sparks before they lead to fire or explosion

$\bullet$ Frequently referred as "Grecon system"

* Explosion protection

* System detects, suppresses, and isolates explosions to prevent further spreading

$\bullet$ Frequently referred as "Fike System"
