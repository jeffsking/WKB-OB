#   Continuous Folding

How does it work?

$\bullet$ The material to be folded enters the infeed plane of the folding board

$\bullet$ (Optional) At the apex, adhesive coated elastic strands meet with the carrier sheet

$\bullet$ Material runs over the wings and fingers before finally wrapping over the finger edges

$\bullet$ The folded material exits the folding board underneath the folding fingers

* Common issues & solutions

* Adhesive & fiber build up

* Examples:

* Flaps and LAWN waist band for BCC
