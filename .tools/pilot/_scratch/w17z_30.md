#   Adhesive Hazards

Burns – Contact with Equipment and Adhesive WILL burn you

Pressure – Normal Pressure can be in the 200-400 PSI range Pressures can exceed 1000 psi

Electrical – 120 to 240 Volts

Vapor – Some personnel may be sensitive to Adhesive fumes

Mechanical – Rotating Components

ALWAYS WEAR PROPER PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT WHEN WORKING WITH ADHESIVES!!!

#   Adhesive Safety

Hot Melt Adhesive and Equipment can
operate in excess of 325°F (165°C)

Do NOT try to wipe away molten adhesive

Do Cool the area with Gel Pack or Water

Do Seek Help & Report the Incident
