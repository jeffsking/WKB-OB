#   MD vs. CD Process Flow... Manufacturing Configurations for Disposable Garments

|MD (Machine - Direction)|CD (Cross - Direction)|
|---|---|
|Products are made "End-to-End"|Products are made "Side-by-Side"|
|Fabrication of ... in the same direction... aligned to the torso or body|Fabrication of ... in the perpendicular direction... across the torso or body|
|Circa 19705|Circa 19905|
|Stretchable Sides are cut and rotated as individual pieces|Stretchable sides are fed in-line as continuous webs|
|Direction of Manufacture|Direction of Manufacture|
