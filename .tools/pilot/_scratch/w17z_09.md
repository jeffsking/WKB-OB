#   Shaped – Water Cutting

* What does it do

* Cuts webs into shapes (diaper leg cutout only)

$\bullet$ How does it work

* High pressure water is forced thru pivoting tip

* Common Issues and Solutions

* Tip Wear – High Pressures (60,000 psi)

* Limitations in cutting fluffy or absorbent materials

* Speed vs highly shaped

#   Straight - Slitting

* What does it do

* Divides continuous webs into continuous webs in the Machine Direction

* How does it work

* Rotary blade shears web against anvil roll (scissors)

* Common Issues and Solutions

* Blade - damage or dulling (replace)

* Robust process

* Controlling the trim waste

#   Elastic Chopping

* What does it do

* Cuts elastic strands without cutting the web (flap elastics, chassis elastics where no stretch is wanted)

* How does it work

* Similar to die cutting but with chopping knives

* Common Issues and Solutions

* Incomplete cutting of elastic strands

* Excessive cutting pressures cut through web materials also
