# Diaper Tech Innovation Roadmap Next 5 Years（未来五年纸尿裤技术创新路径）

## 一句话定位

A synthesis of the **four main drivers** reshaping diaper R&D — consumer value-shift, accelerating materials/process innovation, cost-vs-sustainability rebalancing, and the resulting R&D focus. Good for trend/landscape concept pages.

> 说明：原文为 IMA 知识库文章，无明确发布日期，按内容（引用 2024-2025 数据）估算 2026-06，待核实。

## 摘要

The article frames the industry's shift from basic protection to a **systematic solution** built on four converging drivers. **Driver 1 — consumer value shift**: Gen-Z parents want green + smart + premium; they pay **20-30% premium** for biodegradable/plant-based and expect IoT wetness sensors (module cost forecast to fall from RMB 23-66 to 5-15 in 5 yrs). **Driver 2 — tech acceleration**: SAP moves to bio-based (bioSAP, carbon −15-25% but +20-50% cost) and functionalized; PLA fiber reaches full-chain application; **ultrasonic welding** replaces hot-melt adhesive, saving up to **90% adhesive cost** with >80% domestic equipment. **Driver 3 — cost/sustainability balance**: raw material is **>70% of total cost (SAP 35-50%)**; front-edge green materials still carry a premium that only scale can erode; Unicharm's "dry-clean recycling" hints at circular economy. **Driver 4 — systemic R&D**: cores evolve to 4th-gen intelligent zoned structures; leak systems become integrated 3D engineering.
