# IPC BCC OI Materials Development Team 2026 Annual Review Outline（v7）

## One-line Positioning 一句话定位

The build specification + content dossier for the **IPC BCC OI Materials Development Team 2026 Annual Review**（v7，prepared 2026-09-17）：a 32-page deck plus 2 appendices covering **25 open-innovation projects across 5 technology platforms**. In this library it is the **broadest OI portfolio panorama** in the WKB set — it adds a **priority convention** and a **roadmap-calibration layer** (external TRL ruler, four-phase commercialisation, six new directions) on top of the project list already captured in [[IPC BCC OI 项目清单 2026 v2（2026-09-17）]] and [[OI项目清单与优先级]].

（中文：本库 OI 项目组合**最全的一版全景底稿**——在既有「项目清单」之上，新增①优先级规范化口径、②外部 TRL 标尺与四阶段产业化路线图校准、③项目级关键数据；同时给出全套汇报版式规范（红白配色 + 字号规范）。）

## Summary 摘要

The deck is organised as **opening panorama → project detail (P5–P29) → close**, with each detail page laid out as "left-column body ①–⑤ + right-column dual 60×90 mm images + bottom full-width band ⑥ (Knowledge Base & Gap ｜ Compliance & IP risk)". Part I is a **WKB knowledge review**: it distils 48 knowledge assets into industry benchmarks (unit price, market share, ultrasonic bonding, SMS acquisition layer, etc.), a **TRL anchor table** (9 cognate technologies at TRL 9, PLA/PHA film at TRL 8, belt-core separable design at TRL 7–8), a **four-phase 2026–2029 commercialisation roadmap** and a **dual-track evolution** (green/sustainable vs functional premiumisation). From this it reverse-engineers **six new technology-roadmap directions** that become the basis of the 2027 initiation proposals. Part II documents the update scope and source list (13 first-tier documents extracted in full); Part III gives the page map, per-project key data, and a 15-item benchmark/gap table. v7's headline change is a **design-layer correction**: after page-by-page measurement of the 19 FEI reference pages the deck's palette is switched from "deep blue #1A237E + purple #7B1FA2" to a **red-white system (#DA281C primary red / #A80000 headline)**, the header bar is doubled (11 → 22 mm), and a type specification (Poppins + Calibri English / Source Han Sans Chinese) is added — these are layout conventions, not materials knowledge.

（中文：Part I 是「知识库有什么」的盘点——48 项知识资产 → 行业基准 → TRL 标尺 → 四阶段路线图 → 反推六大新方向；Part II/III 是更新范围、来源清单与页面地图。v7 的核心改动是**版式层**（红白配色纠正、导航栏高度翻倍、字号规范），属汇报规范而非材料知识，故本库只做摘记、不单独页面化。）

## Key Claims 关键论断

- **Portfolio is broad but early** — 25 projects, all at Exploration, no TRL rating → the review introduces an external TRL ruler. 支撑页：[[IPC BCC OI Portfolio（开放创新项目组合）]]、[[Technology Roadmap 2026-2029（技术路线图）]]
- **Six directions reframe the portfolio** — zoning > homogenisation; ultrasonics as the cost-down mainline; degradables counting down to parity; smart indication as visual language; compliance as a precondition; patents & exclusivity as the first deliverable. 支撑页：[[Technology Roadmap 2026-2029（技术路线图）]]
- **Compliance moved from post-hoc testing to a precondition** — Class 1 / Class 2 dual-track control + COC watch list; the formamide incident and Korea's wet-wipes precedent are the cautionary cases. 支撑页：[[Material Safety Control（材料安全双轨管控）]]
- **Zoned density is now an in-flight requirement, not a direction** — OI-04/OI-14/OI-25 all carry it. 支撑页：[[分区密度设计]]
