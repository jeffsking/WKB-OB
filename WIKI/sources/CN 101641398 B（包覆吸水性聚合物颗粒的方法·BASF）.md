# CN 101641398 B（包覆吸水性聚合物颗粒的方法·BASF / Method for coating SAP particles — BASF）

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

A granted Chinese invention patent (assignee: **BASF SE / 巴斯夫欧洲公司**) on a **process for coating superabsorbent polymer (SAP) particles** — spraying a liquid below the product-bed surface in a mixer with movable tools. The variant copy in this batch additionally exposes an **SFC (surface-crosslinking fraction [待验证]) calculation formula**, of direct interest to OEM SAP test protocols.

> Note 说明：本专利两副本（`...10_Nippon_Shokubai_CN_101641398_B___SFC_计算公式公开.pdf.md` 与 `10_包覆吸水性聚合物颗粒的方法（含 SFC 详细计算）.pdf.md`）为同一授权公告的重复提取，已合并至本页。

## 摘要 / Abstract

The invention relates to a method for preparing superabsorbent polymer particles by polymerising a monomer solution or suspension. In a mixer equipped with a movable mixing tool, a liquid is sprayed through at least one nozzle to a position below the surface of the product bed. The disclosed variant further details an SFC calculation formula (surface-crosslinking fraction, abbreviation expansion [待验证]) for characterising the coated particles.
