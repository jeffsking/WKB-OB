# AATCC 100 抗菌整理定量测试方法

## 一句话定位

**AATCC 100（抗菌整理定量测试）** 方法的标准/出版物资料，与 TM100-2019 同源，是本库抗菌评估方法的信息源。

## 摘要

The file is an AATCC standards/publications catalog listing **AATCC 100 — Test Method for Antibacterial Finishes on Textile Materials, Quantitative**. AATCC (American Association of Textile Chemists and Colorists) is a nonprofit serving the global textile industry since 1921; its test methods are international standards cited in government, industry and company specifications. AATCC 100 quantifies antibacterial activity of finished textiles (inoculation, incubation, plate count, reduction %). The read portion is a standards catalog; the detailed procedure should be taken from the method text.
