# US2022-0339044A1 Pant-Type Absorbent Article with Waist Guard (P&G)

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

P&G application publication that **quantifies blowout leakage**（稀便爆漏）as a measurable article property and claims a **waist guard + pocket** structure on the back elastic belt to suppress it — the source that connects diaper top-of-back architecture to a testable leakage number.

## Abstract 摘要

The publication discloses a pant-type absorbent article having a **waist guard**（腰围护挡）on the back elastic belt, the waist guard comprising a waist guard elastic portion. Claim 1 defines the article by: front and back elastic belts whose transverse edge lengths are fully seamed over a seam length LS; the back elastic belt divided into zones as a percentage of LS (distal edge = 0%, proximal edge = 100%); a waist guard extending from a closed base line toward an open edge, **partially bonded to the back elastic belt to define a pocket** whose complete extent lies within about 5%–60% of LS and overlaps the backsheet; and a waist guard elastic portion whose **tensile stress differs from the correlated portion** of the belt. The article must show **Blowout Percent Leakage <4%** (the abstract states less than 1%) and a Waist Circumference Force >5.6 N. Claim 2 adds an **Efficiency Factor <15%** (leakage weight relative to diaper weight). The worked examples show that adding the waist guard elastic portion at 29–57 N/m drops Blowout Percent Leakage from the 4–11% range of the comparative examples to **0.17–0.90 %**, while the dry diaper weight stays 28.6–29.9 g.

## Key claims 关键论断

- **Blowout leakage is quantified, not asserted** — the patent supplies a test method (artificial BM on a tared paper towel) and a numeric gate. → [[Blowout Leakage Test（爆漏测试）]]
- **Decoupled elastic zones are the mechanism** — the waist guard elastic portion and the correlated belt portion must have different tensile stress, otherwise the pocket cannot form/act. → [[Waist Guard（腰围护挡）]]
- **The pocket is a geometric feature with a bounded location** (5%–60% of LS) — this is the claim-critical limitation. → [[Waist Guard（腰围护挡）]]
- **Leakage control is separated from absorbency** — the comparative examples have heavier diapers yet leak far more, i.e. blowout is an architecture problem, not a capacity problem. → [[Waist Guard（腰围护挡）]]
