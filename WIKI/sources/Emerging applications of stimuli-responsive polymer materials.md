# Emerging applications of stimuli-responsive polymer materials

## One-line Position 一句话定位
A 2010 Nature Materials review of polymer architectures that adapt to external stimuli, with applications in coatings, sensors, actuation and drug delivery.（综述可随外界刺激自适应的聚合物结构及其在涂层、传感、驱动与给药中的应用。）

## Summary 摘要
- Stimuli-responsive polymers adapt to surroundings, regulate ion/molecule transport, change wettability/adhesion, and convert chemical/biochemical signals into optical/electrical/thermal/mechanical signals.（刺激响应聚合物可将化学/生化信号转译为光/电/热/机械信号。）
- Architectures reviewed: polymer brushes (homopolymer, block, mixed), thin gel/network films, electrostatic layer-by-layer (LbL) multilayers, and responsive nanoparticles (micelles, nanogels, capsules, core-shell).
- Switching is triggered by temperature, pH, ionic strength, mechanical force, light, electric/magnetic fields; response dynamics and reversibility depend on architecture and grafting density.
- Applications: smart/self-healing coatings, biointerfaces & bioseparation, plasmonic/mechanical sensors, micro/nanoactuation, and stimuli-triggered drug delivery.
