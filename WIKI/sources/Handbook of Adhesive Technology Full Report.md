# Handbook of Adhesive Technology — Full Report

> **Book:** *Handbook of Adhesive Technology* (Anthony J. Pizzi & K. Mittal, eds.), Marcel Dekker, 2003. 680 pages, 50 chapters, 4 parts.
>
> **Compiled from:** 4-part full report (global synthesis of Parts 1–4). This source page consolidates the most relevant findings for hygiene-product R&D.

---

## 1. Executive Summary 全书要点

**Core thesis:** Adhesive strength is governed by **viscoelastic dissipation**, not just interfacial thermodynamics. The governing equation is:

$$G = G_0 \cdot \psi(v, T)$$

where $G$ = peel strength, $G_0$ = intrinsic fracture energy, $\psi(v,T)$ = viscoelastic function (frequency/temperature dependent). The value of $\psi$ is "usually far higher than that of E" [Part 2, Line 5182–5213] — meaning process parameters (temperature, peel rate) dominate core-bonding performance.

**Six adhesion theories** are reframed as complementary rather than competing:
> "Each of these theories is valid to some extent... they do not negate each other" [Part 2, Line 57–62]

**Adhesive joint is not a thin line** — it is a complex three-dimensional interphase:
> "An adhesive joint is not a 'thin line' — it is a complex three-dimensional 'interphase'" [Part 2, Line 2900–2920]

## 3. Key Conclusions (Cross-Part) 全书最重要的5个跨Part结论

### 3.1 Viscoelastic Dissipation Dominates Peel Strength 粘弹性耗散主导胶接强度

$$G = G_0 \cdot \psi(v, T)$$

**Hygiene implication:** Temperature and peel rate directly determine core retention. Formulation core is designing the $\psi(v,T)$ peak, not just interfacial thermodynamics.

### 3.2 Adhesive-Substrate Integration 胶-密一体化趋势

> "Many so-called adhesives also serve as sealants, and all sealants have adhesive properties" [Part 1, Line 98–103]

> "Multifunctional adhesives will be welcome" [Part 4, Ch.50 §Future]

**Hygiene implication:** Leak-guard edges require "bonding + sealing" integration — procurement cannot separate pricing.

### 3.3 Water-Based Transition Is Irreversible 水性化转型不可逆

> "Because waterborne polyurethane adhesives have no VOC emissions and are nonflammable, they are more environmentally friendly" [Part 3, Line 11060–11061]

> "Nontoxicity, low, or no, volatile-product content" [Part 4, Line 426–430]

**Hygiene implication:** Directly addresses VOC regulations tightening + skin safety compliance.

### 3.4 Process Parameters Are Half the Story 工艺与化学同等重要

> "In wood adhesives the application parameters other than the characteristics of the adhesive itself account for around 50% of performance" [Part 4, Line 504–506]

**Hygiene implication:** Adhesive weight, application method, and open time each contribute ~50% alongside the adhesive itself — R&D and procurement must invest in both.

### 3.5 Hydrolytic Stability Is the Core Challenge 耐水解是核心挑战

> Organic coatings lose up to **85% adhesion** after water immersion [Part 2, Line 6672–6673]

**Hygiene implication:** Hydrolytic resistance is the core of anti-rewet performance. Silicon coupling agents form Si-O-M primary chemical bonds (50–250 kcal/mol) to significantly improve post-wetting non-lamination.

## 7. Business Integration for Hygiene Industry 卫材行业应用

### 7.1 Core Structure (Fluff Pulp + SAP) 芯体结构

- **Mechanism:** Mechanical interlocking dominates — "penetration of the protruding fiber ends into the rubber was the most important parameter" [Part 2, Line 71–75]
- **Key adhesives:** EVA hot melt / PO hot melt / starch-modified / water-based acrylic point-adhesive
- **Critical parameters:**
  - Adhesive layer thickness: **0.07–0.08 mm** [Part 2, Line 11904–11907]
  - G = G₀·ψ(v,T) determines "no-leak at low speed, no-delamination at high speed" trade-off
  - Small plasticizer addition (20 phr) can improve toughness **340%** [Part 2, Line 11546–11550]
  - Aging challenge: water immersion → up to **85% strength loss** [Part 2, Line 6672–6673]

### 7.2 SAP (Super Absorbent Polymer) SAP

- **Interface chemistry:** SAP particles (50–400 μm sodium polyacrylate) form chemical-physical composite interphase with adjacent adhesive layer [Part 2, Line 2900–2920]
- **Key constraint:** Adhesive must **wetting + mechanically encase** SAP particles — determines bulk absorbency under pressure
- **Formulation principle:** Tg must be "substantially below the application temperature" [Part 3, Line 16067–16068]
- **Chemistry:** Unmodified acrylic dispersions + hot melts dominate [Part 3, Line 16549–16553]

### 7.3 Nonwoven (Spunbond / Meltblown / SMS) 无纺布

- **Lamination mechanism:** Same-material (PP-PP) hot-press uses **diffusion theory** [Part 2, Line 832–837]; different-material uses coupling agents
- **Wetting threshold:** PP nonwoven γS ≈ 29–31 mJ/m² — point-adhesive hot melt γL must be < γS for 100% contact
- **Hydrolysis-resistant solution:** Silicon coupling agents form **Si-O-M primary chemical bonds** (50–250 kcal/mol) [Part 2, Line 6681–6683]
- **Toughness optimization:** Flexible adhesive layer expands fracture process zone to reduce peak stress [Part 2, Line 11570–11580]
- **Adhesive choices:** PVAc emulsion (Tg ≈ 28°C, watch for creep), water-based acrylic PSA, hot melt (EVA/PO/SBS/SEBS)

### 7.4 Adhesive Types (Full Hygiene Landscape) 胶黏剂全景

**Pressure-Sensitive Adhesives (PSA):** Largest and fastest-growing segment (44.6%)
> "Disposable diapers make use of self-adhesive strips... unmodified acrylic dispersions as well as hot melts dominate" [Part 3, Line 16549–16553]

**Hot Melt:** Core adhesive for hygiene — EVA/PO/SEBS mainstream
- **Curing hot melt** (NCO-end PU) two-step curing for wet-wipe seals [Part 4, Line 2361–2397]

**Water-based systems:**
- Water-based PU: no VOC, nonflammable [Part 3, Line 11060–11061]
- Water-based acrylic emulsion: widely used in wet-wipe lamination
- Starch adhesive: synergistic with pulp procurement, $730M market [Part 3, Line 3531]
