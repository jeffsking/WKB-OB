# MR Fiber 福建闽瑞新合纤公司介绍（KC 2026-07-06）

## 一句话定位

**MR Fiber（福建闽瑞新合纤股份有限公司）** 面向 KC 的 2026-07-06 公司介绍，定位「**全球双组分（bi-co / ES）纤维供应商**」。本批作为「双组分纤维供应方」信息源。

## 摘要

MR Fiber (Fujian Minruixin Synthetic Fiber Co., Ltd.) was **established in 2012** and **listed on NEEQ in 2015** (code **834720**), with **two production sites in Fujian** (Songxi and Jianyang) and **600+ employees**; its stated vision is to be the global bi-co fibre supplier. Capacity build-out: **Songxi** 4 lines (2019–2021), **Jianyang** Phase I 4 lines (2022–2024), Phase II 4 lines (2025), Phase III 8 lines (2027–2028); **Thailand** (Chonburi, ~100 km to BKK airport, 60 km to Laem Chabang port) first overseas line with SOP in **Q1 2026**; **Huai'an** 8 lines in 2026. Current total capacity is stated at **tens of thousands of tonnes per month scale (annual hundreds of kilotonnes)**, with an expected 2029 total several times the present level. Certifications: **OEKO-TEX® STANDARD 100 (Annex 6, Class I – Baby Articles)**, **ISO 9001 / 14001 / 45001**, and **Sedex 4-Pillar** compliance. IP: **169 patent applications** (18 invention, 151 utility model), including WO2009/051283 (polyester thermal-bonding composite fibre) and WO2014/192976 (soft thermal-bonding composite fibre). R&D: **3.63% of revenue**, **63 R&D personnel**, and pilot capability (mini-pilot 20 kg, fibre pilot 300 kg, NW pilot 20 kg) enabling **1-week fibre trials and 1-day nonwoven trials**. Product portfolio: Basic / Softness / Dryness / Functional / Shortcut / Homecare in **PE/PP and PE/PET**, fineness **0.4–6.0 dtex**, with eccentric cross-sections and functional variants (**Sb-free 无锑, weak acid 弱酸, high opacity, skincare, concealment**).
