# 会议纪要：NIFO visit - 可视化材料技术在纸尿裤/卫材产品上的应用探讨

## Summary 摘要

Meeting note from the **NIFO visit** (technology exchange, recorded 2026-10-12): a Chinese visualization-material company (**乙方**, founded 2015, R&D team dominated by Donghua University PhDs; chief scientists from Harvard and CAS) introduced its six chromic/visualization technologies to the **diaper / hygiene R&D side (甲方, 王总 team)**. The purpose was to explore applying **colour-change materials to diapers** for temperature / humidity / cleanliness indication. （中文对照：可视化材料技术公司在 NIFO 参观交流中向纸尿裤研发方介绍六类变色/可视化技术，探讨在卫材产品上实现温度、湿度等指示功能的合作机会。）

Nothing was committed; both sides agreed the **temperature- and humidity-visualization directions have a combination window**, with the specific scheme (display position, colour-change interval, SAP-monitoring feasibility, cost) to be studied further.

## 一、乙方六大技术总览 Vendor's six technologies

| # | Technology 技术 | Principle 原理 | Relevance to hygiene 与卫材相关性 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fibre tracing 纤维示踪（换新标） | Nanosphere active groups break down under wash friction / light / sebum / oxidation → molecular conformation change → colour shift (blue→red→pink) | Use-life / shelf-life indicator; EU "period-after-opening" concept could map to "optimal use period after opening" for hygiene items |
| 2 | Return-fraud ID 退换货识别（正品标） | Irreversible colour change on first wash (black logo → red indicator bar) | Brand protection, anti-return-fraud |
| 3 | Thermochromic cleaning indicator 感温显色（感悟变色） | Blue when clean; turns red on sebum / sweat / fatty-acid contact; **reversible** (recovers blue when clean) | **Visual nonwoven (可视化无纺布) already developed** — directly relevant: make-up removal / hygiene monitoring |
| 4 | UV monitoring 紫外监测（光护盾） | UV-induced heterolysis → coplanar structure → colour by radiation level (colourless → light purple → deep purple) | Outdoor goods; limited hygiene fit |
| 5 | Temperature sensing 温感标 | Environment temperature → colour change; **instant colour change on reaching temperature**, slow recovery | "Baby cannot express temperature" → visualize textile/micro-environment temperature |
| 6 | Humidity sensing 湿度标 | Organic/inorganic/hybrid material + water → conformation change → colour; response speed tunable | Humidity ≥50% (faint pink) to 80% (clear red) change-reminder label |

Supplementary: dual-function coupling (UV+temperature) already commercialised on umbrellas; cost ≈ **RMB 0.1–1 per label**, floor at sub-cent; can be added **inside the fibre** or as a **finish**; majority of 200+ brand clients are in textiles, few in hygiene.

## 二、纸尿裤应用探讨 Diaper application discussion

1. **Pain point**: diapers already have temperature-control technology but the **wearer (baby) and buyer (mother) are separated** — the baby cannot express comfort. Core category pain is **red rash (红屁屁)**, whose key driver is a **hot/humid micro-environment**; a visualization means is needed to **show the change to the mother** (temperature & humidity prioritised).
2. **Four monitoring targets** — **temperature, humidity, pH, odour**; no clear down-selection yet.
   - Temperature: skin below ~35 °C considered comfortable; 35–37 °C may need warning; if set at 35 °C with ±2 °C sensitivity, the colour-change band must be resolved.
   - Display position: outer-layer mounting involves a **trans-thickness temperature gradient** (skin temp ≠ outer reading) → chromic ink change point must be calibrated.
   - Humidity: distinguish contact vs ambient humidity; a **full-surface spray is impossible on a disposable** → consider a visible position (e.g. backsheet, humidity-permeable, responds within minutes).
3. **Usage-rate / SAP-saturation visualization (new direction, important)**: show e.g. **SAP 30% / 60% used** rather than a binary "change now". Basis: after absorption SAP becomes an acrylic-based hydrogel; SAP swelling correlates with hydrogel relative-humidity change, so adding a display material to the SAP powder could give a fairly precise readout — but the cost of doing this for **every SAP particle** must be evaluated. Alternative: a **front-waistband humidity indicator** (lowest wetting probability) as reference, plus transport simulation (1st/2nd/3rd void humidity propagation).
4. **Urine/faecal targeting & health signals**: urine/faecal load is large and better than sebum for targeting; which effective components can serve as targets is unresolved; extended idea — whether urinary markers correlate with health/disease is worth mapping.
5. **Feasibility verdict**: **humidity** — vapour permeates (module WVTR differs) and inner/outer humidity equilibrates → **feasible**; **temperature** — foam insulation is significant, needs handling; **suggest first securing humidity + temperature, focusing on the display method**.
6. **Core consensus**: **"internal monitoring, external display"** — functional chromic material inside, a synchronised visible change outside so the mother can see it; otherwise the added material is meaningless. Electronic-signal implantation is **too costly**; the material itself should fuse inside/outside at sufficiently **low cost**. Disposables are low-unit-price but **high volume** → scale advantage.

## 三、后续跟进 Follow-up

- Both sides preliminarily confirm a combination window for **temperature/humidity visualization on diapers**; concrete scheme (display position, colour-change band, SAP-monitoring feasibility, cost) pending further study.
- 乙方 may provide a thermochromic-label demo sample and a humidity-monitoring feasibility check; 甲方 to organise core-structure parameters and target colour-change conditions (no firm timetable from the recording).
