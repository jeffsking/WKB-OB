# SDP Global 控制自由基聚合 SAP 工业化公告（三洋化成 No.1814 · 2018-05-21）

## Positioning 一句话定位

三洋化成工业（4471）子公司 SDP Global 与碘厂商合同资源的**联合公告**（No.1814，2018-05-21，日文）：宣布**控制自由基聚合（制御ラジカル重合）SAP 工业化成功**——均一网目结构路线的一手厂商口径；在本库中支撑 [[Controlled Radical Polymerization SAP（控制自由基聚合 SAP）]] 概念与 SAP 高性能化路线讨论。

## Summary 摘要

Japanese press release. Background: SAP is crosslinked hydrophilic polymer absorbing/retaining several hundred to 1000× its weight in water; almost all diaper SAP is polyacrylic-acid made by aqueous free-radical crosslinking copolymerization, which yields **heterogeneous network structures** (side reactions: termination, chain transfer → mixed molecular weights) that suppress absorption performance. Sanyo targeted network homogenization: controlled radical polymerization was known to minimize side reactions, but applications to crosslinking polymerization — especially aqueous systems — were rare; diaper-grade SAP further requires safety (low toxicity), no odour/colouring, and industrial economy. Using **organic iodine compounds**, and jointly with Godo Shigen（合同资源，日本碘制造先驱）, the companies found optimal control agents and achieved industrialization; the theme "高性能化 of SAP by controlled radical polymerization using organic iodine compounds" won the **2018 Iodine Society Prize（碘学会賞）**. Result: polyacrylic-acid SAP with homogenized network, greatly improved absorption vs conventional; combinable with surface crosslinking etc. for further functionalization. Company note: SDP Global = JV of Sanyo Chemical and Toyota Tsusho（豊田通商）, founded 2001, SAP for diaper/lifecare hygiene; global rollout planned for high-function diaper needs. ※ 本文件在批次中被命名为 "NMP-based SAF"，**正文实为 SAP + 有机碘控制自由基聚合，无 NMP/SAF 内容**——文件名误标。
