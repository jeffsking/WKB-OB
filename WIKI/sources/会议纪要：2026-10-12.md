# 会议纪要：2026-10-12

## Summary 摘要

Internal engineering meeting note (2026-10-12) on the **COCO V6** project (equipment layout / forming process / procurement), attended by Bryan, Kevin, Michelle and others. （中文对照：COCO V6 项目工程决策会议纪要，覆盖设备布局、成型工艺与采购流程。）

### 1. Project plan & milestones 项目计划与里程碑
- Project plan discussed: 2–3 focused days at 恒昌 in the week of **10 Nov** or **26 Nov**; goal is to complete **PU release no later than 5 Nov 2026** and enter detailed design. （中文对照：目标本月最晚 11 月 5 日前完成 PU release 并进入详细设计阶段。）
- Scope covers Shen shuffle, Ting equipment, high-speed converter and Air-system review of the Webpass proposal.

### 2. Equipment layout & space 设备布局与空间
- 353639: due to lack of space, pulp-sheet unwinder placed on the floor; fluff drawn ~90° to the platform by fan.
- KC forming zone ≈ 2.7 m of ≈ 5.04 m; remaining space must also allow gun overhang.
- Bryan: compress the ~5.2 m (3rd drawing) equipment length by ≥ ~700 mm to ~2 m; raise purple forming-bucket locating shaft; change 龙毛浆 feed to **top-down** to save scarfing space; two-disc top/bottom layout preserves gun working space above and frees the branched "树枝状" pattern after air compression.
- Consolidate two small crushing feed bins behind the Polysight area into one large bin; move two small coating tanks forward; purple tank no longer mixes SAP (SAP mixing moved to front feed bin) to simplify the experimental machine.

### 3. Forming-strand configuration & size changeover 成型股配置与换码
- 11 changeover configurations → risk in forming-strand OD / blank / registration / engagement; key check = centre distance and nip engagement when switching between **max 420** and **min 280** specs.
- Kevin: ~7–8 specs can share one diameter; others may need different strand diameters.

### 4. Process feasibility 成型工艺可行性
- For drawing-1 process: 10% 龙毛浆 + graded extraction can produce the previously discussed "素压状" (press-like) pattern.
- New product route: wrap nonwoven around the outside of the forming drum, then deposit 龙毛浆 + SAP onto the nonwoven — solves the registration problem between base fluff and pattern of the conventional route.
- 55/50 ratio process already done; 70/30 also has practical experience and can make the target pattern.
- Blue forming-strand diameter equals drawing-1 strand diameter; drawing-1 has less pulp and no brush (no flatness concern), but adding a brush exceeds available space → switch to process-3 with added scarfing.

### 5. Equipment quotes & configuration 设备报价与配置
- **Production machine**: strictly KC config, estimate only. **Experimental machine**: order this or early next month, switch to 恒昌 self-built system, precise quote usable directly for PO; price the **minimal configuration**; light/coating gun and pioneering equipment brands may be swapped for cost.
- Experimental machine: quote for **1 unit**; feed system **3 sets**, 4th reserved with expansion capability in the lower pipe-mixing section; target ~**1000 ppm**.
- System channel keeps the **AB** scheme for maintainability; delivery system not restricted to Vista / Vista Blue — Nordson and ITW both acceptable, choose the cheaper.

### 6. Crushing & scarfing 粉碎与 scarfing 工艺
- Cost difference between process-1 and process-3 is mainly crusher power + whether a brush is added; placement angle / mounting have minor price impact. Discussed switching process-1 to an 800 kg or 1000 kg class crusher.
- Open issues: verify forming-strand OD / centre distance / nip engagement across 11 specs; re-evaluate drop angle and minimum equipment length; verify one crusher handles both 10% and 50% 龙毛浆; confirm free loan or discounted purchase of the winder; define an independent confidential manufacturing area for the experimental machine.
- Action items assigned to Kevin / Michelle / Bryan (timing, contract template, PR & procurement process, PO payment plan by 1 Nov).
