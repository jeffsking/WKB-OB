# Synthesis and Characterization of a New Family of Photoactive Liquid Crystalline Polyesters based on α-methylstilbene

> Chapter 1 (reprint of Tylkowski, Bogdanowicz, Ambrogi, Lederer, Patroniak, Giamberini, *Polymer International* 2014, 63, 315–326). Material-platform chapter: synthesizes UV-sensitive main-chain nematic polyesters P1–P4 as precursors for photo-triggered-release microcapsule shells.

## One-line Position 一句话定位

Establishes the photoresponsive main-chain LCP polyester family (P1–P4) and proves that α-methylstilbene E–Z isomerization is the switch for reversible liquid-crystalline order and surface wettability — the material basis for later microcapsule/permeability work.
（确立了光响应主链液晶聚酯 P1–P4 家族，并证明 α-甲基芪 E–Z 异构是液晶序态与表面润湿可逆切换的开关，是后续微胶囊/透过性研究的材料基础。）

## Summary 摘要(3-6句忠实原文)

- The goal is a UV-sensitive main-chain nematic polyester family using 4,4′-dihydroxy-α-methylstilbene as the photoactive mesogen, as precursor shells for light-triggered-release microcapsules.（目标是以 4,4′-二羟基-α-甲基芪为光活性介晶制备主链型 UV 敏感向列液晶聚酯，作为光触发释放微胶囊壳材前驱体。）
- Homogeneous condensation yields only oligomer (P1, DP≈7.9, 14% yield); liquid–liquid phase-transfer-catalysed interfacial polyesterification (Jegal & Blumstein) gives high-Mw P2 (Mw≈41,400 g/mol, 68% yield) — synthesis route is the key process conclusion.（均相缩聚只得低聚体；相转移催化界面缩聚才得到高分子量 P2，合成路径是关键工艺结论。）
- Copolymerizing flexible/rigid spacers (P3 glutaryl, P4 isophthaloyl) lowers Tg and suppresses crystallization: P4 Tg drops to 45 °C, fully amorphous, retains nematic phase 30–240 °C — the softest, most readily photoisomerizable system.（共聚柔性/刚性间隔基降低 Tg 并抑制结晶；P4 的 Tg=45 °C、全无定形、全程向列相。）
- E–Z isomerization makes the molecule rod→bent, causing isothermal clearing; P4 film under alternating 364/254 nm repeatedly "clears–rec overs" via POM, proving a cyclable light-controlled order switch.（E–Z 异构使分子由棒状变弯曲，导致等温消晶；P4 膜经 364/254 nm 交替可反复消晶–复相。）
- Photoisomerization is first-order (k ~10⁻⁴ s⁻¹); in bulk film P2 UV 30 min drops water CA from 95±2° to 85±2° (more hydrophilic), indicating light-controllable wettability/permeability.（光异构为一级动力学；P2 致密膜 UV 30 min 后水接触角由 95° 降至 85°，提示光控润湿/透过性。）
- These polyesters are explicitly proposed as candidates for the shell of photoactive microcapsules for light-triggered release.（该聚酯被明确提议为光触发释放微胶囊壳材候选。）
