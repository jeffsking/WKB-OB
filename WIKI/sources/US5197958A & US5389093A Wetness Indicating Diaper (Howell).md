# US5197958A & US5389093A Wetness Indicating Diaper (Howell)

> **Patent-family page 专利族合并页**：本页合并同一美国专利族的 **2 个原始文件**（US5389093A 明确记载为 "[63] Continuation of Ser. No. 861,475, Apr. 1, 1992, Pat. No. 5,197,958"，即本族延续案），依区块规则「同一专利族多成员合并到同一页，不建重复页」。

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

本批少见的**个人发明人授权专利**（Wesley A. Howell）构成的同族：给**可重复使用的布尿裤**做一条**用温度而不是用 pH、也不用电子线路**的尿湿指示——外鞘上丝印**热致变色油墨**，靠"干燥时不导热、湿了才导热"的层，把排泄物的热量导到外表面越过阈值而变色。它同时是一本**罕见完整的隐色染料热致变色配方手册**（脂肪酸转变温度表、1:5:50 配比、八种荧光母与色相、以及"必须微胶囊化"的明确论断）。

## Abstract 摘要

**US 5,197,958（母案，授权日 1993-03-30，23 项权利要求）** claims a diaper for indicating wetness comprising an inner surface adapted to be positioned against the skin of a baby, an outer surface opposed to it, **material positioned between them which, when dry, is insufficiently thermally conductive for a normal body temperature to raise the outer surface above a given threshold, but which is sufficiently thermally conductive when wet to do so** — i.e. the material at least partially thermally insulates the outer surface from the inner surface — plus **a thermal indicator on the outer surface that gives a visual indication above a given temperature threshold**, the threshold chosen so the indication is "generally not present when a baby wearing said diaper is dry, but generally present when the baby secretes waste material". The summary adds the physical picture: a layer provides **at least some thermal insulation capacity when dry and a lower insulation capacity when wet**, and the material also **wicks** moisture outward. In the preferred construction the **inner pad is attached to the outer shell solely at the front and back portions**, so air can pass between pad and shell — the specification states this ventilation lets the child's body heat escape and **helps prevent the heat-sensitive ink from changing colour when the diaper is not wet** (i.e. it is a false-positive suppression feature). The **inner pad has a thickness of at least 1/… of an inch** (the printed text is damaged in this copy — see note below) and comprises a sorbent layer, a protective cover defining the inner surface, and a wicking layer between them, the protective cover surrounding the sorbent layer. The preferred ink is a **thermochromic ink similar to that of U.S. Pat. No. 4,826,550 (Sakura Color Corporation)**, **silk-screened** as a **plurality of discrete indicators forming a repeating pattern** (e.g. small cartoon figures) over substantially the entire outer surface, so wetness reads from most viewing angles. The **threshold ladder**: profitable above **60 °F**, desirable above **70 °F**, more desirable above **80 °F**, preferably above **87 °F**; the indication is preferably a **change in colour**, and is **reversible** (the diaper returns to the non-indicating colour below the threshold). Background criticism of the prior art: single-location indicators rely on wicking from far away and can read while clothed only with difficulty; **electronic** detectors (US 4,356,818, Macias) add multiple components and provoke fear of electrocution; most systems are single-use and cannot survive repeated washing with detergent and heat.

**Chemistry section (the most transferable content of this document).** The reversible thermochromic system is explained as **colouring matter + colour-developing matter + desensitizer**: the electron-donating colour former (**leuco dye**, itself colourless) is developed by a **phenol compound or acid clay**, and the **discolouring temperature** is set by a desensitizer from **alcohol / fatty acid / amide / ester** compounds. The **discolouring temperature corresponds to the melting (fusing) temperature of the fatty acid**: heating melts it and triggers the colour change; on cooling it re-solidifies and the original colour returns — and **mixing two or more fatty acids allows minute regulation of the transition temperature**. The general composition is stated numerically as **colouring matter 1 group : phenols 5 groups : fatty acids 50 groups**. Crucially: "in the case where the balance of composition is lost through the repeating process of exhibiting and disappearing colour, thermochromic ink can no longer discolor to the set color at the set discoloring temperature", because the molten fatty acid tends to migrate out of the system — **"the composition of the three components is required to be encapsulated in a microcapsule… this technique is referred to as microencapsulation, and is necessary for putting the thermochromic ink into practical use."**

**US 5,389,093（延续案，授权日 1995-02-14，9 项权利要求）** broadens from "diaper" to a **diaper system** (outer shell + insulation layer + thermal indicator) and to a **method of detecting when a wearer has excreted moisture between its legs**; its granted thresholds are narrowed to **above 80 °F** and **above 87 °F**, it adds that the indicator may be **a mixture of thermochromic inks, one of which becomes colourless above the threshold**, and the file carries a **Certificate of Correction** (dated June 13, 1995) replacing "material" with "insulation layer" in claim 1 (column 10 line 54) and amending the insulating language in column 12 line 1.

## Key claims 关键论断

- **这是一条不依赖 pH 的尿湿指示路线**：靠**热量传导**而非化学反应指示——"湿了才导热"是本族唯一的技术支点。→ [[Wetness Indicator Ladder（尿湿指示阶梯）]]
- **误报是被结构性地压住的**：内垫只在前后端固定、留出通风层让体热散逸，这解释了为什么"体温"本身不会触发阈值。→ [[Wetness Indicator Ladder（尿湿指示阶梯）]]
- **这也是一份隐色染料体系的完整配方/失活手册**：脂肪酸决定脱色温度、两种以上混用可微调、**配比失衡即永久失效**、因此**必须微胶囊化**。→ [[Thermochromic Microcapsule (Leuco Dye System)]]、[[Microencapsulation]]
- **它的产品对象是可反复水洗的布尿裤**：因此对指示体系的隐含要求是**耐洗涤剂与耐热**（BACKGROUND 明确指出在先方案不耐反复洗涤），这与一次性纸尿裤的失效模式不同。
