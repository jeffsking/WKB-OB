# 会议纪要：新产线高速运行方案（Meeting Minutes - New Pilot Line High-Speed Running Solution）

## Summary 摘要

Online technical meeting (2026-10-13, screen sharing + video demo) on the **design of a new high-speed pilot line** for diaper absorbent cores. The vendor/工程 side presented the machine layout, the 5-structure process architecture, the SAP dosing system, and **four product/process routes**, plus the adhesive system, substrate/screen design and forming-zone direction. （中文对照：线上技术会议，讨论新 Pilot Line 高速设备方案、工艺结构、SAP dosing 配置及四种产品/工艺路线。）

The meeting did not close out any decision; it ended mid-discussion of the forming-zone diameter. A number of ASR terms remain uncertain and are kept verbatim with flags. The talk targets a line that is **not yet built** — pilot machine expected ready in **H2 2027**.

## 二、机器工艺结构与 dosing 系统 Machine process structure & dosing

- Machine adopts a **5-structure process** with two cores: **light side** and **poly side**.（5 段结构工艺，含轻质侧与 poly 侧两个 core。）
- **Three separate dosing systems** planned on top; positions not yet defined. Design intent:
  1. Solve the **"hydrogen block"** issue (transcribed as-is; likely hydration / gel blocking) from the SAP side → use two different SAPs on the poly side or even the liner side for added process flexibility;
  2. Or keep SAP on one side only (liner or poly) and reserve the extra dosing system for future development — e.g. **control granules or PCM phase-change granules** for temperature regulation / functional modification.
- Absorbent-material configuration: demo machine supports **4 different absorbent materials** (2 top + 2 bottom); production machines configured per country need (as few as 2 or even 1 SAP type); dosing systems removable/addable as required; **mixing chamber supports up to 4 dosing streams**.（混合腔最多支持 4 路 dosing。）

## 三、产品结构与工艺路线 Product structure & process routes

Base structure (layout, upper left): **core + skin-side proprietary SAP + AI/EL reinforced layer + poly-side proprietary SAP**.

| # | Route 路线 | Key content 要点 | Equipment 设备 | Constraint 约束 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **HCH process** | Add **10% fluff** on the poly side to raise caliper/absorbency; minimal fluff addition to mitigate SAP gel-blocking | Heavy hammer mill on top; fluff enters the poly forming zone (2nd poly SAP side) | Hammer mill ≈ **200 kg/h** |
| 2 | **Top VQ + pattern** | For near-future country markets: only top VQ + patterns on the top SAP, **retaining the existing blaz process**; one forming zone removable, kept as a "blanket black box" spare block for switching between poly-side forming and the kc bla process | — | **+2.7 m** machine length for blaz |
| 3 | **High-absorbency fluff + SAP** | Not yet confirmed/commissioned (transcribed "not in neutral yet"); poly-side bottom SAP layer becomes **50% SAP + 50% fluff** or **70% SAP + 30% fluff**; like a conventional 2nd forming draw, but the screen opening is large and fluff+SAP land on a **substrate (nonwoven) wrapped around the drum** rather than directly on the drum | CA mill **≥800 kg/h** (vs 200 for Route 1) | Extra **scaling roll** needed (latex patterns need none) — the only difference from Route 1 |
| 4 | **Co-development ("code development")** | Fluff + SAP patterned development; a **Phase-2** development on **COCO V6**, providing additional VQ and capability | — | — |

## 四、胶粘剂系统 Adhesive system

- Before the core roll is fed in / wrapped onto the drum: a **basic glue** application; another adhesive system follows after the forming zone.
- Previously the casec-blaz process glued the blaz **onto the conveyor**; **COCO V6 intends to try wrapping the blaz directly onto the drum** instead of the conveyor.
- The machine carries **1–5 adhesive applicators**, roughly two above each forming drum.
- Process requirement: **high temperature and pressure** → adhesive behaves waxy (**wax adhesive**) with finer particle size, ensuring good penetration into the **SAP gaps**.

## 五、衬底与筛网设计 Substrate & screen design

- Current state: **no substrate** outside the forming drum — the core wrap is wrapped directly around the drum and SAP lands directly on the track.
- Fluff screens have **very small openings**; COCO V6 requires **larger openings** for higher airflow/pressure.
- Therefore the substrate (nonwoven) is wrapped **outside the forming drum**, avoiding contamination issues.
- Video demo: the white part is the substrate wrapped around the drum; SAP lands on the substrate and accumulates **only in the open-area regions**.

## 六、成形区设计方向 Forming-zone design direction

To keep the process lean, the **COCO V6 forming zone is designed significantly smaller** than conventional forming zones. The presenter's assumption about the current kc forming-zone diameter is where the **recording transcription is interrupted** — content incomplete.
