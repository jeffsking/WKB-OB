# Ebewole Polymer Science Ch03 Chemical Bonding and Polymer Structure

> Source: Ebewole, *Polymer Science and Technology*, Ch.3 (Chemical Bonding and Polymer Structure). 章节主题：化学键类型、主键/次键分类与键能谱、聚合物三级结构观。

Chapter 3 is the **structure–property bridge 结构-性质桥梁** of the book: it starts from chemical bonding, passes through three levels of structural description (primary / secondary / tertiary), and builds the hierarchy **bond → chain → aggregated state 键 → 链 → 聚集态**.

本章回答"为何高分子具备独特性质"：链骨架由强主键决定，而热行为与物理态由弱次键的长链聚合效应决定。

## Three Levels of Polymer Structure 三级结构观

> "From Table 3.1, it is obvious that moving from the low- to the high-molecular-weight end of the molecular spectrum, members of the series change progressively from the gaseous state through liquids of increasing viscosity (decreasing volatility) to low melting solids and ultimately terminate in high-strength solids. Polymers belong to the high-molecular-weight end of the spectrum." (Ch.3, p.60)

> "we will attempt to illustrate how the unusual properties of high polymers are developed. To do this, it will be convenient to consider the chemical and structural aspects of polymers at three different levels: 1. The chemical structure (atomic composition) of the monomer (primary structure); 2. The single polymer chain (secondary level); 3. Aggregation of polymer chains (tertiary structure)." (Ch.3, p.60)

| Level 层级 | Object 对象 | Determinants 决定因素 |
|---|---|---|
| Primary structure 一级结构 | monomer atomic composition 单体原子组成 | chemical synthesis 化学合成 |
| Secondary level 二级层次 | single chain configuration / conformation 单链构型与构象 | stereochemistry, chain flexibility 立体化学、链柔性 |
| Tertiary structure 三级结构 | aggregation of chains 多链聚集态 | secondary bonds, crystallinity 次键、结晶度 |

## Chemical Bonding 化学键

> "Valence electrons are involved in the formation of primary bonds. This results in a substantial lowering of the potential energies. Consequently, primary bonds are quite strong. On the other hand, valence electrons are not involved in the formation of secondary bonds — leading to weak bonds." (Ch.3, p.60)

Subdivision given by the source (Ch.3, p.60): **Primary bonds 主键** — a. Ionic; b. Covalent; c. Metallic. **Secondary bonds 次键** — a. Dipole; b. Hydrogen; c. Induction; d. van der Waals (dispersion).

### Primary bonds 主键

- **Ionic bond 离子键** — "The bonding force in sodium chloride is a result of the electrostatic attraction between the two ions." (Ch.3, p.61). Exception in polymers: "Ionic bonds are not common features in polymeric materials. However, divalent ions are known to act as cross-links between carboxyl groups in natural resins. The relatively new class of polymers known as ionomers contain ionic bonds." (Ch.3, p.61)
- **Covalent bond 共价键** — "Bonds formed by electron sharing are called covalent bonds." (Ch.3, p.61); "This is the predominant bond in polymers." (Ch.3, p.61) — 聚合物主链的主导键型。
- **Dissociation energy 解离能** — "Recall that dissociation energy is that required to break the bond. It has a direct relationship with the thermal stability of polymers." (Ch.3, p.61); Table 3.2 lists covalent bond energies.

### Secondary bonds 次键

- **Dipole force 偶极力** — "Molecules … will have a permanent dipole if the centers of the positive and negative charges do not coincide; this arises if the electrons shared by two atoms spend more time on one of the atoms due to differences in electronegativity." (Ch.3, p.62); HCl is used as the model molecule.
- **Hydrogen bond 氢键** — "The hydrogen is generally part of such groups as carboxyl, hydroxyl, amine, or amide, while the other atom in the hydrogen bond is frequently oxygen (in carbonyl, ethers, or hydroxyls) or nitrogen (in amines, amides, urethanes, urea)." (Ch.3, p.62); "The hydrogen bond plays a vital role in the structure and properties of polymers, particularly proteins." (Ch.3, p.62)
- **Induction force 诱导力** — "Every dipole has an electric field associated with it. This electric field is capable of inducing relative displacements of the electrons and nuclei in neighboring molecules. The result is that the surrounding molecules become polarized, i.e., possess induced dipoles." (Ch.3, p.62); "Induction forces are weak and temperature independent." (Ch.3, p.62)
- **van der Waals (dispersion) force 范德华/色散力** — "Electrons are usually in constant motion about their nuclei. At any particular instant, the centers of negative charge of the electrons cloud may not coincide with those of the nuclei. Consequently, instantaneous (fluctuating) dipoles exist even in nonpolar materials." (Ch.3, p.62); "Van der Waals forces are present in all molecules and, as we shall see later, they contribute significantly to the bonding in polymers." (Ch.3, p.62)

### Interaction energy spectrum 键能谱（Ch.3, Table 3.3）

| Bond type 键型 | Interaction energy 相互作用能 (kJ/mol) | Temperature dependence 温度依赖性 | Where it matters 作用对象 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ionic bond 离子键 | 560–1000 | 弱 | ionomer、Ca²⁺-交联羧基体系 |
| Covalent bond 共价键 | 60–600 | 弱 | 所有聚合物主链 |
| Hydrogen bond 氢键 | ≤50 | 强 | 蛋白质、纤维素、尼龙 |
| Dipole–dipole 偶极-偶极 | ≤20 | 中 | PVC, PMMA 等极性侧基聚合物 |
| Induction 诱导力 | ≤2 | 无 | 极性-非极性界面 |
| van der Waals 范德华力 | 0.08–4.0 | 中 | PE, PP 等非极性聚烯烃 |

### Example 3.1: PE vs. fluorine 经典对比

> "both fluorine and PE molecules are held by van der Waals forces. In the case of fluorine molecules, these forces are readily overcome by thermal agitation … However, because of the macromolecular sizes of PE molecules, in the aggregate the van der Waals forces become very large. This, coupled with extensive physical entanglements, results in a high melting point." (Ch.3, Example 3.1, p.63)

本章对比例的量化解读（转述自 Example 3.1 的论述）：PE 的熔点约 135 °C，而 F₂ 约 −219.6 °C，尽管两者都由 van der Waals 力维系；差异来自**链长与链缠结的聚合效应**，而非单键强度。这是高分子学科最核心的认识之一：弱次键在长链上可加和成宏观强作用。

## Primary Structure 一级结构

> "Primary structure refers to the atomic composition and chemical structure of the monomer — the building block of the polymer chain." (Ch.3, p.64)

一级结构是 polymer 性质的"化学基因 chemical gene"：官能度、链节化学、取代基与异构化（isomerism）都在此层面决定后续二级/三级结构。

## Academic Context and Critique 学术对话与批判

- **Pauling chemical-bond theory (1930s–40s)** — the primary/secondary bond dichotomy derives from it.
- **London dispersion (1930)** — the quantum-mechanical explanation of van der Waals forces.
- **IUPAC three-level structure framework (1958)** — primary / secondary / tertiary; the source follows it strictly.
- **Flory polymer physics** — the relation between physical properties and the aggregate effect of secondary bonds is a cornerstone.
- **Tension: bond strength vs. aggregate effect 键强 vs. 聚合效应.** The PE vs. F₂ comparison challenges the naive view that "bond strength determines properties", but raises the methodological question of whether secondary bonds are truly additive over long range.
- **Boundary of ionic cross-linking.** Whether ionomer "ionic multiplets 离子聚集体" strictly count as ionic bonds is a long-standing debate in polymer physics.
- **Three vs. four levels.** IUPAC later introduced a quaternary structure for biopolymers; this 1997 text still uses the three-level framework.

## Hygiene Implication 卫材关联

1. **Secondary bonds and thermal bonding windows 热轧/热风粘合窗口.** 聚烯烃（PE/PP）非极性，链间只有 van der Waals 力（Table 3.3: 0.08–4.0 kJ/mol）；其熔点却因长链聚合效应而高（Example 3.1）。这解释了热轧粘合必须把纤网加热到接近 Tm 才能使界面链段互相扩散缠结——粘合强度来自"链缠结 + 次键重建"，而非化学键。参见 [[Polypropylene Handbook Spunbond and Meltblown Nonwoven Resins]]。
2. **Hydrogen bonding in cellulosics 纤维素材料的氢键.** 绒毛浆、纤维素纤维的性质由氢键（≤50 kJ/mol，温度依赖性强）主导；这是纤维素亲水、吸湿并在润湿时强度下降的结构根源。
3. **Ionic cross-linking and SAP 离子交联与超吸水性树脂.** "divalent ions are known to act as cross-links between carboxyl groups" (Ch.3, p.61) 是离子交联型水凝胶与 ionomer 类材料的机理基础；聚丙烯酸盐 SAP 网络中 –COO⁻ 与多价阳离子之间的离子作用，正是这一键型的工程应用。
4. **Adhesion fundamentals 热熔胶粘接的次键本质.** 热熔胶对被粘物的粘附主要依赖次键（偶极、诱导、色散、氢键）而非主键；次键弱且温度依赖，是粘接接头耐热性受限的结构原因。参见 [[Adhesive Handbook Part 2 Fundamental Aspects]]。
