# GB 5296.5-2006 Instructions for Use Part 5 Toys（消费品使用说明 第5部分 玩具）

## 一句话定位

China's labeling standard for **toys**（玩具使用说明）— partially mandatory; for this library it documents the toy-side labeling regime (age range + safety warnings per GB 6675) that borders the children's paper-product safety space.

## Summary 摘要

GB 5296.5-2006 governs instructions-for-use of toys for children under 14, replacing the 1996 edition. Six content blocks are compulsory (4.1, 4.4, chapter 5, 6.2, 6.3, 8.2): the chapter-5 list covers product name/model/standard number, age range and safety warnings (both delegated to GB 6675), plush-toy material disclosure, assembly instructions, maintenance, safe-use period, and producer identity. Safety warnings must live on durable labels and follow the font-size rules (警示词 ≥四号黑体). Historical note: this 2006 text lists the GB 5296 series with Part 3 = textiles and Part 4 = cosmetics, whereas the 2012 Part 4 text re-numbers Part 3 = cosmetics and Part 4 = textiles — a series-structure shift to keep in mind when citing "GB 5296.x". For the hygiene library this is a boundary document: toys are excluded from diaper/wipe scopes, but children's paper books under GB 43631-2023 borrow the GB 6675.4 migratable-element scheme from the toy-safety world.
