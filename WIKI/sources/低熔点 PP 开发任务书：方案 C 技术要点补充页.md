# 低熔点 PP 开发任务书：方案 C 技术要点补充页

## Positioning 一句话定位

This Chinese-language Plan C supplement is the source-equivalent counterpart of [[LMP-PP Brief — Plan C Technical Supplement]] and records the same sheath-resin architecture, process risks, and acceptance window.  
（中文：本中文方案 C 补充页与英文版对应，记录同一皮层树脂架构、工艺风险与验收窗口。）

## Summary 摘要

Plan C changes the development object from a single-component low-melt PP fiber to a low-melt sheath resin for an all-PP sheath–core fiber. The sheath melts to form bonding bridges while the approximately 165°C PP core remains intact as the load-bearing skeleton. Three resin-side levers are combined: high ethylene-plus-butene comonomer content, a controlled low-isotacticity fraction, and no nucleating agent. The document identifies excessive xylene solubles, insufficient quenching, a narrow heat-setting window, and inherited homopolymer draw settings as the four major failure modes. It further treats sheath ratio as a structural buffer: a smaller sheath share lowers low-melt resin consumption and reduces the need to force the sheath to 130°C. Acceptance covers air-through bonding, tenacity, elongation, shrinkage, denier, cut length, and hydrophilic finishing.
