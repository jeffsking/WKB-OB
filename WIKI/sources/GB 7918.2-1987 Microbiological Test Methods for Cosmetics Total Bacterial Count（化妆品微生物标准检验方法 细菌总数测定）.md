# GB 7918.2-1987 Microbiological Test Methods for Cosmetics Total Bacterial Count（化妆品微生物标准检验方法 细菌总数测定）

## 一句话定位

Historical CN method standard for **cosmetic total plate count**（化妆品细菌总数，卵磷脂-吐温80 平板计数法）— the 1987 ancestor of the plate-count method family still behind CN hygiene-product microbiological limits.

## Summary 摘要

GB 7918.2-87 defines bacterial total count（细菌总数）as viable bacteria per gram or millilitre of cosmetic and prescribes the standard plate count on lecithin-Tween-80 nutrient agar — the lecithin and Tween neutralize cosmetic preservatives so the true microbial load is recovered — incubated 48 h at 37 ℃, capturing mesophilic aerobes and facultative anaerobes. Serial 1:10 dilutions to 1:10000+, double plates per dilution, blank control, and a full counting/reporting decision tree (30–300 selection window, dual-dilution ratio ≤2 averaging rule, sub-10 reporting as "<10 CFU/g") are specified, with worked examples. The design — 37 ℃/48 h, lecithin-Tween agar — persists in today's GB 15979-2024 microbiological methods and downstream hygiene-product standards, making this text the historical calibration point for the CFU limits used in wipes/diaper compliance.
