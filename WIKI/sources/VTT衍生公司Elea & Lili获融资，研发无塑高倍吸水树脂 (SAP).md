# VTT衍生公司Elea & Lili获融资，研发无塑高倍吸水树脂 (SAP)

VTT Spinout Raises Funds for Plastic-Free SAP

VTT衍生公司获融资，研发无塑高倍吸水树脂 (SAP)

Cellulose Super Absorbent offers alternative for diapers, agricultural applications

纤维素基超强吸水体为纸尿裤及农业应用提供替代方案

Backed by over a decade of biomaterials research at VTT Technical Research Centre of Finland, Elea & Lili is commercializing a cellulose-based alternative designed to replace fossil-based superabsorbent polymers in disposable diapers at an industrial scale – without compromising performance.

依托于芬兰 VTT 技术研究中心超过十年的生物材料研究积淀，Elea & Lili 正致力于将一种纤维素基替代材料商业化。该产品旨在工业规模上取代一次性纸尿裤中的化石基超强吸水树脂（SAP），且完全不牺牲吸纳性能。

Elea & Lili, a deep-tech startup originating from VTT Technical Research Centre of Finland, has raised €2.5 million in seed funding to industrialisz its patented Cellulose Super Absorbent (CSA) – a microplastic-free, biodegradable alternative to conventional superabsorbent polymers (SAP). VTT has transferred the underlying technology and IP to the newly established company.

Elea & Lili 是一家源自芬兰 VTT 技术研究中心的深科技初创公司。近日，该公司宣布已获得 250 万欧元 的种子轮融资，用于其专利技术——纤维素超强吸水体（Cellulose Super Absorbent, 简称 CSA） 的工业化进程。CSA 是一种无微塑料、可生物降解的材料，旨在替代传统的超强吸水树脂（SAP）。目前，VTT 已将相关核心技术及知识产权（IP）正式转让给这家新成立的公司。

The company's first commercial applications target two massive global markets: disposable diapers and agriculture, which both currently dependent on fossil-based absorbent materials that generate long-lasting plastic waste and microplastic pollution. It is working with leading cellulose and biomaterials companies to scale CSA from pilot to industrial production. These partnerships support the company's ambition to build a new global material category.

公司的首批商业应用瞄准了两大全球性海量市场：一次性纸尿裤和农业。目前，这两个领域高度依赖化石基吸水材料，这些材料会产生持久性的塑料垃圾和微塑料污染。Elea & Lili 正与领先的纤维素及生物材料公司合作，推动 CSA 从试点阶段向工业化生产跨越。这些合作伙伴关系支持了公司构建全球性新材料类别的雄心。

The funding was led by Lifeline Ventures and will accelerate pilot production, industrial partnerships, regulatory validation, and first commercial launches in the U.S. and Europe. The round also included Ikorni Invest Oy Ab, and Baltiska Handels Sverige AB, representing long-term industrial and sustainability-focused capital.

本轮融资由 Lifeline Ventures 领投，资金将用于加速试点生产、工业伙伴关系建立、法规验证，以及在美、欧市场的首次商业发布。跟投方还包括 Ikorni Invest Oy Ab 和 Baltiska Handels Sverige AB，代表了长期工业及可持续发展导向的资本支持。

Elea & Lili is fundamentally a biomaterials technology company. Its core innovation, Cellulose Super Absorbent (CSA), replaces the last non-biodegradable component in modern diapers: fossil-based superabsorbent polymers. CSA, made from cellulose, is biodegradable and microplastic-free, compatible with existing diaper production lines, and demonstrates absorption performance comparable to conventional SAP. The material has undergone safety and skin compatibility testing in accordance with relevant ISO standards.

作为一家生物材料技术公司，Elea & Lili 的核心创新产品 CSA 替代了现代纸尿裤中最后一种不可降解的成分：化石基 SAP。

- 环保属性： 由纤维素制成，可生物降解且不含微塑料。

- 兼容性： 可直接兼容现有的纸尿裤生产线。

- 性能表现： 吸收性能与传统 SAP 相当。

- 安全性： 已根据相关 ISO 标准通过了安全性和皮肤兼容性测试。

In agriculture, superabsorbent polymers are widely used in agriculture to retain water and improve nutrient efficiency. The fossil-based polymers in current products are mixed directly into the soil, resulting in permanent plastics ending up in farmland. From 2028 onwards, EU regulation will restrict the use of fossil-based plastic components that persist in soil. CSA™provides a biodegradable solution for water retention, reduced irrigation demand, improved nutrient delivery, and regenerative agriculture solutions – without leaving persistent plastic residues in soil.

在农业领域，SAP 被广泛用于保持土壤水分和提高养分利用率。然而，现有的化石基聚合物直接混合在土壤中，导致永久性塑料残留在农田。从 2028 年起，欧盟法规将限制在土壤中使用持久性化石基塑料组分。 CSA™ 为再生农业提供了理想方案：它能锁水、降低灌溉需求、优化养分输送，且不会在土壤中留下持久性塑料残留。

"Hygiene and agriculture are equally strategic entry points for us. In both markets, absorbent materials are mission-critical components – and today they are fossil-based. We are replacing them with a scalable biomaterial," says Tatu Miettinen, CEO and Co-Founder.

"卫生用品和农业对我们而言具有同等战略地位。在这两个市场，吸水材料都是核心关键组分。我们正在用可扩展的生物材料取代目前的化石基材料，"首席执行官兼联合创始人 Tatu Miettinen 表示。

The idea for Elea & Lili began in 2017 when CEO Tatu Miettinen became aware of the environmental footprint of disposable diapers after the birth of his first child.

Elea & Lili 的构思始于 2017 年，当时 Tatu Miettinen 在第一个孩子出生后，意识到了一次性纸尿裤对环境的影响。

"In 2019, the birth of my daughter Elea was medically complex and involved time in neonatal intensive care. The experience profoundly reshaped our family's perspective. At that moment I decided: if she recovers, I will dedicate myself to building something meaningful and sustainable for the next generation," says Miettinen.

"2019年，我女儿 Elea 的出生过程医学情况非常复杂，曾在新生儿重症监护室度过了一段时间。这段经历彻底重塑了我们家庭的价值观。那一刻我决定：如果她康复了，我将致力于为下一代创造一些有意义且可持续的事业，"Miettinen 动情地回忆道。
