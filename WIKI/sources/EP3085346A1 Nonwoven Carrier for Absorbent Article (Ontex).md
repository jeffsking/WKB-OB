# EP3085346A1 Nonwoven Carrier for Absorbent Article (Ontex)

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

Ontex 的**欧洲专利申请（A1，公开文本）**：用**「梳理短纤顶层 + 纺熔（优选 SMS）底层 + 水刺复合」**构成的双层无纺布载体来**免绒毛浆固定 SAP 颗粒**。本页是本库「hydraulic 孔径差 + 水刺缠结」这一 SAP 固定路线的一手 construction 与参数来源。

## Abstract 摘要

The application titled "NONWOVEN CARRIER FOR ABSORBENT ARTICLE" relates to a nonwoven carrier (1) for immobilizing superabsorbent polymer particles (28) in an absorbent article. The carrier comprises a nonwoven top layer (7) penetrable by the particles and a nonwoven bottom layer (8); the top layer is **mechanically bonded by hydroentanglement**（水刺）to the bottom layer, the bottom layer is porous with a **pore size smaller than said particles**, and — this is the characterization — **the top layer is a carded nonwoven made of staple fibers (19) and the bottom layer is a spunmelt fabric**. The specification explains that the two layers have deliberately asymmetric properties: the top layer is porous, soft and bulky so particles can be trapped between its fibers (blocking movement in the x–y plane), while the bottom layer has much smaller pores and considerably higher mechanical strength, blocking movement in the z-direction (throughfall). Particles are therefore distributed **inside** the fibre web rather than merely lying on its surface. Independent claims additionally cover the absorbent article, the manufacturing process (including rolling the carrier up, and surfactant treatment) and the use of the carrier for immobilizing SAP in a diaper. Filed 31-01-2014 as application 16167106.0; the remarks note it was filed 26-04-2016 as a **divisional application** to earlier application 14153467.7 / EP 2 901 991.

## Key claims 关键论断

- **不用绒毛浆也能固定 SAP：靠孔径差 + 水刺缠结两件事** ——顶层可被颗粒穿透、底层孔径小于颗粒，再用水刺把短纤机械锚固到底层上。→ [[Hydroentangled Nonwoven Carrier for SAP Immobilization（水刺复合 SAP 固定载体）]]
- **SMS / SMMS 是这里明确涵盖的底层方案** ——权利要求 2 直书 "spunbond melt-blown spunbond fabric"。→ [[SMS复合结构]]
- **克重不对等是设计要点，不是误差** ——顶层 15–40 gsm、底层 4–12 gsm（各自最优选），即底层被刻意做到「够挡住 SAP 就好」的轻量。→ [[Fluff-Free 4th-Gen Absorbent Core（无木浆第四代芯体）]]
