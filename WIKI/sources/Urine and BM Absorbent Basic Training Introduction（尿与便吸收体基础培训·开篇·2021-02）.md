# Urine and BM Absorbent Basic Training Introduction（尿与便吸收体基础培训·开篇·2021-02）

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

2021 年 K-C「Urine and BM Basic Absorbent Training」系列的**开篇/时间表讲义**：给出席规则、四场时间与全场 13 个讲题的分工总表；与 Absorbency Core 议程表互为印证（议程的 PPT 版镜像）。

## Summary 摘要

The deck lists the four sessions with two attendance options each: Session 1 Absorbent Foundations (Feb 25, 2.5 h), Session 2 Absorbent Material Basics (Mar 3/4, 1 h), Session 3 Fluid and BM Foundations (Mar 9/11, 1 h), Session 4 Absorbent Bench and Modeling Tools (Mar 16/17, 1.5 h). Attendance rules: attend either option, only sessions of interest; every session recorded (Panopto) with slide links shared; PCM members count each session toward personal training objectives. The Training Overview table (13 elements) assigns: Bowers 15′ materials overview; Krautkramer 30′ urine leakage design principles (plus 35′ BM/urine overview & user fundamentals); Nettekoven 20′ AC consumer insights; Sperl 20′ BCC consumer insights (wetting pattern & single insult studies); DeGrave 20′+20′ pad forming technology and pad zoning (flat vs pocketed); Lee 15′ SAM technology; Bowen 15′ pulp fiber technology; Keck 25′ nonwovens technologies; Chaudhary 20′ BM leakage design principles & test methods; Brubaker 45′ absorbent test methods; Baker 45′ absorbent modeling applications（出席规则与 13 讲题分工如上）。
