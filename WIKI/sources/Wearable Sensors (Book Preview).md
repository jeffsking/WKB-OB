# Wearable Sensors (Book Preview)

## One-line Position 一句话定位
A book-preview extraction of the Elsevier monograph *Wearable Sensors*, mapping the full systems stack — from e-textile sensing and energy harvesting to algorithms and body-area networks — that underpins smart-textile and wearable R&D.（Elsevier 专著《可穿戴传感器》的书目预览，勾勒从电子纺织到体域网的完整系统栈。）

> ⚠️ **[节选/书目预览]** — Extraction contains only front matter and the Contents (TOC). Full text was unavailable; metadata and chapter structure are reconstructed from the table of contents.

## Summary 摘要
- 7 chapters + index (p. 617); a discipline-level reference, not a single paper.（7章+索引，系统性专著。）
- Ch.1 Wearables: fundamentals, roadmap, social/HCI aspects (incl. Google Glass), haptics.（基础、路线图、人机交互与触觉。）
- Ch.2 Wearable Sensors: bio/chemical sensors, inertial sensors, optical heart-rate (PPG), body thermal-flux energy expenditure.（生物/化学传感、惯性、PPG、热流能耗。）
- Ch.3 E-textiles: knitted and woven electronic textiles, non-invasive sweat monitoring, and flexible electronics "from foils to textiles" (a-IGZO TFTs, plastic electronics).（针织/机织电子织物、柔性电子从箔到织物。）
- Ch.4 Energy Harvesting: human-body harvesting (thermal gradient, foot motion, light, RF/WPT), low-power ICs.（人体能量采集与低功耗集成电路。）
- Ch.5 Algorithms & Data: overview, BSN data-warehouse mining (machine learning), physical-activity behaviour modelling.（算法、体感网络数据挖掘、行为建模。）
- Ch.6 Communications: human-body communication, IEEE 802.15.6 TG6 channel model, WBAN trust.（人体通信、WBAN 标准与信任。）
- Ch.7 Applications: daily physiological monitoring, early disease detection (cardio/neuro/GI), Tongue Drive System, food-intake detection.（应用：监测、疾病早筛、辅助技术、进食检测。）
