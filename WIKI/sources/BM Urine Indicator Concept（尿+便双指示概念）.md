# BM Urine Indicator Concept（尿+便双指示概念）

## One-line Positioning 一句话定位

The dual urine + BM visual indicator concept for a diaper core carrying an effective odour-control granular — it frames the odour-vs-indicator molecule conflict, screens three BM mechanisms and specifies material composition, placement, converting process, cost model and validation protocol.

（中文：面向「已含高效除臭颗粒」芯体的**尿+便双视觉指示**概念——立起「除臭 vs 指示争分子」的核心矛盾，筛查三种便指示机理，并给出配方、布置、转换工艺、成本模型与验证方案。）

## Summary 摘要

The problem framing is a self-inflicted one: the odour granular (zeolite / silica / cyclodextrin in the core) scavenges NH₃, amines, H₂S and volatile fatty acids, which removes Mom's learned head-space signal for a BM. Change intervals stretch, BM sits against skin longer, and faecal protease + lipase and rising pH drive dermatitis — with the blame attaching to the diaper rather than to the missing cue. The design trap: the odour sorbent and the indicator compete for the same molecules, and intermediate zeolites are chosen precisely for high amine capacity over a 700–800 m²/g surface. Hence the design rule — **read the liquid phase by direct contact, physically decoupled from both the granular and the SAP**.

Signal selection compares urine and infant BM across seven properties. pH is a WEAK discriminator (ranges overlap); titratable acidity is GOOD (buffer capacity, not pH); bile pigment is BEST (binary presence); active enzyme is BEST but needs an aqueous-coated substrate; rheology (urine ≈1 mPa·s vs BM 3–60 Pa·s) is GOOD and drives sampling geometry; temperature is NONE; head-space volatiles are BLOCKED by the granular.

Mechanism options are ranked by cost per unit of certainty: **A titratable-acidity lane** (buffered halochromic hot melt, ~US$0.4/1,000, Phase 1, sensitivity ~65–80 %, misses low-VFA formula stool), **B bile-pigment mordant lane** (quaternised cationic polymer as an anion-exchange lane, ~US$0.3/1,000, binary marker independent of feeding mode, but meconium and firm stool release little liquor) and **C protease-cleavage film** (crosslinked gelatin 12–20 µm over a dark printed field, ~US$2.5/1,000, Phase 3 / premium, kinetics 10–30 min). The recommendation is to run A and B as two adjacent lanes on the same hot-melt station — two independent chemistries, one applicator, no registration — reading "both lanes dark = change now, one lane dark = suspect". The two lanes fail differently on purpose, so the combined miss rate is the product, not the sum.

The architecture is sample → isolate → react → read: a 10 mm hydrophilic wick at topsheet level in the rear zone; an 18 gsm PE barrier under the wick to stop SAP stealing the sample water and to keep the granular ≥20 mm away in MD; a 90 mm MD travel to the two hot-melt lanes; and read-out under an unprinted clear channel in the backsheet artwork at the rear waist. Two jobs are kept separate — the pH ladder is a load gauge ("change at the right time"), the BM lanes override it ("change now").

Cost model per 1,000 diapers totals US$0.54 (ladder 0.07, lanes A+B 0.10, ADL tail 0.23, barrier film 0.14, clear channel 0.00), i.e. ≈US$0.00054/diaper or 0.6–0.9 % of a typical US$0.06–0.09 ex-works diaper. Phase-1 capex is US$38–60 k; annualised running cost at 300 M diapers/year is ≈US$108 k. The deck is explicit that this is a claim and complaint-avoidance project, not a cost-out project.

Validation pairs bench simulants (breastfed BM simulant: 78 % water, 3–8 Pa·s, lactate 60 mM + acetate 60 mM, pH 5.0, bilirubin 1.5 mg/g, pancreatin 1.5 mg/g, mucin 1 %; formula-fed: 72 % water, 20–60 Pa·s, total VFA 45 mM, pH 6.8, bilirubin 0.8 mg/g) with a 40-sample real-stool panel and a 60-infant in-home use test, against acceptance criteria of ΔE*ab ≥ 15 within 90 s on BM insult and ≤ 5 at 4 h on any urine challenge, false positive < 2 %, false negative < 10 % breastfed / < 20 % formula-fed.

（中文摘要：问题源自「自造」——芯体中的除臭颗粒（沸石/硅胶/环糊精）吸附 NH₃、胺类、H₂S 与挥发性脂肪酸，抹掉了妈妈赖以判断便便的气味线索；换尿间隔被拉长，便便更久贴肤，粪便蛋白酶/脂肪酶与 pH 上升推动皮炎，投诉却落到纸尿裤本身。设计陷阱是吸附剂与指示器争夺同一批分子，且沸石（700–800 m²/g 表面）正因胺类容量高而被选用。故设计铁律为：**读液相、靠直接接触，并与颗粒和 SAP 物理隔离**。信号筛选用七项性质对比尿与婴儿便：pH 为弱判别（区间重叠）、可滴定酸度为良（看缓冲容量而非 pH）、胆色素为最佳（二值存在性）、活性酶为最佳但需水性涂覆底物、流变（尿约 1 mPa·s vs 便 3–60 Pa·s）驱动采样几何、温度为无效、顶空挥发物被颗粒阻断。机理三选按「单位确定性成本」排序：A 可滴定酸度通道（缓冲型卤致变色热熔胶，约 US$0.4/千片，Phase 1，灵敏度约 65–80%，漏检低 VFA 配方奶便）、B 胆色素媒染通道（季铵化阳离子聚合物作阴离子交换通道，约 US$0.3/千片，与喂养方式无关的二值标记，但胎便与硬便释液少）、C 蛋白酶裂解膜（交联明胶 12–20 µm 覆于深色印刷面，约 US$2.5/千片，Phase 3 高端，动力学 10–30 分钟）；建议 A+B 在同一热熔工位作相邻双通道，两通道「同时变深＝立即更换，单通道变深＝可疑」，二者失效模式不同故联合漏检率为乘积而非求和。架构为采样→隔离→反应→读出：后腰面层位 10 mm 亲水芯吸条；芯吸条下 18 gsm PE 阻隔层防 SAP 抢水并把颗粒在纵向上隔开 ≥20 mm；沿 MD 90 mm 到两条热熔通道；读出位于底膜图案的未印刷透明窗口。尿湿阶梯为「载量仪表」，便双通道为「立即更换」并覆盖阶梯。成本合计 US$0.54/千片（阶梯 0.07、双通道 0.10、ADL 尾 0.23、阻隔膜 0.14、透明窗 0），即约 US$0.00054/片，占典型出厂价 US$0.06–0.09 的 0.6–0.9%；Phase 1 capex 3.8–6 万美元，3 亿片/年下运行成本约 10.8 万美元/年。文中明确这是宣称与投诉规避项目，不是降本项目。验证以台式模拟液（母乳便：78% 水、3–8 Pa·s、乳酸 60 mM + 乙酸 60 mM、pH 5.0、胆红素 1.5 mg/g、胰酶 1.5 mg/g、黏蛋白 1%；配方奶便：72% 水、20–60 Pa·s、总 VFA 45 mM、pH 6.8、胆红素 0.8 mg/g）+ 40 例真实粪便 + 60 例婴儿家用测试，验收为便刺激 90 s 内 ΔE*ab ≥15、任何尿液挑战 4 h 时 ≤5、假阳性 <2%、假阴性母乳 <10%/配方奶 <20%。）

## Extracted Numbers and Conclusions 提炼的数字与结论

### A. Discriminators 判别性质

| Property 性质 | Urine 尿 | Infant BM 婴儿便 | Usable as discriminator? 可判别性 |
|---|---|---|---|
| pH | 5.8–7.4 fresh; rises past 8 as urease acts | Breastfed 4.5–5.5 (modern cohorts 5.0–6.5); formula-fed 5.5–7.0 | **WEAK** — ranges overlap |
| Titratable acidity 可滴定酸度 | Low; diluted by SAP gel water | High — lactate + acetate + butyrate from bifidobacterial fermentation | **GOOD** — buffer capacity, not pH |
| Bile pigment 胆色素 | None | Bilirubin / biliverdin / stercobilin, strongly anionic | **BEST** — binary presence |
| Active enzyme 活性酶 | Negligible | Trypsin, chymotrypsin, lipase — high activity | **BEST** — needs aqueous-coated substrate |
| Rheology / wicking 流变 | ≈1 mPa·s, wicks instantly | 3–60 Pa·s, stays at the topsheet | **GOOD** — drives sampling geometry |
| Temperature 温度 | ≈37 °C at insult | ≈37 °C at insult | **NONE** |
| Head-space volatiles 顶空挥发物 | NH₃ after urease lag | Indole, skatole, H₂S, VFA | **BLOCKED** by the odour granular |

## Key Claims 关键论断

- **除臭颗粒与指示器争分子** —— 这是决定整个设计的陷阱，也是本批最有价值的工程警示。支撑页：[[Odour-Control Granular Interference（除臭颗粒干扰）]]
