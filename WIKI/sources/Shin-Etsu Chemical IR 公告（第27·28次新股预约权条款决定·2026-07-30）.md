## 一句话定位

## 摘要

The document is an English-language convenience translation of a Japanese IR release. Shin-Etsu Chemical announces that, following its off-auction own-share repurchase via TSE's ToSTNeT-3, the board resolution of 2026-07-24 fixed the terms of the **27th and 28th issuance of stock acquisition rights via third-party allotment**, a structure used for a **Fully Committed Share Repurchase (Japanese ASR)**. The release details the exercisable-share maximum, the repurchase value and the average-price calculation (VWAP-based, with dividend and adjustment-factor components). It carries the standard disclaimer that it is not an investment solicitation.

**No SAF-related content** — no superabsorbent fibre, no surface-treatment agent, no patent text — appears in the file.
