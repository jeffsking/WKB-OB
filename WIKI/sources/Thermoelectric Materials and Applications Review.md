# Thermoelectric Materials and Applications Review

## One-line Position 一句话定位
A 2011 *Annual Review of Materials Research* review by Terry Tritt mapping thermoelectric (TE) physics, the ZT figure of merit, the material family tree, and application landscape — relevant to wearable energy harvesting and self-powered sensing.（Tritt 的综述，梳理热电物理、ZT优值、材料谱系与应用，对可穿戴能量采集有参考价值。）

## Summary 摘要
- TE = direct, bidirectional heat↔electricity conversion: Seebeck (generation from waste heat) and Peltier (solid-state refrigeration); compact, quiet, no moving parts.（无移动部件的固态转换。）
- Performance governed by dimensionless figure of merit ZT = α²σT/κ (α Seebeck, σ conductivity, κ total thermal conductivity); α, σ, κ are mutually coupled via the Wiedemann–Franz law (κE = L0σT).（ZT 受三参数互耦约束。）
- ZT≈1 is a 30-year practical ceiling with no theoretical/thermodynamic limit; author's ultimate goal is stable bulk n/p materials with ZT≈2–3, where the biggest return lies.（ZT≈2–3 收益最大。）
- Paradigm: phonon-glass–electron-crystal (PGEC) — crystalline electrical transport + glass-like low lattice thermal conductivity; routes = rattler-filled cages (skutterudites ZT≈1.2), nanocomposites, thin-film superlattices (Bi₂Te₃/Sb₂Te₃ ZT≈2.4).（PGEC范式及多条突破路线。）
- Material family: Bi₂Te₃ (ZT≈1, room-temp; nanostructured 1.4), PbTe (0.7→1.5 Tl-doped), SiGe (NASA RTGs), LAST (>2), half-Heusler, filled skutterudites, layered oxides, superlattice films.（材料谱系与ZT标杆。）
- Applications: waste-heat recovery, TE–solar hybrids, biothermal batteries (pacemakers), deep-space RTGs (Voyager/Cassini), automotive exhaust, localized electronics cooling (CMOS +30–200%), IR detectors.（应用全景。）
- Reality check: no new material has displaced Bi₂Te₃/PbTe/SiGe in commercial devices in >30 years; thin-film ZT is high but suits only low-power microelectronics; bulk materials needed for scale.（商用30年未换代。）

## Key Claims 关键论断
- TE offers battery-free temperature sensing (Seebeck voltage ∝ ΔT) and micro-energy harvesting for wearables — but at body-scale ΔT (~5–10 K) only microwatt power at ZT≈1.（自供电温度传感可行，但人体温差下仅微瓦级。）
- For hygiene-product evaluation, prefer mature Bi₂Te₃-based systems over headline-ZT materials; verify operating-temperature window and thermal stability.（评估方案优先成熟体系，核对温度窗口与热稳定性。）
