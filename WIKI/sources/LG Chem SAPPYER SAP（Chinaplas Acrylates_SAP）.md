# LG Chem SAPPYER SAP（Chinaplas Acrylates/SAP）

## 一句话定位

LG Chem 的 SAP（高吸水树脂，品牌 **SAPPYER**）产品/企业资料，归档于 v3 非传统资源 / ① SAP 厂商。本批作为「SAP 供应商」信息源。

## 摘要

The document is LG Chem's Chinaplas / acrylates-SAP material. LG Chem lists Acrylates / SAP among its petrochemical portfolio (NCC, PVC, ABS, SAP, PE, AA, etc.). Its SAP, used in diapers and hygiene products, has applied plant-based raw materials and obtained the **world's first ISCC+ international certification**, and LG Chem states the **world's first commercialization of BCB (Bio-Circular Balanced) SAP**. This positions LG Chem as a major bio-balanced SAP supplier.
