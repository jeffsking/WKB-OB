# Chapter 37 — Drying in Mineral Processing

> Source: Mujumdar (ed.), Handbook of Industrial Drying, 4th ed., CRC Press, 2014, Ch.37, pp.877–884.
> Author: A.S. Mujumdar (handbook editor).
> 注：页码锚定已由源文件头部确认（"PDF pages 898-905 | print pages 877-884 | offset 21"），仍建议抽查印刷版。

## Scope & Position 范围与定位

Survey of dryer types in the mineral/metallurgical industry — deliberately economic and selection-oriented. Conventional dryers (hearth, grate, shaft/tower, rotary) plus less-common types (spray, vacuum, fluid-bed, flash, conveyor, screw, drum, rotating-shelf), with thermal-performance envelopes and rules of thumb.
主线是散粒体经济学：干燥是低价值、高吨位、高能耗的单元操作，不是产品质量工序。
定位：本书"低单位价值散粒体"锚点；与 Ch32（生物质）同为能源/选型导向，与 Ch31/Ch33/Ch36 的材料-机理导向形成对照。

## Ground Rules 基本原则 (§37.1, p.877–878)

"Thermal removal of water from solids or slurries is an important operation… Although drying is a highly energy-intensive operation that is also increasingly difficult at lower moisture contents, no special attention is generally given to the technical and economical aspects of the drying process employed in the mineral- or metallurgical-processing industry." (p.877)

**先脱水后干燥（全书级原则）**：
"As a general rule, one must establish the necessity of drying before undertaking further action. If it is deemed necessary, it is essential to maximize mechanical dewatering (by filtration, centrifugation, pressing, and other processes) to reduce the thermal load in drying." (p.878)

**两条通用危害**：
- "below a typical solid moisture content of 4%, dusting may pose serious handling and environmental problems." (p.878)
- "Fire and explosion hazards as well as hazards associated with the handling of toxic materials are commonly encountered in the drying of solids in the mineral and metallurgical industry." (p.878)

## Performance Envelopes 性能包络 (§37.2–37.3, p.878–884)

| Dryer | Evaporative capacity | Thermal efficiency / energy |
|---|---|---|
| Hearth 炉床 | 5–25 kg water/h/m² | 1500–4500 kcal/kg 水；10–30% |
| Grate 篦板 | 25–75 kg/h/m²（至少炉床 3 倍） | 30–60% |
| Shaft/Tower 竖炉 | — | 35–60% |
| Rotary 回转（主导型） | 30–120 kg/h/m³；直径 1.5–4 m、长 6–30 m | 35–70%；直接加热 1200–2200 Btu/lb |
| Fluid-bed 流化床 | 需可流化、非黏性进料（≤3/4 in） | 低至 1500 Btu/lb |
| Spray 喷雾 | 浆料 55–70% 固含、可泵送；一步至骨干（<0.5% MC） | — |
| Vacuum 真空 | 低投资、无尘、泄漏只进不出的安全特性 (p.882) | — |

**流动方向选择规则**（p.879–880）：
"For product moistures below 1%, a counterflow dryer is recommended if the product is not heat-sensitive. For thermolabile materials with product moisture in excess of 1%, a parallel-flow dryer is desirable as the feed is in contact with hot gases while drying the surface moisture."
→ 逆流=干品遇最热气（极限干燥，需热稳定）；并流=湿料遇热气但固体温度不超过湿球（热敏安全）。

**黏性/结块进料**： "For sticky or caking solids, which do not shower well, it is often necessary to allow more residence time in the dryer."；新设计 "special internals within the shell to break up the formation of large agglomerates" (p.880)。

**过热蒸汽**： "With superheated steam, the mass-transfer resistance offered by the air film… is eliminated. The exhaust is also steam, which can be condensed to recover heat or compressed or reheated for total or nearly total recycling." (p.884)

**选型经济学**： "Better thermal economy is obtained with fluid-bed, spray, and flash dryers, but the rotary is currently more economical for very-high-tonnage operation of products with low unit value." (p.883–884)；"Care should be exercised in selecting the most cost-effective drying system (e.g., nonthermal dewatering using filtration or centrifugation preceding thermal drying)." (p.884)

## Limitations 局限

- 所有数字均为经验断言，无推导、无不确定度；"thermal efficiency" 未定义基准（如对 LHV），跨炉型对比只是近似。
- 燃料叙事停留在 2001 年视野（煤化趋势），作为历史采购基线读，不作前瞻建议。
- 排放控制仅一句带过（ESP/湿式洗涤），深度内容在别章。
- 与卫材的非迁移部分：矿石/精矿/煤基材本身、闪速熔炼骨干要求、 pyrophoric 物料专项。

## Hygiene Implication 卫材关联

总评：★☆☆☆☆ — 基材（矿粉、煤、高岭土、TiO₂）与吸收性卫材无关；仅工厂经济学与粉体操作原理可迁移，诚实评估不拔高。

- **★★☆☆☆ "先机械脱水、后热力干燥"是全书级原则的独立重复验证**（p.878、p.884，与 Ch32 的"自然晾晒优先"同构）：对任何卫材浆料/湿法成网体系 — 在穿透风/热烘之前先用压榨/过滤/离心把热负荷降下来；干燥必要性先证明、后行动。
- **★★☆☆☆ 粉尘下限 vs 干度目标的张力**：MC<4% 起尘（p.878）→ 细粉（SAP 细粉、绒毛 fines、类矿物辅料）的最终水分是**区间**而非越低越好 — 太湿=结块，太干=粉尘/爆炸。据此设定最低允许水分而非单点干度指标（安全侧详 [[Industrial Drying Ch48 Safety Aspects]]）。
- **★☆☆☆☆ 逆流/并流规则用于热敏物料**：干燥热敏胶黏剂/涂层时选并流（固体不超过湿球温度）；热稳定、需极低最终水分时才用逆流（p.879–880）。
- **★☆☆☆☆ 黏性进料需破团聚结构设计**：干燥黏性 Coated-fiber/SAP 母粒进料时，预期需要更长的停留时间或破团聚内构件/回混（p.880）。
- **★☆☆☆☆ 过热蒸汽/闭环热回收是能耗敏感产线的备选**：消除气膜传质阻力 + 排汽可冷凝回收/再压缩循环（p.884；机理详见 [[Industrial Drying Ch19 Superheated Steam Drying]]）。
- 适用性边界：无任何纤维/聚合物/吸收性基材数据；以上均为经济学与安全原理级迁移 [待验证 — 具体阈值需按卫材粉体实测]。
