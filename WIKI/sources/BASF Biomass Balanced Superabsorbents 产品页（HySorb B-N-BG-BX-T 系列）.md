# BASF Biomass Balanced Superabsorbents 产品页（HySorb B/N/BG/BX/T 系列）

## Positioning 一句话定位

BASF Personal Care & Hygiene 的**生物质平衡（Biomass Balanced）SAP** 单页宣传：HySorb® B/N/BG/BX/T 系列的可持续化路线说明；在本库中对应 SAP 供应格局中的**低碳/可再生牌号维度**（对照 [[Biodegradable SAP（可降解高吸水树脂）]]）。

## Summary 摘要

One-page advertisement. The biomass balance approach replaces fossil with renewable feedstock at the very first steps of BASF's integrated production Verbund（生产联合体）; the certified renewable portion is then allocated to selected products (mass-balance allocation). Claimed benefits: saves fossil resources, reduces greenhouse-gas emissions, drives certified sustainable renewable feedstock use, readily available high-performance **drop-in** solution requiring no re-formulation, third-party certified, adds sustainability value proposition to hygiene products. Main applications: baby care, incontinence, feminine care. Grades: HySorb® B / N / BG / BX / T series; concrete product grades "available on request"（本页未给任何牌号参数或数字）。
