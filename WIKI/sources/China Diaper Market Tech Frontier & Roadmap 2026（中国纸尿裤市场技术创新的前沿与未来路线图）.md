# China Diaper Market Tech Frontier & Roadmap 2026（中国纸尿裤市场技术创新的前沿与未来路线图）

## 一句话定位

A strategy deck (Chinese) mapping China's diaper market status, supply-chain evolution, and a **dual-track tech roadmap** for a global CTO audience. Core evidence base for the China-market and fluff-free-core concept pages.

## 摘要

The deck argues China's market has moved from "basic performance" to "experience & value", with consumers willing to pay for segmented functions. **Market status**: domestic share surged from <1% (2019) to 8-10% (2024); dual-track price+tech war, unit price **RMB 1.5-4.5**. **Supply-chain evolution**: ODMs (HaoYue, Reliable) upgraded from manufacturers to **technology partners**, dev cycle compressed to **6-12 months**, with joint R&D and patent sharing. **Tech dual-main-line**: green sustainability (degradable materials 70-80% at Hengan; PLA/PPC industrialization) and functional premiumization (probiotic skincare, 0.003mm ultrafine fiber, seamless waistband). **China's role**: largest market + innovation source + manufacturing center; HaoYue 181 patents (16 invention). Roadmap converges on **thin + strong + green**.
