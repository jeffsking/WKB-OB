# Smart Textile Visual Perception (GRID)

## One-line Position 一句话定位
A 2018 GRID invited lecture by Vladan Koncar mapping flexible textile displays — woven optical-fiber (OFFD) and electrochromic fabric displays — and the chromic-material mechanisms behind visual smart-textile functions.（Koncar 的 GRID 报告，介绍机织光纤与电致变色两类纺织柔性显示及其变色机理。）

## Summary 摘要
- Smart textiles ("textronics" = electronics + textiles) are pivotal to the shift of European/US textile industry toward a knowledge-driven sector; existing sensor/actuator devices are incompatible with textile processes and must be transformed into textile-compatible structures.（电子功能须"纺织兼容化"。）
- Definition: textiles that sense and respond to environmental change; classified passive (property change only, e.g. shape-memory, hydrophobic/hydrophilic) vs active (sensor+actuator, decision local or cloud/IoT).（被动/主动智能纺织品分类。）
- Focus on two flexible-display routes: (1) active = woven optical-fiber (PMMA) screen matrix; (2) passive = electrochromic compounds inside the textile structure.（两大纺织显示路线。）
- OFFD: 0.5 mm PMMA optical fiber woven on a 2D loom, side-emitting via mechanical (particle jet) or chemical (solvent) cladding micro-perforation; pixels driven by 3 mm LEDs (2–4 V).（机织光纤显示：打微孔侧向发光，LED点亮。）
- First OFFD demo on a jacket: a single row of 3 alphanumeric characters (60×60 mm), 0.5 mm fiber at 7 fibers/cm, each pixel = 4 fiber segments controlled by one LED in the lining.（首个夹克OFFD：3字符。）
- Electrochromic displays: traditional ECD = 7-layer rigid sandwich; flexible progress (PEDOT all-polymer 5-layer); GEMTEX built five EC-display generations, gen-4/5 fully textile-substrate (3-layer functionalized fabric → single-layer locally functionalized fabric).（电致变色显示柔性化五代演进。）
- Applications: fashion, home, automotive interiors, fire/police signaling, architecture; OFFD bottleneck = cost & integration; ECD needs longer life & reliability.（应用与瓶颈。）

## Key Claims 关键论断
- Chromic materials (reversible colour change under stimulus) enable visual/indicator functions on textiles — directly relevant to wetness/temperature indicators.（变色材料支撑纺织可视化指示功能，对湿感/温度指示有直接迁移价值。）
- Electronic functions must be transformed into textile-compatible structures — the same integration challenge faced when adding sensors/indicators to nonwoven hygiene substrates.（电子功能纺织兼容化，与卫材无纺布基材集成同构。）
