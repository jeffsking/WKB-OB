# 温变粉如何应用于纱线染色/纱线印染？（How Thermochromic Powder Is Applied in Yarn Dyeing/Printing）

## 一句话定位 Positioning

Vendor explainer (WeChat official account of 东莞市亮科新材料有限公司 — a thermochromic/photochromic powder maker) on **how thermochromic powder (温变粉) is used in yarn dyeing and fabric printing** — useful as a application-method and process-parameter reference for the library's chromic-material workstream. （中文：供应商（亮科新材料）公众号科普文，讲温变粉在纱线印染/织物印花中的工艺方法与参数。）

## 摘要 Summary

The article states that thermochromic powder currently reaches textiles through two mature routes — **fabric printing (织物印花)** and **yarn dyeing (纱线染色)** — plus a coating/padding (涂层/浸轧) finishing route. Printing is the mainstream because it gives stronger colour change and less damage to the fabric, at the cost of slightly stiffer hand-feel and marginally lower air permeability. Yarn dyeing (ref. patent CN103590263A) yields better colour fastness and a softer hand but is complex and costly; a cationic modification of the yarn is required first. Coating/padding via a stenter is simple but has poor wash resistance. The article also gives dosing, microcapsule-handling and curing parameters, and a market-outlook section.

（中文对照：温变粉在纺织领域有印花、纱线染色、涂层/浸轧三条路线；印花最主流，纱线染色工艺复杂成本高、色牢度好手感软；给出添加比例、微胶囊防护、固色条件及市场规模。）
