# Citric Acid Crosslinked Cellulose Superabsorbent Hydrogels (CEJ 2025)

> ⚠️ **来源命名说明**：原始文件名为 `NEW SAP idea.pdf.md`（整理者临时命名）。本页标题取自**论文正式标题**（Chemical Engineering Journal 526 (2025) 170721）。

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

Peer-reviewed journal article that benchmarks a **citric-acid–crosslinked cellulose superabsorbent hydrogel (SAH)** head-to-head against commercial sodium-polyacrylate SAP in both DI water and 0.9 % saline — the library's cleanest **saline-based performance comparison** for the biodegradable-SAP route.

## Abstract 摘要

The study develops citric acid (CA)-crosslinked superabsorbent hydrogels based on cellulose derivatives as sustainable alternatives to conventional petroleum-based SAPs for personal hygiene. **Sodium carboxymethyl cellulose (Na-CMC)** is the primary polymer and **hydroxyethyl cellulose (HEC)** the secondary polymer, blended at varying ratios and crosslinked with CA as a green crosslinking agent. The authors systematically evaluate polymer composition, swelling medium, pH and **absorption under load (AUL)**, using thermal analysis, rheology, water absorption capacity, swelling/deswelling kinetics and soil biodegradation tests, plus an indirect-contact cytotoxicity assay on mouse fibroblasts. The optimised formulation — **CMC–1.5 % CA** — gives a high equilibrium water absorption (Q_eq) of **110 g/g in saline at 38 ℃**, favourable swelling/deswelling behaviour, rheological robustness, **72 % biodegradation within 7 weeks** under composting conditions and non-cytotoxicity (>80 % cell viability). The authors single out the **low crosslinker proportion (1.5 wt% CA relative to total polymer mass)** as the novel finding versus prior CA-crosslinked hydrogel literature, and argue the material suits eco-friendly hygiene applications aligned with the UN SDGs. Included literature benchmarking (Table 5) sets this work against PVA–CA (2.9–6.5 g/g), starch–CA (93–188 g/g) and cellulose–CA (95–99 g/g) systems.

## Key claims 关键论断

- **The advantage is saline-specific, not universal** — in DI water the commercial polyacrylate is ~3.6× better, but in 0.9 % saline the CA-crosslinked cellulose hydrogel slightly exceeds it. → [[Citric Acid Crosslinked Cellulose SAH（柠檬酸交联纤维素超吸水凝胶）]]
- **Low crosslinker loading (1.5 %) is the claimed novelty** — it maximises swelling while minimising crosslinker consumption. → [[Citric Acid Crosslinked Cellulose SAH（柠檬酸交联纤维素超吸水凝胶）]]
- **Saline performance hinges on ion shielding** — Na⁺ from the medium screens the ionised —COO⁻ groups, cutting absorption more than threefold versus DI water. → [[SAP（高吸水树脂）]]
- **Cellulose-based SAH performs relatively better under load** — its smaller load-induced loss (32–39 % vs 81.6 %) is the most transferable finding for diaper-relevant AUL. → [[SAP性能指标（CRC AUL GBP Vortex）]]
- **The route is genuinely biodegradable and non-cytotoxic in this test set**, at 1.5 % CA the best-performing formulation. → [[Biodegradable SAP（可降解高吸水树脂）]]
