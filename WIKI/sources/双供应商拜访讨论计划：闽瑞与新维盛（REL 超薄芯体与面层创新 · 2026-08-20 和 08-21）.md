# 双供应商拜访讨论计划：闽瑞与新维盛（REL 超薄芯体与面层创新 · 2026-08-20 和 08-21）

## Positioning 一句话定位

REL 超薄芯体与面层创新项目的**双供应商拜访预案**（v1.0，2026-08-19 编制，内部保密）：8/20 闽瑞（ES 纤维 + REL 打样 + 功能纤维选项）、8/21 新维盛（三层切割 + 3D 压花降克重）；在本库中定义了两条面层候选技术的公开参数基线与 G1–G8 现场确认缺口。

## Summary 摘要

Internal discussion plan. Minrui session (8/20): role framing — ES fibre supplier to Yanjan, which serves KC; also direct ES supplier to Xinweisheng, creating a four-node chain and a **potential information-boundary conflict (G1)** when both suppliers serve KC. Question set covers C2 anchor-recipe ES denier/crimp/hydrophility batch variation (Q1), REL trial round status vs C1 baseline wet-tensile/first-strike/rewet (Q2, G2), hydrophilic treatment durability (Q3), antibacterial ES mechanism and OEKO-TEX/SGS certification (Q4), share of supply to Xinweisheng (Q5), sub-1.0D stability/yield (Q6). Xinweisheng session (8/21): topics are Triple Cutting and 3D in-line embossing. Triple Cutting = slitting continuous hot-air/SMS nonwoven into three functional narrow strips (skin layer / diffusion layer / core-contact layer) then recombining; proposed as an **alternative path** to the REL diffusion-card design based on shaped fibre + ES — key open question whether its diffusion ceiling can close the REL 17 N/m wet-tensile gap (G4/G5). 3D embossing = online male-female roller embossing of two-layer hot-air cloth; public data: permeability +30–50%, spread +20–40%, tensile +15–25%, aperture 0.1–1.0 mm, industry first-strike benchmark 0.18–0.2 s at 20–22 gsm, ~85% adoption in premium Pampers/Huggies lines; basis weight 45 → 38–35 gsm (G6). Note: embossing solves the topsheet problem, not the diffusion-card problem — the two routes may run **in parallel, not as substitutes**. Gaps G1–G8 tabulated with priorities (six rated high).

## Key Claims 关键论断

- **三层切割是 REL 扩散卡层（异形纤维+ES）的替代候选，但两条路线更可能并行而非互斥** —— 支撑页：[[Triple Cutting Topsheet（三层切割面层）]]、[[REL增强层]]
- **3D 压花解决的是「面层」问题（降克重+透气），不解决「扩散卡层」问题** —— 支撑页：[[3D In-line Embossing Topsheet（3D 在线压花面层）]]
- **闽瑞同时供货延江与新维盛，双供应商均服务 KC 时存在信息边界风险（G1）** —— 支撑页：[[福建闽瑞新合纤]]
