# Toyobo Group Integrated Report FY2025（东洋纺）

## 一句话定位

Toyobo（东洋纺）FY2025 整合报告（年报级公司资料）。虽被归档在 SAF/丙烯酸纤维供应商文件夹下，正文实为集团年报，用于界定 Toyobo 作为日本综合材料/纤维集团的基本盘与供应能力边界。

## 摘要

The document is Toyobo Group's Integrated Report for the fiscal year ended March 31, 2025. It covers corporate philosophy, the "Sustainable Vision 2030", value-creation strategy, a medium-term management plan, and segment overviews. Toyobo positions itself across four business segments — Films, Life Science, Environmental and Functional Materials, and Functional Textiles and Trading. The report states the group runs 37 global production sites and emphasizes advanced production capabilities, quality assurance, and digital transformation of manufacturing. No acrylic-SAF-specific product datasheet (e.g., absorbance, denier, gel strength) appears in the read portion; the file is therefore treated as a corporate-profile source, not a product TDS.
