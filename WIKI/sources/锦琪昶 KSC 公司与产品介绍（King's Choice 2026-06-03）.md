# 锦琪昶 KSC 公司与产品介绍（King's Choice 2026-06-03）

## 一句话定位

**KSC / 锦琪昶（King's Choice，江苏锦琪昶新材料有限公司）** 面向 KC 的公司与产品介绍（2026-06-03），主打**热风粘合（ATB）非织造布**的**视觉差异化 + 高性能**路线。本批作为「ATB 非织造布供应商」信息源。

## 摘要

KSC presents itself as an ATB nonwoven specialist. **Jiangsu KSC** was established in Nanjing in **Aug 2015**, **Guangdong KSC** in Jiangmen in **June 2024**; the line build-out runs from Lines 1–2 (2016, ultrasonic) through Line 8 (2022, breathable NW / baffle) to Lines 11–14 (2025, 4.6 m width), with further lines planned for 2026. Equipment comes from **Shyng Wei** (double carding, 2,850 mm) and **Trützschler** (single carding, 3,000 mm), with denier coverage roughly **0.4D–7.0D**; post-treatment (**ultrasonic lamination** and **embossing & aperture**) is jointly developed in-house. Product families: **Velvet NW** (0.4–2.0 D, marketed as 琪鹅柔 Super Velvet, positioned for dry topsheet), **Backsheet** (hydrophobic, 0.4–2.0 D, 14–30 gsm) and **Topsheet** (hydrophilic, 1.0–2.0 D, 22–35 gsm), plus **Ultrasonic NW** (glue-free, lower odour, higher peel force, better pattern retention, and cost benefit from removing the barrier layer). Certifications: **ISO 9001 / 14001 / 45001** and **EDANA-QAP**; **140+** authorized patents. Global roadmap: **Fujian → Egypt → Brazil**.
