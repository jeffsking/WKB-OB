# Mechanochromic Waistband Tension Indicator Proposal（2026-08-19）

The decision-gate proposal for the waistband tension indicator — it converts the technical deep-dive into a one-page verdict (Track B proceeds, Track A maintained) with market sizing, risk register, 24-week roadmap and business impact. This is the **project-approval document** of the Mechanochromic block.

（中文：腰围张力指示器的决策门径提案——把技术深化报告转成一页裁决（Track B 推进、Track A 维持），并附市场测算、风险矩阵、24 周路线图与业务影响。是本批 Mechanochromic 的**项目立项文件**。）

## Summary 摘要

The proposal frames an invisible problem: waistband tension is a continuous variable (1.4–3.9 N) with zero feedback to the caregiver; studies cited in the deck put 67 % of caregivers as uncertain about optimal tightness. Too-loose panels (< 38 % strain) sag and leak; too-tight panels (> 101 %) restrict movement, and the 10 mm waist elastic at 2.5 N produces ~29 mmHg, above the 20–30 mmHg discomfort threshold. No current product signals either state.

The technical verdict rejects molecular mechanophores on three counts — the ~240 pN/bond threshold equals ~40,000 psi, two orders of magnitude above the 20–150 kPa diaper target; the merocyanine state is thermally transient at 37 °C and polar-sensitive (hygiene moisture accelerates relaxation → false negatives); and the signal fades after ~3 stretch cycles. It notes the counter-evidence too: Orado et al. (2025) incorporated spiropyran into a polyurethane shape-memory polymer (PUR-SP) with +56 % fluorescence, cytocompatible and reversible. The proposal therefore maintains the molecular route as Track A (university partnership, 12–24 months) and proceeds with Track B, the geometric optical mask already specified in the deep-dive.

The business case repeats the deep-dive economics: ≈0.09 ¢/pant add-on, US$38 K new capex, no new chemistry or toxicology package, K-C concept patent expiring Oct 2026. Business impact estimated at +3–5 % price premium, −42 % return/complaint rate, first-to-market in a US$4.1 B growing category and zero new chemical substances.

The deck also publishes the mechanochromic market trajectory (US$1.2 B 2024 → US$4.1 B 2033; 14.6 % global CAGR, 17.2 % APAC CAGR), a five-candidate screening table that selects the geometric mask, a seven-row risk matrix (R1 threshold drift and R2 signal relaxation both HIGH), quantified milestones (M1 bench protocol at month 4, M2 line integration at month 9, M3 compliance/FTO at month 16, M4 commercial launch at month 24) and a bibliography that this page records as the block's literature anchor.
