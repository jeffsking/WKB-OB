# Depend Rewet Over Time.xlsx

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

A longitudinal rewet (dryness) measurement workbook for Depend adult-care products (ATACAMA+ and BAYMAX platforms), used to support "2× drier" claim substantiation and track machine/lot variability over 2021–2023.（Depend 成人护理产品（ATACAMA+ / BAYMAX）的纵向回渗测定工作簿，用于支撑「2 倍更干」宣称并追踪 2021–2023 年设备/批次波动。）

## Summary 摘要

- Two sheets: **Sheet3** is a pivot of average rewet by format/product/gender; **Sheet1** is the raw row-level log (Project, Product, Gender, Size, Format, Machine, Month/Year, STM, Sample, Rep, Value).（两表：Sheet3 为按格式/产品/性别的平均回渗透视；Sheet1 为行级原始日志。）
- Format/platform codes: DPU2, DPU3, DPU-ULT (Ultimate); products ATACAMA+ and BAYMAX; studies span 2021 Audit, 2022 Audit, Benchmarking, Competitive, Riddler, Product Release, Costco Support, Predictive Leakage, ATACAMA Ad Claims.（格式/平台：DPU2、DPU3、DPU-ULT；产品 ATACAMA+ 与 BAYMAX；研究含 2021/2022 审计、对标、竞品、上市、Costco 支持、预测渗漏、ATACAMA 宣称等。）
- "Used in 2x Drier Claims?" flag marks rows feeding the dryness claim; both Jennifer's and Kerry's original-data flags appear.（「是否用于 2 倍更干宣称」标记了支撑宣称的行；含 Jennifer/Kerry 原始数据标记。）
- Rewet values are single-number outcomes of a rewet test (units not stated in extract; treat as relative index).（回渗值为回渗测试单值结果，提取件未注明单位，按相对指数理解。）

## Extracted numbers & conclusions 提炼的数字与结论

| Item 项目 | Value / Conclusion 数值/结论 | Note 备注 |
|---|---|---|
| ATACAMA+ grand-total rewet | 0.268 | DPU2, Sheet3 |
| BAYMAX grand-total rewet | 0.561 | DPU2, Sheet3 |
| Male / Female (ATACAMA+, DPU2) | 0.220 / 0.273 | Sheet3 |
| Male / Female (BAYMAX, DPU2) | 0.514 / 0.552 | Sheet3 |
| ATACAMA+ L, DPU-ULT | 0.19–0.29 | lower than DPU2 (~0.26–0.29) |
| BAYMAX Control L (Jan-2023) | 0.46–0.72 (one outlier 1.62) | lot variability |

> Rewet is a "higher = wetter" metric in this dataset, so ATACAMA+ is drier than BAYMAX; ULT tier is drier than base tier.（本数据集中回渗「越高越湿」，故 ATACAMA+ 比 BAYMAX 更干；ULT 档比基础档更干。）

## Key claims 关键论断

- ATACAMA+ demonstrates roughly half the rewet of BAYMAX on the same DPU2 format — the quantitative basis for the "drier" claim.（ATACAMA+ 在同格式下回渗约为 BAYMAX 的一半，是「更干」宣称的量化依据。）—— 支撑页：[[Rewet Test（回渗测试）]]
- Lot-to-lot and machine variability exist (e.g. a 1.62 outlier in a BAYMAX control lot), underscoring the need for replication in claim substantiation.（批次与设备间存在波动（BAYMAX 控制批出现 1.62 离群值），凸显宣称佐证需重复验证。）
