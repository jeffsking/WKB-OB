# US20220125319A1 Moisture and Fever Sensing Apparatus (Farnaz Zand)

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

个人申请人（inventor = applicant）**Farnaz Zand 的美国申请公开**（US 2022/0125319 A1）：一条**可抛弃的毛细虹吸条**同时承担两件事——尿湿检测（丝印导电油墨带 + 可重复使用的报警单元）与**发烧监测**（另一面的一条热致变色油墨带 + **颜色传感器** + 无线通知）。它的参考价值在于把"温变油墨"从**给人看**升级为**给机器读**，并明确给出 **>98.6 °F（高于正常体温）** 这一数值判据。

## Abstract 摘要

Title: "MOISTURE AND FEVER SENSING APPARATUS". The apparatus combines **(a) fever/temperature monitoring** with **(b) urine sensing** in a diaper insert. Structurally it comprises an **elongated sensing strip made of highly efficient liquid absorption and transfer capillary wick material**, with a first end placed in engagement with the diaper. One side of the strip carries **a pair of screen-printed, spaced-apart conductive ink bands** that couple electrically at one end with a **portable alarm unit**; the opposite side carries **an elongated band of thermochromic ink**, a segment of which stays visible near the second end of the strip. In one embodiment a **colour sensor** senses the colour change of the thermochromic ink (indicating a temperature rise) and signals a **receiving and transmitting means**, which wirelessly forwards the notice to a computer, speaker or cell phone. Claims 1 and 7 are independent apparatus claims built on **(c) a colour sensor** plus **(d) receiving/transmitting means**; claims 19-20 add the capillary wick with three portions (short first/crotch portion, long enclosed second portion, third portion that protrudes for electrical coupling) together with the conductive ink bands and the thermochromic band. Theprior art recounted includes the inventor's own **US 6,559,772** (urine sensing), **US 6,241,386 (Limburg)** on thermochromic liquid crystals, **US 4,744,113 (Kogut)** on a warm-liquid toilet-training picture, and **US 4,725,462 (Kimura)** on heat-activated textile indicia; the background also lists the known failure modes of electrode-type sensors — conductors must pierce the diaper's plastic outer sheath, possible exposure of skin to high voltages, sensitivity limited to one area, and false response from sitting on a wet or metal bench or park slide.

## Key claims 关键论断

- **把"温变油墨"从人眼读数升级为机器读数**——Claim 1 的核心不在油墨本身，而在**颜色传感器 + 无线传输**这一级；这一点补上了本库 [[Thermochromic Microcapsule (Leuco Dye System)]] 现有讨论里偏视觉的读法。
- **两个传感器共用一条耗材**：尿湿走导电油墨带（电学），温度走热致变色油墨带（光学），耗材可抛、报警单元可重复使用——减少了"每次换裤子都要换掉电子部件"的成本。→ [[Sensor-Equipped Absorbent Article（含传感器吸收性物品）]]
- **>98.6 °F 是一个可直接落地的数值判据**：这意味着"高于正常体温"这一发烧阈值可以被直接写成权利要求。→ [[Diaper Microclimate]]
- **背景误报清单值得抄进坑表**：电子式在先方案会因"坐在潮湿或金属的长椅/公园滑梯上"而误触发——这与本案改用毛细虹吸条迁移液体、而非用暴露电极直接测量的思路相呼应。
