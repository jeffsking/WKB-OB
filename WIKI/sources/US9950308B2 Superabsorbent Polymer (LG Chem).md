# US9950308B2 Superabsorbent Polymer (LG Chem)

## 一句话定位

Granted US patent (LG Chem) on a **superabsorbent polymer (SAP)** — one of three LG Chem SAP patents in this batch, reinforcing LG Chem as a core SAP patent holder in the library.

## 摘要

The patent grants a superabsorbent polymer (title: "SUPERABSORBENT POLYMER"). Detailed claim language (composition monomers, crosslinking, surface-treatment specifics) is in the full text and was not fully extracted in this pass; this summary captures the bibliographic and classification scope. It is the US national phase of PCT/KR2014/007068 (WO2015/016643), claiming priority to KR 10-2013-0091627 (Aug. 1, 2013) and KR 10-2014-0098044.
