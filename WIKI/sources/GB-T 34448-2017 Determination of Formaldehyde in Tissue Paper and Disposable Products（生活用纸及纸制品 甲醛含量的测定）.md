# GB-T 34448-2017 Determination of Formaldehyde in Tissue Paper and Disposable Products（生活用纸及纸制品 甲醛含量的测定）

## 一句话定位

The **method standard** for formaldehyde in tissue/wipes/diapers — its HPLC branch (LOD 6.0 mg/kg) is the enforcement method behind the ≤6 mg/kg formaldehyde limits in GB 43631-2023 and GB/T 28004.1-2021, and its spectrophotometry branch (LOD 10 mg/kg) serves tissue-class limits.

## Summary 摘要

GB/T 34448-2017 (SAC/TC 141) defines water extraction of free and partially hydrolysable formaldehyde from disposable paper products, with two quantification routes. The acetylacetone method derivatizes formaldehyde to a yellow chromophore read at 412 nm (LOD 10 mg/kg, double-acetone/dimedone confirmation test available for colored samples); the HPLC method derivatizes with 2,4-dinitrophenylhydrazine at 60 ℃ for 30 min and reads the hydrazone at 355 nm on a C18 column (LOD 6.0 mg/kg). Extraction conditions differ by product type: SAP-containing products (diapers) use 2.0 g sample with 0.9% saline to avoid gel swallowing the water, others 2.5 g with water; both extract 60 min at 40 ℃. Results below the LOD are reported "未检出" — which matters contractually because GB 43631-2023 sets the wipe/diaper limit exactly at 6 mg/kg: the limit equals the HPLC LOD, so compliance evidence must come from the HPLC branch, not the spectrophotometric one.

## 关键论断（key_claims）支撑页

- **限值=检出限意味着方法即合规路径**：GB 43631-2023 的湿巾/纸尿裤甲醛 ≤6 mg/kg 只能用本标准 HPLC 法证明 —— 支撑页：[[GB 43631-2023（婴幼儿及儿童纸品化学安全总门槛）]]
- **含 SAP 产品的萃取必须用生理盐水**，普通水萃取会让 SAP 吃掉自由水造成假阴性 —— 支撑页：[[GB 43631-2023（婴幼儿及儿童纸品化学安全总门槛）]]
