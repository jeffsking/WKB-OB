# 莱博药妆（上海直纳）公司介绍及研发合作洽谈 — 现场拜访纪要 2026-10-13

## Summary 摘要

An on-site visit to Labio (莱博药妆) and its raw-material arm 上海直纳, assessing them as a potential active-ingredient and formulation partner for the Skincare platform. The company is a Symrise-derived, technology-led house with ~100 patents, a 1,000 m² R&D platform and a 100k-class cleanroom, already supplying P&G directly with side-plant extract. Its commercial logic is instructive for our own program: it deliberately sells **compound formulas** rather than single plants because single-plant actives get copied within months, and it picks **less-crowded targets** (IGF-1) instead of already-swept ones (5α-reductase, tyrosinase). 对我们最有用的一条：复方+专利化是对抗被替代的手段，与 Biocare 复配路线的商业逻辑一致。

## R&D and IP 研发与专利

- ~**100** self-owned patents (excluding transferred/licensed); a dozen new invention patents this year.
- Grant speed: 2–3 years normal, 1–2 years fast track, 6–12 months with pre-examination qualification, **fastest case 75 days**.
- Platform ~**1,000 m²**: Chinese-herb screening, cell research, fermentation, encapsulation.
- Equipment: Malvern particle sizer (~RMB 600k, for carrier particle size), fluorescence qPCR (drug loading / viral content), microplate reader (β-glucan etc.).
- Technical case: water-soluble transparent ceramide formula (~7 years ago) with ceramide patents.

## Manufacturing & customers 工厂与客户

- Cleanroom built to **100k-class** although raw-material norm is only 300k-class; four-stage layout: freeze-dryer, extraction, carrier workshop.
- Audited by P&G, Huaxi, Freda, Huanya, Infinitus.
- **P&G direct supplier ~2 years**, side-plant (侧柏叶) for anti-hair-loss wash products; order size ~**2 t**, 100% powder, dosage sub-1%.
- Open R&D platform: accepts customisation and specified-indicator addition; P&G's product is a custom grade unavailable to others.
- Cosmetics coverage: all major houses **except L'Oréal** — L'Oréal's frequent personnel changes and refusal to pay for predecessor projects led the owner to withdraw.

## Product & business strategy 产品与商业策略

- **Compound (2–3 plants) formulas are the house signature**, almost every catalogue item carrying an invention patent; single-plant patents are rarer.
- Rationale: single plants are easy to substitute — the 马齿苋 freeze-dried powder sold last year was decoded within half a year and undercut by third-party quotes.
- Positioning: not price-competitive but excellent quality control; offers decolourising, deodorising and desalting customisation.
- Anecdote: a northern customer doubted an anti-hair-loss raw material ("looks like water with a bit of yellow") because they were used to unfiltered, suspended material; two weeks of use converted them to repeat orders.
- Custom products for Infinitus, Perfect etc. are deliberately kept off the catalogue.

## R&D project mechanism 研发立项机制

- Not large in-house screening budgets; leverages **university postgraduate theses** as the screening engine.
- Hot targets (5α-reductase, tyrosinase inhibition) have already been swept across pharmacopoeia plants by universities nationwide — repeating them adds nothing.
- Differentiated play: partner with **Prof. Ding** on the upstream **IGF-1** target, screening plants across oil-acne / oil-dandruff / oil-sensitive.

## Industry topic — exosomes 外泌体/干细胞

- Company renamed its direction from "exosome" to **extracellular vesicle (外囊泡)**; **no approval pathway in China**, industry operates under "extract" grade numbers.
- Cosmetics explicitly ban stem cells; exosomes are loosely "stem-cell clothing".
- The 2025 (cited as "this year") 315 exposé covered medical-aesthetic exosome abuses: mismatched product, unlicensed processing, unclear sourcing, extreme prices; animal/human-derived do outperform plant-derived but cost ~RMB 200k+ per vial.
- Plant-derived has no ethical issue; company advises customers **not** to claim "exosome" — regulation does not allow it.
