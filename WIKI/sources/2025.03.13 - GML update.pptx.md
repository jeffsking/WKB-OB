# 2025.03.13 - GML update.pptx

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

GML-site update on the **GRIFFIN rewet** program (2025-03-13): a Feb-2025 GML trial tearing down SAM/Fluff vs ActivTech Hybrid cores and liner bond/treatment levers for rewet, benchmarked against Always Discreet on TTM-00672 Closed Cradle.（GRIFFIN 回渗项目的 GML 站点更新（2025-03-13）：2025 年 2 月 GML 试产拆解 SAM/Fluff 与 ActivTech Hybrid 芯体及面层键合/处理杠杆，以 TTM-00672 Closed Cradle 对标 Always Discreet。）

## Summary 摘要

- **Background 背景**: An earlier ActivTech (AT) Hybrid core test showed higher rewet than SAM/fluff; the team first believed AT Hybrid lacked embossing, but a double-check confirmed it was present.（背景：早前 ActivTech Hybrid 芯体回渗高于 SAM/fluff；曾误以为缺压花，复核确认其实有。）
- **Build 试产**: Nov-2024 GRIFFIN HUT product; ADL-produced cores, GML-produced finished product, tested at GADT lab.（2024-11 GRIFFIN HUT 产品；ADL 制芯、GML 制成品，GADT 实验室测试。）
- **Liner hypotheses 面层假设 (Feb trial)**: Fitesa 12gsm SB liner with (a) Oval bond 18% + 0.65% Lertisan = control; (b) CD Rod bond 10% + 0.65% Lertisan (more open bond → fewer pockets); (c) CD Rod 10% + 0.3% Lertisan; (d) CD Rod 10% + 0% Lertisan (repel residual fluid, risk to intake/leakage); apertured film liner (Fitesa 22gsm TS3) was not manufacturable (adhesive burn-through).（面层假设：Fitesa 12gsm SB，椭圆 18%+0.65% Lertisan 为对照；横向杆 10%+0.65%/0.3%/0% Lertisan 等，更开放键合减少小袋，无处理则斥液但有摄入/防漏风险；冲孔膜面层不可制造。）
- **Codes 代号**: SAM/Fluff = 1, 15, 16, 17, 18; AT Hybrid = 21, 22, 23, 24. Treatment 0/0.3/0.65%; bond Oval 18% or CD Rod 10%.（代号：SAM/Fluff 为 1/15/16/17/18，AT Hybrid 为 21/22/23/24。）

## Extracted numbers & conclusions 提炼的数字与结论

| Attribute 属性 | SAM/Fluff | ActivTech Hybrid |
|---|---|---|
| Wrapsheet 包覆 | 17gsm tissue | 12gsm dSB |
| Adhesive 胶黏剂 | n/a | Henkel 5402U |
| SAP grade | Stockhausen 5660 | BASF T5620 |
| Fluff 木浆 | 8.5 g | 3.8 g |
| SAP | 19.9 g | 15.3 g |
| SAP adhesive add-on | n/a | 4% → 0.61 g |
| Capacity 容量 | 735 g | recent HUT target 450 g |

- Rewet measured by **TTM-00672 Closed Cradle**; Always Discreet (25-0309) benchmark rewet ≈ 0.4 g. AT Hybrid CD-Rod variants measure ~0.1–0.3 g.（回渗按 TTM-00672 Closed Cradle；Always Discreet 基准约 0.4 g，AT Hybrid 横向杆方案约 0.1–0.3 g。）
- 1st Closed Intake also on TTM-00672 Closed Cradle.（首次闭合摄入同按该方法。）

## Key claims 关键论断

- Rewet in the AT Hybrid platform is tractable via liner bond openness and Lertisan treatment level, with a clear intake/leakage trade-off.（AT Hybrid 平台回渗可通过面层键合开放度与 Lertisan 处理量调控，存在明确的摄入/防漏权衡。）—— 支撑页：[[Rewet Test（回渗测试）]]、[[ActiveTech Platform（ActiveTech 在线薄芯体平台）]]
- The AT Hybrid construction reduces fluff (8.5→3.8 g) and SAP (19.9→15.3 g) vs SAM/fluff, consistent with a thinner, adhesive-bound core.（AT Hybrid 较 SAM/fluff 减少木浆（8.5→3.8g）与 SAP（19.9→15.3g），符合更薄、胶黏结合的芯体思路。）
