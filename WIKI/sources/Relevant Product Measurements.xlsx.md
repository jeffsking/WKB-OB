# Relevant Product Measurements（DEPEND/POISE 相关产品尺寸规格表）

## Positioning 定位

Released-variant dimension workbook (extracted 2026-09-28) listing per-code geometry for **DEPEND pull-up garments（DPU2 / DPU3 / DPU-ULT，男女各尺码）** and **POISE pads（Originals 4–8 滴 / UltraThins 3–6 滴）**: pad crotch width, surge（导流层）width/position/length, pledget（芯垫）dimensions, endseal lengths, and computed insult-point geometry relative to pad/surge. It is the **spec-side companion** to the wetting-pattern research dataset [[R168596_TL-16794_Adult Care Wetting Pattern Fundamentals 2015-2016 New Methodologies and Target Zones.pdf]] — the "where fluid goes" counterpart of "what the product looks like".（润湿图谱研究的规格配套底表。）

## Summary 摘要

The workbook contains four blocks: (1) released variant assets for DEPEND L14–L19 codes with full dimension chains (product length 689–867 mm by size; pad length uniform 396 mm; insert length 446 mm; crotch width 75 mm male DPU2/DPU-ULT, 95 mm DPU3); (2) a Depend Summary computing surge registration vs insult point — DPU2/DPU-ULT surge sits 30 mm from the front absorbent edge (length 250 mm female / 288 mm male), DPU3 surge sits 15 mm from front edge (length 350 mm, crotch 95 mm), confirming the surge-phasing move documented in the 2015-2016 wetting pattern research; (3) a Poise spec table with pledget dimensions scaling by absorbency level (207×55 mm at 4/5-drop, 240×70 mm at 6/7-drop, surge lengths 135–225 mm); (4) a Poise Summary computing insult-point distances (insult ~17–31 mm forward of absorbent middle).（四块数据：DEPEND 变体规格、DEPEND 导流层-加液点几何、POISE 规格表、POISE 加液点几何汇总。）

## Key Numbers & Conclusions 提炼的数字与结论

| 项目 Item | 数值 / 结论 Value | 备注 Notes |
|---|---|---|
| DEPEND pad length | 396 mm（all codes uniform） | pad length uniform |
| DEPEND crotch width | 75 mm（DPU2/DPU-ULT male）；95 mm（DPU3 male & female） | DPU3 95 mm matches R168596 design note |
| DPU2/DPU-ULT surge | width 74 mm, position 30 mm from front edge, length 250 mm (F) / 288 mm (M) | surge registration data |
| DPU3 surge | width 74 mm, position 15 mm from front edge, length 350 mm | vs R168596 "83 mm wide" — 见矛盾注 |
| Insult point (Depend summary) | female 95 mm in front of midpoint; male 200 mm in front of midpoint | computed from spec |
| POISE pledget | 207×55 mm（4/5 滴）；240×70 mm（6/7 滴；8L 240×70） | Originals line |
| POISE surge | 135–225 mm long; surge width effective 49 mm（多数）/ 62 mm（8L） | surge to pad MD placement 32–69 mm |
| POISE insult geometry | insult 16.7–31.3 mm forward of absorbent middle | computed |

> ⚠️ **矛盾记录（2026-09-29）**：DPU3 surge width — 本表记 **74 mm**，[[R168596_TL-16794_Adult Care Wetting Pattern Fundamentals 2015-2016 New Methodologies and Target Zones.pdf]] 记 "100gsm surge, 350 mm long, **83 mm** wide"。口径差异（规格 vs 研究）待人工确认，本页不裁决。

## Key Claims 关键论断

- **DPU3 的 surge 更长（350 mm）更靠前（15 mm）**，是三条 DEPEND 代码中护围覆盖最强的设计 —— 支撑页：[[Wetting Pattern Target Zone（润湿图谱目标区）]]
