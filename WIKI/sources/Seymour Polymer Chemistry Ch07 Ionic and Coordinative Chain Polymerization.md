# Seymour Polymer Chemistry Ch07 Ionic and Coordinative Chain Polymerization

> Source: Seymour/Carraher, *Polymer Chemistry*, Ch.7 — Ionic Chain-Reaction and Complex Coordinative Polymerization (Addition Polymerization).
> 定位说明 / Citation convention: 原文为 .txt 抽取件，**无正式印张页码**，本报告以文件行号（line no.）定位；原书页码缺失处标注 [待验证]。

## Executive Summary 内容概要

Chapter 7 is the mechanistic foundation of **chain-reaction polymerization**: it covers **cationic**, **anionic** and **complex coordinative（Ziegler-Natta）** mechanisms under the common skeleton of **initiation–propagation–termination**, deriving pathway selection from monomer electronic effects (electron-releasing → cationic; electron-withdrawing → anionic). 本章对卫材的直接价值在于 **§7.4 — Ziegler-Natta 配位聚合是现代等规 PP 工业的全部理论基础**：等规度、氢气控分子量、叔碳原子导致的抗氧化弱点，都在这里定义。

## Cationic Polymerization 阳离子聚合

> *"Cationic polymerizations require monomers that have electron-releasing groups such as an alkoxy, a phenyl, or a vinyl group."* (§7.1)

- **动力学**：*"the rate of polymerization is proportional to the square of the monomer concentration and to the first power of initiator."*
- **丁基橡胶 / Butyl rubber (IIR)**：*"Butyl rubber (IIR), widely used for inner tubes and as a sealant, is produced by the cationic low-temperature copolymerization of isobutylene in the presence of a small amount of isoprene (10%)... Thus, the random copolymer chain contains a low concentration of widely isolated double bonds which assume a low cross-linked density."* — 异丁烯均聚物无双键、无法硫化，加入 10% 异戊二烯正是为了引入可硫化交联点。
- **聚甲醛 / Polyacetal**：三聚甲醛经环开裂阳离子聚合可得聚甲醛；但 *"The commercial polymer Delrin is produced by anionic polymerization."*
- 阳离子增长速率常数示例：vinyl isobutyl ether 的 kₚ = **6.5 L mol⁻¹ sec⁻¹**。

## Anionic Polymerization 阴离子聚合

> *"Anionic polymerization occurs with monomers containing electron-withdrawing groups such as carboxyl, nitrile, or halide."* (§7.2)

- **单体活性顺序**：*"acrylonitrile ≫ methyl methacrylate ≫ styrene ≫ butadiene"*
- **Living polymer 活性聚合物**：*"Szwarc has used the term 'living polymers' to describe these active species."*；*"No formal termination step was shown in the previous equations, since in the absence of contaminants the product is a stable macroanion."*
- **Kraton**：*"Kraton is an ABA block copolymer of styrene (A) and butadiene (B)."* — SBS 三嵌段热塑性弹性体的代表。
- **Group transfer polymerization**：*"Group transfer polymerizations make use of a silicon-mediated Michael addition reaction."*

**Living polymer 五特征 / Five characteristics**：

> *"Living polymers are generally characterized by (1) an initiation rate that is much larger than the polymerization rate; (2) polymer molecular weight is related to [monomer]/[initiator]; (3) linear molecular weight conversion relationship; (4) narrow molecular weight range; (5) stabilization of the living end groups allowing the formation of telechelics, macromers, block copolymers, and star polymers."*

**阴离子速率与聚合度（Eq. 7.19 / 7.20）**：Rp = k′[M]²[C]/[NH₃]；DP = k″[M]/[NH₃]。

**阴离子开环 / Anionic ring-opening**：Nylon-6 由 ε-己内酰胺阴离子开环聚合生产，*"Nylon-6 is the most important monadic nylon and is produced at an annual rate of about 5 million metric tons."*

## Ziegler-Natta Coordination Catalysis Ziegler-Natta 配位催化（PP 合成核心）

> *"Ziegler prepared high-density polyethylene by polymerizing ethylene at low pressure and ambient temperatures using mixtures of triethylaluminum and titanium tetrachloride."* (§7.4)

> *"Natta, used Ziegler's complex coordination catalyst to produce crystalline polypropylene."*

**催化剂定义**：*"a Ziegler-Natta catalyst may be described as a combination of a transition metal compound from groups IV to VIII and an organometallic compound of a metal from groups I to III."*

**插入机理**：*"a monomer molecule (H₂C=CHCH₃) is inserted between the titanium atom and the terminal carbon atom in the growing chain and that this propagation reaction takes place on the catalyst surface."*

**等规度与分子量控制**：

- *"At least 98% of the isotactic polymer is produced when propylene is polymerized in the presence of triethylamine, β-titanium(III) chloride, and diethylaluminum chloride."*
- *"hydrogen is added in the commercial process to terminate the reaction."* — 氢气作链转移剂控制分子量（即工业上 MFR 的调节手段）。

**PP 的抗氧化弱点**：*"Polypropylene, TPX, and LDPE are less resistant to oxidation than HDPE because of the tertiary carbon atoms present in the chain."*

**为什么 PP 不能靠自由基聚合商业化**：*"For high polymer to be formed, the activated monomer and growing polymer chain must be stable and growth rapid enough to allow long chains to be formed."* 丙烯是非极性单体，自由基路线产物为无规立构（atactic）PP、熔点低、强度差；Ziegler-Natta 通过活性中心的几何约束迫使丙烯定向插入，才得到等规 PP。

## Stereochemistry, Metathesis and Product Spectrum 立体化学与产物谱系

- **Alfin 催化剂**（allyl sodium + sodium isopropoxide + sodium chloride）：*"The production of cis- and trans-polydienes, as well as stereospecific poly-1,2-dienes, is influenced by the proper choice of Ziegler-Natta catalysts and the polymerization temperature."*
- **聚丁二烯立体结构与 Tg**：trans-1,4-polybutadiene Tg ≈ **−14°C**；cis-1,4-polybutadiene Tg ≈ **−108°C**。
- **HIPS**：*"HIPS is mainly a polystyrene intense material. Here the polybutadiene polymer is dissolved in a liquid along with styrene monomer."*
- **氯丁橡胶 / Polychloroprene**：*"Polychloroprene was the first commercially successful synthetic elastomer introduced in 1932 under the tradenames of DuPrene and Neoprene by Dupont."*，含 85% 反式-1,4 单元，经乳液自由基聚合生产。
- **顶温 / Ceiling temperature Tc**：*"The polymer does not exist above this temperature, which is called the ceiling temperature, Tc."* — 高于 Tc 时解聚速率超过聚合速率；Polychloral 的 Tc = 58°C。

**Table 7.1 — 主要乙烯基聚合物的生产技术占比（原文数据）**：

| 技术 Technique | 占比 Share（原文给出） | 代表产物 Representative products |
|---|---|---|
| Free radical 自由基 | ~50% | LDPE、PVC、PVAc、PAN、PMMA、PS、PTFE、ABS、SAN、SBR |
| Cationic 阳离子 | 8–12% | PS、PIB、IIR、Polyacetal |
| Anionic 阴离子 | 10–15% | Thermoplastic olefin elastomers、Polyacetal、SBS |
| Complex coordinate 配位 | 12–15% | HDPE、PP、Polybutadiene、Polyisoprene、EPR |

## Energetic Window and Mechanism Comparison 能量窗口与机理对比

> *"The 'energetic window' that allows the formation of such charged species is narrow."* — 离子聚合的活性种既不能太活泼（不稳定）也不能太稳定（无法引发），窗口极窄，这正是离子聚合对溶剂极性、温度与杂质极端敏感、工业占比远低于自由基法的根本原因。

| 维度 | Cationic 阳离子 | Anionic 阴离子 | Free radical 自由基 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 活性种 Active species | Carbonium ion | Carbanion | Free radical |
| 单体要求 Monomer | 供电子基团 -OR, -Ph, -vinyl | 吸电子基团 -COOH, -CN, -halide | 多数乙烯基单体 |
| 分子量控制 MW control | 链转移 chain transfer | [M]/[I] 比值 | 链转移剂/浓度 |
| 立体规整 Stereoregularity | 溶剂极性依赖 | 可（如 cis-polyisoprene） | 一般无规 generally atactic |
| 代表产物 Products | PIB、IIR、Polyacetal | Polybutadiene、SBS、Delrin | PS、PE、PVC、PMMA |

## Methodology Critique 方法论批判

- **Table 7.1 占比之和超过 100%**：原文四类技术占比（~50% + 8–12% + 10–15% + 12–15%）并非严格互斥归一统计，引用时宜作为量级参考而非精确份额。
- **诺奖年份陈述需核实**：原文称 Ziegler-Natta 获 1963 年诺贝尔化学奖（可核对），并称 Szwarc 的 "living polymer" 概念获 2001 年诺贝尔奖——后者与 2001 年诺贝尔化学奖实际得主不符，标注 [待验证]。
- **"工业占比"的时效性**：Table 7.1 反映的是原书写作年代的产业格局，茂金属催化等后续技术未纳入。
- **原书出版年缺失**：本抽取件未给出出版年份（报告末出现 "Seymour（2003）" 字样，属报告转述），date_published 记为 0 [待验证]。

## Hygiene Implication 卫材关联

- **PP 树脂规格的三个化学源头全在本章**：① 等规度由催化剂体系（TiCl₃/烷基铝 + 给电子体）决定，可达 ≥98%；② 分子量由氢气链转移调节，直接决定 MFR 与纺丝行为；③ **PP 因主链含叔碳原子，抗氧化性低于 HDPE，必须添加抗氧剂**——这是 PP 无纺布在加工（高温熔纺）与储存（长期热氧老化）中必须稳定化的化学根因。
- **等规度 → 结晶度 → 纤维性能链**：等规 PP 的高结晶度赋予其高熔点（Ch.2 给出 Tm = 165°C）与高刚性；等规度下降会增加非晶含量、改善柔软度但牺牲热稳定性与强度。这是 PP 无纺布树脂选型与双组分（bico）纤维 sheath/core 设计的核心权衡。
- **抗氧化与降解**：叔碳原子是氧化攻击的优先位点，PP 的氧化降解链始于此处。卫材 PP 无纺布的加工稳定剂（受阻酚/亚磷酸酯）与长期热氧稳定化配方，本质上都是针对这一结构弱点。
- **弹性体与热塑性弹性体**：SBS（Kraton）等 ABA 嵌段共聚物来自 living polymer 路线，是热塑性弹性体的分子设计范式；卫材弹性腰围、弹性膜与热熔胶的苯乙烯嵌段体系（SBS/SIS/SEBS）均源于此。
- **SAP 单体路径说明**：丙烯酸含吸电子羧基，理论上适合阴离子聚合（§7.2），但工业 SAP 主要采用自由基聚合（Ch.8）——原因是成本与条件宽容度。living polymer 概念对设计嵌段结构 SAP（疏水-亲水嵌段）有指导意义，但**工程化路线仍以自由基为主**。
