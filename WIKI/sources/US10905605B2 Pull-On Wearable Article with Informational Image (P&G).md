# US10905605B2 Pull-On Wearable Article with Informational Image (P&G)

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

P&G's granted patent covering the **"grab here" hand graphic on the side panel of a pull-on diaper, legible in the relaxed state and reinforced by a texture feature** — the library's reference for the *user-communication* (as opposed to leakage-indication) use of graphics on a diaper.

## Abstract 摘要

A pull-on wearable article includes a main portion comprising an outer cover, defining front waist, rear waist and crotch regions. First and second extendable side regions connect the front and rear waist regions to form the article in a closed waist configuration, at least a portion of each side region being extendable between a relaxed state and an extended state. An **informational image**（信息图形）is disposed in at least the first side region. The article may further include a **texture feature**（纹理特征）formed in an outer surface of the article, positioned proximate to the informational image to form a composite image; alternatively or additionally, the image includes a **cognitively functional graphic**. The description explains that these images are intended for pre-literate children and should communicate through symbols, graphics or other markings rather than words; images should be legible in the relaxed state when in-use legibility matters (relevant to how the image is applied to a zero-strain laminate, an elastomeric film, or a pre-stretch laminate). The granted claim 1 is much narrower than the disclosure: it requires a hand image on each side region, at least partly on the laminate and on the first/second transverse regions, legible when the laminate is relaxed, each proximate to a texture feature built from an outer nonwoven layer + inner nonwoven layer + elastomeric element, **with apertures in the outer layer exposing portions of the elastomeric element**, such that the graphics + texture feature tell the child the article is to be grasped at the image. Claim 2: the graphic is printed directly on the laminate. Claim 3: the elastomeric element includes at least one of an elastic film and a strand.

## Key claims 关键论断

- **This is a communication patent, not a leakage-indicator patent** — the graphic tells the caregiver/child *how to use* the article; it does not measure or signal wetness. → [[Informational Image on Pull-On Article（可穿脱制品信息图形）]]
- **Relaxed-state legibility is the design constraint** — images are placed/made for the rest state, which drives how they are printed onto elastic laminates. → [[Informational Image on Pull-On Article（可穿脱制品信息图形）]]
- **Graphic + texture = composite image** — the aperture texture near the graphic reinforces the message tactilely, not just visually. → [[Informational Image on Pull-On Article（可穿脱制品信息图形）]]
- **The granted scope is far narrower than the disclosure** — the independent claim is pinned to the hand image and the aperture/texture combination, so the broad "informational image" idea alone is not what is protected here. → [[US11071659B2 Underpants-Type Disposable Diaper with Graphic Sheet (Daio Paper)]]
