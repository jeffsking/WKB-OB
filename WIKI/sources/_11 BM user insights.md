#   BM Flow: Pattern

$\bullet$ Breast-fed infants

* Age: 1-7 mons

* BM pattern:

* Short bursts of BM flow followed by intervals of no-flow that increased in duration throughout insult time

* Faster velocity at beginning, decreasing to slower velocities at end

* Insult length: between 0.3 and 8.7 sec

* Urine flow pattern:

* Continuous with velocity variations

* periods of no-flow were rare

* velocity during insult in form of a bell curve
