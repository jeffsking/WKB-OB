# New Diaper Product Survey Report 2024-2025（纸尿裤新品调研报告2024-2025）

## 一句话定位

A field survey (survey date 2026-04-10) of ~one year of launched and in-development diaper products — concrete product/brand/feature matrix feeding the probiotic-topsheet and China-market concept pages.

## 摘要

The report catalogs **20+ new products (2024-2025)** and HaoYue's disclosed R&D pipeline. Launched highlights: **Babycare** (Golden Camellia — 0.003mm ultrafine fiber + jojoba oil + full-perimeter thin rubber; Camellia series — 0.8D fiber + bionic-core), **Kao** (0.1cm thin magic core, −20% crotch bulk), **YiYing / Shishuang / Bulangni** (probiotic microcapsule, L.reuteri), **BeBeBus** (seamless waist + rain-drop wetness indicator + squalane/panthenol), **Pampers** (mousse waist, malic-acid essence), **Daio** (silver-ion antibacterial), **Qiaoshi / Schobic / Qadoh / Rikang / SiYi / October Crystal / CottonLane**. It summarizes six core tech trends: (1) probiotic skincare, (2) ultrafine fiber, (3) glue-free/ultrasonic fixation, (4) petal/bubble waistband, (5) smart wetness indicator, (6) medical-grade standard. HaoYue's pipeline validates the degradable-core (PLA), ultrasonic waistband, and bionic dual-flow-channel directions.
