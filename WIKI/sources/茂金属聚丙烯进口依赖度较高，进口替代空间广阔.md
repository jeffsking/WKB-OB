# 茂金属聚丙烯进口依赖度较高，进口替代空间广阔

## Positioning 一句话定位

This industry weekly provides a 2023 snapshot of metallocene polypropylene (mPP，茂金属聚丙烯) demand, import dependence, catalyst barriers, domestic production actors, and hygiene-product applications.  
（中文：该行业周报提供了 2023 年视角下 mPP 的需求、进口依赖、催化剂壁垒、国产生产主体及卫材应用快照。）

## Summary 摘要

The report distinguishes mPP from conventional polypropylene by its single-site metallocene catalyst, which enables tighter control of molecular architecture. It identifies meltblown media, medical and hygiene products, premium infant-care products, diapers, and sanitary pads as important end uses. China’s 2020 mPP demand was reported at 102 kt, while domestic production met only 12.7%, implying an 87.3% import-dependence ratio. The report attributes the gap to foreign technology control, high catalyst-system complexity, patent barriers, and the lack of domestic high-efficiency methylaluminoxane (MAO，甲基铝氧烷) and modified MAO. It lists Yanshan Petrochemical, Dushanzi Petrochemical, Lanzhou Petrochemical, and Yueyang Xingchang among domestic producers at the time of publication. The broader PP market faced capacity growth faster than consumption growth, which increased the strategic importance of higher-value grades.
