## 一、全书地图（Book Map — 各章聚合科学交叉参考）

### Ch1 — Development of the nonwovens industry

> 交叉概要：- concept: polypropylene (PP) as dominant nonwoven polymer; polyester (PET); viscose rayon / Lyocell (cellulosic)
- related PoP (Principles of Polymerization, Odian 2004) topic: Ch1 classification & "compromise molecular weight"; Ch2 step-growth (PET fibre grade, Xn=1/(1−p)); Ch3 free-radical / Ch8 Ziegler–Natta stereoregularity (isotactic PP); Ch4 emulsion (latex, viscose xanthate route)
- weave note: This chapter states PP = 63% of nonwoven fibres (p.10) and lists "low glass transition and melting temperature, which is attractive for thermal bonding" (p.10) as a key reason. PoP Ch8 explains the polymer-science basis: commercial PP is isotactic (head-to-tail methyl placement, helical crystalline) produced by Ziegler–Natta / metallocene catalysis (PoP §3 Ch8; action card 10), and PoP Ch1 notes the "compromise molecular weight" principle — directly relevant to PP MFR/thermal-bonding-window selection developed in Ch4. The cellulosic fibres (viscose/Lyocell) named here are regenerated celluloses (viscose via xanthate / Lyocell via amine-oxide solvent, PoP Ch1 biopolymers) whose hydrogen-bonding is exploited in wetlaid/airlaid/hydroentangled hygiene webs (Ch3, Ch5).
- concept: nonwoven definition & fibre L/D>300 criterion (ISO/EN 29092)
- related PoP topic: fibre-forming polymers (PET, PP, PA, cellulosics) — PoP Ch1 ternary plastics–elastomer–fibre and fibre Tm window 200–300°C
- weave note: the L/D>300, density<0.40 g/cm³ thresholds (p.1–2) implicitly assume fibre-forming polymers; PoP Ch1's "fibre Tm window 200–300°C" and the plastics–fibre–elastomer ternary frame help an R&D reader connect resin grade (MFR, Tm) to whether a polymer can be spun into a nonwoven fibre at all.。
