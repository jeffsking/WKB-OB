## Gravure Coating

$\bullet$ Process that uses a gravure roll to apply a thin, uniform liquid film to a moving substrate

$\bullet$ Consistent coating weights

$\bullet$ Suitable for high-speed processes

## Main Forms of Gravure Coating

$\bullet$ Direct = gravure roll to substrate ...... DRC and OLU

$\bullet$ Indirect = gravure roll to applicator roll to substrate.... Facial and Embossing winders
