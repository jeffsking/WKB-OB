# Handbook of Adhesive Technology — Part 2: Fundamental Aspects (Ch. 3-19)

## One-line Position 一句话定位
The scientific engine of the handbook: 17 chapters that convert "it sticks" into the design equations — viscoelastic peel strength, surface energy, coupling-agent primary bonds, and optimal bondline thickness — directly transferable to absorbent-core and nonwoven adhesion.

## Core Mechanisms 核心机理

- **六大理论互补不互斥** — "each of these theories is valid to some extent, depending on the nature of the solids in contact and the conditions of formation of the bonded system. Therefore, they do not negate each other" (Line 57-62)
- **机械互锁是橡胶-纺织物胶接的主导** — "penetration of the protruding fiber ends into the rubber was the most important parameter in such adhesive joints" (Line 73-75) — 直接对应 SAP 层热熔胶点到浆纤维的锚固深度
- **粘弹性耗散主导强度** — "the value of ψ is usually far higher than that of E and the energy dissipated can then be considered as the major contribution to the adhesion strength G" (Line 5178-5213)
- **接头是三维过渡层而非一条线** — "An adhesive joint is not a 'thin line' — it is a complex three-dimensional 'interphase' in which mechanical, chemical, and morphological transitions all occur."
- **只有偶联剂形成一次键** — "Only coupling agents form primary bonds and can therefore be expected to produce water-resistant bonding. A coupling agent is therefore defined as a compound capable of chemical reaction with both the polymer and the substrate." (Line 6681-6688) — 注意反向不成立：偶联剂可作促进剂，促进剂不都是偶联剂
- **粗糙度的反作用** — "These unbonded regions limit joint performance by reducing both chemical and physical bonds and serving as stress concentrators." (Line 9500-9509)
- **柔性胶的韧性优势** — "Flexible or semirigid adhesives produce joints having higher short-term strength and fracture toughness compared to rigid adhesives." (Line 11559-11564)
- **PSA 的速率/温度敏感性** — "conditions under which any pressure-sensitive adhesive is both prepared for testing, then tested, must be rigidly controlled" (Line 8623-8628)
- **老化机理** — "The mechanism of autooxidation is cyclic in nature and leads to an exponential growth of free radicals." (Line 15337-15342)

## Business Connection Points 业务连接点

- **芯体点胶工艺的核心方程** — 婴儿抓挠与穿着运动是高速剥离，ψ(v,T) 的峰值位置决定"低速不漏、高速不脱层"；热熔胶（POP/EVA/SEBS）中蜡/树脂/弹性体比例调节本质就是设计 ψ(v,T)。
- **胶量最优区间** — 0.07-0.08 mm 最优，过低漏胶、过高浪费并引入固化收缩内应力 → 直接用于芯层点胶克重设定与耗材成本优化。
- **柔化助剂的高杠杆性** — 20 phr 硫橡胶使模量 2200→160 MPa、韧性 +340 %；少量高价助剂替代整体高成本胶基；随机共聚物比嵌段共聚物更便宜且同样可增韧界面。
- **湿态强度与保质期** — 卫材长期处于尿液/汗液高湿环境；未改性 POP 热熔胶与水基胶在高湿下的强度衰减可类比 85 % 损失。硅烷偶联剂形成的 Si-O-M 一次键（50-250 kcal/mol）显著改善浸液后层合稳定性。
- **无纺布润湿条件** — PP 无纺布 γS ≈ 29-31 mJ/m²（低表面能），点胶热熔胶的 γ_L 必须低于此值才能保证 100 % 接触。
- **SAP 界面的"包裹 + 机械"复合** — SAP 颗粒（50-400 µm 聚丙烯酸钠）与相邻胶层形成化学-物理复合 interphase；胶黏剂对 SAP 颗粒的润湿与包裹程度决定芯体承压吸收能力（BAP）。
- **木浆纤维方向性** — 纤维倾角效应（0°→45° 韧性翻倍）对应木浆纤维取向度：高取向纤维束端头胶接强度最低，需特殊工艺。
- **配方开发提效** — 凝胶理论（Flory）估算交联度与凝胶点比纯经验试错"much more readily and with much less effort"，缩短配方周期。
- **稳定性助剂** — 抗氧剂 0.5-1.0 %（受阻酚/亚磷酸酯/硫醚）+ UVA/HALS 可延长医院库存与长期储运条件下的保质期。
