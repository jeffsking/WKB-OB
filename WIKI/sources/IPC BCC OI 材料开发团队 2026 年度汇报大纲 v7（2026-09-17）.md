# IPC BCC OI 材料开发团队 2026 年度汇报大纲 v7（2026-09-17）

## One-line Positioning 一句话定位

The **annual-review outline (v7) of the IPC BCC OI Materials Development Team**, and the only source in this batch that carries both halves of the picture: the **portfolio/project facts** (25 projects, KPIs, supplier and legal status) and the **knowledge-base-derived direction judgements** (6 route shifts, 15 industry benchmarks, TRL anchor table). It is the master document behind the OI pages.
（IPC BCC OI 材料开发团队**年度汇报大纲 v7**，本批唯一同时承载"项目事实"与"知识库反向修正的方向判断"的来源，是 OI 系列页面背后的总纲。）

## Summary 摘要

The document has three parts: a WKB knowledge inventory, an update-scope/justification part, and the full 32-page + 2-appendix outline. **Scale 规模**：25 OI projects (24 in the source Excel + OI-25 added from the Coco V6 trial material of 2026-09-16), 33 supplier engagements, 5 technology platforms, 12 material categories, 5 signed legal documents (NDA/NNN), 4 on-hold, 48 knowledge assets. **Reference frame 参照系**：the industry roadmap gives a 4-stage commercialisation path (2026–2029) and a TRL table in which **nine technologies are already TRL 9** (ultrasonic welding, double-layer SMS ADL, fourth-generation SAP≥70% core, AI-vision QC…) — against which **every OI project is still at Exploration with no TRL grading**, which is exactly why the deck introduces an external TRL yardstick. **Six route shifts 六条路线级方向**：zoning over uniformity; ultrasonics from fallback to cost mainline; degradables entering a cost-crossover countdown (PLA vs PP par in 2028–2029, bio-based SAP after 2030); smart indication demoted from sensor to visual language (acceptance redefined as "consumer decodable rate ≥70%"); compliance promoted to a pre-condition of material selection; and patents/exclusivity as the OI's first deliverable. **Core project facts 核心项目事实**：Coco V6's pilot line started 2026-04 in Tianjin, pattern channels Tesla / Aquaman, Step 1 improved MD distribution and cut high-pressure intake time with rewet/distribution moving 250 → 300+; the pilot line can only do full-width uniform SAP density, and **V7 is planned to deliver zoned density**; launch pulled forward from 2029 to 2028, benchmarked against Pampers 2.5. REL is now a single-option direct purchase (Minrui 28 gsm, 6 rolls); the liner converged to a single Code 1 (50 gsm) at the 2026-09-16 version. The deck also fixes two governance items: a **data-version ruling** (the 2026-09-16 Coco V6 summary is the sole authoritative version; the 09-11 values are void) and a **layout/colour standard** (16:9 338.67 × 190.5 mm, red-and-white FEI palette #DA281C, Poppins + Calibri / Source Han Sans, double-height header bar).

**Six route shifts 六条技术路线新方向**

1. **Zoning over uniformity 分区化取代均匀化** → OI-02 / OI-20 / OI-14 / OI-25
2. **Ultrasonics from fallback to cost mainline 超声波升为降本主线** → OI-05 / OI-04
3. **Degradables at the cost-crossover countdown 可降解进入成本拐点倒计时** → OI-24 / OI-06 / OI-12 / OI-13
4. **Smart indication demoted to "visual language" 智能指示降级为视觉语言** → OI-15/16/21/22/23 (acceptance = decodable rate ≥70%)
5. **Compliance as a pre-condition 合规升为选材前置条件** → all 25 (high risk: OI-09/15/16/21/22)
6. **Patents & exclusivity as the first deliverable 专利与排他成为第一交付物** → OI-07 / OI-10 / OI-12 / OI-23 / OI-25
