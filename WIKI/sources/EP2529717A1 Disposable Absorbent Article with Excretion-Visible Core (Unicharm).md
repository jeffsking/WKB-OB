# EP2529717A1 Disposable Absorbent Article with Excretion-Visible Core (Unicharm)

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

**Unicharm 的欧洲申请公开**（EP 2 529 717 A1，2012-12-05）：它的独立权利要求不是结构限定，而是**一个光学数值**——从外片经吸收结构体到内片的**全光线透过率必须在 15%–65%**，目的是让人**从产品外侧直接看到是否已排便**。是本库少见把"光学可视性"写成权利要求的尿裤专利，也是 [[Total Luminous Transmittance for Excretion Visibility（排泄可视·全光线透过率）]] 的唯一一手出处。

## Abstract 摘要

Title: "DISPOSABLE ABSORBENT ARTICLE". The stated improvement is that **occurrence of defecation may be easily perceived from the outside**. The diaper 1 comprises a crotch inner sheet 28 on the skin-facing surface, a crotch outer sheet 29 on the non-skin-facing surface and a liquid-absorbent structure 80 between them; the **total luminous transmittance from the outer sheet 29 through the liquid-absorbent structure 80 to the inner sheet 28 is 15% to 65%**. Claim 2 defines the enabling structure: the liquid-absorbent structure has longitudinal, transverse and thickness directions and comprises **absorbing regions containing absorbent polymer particles between a first sheet and a second sheet, arranged alternately in the longitudinal direction**, together with **sealing regions surrounding each absorbing region** (formed by sealing the first and second sheets). Claim 3 constrains it mechanically: **thickness dimension 0.5–9.0 mm** and **cantilever bending resistance in the longitudinal direction 15–140 mm**. Claim 4 covers the pants-type form with an annular waist member and a crotch member bonded to the non-skin-facing surface of the waist member; claim 5 adds side flaps folded onto the skin-facing surface, continuously coated with adhesive in the longitudinal direction so that a **rear pocket** may be formed between the waist member and the crotch member; claim 6 specifies an **unpatterned white fibrous nonwoven fabric** for the inner sheet, the outer sheet and the waist outer sheet. The worked example builds the stack from **hydrophilized SMS 10 g/m² (first sheet) + hydrophobized SMS 11 g/m² (second sheet)** with SAP particles fixed by a **second adhesive at 3 g/m²** applied over the whole second sheet and a **third adhesive at 10 g/m²** in the sealing regions; **crotch inner sheet = hydrophilized spunbond 18 g/m²**; **crotch outer sheet = hydrophobized SMS 11 g/m² laminated with an unpatterned white moisture-permeable plastic film 18 g/m²**. Measured with 50 mm × 50 mm pieces on a **Nippon Denshoku A300/ZE-2000**, average of three: **26.9% (absorbing regions, Example 1-A)** and **28.8% (sealing regions, Example 1-B)**; comparative conventional cores made of SAP particles + absorbent fibres in a **continuous core member** gave **10.2% (thickness 5.8 mm)** and **13.1% (thickness 3.2 mm)** — i.e. below the claimed floor.

## Key claims 关键论断

- **把"看得见"变成可验收的光学指标**：15–65% 全光线透过率直接写在权利要求 1，等于把"外侧可视排便"从营销话术转成了研发可测的可验收指标。→ [[Total Luminous Transmittance for Excretion Visibility（排泄可视·全光线透过率）]]
- **要看见，必须先变薄**：两例传统绒毛浆连续芯（5.8 / 3.2 mm）只有 10.2% / 13.1%，落在窗口之外；本案 0.9 mm 的分区 SAP 结构才达到 26.9% / 28.8%。→ [[Fluff-Free 4th-Gen Absorbent Core（无木浆第四代芯体）]]
- **父权结构很简单：交替的 SAP 吸收区 + 环绕封合区**——用两张 SMS 片材夹 SAP，再以封合区把每个吸收区围住，既固定 SAP 又保留整体薄度。→ [[SMS复合结构]]
- **方向与本库 [[Geometric Optical Mask（几何光学掩膜）]] 相反**：一个追求"透出来看得见"，一个追求"遮住直到触发才露出"。两者可并存于同一产品的不同区域。
