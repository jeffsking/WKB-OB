# CN 117024899 A（抗菌除臭高吸水树脂及其制备方法·中国制浆造纸研究院）

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

A Chinese research-institute patent application（发明专利申请）claiming an **antibacterial + deodorizing SAP** in which aminated cellulose nanocrystals act both as a covalently-grafted absorption/speed enhancer and as a **carrier that immobilises antimicrobial and deodorant actives**（抗菌/除臭成分的固载载体）— the leaching-free carrier story is the differentiator versus post-blending approaches. 一件「纳米晶固载抗菌除臭成分」的功能化 SAP 申请，卖点是不溶出。

## Technical breakdown 技术拆解

1. **Step 1 – base SAP（基础树脂）**：AA neutralisation 50–90%; functional monomer（PVA / AN / AM / AMPS 类）1–10 wt% for salt tolerance; crosslinker 0.01–0.1 wt%; persulfate initiator 0.05–0.5 wt%; gel at 30–50 °C, then cut–dry–mill–sieve.
2. **Step 2 – aminated CNC grafting（氨基化纳米晶接枝）**：EDC (0.02–0.1%) then NHS (0.02–0.1%) activation, spray aminated-CNC solution, 30 min RT reaction; CNC dosage only **0.01–0.1 wt%** of granules. CNC made by silane coupling agents（γ-氨丙基三乙氧基硅烷等，10–40 wt% on CNC）from wood/reed/cotton/bamboo/bagasse pulp.
3. **Actives loading（成分负载）**：antibacterial 0.05–0.5 wt%（壳聚糖/聚六亚甲基胍/羧甲基壳聚糖/两性甜菜碱）; deodorant blend 0.5–10 wt% = 10 蓖麻油锌酸酯 : 10 丙二醇 : 20 吐温20 : 3 二丙二醇 : 3 聚乙二醇 : 49 水 : 5 EDTA-2Na（质量份）。
4. **Test methods cited（测试口径）**：GB/T 22875-2018（吸收量/保水量/加压吸收量）、GB/T 42702-2023 附录9（抑菌）、自建 37 °C/50%RH 采气袋 NH3 与醋酸去除率法。

## Key claims 关键论断

- **CNC 在这里既是增强相也是固载载体**——0.01–0.1 wt% 的接枝量即带来吸收速度 45 s → 20 s 的改善 → [[抗菌除臭高吸水树脂（CNC 载体双功能 SAP）]]
- **「抗菌≠除臭」是本申请的立项论证**：抗菌只减少细菌产臭，排泄物自身气味需化学中和+物理吸附 → [[抗菌除臭高吸水树脂（CNC 载体双功能 SAP）]]
