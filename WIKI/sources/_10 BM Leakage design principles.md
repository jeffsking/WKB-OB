Design Principle

$\bullet$ Intake BM: Apertures to channel runny BM toward the core

$\bullet$ Control BM: Connected void volume underneath to distribute and store runny BM

$\bullet$ Block flow: A mean to provide compression resiliency for maintaining void volume
