# CN 111890767 A（一种超薄聚乙烯微孔透气膜及其制备方法 / Ultra-thin PE microporous breathable film — Hubei Hongyu）

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

A Chinese published patent application (applicant: **湖北宏裕新型包材股份有限公司**) on an **ultra-thin PE microporous breathable film** (10–14 gsm) for baby-diapers backsheet — an upgrade over cast breathable film. Backsheet-film manufacturing patent → FTO-relevant.

## 摘要 / Abstract

The invention provides an ultra-thin PE microporous breathable film co-extruded by a blown-film machine with outer/middle/inner layers: inner layer = CaCO3 masterbatch 45–60 kg + PE 25–30 kg + ethylene-octene copolymer 18–25 kg + lubricant 1–3 kg; middle layer = CaCO3 masterbatch 60–70 kg + PE 25–30 kg + lubricant 1–3 kg; outer layer = same as inner. The product shows basis weight 10–14 gsm, WVTR 1800–3200 g/24h/m², hydrostatic resistance ≥50 mmH2O, with low basis weight, high breathability and leak resistance; small dimensional shrinkage enables flexo-printed colourful patterns for infant diapers.
