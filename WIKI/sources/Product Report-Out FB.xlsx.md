# Product Report-Out FB（摄入战略汇报反馈表）

## Positioning 定位

One-sheet feedback log（Sheet1：Forum / Feedback / Follow-ups）capturing cross-team reactions to the absorbent intake strategy report-out across FEI BCC Coordination, BCC/AFC Global Collaboration, and AFC Skin Protection meetings. Useful as a demand-side steering record for intake-system (摄入系统) goal-setting.（摄入战略汇报的反馈与跟进清单。）

## Summary 摘要

Feedback themes: (1) alignment across FEI/BCC forums with a request to rank "Time to Change（更换提示）" above "Skin Dryness（肌肤干爽）" in gap analysis — tied to a PC-materials work-intake request, to be folded into a broader fluid-distribution brief; (2) BCC global asks to connect intake requirements with core & chassis strategies（fit-to-body changes alter intake requirements; bucket-volume changes alter intake）and to clarify goal definitions — is skin healing inside the actives sub-benefit? Is responsive fit inside "no feel"?; (3) EY's preference for fewer goals: leakage protection / zero leaks over BM-test improvements — no change in plans; fluid-distribution kick-off precedes RBM; skin-health/actives needs stay broad and high; (4) AFC asks: global menses simulant / Z-date enablement（Ana follow-up; should regional teams run their own BM tests?）, vaginal health & probiotics scope, "no feel" confirmation; urine irritation for heavy-end DEPEND users — covered; (5) Family Care has applied actives to tissue（anti-viral printing, lotion applications）— connect Su, bring to Kerry & Joe.（反馈要点：目标聚焦、定义澄清、跨平台经验搬运。）
