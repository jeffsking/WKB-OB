# GB 5296.3-1995 General Labelling of Cosmetics（消费品使用说明 化妆品通用标签）

## 一句话定位

Historical CN standard for **cosmetic labels**（化妆品通用标签，1995 版）— defines the two-mode date marking and the no-medical-claims principle that survives (in updated form) in today's cosmetic labeling regime; relevant to wipes because baby-wipe lotion labeling borrows cosmetic vocabulary.

## Summary 摘要

GB 5296.3-1995 (replacing the 1987 original) applies to labels on cosmetic sales packages in China. Six mandatory blocks: product name, manufacturer identity (with importer/agent details for imports), net content, date marking — in one of exactly two modes (production date + shelf life, or batch number + use-by date) — licence and standard numbers, plus special-use approval numbers for special-function cosmetics. Two principles remain influential: labels must never use medical terminology or drug-confusable wording (5.2), and special-raw-material disclosure follows GB 7916 (7.2). Cosmetic definition here (涂抹/喷洒于人体表面起清洁保养美化消除不良气味作用) is the historical text that later framed wet-wipe lotion debates. Status caution: a 2008 revision of GB 5296.3 exists in the public record; the 1995 text is recorded here as a historical calibration point with validity `[待验证]`.
