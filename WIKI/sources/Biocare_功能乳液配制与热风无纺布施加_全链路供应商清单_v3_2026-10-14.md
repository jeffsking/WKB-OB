# Biocare 技术平台 · 全链路供应商清单 v3（功能性溶液/乳液配制与向热风无纺布面层施加）

## Summary 摘要

v3 is the **scope-corrected** version of the Biocare supplier landscape (supersedes v2; corrects v2's misleading use of "screen printing" as the shorthand for the apply-on-web step). After 王总's 2026-10-14 feedback, the命题 was restated as: **develop the capability to prepare functional additive solutions/emulsions (aqueous and oily) from upstream actives and transfer them uniformly, quantitatively and controllably onto air-through nonwoven topsheets while preserving activity**. Knowledge base: ① the 2026-10-13 meeting minutes; ② WKB WIKI (2,246 pages; 14 pages newly hit and closely read in this round); ③ 24 groups of web searches (2026-10-13/14). Evidence grading `[F]`fact / `[I]`inference / `[A]`assumption / `[E]`estimate; supplier levels E1/E2/E3.（中文对照：v3 是本轮口径纠偏版，把命题从「丝网印刷」改为「功能性溶液/乳液的配制与向热风无纺布面层的施加」，并把「整理液/乳液制造」新增为全链路第二环。）

Key change vs v2: **stage ② "finishing-liquor / emulsion formulation" is newly inserted and elevated** — because the meeting is blocked on the *formulation system*, not on raw materials ("配方已配出来…但配方体系无法很好地可视化" `[F]`), and plant extracts are only an input to it.

## 二、工艺族谱与热风布适配（本版新增核心）Process families & air-through fit

Ten liquid-application families compared (kiss roll, rotor spray, spray, foam, roll/anilox, slot die, flexo, rotary screen, flat screen, impregnation; plus gravure and print bonding). Three selection criteria:

1. **Need pattern** → printing families (flexo / gravure / screen); **need uniform full coverage at low add-on** → kiss roll / rotor spray / foam.
2. **Aqueous system** → kiss roll, spray, foam, flexo, screen, impregnation all work; **but not high-speed slot die** (requires high-viscosity oil or solid) `[F]`.
3. **Oily system** → slot die optimal (high-speed in-line); kiss roll / spray also possible.
→ **Recommended combination for this project**: aqueous-first → choose or combine **kiss roll / contactless rotor spray / foam**; if high-speed in-line is mandatory → switch to **oily + slot die**.

**Air-through-specific constraints**（热风（蓬松）布特有约束）:

| Constraint 约束 | Data 数据 | Implication 含义 |
|---|---|---|
| Bulky structure 结构蓬松 | bulk **3–5 cm³/g**, air permeability **300–500 L/m²/s** | high-pressure rollers collapse it → **exclude impregnation/padding**; prefer non-contact / low-contact-pressure |
| Additive maldistribution 助剂分布不均 | "surface-rich, interior-poor" gradient harder than flat nonwovens | need atomising/foam penetration, not surface coating |
| Low basis weight 克重薄 | topsheet **12–25 gsm** (down to 12) | high add-on meaningless; **low add-on + high efficiency** is the key |
| Oven temperature 烘箱温度 | Zone 1 **128–132 °C**, Zone 2 **138–143 °C** | finishing must come **after web formation**; if in-line → rear low-temp zone or off-line |
| Active heat-sensitivity 活性物耐温 | Echinacea **fully deactivated above 65 °C** `[F]` | **drying ≤65 °C** or ambient curing → physically incompatible with the bonding zone |
| Denier sensitivity 旦尼尔 | 0.8D/1D/1.2D tried; **finer denier wets better** | fine-denier fabric more sensitive to uniformity |
| Substrate mismatch 基布匹配 | visualisation works on **SMS**, poor on bulky air-through | same system behaves very differently by substrate → must test with target fabric |

**Patent precedent**（现成工程先例）: **[[CN 108035071 A（热风无纺布及其在纸尿裤上的应用和纸尿裤·杭州可靠护理）|CN 108035071 A]]** (杭州可靠护理) — print-bond a durable hydrophilic finish locally onto a non-hydrophilic bicomponent air-through nonwoven, then punch **conical (top-large, bottom-small) through-holes only in the hydrophilic zones**. Finish ratio **亲水剂:渗透剂:水 = 10–15 : 0.5–1 : 84–89.5** (Goon883); **32 holes/in²**; groove depth 2–4 mm; cure **80–100 °C × 10–20 s**; topsheet 18–35 g/m², 0.35–0.7 mm; the perforating roller at 160–180 °C simultaneously cures the finish → **print and perforation must be zone-aligned**.

## 四、甄选逻辑 Selection logic

**Hard gates 硬性门槛（否决项）**: H1 system machine-runnable (five parameters: solids / viscosity / d90 / pH / surface tension + window width); H2 air-through fabric实测; H3 drying ≤65 °C or ambient curing; H4 add-on capability (no "cannot carry enough"); H5 compliance (GB 15979 series; cosmetic path needs ingredient-listing; Class 1/2 dual-track or COC clarification); H6 bulk not collapsed (**thickness loss <8%**); H7 volume capability four questions (which line / capacity / own or outsourced / TDS + CoA); H8 cost <5 US cents/piece.

**Weighted scoring** — stage ②: extract-formulation experience 30% / parameter completeness 25% / low-temp compatibility 20% / storage stability & scheduling 15% / cost 10%. Stage ③: add-on ability 30% / air-through实测 20% / uniformity & penetration 15% / low-temp drying 15% / compliance 10% / cost 10%. Stage ④: process fit 35% / speed & width 25% / low contact pressure & bulk retention 20% / service 10% / price 10%. Stage ⑤: instruments 30% / accreditation scope 25% / method traceability 20% / lead time 15% / price 10%.

**Rating**: A ≥4.2 direct validation | B 3.5–4.2 small-batch trial | C 3.0–3.5 pending | D <3.0 reject. ⚠️ **No A/B rating may be issued before H1 five parameters and H2 air-through实测 are obtained.**

## 五、数量策略、风险与下一步 Strategy, risks, next steps

- **Dual-source + dual-route**: stage ② **one aqueous line + one oily line**; stage ③ **one offline finishing line + one in-line retrofit**; stage ① multi-source; stage ⑤ ≥2 labs split by capability.
- **Risk register**: R1 system cannot carry enough (very high — same root as the three screen failures); R2 air-through bulk → "surface-rich, interior-poor" (high); R3 low-temp vs line-speed conflict (high); R4 powder stability in emulsion / sedimentation (high); R5 transfer rate only ≈2% (high); R6 compounding–scheduling coupling, make-to-use + no preservative (high); R7 air-through suppliers avoiding wet processes needing drying (medium-high); R8 transliteration name pollution (medium-high).
- **Open items**: Q8 **aqueous vs oily** and Q9 **in-line vs off-line** are the first decision points (due this week, gating all downstream sourcing); Q10 active physical properties; Q11 Gefilin spray-line availability & MOQ; Q12 Xinweisheng spray workshop; Q13 Xianda carrying plant actives; Q14 Shengbeng/Mingchen suitability for air-through and ≤65 °C drying.
- **Next actions**: decide Q8/Q9; verify transliterated names (Q1–Q7); technical对接 with the four suppliers already holding spray/finishing lines (Gefilin, GFL, GHC, Taizhou); RFI to formulation candidates (Xianda, Croda, Lianzhuang, Hengxing, Mikan); equipment route询价 (ANDRITZ neXkiss / WEKO WFA / Shengbeng / Mingchen); build the four-stage validation scheme (system properties → machine window → add-on → transfer rate); air-through fabric matrix实测 (basis weight × denier × process).
