# CN 104004521 B（一种蔗髓纳米纤维素基复合保水剂的制备方法 / Bagasse-pith nanocellulose composite water-retainer）

## One-line positioning 一句话定位

A granted Chinese invention patent (assignee: Kunming University of Science & Technology) on a **bagasse-pith nanocellulose composite superabsorbent** made by graft copolymerisation onto cationic cellulose ether. It is an agronomy water-retainer, but the nanocellulose–SAP route is adjacent to the KB's bacterial-nanocellulose (BNC) work.

## 摘要 / Abstract

The invention discloses a preparation method for a bagasse-pith nanocellulose composite water-retaining agent. Sugarcane bagasse is air-dried and sieved to obtain bagasse-pith raw material; cellulose is extracted (acetic acid / hydrogen peroxide method) and deagglomerated by high-intensity ultrasound to obtain nanocellulose; the nanocellulose is etherified under alkaline conditions to a cationic cellulose ether; the cationic cellulose ether is graft-copolymerised / crosslinked by aqueous-solution polymerisation; after reaction, nanocellulose is compounded in to obtain the composite water-retainer. The agent shows good liquid uptake and salt tolerance in weak-acid aqueous samples (pH 5–6.5) vs deionised water, 0.1 mol/L KCl, 0.1 mol/L NH4Cl and 0.1 mol/L KH2PO4, and is intended for acidic-land desertification control, agriculture/forestry and landscaping.
