# KC 访厂纪要 2026.7.1（江南高纤）

## 一句话定位

2026-07-01 KC 对 **江南高纤（Jiangnan High Polymer Fiber）** 的访厂纪要，本批作为既有「江南高纤」实体页的追加信息源。

## 摘要

KC visited 江南 on 2026-07-01. The Jiangnan group structure: **Jiang-Nan → JN High Polymer Fiber Ltd.（复合/bico 纤维）, ChemFiber Group（集团）, Power Station**; JN High Polymer makes bico fiber and PET top (毛条); ChemFiber Group makes Xinsu PET staple & chip and recycle PET fiber. **Bico total capacity ≈ 23 万吨/年 (230 KTon/year)** — recorded as 二十万吨级 because the exact figure is business-sensitive → `manual_review`. The site also operates conventional A plus B/C/D/E/F shaped spinnerets to provide bulk/loft characteristics. Product families include PET coarse-denier fiber and bico coarse-denier fiber.
