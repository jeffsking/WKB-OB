# BASF-YPC HySorb N7059 SAP 产品介绍（扬子石化-巴斯夫）

## Positioning 一句话定位

扬子石化-巴斯夫（BASF-YPC，南京）的 **HySorb® N7059 SAP 产品介绍**：国产化 BASF SAP 牌号的一手典型值（CRC/AAP/残留单体）；在本库中是 [[SAP性能指标（CRC AUL GBP Vortex）]] 的供应商标尺之一，也是 SAPP/吸水纸芯体路线的候选料源。

## Summary 摘要

Product introduction from BASF-YPC Company Limited（扬子石化-巴斯夫有限责任公司，南京江北新区新华东路 8 号）. HySorb® N7059 = SAP with **high Centrifuge Retention Capacity and high Absorption Against Pressure**; hygiene products using it claim high saline retention, strong absorption, long-lasting dryness. Applicable to baby diapers, adult incontinence, female hygiene; usable in **absorbed paper cores**（吸水纸芯体）as well as traditional cores. Typical values (not specifications): CRC 35.8 g/g（EDANA NWSP 241）; AAP 0.3 psi 29.0 g/g（NWSP 242）; residual monomer 320.0 mg/kg（NWSP 210）. Company support: fully functional quality-testing lab, whole-process QC, and an application lab **with absorbed-paper-core equipment** providing QC and formulation solutions together with BASF R&D. Sales & technical contacts are named in the original (not page-ized).
