# General Introduction and Objectives

> Chapter 0 of the PhD thesis *Liquid Crystalline Polymers for Smart Applications* (Bogdanowicz, URV, 2015). This page is the opening chapter of the book; it builds the conceptual ladder "smart materials → liquid crystals → LCP" and frames the two application threads.

## One-line Position 一句话定位

This is the thesis's conceptual foundation: it defines stimulus-responsive "smart materials" via liquid-crystalline polymers and sets the two application threads (photoresponsive microcapsules + proton-conductive membranes).
（本章是全书的立论基础，界定"智能材料"并以液晶聚合物为核心载体，引出光响应微胶囊与质子导电膜两条应用主线。）

## Summary 摘要(3-6句忠实原文)

- "Smart materials" historically trace to 1800s reports of magnetostriction; the thesis narrows the definition to polymers that can moderately reorganize their structure under stimuli, with novel liquid crystalline polymers (LCPs) as the central carrier for new material design, synthesis and system integration.（"智能材料"概念可溯至 1800s 磁致伸缩；本书将其收窄为可在刺激下中等程度重组结构的聚合物，并以新型液晶聚合物为核心载体。）
- Liquid crystals are an intermediate state between isotropic liquids and 3D solids — partial mobility plus short-range order; they split into thermotropic (temperature-driven) and lyotropic (solvent-driven) classes.（液晶是兼具流动性与短程有序的中间态，分热致与溶致两类。）
- LC molecules are discotic (disk) or calamitic (rod); their length/diameter (or diameter/thickness) ratio must be ≥ 5, with a rigid core granting geometry and flexible tails granting mobility.（液晶分子呈盘状或棒状，尺寸比≥5，刚性核决定几何、柔性尾保证流动。）
- LCPs = macromolecule + mesogen; by mesogen position they are main-chain (MC-LCP) or side-chain (SC-LCP), and by shape calamitic or discotic polymers.（LCP 是含介晶基元的大分子，按介晶位置分主链/侧链型，按形状分棒状/盘状。）
- The thesis strategy focuses on two smart applications: photoresponsive microcapsules (rod-like LCP) and proton conductive membranes (discotic LCP self-assembled into columnar ion channels), structured in two parts each with its own introduction and experimental chapters.（研究策略聚焦两类应用：光敏微胶囊（棒状 LCP）与质子导电膜（盘状 LCP 自组装柱状离子通道），分两部分展开。）

## Key Claims 关键论断

- Smart materials (narrowed): polymers able to moderately reorganize structure under stimuli — support page: [[Liquid Crystalline Polymers Smart Applications]].
- LCP classification (MC/SC × calamitic/discotic) governs which smart application is pursued — photoresponsive microcapsules use calamitic main-chain LCP; proton membranes use discotic side-chain LCP.
