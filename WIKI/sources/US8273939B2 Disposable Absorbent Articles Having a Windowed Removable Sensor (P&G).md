# US8273939B2 Disposable Absorbent Articles Having a Windowed Removable Sensor (P&G)

## 一句话定位

Granted US patent (Procter & Gamble) on a **disposable absorbent article with a windowed removable sensor** — an in-article sensing approach, paired in this batch with Kimberly-Clark's separate leakage-detection garment (US11013641B2) under the "sensor-equipped article" theme.

## 摘要

The patent (title: "DISPOSABLE ABSORBENT ARTICLES HAVING A WINDOWED REMOVABLE SENSOR") discloses a disposable absorbent article with a windowed, removable sensor. The bibliographic header lists assignee The Procter & Gamble Company, inventors Klofta, McKiernan, Schmidt, and classification A61F 13/15. It claims priority to provisional 60/832,549 (filed Jul. 21, 2006). Detailed claim language on the windowed sensor is in the full text; this summary captures the bibliographic and classification scope.
