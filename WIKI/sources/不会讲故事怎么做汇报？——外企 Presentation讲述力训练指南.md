# 不会讲故事怎么做汇报？——外企 Presentation讲述力训练指南

在外企做presentation，总被说内容不错，但讲得不够有说服力。汇报完感觉别人没兴趣、没记住、也没采取行动。根源是你不会讲"一个能打动人"的故事。

## 不会讲故事怎么做汇报？

数据再多，图表再美，都比不上一个讲得出、听得懂、记得住的故事。

## 为什么外企老板最爱会讲故事的人？

因为老板要的不是你汇报进度，而是你：让人理解问题，看到价值，被你的逻辑说服。

而讲故事（storytelling）正是你连接信息与情感的桥梁。

## 外企 Presentation 中的 3 种关键故事结构

## 1、What → So what → Now what

最适合快速有力的业务汇报

We noticed a 20% drop in engagement. So what? It mainly affects paid users, risking $80K in revenue. Now what? We propose simplifying the onboarding flow by next Friday.

## 2、Problem → Insight → Solution

最适合汇报发现 + 建议方案

The adoption rate for the new tool is low. After interviews, we realized users feel overwhelmed by too many features. We suggest a 'lite mode' option to improve early engagement.

## 3、Before → Tension → After

最适合展示成果转化与影响力

Before, support tickets took 48 hours to resolve. With manual tracking and unclear hand-offs, frustration grew. Now, with our new triage system, resolution time dropped to 12 hours.

## 外企讲述力的 3 个关键词

【关键词表】Context（发生了什么）："Here's the background…" "Previously, we…"；Conflict（为什么这是个问题）："The challenge is…" "What we didn't expect…"；Change（怎么解决了，有什么结果）："So we…" "As a result…" "Now we…"。

## 数据图表也要讲故事！

As you can see from this chart…（冷场经典句）改成：

Let me walk you through this chart—it tells an interesting story. This spike? It happened right after we launched the new feature. What surprised us here is that mobile users converted 3x better.

## 英文表达力强化：让你说得像个讲故事的人

【句型表】引入问题："Here's what we were trying to solve…" / "We started with a question…"；营造转折："But then something unexpected happened…" / "However, we ran into this…"；强调发现："What really stood out to us was…" / "The real insight came when…"；强调结果："As a result, we were able to…" / "That's when things changed."

## 外企 Presentation 常见误区 vs 高效讲述

【对比表】误区"Here are the features."→优化"Let me show you how this solves our user's biggest pain point."；误区"This is the timeline."→"This timeline helped us avoid last-minute chaos last time."；误区"The data shows…"→"This number represents 200 real users who dropped off. That's a problem."。

## 汇报时加入一个人的故事，打动人心。

Instead of：Churn rate increased by 5%.

Try：A user told us she gave up after trying to verify her email 3 times. She's one of the many behind that 5% churn spike.

把数据背后的"人"说出来，情感链接就出现了。

## 汇报前先写一句话故事主线

This is a story about how we turned a failing feature into our best conversion tool.

This is a story about finding out what users really wanted—not what we thought they wanted.

用这句话把整个汇报穿起来，讲出来才不散。

不会讲故事的人，只能讲数据；会讲故事的人，能讲出价值、意义和影响力。外企Presentation，赢的不是你说了多少，而是别人记住了什么。
