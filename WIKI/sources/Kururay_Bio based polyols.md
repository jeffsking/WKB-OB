# Kururay_Bio based polyols.pdf

kuraray
Diol & Polyols
Bio-based polyols

Kuraray Polyol:
Kuraray provides Polyester Polyols (P and F series) and Polycarbonate Polyols (C series) which have unique and beneficial properties. Kuraray Polyols bring outstanding features to polyurethane technologies in softness, transparency, low compression set, durability and more.

What are Kuraray bio-based polyols?
Kuraray Polyol P-50 series are bio-based polyols made from sebacic acid, derived from castor oil, and MPD. These bio-based polyols have a biomass content of approximately 60%.

MPD + Sebacic acid from Castor oil →

Kuraray P-50 series
Kuraray Polyol P-50 series is amorphous and transparent with well-balanced performance in terms of water resistance and flexibility.

Features of PU made from Kuraray Polyol P-2050 (5 Very Good / 4 Good / 3 Fair / 2 Poor / 1 Very poor):
Heat resistance 5；Softness 4；Hydrolytic stability 5；Abrasion resistance 3；Low Compression Set 5；Low Water absorption 5。

Benefits for:

PUD: Solvent reduction
Composition: Polyol (Mw:2000)/DMPA/IPDI = 1/1/3 (molar ratio)；Solvent: MEK。
kg-Solvent/MT：PBA 300；Kuraray P-2050 250。
Using less solvent is possible as Kuraray Polyol P-2050 has a lower viscosity than conventional polyols.

TPU: Lower water absorption
Composition: Polyol (Mw:2000) : MDI : 14BD = 1:3:2；Specimen - 10 mm * 20 mm * 2 mm；Immersion in water for 4 days (room T°C)；Measurement method: Weight change。
Water absorption (g/m2)：Kuraray P-2050 0.0；PBA 8.2；PCL 7.0。
Lower water absorption reduces pre-drying time and can potentially reduce energy consumption.

Note:
PTMEG: poly tetramethylene ether glycol
PBA: poly (1,4-butylene adapate)-diol
PCL: polycaprolactone
DMPA: dimethylolpropionic acid
MDI: methylene diphenyl diisocyanate
Kuraray Polyol P-50: MPD/sebacate type, bio-based
PUD: polyurethane dispersion
TPU: thermoplastic polyurethane
MEK: methyl ethyl ketone
