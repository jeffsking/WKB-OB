# GHC 福建冠泓·冠和 公司介绍（KC 2026）

## 一句话定位

**GHC（福建冠泓工业 + 冠和卫生用品，泉州晋江）** 面向 Kimberly-Clark 的 2026 年公司介绍，主体为**纺粘/热风非织造布**及其**柔软、压花、弹性、凉感**四条技术平台。本批作为「非织造布供应商」信息源。

## 摘要

GHC groups two Jinjiang (Quanzhou, Fujian) companies: **Fujian Guanhong Industrial Co., Ltd.**（Yonghe town；约 160 名员工，厂区约 49,101 m²）and **Guanhe Hygiene Products Co., Ltd.**（Longhu town；约 140 名员工，约 86,000 m²），two sites roughly 2 km apart. Timeline: Guanhong founded **2003**, roughly one new line per year **2010–2017**, nonwoven technology R&D centre **2018**, Guanhe plant **2019–2023**; the deck shows **SGS** certification marks. Production equipment cited: **Beijing Hongda** and **Reifenhäuser** lines at 3,200 mm, plus **Trützschler / TW Jiesheng** for ATB and other capacity. Four technology platforms are presented:

1. **Soft NW** — proprietary **fine-denier formulation** on the **Reicofil RF5** platform with a **unique calender** (claimed unique among RF5 lines worldwide), using a material that slows crystallisation; advanced open-dot embossing; continuous-filament structure with no hard points.
2. **Embossing NW** — single/double-layer 3D patterns (pearl, four-leaf clover, waffle, diamond) at 22–72 gsm; noted difficulty is short roll length → spooling introduced.
3. **Elastic NW** — **SEBS** replacing traditional PU; MD elastic 70 gsm (2.5×) and CD elastic 40 gsm (>4.0×); positioned as **zero glue / zero elastic band**, breathable, "hug fit".
4. **Cooling NW** — **microencapsulated PCM (28 °C)** applied by **immersion coating** on 18–30 gsm ATB/spunbond, dry add-on 6–15 g/m²; **Qmax 0.2–0.32 W/cm²** (GB/T 35263-2017), enthalpy **25–45 J/g**; difficulty is PCM pick-up and capsule-wall strength (leakage).

ATB grades shown: 18 gsm phobic / 23–24 gsm philic (incl. olive-essence variant). SSMMS hydrophilic at 7 gsm.
