# PP Handbook — 核心概念词典 (PP Handbook Concepts)

说明：概念术语首次出现采用 英文原文 + 中文释义；定义尽量保留原文表述，避免意译失真。因本输入为预览版，第4–20章正文未完整收录，概念主要来自 Ch.1–3 原文 + Preface 摘要 + 交叉引用。

## 一、PP 材料基础类

### 1. Polypropylene (PP)

中文释义：聚丙烯，一种热塑性聚合物
原文定义 (Ch.2, p.11): "Polypropylene (PP) is a thermoplastic material that is produced by polymerizing propylene molecules, which are the monomer units, into very long polymer molecule or chains."
关键属性：密度约 0.9 g/mL（Ch.2 p.34）、熔点较高、刚性较好、冲击性能优良。

### 2. Semicrystalline (半结晶)

中文释义：同时包含结晶区和非晶区的热塑性材料
原文 (Ch.2, p.12): "Semicrystalline PP is a thermoplastic material containing both crystalline and amorphous phases."
作用：结晶/非晶比例决定 PP 的刚度、热性能、透明度。

### 3. Isotactic PP (i-PP) / Atactic PP (a-PP)

中文释义：等规聚丙烯（可结晶、商品化主体）/ 无规聚丙烯（不可结晶、粘性副产物）
原文 (Ch.2, p.11): "'isotactic' (crystallizable) PP (i-PP) and 'atactic' (noncrystallizable) PP (a-PP)."

### 4. Homopolymer PP (HPP) / Random Copolymer (RCP) / Impact Copolymer (ICP)

中文释义：均聚物 / 无规共聚物（含 1–8% 乙烯）/ 抗冲共聚物（含 45–65% 乙烯相）
原文 (Ch.2, p.12): "Polypropylene containing only propylene monomer in the semicrystalline solid form is referred to as homopolymer PP (HPP)...random copolymer (RCP)...impact copolymer (ICP)."
卫材关联：纺粘工艺通常用 CR 均聚物 HPP（MFR≈35 g/10min）；熔喷用高 MFR 均聚物（MFR 500+ g/10min）。

### 5. Tacticity (立构规整度)

中文释义：聚合物链上取代基的立体排列规律
原文 (Ch.2, p.12): "These properties can be varied...by altering the chain regularity (tacticity) content and distribution."

### 6. Crystallite / Lamella / Spherulite

中文释义：晶粒（lamella，片晶）/ 球晶
原文 (Ch.2, p.13): "PP polymer chains weave up and down into close-packed arrays called crystallites ('little crystals'), which are called lamella by morphologists."

### 7. Tie Molecule（系带分子）

中文释义：连接相邻晶粒的非晶区分子链，承担应力传递
原文 (Ch.2, p.13): "The soft strands linking the pieces of stiff cardboard are polymer chains that exit one crystallite, enter another..."

## 二、加工与结构表征类

### 8. Melt Flow Rate (MFR, 熔体流动速率)

中文释义：以 ASTM/ISO 方法测定的聚合物流动性指标，间接反映平均分子量
原文 (Ch.2, p.13): "Melt flow rate is an indicator of the weight-average molecular weight as measured by the ASTM or ISO MFR test method."
卫材关联值：
- 纺粘（spun-bonded）：~35 g/10min
- 熔喷（melt-blown）：~500+ g/10min
- 长丝（staple）：10–30 g/10min
- 单丝（monofilament）：1–4 g/10min
- 注塑包装容器：~2.5 g/10min
- BOPP 膜：~3 g/10min

### 9. Molecular Weight Distribution (MWD, 分子量分布)

中文释义：聚合物分子量分布宽度
原文 (Ch.2, p.28): "A resin of narrow MWD is also desirable."（熔喷工艺要求）

### 10. Calender Rolls / Calender Bonding（压光/热轧粘合）

中文释义：通过加热的压光辊对非织造布进行热粘合
原文 (Ch.2, p.28): "Bonding of the fibers is accomplished by passing the fabric through heated calender rolls."
卫材关联：纺粘非织造布的主要热粘合方式。

## 三、非织造布工艺类

### 11. Spun-bonded（纺粘工艺）

中文释义：高 MFR 熔体纺丝 → 气流冷却拉伸 → 铺网 → 热轧粘合
原文 (Ch.2, p.28): "In this process, molten filaments are air quenched and then drawn by air at high pressure. To form a fabric, the filaments are then deposited on a moving porous belt to which a vacuum is applied underneath. Bonding of the fibers is accomplished by passing the fabric through heated calender rolls. The type of resin usually used is a CR homopolymer of about 35g/10min MFR."
特性：强度高、克重低、表面平整。
卫材应用：卫材面层/导流层。

### 12. Melt-blown（熔喷工艺）

中文释义：高 MFR 熔体经特殊喷丝板由高温高速气流拉伸成极细纤维，铺设到移动筛网上成布
原文 (Ch.2, p.28-29): "In the melt-blowing process, polymer is extruded through a special melt-blowing die. The die feeds high-temperature air to the exiting filaments at a high velocity, and, in addition, the exiting filaments are quenched with cool air...The melt-blowing process requires resins that have very high melt flow rates, on the order of 500g/10min or higher."
特性：纤维极细、柔软、蓬松度高、强度低。
关键功能：透湿阻液（"allow the passage of water vapor but prevent the penetration of liquid water and aqueous solutions"）。
卫材应用：卫材吸液层隔湿、医用防护服过滤层。

### 13. Thermobonded（热轧粘合法）

中文释义：短纤维铺网后经热轧粘合
原文 (Ch.2, p.28): "There are three types of nonwoven fabrics: thermobonded, from staple fibers; spun-bonded; and melt-blown."

### 14. SMMS / SMS 结构（非织造布组合结构）

中文释义：Spun-bond/Melt-blown/Melt-blown/Spun-bond 等组合；原文提及 "often combinations of two types are used together."
原文 (Ch.2, p.28): "The fabrics from each process differ from each other in properties and appearance, and often combinations of two types are used together."

## 四、复合与偶联类

### 15. Chemical Coupling Agent（化学偶联剂）

中文释义：用于改善无机填料/玻纤与 PP 基体界面结合的多功能高分子
原文 (Ch.3, p.51): "Chemical coupling involves two types of interactions that provide effective stress transfer from the relatively weak polymer matrix to filler reinforcement to give improved composite mechanical and thermal properties."

### 16. Maleated Polypropylene (PPgMAH)

中文释义：马来酸酐接枝聚丙烯，最常用的 PP 复合偶联剂
原文 (Ch.3, p.37): "It is estimated that around 90% of short fiberglass reinforced polypropylene composites contain maleated polypropylene."
高效性 (Ch.3, p.57): "...mechanical property improvement with as little as 1% addition."

### 17. Acrylic Acid Grafted Polypropylene (PPgAA)

中文释义：丙烯酸接枝聚丙烯偶联剂
原文 (Ch.3 提要): 与 PPgMAH 并列的两大接枝体系。

### 18. Interphase / Interface（界面/过渡相）

中文释义：填料与基体间的过渡层，承担应力传递
原文 (Ch.3, p.54): "As the molecular weight of the coupling agent increased, so did the thickness of the interphase layer along with the mechanical properties."

### 19. Transcrystalline Region（横晶区）

中文释义：玻纤表面诱导形成的横晶，增强界面
原文 (Ch.3, p.54): "Evidence for transcrystalline regions next to the glass fiber has been observed. Increases in transcrystallinity and decreased spherulite size have resulted in the enhancement of tensile strength."

### 20. Debonding（界面脱粘）

中文释义：未偶联复合体系中玻纤与基体界面分离
原文 (Ch.3, p.52): "...there is evidence at the base of the glass fiber that debonding has occurred between the glass fiber and the polymer."

## 五、填料与增强类

### 21. Talc（滑石粉）

中文释义：层状硅酸盐矿物填料，用于 PP 增强/增刚
关键发展 (Preface): R-Talc 新品提升 TPO 复合材料的抗划伤和抗冲击性；Zero Force 技术用于微粉造粒。

原文 (Preface): "composites with enhanced mechanical properties and minimum warpage of mold parts."
