## Technical Field 技术领域

**Non-electronic, visually-read health indication integrated into a disposable absorbent article（集成到一次性吸收制品的非电子目视健康指示）** — a **window** in the article's outer surface allowing a **removable thermochromic sensor** to be read from outside.

Conditions the sensor may indicate（说明书列举）: "**body temperature, environmental temperature, article humidity or wetness, pH, skin condition, analyte levels, etc.**"

## Core Mechanism 核心机理

**Thermochromic clearing-point transition read through a window（透过窗口读取的热致变色 clearing point 转变）** — two distinct chromic mechanisms are described:

1. **Cholesteric liquid crystal, clearing-point type（胆甾型液晶·清亮点型，温度不敏感型）** — "常温有色、升温变透明": at normal temperature the LC reflects a colour; on reaching its **clearing point** it becomes **transparent**. This is the mechanism used in the worked EXAMPLE.
2. **Temperature-sensitive liquid crystal（温度敏感型液晶）** — colour sequence "无色→红→橙→黄→绿→蓝→紫→无色" as temperature rises.
3. **Thermochromic inks / leuco dyes（热致变色油墨 / 隐色染料）** — leuco dyes "可直接混入薄膜、无纺布和弹性体"（may be incorporated directly into films, nonwovens and elastomers）.
4. **The optical trick（图形翻转机理）** — the LC layer is **transparent above** the trigger, so it **reveals what is underneath**. In the EXAMPLE: an **overlayment printed with a smiley face in black ink and a frowning face in green ink**, over a **black underlayment**. Below ~100 °F the LC **reflects green** (appears green) → green frown is masked / smiley reads; above ~100 °F the LC turns **transparent** → the **black underlayment** shows through → the graphic flips from **smiling to frowning**. （源文描述：绿色液晶变透明，露出黑色底层，图形由"微笑"变为"皱眉"）

**No battery, no circuit** — this is the non-electronic branch of "smart" diaper sensing.

## Hygiene Implication 卫材关联

1. **The closest competitive patent to the chromic-ink programme's "smart but non-electronic" branch.** P&G's architecture delivers a **battery-free, circuit-free visual readout** — the same value proposition as a chromic wetness/comfort indicator. It is the benchmark to read before positioning our own chromic concept. See [[Thermochromic & Hydrochromic Materials for Diapers (Feasibility Review v3)]] and [[Smart Sensing Diaper Technology Research Report（智能感知纸尿裤技术研究报告 · 2026-04）]].
2. **Two different chromic jobs — don't conflate them.** Here thermochromics measure **body temperature (~37.8 °C / 100 °F)**, i.e. a **health/wellness** signal. Our programme targets **wetness/comfort**. The trigger windows are adjacent but distinct: **99.5–102 °F (≈37.5–38.9 °C)** here vs **95–104 °F (35–40 °C)** in [[US20070270773A1 Thermochromic Wetness Indicator]] and **>87 °F (≈30.6 °C)** in [[US5197958 Wetness Indicating Diaper]]. A wetness indicator tuned to fire at body temperature risks being indistinguishable from a fever indicator — worth an explicit design decision.
3. **Clearing-point LC vs leuco dye — a genuine material choice.** The EXAMPLE uses an **unencapsulated cholesteric LC** with **±0.1 °C accuracy, ±0.05 °C repeatability, ~50 μm coating and ~30 s lag**. That precision is far tighter than the **1–2 °C sensitivity** disclosed for the leuco-dye/fatty-acid system in [[US5197958 Wetness Indicating Diaper]]. If the product requirement is a **sharp, single-threshold endpoint**, the LC route is technically superior; if it is a **cheap, printable, robust graphic**, the leuco-dye route wins. This is the key supplier question — take it to [[Hygiene Chromic Ink Supplier Landscape（卫生护理变色油墨供应商调研 · 2026-06-12）]].
4. **Real numbers to reuse as acceptance criteria.** **±0.1 °C accuracy / better than ±0.05 °C repeatability / ~30 s response / ~50 μm coat weight / 1.5 cm read-out diameter / sensor centre 4–5 cm below the front edge (Pampers size 2)** — these are quotable, source-backed specification targets for any chromic patch we specify.
5. **Integration options: window + removable patch vs direct printing.** The claimed route needs a **translucent/transparent window (PE/PP/PET/PS/PVC/PU/PC/polyacrylate/PTFE)** plus a removable attachment (adhesive or hook-and-loop) with an optional cover/flap. That means **backsheet开窗 + 防渗 + 粘合工序** — added converting complexity and a leak-path risk. A **directly printed chromic graphic on the outer backsheet** avoids the window entirely. That trade-off is a core process decision for our programme, and it is the same "don't modify the core" logic seen in [[US20070270773A1 Thermochromic Wetness Indicator]].
6. **Printing process list maps to existing converting capability.** Gravure, flexo, inkjet, slot and screen are named for applying thermochromic material — useful when screening which suppliers can run on our lines.
7. **Reversibility and hysteresis are explicit here.** "Clearing point formulation **with hysteresis**" and a **~30 s lag** mean the display does not snap back instantly. For a wetness indicator this raises the same consumer-perception risk flagged on the Howell pages: a signal that fades or lags can read as "dry again".
8. **Safety/regulatory: NOT addressed by this source.** No migration, skin-contact, sensitisation or toxicology data is disclosed, and the exemplified LC is **unencapsulated** — direct-contact qualification would be required for anything worn against or near skin. Flagged `[待验证]`; settle from supplier dossiers and internal testing.
9. **Freedom-to-operate（自由实施）: NOT assessed — no conclusion is drawn.** Recorded from the source only: filed 2007-07-20, granted 2012-09-25, **PTA 920 days**, **subject to a terminal disclaimer**. **Expiry, maintenance, and the scope of any WO/EP/CN/JP family members are not established by this document** — the source states no family list was found in the text（"[未找到原文]"）— and are flagged `[待验证]` / manual review.
