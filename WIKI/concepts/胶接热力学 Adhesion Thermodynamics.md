# 胶接热力学 Adhesion Thermodynamics

## Definition 定义

**定义待补（引用来源未给出明确定义）** —— the cited-by source names **胶接热力学** only as one of the **coverage topics** of the *Handbook of Adhesion* (Packham, 2nd ed.), listed alongside surface forces, wetting, fracture mechanics, adhesion theories and quantitative measurement; it does not give a formal definition（源：[[Book Review Handbook of Adhesion by Packham]]）.

**工作口径（可溯源的操作内容）**: within the adhesion literature, the thermodynamic layer is the **thermodynamic work of adhesion（Dupré）** — `W = γ_S + γ_L − γ_SL`, measurable from the contact angle via Young's equation — which sits **below** the mechanical strength layer（源：[[Adhesive Handbook Part 2 Fundamental Aspects]]）.

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

- **Core thesis**: adhesive strength is governed by **viscoelastic dissipation**, not just interfacial thermodynamics — so **peel rate and temperature are the primary variables**（源：[[Handbook of Adhesive Technology Full Report]]、[[Adhesive Handbook Part 2 Fundamental Aspects]]）.
- **Flexible / semirigid adhesives** produce joints with higher short-term strength and fracture toughness than rigid adhesives, by expanding the fracture process zone and lowering peak stress（源：[[Adhesive Handbook Part 2 Fundamental Aspects]]）.
- **Thermodynamics is the lower layer**: the Dupré work of adhesion enters the strength only through G₀ ∝ W_A², which is dominated in service by the ψ(v,T) dissipation term（源：[[Adhesive Handbook Part 2 Fundamental Aspects]]）.
- **Six adhesion theories are complementary, not competing**: "each of these theories is valid to some extent… they do not negate each other"（源：[[Adhesive Handbook Part 2 Fundamental Aspects]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **Temperature and peel rate directly determine core retention**; the formulation target is **designing the ψ(v,T) peak, not just interfacial thermodynamics**（源：[[Handbook of Adhesive Technology Full Report]]）.
- Baby scratching and wearing motion are **high-speed peel** events — the ψ(v,T) peak position decides "no leak at low speed, no delamination at high speed"（源：[[Adhesive Handbook Part 2 Fundamental Aspects]]）.
- **Wet-strength / shelf-life**: hygiene products sit long-term in high-humidity urine / sweat; the Si-O-M primary bonds from [[硅烷偶联剂 Silane Coupling Agent]]（50–250 kcal/mol）markedly improve post-immersion laminate stability（源：[[Adhesive Handbook Part 2 Fundamental Aspects]]）.
