定义待补（引用来源未给出明确定义）。（No cited page gives a formal definition of "preservative"; the pages treat it as a regulated functional group — preservatives are handled via positive/permitted lists and a maximum-use-concentration table rather than a definition.）

What the sources **do** state（源侧口径）：
- In the Chinese cosmetic framework, preservatives appear as a **listed restricted category（限用防腐剂）** — GB 7916's 表4 covers 苯氧乙醇、Parabens、山梨酸、苯甲酸。（源：[[中国化妆品卫生标准体系（GB 7916 表1-表6 清单结构）]]）
- Cosmetic Science and Technology positions preservation as a system topic: **防腐剂体系、防腐挑战值、微生物控制**（preservative system, preservative-challenge value, microbial control）.（源：[[Cosmetic Science and Technology]]）

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理
- **Positive-list + maximum-concentration model（准用清单 + 上限浓度）**: preservatives are managed by an approved list with a per-substance maximum, not a functional definition — China's 2015 规范 lists **51 permitted preservatives** and a separate restricted table.（源：[[化妆品安全技术规范（2015年版）]]）
- **Sum rule Σ ≤ 1（叠加规则）**: when two or more substances from 表3–表6 with the same action are used together, the sum of (actual use ÷ listed limit) must not exceed 1 — the source flags this as **the most easily missed calculation when formulating a preservative blend**; checking single-item compliance alone under-judges.（源：[[中国化妆品卫生标准体系（GB 7916 表1-表6 清单结构）]]）
- **Analytical determination（含量测定）**: the 2015 spec's chromatographic method targets 12 preservative components — 甲基氯异噻唑啉酮、2-溴-2-硝基丙烷-1,3-二醇、甲基异噻唑啉酮、苯甲醇、苯氧乙醇、4-羟基苯甲酸甲酯、苯甲酸、4-羟基苯甲酸乙酯、异丙酯、丙酯、异丁酯、丁酯 — with retention times as the qualitative/quantitative basis.（源：[[化妆品安全技术规范（2015年版）·检验方法篇]]）
- **Haematotoxicity as the key toxicology endpoint（血液毒性为主要毒理特征）**: for phenoxyethanol, dermal exposure is the main cosmetic-relevant route and the rabbit is the most sensitive species; SCCS judged 1.0% as a preservative **safe**, covering susceptible age groups via MoS (adult ≈130, child ≈50).（源：[[SCCS Final Opinion on Phenoxyethanol in Cosmetic Products (SCCS-1575-16, 2016)]]）

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联
- **Wipes lotion formulation（湿巾配方）**: preservative selection is a core wet-wipes formulation decision; Cosmetic Formulation of Skin Care Products and Cosmetic Science and Technology both cross-reference hygiene products via **防腐体系、非织造基材卫生学、微生物控制**.（源：[[Cosmetic Formulation of Skin Care Products]]）；（源：[[Cosmetic Science and Technology]]）
- **Regulatory track split（法规轨道分野）**: 湿巾/卫生用品走 **GB 15979 + GB 38598**，与化妆品体系不同轨 — the cosmetic positive lists are the analogue, not the governing text, for hygiene wipes.（源：[[中国化妆品卫生标准体系（GB 7916 表1-表6 清单结构）]]）
- **Children's paper products（婴幼儿纸品化学门槛）**: GB 43631-2023 is the mandatory chemical-safety floor for children ≤6 paper products, on which wipes / diapers / tissue SKUs must clear rows for heavy metals, FWAs, acrylamide, formaldehyde, solvents, **preservatives**, phthalates and aromatic amines.（源：[[GB 43631-2023 General Safety Technical Specification for Infants and Children Paper Products（婴幼儿及儿童用纸品基本安全技术规范）]]）
- **Supplier COA / MSDS obligation（供应商文件义务）**: GB/T 36420-2018 requires suppliers to deliver quality certificates plus MSDS (or a CAS-anchored ingredient list); the source notes **苯氧乙醇 ≤1.0% 的湿巾限值与化妆品规范一致**.(源：[[GB-T 36420-2018 Management System for Safety Evaluation of Chemicals and Raw Materials（生活用纸和纸制品 化学品及原料安全评价管理体系）]]）
