# 全息存储 Holographic Storage

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 2 citing sources in the vault. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced in the vault is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

In this vault the term is used in the sense of **holographic recording** — writing a **periodic (grating) structure** into a photopolymer film so that the film develops **structural colour**. The anchor source defines it as a recipe space for "holographically written structural-colour films that shift colour monotonically with strain": **holographic recording writes a periodic structure into a PEGDA-TDE film, giving mechanochromic structural colour with a monotonic strain → wavelength map**（源：[[Holographic Structurally Colored Mechanochromic Films]]）. Note the vault does **not** use the term for optical **data** storage — no such content was found（待补：术语与"全息数据存储"无来源支持）.

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

- **Recording → periodicity → colour.** Holographic exposure writes a periodic grating into the film; the grating period sets the reflected wavelength, so colour is produced **without pigment**, by periodic structure（源：[[Holographic Structurally Colored Mechanochromic Films]]）.
- **Strain reads out as a blue-shift.** Stretching the film changes the grating period; 0 → 20% strain blue-shifts the peak ~58 nm and drops reflectance ~68%, giving **three simultaneous read-out channels** (λmax, reflectance, relative bandwidth ΓR/λP)（源：[[Holographic Structurally Colored Mechanochromic Films]]）.
- **The process window is wide and quantitative** — thickness 30–190 µm, dose 1020–5100 mJ/cm², recording monomer 10–60% w/w, PEGDA/TDE 1.2–1.8, angle 0/15/30/45°（源：[[Holographic Structurally Colored Mechanochromic Films]]）.
- **Source caveat.** The compiled page is built **from the paper's Supporting Information only** (Fig. S1–S17, Table S1–S5); one OCR/figure-title contradiction is flagged (Fig. S9）— verify against the original PDF before quoting（源：[[Holographic Structurally Colored Mechanochromic Films]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **Anti-counterfeit / brand label.** Colour arises from holographic recording, tunable by recording angle (0/15/30/45°) and by a large thickness/dose window — a hard-to-copy, angle-dependent optical feature suited to premium diaper/wipes packaging anti-counterfeit and traceability (推演)（源：[[Holographic Structurally Colored Mechanochromic Films]]）.
- **Humidity-stable visual label.** Reflectance spectra remain measurable at **RH 100% and 80 °C for 120 h**, a precondition for a label surviving the wear environment (body heat, urine humidity) and summer storage. **Caveat: the SI only proves *non-degradation*, not humidity *response* — it cannot be used directly as a humidity sensor**（源：[[Holographic Structurally Colored Mechanochromic Films]]）.
- **Route to a wetness indicator.** To make it humidity-responsive, a swelling component (e.g. an SAP-type water network) would have to be introduced into the PEGDA matrix so strain arises on moisture uptake — a **formulation change, not a direct carry-over** (推演)（源：[[Holographic Structurally Colored Mechanochromic Films]]）.
- **SAP-network same-origin.** PEGDA is a common cross-linking monomer in superabsorbent / hydrogel systems, and the page's "参数 → 反射率 SD" calibration approach is proposed as transferable to SAP network DOE (cross-link density, molecular weight, absorbency/mechanics trade-off) (推演)（源：[[Holographic Structurally Colored Mechanochromic Films]]）.
