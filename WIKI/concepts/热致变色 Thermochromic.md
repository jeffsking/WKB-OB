# 热致变色 Thermochromic

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 9 citing sources in the vault. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced in the vault is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

**Thermochromic（热致变色）** is a reversible colour change triggered by **temperature**, used in diapers as a **thermal-mediation wetness/comfort indication** route（以温度为媒介的尿布湿态指示）rather than a hydrochromic one（源：[[US5197958 Wetness Indicating Diaper]]）. The mainstream system is a **reversible leuco-dye microcapsule**: **colouring matter（leuco dye，供电子体，本身无色）+ colour-developing matter（受电子体：phenol compound or acid clay）+ desensitizer（溶剂）**, where the **discolouring temperature corresponds to the melting (fusing) temperature of the desensitizer**（源：[[US5197958A & US5389093A Wetness Indicating Diaper (Howell)]]）. Below threshold the system is stable; at or above threshold electron transfer / phase change occurs, and the change reverses on cooling（源：[[Thermochromic & Hydrochromic Materials for Diapers (Feasibility Review v3)]]）.

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

**Thermal-mediation chain（热媒介机理链）：** urine/body waste contacts the article → **local temperature rises** → the thermochromic compound crosses its transition → **visual colour change**. Claim 8 of US20070270773A1 requires activation "upon exposure to **temperature change generated by urine, and/or bodily wastes and/or bodily excretions**, either with **direct or indirect contact**"（源：[[US20070270773A1 Thermochromic Wetness Indicator]]）.

**The Howell insulation principle:** an insulation layer between wearer and outer shell is **insufficiently thermally conductive when dry** for normal body temperature to raise the outer surface above the threshold, but **sufficiently conductive when wet** to do so（源：[[US5389093 Wetness Indicating Diaper System]]、[[US5197958A & US5389093A Wetness Indicating Diaper (Howell)]]）.

**Two further thermochromic mechanisms** are documented on the windowed-sensor page: (1) **cholesteric liquid crystal, clearing-point type（胆甾型液晶·清亮点型）** — "常温有色、升温变透明"; (2) **temperature-sensitive liquid crystal（温度敏感型液晶）** — colour sequence "无色→红→橙→黄→绿→蓝→紫→无色"（源：[[US8273939 Windowed Removable Sensor Absorbent Article]]）. **Leuco dyes may be incorporated directly into films, nonwovens and elastomers**（source: same page）.

**Feasibility framing:** the review defines three routes all enabled by microencapsulation — **R1 thermochromic ink/pigment**（printed stripe or laminated label patch; reversible for comfort cues）, **R2 hot-melt wetness glue**（backsheet inner face; urine triggers irreversible yellow/orange→blue; 84% of market）and **R3 hydrochromic ink**. Its stated verdict: **microencapsulation is the differentiator, not the colour**, and **the 'wet' route is mature while the 'thermal' route is the frontier**（源：[[Thermochromic & Hydrochromic Materials for Diapers (Feasibility Review v3)]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

Directly relevant to the **hygiene chromic-ink programme** — thermal-comfort and wetness indication layers on the outer surface of the article（[[湿感指示条 Wetness Indicator]]、[[Wetness Indicator Ladder（尿湿指示阶梯）]]、[[Active Diagnostic Zones（主动诊断分区）]]）. The route sits alongside [[光致变色 Photochromic]]、[[机械变色 Mechanochromic]]、[[电致变色 Electrochromic]] under [[Chromism Taxonomy（变色现象分类）]]. Primary thermo suppliers ranked by hygiene fit: **[[CTI (Chromatic Technologies)]]** and **[[Matsui Shikiso (松井)]]**（the latter with a formaldehyde caution）; wet-route suppliers Bostik / Henkel / H.B. Fuller（源：[[CTI (Chromatic Technologies)]]、[[Matsui Shikiso (松井)]]）. The **real feasibility risk is decoding, not chemistry**（源：[[Thermochromic & Hydrochromic Materials for Diapers (Feasibility Review v3)]]）and the indicator **must not touch skin**（源：[[智能尿裤功能层]]）.
