# 智能织物 Smart Textile

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 8 citing sources in the vault. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced in the vault is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

**No settled definition of smart textiles exists.** Some sources treat it as a single grouping; others split it into **smart** vs **intelligent**, where both sense stimuli but *intelligent* additionally responds with a **pre-programmed reaction**（源：[[Intelligent Textiles and Sports Clothing]]）。The working definition used by the smart-polymer literature is verbatim: "Smart or intelligent materials have the capability to sense changes in their environment and respond to the changes in a preprogrammed and pronounced way"（源：[[Smart Polymers Ch13 Affinity Precipitation of Proteins]]）。A three-tier taxonomy is used to grade ambition — **passive** smart textiles sense; **active** ones sense *and respond*; **ultra-smart** ones sense, react and adapt（源：[[Smart Fabric Textiles Recent Advances and Challenges]]）。Smart materials historically trace to 1800s reports of magnetostriction（源：[[General Introduction and Objectives]]）。

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

**Core architecture** is stated as **sensor + activator (starter) + control unit**, executing five functions — receiving the environmental stimulus, capturing parameters, data processing, initiating response, storing data/energy, and communication/response output（源：[[Intelligent Textiles and Sports Clothing]]）。Two insertion points in the garment manufacturing chain matter: **cutting** (separating plies destined for printing of smart components) and **sewing** (adding electronic components)（源：[[Intelligent Textiles and Sports Clothing]]）。

**Chromic classification by stimulus** names photochromic (light), thermochromic (heat) and electrochromic (applied voltage) textile applications: photochromics to notify UV radiation levels in solar protection; thermochromic wallpaper that "grows" flower buds when heated; photochromic pillow covers revealing a motif in sunlight — all reversing when the stimulus is removed（源：[[Smart Textiles and Their Applications]]）。The 2022 survey extends this to seven chromic mechanisms (see table above)（源：[[Smart Fabric Textiles Recent Advances and Challenges]]）。

**Embedded-electronics cases cited.** Electronics monitor heart, lungs and muscles; data stored locally or transferred wirelessly. Examples: an Australian **CSIRO** basketball shooting sleeve using rhythmic sound waves; a cycling garment with Bluetooth-driven grouped LEDs on the back showing direction, powered by batteries or small solar panels（源：[[Intelligent Textiles and Sports Clothing]]）。

**The integration thesis** is producing fibres with electronic function *without compromising strength, comfort and aesthetic appeal* — stated as the core challenge for seamless, invisible electronics（源：[[Smart Fabrics and Networked Clothing]]）。

**Cost verdict (negative result).** Both the active-fibre and electrochromic routes are explicitly blocked — on cost/integration and on lifetime/reliability respectively（源：[[Smart Textiles and Their Applications]]）。As-prepared CNT fibres are also reported to **fail** on softness and processability（源：[[Smart Fabrics and Networked Clothing]]）。

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

**Direct mechanism transfer — wetness indication.** The halochromic colour change demonstrated on polyamide fabric with a stated pH window is the direct mechanism ancestor of a urine indicator, alongside thermochromy which is what the programme actually uses（源：[[Smart Fabric Textiles Recent Advances and Challenges]]）。Concrete disposable implementations to read alongside: [[US20070270773A1 Thermochromic Wetness Indicator]] and the purchasable-ink view in [[Hygiene Chromic Ink Supplier Landscape（卫生护理变色油墨供应商调研 · 2026-06-12）]]。

**Fibre-level function without losing handle** is the one idea with genuine pull — making the fibre itself carry function while keeping softness, strength and flexibility is exactly the design problem a functional nonwoven faces（源：[[Smart Fabrics and Networked Clothing]]）。

**Adjacent SAP/ADL modelling.** The same smart-hydrogel physics is read across to a confined microfluidic system — an absorbent core is a superabsorbent hydrogel plus fibrous channels, and the parameter space (pore size, cross-link density, ionizable-group distribution, geometry) is the SAP/ADL parameter space（源：[[Smart Polymers Ch14 Hydrogels in Microfluidics]]）。SAP can be modelled as a dry hydrogel, with imbibition from 10–20% up to thousands of times dry weight and the chemical tie-point vs physical entanglement cross-link distinction framing cross-link density ↔ swelling ratio ↔ retention-under-load mapping（源：[[Smart Polymers Ch12 Polymeric Carriers Regional Therapy]]）。

**Contra-indications (where the analogy fails).** The sports-clothing review contains **no absorbency content at all** — nothing on fluff pulp, SAP, core structure, acquisition, strike-through or rewet; its performance axes are protection, thermoregulation, motion and electronics（源：[[Intelligent Textiles and Sports Clothing]]）。It also has **no polyolefin, bicomponent or ES-fibre content** — the named fibres (aramid, UHMWPE, polyester, PA) are outside the hygiene material set and serve permanent-garment rather than disposable function（源：[[Intelligent Textiles and Sports Clothing]]）。The CNT route is judged **premature for a disposable**（源：[[Smart Fabrics and Networked Clothing]]）。

**Trigger tolerances usable for design.** The narrow pH window (ΔpH ≤ 0.5) and the 0.4 M NaCl tolerance set budgets for any pH-active or ion-triggered acquisition layer — urine pH fluctuation must be managed to avoid false triggering（源：[[Smart Polymers Ch13 Affinity Precipitation of Proteins]]）。
