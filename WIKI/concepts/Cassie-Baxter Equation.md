# Cassie-Baxter Equation

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 4 citing sources. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

The **Cassie–Baxter** relation, as recorded in the vault：**cos θCB = f1 · cos θY + f2 · cos 180° = f1(cos θY + 1) − 1** — the **composite / gas-trapping** branch of non-ideal wetting（源：[[Surface Tension and Surface Energy]]）.

Its stated meaning：unlike Wenzel roughness（which amplifies the underlying tendency）, Cassie–Baxter lets a **modestly hydrophilic polymer behave hydrophobically through geometry** — *"a structured backsheet beats a chemically hydrophobic one"*（源：[[Surface Tension and Surface Energy]]、[[底膜 Backsheet]]）.

（中文：Cassie–Baxter 复合/截气模型 cos θCB = f1·cos θY + f2·cos 180°。与 Wenzel（放大原有倾向）相反，它让**中等亲水的聚合物靠几何结构表现为疏水** —— "结构化背层优于化学疏水"。）

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

1. **It is the gas-trapping branch, not the roughness branch.** Wenzel multiplies the base tendency（hydrophilic → more hydrophilic）; Cassie–Baxter is a **composite surface** in which the f2 fraction is a gas/liquid interface at **cos 180° = −1**, so an underlying hydrophilic polymer can present a **hydrophobic** apparent angle（源：[[Surface Tension and Surface Energy]]）.
2. **Design consequence — geometry beats chemistry.** *"Cassie–Baxter lets a modestly hydrophilic polymer behave hydrophobically through geometry — cheaper and more durable than chemistry"*, and it is the **design principle behind anti-rewet backsheet structures**（源：[[Surface Tension and Surface Energy]]、[[底膜 Backsheet]]）.
3. **The ideal SAP particle is a Cassie–Baxter doublet.** A thin hydrophobic shell（wax / siloxane）guarantees **dry flowability** while the hydrophilic core keeps θeq → 0 for maximum adhesion — flowability and absorption solved on one particle（源：[[Surface Tension and Surface Energy]]、[[SAP（高吸水树脂）]]）.
4. **Contrast with the Wenzel route.** The vault pairs the two as the rough vs composite branches of non-ideal wetting（源：[[Surface Tension and Surface Energy]]、[[Wenzel Equation]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **Anti-rewet backsheet design law**：a structured surface via Cassie–Baxter beats a chemically hydrophobic one — cheaper and more durable（源：[[底膜 Backsheet]]、[[Surface Tension and Surface Energy]]）.
- **Two barrier mechanisms, not one**：a film's barrier is a **solid wall**, a nonwoven's barrier is **capillary** — the film holds **> 1000 mbar** across all soil columns while the nonwoven collapses as surface tension falls; the two differ *"not by a little, but by two orders of magnitude"*（源：[[底膜 Backsheet]]）.
- **SAP dry-handling**：the Cassie–Baxter doublet（hydrophobic shell + hydrophilic core）is the vault's model for a SAP particle that flows dry yet wets fully（源：[[Surface Tension and Surface Energy]]、[[SAP（高吸水树脂）]]）.
