# 气铺 Airlaid

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 10 citing sources. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced in the vault is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

Airlaid（air-laid / airlaying，气铺/气流成网）is the **dry-laid web-formation route based on aerodynamic web formation**（气流成网）. The dry-laid chapter progresses from carding physics through feed/weight control to cross-lapping and perpendicular laying, and **"culminates in airlaying (aerodynamic web formation)"** whose raw-material science is **wood pulp, man-made fibres, superabsorbents (SAP) and bicomponent blends**（源：[[Dry-laid web formation]]）. In the hygiene handbook it is one of the forming processes matched to diaper components — the process matrix pairs **airlaid / spunbond / meltblown / spunlace / SMS** with the four parts (Top Sheet / Core / ADL / Back Sheet)（源：[[Absorbent Technology]]、[[Nonwovens in Absorbent Materials]]）.

The commercial product form is **airlaid paper（无尘纸）**, defined by the Chinese product standard as the material supplied as **jumbo rolls or sheets** for disposable hygiene products, used in **wipes, diaper acquisition/absorption layers and absorbent cores**, and split into **普通无尘纸 (cellulose-only)** and **合成无尘纸 (with SAP)**（源：[[GB-T 24292-2009 Airlaid Paper for Sanitary Products（卫生用品用无尘纸）]]）.

As a web it is **isotropic**, unlike carded webs — "Airlaid webs are isotropic; carded webs are not"（源：[[Dry-laid web formation]]）.

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

- **Aerodynamic web formation**（气流成网）: the endpoint of the dry-laid route; input materials are wood pulp, man-made fibres, SAP and bicomponent blends（源：[[Dry-laid web formation]]）.
- **Four-factor causal chain** for nonwoven absorbent structures: Constituent Fiber → Assembly Structure (Web Assemblage) → Type of Bonding → Finish — "Each of these factors can have an effect on the absorbency of the resulting structure"；采购评审须按序追问而非单一比价（源：[[Nonwovens in Absorbent Materials]]）.
- **Binding–absorbency trade-off**: most binders are hydrophobic and "the net effect usually is some reduction in absorbency"；mechanical bonding preserves intrinsic absorbency but limits swelling and improves compressive rebound（源：[[Nonwovens in Absorbent Materials]]）.
- **Fibre imbibition duality**: hydrophilic swelling fibres absorb by fibre imbibition + fibre swelling; hydrophobic fibres only via capillary and structure voids（源：[[Nonwovens in Absorbent Materials]]）.
- **Why nonwovens suit absorption**: two root reasons — high bulk（单位质量储液能力高）and low raw-material→finished conversion cost（原料-成品转换成本低）（源：[[Nonwovens in Absorbent Materials]]）.
- **Pre-formed vs in-line airlaid**（两条方向相反的趋势）: one source states preformed airlaid pulp fabric can be fed **directly to the converting line**, eliminating in-line pulp milling（"大而吵且粉尘"）；unit raw-material price is slightly higher, but the simplified integrated **total machine operating cost can offset it**（源：[[Nonwovens in Absorbent Materials]]）. Another source states **"在线 airlaid 芯（fluff + SAP）正在取代预制 airlaid 芯"**, a cross-category trend starting from femcare（源：[[High-Density Fluff Core（高密度绒毛浆芯体）]]）.
- **Modeling airlaid web-forming**: Chapter 4 (pp. 146–165; author Sach Kangovi, Simutel Associates) uses airlaid web-forming as one of two worked cases — physics-based modeling "is a powerful and cost-effective tool to study a problem with considerable accuracy in a short time" and "far more convenient way of investigation than experiments," chiefly because experiments "would require building many such expensive machines"（源：[[Computational Modeling in Absorbent Technology]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **ADL / acquisition layer**: hydrophobic synthetics preserve wet bulk; pulp + SAP blends form the nappy acquisition layer（源：[[Dry-laid web formation]]）. The ADL density gradient — upper 1/3 low density（大孔隙、快速获取）, lower high density（细密毛细管、高压差）— is the chapter-3 capillary-pressure principle engineered into the product（源：[[Nonwovens in Absorbent Materials]]）.
- **Composite-core build**: a typical composite-core stack is **Airlaid / SAM / TABCW / SAM / 硬挺非织造 / NW 芯体包覆**（源：[[Complex Core Landscape in China（2017-08-07 COCO F2F · 复合芯体格局与专利谱系）]]）.
- **Pre-formed SAF airlaid core sourcing**: [[Main S.p.A.（SOFTCARE HAF）]] is listed as one of two sources for **path B (pre-formed SAF Airlaid core direct purchase)**（另一来源为 TAL 自身），价值为"省去芯体开发环节，快速验证 SAF 概念"，适用于概念验证、小批量试产、快速进入市场（源：[[Main S.p.A.（SOFTCARE HAF）]]）.
- **SAP immobilisation**: SAP immobilises fluid under compression — the core must not rewet under buttock pressure（源：[[Dry-laid web formation]]）.
