## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

**Three classification axes（三条分类轴）**（源：[[Chromism Taxonomy（变色现象分类）]]）
1. **Trigger quantity（触发量）** — classify by what physical quantity arrives at the material, not by chemistry family.
2. **Reversibility（可逆性）** — reversible vs irreversible / one-shot；continuous monitoring needs a reversible route, a single alarm may use a semi-reversible or irreversible one。**"This is the axis that eliminates designs, not the chemistry family."**
3. **Threshold（阈值）** — industrial metrology materials are engineered for structural-failure detection above 40,000 psi, whereas absorbent-hygiene products live in a low-tension window (20–150 kPa for waist…[原文残缺])。

**Hygiene-usable classes（卫品可用类别，源：[[Chromism Taxonomy（变色现象分类）]]）**
- 温致 thermochromic（temperature）——leuco dye : developer : fatty acid ≈ 1:5:50 microcapsules and cholesteric liquid crystals; urine-wetness indication, **already in mass production**。
- 湿致 hydrochromic（water / humidity）——hydrophilic indicator + hygroscopic matrix；hydrogel swelling。
- 卤致 halochromic（pH / proton activity）——bromophenol blue, bromocresol green / purple；the main body of urine-wetness strips。
- 力致 mechanochromic、光致 photochromic（in hygiene, light is an **interference source rather than a function**）、电致 electrochromic、化学致 chemochromic（not yet landed in hygiene）。

**Ink routes（油墨路线，源：[[变色油墨]]）**
- **Thermochromic（温敏）**: mainstream is the **leuco-dye microcapsule system** — electron donor : electron acceptor : fatty acid ≈ 1:5:50, colour developed / erased via a reversible melt–crystallise transition; typically used for urine-wetness indication。
- **Mechanochromic（力敏）**: mechanical force changes molecular arrangement or photonic-crystal structure; representative applications are **stretch-triggered colour change**（K-C US7704589B2, **lapsed / 已失效**）and liquid-crystal-elastomer colour-changing fibre（machine-washable strain visualisation）。
- **Hydrochromic / chemical colorimetric（湿敏/化学比色）**: pH or ionic-strength change drives a colorimetric reaction（e.g. CN114209501A pH nano-microcapsule nanofibre membrane）。
- **Carrier form（载体形态）**: printed ink / coating（screen-printed on the outer backsheet, elastic side panels, etc.）or a loading phase of a film / fibre substrate; the functional layer is mostly on the **non-skin side**, so material-safety requirements are comparatively lower。

**Mechanochromic candidate short-list（2026-09-29 addendum，源：[[变色油墨]]）**
- HPC（viscoelastic lattice-spacing change → compression blue-shift；edible / non-toxic / degradable）
- Naphthopyrans（covalent bond cleavage → modular colour change；best stability and synthesis accessibility）
- Spiropyrans（6π electrocyclic ring-opening → strong colour；**thermochromic transient**）
- Cu-based HCP（luminescence shift；resistant to environmental contamination）
- Common bottleneck = the **force gap**: industrial mechanophores need **40,000 PSI**, the hygiene target is only **20–150 kPa**；a "stabilization bridge"（gelling agent, e.g. gelatin）is needed to shift the rheology and lower the threshold。

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **Wetness indication（尿湿指示）**: halochromic colour change demonstrated on polyamide fabric with a stated pH window is the direct mechanism ancestor of a urine indicator, alongside thermochromy — which is what the programme actually uses（源：[[Smart Fabric Textiles Recent Advances and Challenges]]）。
- **Selection criterion**: hydrochromic is the **only class dedicated to diaper wetness display**, hence the first screening condition in ink selection；baro-chromic ink is already on the CTI product line, creating a category overlap with the mechanochromic direction（源：[[Hygiene Chromic Ink Supplier Landscape（卫生护理变色油墨供应商调研 · 2026-06-12）]]）。
- **Supply-base structure**: a "three-tier" structure — international benchmark buys **technical certainty**, domestic main force buys **cost**, regional players buy **responsiveness**（源：[[Hygiene Chromic Ink Supplier Landscape（卫生护理变色油墨供应商调研 · 2026-06-12）]]）。Named profiles in that source include **Qiansebian / 千色变**（国内主力；湿敏墨水 for diaper wetness labels）+ **Intellego Technologies AB**（国际标杆；expanding India plant capacity）+ **H.B. Fuller**（mentioned only by the 2026-06-12 report；no cooperation, sampling, validation or procurement record in-vault）（源：[[Qiansebian（千色变新材料）]]、[[Intellego Technologies AB]]、[[H.B. Fuller（富乐）]]）。
- **Where to print**: US10905605 is a "where-to-print" reference — a chromic wetness / comfort indicator placed on a stretch laminate must meet the same **relaxed-state legibility（松弛态可读）** and **print-durability（耐水/尿液不洇渗、不转移）** requirements（源：[[US10905605 Pull-On Wearable Article with Informational Image]]）。
- 关联概念（导航）：[[Water-Activated Ink（水变／湿变油墨）]]、[[Thermochromic Microcapsule (Leuco Dye System)]]、[[Chromism Taxonomy（变色现象分类）]]、[[Wetness Indicator Ladder（尿湿指示阶梯）]]、[[Mechanochromic变色纤维]]、[[Light-Scattering Masking Wetness Indicator（光散射遮蔽型尿湿指示）]]、[[Active Diagnostic Zones（主动诊断分区）]]。
