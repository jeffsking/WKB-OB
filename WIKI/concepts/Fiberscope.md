## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

In the virtual-prototyping methodology, physics-based modeling (penetration absorbency and airlaid web-forming worked cases) is closed-loop validated against experiments using high-speed video, Fiberscope/Videoimagescope, and static-pressure probes（源：[[Computational Modeling in Absorbent Technology]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

Fiberscope-type observation is part of the validation toolkit for virtual prototyping of absorbent products — replacing "building many such expensive machines" with model + targeted visual verification（源：[[Computational Modeling in Absorbent Technology]]）.
