# 胶接理论 Adhesion Theories

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 1 citing source, expanded with cross-referenced vault pages. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

**胶接理论（adhesion theories）** is the family of mechanisms that explain why an adhesive joint holds. Per the cited-by source, the theories are **complementary, not competing**: `"each of these theories is valid to some extent, depending on the nature of the solids in contact and the conditions of formation of the bonded system. Therefore, they do not negate each other"`（源：[[Adhesive Handbook Part 2 Fundamental Aspects]]）.

- **六大胶接理论（six theories）口径**: the vault records that there are **six** adhesion theories, used together rather than singly（源：[[卫材胶黏系统 Hygiene Adhesive Systems]]）.

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

- **Mechanical interlock dominates rubber–textile joints**: `"penetration of the protruding fiber ends into the rubber was the most important parameter in such adhesive joints"` — maps to hot-melt adhesive anchoring into pulp fibres / SAP particles（源：[[Adhesive Handbook Part 2 Fundamental Aspects]]）.
- **Adsorption / thermodynamic layer**: the Dupré work of adhesion W = γ_S + γ_L − γ_SL, measurable from the contact angle via Young's equation（源：[[Adhesive Handbook Part 2 Fundamental Aspects]]）.
- **Chemical bonding**: only **coupling agents** form primary bonds（50–250 kcal/mol）and can therefore deliver water-resistant bonding; organic coatings lose up to **85%** of initial adhesion under water-soaked conditions（源：[[Adhesive Handbook Part 2 Fundamental Aspects]]）.
- **Roughness is double-edged**: `"These unbonded regions limit joint performance by reducing both chemical and physical bonds and serving as stress concentrators."`（源：[[Adhesive Handbook Part 2 Fundamental Aspects]]）.
- **Flexible / semirigid adhesives** yield higher short-term strength and fracture toughness than rigid ones（源：[[Adhesive Handbook Part 2 Fundamental Aspects]]）.
- **Joint geometry**: an adhesive joint is not a thin line but a **three-dimensional "interphase"**（源：[[Adhesive Handbook Part 2 Fundamental Aspects]]、[[Handbook of Adhesive Technology Full Report]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **The six theories give no single answer for adhesive selection** — the choice must be judged by substrate and forming conditions（源：[[卫材胶黏系统 Hygiene Adhesive Systems]]）.
- **Mechanical interlock is the operative theory for core bonding**: hot-melt dots anchored into pulp fibres / SAP particles; anchoring depth is the key variable（源：[[Adhesive Handbook Part 2 Fundamental Aspects]]）.
- **Hydrolysis resistance** rests on the chemical-bond theory: only coupling agents（e.g. silane, Si-O-M primary bonds）deliver post-immersion laminate stability（源：[[Adhesive Handbook Part 2 Fundamental Aspects]]、[[硅烷偶联剂 Silane Coupling Agent]]）.
