## Definition 定义

定义待补（引用来源未给出完整定义）。

The vault's only structural statement is that **polyurethane is an addition polymerisation whose product nonetheless contains "condensation-type" repeat units** — used by the polymer source as the example that the addition/condensation split is not total ("There is a large but not total overlap")（源：[[Seymour Polymer Chemistry Ch06 Step-Reaction Polymerization]]）。Functionally, it appears in three hygiene forms — **elastic fibre/film, hot-melt/composite adhesive, and functional coating/membrane**（源：[[Polyurethane Elastomer Handbook]]）。

中文：本库未给聚氨酯的完整定义；唯一结构口径是「加成聚合但产物含缩合型重复单元」（Seymour 用它说明加成/缩合分类并不完全互斥）。功能上它在卫材中以弹性纤维/膜、热熔胶/复合胶、功能性涂层/膜三种形态出现。

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

**Elastomer chemistry and the casting route.** The PU-elastomer deck splits the material into **raw material → chemistry → classification → process → application**, with its practical value concentrated in the **casting type (CPU)**: raw-material selection criteria (**polyester vs polyether**), the process sensitivity of the **chain-extender coefficient and cure conditions**, and six process factors affecting performance（源：[[Polyurethane Elastomer Handbook]]）。

**Hard–soft segment microphase separation.** Spandex is a **polyurethane–polyester block copolymer**; the hard-soft segment microphase separation governs elastic recovery, which is why the TPE/TPU class combines *thermoplastic processing with rubber elasticity*（源：[[Seymour Polymer Chemistry Ch09 Copolymerization]]、[[Ebewole Polymer Science Ch15 Polymer Properties and Applications]]）。The same source names PU/TPE for **nonwoven bonding adhesives, elastic components and coatings**（源：[[Ebewole Polymer Science Ch15 Polymer Properties and Applications]]）。

**Waterborne shift.** Waterborne PU adhesives emit **no VOC** and are nonflammable, hence "more environmentally friendly"; the shift was driven by rising solvent prices（源：[[Adhesive Handbook Part 3 Adhesive Classes]]）。

中文：PU 弹性体按「原料→化学→分类→工艺→应用」拆分，实用价值集中在浇注型 CPU（聚酯/聚醚选型、扩链系数与固化敏感性）；氨纶是 PU–聚酯嵌段共聚物，硬-软段微相分离决定弹性回复；水性 PU 胶因无 VOC、不燃而更环保，趋势由溶剂涨价推动。

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **Three in-hygiene forms（三种形态，均关键）.** Elastic fibre/film（氨纶、PU elastic film for 弹性腰围 and 防漏隔边）、hot-melt/composite adhesive（PU 类胶）、and functional coating/membrane（透气膜、PU 膜复合）；the deck rates this **high relevance** as the raw-material/process basis for PU elastic parts and PU adhesives（源：[[Polyurethane Elastomer Handbook]]）。
- **Elastic components.** Spandex and SBS/SEBS TPE are named as the material basis of the **elastic waistband, leak-guard side cuffs and elastic ear tabs**（源：[[Seymour Polymer Chemistry Ch09 Copolymerization]]）。See [[氨纶干法纺丝（Spandex Dry Spinning）]]、[[无溶剂氨纶（Solvent-free Spandex）]]、[[卫材氨纶供应商对比（华峰千禧 vs 莱卡 HYFIT vs 泰和新材 vs 中柏 vs 河北邦泰）]]。
- **Microcapsule wall material.** PU / polyurea is the **high-end wall material** for chromic microcapsules (formaldehyde-free, fatigue-resistant, thin-and-strong)（源：[[温变湿变微胶囊技术参数对比表（X-Chromic 版 · 2026-06-18）]]）。See [[Microencapsulation]]、[[Thermochromic Microcapsule (Leuco Dye System)]]。
- **Adhesive class.** PU is a listed adhesive chemical class with a $960 M US market share (1998) and a waterborne, VOC-free route（源：[[Adhesive Handbook Part 3 Adhesive Classes]]）。
- **Contra-indication.** In flexible/stretchable electronics, PU is named as a substrate material alongside Si, polyimide and PDMS, and the vault explicitly states these **are not used in disposable hygiene constructions**（源：[[Flexible and Stretchable Electronics]]）。
