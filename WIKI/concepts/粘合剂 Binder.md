# 粘合剂 Binder

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 1 citing source, expanded with cross-referenced vault pages. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

**Binder（粘合剂）** is the adhesive polymer applied to a fibre web to consolidate it — the dominant alternative to thermal consolidation and the route of choice **when a broad property palette must be engineered**（柔软 / 防水 / 透气 / 阻燃 / 可回收）（源：[[Chemical bonding]]）. A binder **does far more than hold fibres**: it sets strength, stiffness, softness, waterproofness, breathability, flammability and end-of-life recyclability（源：[[Chemical bonding]]）.

- **In nonwoven absorbent structures**: most binders are hydrophobic, so **"the net effect usually is some reduction in absorbency"** — chemical bonding vs. mechanical bonding is a core material-selection trade-off（源：[[Nonwovens in Absorbent Materials]]）.
- **In airlaid cores（per STM-00429）**: binder is usually **absent in airlaid（气铺）cores**; in dry-laid（airlaid）materials it is the **bicomponent fibre that confers dry/wet strength**; in a near-100% SAM core it is **the glue that holds the SAM**（源：[[STM-00429 Void Volume as a Function of Saturation（方法正文）]]）.

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

- **Binder polymer chemistry**：vinyl, acrylic, butadiene（SBR / NBR / natural rubber）and specialty systems（PU, phenolic, epoxy）; emulsion polymerisation, MFFT, coagulation/migration and crosslinking（源：[[Chemical bonding]]）.
- **Chemical-bonding mechanism**：wetting → binder cohesion → adhesion → distribution → binder:fibre ratio, including the FRP（fibre-reinforced-plastic）transition at high add-on（源：[[Chemical bonding]]）.
- **Latices are the workhorse**：water-borne for low-viscosity penetration and energy-efficient drying（源：[[Chemical bonding]]）.
- **Copolymer Tg（Fox）sets handle and wet stability**：softer monomers → softer fabric（源：[[Chemical bonding]]）.
- **Binder migration during drying** creates a **binder-rich skin / starved core → delamination risk**（源：[[Chemical bonding]]、[[湿迁移 Wet Migration]]）.
- **Application methods**：saturation, foam, spray, print, coating, solution, followed by dryers（源：[[Chemical bonding]]）.
- **Contrast route**：[[Thermal bonding]] eliminates externally applied chemical adhesive in many cases — the dominant route for diaper topsheet/ADL and many wipes（源：[[Thermal bonding]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **Airlaid pulp is the binder-intensive hygiene substrate**: binder at 13–25% wt is **15–25% of airlaid purchase price** — a procurement negotiation focus（源：[[Nonwovens in Absorbent Materials]]）.
- **Explicit hygiene/medical and wipes applications**, including **flushable-wipe and incontinence binder chemistry**（源：[[Chemical bonding]]、[[可冲散拭布 Flushable Wipe]]）.
- **Bonding–absorbency trade-off**: chemical bonding（binder 多疏水）usually weakens absorbency; mechanical bonding preserves intrinsic absorbency but restricts swelling and raises compression recovery（源：[[Nonwovens in Absorbent Materials]]）.
