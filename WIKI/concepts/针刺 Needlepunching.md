# 针刺 Needlepunching

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 1 citing source ([[Mechanical bonding]]). All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义
Needlepunching is one of the three **mechanical bonding** consolidation technologies for nonwovens: fibres are mechanically interlocked into a fabric **without adhesive, thermal or chemical binders**（针刺是一种机械加固成布方式，不依赖粘合剂/热粘合/化学粘合剂，而是让纤维互锁）. The other two members of the family are **stitch-bonding 缝编** (Maliwatt / Malimo / Malipol) and **hydroentanglement 水刺**. （源：[[Mechanical bonding]]）

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理
- In the source chapter's organisation, the three mechanical-bonding routes are presented together; needlepunching targets **durable / industrial goods 耐用与工业制品**, while hydroentanglement is the growth engine most relevant to disposables. （源：[[Mechanical bonding]]）

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联
- 按来源定位，针刺主要服务耐用/工业用途；一次性卫材（disposables）相关的机械加固路线是水刺（见 [[水刺 Hydroentanglement]]）。针刺在本库中仅作为机械加固家族的分类学成员出现。 （源：[[Mechanical bonding]]）
