# 吸收芯体

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 10 citing sources in the vault. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced in the vault is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

The vault has no standalone generic definition of 吸收芯体 (the absorbent core); the working sense used across the citing sources is **the liquid-storing middle layer of a disposable absorbent article**, built from a fibre phase plus superabsorbent polymer. For the composite variant, the vault *does* give an explicit regulatory definition: 复合吸收芯体（Composite Absorbent Core）是**以无尘纸/无纺布包裹 SAP + 绒毛浆的预制芯**（a pre-formed core of airlaid/nonwoven wrapping around SAP plus fluff pulp）（源：[[QB-T 5650-2021 Composite Absorbent Core for Disposable Paper Sanitary Products（一次性纸制卫生用品用复合吸收芯体）]]）。In the REL ultra-thin architecture, the fibre phase is stated to be **绒毛浆（fluff pulp）+ ES/PE-PP 双组分热粘合纤维**（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch15 Acrylic Fibres]]）。

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

**Void-volume (VV) characterisation 孔隙体积表征.** Pore-space modulus (TTM-00038 / TTM-00437) measures the **resiliency 回弹性** of the absorbent core and intake-system materials after repeated compression cycles; its direct output is **thickness as a function of pressure**, from which VV is derived by post-processing. It applies to fluffy materials such as the absorbent core, ADL/surge and REL（源：[[TTM-00038 Pore Space Modulus to Void Volume Conversion（换算逻辑）]]）。

**Saturation-state display architecture 饱和度显示结构.** CN 116327488 B（露乐健康科技）lays a **饱和度显示层（saturation display layer）** between the absorbent core and the second topsheet: a substrate carrying a **reversible 温敏变色部 in the middle**（records voiding *count*）and an **irreversible 温敏变色部 at the end**（records voiding *total*）, plus a third **示温记录标签**. Claim 6 guarantees visibility by a transparent/windowed second topsheet, with the label offset from the two colour zones; claim 8's decision rule compares `n ≥ N`, `a ≥ A`, `t < T₂` — any two held true ⇒ [原文残缺]（源：[[CN 116327488 B（便于识别吸湿状态的吸收性物品及其方法·露乐健康科技）]]）。

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **Core as a distinct standard layer 芯体作为独立标准层级.** QB/T 5650-2021 places the composite absorbent core at its own层级, and its **吸收速度（≤40 s）比婴儿成品（≤50 s）更严**（源：[[QB-T 5650-2021 Composite Absorbent Core for Disposable Paper Sanitary Products（一次性纸制卫生用品用复合吸收芯体）]]）——芯体作为「中间品」层级是供应商准入与技术协议的直接判定依据。
- **Core covering process 芯体包覆工艺.** 热风法非织造布是纸尿裤吸收芯体包覆层的典型场景，其蓬松结构提供导流空间（源：[[无纺布五大工艺对比]]）；复合结构上，**热风棉 + SAP + 底膜**构成纸尿裤/卫生巾吸收芯体的整体结构（源：[[SMS复合结构]]）。
- **Interfaces with the intake system 与摄入系统的界面.** ADL 对芯体的三层职责为 ACQUIRE → DISTRIBUTE（MD 横向铺展）→ TRANSFER（下送芯体），共同约束是 **空隙体积（void volume）**（源：[[ADL（导流层）]]）。组织口径上，吸收芯体技术（SAM / Fluff / Adhesives / CIRRUS）被摄入系统战略章程列为 **Out of Scope**，但章程同时要求「consider how these impact the intake systems strategy」——即**分域但必须做影响传递**（源：[[Intake System（摄入系统）]]）。
- **Fibre phase 纤维相.** REL 超薄芯体的纤维相为绒毛浆 + ES/PE-PP 双组分热粘合纤维；SAF（超吸水纤维）是吸收芯体领域真实存在的技术路线，但腈纶（PAN）与卫材芯体无关（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch15 Acrylic Fibres]]）。
- **Compliance umbrella 合规总纲.** 复合吸收芯体（Composite Absorbent Core）是 GB 15979-2024 的下位/被引对象之一（源：[[GB 15979-2024（一次性使用卫生用品卫生要求）]]）。

## Open Questions 待补

- 库内**未给出「吸收芯体」的通用（非复合）定义**——现有定义仅限于 QB/T 5650-2021 的复合吸收芯体口径，需人工补充通用定义。
