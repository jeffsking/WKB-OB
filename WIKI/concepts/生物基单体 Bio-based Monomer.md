# 生物基单体 Bio-based Monomer
> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 2 citing sources (+ adjacent vault pages). All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义
A **bio-based monomer** is a polymerizable monomer derived from renewable feedstock — the library names **starch, cellulose, proteins and bio-based monomers** as the substitutes for, or partial replacements of, the **petroleum-based acrylic-acid route** in superabsorbent polymers.（生物基单体指来自可再生原料（淀粉、纤维素、蛋白质、生物基单体）的可聚合单体，用以替代或部分替代石油基丙烯酸路线。）
**Critical distinction（关键澄清）: 生物基 ≠ 可降解** — a bio-based acrylic SAP matches conventional SAP in performance and lifetime and does **not** degrade; the two concepts are orthogonal and are the report's most-needed clarification.（源：[[生物基SAP-SAF技术研究报告（2026-04 · 生物基 SAP 路线·产业化与成本结构）]]）
The industrial path is stated as **生物基单体 → 生物基聚合物 → 可降解 SAP**.（源：[[生物基SAP-SAF技术研究报告（2026-04 · 生物基 SAP 路线·产业化与成本结构）]]）

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理
- **Monomer structure selects the polymerisation route（单体结构决定聚合路线）**: monomer electronic effects dictate pathway — electron-releasing monomers favour cationic, electron-withdrawing favour anionic; chain-reaction polymerisation follows the common **initiation–propagation–termination** skeleton.（源：[[Seymour Polymer Chemistry Ch07 Ionic and Coordinative Chain Polymerization]]）
- **Green synthesis uses bio-based monomers（绿色合成采用生物基单体）**: cited bio-based monomer sources include **chitosan / cellulose / starch derivatives**, alongside microwave, photo-initiated and radiation-crosslinking routes.（源：[[Acrylic-Based Superabsorbent Polymers]]）
- **Industrial bio-based routes（工业化生物基路线）**: PLA via ring-opening polymerisation of lactide; PPC via CO₂ + propylene oxide alternating copolymerisation. PPC's feedstock cost is dominated by **PO, not CO₂**, so its price tracks propylene-oxide markets.（源：[[01_PLA_PPC_可降解材料研究报告.md]]）
- **Monomer-level chemistry of adjacent polymers（同源单体化学）**: nylon 6 is built from **caprolactam (CL)** (AB-type monomer, ring-opening); nylon 66 from **hexamethylene diamine + adipic acid** via the AH salt.（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch13 Nylon 6 and Nylon 66 Fibres]]）

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联
- **SAP cost is single-dominated by acrylic acid（SAP 成本由丙烯酸单一主导 >50%）**, so any cost-reduction / sustainability path must either change the **monomer** (bio-based) or change the **dosage** (core design).（源：[[生物基SAP-SAF技术研究报告（2026-04 · 生物基 SAP 路线·产业化与成本结构）]]）
- **Bio-based polymer targets for hygiene**: bio-based SAP, bio-based (>70%) spandex, and degradable/bio-based/natural diaper materials are the consumer-facing "green consumption" trend in the China diaper market.（源：[[China Baby Diaper Product & Tech Panorama Report 2026（China_Diaper_Tech_Report_2026）]]）；（源：[[August R&D Meeting 2026（华峰研发会议）]]）
- **Cellulose-derived route（纤维素衍生路线）**: a fully bio-based cellulose aerogel (CMC-Na + citric-acid crosslinker + CNF scaffold) reaches **153 g/g** absorption — a bio-based, microplastic-free alternative to petro-acrylic SAP.（源：[[CMC-Na CA CNF SAP original article.pdf]]）
