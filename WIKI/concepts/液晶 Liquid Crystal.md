# 液晶 Liquid Crystal

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 8 citing sources in the vault. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced in the vault is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

**Liquid crystals are an intermediate state between isotropic liquids and 3D solids — partial mobility plus short-range order**（源：[[General Introduction and Objectives]]）。The mesophases named in the vault are **nematic/cholesteric, smectic and columnar**; the constituent molecules are **calamitic (rod-like)** or **discotic**, and a polymer may place its mesogen in the **main chain (MC)** or the **side chain (SC)**（源：[[Liquid Crystalline Polymers Smart Applications]]）。A liquid crystalline polymer (LCP 液晶聚合物) is treated as a *programmable* smart material because **mesophase order couples an external stimulus to macroscopic function**（源：[[Liquid Crystalline Polymers Smart Applications]]）。

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

**Design lever = mesogen placement and shape.** Both the position of the mesogenic group in the polymer architecture (MC-LCP vs. SC-LCP) and its shape (calamitic vs. discotic) determine the liquid crystalline properties and therefore which application is reachable（源：[[Liquid Crystalline Polymers Smart Applications]]）。This classification governs the smart application pursued: **photoresponsive microcapsules use calamitic main-chain LCP; proton membranes use discotic side-chain LCP**（源：[[General Introduction and Objectives]]）。

**Two thesis application threads.** Part 1 uses **calamitic** (rod-like) LCPs for light-triggered release; Part 2 uses **discotic/columnar** LCPs mimicking biological ion channels for proton transport（源：[[Liquid Crystalline Polymers Smart Applications]]）。Three nematic LCPs with α-methylstilbene in the main chain and aliphatic or aromatic spacers were synthesised; two synthetic routes were compared for yield and molecular weight（源：[[Liquid Crystalline Polymers Smart Applications]]）。

**Bio-inspired columnar proton channels.** Bio-inspired discotic/tapered-dendron liquid-crystalline polymers self-assemble into **homeotropically oriented columnar proton channels** with water-independent conductivity and size-selective ion transport（源：[[Applying Nature's Genius to Discotic Liquid-crystal Polymers for Proton Conducting Membrane]]）。Dendronized polyethers with tapered (discotic) side groups form columnar liquid-crystalline phases over a **wide temperature range**; hydrophobic mesogenic side chains + hydrophilic ether linkages constitute the ion pathway（源：[[Applying Nature's Genius to Discotic Liquid-crystal Polymers for Proton Conducting Membrane]]）。

**Phase-transition identification loop.** The standard discipline is: **synthesise a structural series → locate the transition temperatures by DSC → confirm each phase by polarising-microscope texture → iterate until the transition sits inside the operating window**（源：[[Introduction to Liquid Crystals]]）。**The method is the transferable asset, not the molecule**（源：[[Introduction to Liquid Crystals]]）。

**Mechanochromic route.** A nematic liquid crystal (**5CB**) dispersed in a PDMS matrix (PDLC) is the material basis of a mechanochromic elastomer（源：[[Mechanochromic Elastomer from PDLC 5CB PDMS]]）。

**Thermal-transition bridge.** Polymer thermal behaviour is bridged by **Tg** and **Tm**: a semicrystalline polymer between Tg and Tm is a composite of rigid crystals + a rubbery amorphous phase（源：[[Ebewole Polymer Science Ch04 Thermal Transitions in Polymers]]）。

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

**Chromic-ink tuning (the main read-across).** Exactly the "synthesise → DSC → polarising microscope → iterate" loop is what the thermochromic/chromic-ink programme needs when tuning a colour-change onset to a specific skin- or urine-temperature（源：[[Introduction to Liquid Crystals]]）。The disposable-implementation counterpart is [[US20070270773A1 Thermochromic Wetness Indicator]]（源：[[Introduction to Liquid Crystals]]）。

**Ion transport.** The bio-inspired discotic LCP proton channels (water-independent, Nafion-comparable, size-selective) are the materials foundation of the ion-transport thread — connected at concept level to the electrolyte/ion behaviour of wet SAP networks, though the vault states no direct SAP measurement `[待补]`（源：[[Applying Nature's Genius to Discotic Liquid-crystal Polymers for Proton Conducting Membrane]]）。

**Adjacent chromic/optical material families** that share the LC chromic chemistry主线: [[Chromic Phenomena Solar Energy and Liquid Crystals]] is described as the base page for the library's LC / chromic-chemistry main line, and [[Mechanochromic Elastomer from PDLC 5CB PDMS]] is the pressure-responsive branch（源：[[Introduction to Liquid Crystals]]）。Note the antiferroelectric work is aimed at a **room-temperature** device — i.e. pushing the $S_{Calt}$ phase window down into ambient conditions — which is the same "move the onset into the operating window" problem hygiene chromics face（源：[[Introduction to Liquid Crystals]]）。
