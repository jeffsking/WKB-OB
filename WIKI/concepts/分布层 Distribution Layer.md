## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理
- The distribution layer sits between the acquisition and retention layers in the vault's three-layer functional model（源：[[Products and Technology Perspective]]）.
- Near/mid-term mechanism work is framed as **gradient design**: a density gradient plus a capillary-pressure gradient across the three layers, together with a designed hydrophilic/hydrophobic layer sequence（源：[[Products and Technology Perspective]]）.
- Quantifying the effect of [[Gel Blocking（凝胶阻塞）]] on the acquisition rate is listed as part of the same near/mid-term agenda（源：[[Products and Technology Perspective]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联
- Applies to the REL ultra-thin core as one element of the acquisition–distribution–retention architecture（源：[[Products and Technology Perspective]]）.
- Mid-term commercial read-out: the **baby-diaper** line is cautious about distribution-layer incremental investment, while the **feminine-care** line may introduce micro-groove fibres subject to an incremental-cost evaluation（源：[[Products and Technology Perspective]]）.
