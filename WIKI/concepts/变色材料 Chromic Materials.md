# 变色材料 Chromic Materials
> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 8 citing sources in the vault. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

The vault gives no single chemistry-based definition; chromic materials are instead framed as a **classification by trigger quantity**（按触发量分类）rather than by chemistry family — **the same molecule can fall into two or more classes**（e.g. spiropyran is photo-, thermo- *and* mechano-chromic depending on which physical quantity actually reaches the material）（源：[[Chromism Taxonomy（变色现象分类）]]）。

**Three classification axes（三条分类轴）**（源：[[Chromism Taxonomy（变色现象分类）]]）：
1. **Trigger quantity 触发量** — classify by *what physical quantity arrives at the material*, not by chemistry.
2. **Reversibility 可逆性** — reversible vs irreversible / one-shot；continuous monitoring needs a reversible route, a single alarm may use a semi-reversible or irreversible one. 「**This is the axis that eliminates designs, not the chemistry family.**」
3. **Threshold 阈值** — industrial metrology materials are engineered for structural-failure detection above 40,000 psi, whereas absorbent-hygiene products live in a low-tension window（源：[[Chromism Taxonomy（变色现象分类）]]）。

**Hygiene-usable classes（卫品可用类别）**：温致 thermochromic（温度）、湿致 hydrochromic（水/湿度）、卤致 halochromic（pH/质子活度）、力致 mechanochromic、光致 photochromic、电致 electrochromic、化学致 chemochromic（源：[[Chromism Taxonomy（变色现象分类）]]）。

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

- **Thermochromic leuco-dye microcapsule（温致隐色染料微胶囊）**: colouring matter（leuco dye，供电子体，本身无色）+ colour-developing matter（受电子体：phenol compound or acid clay）+ desensitizer（溶剂）；discolouring temperature corresponds to the melting of the… 〔原文截断 [原文残缺]〕（源：[[热致变色 Thermochromic]]）。
- **Thermal-mediation chain（热媒介机理链）**: urine / body waste contacts the article → local temperature rises → the thermochromic compound crosses its transition → visual colour change（源：[[US20070270773A1 Thermochromic Wetness Indicator]]）。
- **Microencapsulation（微胶囊化）**: in-situ polymerisation with **urea-formalin resin** as wall material; aqueous TC slurry ≈ 45 wt% capsule particles, formulated with a water-based binder into a water-based thermochromic ink（源：[[US5197958 Wetness Indicating Diaper]]）。
- **Cholesteric / temperature-sensitive liquid crystal（胆甾相/温敏液晶）**: clearing-point type「常温有色、升温变透明」；温敏型颜色序列 无色→红→橙→黄→绿→蓝→紫→无色（源：[[热致变色 Thermochromic]]）。
- **Mechanochromic branches（力致变色分支）**: mechanophore mechanochromism、structural-colour mechanochromism、geometric optical mask（源：[[Chromism Taxonomy（变色现象分类）]]）。

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **Wetness indication is already in mass production** — the thermochromic（温致）route for urine-wetness indication；the review verdict is that the **'wet' route is mature while the 'thermal' route is the frontier**, and **microencapsulation is the differentiator, not the colour**（源：[[Thermochromic & Hydrochromic Materials for Diapers (Feasibility Review v3)]]）。
- **Three implementation routes**, all enabled by microencapsulation: **R1 thermochromic ink/pigment**（printed stripe or laminated label patch；reversible for comfort cues）；**R2 hot-melt wetness glue**（backsheet inner face；urine triggers irreversible yellow/orange→blue；84% of market；lowest risk, fits existing converting lines）；**R3 hydrochromic ink**（源：[[Thermochromic & Hydrochromic Materials for Diapers (Feasibility Review v3)]]）。
- **Placement & safety gates**: the indicator MUST sit on the non-skin-contact face（outer cover / backsheet window）；safety gates named — ISO 10993（cytotoxicity / skin irritation / sensitisation）、EU REACH（SVHC + full substance declaration for the chromic layer）、FDA guidance、China GB/T 35613-2017 & GB/T 36420-2018；**Matsui Shikiso（松井）caution** — some legacy products contain trace formaldehyde and official guidance excludes baby direct contact（源：[[Thermochromic & Hydrochromic Materials for Diapers (Feasibility Review v3)]]）。
- Sits in the smart-textile neighbourhood（源：[[智能织物 Smart Textile]]）；sibling concepts 光致变色 [[光致变色 Photochromic]]、[[机械变色 Mechanochromic]]、[[电致变色 Electrochromic]]、[[离子变色 Ionochromic]]。
