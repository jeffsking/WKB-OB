# 每克厚度 T／W

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 2 citing sources. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

**T／W (thickness per gram) is the bulk/thickness-per-unit-mass parameter of an absorbent web.** In the vault's reading of *Absorbent Technology* Ch.3, **Capacity is predictable from thickness-per-gram while Rate is not** — that asymmetry is why the structural constant K must be introduced for rate（源：[[Fluid Absorption in High Bulk Nonwovens]]）. A precise formula-level definition of T/W is not given verbatim in the sources read `[待补]`.

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

- Ch.3 of *Absorbent Technology* converts "fast and full core"（吸得快、吸得多）into two measurable numbers (C, Q); the per-mass thickness view underpins the capacity prediction, while rate needs the [[结构性常数 K]]（源：[[Fluid Absorption in High Bulk Nonwovens]]）.
- T/W sits on the same per-mass void basis as [[Specific Air Volume]]（V_s，比空气体积）；the two pages cross-reference each other as capacity-predictors（源：[[Specific Air Volume]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- For REL absorbent-core design, T/W is the early-stage capacity lever: bulk (areal density / thickness per gram) is the design variable to lock in early, since thinning the core costs ~34% capacity and ~54% rate（源：[[Fluid Absorption in High Bulk Nonwovens]]、[[Specific Air Volume]]）.
