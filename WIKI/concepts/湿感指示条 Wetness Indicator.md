# 湿感指示条 Wetness Indicator
> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 2 citing sources. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义
A wetness indicator (WI) is a **consumer-visible visual signal on an absorbent article** that tells the caregiver that wetness/urination has occurred — a "reason to believe" (RTB) for wetness beyond basic absorbency.（湿感指示条是吸收制品上**消费者可见的尿湿视觉信号**，告知照护者已发生排尿，是超越基础吸收力的差异化卖点。）
The library's mainstream commercial format is the hot-melt **wetness-indicator glue 尿显胶**: a glue line printed on the backsheet inner face whose colour turns on urine contact.（本库记载的主流商用形态是热熔**尿显胶**：印于底膜内侧的胶线，遇尿变色。）

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理
- **Physical trigger is liquid contact, not a humidity sensor（触发为液体接触，非湿度传感器）.** The review states liquid-contact triggering is "far more realistic than a true RH sensor".（源：[[Wetness Indicator Glue]]）
- **Lowest pilot risk of the three routes（三路线中中试风险最低）**: the hot-melt glue runs on existing converting lines — no new printing step, no new substrate.（源：[[Wetness Indicator Glue]]）
- **Thermal-mediation mechanism（以温度为媒介）**: a thermochromic ink sits on the outer surface; the heat of the excreted liquid conducts through a layer whose thermal conductivity changes on wetting — a dry/wet thermal-conductivity switch plus a thermal-threshold indicator.（源：[[US5197958 Wetness Indicating Diaper]]）
- **Thermochromic chain（热致变色机理链）**: urine/bodily waste contacts the article → local temperature rises → the thermochromic compound crosses its transition → visual colour change.（源：[[US20070270773A1 Thermochromic Wetness Indicator]]）
- **Thermochromic temperature-indicator operating window（温度指示工作窗）**: activation window 30–37 °C with design targets 32/34/36 °C, response time ≤120 s, readable after ≥10 thermal cycles.（源：[[Diaper Microclimate Visualization Feasibility (Dual-MVP)]]）

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联
- **Incumbent baseline, not to be replaced（既有基线，不作为替代目标）**: the programme's rule is **do not replace the existing wetness indicator (WI)** — new indicators are additive differentiation only.（源：[[Diaper Microclimate Visualization Feasibility (Dual-MVP)]]）；（源：[[智能尿裤功能层]]）
- **Skin-contact hard gate（皮肤接触硬门）**: the indicator MUST sit on the non-skin-contact face (outer cover / backsheet window); zero back-transfer, visible colour bleeding or direct skin contact.（源：[[Thermochromic & Hydrochromic Materials for Diapers (Feasibility Review v3)]]）
- **Route taxonomy（路线分类）**: R1 thermochromic ink/pigment · R2 hot-melt wetness glue · R3 water/solvent hydrochromic ink; microencapsulation is the shared enabler.（源：[[智能尿裤功能层]]）
- **Safety framework（安全框架）**: ISO 10993 (cytotoxicity / skin irritation / sensitisation), EU REACH (SVHC), FDA, China GB/T 35613-2017 & GB/T 36420-2018.（源：[[Thermochromic & Hydrochromic Materials for Diapers (Feasibility Review v3)]]）
- **Caution — legacy formaldehyde**: some legacy chromic products contain trace formaldehyde and official guidance excludes baby direct contact; resolve baby-safety before any skin-adjacent use.（源：[[Thermochromic & Hydrochromic Materials for Diapers (Feasibility Review v3)]]）
