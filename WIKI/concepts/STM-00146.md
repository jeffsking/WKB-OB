# STM-00146

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 3 citing sources. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

**STM-00146 is the KC internal Standard Test Method for nonwoven tensile testing**（KC 内控 STM 之一，用于无纺布拉伸）（源：[[Raw Material STM - Nanjing Plant（原材料STM-NJ）]]）. At core level it is cited as the **湿态 MD 抗张（wet MD tensile）** method（源：[[芯体测试方法与验收基准（STM）]]）. The distinction in scope between the two citations is noted but not reconciled by any source read here `[待补]`.

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

- In the plant STM families, STM-00146 is filed under **KC 内部 STM**: Nanjing lists it as 「STM-00146 无纺布拉伸」；Tianjin lists it as 「STM-00146 热风无纺布拉伸」（源：[[Raw Material STM - Nanjing Plant（原材料STM-NJ）]]、[[Raw Material STM - Tianjin Mill WI-60632（原材料标准测试方法-TJ）]]）.
- Per the STM principle page, an STM number locks "how to test"（设备、载荷、液量、时序）; the acceptance basis is a separate paired item — numbers are only comparable when both are cited（源：[[芯体测试方法与验收基准（STM）]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- Nonwoven tensile strength (wet, MD) is an incoming-material QC gate at both Nanjing and Tianjin plants; the 75 N/m threshold anchors liner/nonwoven mechanical acceptance（源：[[Raw Material STM - Nanjing Plant（原材料STM-NJ）]]、[[芯体测试方法与验收基准（STM）]]）.
