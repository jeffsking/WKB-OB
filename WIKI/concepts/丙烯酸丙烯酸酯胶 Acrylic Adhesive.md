## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理
- Tack in acrylic PSA systems is attributed to **viscoelasticity** — the polymer conforms to and "wets" the substrate even in the dry state（源：[[Adhesive Handbook Part 3 Adhesive Classes]]）.
- Peel strength is governed by viscoelastic dissipation **G = G₀·ψ(v,T)**; temperature and peel rate therefore set performance（源：[[Handbook of Adhesive Technology Full Report]]）.
- In fibre/非织造 substrates the dominant mechanism is **mechanical interlocking** — penetration of protruding fibre ends（源：[[Handbook of Adhesive Technology Full Report]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联
- Primary role: the skin-contact fastening adhesive (waist/side/top tape) of diapers, where "skin non-irritation" is the first-order constraint（源：[[Adhesive Handbook Part 3 Adhesive Classes]]）.
- Water-based acrylic point-adhesive is listed among the standard hygiene adhesives alongside EVA/PO hot melts and starch-modified types（源：[[Handbook of Adhesive Technology Full Report]]）.
- Sits inside the broader [[卫材胶黏系统 Hygiene Adhesive Systems]] together with [[压敏胶 PSA]] and [[热熔胶 Hot Melt Adhesive]]（源：[[卫材胶黏系统 Hygiene Adhesive Systems]]）.
