# 协同吸附 Cooperative Adsorption

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 2 citing sources. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

Cooperative adsorption is the **cooperative binding of surfactant molecules to polymer chains** — the source states that surfactants "bind to polymer chains cooperatively, i.e., in the form of micellelike nanoaggregates," beginning above a threshold concentration（源：[[Smart Polymers Ch02 Responsive Polymer Brush Theory]]）.

## Key Parameters and Data 关键参数与数据

| Parameter 参数 | Value 数值 | Source 来源 |
|---|---|---|
| Binding mode 结合方式 | Cooperative; surfactant binds as **micellelike nanoaggregates** on the polymer chain | [[Smart Polymers Ch02 Responsive Polymer Brush Theory]] |
| Onset threshold 起始阈值 | Above the **critical association concentration (CAC)** | [[Smart Polymers Ch02 Responsive Polymer Brush Theory]] |
| Threshold ordering 阈值关系 | **CAC < CMC** | [[Smart Polymers Ch02 Responsive Polymer Brush Theory]] |
| Bulk-phase reference 体相参照 | Below CMC: linear monomer activity; near CMC: saturated interfacial adsorption (best free-energy reduction) | [[The Role of Surfactants]] |

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

- **Cooperative (aggregate) binding.** Rather than binding as isolated monomers, surfactant molecules associate onto the polymer chain in micelle-like nanoaggregates once the CAC is exceeded — a threshold distinct from, and lower than, the CMC（源：[[Smart Polymers Ch02 Responsive Polymer Brush Theory]]）.
- The companion surfactant chapter frames the wider causal chain — surfactant → interfacial adsorption → free-energy change → wetting/penetration kinetics → macroscopic absorbency — within which cooperative/polymer binding sits alongside monomeric interfacial adsorption（源：[[The Role of Surfactants]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- **Fluff-pulp and core-layer chemistry.** Surfactant–polymer interaction is directly relevant to fluff-pulp treatment and core-layer chemistry, where surfactant dose near (never above) CMC controls the operating cost of the finish（源：[[The Role of Surfactants]]）.
- Cooperative binding is the mechanism behind surfactant–polymer complexation behaviour that can affect finish retention on fibres（源：[[Smart Polymers Ch02 Responsive Polymer Brush Theory]]）.
