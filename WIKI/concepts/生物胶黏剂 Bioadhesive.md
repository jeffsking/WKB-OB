## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理
- **Mesh-size concept（网孔尺寸概念）**: adhesion strength is directly proportional to mesh size — the source notes this corresponds conceptually to SAP gel-network dynamics (**crosslink density → mesh size → absorption-speed / capacity trade-off**).（源：[[Adhesive Handbook Part 4 Applications]]）
- **Five bonding mechanisms（五类粘接机理）**: WLB wetting, surface energy, penetration, anchoring, electrostatic — applicable to all bonding scenarios.（源：[[Adhesive Handbook]]）
- **Process matters as much as chemistry（工艺与胶料同等重要）**: "the application parameters other than the characteristics of the adhesive itself account for around 50% of performance" (Ch47).（源：[[Adhesive Handbook Part 4 Applications]]）

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联
- **Skin-contact-adhesive commonality（皮肤接触胶的共通性）**: Ch48 bioadhesives share technical commonality with skin-contact adhesives used on absorbent articles.（源：[[Adhesive Handbook Part 4 Applications]]）
- **Natural-based → adhesive-free / bio-based hot melt（天然基 → 无胶复合 / 生物基热熔胶）**: tannin / lignin / laccase routes are flagged as the analogue for the library's "无胶复合" (adhesive-free lamination) and bio-based hot-melt directions.（源：[[Adhesive Handbook Part 4 Applications]]）
- **Hot-melt adjacency（热熔胶邻接）**: the same part identifies EVA / APAO / polyamide / TPU / curing hot melt as the most-used adhesives for core lamination and wipe sealing.（源：[[Adhesive Handbook Part 4 Applications]]）
