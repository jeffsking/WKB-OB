# 胶黏剂 Adhesive
> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 4 citing sources. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义
`定义待补（引用来源未给出明确定义）` — the vault does not give a dictionary-style definition. It defines the adhesive **by its joint**, not by the material: "An adhesive joint is not a 'thin line' — it is a complex three-dimensional 'interphase'"（接头不是一条线，而是三维过渡层）（源：[[Handbook of Adhesive Technology Full Report]]）.

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理
- **Strength is governed by viscoelastic dissipation, not interfacial thermodynamics alone**; temperature and peel rate directly determine core retention（源：[[Handbook of Adhesive Technology Full Report]]）.
- The joint is a **three-dimensional interphase** — the SAP particles (50–400 μm sodium polyacrylate) and the adjacent adhesive layer form a chemical–physical composite interphase（源：[[Handbook of Adhesive Technology Full Report]]）.
- On nonwoven/fluff substrates, **mechanical interlocking** (fibre-end penetration) dominates over pure wetting（源：[[Handbook of Adhesive Technology Full Report]]）.
- **Process parameters matter as much as the adhesive**: adhesive weight, application method and open time each contribute, so R&D and procurement must invest in both（源：[[Adhesive Handbook Part 4 Applications]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联
- Adhesives carry the full [[卫材胶黏系统 Hygiene Adhesive Systems]] load: core bonding, top-sheet / ADL / back-sheet lamination, SAP wrapping, and skin-contact self-adhesion ([[压敏胶 PSA]])（源：[[卫材胶黏系统 Hygiene Adhesive Systems]]）.
- Hot-melt adhesive is the most-used family for core lamination, SMS lamination and wipe sealing; common classes are EVA / APAO / polyamide / TPU / curing hot melt（源：[[热熔胶 Hot Melt Adhesive]]）.
- [[聚氨酯 Polyurethane]] appears in hygiene as an elastic fibre/film, a hot-melt/composite adhesive, and a functional coating/membrane; waterborne PU emits no VOC (nonflammable)（源：[[Adhesive Handbook Part 3 Adhesive Classes]]）.
- Where application parameters are ~50 % of performance, "changing the adhesive without changing the process" is not a valid optimisation（源：[[Adhesive Handbook Part 4 Applications]]）.

## Open Questions 待补
- Class boundaries between structural hot-melt adhesive and PSA are blurred in hygiene leak-guard/waist applications (SBS/SIS hot melts serve both roles)（源：[[卫材胶黏系统 Hygiene Adhesive Systems]]）.
- **Long-term strength decay in wet/urine environments**: only an analogy (85 % loss, an organic-coating figure) exists — no hygiene-specific decay curve（源：[[卫材胶黏系统 Hygiene Adhesive Systems]]）.
- **No standard test method for SAP–adhesive interfacial compatibility / anti-mould** is recorded in the vault（源：[[卫材胶黏系统 Hygiene Adhesive Systems]]）.
