## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

- **Wet-strength inversion.** Cotton *stiffens* when wet, while viscose *collapses* (low wet modulus); the two behaviours are presented as direct inverses（源：[[Fibers and Fibrous Materials]]、[[粘胶纤维 Viscose Fiber]]）.
- Wet strength — not dry strength — is the property flagged as relevant for wet-wipes and core layers（源：[[Fibers and Fibrous Materials]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- Cotton appears as a natural-fibre option in the topsheet material taxonomy for disposable hygiene products（源：[[Topsheet（面层）]]）.
- Its wet stiffening is the design-relevant property wherever wet-state softness matters — the opposite design problem to viscose（源：[[Fibers and Fibrous Materials]]）.
