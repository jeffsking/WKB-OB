# 双组分纤维 Bicomponent

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 9 citing sources in the vault. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced in the vault is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

A **bicomponent（双组分/复合）fibre** is a fibre formed by **conjugate spinning** of two polymers into one filament, classified by cross-section into **side-by-side (S/S, 并列)**, **sheath-core (S/C, 皮芯)** and **matrix-fibril (M/F, 基质-原纤)** architectures（源：[[Bicomponent Fibre Technology Development Trends]]）. Sheath-core variants are **concentric 同心型**（core centred）and **eccentric 偏心型**（core off-centre, enabling self-crimping）. The hygiene workhorse is the **low-melting bonding fibre**: the source states verbatim "皮芯型复合纤维可制得具有粘合功能的低熔点纤维，已广泛用于梳理型非织造布生产。"（sheath-core composite fibre yields a low-melting bonding fibre, already widely used in carded nonwoven production）（源：[[Bicomponent Fibre Technology Development Trends]]）.

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

**Thermal-bonding mechanism（热粘合机理）：** during **air-through or calender bonding** the low-melting **sheath melts to form bond points** while the high-melting **core retains fibre integrity** — the structural basis of through-air bonding for sheath-core bicomponents（源：[[Bicomponent Fibre Technology Development Trends]]）. The bond is **intrinsic to the fibre polymer, not an added adhesive**; the target is **surface sintering**, and **over-bonding embrittles the web and causes point-bond "popping"**（源：[[Thermal bonding]]）. **Sheath-core PP/PE and CoPET/PET bicomponents dominate hygiene webs**（源：[[Thermal bonding]]）.

**Conjugated variants:** **island-in-sea（海岛型）** and **radial/split-pie（径向瓣/桔瓣型）** configurations are made spinnable and separable by bicomponent design, and are the direct ancestors of **splittable nonwovens** and high-coverage coverstock/wipe webs（源：[[Manufactured Fibre Technology Ch14 Speciality Polyamide and Polyester Yarns]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

Bicomponent fibres are the bonding and functional backbone of the hygiene stack: **carded / air-through webs**（[[热风粘合无纺布]]）, **topsheet**（[[Topsheet（面层）]]）, **ADL**（[[ADL（导流层）]]）and spunbond/meltblown layers（[[纺粘 Spunbond]]、[[Meltblown Nonwoven（熔喷非织造布）]]）all depend on low-melting sheath fibres — the industrial form of which is **ES fibre（PE/PP 皮芯）**（[[ES Fibre Hydrophilic Function and Applications]]、[[皮芯复合纤维]]）. In the drylaid branch, bicomponent blends are named alongside fluff pulp and SAP as the raw-material science that defines modern hygiene cores and ADLs（源：[[Dry-laid web formation]]）. Sheath and core can each carry **different functional additives/masterbatches** (hydrophilic, antimicrobial, colour)（源：[[Ebewole Polymer Science Ch09 Polymer Additives and Reinforcements]]）, and non-circular/shaped cross-sections tune lustre, coverage and handle for topsheet fibre selection（[[三叶形截面纤维（Trilobal Fibre）]]）.
