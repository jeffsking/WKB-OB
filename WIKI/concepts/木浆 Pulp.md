# 木浆 Pulp

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 6 citing sources. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义
**Wood pulp = the cellulose fibre material obtained from wood by pulping.** The vault gives this through composition rather than a quoted dictionary entry: wood is **cellulose 40–55% + lignin 15–35% + hemicellulose 25–40%**, and cellulose itself is a polydisperse polymer of **DP 3,500–36,000**（源：[[Seymour Polymer Chemistry Ch10 Naturally Occurring Polymers]]）. Pulping is by the **sulfite** or **kraft** process (kraft stated as the more common and more eco-friendly route)（源：同上）. A clean standalone definition is not given → 定义待补（引用来源未给出明确定义的词条）.

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理
- **Pulping 制浆**：sulfite vs kraft; kraft is the more common and more eco-friendly route（源：[[Seymour Polymer Chemistry Ch10 Naturally Occurring Polymers]]）.
- **Insoluble but hydrophilic.** Cellulose is insoluble in water "because of the presence of strong intermolecular hydrogen bonds"; the β-1,4 rigid linear chains pack into crystalline regions — so pulp absorbs in inter-fibre capillaries and amorphous-region hydroxyls, **not by dissolution**（源：同上）.
- **α/β/γ classification.** α-cellulose (insoluble in 17.5% NaOH) is the high-crystallinity "true cellulose" of pulp（源：同上）.
- **Fibre-as-composite.** Wood pulp fibre is itself a composite — "the cellulosic fibrils are effectively dispersed in a lignin matrix"（源：[[Fibers and Fibrous Materials]]）.
- **Wood pulp as airlaying feedstock.** Aerodynamic web formation's raw-material science is wood pulp + man-made fibres + SAP + bicomponent blends（源：[[Dry-laid web formation]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联
- **Fluff pulp IS cellulose**: its DP, α-cellulose content and crystallinity directly set fibre **stiffness, web-forming behaviour and capillary wicking rate**（源：[[Seymour Polymer Chemistry Ch10 Naturally Occurring Polymers]]）.
- **Fluff-pulp acceptance** (GB/T 21331-2021) requires the chemical-safety four-piece — migratable fluorescent substances / AOX / heavy metals Pb·As·Cd·Hg / acetone extractables — plus dry bulk, absorption time and capacity（源：[[GB-T 21331-2021 Fluff Pulp（绒毛浆）]]）.
- **Treated vs untreated** is essentially a **bulk/absorption-speed vs strength** trade-off: treated pulp has the higher dry-bulk floor (17.0 vs 15.0 cm³/g) but the lower burst resistance（源：同上）.
- Plain pulp's 1–2 g/g absorbency is why commercial cores are pulp + SAP composites, typically 80/20 to 90/10（源：[[Cross-linked Cellulose and Its Derivatives]]）.
- Cellulose is a **non-customer for wetting agents** — the advancing contact angle of water on pure cellulose fibre is 0°; rewetting agents work only against residual hydrophobic impurities（源：[[The Role of Surfactants]]）.
- Wet-strength resin (UF) applied to paper/pulp gives **2–5×** wet strength — the wet-wipe application（源：[[湿强剂 Wet-Strength Resin]]）.
