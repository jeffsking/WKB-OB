# 离子对吸附 Ion Pair Adsorption

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 1 citing source. All figures traced to their source page. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

**离子对吸附（ion-pair adsorption）** is one of the **four surfactant adsorption mechanisms** at the solid–liquid interface in fibrous / absorbent systems. Per the cited-by source, it **"occurs only at very low bulk surfactant concentrations and in the absence of additional electrolyte"**（源：[[The Role of Surfactants]]）.

The four mechanisms the source lists are, in order: **amphipathic adsorption**（疏水基锚定、亲水基朝外，主力机制）→ **ion-pair adsorption** → **ion exchange**（表面活性剂离子置换 Stern 层反离子，"by itself, does not effect a change in surface charge at the Stern plane"）→ **cooperative adsorption**（形成第二层半胶束，"the surface charge is completely neutralized and then reversed in sign during this step"）（源：[[The Role of Surfactants]]）.

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

- **Sequence matters**: the debonding / rewetting formulation conflict is **resolved only if the ion-pair step precedes the hydrophobic one** — i.e. ion-pair adsorption is a prerequisite step, not an alternative to amphipathic adsorption（源：[[The Role of Surfactants]]）.
- **No numeric parameter** (rate constant, surface excess, CMC-linked threshold) for the ion-pair step is recorded in the vault `[待补]`.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- The surfactant adsorption chain（surfactant → interfacial adsorption → free-energy change → wetting/penetration kinetics → macroscopic absorbency）is **directly actionable for fluff-pulp treatment and core-layer chemistry**（源：[[The Role of Surfactants]]）.
- **Compatibility red line**: mixing anionic and cationic surfactants produces a hydrophobic outer double layer and **destroys rewetting** — a hard constraint on core / ADL / SAP layer chemistry, where ion-pair interactions are involved（源：[[The Role of Surfactants]]）.
