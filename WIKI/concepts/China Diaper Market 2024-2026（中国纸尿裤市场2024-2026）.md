# China Diaper Market 2024-2026（中国纸尿裤市场2024-2026）

> 一句话定义（Definition）：China's baby-diaper market is the world's largest by volume and is rapidly shifting from "basic performance" to "experience & value" — characterized by **domestic-brand rise, dual-track tech war (price + function), ODM-led agile innovation, and heavy raw-material cost exposure**（全球最大体量，正从基础性能转向体验与价值，特征是国产品牌崛起、价格+技术双轨、ODM 主导的敏捷创新、原材料成本敞口大）.

## 规模与增速 Market Scale

China's baby-diaper market is estimated at **~RMB 70B (2025E) with 8-10% CAGR**; domestic-brand share jumped from **<1% (2019) to 8-10% (2024)**. Unit price spans **RMB 1.5-4.5**, reflecting a simultaneous price war and technology war.

## 三大趋势 Three Trends

1. **消费升级 Trading-up**：from "usable" to "skincare/nourishing" — premium brands gain pricing space.
2. **绿色消费 Green consumption**：degradable, bio-based, natural materials attract young parents.
3. **国产替代 Domestic substitution**：Babycare, BeBeBus, Bibi (碧芭), Qiaoshi (雀氏) etc. significantly improved tech capability.

## 供应链变革 Supply-Chain Shift

**ODM firms (e.g. HaoYue 豪悦, Reliable 可靠) upgraded from OEM to technology partners** — dev cycle compressed to **6-12 months**, with joint R&D and patent sharing. This "scroll-style" competition accelerates the whole industry.

## 挑战与机遇 Challenges & Opportunities

- **挑战**：birth-rate decline (婴儿纸尿裤增量收窄); 国际巨头竞争 (P&G, KC, Kao); 原材料价格波动 (SAP, fluff pulp).
- **机遇**：老龄化 → 成人失禁产品增长; 消费升级溢价; 国产品牌技术反超 (无木浆芯体、可降解材料、智能结构).
