## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理
- **Format split shapes the intake architecture 形态决定摄入架构**：KC Open Diaper 架构在 **T4 及以下**用 **Surge / Liner 两件式**，**T5 及以上**升级为 **Support Layer / Topical liner（3-Piece Liner 三件式面层）**，产线含 **T5 的 Double Turn Bar（双转向杆）工位**（源：[[TJ Mill Visit Pre-Brief — GRID × Kirigami, Coform & Gasket Deep-Dive（2026-09-07 v3.1）]]）。
- **Registration is the process discipline 套准是工艺纪律**：the `Open Diaper Process Introduction--202104.pdf` figures derive from **single-layer** graphic registration and must **not** be cross-quoted against the two-layer post-changeover phase-loop numbers (15–20 片 vs 17 片 — 成因、系统、对策均不同)（源：[[85 双层pattern芯体套准工程规格]]）。
- **Drum/pitch decoupling 鼓距-节距解耦**：the OD process文件 gives the core-wrap length rule as a **special case** (n fixed, single specification) of the generalized forming-drum ups selection; the two must not be substituted for each other（源：[[81 Forming Drum Ups Selection（鼓距·端区·11 尺寸分配）]]）。

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联
- OD and Pants share one method index but not all methods: the finished-product testing summary lists method numbers in two columns (OD / Pants) so shared vs format-specific methods are visible at a glance（源：[[Finished Product Testing Method Summary（成品测试方法汇总表）]]）。
- Dimensional standards are format-specific: **STM-01078** standardises folded length/width of an **OD** product using a push board against a ruled plate, to stop folding artefacts being read as real dimensional deviation（源：[[STM-01078 OD产品折叠后长度、宽度测量方法（成品·NJ）.docx]]）.
- OD intake gains were validated commercially through the ActivTech hybrid route（源：[[ActiveTech Platform（ActiveTech 在线薄芯体平台）]]）。
