# Stimuli-Responsive Polymers

> 📌 **Page completed 2026-09-30** from 1 citing source. All figures traced to their source pages. Anything not evidenced is marked `[待补]`.

## Definition 定义

**Stimuli-Responsive Polymers** are polymers whose value lies in "signal translation": they translate chemical/physical signals into optical / electrical / thermal / mechanical signals, and vice versa — the material is simultaneously a **sensor and an actuator**（源：[[Emerging Applications of Stimuli-Responsive Polymer Materials (Nature Materials 2010)]]）。

中文：刺激响应聚合物把化学/物理信号转译为光/电/热/力信号（反之亦然）——材料本身既是传感器又是执行器。本库另见其热力学口径页：[[刺激响应聚合物 Stimuli-Responsive Polymer]]（smartness = 可逆相变 + 临界点敏感性）（源：[[Smart Polymers Ch01 Phase Transitions and Light Scattering]]）。

## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理

- **Four architectures & the response-time trade-off.** 四类架构：本体聚合物表面（分钟~数十小时）→ 聚合物刷（快速可逆开关）→ 聚合物网络薄膜（Tanaka–Fillmore t ∝ L²，<10 µm 时 ~秒级）→ 自组装多层薄膜（LbL，溶胀度高达 400%）（源：[[Emerging Applications of Stimuli-Responsive Polymer Materials (Nature Materials 2010)]]）。
- **Polymer-brush physics.** 接枝密度高 → 排除体积使链伸展 → 构象变化被放大为宏观可测的厚度/润湿/渗透变化（源：[[Emerging Applications of Stimuli-Responsive Polymer Materials (Nature Materials 2010)]]）。
- **Thin-film breakthrough.** 薄膜化是响应速度的物理突破点——本体表面重构太慢，纳米薄膜可调到秒级（源：[[Emerging Applications of Stimuli-Responsive Polymer Materials (Nature Materials 2010)]]）。
- **Theory bottleneck.** 平衡态预测良好（SCFT、粗粒化 DPD 等），但动力学预测能力不足，工业"设计→制造→验证"仍需大量试错（源：[[Emerging Applications of Stimuli-Responsive Polymer Materials (Nature Materials 2010)]]）。

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联

- LbL 薄膜溶胀度高达 400%，与 SAP 凝胶网络动力学概念相通，是刺激响应功能层的关键架构（源：[[Emerging Applications of Stimuli-Responsive Polymer Materials (Nature Materials 2010)]]）。
- PNIPAAM 刷 LCST ≈32 °C，接近体温，在温致变色/温响应材料中具独特地位——与卫生用品的温/湿指示方向同源（源：[[Emerging Applications of Stimuli-Responsive Polymer Materials (Nature Materials 2010)]]）。
- 系统本身既是传感器又是执行器，是把"功能性高分子"从结构材料拉升为"智能材料"的定位（源：[[Emerging Applications of Stimuli-Responsive Polymer Materials (Nature Materials 2010)]]）。
