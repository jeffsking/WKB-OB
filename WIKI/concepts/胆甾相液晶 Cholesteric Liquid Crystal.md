## Method / Mechanism 方法 / 机理
- Colour is set by the **pitch p and birefringence Δn**: the cholesteric helix selectively reflects wavelength λ = np, with bandwidth Δλ = p·Δn（源：[[Chromic Phenomena Solar Energy and Liquid Crystals]]）.
- "Selective reflection of light" is the interaction mode that maps cholesteric LC to **thermochromic cholesteric devices** (Table 5.1)（源：[[Chromic Phenomena Solar Energy and Liquid Crystals]]）.
- Worked example — BASF rewritable full-colour system: UV exposure shifts the reflection red → green → blue at 0 / 33 / 246 mJ/cm², and the record is erased at ~120 °C（源：[[Chromic Phenomena Solar Energy and Liquid Crystals]]）.

## Hygiene Relevance 卫材关联
- The source book states cholesteric LC is **microencapsulated for thermographic use such as "baby thermometers"** — a direct functional parallel to the temperature/wetness indicators used on hygiene products（源：[[Chromic Phenomena Solar Energy and Liquid Crystals]]）.
- It is grouped with the reversible-colour-change materials ([[热致变色 Thermochromic]], [[光致变色 Photochromic]]) that underpin passive, power-free indication（源：[[Chromic Phenomena Solar Energy and Liquid Crystals]]）.
