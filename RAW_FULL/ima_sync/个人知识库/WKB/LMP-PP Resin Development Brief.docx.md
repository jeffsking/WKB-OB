# Development Brief: Low Melting Point Polypropylene (LMP-PP) Resin

Date: 2026-09-21 Sponsor: Hygiene Materials Innovation R&D Basis: Polypropylene Fiber Grade structure–property technical data

# 1. Background and Objective

Thermal-bonding components for hygiene nonwovens (air-through / thermal calendering / spunbond) have long relied on imported low-melting-point PP or sheath-core bicomponent solutions based on low-melt PE. This project aims to develop a domestic fiber-grade low-melting-point PP resin to fill the gap in the domestic resin supply (existing domestic fiber-grade PP is all homopolymer, melting point 164–165°C).

Technical principle: comonomers (ethylene or C4–C10 α-olefins, 0.8–10 wt%) interrupt isotactic sequences and reduce lamellar crystalline perfection, lowering the melting point from 160–165°C (homopolymer) to the target range.

# 2. Three-Tier Development Plan

# Plan A: Conventional Low-Melt Grade (150–158°C) — lowest risk, for early validation

|Parameter|Target|
|---|---|
|Comonomer|Ethylene 0.8–5 wt% (random copolymer, RCP)|
|MFR|20–40 g/10min (within the spunbond range 6–150; achievable via peroxide-initiated controlled rheology from base resin)|
|Isotacticity|≥95%|
|Xylene solubles|<6 wt% (copolymer control line)|
|Applicable process|Direct switch on existing spunbond/thermal-bonding lines|

# Plan B: Intermediate-Temperature Binder Grade (135–150°C) — core development target

|Parameter|Target|
|---|---|
|Comonomer|Ethylene + butene total 5–10 wt% (butene is more efficient at lowering Tm)|
|Low-isotacticity fraction|5–30% (deliberately introduced via catalyst system; further suppresses Tm and improves low-temperature flow and fiber softness)|
|MFR|30–50 g/10min (for thermal calendering / air-through bonding)|
|PDI|2.8–4.5 (dual control: hydrogen regulation + metallocene catalysis)|
|Xylene solubles|<6 wt%|

# Plan C: Ultra-Low-Melt Experimental Grade (≤135°C) — replacement for PP/PP sheath-core sheath resin

|Parameter|Target|
|---|---|
|Comonomer|~10 wt%|
|Crystallinity|<60% (baseline 60–75%)|
|Nucleating agent|None added (opposite of heat-setting shrinkage-reduction formulations)|
|Application|Replacement for the sheath resin of PP/PP bicomponent ES fibers (EPC)|

# 3. Process and Formulation Key Points

1.Antioxidant system: 0.1–0.5 wt% antioxidant is mandatory within the 200–320°C extrusion window; the higher the comonomer content, the closer to the upper limit (0.5%).

2.Drawing / heat-setting re-tuning: For low-crystallinity materials, the lower temperature limits of the two-stage drawing (Stage 1: 110–160°C, 4×–14×; Stage 2: 80–135°C, 1.2×–3×) can be shifted downward; heat-setting temperature must be lowered in line with the melting point, otherwise the low-melt component softens during setting.

3.Solubles control: Xylene solubles rise as melting point falls and directly correlate with fiber strength. Plans B/C must hold solubles <6 wt% to keep strength above the standard-grade floor.

4.Enhanced quenching: Low-crystallinity-tendency materials crystallize more slowly; quench rates must be increased (chill roll / quench air) to prevent surface tack and roll sticking.

# 4. Acceptance Criteria

|Item|Specification|
|---|---|
|Fiber tenacity|4–6 cN/dtex (standard grade, 350–525 MPa)|
|Elongation at break|10–30%|
|Thermal shrinkage|<5% (under tensile stress at 117°C)|
|Melting point|Per target range of Plan A / B / C|

# 5. Benchmarking and Validation Path

•Benchmark grade: TotalEnergies Lumicene MR 2002 (mPP, MFR 15, melting point 151°C, dedicated spunbond hygiene nonwoven grade) serves as the ready-made reference standard.

# •Implementation path:

# 1.Plan A: minor modifications to existing plant; samples in 1–2 months;

2.Plan B: adjust catalyst / hydrogen-regulation scheme; primary development target;

# 3.Plan C: joint validation with a sheath-core bicomponent fiber producer.

•Application validation partners (fiber end): Yizheng Chemical Fibre (low-melt fiber series 110–230°C, raw material supplied internally by Sinopec), Shanghai Defulun (low-melt specialty fibers, small-batch sampling available).

•Pilot resources: Sinopec system (Zhenhai Refining, Maoming, Changling Refining) or private plants (Fujian Zhongjing Petrochemical, etc.).

# 6. Risk Notes

•Heat-seal film-grade low-melt PP grades (e.g., JPP terpolymers at 125.5–137°C, TotalEnergies 8573/Z9450) generally have MFR <10; their parameters must not be applied directly. Fiber-grade MFR of 20–50 requires a complete resin redesign.

•Grade names containing “low-melt copolymer” in the market (e.g., K8303) refer to the colloquial term for impact copolymers, with melting points still above 160°C — do not confuse them during grade selection.
