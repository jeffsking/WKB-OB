04 - Technical Principle I

# Molecular Mechanophore: Spiropyran Activation

The classical molecular approach — and why it was evaluated and ultimately rejected for production

### Mechanism

1. 6π electrocyclic ring-openingunder tensile force — the spiro C-O bond scissions at ~240 pN per bond

2. Spiropyran (SP, colorless) $\rightarrow$ Merocyanine (MC, intense purple/violet)

3. Reversible upon release of force — MC relaxes back to SP, fluorescence drops up to56%(Orado et al., 2025)

### X Why Rejected for Diapers

$x$ Threshold too high: ~240 pN/bond ≈ 40,000 PSI — requires 2 orders of magnitude descent for diaper use

$x$ Thermally transient: Merocyanine state is unstable at body temperature (37°C) — signal fades under prolonged wear

$x$ Polar-sensitive: Moisture in hygiene environment accelerates relaxation — false-negative risk

X Low cycle life:Fades after ~3 stretch cycles — insufficient for don/doff frequency

$\bullet$ Processing incompatibility: Cannot survive hot-melt applicator (140-170°C, 20-70 bar)

05 - Technical Principle II

# Engineering Solution: Geometric Optical Mask

The production-ready approach — using the waistband panel's own corrugation as a built-in mechanical shutter

[图片]
## How It Works

[图片]
A rigid opaque hot-melt mask(TB-A ink) is slot-coated over the pre-gathered elastic panel laminate

[图片]
The panel arrives at 2.2× pre-stretch with a 1.2 mm corrugation pitch—a natural geometric actuator

[图片: ✓]
At rest: mask covers corrugation crowns $\rightarrow$ panel readsplain white

[图片]
Under stretch: corrugation flattens $\rightarrow$ printed colorreveals between mask islands

[图片]
Three CD zones with different coat weights $\rightarrow$ three strain thresholds from one ink, one pass

$$\sqrt{\mathbf{x}}\quad\phi(\varepsilon)=1-2\mathbf{a}/\left[\left[\lambda_{0}(1+\varepsilon)\right]\right.$$

Exposed under-print fraction at strain $\epsilon \cdot \lambda_0 = 1.2 \text{ mm} \cdot \phi^* = 0.30 (\Delta E \cdot ab \ge 6)$

### Key Advantages

$\bullet$ $\infty$ reversible cycles

$\bullet$ Zero new chemistry

* Slot-coatable on existing line

$\bullet \approx 0.09\cf/pant add-on

$\bullet$ TRL 8 — near production

$\bullet$ No toxicology package needed

## Critical Control

The lamination pre-stretch ratio must be controlled upstream. Cpk ≥ 1.33 required with per-lot certificate. Above 40 N/m web tension, mask shifts and thresholds drift.

$\rightarrow$ Control conclusion: SPC on incoming panel, not your coater.

### Not a Pressure Meter

Waist elastic (10 mm) at 2.5 N generates~29 mmHg(above discomfort threshold). The indicator is afit and containment guide, not a medical pressure device. Position >15 mm from skin edge.
