TECHNOLOGY ROUTES

#   Four mechanochromic mechanisms — reversible structural color fits repeat monitoring best

Strictly mechanical triggers: tension, pressure, deformation, shear [F]

#   M1

#   Mechanophore activation

Trigger: stretch to critical force threshold | Color: bond scission/rearrangement reveals chromophore | Form: elastomer/polymer networks. Tunable activation threshold — basis of the covalent route.

#   M2

#   Photonic-crystal structural color

Trigger: stretch changes lattice spacing | Color: reflectance wavelength shift (blue$\rightarrow$red) | Form: nanostructured films/fibers. Power-law strain-color relation — reversible and quantifiable.

#   M3

#   CLCE mesogen orientation

Trigger: strain reorients mesogens | Color: birefringence/reflectance change | Form: CLCE fibers/films. Passive and highly sensitive — research-grade only today.

#   M4

#   Surface wrinkling

Trigger: stretch-induced micro-wrinkles | Color: brightness/hue/view-angle dependence | Form: stretchable displays, soft robotics. Limited resolution — unfit for safety indication.

#   Selection implication

Pressure-indicating films (Fujifilm Prescale) are irreversible and for metrology only; most other mechanisms are reversible — continuous fit monitoring requires a reversible route (M2/M3 structural color preferred); one-shot over-tightness alerts can use semi-reversible routes (M1).

ENGINEERING HURDLES

#   Core challenge: down-shift activation thresholds from >=40,000 PSI to 20–150 kPa

From molecular mechanism to engineered threshold — three physical gates to clear [F]

#   T1 Threshold down-shift

Industrial mechanophores target structural-failure detection in high-performance polymers (>40,000 PSI); diaper waistbands need a 20–150 kPa low-tension window (anchored by microactuator studies). A 2-orders-of-magnitude custom descent.

#   T2 The 25-27% strain regime

Barty-King et al. 2025: below 25% strain, HPC stays in a stable viscoelastic pitch-compression regime; beyond 25-27%, it transitions into a flowing/sheared state and sensor integrity collapses.

#   T3 Rheology & signal stability

Above threshold, shear-thinning + inelastic lateral flow can 'relax' the color away — even while stress persists. Gelling agents (e.g., gelatin) must tune rheology to lock the signal.

#   Honest limits

If the signal relaxes under sustained load, caregivers see a 'safe' color while the diaper is still too tight — a false-negative safety reading, worse than no indicator at all.

Therefore: signal-stability validation (holding load within the 25-27% window for at least the product's functional cycle) is the pilot's life-or-death gate. No pass, no scale-up. [F/A]
