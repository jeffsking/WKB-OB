# Layer Architecture: SAP1 Skin-Side + REL + SAP2/SAP3 Blend Poly-Side

CROSS-SECTION

[图片]
Cross-section (thickness exaggerated). REL layer between skin-SAP and blend layer is critical for one-way z-flow.

## SKIN Liner side Non-woven

10% rounded trilobal Mono PET fiber added for better MD distribution

### SAP1 · First responder SAP1 SKIN-SIDE ( separate layer)

Fast-acq · CRC 31 · GBP 53 · low rewet

Direct skin contact under REL. Optimized for speed and low rewet — not for maximum CRC.

## REL FIBROUS LAYER (5-fiber TAB, 28 gsm)

Capillary gradient · anti gel-block · z-flow

## SAP2 + SAP3 BLENDED POLY-SIDE LAYER

Fast-swell + delayed-swell mixed uniformly

SAP2 · Fast-swell (blind) Quick uptake of insults 1–2. Deliberately capped CRC so it doesn't over-swell and choke pore space.

SAP3 · Delayed-swell (blind) Highest CRC reservoir. Slow kinetics keep it out of the way early — engages fully by insult 3-4.

## POLY Poly side Non-woven

Spunlace NW with more natural hydrophilic fibers

## STAGGERED SWELL · the entire capacity-utilization mechanism

SAP2 & SAP3 sit in the same physical layer (mixed, not stacked). Their different swell rates keep the gel bed permeable across all 4 insults instead of sealing after the first.
