Printed on breathable film; urine triggers color / clear | Keywell, MicroCapsule (KR), Qiansebian | Eco, customizable graphics, simplifies process.

MVP route choice &

mechanism comparison

For MVP, prefer the hot-melt route — it is the industrialized 84% mainstream and minimizes pilot risk. Water-based ink is the differentiation / eco option for phase 2 (graphics, sustainability story).

Key difference: hot-melt glue is irreversible (yellow$\rightarrow$blue, stays), suited for 'wetness has occurred' signaling; water-based ink can be designed reversible (custom microcapsule), enabling 'real-time wetness level' cues. The cost delta is ~2-3× (water-based ink vs hot-melt glue) — justify with consumer-test differentiation lift.

MVP DETAIL

#   MVP A & B — product form, position and acceptance criteria

Both sit on the non-skin-contact outer face; coupon-first, not full diaper [A]

#   MVP A · Form

Printed stripe or laminated label patch at core-centerline outer face (front-lower / crotch). Reversible thermochromic ink; indicator only, carries no structure.

#   MVP A · Acceptance

Stable repeatable color in band; ≥70% correctly read 'too hot / too cold / just right'; no curl / blocking / set-off / smudge; readable after 40°C / 75%RH 1 week.

#   MVP B · Form

Hydrochromic patch / window label adjacent to existing WI; dry = hidden, wet = shown (icon option); liquid-exposure trigger first.

#   MVP B · Acceptance

Stable color after set insult; caregivers distinguish from traditional WI; no roller / plate / seal contamination; no abnormal color after 4 weeks in pack.
