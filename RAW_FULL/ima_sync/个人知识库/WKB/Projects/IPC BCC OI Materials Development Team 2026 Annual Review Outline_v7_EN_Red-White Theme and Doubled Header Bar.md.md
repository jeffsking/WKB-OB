## 4. Six "new technology roadmap directions" distilled from WKB (the core increment of this update)

These six are not in the v1 outline. They are directional judgements reverse-engineered from the knowledge base, and they are the basis for the chapters added in v2/v3.

- Zoning replaces homogenisation —— industry consensus has moved from "piling up materials" to "structuring zones": Coco V6 V7 plans zoned SAP density, the dual-layer REL decouples "fast transport" from "strong support", topsheet Z-gradient with dual carding, dual-layer SMS acquisition layer. All OI projects with a single-layer homogeneous architecture (OI-02 / OI-20 being the clearest examples) need their objectives restated accordingly.
- Ultrasonics upgraded from "alternative process" to "the cost-reduction mainline" —— replacing 90% of hot-melt adhesive, equipment localisation >80%, 18-month payback, and the enabling means for the in-line 3D topsheet (30%+ raw material saving) and honeycomb / floating cores. OI-05 (ultrasonic fasteners) and OI-04 need their wording upgraded from "exploration" to "aligned with the process mainline".
- Degradables are counting down to a cost inflection point —— PLA fibre at parity with PP in 2028–2029, bio-based SAP after 2030. This means the initiation timing of OI-24 (PPC-P+PBAT), OI-06 (starch-based SAP) and OI-12/13 (seawater-degradable) aligns closely with the industry inflection, and should be explicitly flagged as a "positioning window".

#### 7.1.8.3 Rationale (item by item)

① English / numerals take Poppins + Calibri —— carrying over the FEI original fonts directly
Poppins and Calibri together account for 62% of all FEI text spans and constitute the deck's de facto two-font system. Carrying them over guarantees "consistency with the FEI layout" without introducing a new stylistic divergence. The division of labour is copied too: Poppins handles headings and labels (geometric sans-serif, with counters close to true circles, a crisp skeleton and a contemporary feel that matches the bright register of the red-white palette), Calibri handles body (large x-height, compact set width, best readability on screen and at small sizes).

② Chinese takes "Source Han Sans / Noto Sans SC" —— three hard reasons
1. Matching character temperament: Source Han Sans has even stroke weight, a square set width and slightly geometric closed counters —— the closest of the Chinese sans-serifs to Poppins' geometric skeleton. Switching to Songti / Fangsong / Kaiti would put the classical Chinese feel directly against Poppins' contemporary geometry, and the red-white palette would look dated as a result.
2. Complete weight range: it offers seven weights from ExtraLight to Heavy, so the seven levels from 9 pt to 40 pt in this scheme can all be resolved within one font family —— no need to combine several Chinese fonts (mixing is the most common source of breakdown in Chinese-English mixed setting).
3. Clean licensing: SIL Open Font License —— usable commercially, embeddable, redistributable. K-C internal review material must pass legal and brand review, so an open-source font avoids copyright risk; it is also a standard font on Google Fonts and Adobe Fonts, so collaborators can almost always obtain it.

③ Fallback plan (cross-machine safety) —— this matters more than the font choice itself
The 25 detail pages will be opened and edited on different colleagues' machines. If they do not have Poppins and Source Han Sans installed, PowerPoint will silently fall back to SimSun and the red-white layout will break on the spot.
- Fonts must be embedded when producing the PPT: PowerPoint → File → Options → Save → tick "Embed fonts in the file" (choose "Embed all characters" so the recipient can edit)
- A step-down safety net: Chinese falls back to Microsoft YaHei (bundled with 100% of Windows installs), English falls back to Calibri (bundled with Office) —— a zero-risk combination with acceptable visual loss
- Recommendation: use Poppins + Source Han Sans for the master (with fonts embedded), and save a "compatible version" using Microsoft YaHei + Calibri for playback on the venue machine
