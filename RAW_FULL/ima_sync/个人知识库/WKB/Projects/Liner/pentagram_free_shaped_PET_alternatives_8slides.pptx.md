## Important platform rules

1

1. Keep PE/PET ES bondable fiber at 80-90 wt% for first trials.

2

For one-component shaped PET, do not start above 20 wt% in a 25 gsm air-through bonded topsheet.

3

Maintain current hydrophilic finish package or equivalent.

4

Match staple length to current carding line, typically 38-44 mm if compatible with existing operation.
