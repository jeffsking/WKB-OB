# BNC based surface crosslink for SAP

Per discussion with Baikerui, they are collaborating with SAP vendor in China to have BNC based surface cross-link chemical. They keep in confidential on SAP vendor name, but agree to check the exclusivity agreement to see if they can directly work with KC suggested SAP vendor in 2 weeks.

According to Baikerui, the new surface cross-link will blend the BNC with size at 50nm x 2um fiber onto SAP surface. Has benefits:

1. cross-link of 50nm x 2um fiber has network on the SAP surface that build on Pressure resistance (AUL) due to BNC's strength.

2. BNC conducts fluid instead of merely spacing. Due to hydrophilic fibril network BNC brings.

3. co-reacts into crosslinked shell. Cellulose hydroxyls react with the same polyol, alkylene-carbonate and epoxide chemistries.
