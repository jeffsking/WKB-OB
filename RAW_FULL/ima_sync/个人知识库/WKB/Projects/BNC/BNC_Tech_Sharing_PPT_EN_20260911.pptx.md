# What BNC is: a nanofiber network "spun" directly by bacteria

04 β-14-glycosidic bonds → nanofibers

## A "network," not "fibers"

Bacteria extrude and assemble nanofibers directly in the broth, then hydrogen-bond them into a 3D network (pellicle membrane) —continuous by nature, with no need to form a web afterwards as CNF does.

## Fundamental difference from plant cellulose

No lignin, no hemicellulose, no pectin — extremely high purity, easy to purify. Plant cellulose must be purified before use; BNCis born pure.

Key insight: BNC's value is not in the word "cellulose," but in thestructural integrityandsurface chemical puritybestowed by the microbe.

# Three nanocellulose types: BNC wins on structure & purity, loses on capacity & co

|Type|Abbrev.|Dimensions|Core advantage|Main challenge|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|Cellulose Nanocrystals|CNC / NCC|Diameter 3-20 nmLength 100-500 nm|Crystallinity >70%, high stiffness(modulus 100-150 GPa)|Hard to disperse, brittle|
|Cellulose Nanofibrils|CNF / NFC|Diameter 10-100 nmLength several µm|High aspect ratio, flexible, strong web formation|High production energy, aggregation ondrying|
|Bacterial Nanocellulose ★|BNC / BC|Diameter 20-100 nmLength several µm|99% purity, 3D network, super water retention,biocompatible|Low capacity, extremely high cost|

How to read this: the three are not substitutes. CNC is a "rigid filler," CNF is a "papermaking fibril," and only BNC is anentire web woven by microbes—in water retention and structural continuity, it stands alone.

# Three industrialization hurdles: 98% is water — drying decides survival

07 / 20

## H1 Fermentation: long cycle, low titer

7-day tray fermentation yields only a2 wt% BNC hydrogel (98% water).

Added demand: decolorization + automated harvesting, or labor eats the margin.

## H2 Drying: energy + pore collapse

Removing 98% water takes huge energy; drying readily causespore collapse (horrification) and loss of porosity.

Hydrogen re-bonding is irreversible — once pores collapse, performance is gone.

## H3 Cost: reference price unverified

99.9% BNC reference price ≈ RMB 50,000/t.

△ Must be re-validated per drying process, for reference only — cannot feed the cost model directly.
