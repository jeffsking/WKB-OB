## 3. Cooperation Model & IP — preconditions, not follow-ups

KC policy: patents cannot be co-owned with suppliers; KC must be the sole rights holder. Where KC proposes the concept and the supplier develops it, KC files as the lead; where it extends the supplier's existing formulations/patents, the supplier leads with possible joint filing, and commercial compensation is negotiable. This creates tension with our own rule of avoiding formulation partners the whole industry uses, to prevent copying. The resolution is scope splitting:

Core active blends stay with us (purple coneflower/citrus; GOS/postbiotic blends). Only "system engineering + application + testing" goes out — precisely their three strongest and our three weakest areas.

Where their base systems are used (micro-emulsion, suspension, gum-based adhesion), they lead — but the contract must include an exclusive/preferential-use window in hygiene products and a no-reuse-within-industry clause.

Scale-up credibility: lab-scale ZnO, self-reported 95 items, no fully successful adhesion case — all conclusions stand on our blind tests and third-party verification.

Both this list and the RFI breakdown can be reformatted as a one-page mind map or split into two separate RFI sets (one on application process and add-on capability, one on five-parameter compounding and stability) if that suits your team better.
