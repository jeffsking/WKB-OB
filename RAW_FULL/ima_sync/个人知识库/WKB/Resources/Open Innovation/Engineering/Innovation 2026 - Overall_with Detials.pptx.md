# In line Perforation

[图片]
[图片]
[图片]
[图片]
Holex-in is the Fameccanica patent solution for the in-line perforation of nonwoven material.

## Main benefits:

● Flexibility in the use of raw materials and in the process with potential cost saving opportunities

● Possible In line Printing before performing avoiding contamination

· Fameccanica In line vision system to check hole presence and dimensions

● Synchronized holes pattern

· Enhanced Web handling going to perforate closed to the material combining

· Easy to use technology

Working principle: two heated grooved rolls working at different speed thus creating friction on material contact surface melting the nw fibers and consequently creating holes

Tested up to 450m/min production speed

## $\blacktriangleright$ Materials tested:

· Nonwoven soft Bico ATB 20-25 gsm PP/PE

· Carded nonwovens

· Material width up to 200 mm

● Hole dimensions: square $d=0,6$ mm x 0,6 mm
