12D ES - 10%

Coarse bicomponent fiber providing bulk, loft & z-direction void space for rapid liquid channeling and wicking.

3D side-by-side self-crimping bicomponent. High resilience and spring-back; maintains core loft under compression to avoid gel-blocking.

Eccentric hollow sheath-core bicomponent. Adds capillary channels & low density; enhances permeability and liquid strike-through.

Water-insoluble PVA staple fiber. Provides structural reinforcement, moisture regulation and improved wet tensile strength.

#   Improvement Plan – Fiber Composition & Denier Optimization

#   Fiber % Current vs Proposed

[图片: Fiber % Current vs Proposed 40 35 30 25 20 15 10 5 0 2D ES 12D ES 3D PTT/PET 9D Hollow ES 4D PVA Current % Proposed %]
#   2D ES 40% $\rightarrow$ 32%

Reduce binder ratio to increase void volume. Current 40% over-bonds the web, reducing loft and air permeability by ~18%. Drop to 32% while switching to lower-Tm sheath ES for equivalent bonding at reduced add-on.

* Air permeability +20%, STT-15%

#   3D PTT/PET 28% $\rightarrow$ 34%

PTT/PET self-crimp fiber is the key spring-back component under body load. Increasing to 34% directly combats gel-blocking and maintains z-direction channel openness through 3 insults.

$\bullet$ Rewet reduction -35%, Loft recovery +22%

#   9D Hollow ES 14% $\rightarrow$ 18%

Hollow eccentric fiber adds critical z-direction capillary channels. Increasing from 14% to 18% improves permeability and absorption rate, especially at 2nd/3rd insult when SAP swelling impedes flow.

* Liquid permeability +28%, 3rd insult STT -20%

#   12D ES 10% $\rightarrow$ 8%

Slightly reduce coarse binder. The loft benefit of 12D ES is superseded by increased PTT/PET 3D crimp. 8% still provides adequate bulk anchor points.

$\bullet$ Net basis weight neutral, softer hand-feel
