# 浅析_ 无纺布湿巾的种类：材料、应用与市场

## Types of Nonwoven Wipes: Materials, Applications and Markets

无纺布湿巾的种类：材料、应用与市场

Diversity of product formats is made possible by variety of nonwovens technology and raw material formats

产品的多样化形态源于无纺布技术及原材料格式的多样性

The diversity of wipes is often framed in terms of end markets, but the more meaningful distinction lies in material engineering. As sustainability pressures and regulatory scrutiny increase, the competitive advantage will shift toward manufacturers that can align nonwoven structure with real-world system constraints, not just product claims.

擦拭巾的多样性通常被归类为终端市场的不同，但更有意义的区别在于材料工程。随着可持续发展压力和监管审查的日益严格，竞争优势将转向那些能够使无纺布结构适应现实系统约束（而非仅仅停留在产品宣称上）的制造商。

## What Are Nonwoven Wipes? 什么是无纺布擦拭巾？

Nonwoven wipes are disposable or reusable cleaning materials engineered by bonding fibers through mechanical, thermal, or chemical processes rather than weaving. This approach allows manufacturers to precisely control performance characteristics such as absorbency, strength, softness, and chemical compatibility. As a result, nonwoven wipes have become essential across a wide range of consumer, healthcare, and industrial applications.

无纺布擦拭巾是抛弃型或可重复使用的清洁材料，它是通过机械、热合或化学工艺将纤维结合在一起，而非通过传统的织造方式。这种方法允许制造商精确控制性能指标，如吸水性、强度、柔软度和化学兼容性。因此，无纺布擦拭巾已成为消费、医疗和工业应用中不可或缺的一部分。

## Nonwoven Wipe Manufacturing Processes

无纺布擦拭巾制造工艺

The diversity of nonwoven wipes is largely made possible by the variety of manufacturing techniques available. Spunlace, spunbond, meltblown, airlaid, and needlepunched processes each produce materials with distinct characteristics that can be engineered to the needs of the final product format. By selecting the appropriate method and fiber composition, manufacturers can tailor wipes to specific uses, whether that means maximizing softness for personal care or enhancing durability for industrial cleaning. In practice, material selection is less about the process itself and more about aligning performance characteristics with end-use requirements.

无纺布擦拭巾的多样性在很大程度上归功于多种多样的生产技术。水刺（Spunlace）、纺粘（Spunbond）、熔喷（Meltblown）、气流成网（Airlaid）和针刺（Needlepunched）工艺各自产出具有独特特性的材料，并可根据最终产品的形式需求进行工程化设计。通过选择合适的工艺和纤维组分，制造商可以量身定制特定用途的擦拭巾——无论是追求个人护理所需的极致柔软，还是增强工业清洁所需的耐用性。 在实践中，材料的选择与其说是关乎工艺本身，不如说是使性能特性与终端使用需求相匹配。

## Types of Nonwoven Wipes by Application

按应用分类的无纺布擦拭巾类型

婴儿护理湿巾Baby Care Wipes

The largest market for nonwoven wipes is baby wipes; however this market is largely commoditized and subject to intense price scrutiny. Currently valued at $6.2 billion, this market is expected to grow 5.4% per year by 2033, reach $9.41 billion. Made from spunlaced or airlaid nonwovens, these products are typically extensions of baby diaper lines and are marketed under the same brand name. In an effort to gain marketshare and drive growth, baby wipes manufacturers are extending their product lines with organic or eco-friendly wipes.

婴儿湿巾是无纺布擦拭巾中最大的市场；然而，该市场已高度商品化，并面临激烈的价格竞争。目前市场估值为62亿美元，预计到2033年将以每年5.4%的速度增长，达到94.1亿美元。这些产品由水刺或气流成网无纺布制成，通常是婴儿纸尿裤产品线的延伸，并沿用同一品牌。为了获取市场份额并驱动增长，制造商正通过有机或环保湿巾来扩展其产品线。

面部清洁湿巾 Facial Cleansing Wipes

Facial wipes are available in a number of varieties ranging from less sophisticated makeup removal applications to more intense skin care applications like exfoliation, toning and dermabrasion. The global facial wipes market is experiencing strong growth, projected to reach approximately $4.81 billion by 2030, growing at a CAGR of around 7.4%

面部湿巾种类繁多，从基础的卸妆应用到更深层的皮肤护理应用（如去角质、爽肤和磨皮）。全球面部湿巾市场增长强劲，预计到2030年将达到约48.1亿美元，复合年增长率（CAGR）约为7.4%。

The industry is increasingly focused on higher-performance and environmentally conscious formulations, balancing convenience with growing scrutiny around single-use products.

行业正日益关注高性能和环保配方，在便利性与对一次性产品日益严格的审查之间寻求平衡。

湿厕纸（可冲散湿巾） (Moist Toilet Tissues)

The market for moist toilet tissue or flushable wipes is rapidly growing, driven by consumer demand for better hygiene and projected to reach over $4 billion by 2033. While that market offers a convenient alternative to toilet paper, it faces ongoing scrutiny over whether these products disperse quickly enough to avoid plumbing and wastewater system issues. The U.S. Environmental Protection Agency has warned that wipes, including some marketed as flushable, can contribute to sewer blockages and infastructure strain.

受消费者对更好卫生体验需求的驱动，湿厕纸或可冲散湿巾市场正在快速增长，预计到2033年规模将超过40亿美元。 虽然该市场为卫生纸提供了便捷的替代方案，但其面临着持续的审查：即这些产品是否能足够快地分散，以避免堵塞管道和废水系统。美国环保署（EPA）曾警告称，包括部分宣称为“可冲散”的湿巾在内，可能会导致下水道堵塞并增加基础设施负担。

These wipes are commonly made with plant-based, plastic-free materials such as cellulose, viscose, and wood pulp through a process that combines hydroentanglement and wetlaid technologies.

此类湿巾通常采用植物基、不含塑料的材料（如纤维素、粘胶和木浆），结合水刺和湿法（Wetlaid）技术制成。

家居擦拭巾 (Household Wipes)

The market for household cleaning wipes, designed for everyday cleaning tasks such as wiping surfaces, removing dust, or disinfecting countertops, is projected to grow 7.2% to reach $2.1 billion by 2032.These wipes are often made from durable nonwoven materials like spunbond or needlepunched fabrics, which provide strength and abrasion resistance. Premoistened antibacterial wipes dominate the market, accounting for 46.2% of the revenue, driven by consumer demand for hygienic and pathogen-free surfaces, but some household wipes are dry, intended to be used with cleaning sprays

用于日常清洁（如擦拭表面、除尘或台面消毒）的家居清洁湿巾市场预计到2032年将增长7.2%，达到21亿美元。这些湿巾通常由耐用的无纺布材料（如纺粘或针刺布）制成，具有良好的强度和耐磨性。预湿润的抗菌湿巾占据了46.2%的营收份额，主要受消费者对无菌、无病原体表面需求的驱动；不过，也有部分家居擦拭巾为干式，需配合清洁喷雾使用。

医疗擦拭巾 (Healthcare Wipes)

Healthcare wipes represent a highly specialized segment of the nonwoven wipes market. These include disinfectant wipes used in hospitals, surgical wipes, and patient care wipes such as bathing cloths. Nonwoven materials used in healthcare wipes are often designed to be lint-free, highly absorbent and compatible with disinfecting chemicals. Spunlace fabrics are again common due to their softness and strength, while airlaid nonwovens may be used for their high absorbency. This market is growing at a rate of 7.7% per year.

医疗擦拭巾是无纺布市场中高度专业化的细分领域。包括医院使用的消毒湿巾、手术擦拭布以及病患护理用的洗澡布。医疗用无纺布通常设计为无屑（lint-free）、高吸收性，且能与消毒化学品兼容。水刺布因其柔软度和强度而常见，而气流成网无纺布则因高吸收性被采用。该市场正以每年7.7%的速度增长。

工业擦拭巾 (Industrial Wipes)

Industrial wipes are another important type, used in manufacturing, automotive and maintenance environments. Engineered for heavy-duty performance, capable of handling oils, solvents, and other tough substances, these wipes are made from materials such as meltblown or spunbond-meltblown composites, which offer excellent absorbency and durability. Industrial wipes may be dry or pre-saturated with specific cleaning solutions tailored to particular tasks, such as degreasing machinery or cleaning precision equipment. This market is projected to rise from roughly $4.2-$5.16 billion in 2024–2025 to over $6 billion by 2029–2033, driven by increasing automation, strict hygiene standards, and demand in manufacturing, automotive, and healthcare sectors

这是另一个重要类别，广泛应用于制造业、汽车业和维保环境。工业擦拭巾专为重型任务设计，能够处理油污、溶剂和其他顽固物质。它们由熔喷布或纺粘-熔喷复合材料（SMS）制成，具有卓越的吸油性和耐用性。工业擦拭巾可以是干式的，也可以根据特定任务（如机械除油或精密设备清洁）预先浸渍特定的清洁溶液。在自动化程度提升和严格卫生标准的推动下，该市场预计将从2024-2025年的约42-51.6亿美元增长到2033年的60亿美元以上。

## Where the Market Is Heading

市场走向

The wipes market continues to expand across consumer and industrial applications, but growth is increasingly shaped by constraints rather than convenience alone. Sustainability expectations, regulatory pressure, and infrastructure limitations are forcing a reassessment of how wipes are designed and used.

擦拭巾市场在消费和工业应用领域持续扩张，但其增长正日益受到“约束条件”而非单纯“便利性”的塑造。可持续发展的预期、监管压力以及基础设施的局限，正在迫使行业重新审视擦拭巾的设计与使用。

In this environment, competitive advantage will depend less on format innovation and more on material engineering—specifically, the ability to balance performance with compatibility across disposal systems, regulatory frameworks, and evolving consumer expectations.

在这种环境下，竞争优势将较少依赖于形式创新，而更多取决于材料工程——具体而言，即在产品性能与废弃处置系统兼容性、监管框架以及不断演变的消费者预期之间找到平衡的能力。
