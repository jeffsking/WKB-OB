#   REL cutting process

REL helps on Pad integrity of 100% SAP core and improves on hand touch feeling softness, mainly made from COPET/PET ES and PE/PET hollow inside ES with Air through bonded process plus durable hydrophilic treatments

Bulky TABCW means more air porosity and less bonding points in between fibers, result in CD shrinking and tension unstable in high-speed converting.

Pattern SAP, ideally has same pattern cutting in REL, where keep the channels for Urine distribution + improved breathability + visual Cue + easier channel embossing for LSN/PSN.

However, pattern cutters in REL has troubles at high converting process:

$\bullet$ Cutting quality due to bulky fiber

$\bullet$ Trimming release issues due to insufficient vacuum because of the fiber to fiber porosities.

$\bullet$ CD shrinking and web path tension control before and after cutting.
