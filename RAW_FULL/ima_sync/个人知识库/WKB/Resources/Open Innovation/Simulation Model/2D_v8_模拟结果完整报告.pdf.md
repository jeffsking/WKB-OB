#   6. Key Insights

1. 1D model overestimates SAP utilization. 1D assumes uniform liquid contact (100% utilization), but 2D reveals actual is 87%. Design should reserve ~13% SAP capacity margin.

2. Edge leakage risk. The 2 outermost slices (6.7 mm each) have near-zero SAP absorption at 240 s. These are potential paths for liquid bypass (reflux). Consider barrier structures or denser SAP at edges.

3. Capillary diffusion speed is limited. At D_cap=1e-9 m2/s, lateral spread +/-40 mm takes ~30 s. Faster transport requires higher k0 or lower mu.

4. 2D model enables design optimization. Tune D_cap, injection width, SAP loading to evaluate utilization vs. spreading tradeoffs.
