# More Plant Based Materials

Sustainability

* Opportunity:

* Sustain some use of bioPE resin to support relationship with Braskem and mitigate future sourcing risks.

* Understand when bio-PP fits within SBP.

* Leverage learning from EMEA FIREFLY in KCNA when need for more bio-based materials arises.

* Barrier:

* Reduced demand for bio-PE now may hinder access to bio-PE and bio-PP for future sustainability goals.

* Continued use of Cabka recycling for our KCNA garment waste streams limits what alternative ingredients we can use.

* Most bio-based liners studied in Feminine Care had worse menses intake & rewet performance.
