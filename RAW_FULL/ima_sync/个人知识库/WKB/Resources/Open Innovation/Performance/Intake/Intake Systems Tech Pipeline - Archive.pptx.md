## Newtonian Fluids

1. Follows Newton's Law of Viscosity

Describes the relationship between shear stress and velocity gradient in a fluid.

$$\tau=-\mu\frac{dv}{dy}$$

where

$\tau$ is the shear stress exerted by the fluid ("drag")

$\frac{dv}{dy}$ is the velocity gradient perpendicular to the direction of shear.

2. Shear Stress linearly dependent on the velocity gradient
   * viscosity does not depend upon the shear stress.

## Non-Newtonian Fluids

1. Follows Power-Law fluid

Described by two parameters rheological model of a pseudoplastic fluid where the viscosity decreases as shear rates increases

$$\tau=A\left(\frac{du}{dy}\right)^{n}+\tau_{y}\ldots\text{Power Law Fluids}$$

Where:

$\frac{du}{dy}$: velocity gradient or shear rate
A: Flow consistency index
n: flow behaviour index
n: appert viscosity

2. Shear Stress is not linearly dependent on the velocity gradient

[图片: TTM 629]

## DSL ("Anatomic absorption")
### TTM 0004

[图片]
The method evaluates product performance under realistic conditions and deformations, such as anatomic shape, motion, pressure and fluid flow (1 gush and 1 continuous trickle). Test ends when product leakage and its analysed g of fluid absorbed.

### HEATED INTAKE ("Anatomic intake") TTM 000386

[图片: HEATED INTAKE ("Anatomic intake") TTM 000386]
A more realistic and in-use like condition test to evaluate the absorption time in sec. Considers anatomic shape, heat and 1 trickle + 1 gush *

### 20° RUNOFF*
TTM 490

[图片]
Method used for wettability performance check. Focused on identifying early leakage potential, through the evaluation of not absorbed fluid (g) in a 20° platform. More focused on material evaluation

COMPRESSION
TTM 676

[图片]
This test is designed to compress an absorbent product in the crotch area to measure the resiliency, flexibility or stiffness, and width recovery of the crotch area of the product..
