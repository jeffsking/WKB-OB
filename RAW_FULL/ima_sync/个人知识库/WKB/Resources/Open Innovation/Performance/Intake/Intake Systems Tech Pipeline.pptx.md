# Current Market

$\bullet$ Most brands have focused on delivering lower rewet through improved surge design and liner-surge embossing (LSE)

* Currently best surge for rewet is Depend CT5 (ATACAMA+), but it has tradeoffs in converting

$\bullet$ Other brands use a family of MOJAVE surges, but will be replaced by hollow bico

* Hollow bico allows basis weight reduction for cost with improved converting
