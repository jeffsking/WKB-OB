# Discussion Highlights

# POISE

$\bullet$ BSL Important: Yes, for competitive advantage

* POISE Originals will need to beat Equate for preference.

* POISE UT & Originals need to offer the same features (other than thickness). Currently in same tier.

$\bullet$ Current State: Off track on Poise Originals, with trickle effects on GFM (GN inserts discontinued).

* Immediate Opportunity: Fix POISE Original BSL

* What's stopping us from fixing POISE Original BSL now?

* What's the minimum consumer information we need to support the business case for a new project?

* Can we afford more expensive materials? Note: Equate is already doing that.

* Additional Opportunity: Go beyond just fixing POISE Original BSL to win in preference vs Equate

* If ActivTech 2.0 for POISE gets approved, link up with POISE UT interests for stronger visual cues (3D materials).

$\bullet$ Barriers: Process capability, cost

$\bullet$ Questions: Will POISE enter super premium space and what would that look like?

# DEPEND

## BSL Important for Tier Differentiation: Yes

* Differentiate DSU through skin health additives. DSU is focused on skin protection & gentle protection and gets the best options. SIROCCO S&H will give insights in May to clarify what other features to differentiate. Considering softness, texture, color, etc.

* Cascade benefits to DPU and scale degree of benefits as cost structure allows. Unlikely to use skin health additives.

## Current State: Off track on DPU and GFM, DSU will be off track on softness after RIDDLER

### Opportunity:

* Bring back softness on DSU after RIDDLER

* Pending S&H insights, upgrade BSL on DPU to strength position vs. Assurance

## Risk: Assurance

### Barriers:

* Vendor capability - How do we effectively do everything we want on BSL? Printing, zoned treatment, additives, softness, etc.

* Process capability - Converting & adhering softer materials

* Cost

* And still want rewet!

* (Added 10/17/23) Build-up can occur with some surfactants containing Stantex H215 (flexo printing) or S6327 (NCSF)

* Next Step: Regroup after May S&H results

# U by Kotex (March 2022, outdated)

$\bullet$ BSL Important for Tier Differentiation: Yes. UBK hypothesized that BSL change on CleanWear was one of reasons for share loss. LORI gets us back on track.

$\bullet$ Current State: Off track on SP, but LORI gets us back on track

$\bullet$ Opportunity: Envision what's next for super premium BSL post-LORI.

* Feel clean & dry: Key focus area that BSL could support. However, knowledge gaps exist on the cues that convey clean & dry for UBK consumer targets.

* For example, consumers picked up on softness & dryness of better stain masking liners, not directly the stain masking benefit.

* Intimate fit: BSL to enable intimate fit to reduce mess on skin and prevent menses from wicking along body to the back. Possibly BSL or other features of the product, not defined or know yet.

* What is the right type of material or product design? Gradient like design?

* What are low costs approaches for intimate fit? Current cost of TLA gives cost wiggle room on the approach.

* Skin health: Potential opportunity, but needs more definition. Actives potential to deliver via pad or via wipe. Otherwise, helpful to have BSLs that draw fluid further away from the skin similar to runny BM needs.

* Sustainability: UBK may follow other brands. Hearing little pull for UBK sustainability now.

$\bullet$ Risk: If LORI is not successful, we'll stay below tier on BSL.

# U by Kotex (New as of 9/26/2023)

* Short-term:

* LORI to assess impact of print quality. New printing needs may arise from that, depending on how success current actions are.

* No BSL changes are needed in the short-term for LORI 2024 or Overnight back bump 2025/2026.

* No formal asks yet for the FEI materials team.

* Future:

* Importance of visual appearance and material type

* Bigger step changes may be required when improving functionality. Degree of changes in LORI was not enough to influence the consumer experience. "Zero menses on skin."

* Similar to POISE UT, UBK may need 3D features, visual cues, layers...and functional performance.

$\bullet$ Have a breathable material, not necessarily need to be full product breathability

* Do we need to tailor the BSL for BONNIE, such as if BONNIE uses U-Bonding with the core or improve fit to body?

* Balance: Protection

* Clean & Secure: Shower fresh concept, or potential to cascade solutions from Balance.

* Printing:

* Discussed how speed impacts printing quality and dpi, and the big role that the graphic design plays on appearance of the product.

* Opportunity: Re-teach the design team on best practices and limitations of graphics design for speed

* Modeling has been done in the past on 3D material printing.

* Wax based print continue to be the better than water based print for rub-off

* Gap: TMD to assess performance when achieving close to body fit.

# Top BSL Barriers & Watch-outs Across KCNA

## Increase process capability

* Eliminate barriers to processing softer materials – adhesion, cutting, etc.

$\bullet$ Increase ability to run different materials – different substrates, different surfactants, topography etc.

## Ask more of vendor capabilities

* Piling on the benefits are going beyond current Fibertext & GNW capabilities – printing + surfactants (& zoning) + additives + softness

## Overcome cost barriers

* Can we afford what we want? Or do focused innovation on it?

* Softness, 3D materials, etc.

## Don't fall behind competition...get a step ahead

* Branded or private label
