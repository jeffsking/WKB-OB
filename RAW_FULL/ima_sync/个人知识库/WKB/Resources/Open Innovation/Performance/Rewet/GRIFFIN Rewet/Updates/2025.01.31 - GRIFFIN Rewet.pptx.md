# GRIFFIN Rewet
Technical Problem Statement

Kerry Clemmer, ADT

Updated 2/12/25

# Problem Statement

$\bullet$ Prior Depend programs have improved rewet, but are not superior.

* Current DPU has a rewet of 0.25g vs P&G Always Discreet's 0.18g.

$\bullet$ Depend needs a holistic approach to achieve rewet superiority.

$\bullet$ Goal: Systematically identify and eliminate every drop of water from every layer to demonstrate ≤0.15g of rewet on Depend Fresh Protection (as measured by closed cradle).

# Summary

$\bullet$ ATACAMA+ made great strides in rewet, but is not best-in-class

$\bullet$ Further rewet reductions means systematically eliminating free fluid from every component of the intake system

* Surge and liner-surge interfaces are in good shape after ATACAMA

* Liner is a known area for improvement

$\bullet$ ActivTech has not needed to be optimized for these ultra-low rewet numbers, so may be an opportunity area as well

* Some mechanisms may transfer from SAM/fluff, but we could need to adapt the playbook for this new core type

# Looking Forward: ActivTech Hybrid

$\bullet$ ActivTech hybrid has never been used where such ultra-low rewet has been a concern, so may not be fully optimized yet

$\bullet$ Early data shows AT hybrid with higher rewet, but the code was lacking liner-surge embossing which was critical to ATACAMA's improved rewet
