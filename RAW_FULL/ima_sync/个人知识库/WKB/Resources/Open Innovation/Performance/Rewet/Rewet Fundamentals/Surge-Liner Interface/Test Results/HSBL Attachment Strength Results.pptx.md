# HSBL Attachment Strength Results

11/18/2025

# Background

Peel strength correlates with the amount of interstitial space between the liner and the surge (i.e. higher peel strength means less interstitial space).

Hypothesis (yet to be validated): Products with less interstitial space (high peel strength) will hold less interstitial fluid and therefore will have lower rewet.

# Results Summary

$\bullet$ Adhesive pattern has the biggest effect on peel strength

$\bullet$ Minibead codes have by far the highest peel strength, summit codes have the least, signature and CF swirl have similar strengths

## Additional Effects:

||Primary Substrate|Adhesive Type|Bonding|
|---|---|---|---|
|Higher Peel Strengths|Surge|Henkel 5402|PICASSO Bonding|
|Lower Peel Strengths|Liner|Rextac RT-8723|No Bonding|

# Conclusions

To reduce liner-surge interstitial space:

$\bullet$ Consider changing adhesive pattern from Summit to Signature or CF swirl

* May have greater impact than liner-surge embossing (pending MicroCT results)

* Note: Summit bond strength may be an anomaly due to possible subpar pattern quality, as it historically yields ≥ bond strength compared to Signature

$\bullet$ Continue applying adhesive to the surge

$\bullet$ Possibly consider increasing adhesive add-on

* Likely to have a very small effect

$\bullet$ Adhesive type is likely not worth changing unless something is observed in microCT testing
