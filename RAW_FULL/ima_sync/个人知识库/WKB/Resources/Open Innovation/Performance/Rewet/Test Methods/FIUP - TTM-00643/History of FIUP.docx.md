# First written as draft STM-00497  then published as TTM-00643 (v0)

oAuthored by Maddie Rutherford (co-op), Jen Pozniak (R&E), and Thea Haessler/VanKopf (TMD)

# oGauge R&R RST 82319

# Parameters & rationale from TL-15297 :

# oInsult location: 5/12 or 7/12 of product length back from product front

Consistent with female anatomy being skewed forward (from anthropometric study AF32161-00, June 2008) and consumer front-to-back flipping

oInsult volume & rate: 3 insults at various add-ons by size (e.g.: ultrathin – 12mL, moderate – 30mL, maximum – 78mL, ultimate – 114mL)

# 75ᵗʰ percentile of post-usage weights (IT-34734).

Three insults was consistent with consumer feedback (studies A-36049, A-35609, see TL-15582).

# Flow rates from literature review (8mL/s for pads).

# 37 degrees to mimic body temp (no other rationale given).

# oWait time: 15 minutes  between insults, 2 minutes before rewet

Time between insults to allow product to come to equilibrium and keep testing time reasonable.

Time from insult to rewet shorter because consumer assessment of whether the pad is wet would reasonably happen before equilibrium and when consumer changed products. (study IT-34734 says this happens soon after feeling wet)

# oRewet media: blotter paper

Sufficient capacity to hold all fluid that is available for flowback/rewet (collagen is not – it has 1/10 capacity as blotter so would bias surface moisture measurements low)

# Can absorb fluid on timescales applicable to rewet (collagen can not)

# Available, preparation, and ease of use is better

# oRewet pressure: 0.7psi

“prior research” says a point of pressure is a better match to in-use conditions than a uniform pressure over the surface

# 1” diameter chosen by pad width

# No information on how weight was chosen

# oRewet time: 2 minutes

# Adequate time for blotter to collect flowback with minimal variability

# Prior to TTM-00001 FIFE Flowback (TL-5517, 1999):

oFIFE flowback test (STM-2414) used a vacuum box to apply 0.5psi of pressure between the 2ⁿᵈ and 3ʳᵈ insult

oRewet test (developmental STM-2408) used a vacuum box to apply 1psi of pressure for 3 minutes

0.5psi was used in TEWL because it was the pressure at which the pressure cuff apparatus best maintained constant pressure (TL-5766, references TL-5391) and was consistent with ret cap and FIFE flowback testing
