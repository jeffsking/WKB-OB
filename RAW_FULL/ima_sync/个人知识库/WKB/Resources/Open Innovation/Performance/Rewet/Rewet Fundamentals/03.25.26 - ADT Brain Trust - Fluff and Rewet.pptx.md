# Background: Ret Cap & Rewet

$\bullet$ GRIFFIN played with ret cap a lot and did not see impacts to closed cradle rewet

$\bullet$ Different rewet mechanism to HPR

* Closed cradle rewet driven by desorption, so residual fluid in the intake system

* High pressure rewet driven by flowback, so loosely held fluid in the core

$\bullet$ Unknown if this would hold true if ret cap continued to drop or if insult size increased

# Background: ATH Fluff & Rewet

$\bullet$ In GRIFFIN, ATH resulted in higher rewet than SAM/Fluff.

$\bullet$ In ATH systems, we hypothesize fluff acts as part of the intake system so residual fluid can be left in the fluff and come back out as rewet.

* Would affect Closed Cradle Rewet and Low Pressure Rewet (desorption)

$\bullet$ Adding SAM to the fluff may capture the residual fluid.
