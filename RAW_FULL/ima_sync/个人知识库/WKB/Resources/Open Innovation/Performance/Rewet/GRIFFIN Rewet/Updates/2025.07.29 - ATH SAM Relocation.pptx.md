# DEPEND Ultra-Low Rewet Update

Matt Valaskey, Richmond Cohen

7/29/25

# Overarching Hypothesis

Overarching
Hypothesis:

AT hybrid core products will have parity or higher rewet than SAM/fluff products because the pulp layer is part of the intake system, any retained fluid in the intake system (including pulp) may be available to be expressed as rewet.

Testing Plan:

Capillary tension, residual fluid mass and microCT were used to explore desorption energy and fluid retention in the intake system.

# Code Terminology

$\bullet$ Whole Absorbent System - Liner, surge, CW, pulp

$\bullet$ LSE+CW – Liner/surge embossing, single layer of upper core wrap

$\bullet$ Wrapped Core – pulp core with wrap

Hypothesis: The desorption energy of AT hybrid products will be higher than that of SAM/fluff core products because of the additional layers of the intake system retaining fluid that may possibly be expressed as rewet.
