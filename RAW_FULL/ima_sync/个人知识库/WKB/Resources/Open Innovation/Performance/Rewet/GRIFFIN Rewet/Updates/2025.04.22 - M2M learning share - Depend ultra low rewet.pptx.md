# Depend Ultra-Low Rewet Learning Share

K. Clemmer, ADT

Updated 4/22/25

# Goal

Systematically identify and eliminate every drop of water from every layer to demonstrate ≤0.15g of rewet on Depend Fresh Protection (as measured by closed cradle).

# Review of 2/25/25 Update

$\bullet$ Further rewet reductions means systematically eliminating free fluid from every component of the intake system

* Surge and liner-surge interfaces are in good shape after ATACAMA

* Liner is a known area for improvement

$\bullet$ ActivTech may be an opportunity area as well as it has not needed to be optimized for these ultra-low rewet numbers previously

* Some mechanisms may transfer from SAM/fluff, but we could need to adapt the playbook for this new core type

Link to deck: M2M 2.25.25 - Depend Ultra Low Rewet.pptx
