# Conclusion

$\bullet$ Rewet is a useful measure of loosely held fluid in the product

$\bullet$ For technical decisions, mostly useful to check absorbent design

* Not typically operating in range of rewet that will be impactful to consumer experience

$\bullet$ Correlations to other types of dryness may be leveraged to strengthen claims in the dryness space

* Pursuing a more robust, purpose-driven study with EWL and sensory on inco pads to strengthen data set

* Discussions with Legal ongoing about claims language and substantiation methods
