# Capillarity of intake system dictates rewet

$\bullet$ Central principle of the rewet design principles

$\bullet$ Capillarity test method tells us much about the residual fluid that causes rewet

* How much is left & where (w/ microCT)

* How easy it is to dry a material

* What pore sizes are in a material

$\bullet$ Paired with fundamentals this reveals material levers

* Major factors are wettability ($\theta$) and pore size ($R$)

[图片: Absorption Desorption *]
Young-LaPlace Equation

$$\Delta P=\frac{-2\gamma\cos\theta}{R}$$

P = pressure

$\gamma$ = surface tension

$\theta$ = contact angle (wettability)

$R$ = pore radius
