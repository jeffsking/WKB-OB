# Cohesive fastener

Softer material for hook & loop replacement

## Consumer Insight & Benefit

The current hook-and-loop material is harsh, noisy, and may scratch a baby's skin.

## Feature

Use cohesive material to replace hook & loop as new generation fastener. The material only sticks to themselves without sticking to clothes and skin. It is easy to peel off with minimal noise while retaining sufficient shear force to keep the diaper securely fastened. The material is light-weighted and will not scratch skin.

# Odor Control

## Multi-path odor management for BCC diaper platform

### Consumer Insight & Benefit

● Odor control is a meaningful unmet need that builds consumer confidence in personal care.

· Fear of odor can matter as much as actual odor — during wear, removal and disposal.

● For BCC: combine true technical odor reduction with reassuring sensory cues.

### Key Claims

● Claim Space: TBD — RTB for clean & fresh perception and better odor management.

- Helps reduce unpleasant odor during wear

· Supports a cleaner, fresher experience

· Reassures caregivers with technical + perceptual cues

### Feature / Technical Solution

Capture: Trap VOCs in structure

Alter: React with VOCs

Microbial: Healthier odor environment

Perception: Sensorial reassurance cue

Platforms: AqFresh liner / film · Carbon-based odor control · Zeolite catalyst / particles

Technical solve + emotional solve

## Advantages / Preliminary Test Results

### Screening learnings

● eNose is a quick, lower-cost screening tool with high relevance to human response.

● Danson SAM,carbon surge & AqFresh-treated liner showed lower odor than control — selected for human panel.

● eNose and human sensory testing showed similar trends.

### Feasibility / Next Steps

· Zeolite granules show promise for absorbent-core direct add-on.

● Particle size ~0.023–0.038 mm is the current consideration for core direct add-on.

Key barriers: process feasibility, odorant definition and external validation method.
