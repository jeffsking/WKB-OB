# Handbook of Polymer Testing – Short-Term Mechanical Tests

## Chapter 2 Report: Test Piece Preparation（试样制备）

Author: Edward N. Brown (Rapra, 2002)

### 2. Key Concepts with Operationalized Definitions（关键概念操作化定义）

#### 2.1 Test Piece（试样/试件）

- English Definition: "before a test can be carried out test pieces have to be obtained in the form and dimensions specified. Essentially, the two possibilities are directly moulding or cutting from a sheet or product." [Line 9-14]
- Location: [Line 9-14] / p.25
- Usage Context: "With the exception of tests on whole products, before a test can be carried out test pieces have to be obtained in the form and dimensions specified." [Line 9-10] — Defines the fundamental requirement that all mechanical tests begin with properly prepared test specimens.
#### 2.2 Moulding vs. Cutting（模塑 vs. 切割）

- English Definition: "The orientation produced in an injection moulded test piece may yield very different results from those from a relatively non-oriented compression moulding process." [Line 70-73]
- Location: [Line 70-78] / p.26
- Usage Context: "When considering moulding of test pieces or test sheets the first consideration is whether to produce by injection or compression." [Line 70-71] — Describes the fundamental choice between injection and compression moulding and how orientation affects test results.
#### 2.3 Multipurpose Test Pieces（多用途试样）

- English Definition: "It is common for the test piece moulded to conform to ISO 3167 which specifies multi-purpose test pieces. Two test pieces are detailed but type A is preferred. Both are tensile dumbbell shapes which are multipurpose because, in addition to the use for tensile properties, various other tests can be carried out by machining the appropriate shape from the moulding." [Line 86-89]
- Location: [Line 86-89] / p.26
- Usage Context: Part 1 includes the ISO A and B moulds, "A being the multipurpose test pieces of ISO 3167 and B for bars." [Line 144-145] — The ISO 3167 standard defines dumbbell-shaped specimens that serve multiple test types.
#### 2.4 Compression Moulding（压缩模塑）

- English Definition: "ISO 293 covers compression moulding of thermoplastic materials and gives fairly basic requirements for the press, moulds and procedure. Both frame type and positive moulds are allowed and there are four choices of cooling rate." [Line 124-127]
- Location: [Line 124-131] / p.27
- Usage Context: "Compression moulding of thermoset materials is covered by ISO 295 which is applicable to phenolics, aminoplastics, melamine phenolics, epoxides and unsaturated polyesters." [Line 174-177] — Compression moulding applies to both thermoplastic and thermoset systems with distinct standards.
#### 2.5 Injection Moulding（注塑成型）

- English Definition: "Injection moulding of thermoplastics is covered by three parts of ISO 294, Part 1 deals with general principles and moulding of multipurpose and bar specimens, Part 2 with small tensile bars and Part 3 with small plates." [Line 137-139]
- Location: [Line 137-156] / p.27
- Usage Context: "Reciprocating screw type injection machines are specified with tolerances for times and temperatures and recommendations for screw diameter and locking force." [Line 149-151] — Defines the three-part ISO 294 standard framework for injection moulding of test specimens.
#### 2.6 Stamping（冲压裁切）

- English Definition: "This operation is only applicable to flexible sheet materials and film but for such materials is the most convenient way of producing test pieces." [Line 224-225]
- Location: [Line 224-263] / p.29-30
- Usage Context: "It is essential that dies are very sharp and free from nicks or unevenness in the cutting edge which would produce flaws in the test piece." [Line 250-252] — Stamping is the preferred method for thin flexible materials but requires meticulous die maintenance.
#### 2.7 Machining（机加工）

- English Definition: "The term machining is used rather broadly here to cover cutting shapes from sheet or products and reduction of thickness. Rigid materials other than very thin film cannot be cut or stamped with a blade." [Line 318-320]
- Location: [Line 316-395] / p.31-33
- Usage Context: "It is usual to rough out rectangular test pieces using a saw or cutting disk... More generally, the final shape is formed by milling or planing." [Line 330-337] — Machining covers sawing, milling, planing, drilling, and grinding for rigid materials.
#### 2.8 Notch Geometry（缺口几何形状）

- English Definition: "Very sharp notches are usually produced by tapping or sliding a razor blade into the machined root of a relatively blunt notch." [Line 383-385]
- Location: [Line 379-390] / p.33
- Usage Context: "As impact strength can be very sensitive to notch geometry it is essential that notches are accurately and reproducibly cut. ISO 2818 specifically excludes the use of abrasives on notches." [Line 388-389] — Critical for impact testing; notch precision directly affects measured impact strength.
#### 2.9 Anisotropy（各向异性）

- English Definition: "A further part of ISO 294 for producing test pieces for determining anisotropy of the properties of thermoplastic mouldings is at a committee draft stage. This is simply using a two cavity mould to produce plates from which tensile test pieces can be machined." [Line 170-172]
- Location: [Line 170-172] / p.28
- Usage Context: Describes how moulding-induced orientation creates directional property differences, requiring specialized test piece preparation for characterization.
#### 2.10 Fibre Reinforced Plastics (FRP) Test Pieces（纤维增强塑料试样）

- English Definition: "Fibre reinforced plastics test pieces are prepared by such methods as hand lay-up, moulding of a prepreg and spiral winding. ISO 1268 has currently 4 parts." [Line 200-204]
- Location: [Line 200-210] / p.30
- Usage Context: "Modelling of injection moulding of fibre reinforced materials has been investigated by Guenther and co-workers who concluded that drastic changes in properties can result from changing moulding parameters." [Line 206-208] — FRP test pieces require specialized preparation methods distinct from neat thermoplastics.

### 4. Chapter Connections（章节衔接）

#### 4.1 前承内容（前置依赖）

- Chapter 1（全书引言/测试概述）: Chapter 2 承接全书测试框架，将"测试方法"从概念层面推进到实际操作层面。本书标题明确指出是"Short-Term Mechanical Tests"（短期机械测试），Chapter 2 作为试样制备章节，为后续所有机械性能测试（拉伸、弯曲、冲击等）奠定操作基础。
- 参考文献 [1-5]: ISO 10350-1/2 和 ISO 11403-1/2/3 涉及可比数据的获取与呈现，Chapter 2 开头即引用这些标准，说明试样制备的一致性是实现数据可比性的前提。[Line 34-36]
- 材料规范标准: Chapter 2 引用了大量特定塑料的 ISO 标准（Table 2.1，18种材料），说明试样制备必须与具体材料标准配套使用。[Line 35-36, 96-119]
#### 4.2 后启内容（为后续章节铺垫）

- 拉伸测试章节: ISO 3167 多用途哑铃形试样是后续拉伸测试的标准试样形式，本章定义的注塑/压缩/冲压/机加工四种制备方式直接决定后续拉伸数据的可比性。[Line 86-89]
- 冲击测试章节: 缺口加工（notching）的精确性和重现性是冲击强度测试的核心变量，本章对此进行了专门论述。[Line 379-390]
- 薄膜测试章节: ASTM D4204 薄膜试样制备标准和冲压操作细节为薄膜类测试做准备。[Line 197-198, 224-263]
- 复合材料测试章节: ISO 1268 系列标准和纤维增强材料试样制备方法为复合材料测试奠定基础。[Line 200-210]
#### 4.3 交叉引用关系

- ISO 294 ↔ ISO 3167: 注塑标准引用多用途试样标准，形成规范层级结构。[Line 86-87, 144]
- ISO 293 ↔ ASTM D4703: ISO 与 ASTM 标准的对应/不完全对应关系贯穿全文。[Line 124-131]
- ISO 2818 ↔ ISO 4661: 机加工通用标准与橡胶专用标准的互补关系。[Line 261-263]
- Table 2.1 ↔ Part 2 of material standards: 特定材料标准中通常包含"Part 2: Preparation Of Test Specimens and Determination of Properties"部分，与通用制备标准（ISO 293/294/295）配套使用。[Line 82-84]
- Chapter 2 ↔ 后续测试方法章节: 本章末尾指出"test pieces are defined in a range of test method standards"，暗示试样定义分散在各测试方法标准中，本章仅提供制备方法的概览。[Line 214-218]
### 5. Business Connections（业务连接点）

业务背景: 卫材（纸尿裤/湿巾）创新研发 + 木浆与纸品战略采购专家；关注点：芯体结构、SAP、无纺布、胶黏剂、采购成本

#### 5.1 芯体结构（Absorbent Core Structure）

适用测试方法/标准:
- 无纺布（非织造布）是卫材芯体的骨架材料，其力学性能（拉伸强度、伸长率）的测试直接受试样制备方法影响。
- 本书中 ISO 2818（机加工） 和 冲压（stamping, Line 222-263）的方法论适用于从无纺布卷材中裁切拉伸试样。书中特别指出裁切方法对结果的影响极大：拉伸强度偏差可达30%，断裂伸长率偏差可达75%。[Line 237-241]
- 业务启示：在评估不同供应商的无纺布样品时，必须确保裁切方法的一致性（建议使用冲裁模 die-cutting），否则测得的拉伸性能差异可能部分源于试样制备而非材料本身差异。

#### 5.2 高吸水树脂（SAP）

适用测试方法/标准:
- SAP 通常以粉末或颗粒形态使用，与热塑性材料的"granules or powder"形态类似 [Line 49-50]。
- 若需对 SAP 压片后测试其压缩强度或吸水膨胀力学，压缩模塑（ISO 293 / ASTM D4703） 的工艺参数（温度、压力、冷却速率）将直接影响测试结果。[Line 124-131]
- 书中指出 "working to this standard alone could produce considerable variation in properties"，强调必须结合具体材料规范确定工艺参数。[Line 127]
- 业务启示：对比不同 SAP 供应商的力学性能数据时，应关注其试样制备条件是否一致；采购时可要求供应商提供基于统一压缩模塑条件的测试数据。

#### 5.3 无纺布（Spunbond/Nonwoven Fabric）

适用测试方法/标准:
- 无纺布的核心测试是拉伸性能和撕裂强度，试样制备方法直接决定测试准确性。
- 书中关于 拉伸强度 vs. 断裂伸长率的变异性 研究具有直接参考价值：Yield strength data 变异性较小（≤10%），而 ultimate tensile strength 可达30%，elongation at break 可达75%。[Line 237-241]
- 这意味着断裂伸长率是无纺布质量管控中最难重现的指标，采购验收时应给予更大的容差范围。
- 冲压刀具维护的重要性（Line 300-309）：卫材生产线上大量使用冲裁工序，刀具磨损直接影响产品边缘质量和后续测试结果。
- 业务启示：
  - 建立无纺布来料检验的标准化裁切流程（推荐 sharp die + slightly yielding backing material，参考 ISO 2818/4661）
  - 对断裂伸长率指标，在采购合同中设置更宽的接受区间

#### 5.4 胶黏剂（Adhesives/Adhesive）

适用测试方法/标准:
- 胶黏剂通常以液态或糊状形式使用，书中关于 PVC糊状物制备（ISO 4612 行星式混合机 / ISO 11468 溶解法） 的标准化混合程序有方法论参考价值。[Line 54-64]
- Harrison等人的研究指出 milling temperature 对塑料化PVC的拉伸强度有"biggest single influence"，类比而言，胶黏剂的混合温度同样可能是影响最终粘结强度的最关键参数。[Line 62-64]
- 业务启示：在评估胶黏剂供应商的测试数据时，应确认其混合/制备工艺参数是否统一；自研配方中应严格控制混合温度作为关键工艺参数（KPP）。

#### 5.5 采购成本（Procurement Cost）

- 标准遵循的经济性：书中指出标准"have not been followed as frequently as they should"，[Line 32-33] 说明行业普遍存在不按标准执行的情况。对于采购方，这既是风险也是机会：

- 风险：供应商若未按标准制备试样，其提供的测试报告可能无法真实反映材料性能。
- 机会：在招标文件中明确要求供应商按照指定 ISO/ASTM 标准制备试样并报告工艺参数，可提升数据可比性，降低因测试偏差导致的误判成本。
- 模具与刀具投资：书中强调 sharp dies 和高质量模具的重要性 [Line 250-256, 300-309]。虽然投资冲裁模和精密模具会增加初期成本，但可显著降低因试样制备偏差导致的质量问题和退货成本。
