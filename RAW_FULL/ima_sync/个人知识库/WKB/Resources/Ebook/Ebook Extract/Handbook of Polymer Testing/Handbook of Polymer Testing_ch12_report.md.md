### 2. Key Concepts with Operationalized Definitions

#### Concept 1: Fracture Mechanics（断裂力学）

- Definition: "Fracture mechanics provides a way of interpreting the material response independently of geometry through consideration of the loads or stresses that cause a crack to propagate." [Line 10-12]
- Usage Context: "The basis of the fracture mechanics approach is that the material contains flaws or cracks at which stress concentrations occur when a load is applied." [Line 26-27]
#### Concept 2: Energy Release Rate, G / Critical Energy Release Rate, Gc

- Definition: "The energy release rate, G, is defined as the rate of energy released by the crack growth. The critical energy release rate, Gc, is the rate for fracture to occur and is expressed as energy per unit test piece thickness and unit crack growth (per unit fractured area)." [Line 35-38]
- Usage Context: "Gc may be constant or vary with crack length and in the latter case a curve of Gc against crack growth (resistance or R curve) is needed to fully characterise the material." [Line 39-47]
#### Concept 3: Critical Stress Intensity Factor, Kc

- Definition: "The critical stress intensity factor, Kc, is the minimum stress intensity for fracture to occur and, although called a factor, has units of Pa m⁰·⁵." [Line 49-50]
- Relationship: "Gc and Kc are related by: Kc = (EGc)⁰·⁵ where E is Young's modulus." [Line 53-55]
#### Concept 4: Mode I Propagation (GIC, KIC)

- Definition: "When the geometry of test is such that the crack faces move apart with the displacement being normal to the crack faces it is termed mode I propagation and Gc and Kc are termed GIC and KIC." [Line 60-62]
- Usage Context: "Largely because of the lower accuracy expected at higher rates multiple test pieces with varying initial crack length are used to obtain GIC." [Line 141-142]
#### Concept 5: Linear Elastic Fracture Mechanics (LEFM)

- Definition: "In linear elastic fracture mechanics (LEFM) all energy dissipation is associated with the fracture process and the deformation that occurs is linear elastic." [Line 34-35]
- Usage Context: "This is based on LEFM using either single edge notch bending (SENB) or compact tensile (CT) test pieces as illustrated in Figures 12.1 and 12.2." [Line 75-76]
#### Concept 6: Crack Tip Opening Displacement (CTOD)

- Definition: "Fracture toughness is characterised by parameters such as crack tip opening displacement (CTOD) or J-integral. With CTOD, a plot of load against crack mouth opening displacement is used to find the plastic component of the crack opening." [Line 66-68]
- Usage Context: Applicable when "significant plastic deformation takes place and elastic–plastic fracture mechanics apply." [Line 64-65]
#### Concept 7: J-Integral / Jc

- Definition: "Jc is the equivalent of Gc for a non-linear elastic material." [Line 68]
- Usage Context: "ASTM also has a method, D6068, for determining J-R curves which is intended to apply to materials that would not give a valid characterisation by LEFM." [Line 156-157]
#### Concept 8: Resistance Curve (R-curve)

- Definition: "A curve of Gc against crack growth (resistance or R curve) is needed to fully characterise the material." [Line 39-40]
- Usage Context: "The same basic test piece configurations as for LEFM are used and the method is based on a multi test piece approach with each being loaded to a different displacement and providing one point on the J-R curve." [Line 163-165]
#### Concept 9: Single Edge Notch Bending (SENB) & Compact Tensile (CT)

- Definition: LEFM test piece configurations: "based on LEFM using either single edge notch bending (SENB) or compact tensile (CT) test pieces." [Line 75-76]
- Usage Context: "For linearity, the limitation specified is arbitrary and equates to better than 10% non-linearity." [Line 79-80]

### 4. Chapter Connections

Connection to preceding chapters (Chapters 1–11 — Traditional Stress-Strain Tests):
- The chapter opens with a direct critique of all prior test methods: "All the traditional standard stress-strain tests for plastics have a severe handicap for design purposes because the results are geometry dependent and they do not yield fundamental properties." ### 5. Business Connections（卫材行业应用价值分析）

#### 5.1 芯体（Core / Fluff Pulp）的断裂韧性

芯体作为卫材的核心吸水支撑层，在婴儿纸尿裤和成人失禁用品的穿着过程中承受反复弯折和拉伸应力。传统应力-应变测试"have a severe handicap for design purposes because the results are geometry dependent" [Line 8-9]，而断裂力学"provides a way of interpreting the material response independently of geometry" [Line 10-12]。对芯体进行断裂韧性评估（GIC/KIC）可量化其在存在微缺陷时抵抗裂纹扩展的能力，从而指导芯体纤维配比和克重的优化设计。

#### 5.2 SAP（高吸水性树脂）的断裂行为

SAP 吸水后体积膨胀数十倍，内部易产生内应力和微裂纹。Griffith 理论中"the material contains flaws or cracks at which stress concentrations occur when a load is applied" [Line 26-27]的机制在 SAP 凝胶中尤为突出。SAP 吸水后的凝胶属于 ductile material，"the energy balance will be with the work done to cause plastic deformation as well as the creation of new fracture surface" [Line 30-32]。采用 J-积分或 CTOD 方法（适用于"materials that would not give a valid characterisation by LEFM" [Line 156-157]）可量化 SAP 凝胶的韧性，防止吸水膨胀后的破裂泄漏。

#### 5.3 无纺布（Nonwoven）的韧性表征

无纺布广泛用于卫材表层（顶棚）和底层（底膜内层）。其纤维毡结构中存在天然的不均匀性，相当于材料中的初始缺陷。断裂力学"through consideration of the loads or stresses that cause a crack to propagate" [Line 11-12]的视角，可将无纺布的撕裂/剥离行为与裂纹扩展理论关联。Mode I propagation（GIC/KIC）[Line 60-62]特别适用于评价无纺布在穿着弯折时的层间剥离抗性。

#### 5.4 胶黏剂（Adhesive）的界面断裂韧性

卫材制品使用多种胶黏剂（条胶、点胶、热熔胶）将不同层次的材料（无纺布/SAP/芯体/PE 膜）粘合。胶黏剂界面的断裂行为是产品可靠性的关键。"The crack will grow when the stress intensity at the tip of the crack exceeds the cohesive strength of the material" [Line 27-28]这一原理直接对应胶黏剂界面失效的临界条件。通过 GIC/KIC 的量化，可以建立胶黏剂选型和涂胶工艺的定量评价体系。

#### 5.5 采购成本与供应商评价

- 标准化优势： ISO 13586 [Line 195-196]和 ASTM D5045 [Line 201-202]提供了国际通用的断裂韧性测试标准，使采购方可以对不同供应商的原材料（无纺布、SAP、PE 膜）进行统一的断裂韧性数据对比，避免因测试方法差异导致的采购偏差。
- 成本-性能平衡： "Inevitably, this is one reason why fracture mechanics test are relatively little used in industry" [Line 126-129]——断裂力学测试成本高于常规拉伸/弯曲测试。在采购决策中，断裂韧性测试可作为高价值材料（如高端 SAP、特种无纺布）的补充评价手段，而对大宗通用材料可继续使用常规测试，形成分层化的供应商评价策略。
- 失效预防： 通过"validated for size criteria before being reported" [Line 122-123]的严谨验证流程，采购方可以确保供应商提供的断裂韧性数据的可靠性，避免因数据失真导致的材料选型失误和批量质量事故。

#### 5.6 动态加载场景

卫材在穿着过程中会经历跳跃、运动等动态载荷。ISO DIS 17281 的"Moderately High Loading Rates (1 m/s)" [Line 198-199]标准可为卫材材料的动态断裂韧性评价提供方法支撑，"Largely because of the lower accuracy expected at higher rates multiple test pieces with varying initial crack length are used to obtain GIC" [Line 141-142]的多试样方法可提高评价可靠性。
