### Ch1 — Technology of Polyolefin Film Production

> 交叉概要：- concept: Polyolefin film grades (LDPE/LLDPE/HDPE/PP) and their catalytic origin
- related PoP (Principles of Polymerization, Odian 2004) topic: free-radical polymerisation (Ch3, high-pressure LDPE radical process) and stereoregular polymerisation via Ziegler–Natta / metallocene (Ch8, isotactic PP tacticity)
- weave note: Ch1 states LDPE is "polymerised by the high-pressure radical process" (p.7) and PP catalysts give ">99% isotactic structure" (p.13). PoP Ch3 notes PE blowing/melt strength hinges on radical-chain branching; PoP Ch8 notes ZN gives >95% α-PP and metallocene single-site raises isotacticity/PDI control. Ch1's qualitative "long branches → melt strength" is exactly the topological consequence PoP explains via chain-transfer/branching in radical PE; Ch1's ">99% isotactic" maps to PoP's stereoregular PP (iPP Tm 165–175 °C, here 162 °C). No page numbers invented; PoP facts are from the distilled module.
- concept: Antioxidant classes (hindered phenol + phosphite; HALS)
- related PoP topic: polymer degradation & stabilisation (Ch9, oxidative degradation via hydroperoxides)
- weave note: Ch1 p.24–25 describes hindered-phenol hydrogen donation and phosphite peroxy-group removal, and HALS radical scavenging (p.25). PoP Ch9 §成分 identifies "氧化降解(氢过氧化物)" and the HALS/nitroxide cycle; the synergist pair (primary radical scavenger + secondary hydroperoxide decomposer) is the same two-class scheme PoP calls out for PVC/PP ageing control. Ch1 gives the application-level mechanism; PoP supplies the peroxy/alkoxy radical chemistry.
- concept: Surface energy / polar-group introduction by corona and maleic-anhydride grafting
- related PoP topic: polymer reactions / grafting (Ch9, graft Onto/From/Through)
- weave note: Ch1 p.21 notes PP "can be grafted with maleic anhydride to increase the adhesion." PoP Ch9 lists grafting Onto/From/Through as the three modification paradigms; MAH-grafted PP tie layers (Ch1 p.37) are a textbook "Onto" graft used in hygiene-film lamination.。

### Ch2 — Processing of Polyethylene Films

> 交叉概要：- concept: PE synthesis routes (high-pressure radical vs Ziegler–Natta/metallocene)
- related PoP (Principles of Polymerization, Odian 2004) topic: free-radical chain polymerisation (Ch3, high-pressure LDPE) and coordination polymerisation / Ziegler–Natta & metallocene (Ch8)
- weave note: Ch2 p.41 states LDPE is made by "free-radical method, usually at high pressures (up to 3500 atm) and high temperatures (up to 300 °C)" and ZN/metallocene for milder conditions. PoP Ch3 explains the radical-chain mechanism (initiation–propagation–termination–transfer, Trommsdorff/gel effect) that makes high-pressure PE branch-rich; PoP Ch8 notes ZN (>95% α-olefin, activity > metallocene 100×) and metallocene single-site give controlled comonomer/tacticity. Ch2's LCB-vs-SCB distinction is the macroscopic consequence of radical chain-transfer branching (PoP Ch3) vs coordination insertion (PoP Ch8). No PoP page numbers invented.
- concept: Melt index as inverse molecular-weight proxy; MWD / shear-thinning
- related PoP topic: molecular-weight averages & PDI (Ch1); rheology link to Mw/Mn (Ch1, Ch3)
- weave note: Ch2 p.42–43 defines MI as inverse chain length and notes MWD broadens shear-thinning. PoP Ch1 states polymers are "mixtures of molecules of different molecular weight" (PDI) and that a specified compromise molecular weight is the design target — Ch2's "higher MI = weaker but easier flow" is the converter-facing expression of PoP's compromise-Mw principle.
- concept: Strain hardening / extensional viscosity in blown film
- related PoP topic: polymer melt rheology & processing (Ch3+Ch8 context); not a separate PoP synthesis chapter but the PoP module's §4 lists rheology under processing. 
- weave note: Ch2 p.49 attributes strain hardening to LCB and notes it aids bubble stability. PoP action card 2 (PE/PP melt-blown) lists "LDPE 高压: 120–300 MPa, 140–325 °C, 单程≤30%" and notes metallocene/iPP MFI 800–1200 for meltblown — complementary: PoP addresses nonwoven meltblowing while Ch2 addresses film blowing, both governed by the same LCB/elongational-rheology physics.。

### Ch3 — Processing Conditions and Durability of Polypropylene Films

> 交叉概要：- concept: Stereoregular (isotactic) PP synthesis via Ziegler–Natta catalysts
  - related PoP (Principles of Polymerization, Odian 2004) topic: Ch.8 Stereochemistry — Ziegler–Natta & metallocene catalysts, stereoregularity, Cossee–Arlman insertion, isotactic/syndiotactic control
  - weave note: The chapter's "Ziegler-Natta achievements made the stereoregular polymerisation of PP possible" (p.74) is the commercial face of PoP Ch.8: stereospecific insertion at a chiral metal centre yields iPP (Tm 165–175 °C, density 0.90–0.91). PoP §6 card 10 adds the QC lever — ¹³C NMR mm/mr/rr to verify isotacticity (>95% for meltblown-grade iPP). A hygiene R&D spec should demand the mm-triad % alongside MFI.
- concept: UV/O₂ autoxidation → chain scission → MW loss
  - related PoP (Principles of Polymerization, Odian 2004) topic: Ch.9 Polymer Reactions — oxidative degradation via hydroperoxides, chain scission / random cleavage, allylic/tertiary H susceptibility
  - weave note: The chapter's 3-step radical scheme (initiation RH+O₂→R•+HOO•; propagation ROO•+RH→R•+ROOH; termination) is exactly PoP Ch.9's hydroperoxide mechanism. PoP §6 card 8 (ageing/shelf-life) notes metal ions (Co/Pb/Mn) catalyse ROOH decomposition and that Irganox 1010 / BHT are the standard primary antioxidants — directly deepens the chapter's p.89 radical-scavenger claim and explains the phenolic-yellowing side effect.
- concept: Hindered-phenol / hindered-amine primary antioxidants + phosphite/thio secondary
  - related PoP (Principles of Polymerization, Odian 2004) topic: Ch.9 Polymer Reactions — stabilisation, 86% maximum conversion ceiling for irreversible polymer reactions (irrelevant here but bounds crosslinking from oxidation)
  - weave note: PoP Ch.9 frames oxidative crosslinking-vs-scission competition; the chapter observes only scission (MW loss, p.88). PoP card 8 qualifies that over-stabilised or UV-exposed films still eventually crosslink/brittle — consistent with the chapter's "totally lost load-bearing capability" after 2-week UV (p.101).
- concept: PP film strain-hardening in tensile (p.94, +25% strength)
  - related PoP (Principles of Polymerization, Odian 2004) topic: Ch.3 free-radical / Ch.8 — melt rheology of α-olefin PP; melt strength for cast/blown film
  - weave note: Strain-hardening (+25%, p.94) reflects oriented amorphous-chain load transfer; PoP §6 card 2 (PE/PP meltblowing) warns iPP meltblowing needs MFI 800–1200 and barrel ≤270 °C to avoid thermal-oxidative degradation — a processing guardrail the chapter's stabiliser discussion implies but does not state.。

### Ch4 — Solubility of Additives in Polymers

> 交叉概要：- concept: Antioxidant solubility, blooming and depletion in polyolefins
  - related PoP (Principles of Polymerization, Odian 2004) topic: Ch.9 Polymer Reactions — oxidative degradation, stabilisation (primary antioxidants BHT / Irganox 1010, hindered phenols & hindered amines), ageing/shelf-life
  - weave note: The chapter's Table 4.1 lists Irganox 1010 (MW 1178) and Tinuvin 770 (HALS) solubilities — exactly the primary/secondary antioxidants PoP §6 card 8 prescribes for PP shelf-life. PoP card 8 adds the why: hydrogen-peroxide radical chain (allylic/tertiary H → OOH → scission or crosslink) and that metal ions catalyse ROOH decomposition. The chapter's blooming result (p.115) is the physical-chemistry reason a formulator must pair PoP's "add Irganox 1010 + UV absorber" advice with a solubility check, or the stabiliser exudes instead of protecting.
- concept: Flory–Huggins χ / regular-solution thermodynamics of additive sorption
  - related PoP (Principles of Polymerization, Odian 2004) topic: Ch.1 Introduction — thermodynamics of mixing (ΔGmix = ΔHmix − TΔSmix; miscibility governed by interaction parameter); relevant to blend/compatibilisation reasoning
  - weave note: Eq. (4.3) χ = V1(δ1−δ2)²/RT is the same interaction parameter that governs monomer/polymer miscibility in PoP Ch.1–Ch.2. PoP does not drill χ numerically, but the chapter's use of δ (solubility parameters) to predict additive sorption is the small-molecule analogue of PoP's polymer-blend miscibility logic — useful when a hygiene formulator blends PP with EVA or TPE and must predict where a stabiliser partitions.
- concept: High-MW additive "apparent solubility increases with MW" (kinetic dissolution)
  - related PoP (Principles of Polymerization, Odian 2004) topic: Ch.9 Polymer Reactions — polymer reactions are diffusion-controlled; rate ∝ 1/particle size
  - weave note: The chapter's time-dependent solubility maximum for HALS 1364–2758 (p.123, "certain 'destruction' of the initial polymer structure") is a diffusion/rearrangement-limited process; PoP Ch.9 notes polymer reactions are diffusion-controlled (rate ∝ 1/particle size), the same slow-relaxation physics that makes high-MW stabiliser uptake kinetic rather than equilibrium.。

### Ch5 — Polyvinyl Chloride: Degradation and Stabilisation

> 交叉概要：- concept: PVC dehydrochlorination / polyene formation (zipper elimination)
  - related PoP (Principles of Polymerization, Odian 2004) topic: Ch.9 Polymer Reactions — dehydrohalogenation / elimination reactions, oxidative degradation, long-range conjugation effects
  - weave note: The chapter's "sequential carbon-carbon double bonds" (p.131) is the polymer-analogous elimination PoP Ch.9 treats as a side reaction of halogenated chains; PoP Ch.9 frames oxidative degradation via hydroperoxides, and the chapter's echo-stabilisation (plasticiser ROOH → RO•+HO•, p.152) is exactly the same peroxy radical chain PoP §6 card 8 warns accelerates in the presence of metal ions. The hygiene takeaway: PoP's "add Irganox 1010 + UV absorber" rule applies to the plasticiser, not just the PVC.
- concept: Oxovinylene as the rate-determining weak link (γ0 ≅ 10⁻⁴, Kp ≅ 10⁻² s⁻¹)
  - related PoP (Principles of Polymerization, Odian 2004) topic: Ch.9 Polymer Reactions — "86% maximum conversion" ceiling and irreversible adjacent-group reactions; local concentration / defect control of polymer reactions
  - weave note: The chapter's "trace defect controls 90% of degradation" is the structural analogue of PoP Ch.9's local-concentration effect (1% PVAc solution ≈ 5× bulk). Both teach that a rare site, not the bulk repeat unit, governs failure — a principle the hygiene formulator should apply to any copolymer impurity (e.g. SAP residual unsaturation, TPE block defects).
- concept: Organotin / Zn / Cd carboxylate HCl-acceptor stabilisers vs toxic-elimination mandate
  - related PoP (Principles of Polymerization, Odian 2004) topic: Ch.9 Polymer Reactions — stabilisation; phenolic/phosphite secondary antioxidants (as in Ch.4 Table 4.1)
  - weave note: The chapter's call to eliminate Ba/Cd/Pb (p.153) dovetails with PoP Ch.9's stabiliser chemistry: replace heavy-metal HCl acceptors with phosphite (secondary antioxidant, Ch.4) trapping of the oxovinylene carbonyl — the same non-toxic, regenerable logic PoP §6 card 8 uses for PP shelf-life.。

## 七、总结（Synthesis）

本书 Handbook of Plastic Films 经深度拆解，在 13/13 个章节中与《Principles of Polymerization》(Odian, 2004) 建立了实质性交叉参考（《塑料薄膜手册》 视角）。核心发现：
1. 最高频交叉概念：老化 / 水解 / 氧化降解、开环聚合 / 可降解、交联 / 凝胶点 —— 这些聚合物科学机理是本书内容在卫材落地的主要支点。
2. 采购与工艺杠杆：SAP（交联聚电解质凝胶 Flory–Rehner/Donnan 渗透压）、PP（等规度→Tm/柔软度，ZN/茂金属催化）、PET（缩聚 Carothers 控制 Xn）、PE（自由基/LDPE 高压工艺）、纤维素（redry hornification）构成卫材基材决策的五大战区。
3. 行动指向：见上方「四、卫材行动卡」与 ../Handbook_of_Plastic_Films_原始引用索引.csv 原始引用索引；需精确公式/原文行号时查同目录 CSV 与《Principles of Polymerization》深度拆解报告。
4. 未关联章节：无 —— 内容为设备/工艺/流体力学等非聚合科学范畴，已标记「无实质关联」不强行交叉。
