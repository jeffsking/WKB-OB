## Module 1：核心论点 (Core Arguments)

### 论点 A — 五机制框架是全书颜色分类基石

"These different ways of producing colour can be grouped into five fundamental mechanisms, as shown in Table I.1."（行 643–646）
"The five groupings can be further split into what Nassau has called 'the fifteen causes of colour'."（行 647–648）

作者明确否定纯"十五因"与纯"技术分类"两法，采用"chromic phenomena 五类色变"作为全书骨架（行 654–661）：
1. Reversible colour change（可逆色变）—— Chapter 1
2. Absorption and reflection of light（光吸收/反射）—— Chapter 2
3. Absorption of energy and emission of light（能量吸收+发光）—— Chapter 3
4. Absorption of light and energy transfer（光吸收+能量传递）—— Chapter 4
5. Manipulation of light（光操纵：液晶/全息/光子晶体）—— Chapter 5

### 论点 B — 色变现象核心："外部刺激 + 可逆"第三参数

"Any changes in the colour of an object... can be easily detected by the human eye... Such changes in colour therefore provide very important visual signals."（行 796–800）
"When a third parameter is introduced, namely an external stimulus... that causes the change of colour... especially when this change is reversible, the potential applications significantly widen."（行 803–808）

核心洞见：颜色的两个传统参数（波长 × 强度）+ 第三参数（可逆外部刺激）= 色变应用爆发性增长的理论基础。

### 论点 C — 四大商业化色变现象

"The most important commercially of these phenomena, namely photochromism, thermochromism, electrochromism and ionochromism, will be covered in some detail in the following sections."（行 817–819）

### 论点 D — Photochromism 五大理想性质

"The ideal properties required from any class of organic photochromic compounds are: 1. Colour development... 2. Control of return back to colourless state... 3. Wide colour range... 4. Long life... 5. Colourless rest state."（行 867–876）

### 论点 E — 光致变色五大化合物家族

"There are five main classes of compounds which can approach these ideal requirements; spiropyrans, spironaphthoxazines, naphthopyrans, fulgides and diarylethenes."（行 878–880）

### 论点 F — 光谱红移（bathochromic）为正向变色常态，可逆性是关键

"Usually the change in colour in the forward direction is to longer wavelength, i.e. bathochromic, and reversibility of this change is key to the many uses of photochromism."（行 849–857）

### 论点 G — Spiropyran 开环机理：C–O 键异裂 → merocyanine

"Irradiation of the colourless spirobenzopyran (1.1) with UV light causes heterolytic cleavage of the carbon–oxygen bond forming the ring-opened coloured species, often called the 'merocyanine' form."（行 887–890）

### 论点 H — 商用量最大仍是经典染料与颜料

"By far the largest commercial application of colour in volume terms still comes via the classical light absorbing dyes and pigments, which are covered in Chapter 2."（行 701–703）

### 论点 I — Thermochromism 分"内在"与"间接"两类

"A technically more appropriate approach is to separate reversible organic thermochromism into 'intrinsic' systems, where heat is the sole cause of the colour change, from 'indirect' systems, in which the colour variation involves changes in the environment around the chromophore brought about by heat."（行 2377–2381）

### 论点 J — 复合热致变色颜料三组分体系

"Composite thermochromic pigments consist of three components; a pH sensitive dye, a proton donor, which acts as the colour developer, and a hydrophobic, non-volatile co-solvent."（行 2619–2621）

### 论点 K — 电致变色双稳态（bistable）

"The cell is bistable; i.e. it remains coloured, even in the absence of applied voltage, until an equal charge is passed in the opposite direction."（行 3544–3547）

延伸关键：氧化钨（WO₃）的电荷注入变色：
> "Pure tungsten trioxide, WO3, is very pale yellow and practically colourless in thin films... The colour changes from blue (x = 0.2), through purple (x = 0.6) and red (x = 0.7) to bronze (x = 0.8–1.0)."（行 3627–3630）

### 论点 L — 溶剂致变色源自基态/激发态偶极矩差

"solvatochromism arises from a difference in dipole moment between the ground and excited states of a molecule"（行 4162–4164）

### 论点 M — Piezo/Tribochromism 商用性极低；Chronochromism 有实际日化应用

"Piezochromism and tribochromism are both colour change phenomena that occur by mechanical means... they have not been exploited in any commercial way."（行 4320–4323）
Chronochromism 牙膏应用（行 4385–4394）：methylene blue + sodium ascorbate 系统。

### 5.4 采购风险 & 合规提示

- BPA 替代：热致变色复合颜料商用核心质子供体是 Bisphenol A（行 2628–2629），目前 FDA/EU 均限制其在婴幼儿用品使用 → 纸尿裤/湿巾功能涂层必须评估BPA-free 替代（如 BPAF、BPAZ、新型 spirolactone）。
- Spirooxazine 温度依赖：商用镜片性能对比表显示 Spiroindolinonaphthoxazines 温度依赖"High"（Table 1.9，行 2170–2175），不宜用于需要宽温区的产品。
- Naphthopyrans 商用首选（行 2167–2168）：温度依赖中等、疲劳性好——纸尿裤湿区温度指示的最优选光致变色体系。
- Fulgides 疲劳性 "Fair"（行 2173）：需要高循环寿命的卫生用品智能层不宜使用 fulgide。
