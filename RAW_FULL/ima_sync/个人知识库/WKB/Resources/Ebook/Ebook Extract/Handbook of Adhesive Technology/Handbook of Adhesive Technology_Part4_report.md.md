# Handbook of Adhesive Technology — Part 4 Report

## Application（应用篇：Chapter 46–50）

Source file: Ebook_Extract/Handbook_of_Adhesive_Technology/chapters/Handbook of Adhesive Technology_Part4.txt
Book: Handbook of Adhesive Technology, A. J. Pizzi & K. Mittal, Marcel Dekker, 2003 (680 pp, 50 ch.)
Part 4 contents: 5 chapters covering adhesives in Electronics (46), Wood (47), Drug Delivery / Bioadhesives (48), Dentistry (49), and Automotive (50).

## 1. Core Arguments / 核心论点

### Chapter 46 – Adhesives in the Electronics Industry（Bauer & Schneider）

- "the application of adhesives in the electronics industry is widespread, the production of printed circuit boards (PCBs) creates a demand for a wide spectrum of properties." — Electronics miniaturization drives a broad spectrum of adhesive requirements. [Line 10-16]
- "the primary fields for application of adhesives in the production of electronic circuits are adhesives for surface-mounted devices (SMDs), binders for laminates and/or adhesives for PCB base material, encapsulating and sealing materials for separate electronic components or complete circuits, and adhesive tapes." [Line 23-28]
- "the thermal expansion coefficients of the adhesives should be in the same range as those of the devices and the PCB." — CTE matching is a primary electronics-adhesive requirement. [Line 45-50]
- "the adhesives have to withstand temperatures of about 260 °C for a short time without remarkable losses in their performance." — Solder-process thermal shock resistance is mandatory. [Line 57-61]
- "Alkaline and chloride ions and residual solvents especially have to be avoided." — Purity dictates reliability of filigree circuitry. [Line 65-67]
- "the most commonly used adhesives in the electronics industry are thermosetting one-component epoxy resins and UV-irradiation cross-linking acrylates." [Line 210-212]
- "Since the metallic particles must touch each other inside the resins to reach a sufficient level of conductivity, a metal content of 70 to 80 wt % is necessary." — Electrical-conducting adhesives need percolating silver fillers. [Line 241-246]
- "The preparation of special adhesives … is one important precondition for the success of SMD techniques … essential to look for new reaction principles for one-component adhesives having higher curing rates at lower temperatures … and to replace the solder process by electrical- and/or thermal-conducting adhesion bondings." [Line 412-419]
- "Harmless technologies and recycling processes for both the adhesives and the entire circuits will have to be developed … nontoxicity, low, or no, volatile-product content during use, suited for repairing and disassembling processes of components and recycling." [Line 426-430]
### Chapter 47 – Adhesives in the Wood Industry（Dunky）

- "What drives this technical development is the search for 'cheaper,' 'faster-curing,' and 'more complex' adhesives." — Three industrial drivers shape wood-adhesive R&D. [Line 471-476]
- "Adhesives play a central role within wood-based panels production. The quality of bonding and hence the properties of the wood-based panels are determined mainly by the type and quality of the adhesives." [Line 476-479]
- "The quality of bonding and hence the properties and performance of the wood-based panels and beams are determined by three main parameters: the wood … the applied adhesive … the working conditions and process parameters." [Line 490-496]
- "In wood adhesives the application parameters other than the characteristics of the adhesive itself account for around 50% of performance." — Process matters as much as chemistry. [Line 504-506]
- "The main challenge has been to reduce the content of formaldehyde in the UF resins and to achieve this without any major changes in the performance of the resins. In theory this is not possible, because formaldehyde is the reactive partner in the reaction of urea and formaldehyde during the condensation reaction as well as curing." [Line 814-818]
- "In UF resins the aminomethylene link is susceptible to hydrolysis and, therefore, it is unstable at higher relative humidity, especially at elevated temperatures." — Explains UF's limited water resistance. [Line 1053-1056]
- "Since phenolic resins for wood bonding harden only thermally, postcuring during hot stacking is very important. … PF-bonded boards should be stacked as hot as possible to guarantee a maximum postcuring effect." [Line 1665-1670]
- "Marcinko et al. … found … that PMDI could penetrate 5–10 times further into wood than PF resins. PMDI not only penetrates the macroscopic hollows of the wood substance, but even penetrates the polymer structure of the wood." [Line 1832-1836]
- "The key to an increased usage of natural products by industry is in the control of the above variables so that the end performance by the industry remains consistent." — Biomass adhesives' industrial barrier is batch consistency. [Line 1992-1994]
- "Just the use of a good adhesive gives no assurance of good bonding in this field. It is the essential interaction of the equally important adhesive and its application technology …" — Wood bonding is an interaction, not a material-selection exercise. [Line 3440-3447]
### Chapter 48 – Bioadhesives in Drug Delivery（Irons & Robinson）

- "Bioadhesives complement drug delivery systems through increased residence time in the various routes of administration. Prolonged contact time can offer very substantial improvements in local drug therapy as well as significant increases in bioavailability for some drugs." [Line 3942-3950]
- "A bioadhesive can be defined as any substance that can adhere to a biological substrate and is capable of being retained on that surface for an extended period of time." [Line 3951-3952]
- "Contact between the adhesive and the mucosal membrane or its coating can be seen as a two-step process, the initial contact between the bioadhesive and substrate and the subsequent formation of bonds between the two surfaces." — Wetting-first-then-bonding paradigm. [Line 3961-3966]
- "The primary adhesive force for most bioadhesives is thought to be hydrogen bonding." [Line 4011]
- "When both toxicity and bioadhesive properties are considered, polyanionic polymers appear to be better bioadhesives than polycations." [Extracted, Ch. 48 §IV, Bioadhesive Polymers]
- "Sufficient hydration of a polymer is also of importance in bioadhesion … If insufficient amounts of solvent are available or hydration is slow, the polymer is not fully hydrated and this limits the flexibility and mobility of the polymer chains, which is crucial to their diffusion and penetration into a substrate." [Extracted, Ch. 48 §Hydration & Interpenetration]
### Chapter 49 – Bonding Materials and Techniques in Dentistry（Neuse & Mizrahi）

- "The success of modern restorative dentistry in the repair, restoration, and replacement of tooth structure is critically dependent on the availability of specialized metallic and non-metallic materials and on procedures for their proficient application." [Line 4620-4630 (Ch. 49 Intro)]
- "With currently available materials, however, the bonding effect is largely or entirely one of micromechanical interlocking …" — Micromechanical interlocking dominates clinical dentistry bonding. [Extracted, Ch. 49 §II]
- "The dentin surface is thus a strongly hydrophilic adherend. The bis-GMA and related resin components of the composite, on the other hand, represent hydrophobic constituents." — Hydrophilic/hydrophobic mismatch is the central bonding problem. [Extracted, Ch. 49 §V.A]
- "Unfilled acrylic resins suffer from considerable volume shrinkage on polymerization (21 to 22%) and consequent microleakage, a high coefficient of thermal expansion (about 10 times greater than observed for tooth substance)." [Extracted, Ch. 49 §IV.B]
- "Although numerous adhesive compositions have been, and continue to be, commercialized, no perfect universal bonding systems have as yet reached the market." [Extracted, Ch. 49 Conclusion]
- "Emphasis in future development work will focus less on the achievement of ever-greater bond strengths than on perfection of adhesion in terms of complete surface wetting, absence of interfacial microleakage … and enhanced durability of both the adhesive interface and the restorative." [Extracted, Ch. 49 §Future]
### Chapter 50 – Adhesives in the Automotive Industry（Cordes）

- "The requirements for adhesive bonds have increased due to the extended life of the car. In adhesive processing, industrial health and environmental protection aspects have become more and more important." [Line 6040-6048]
- "Due to lightweight construction, which will be more and more important, outside panels must be used as supporting parts of the body structure." [Line 6088-6092]
- "Multifunctional adhesives will be welcome: for example, hem flange adhesive bonding and seam sealing with only one material in one procedure." — One-material-one-process convergence. [Extracted, Ch. 50 §Future]
- "Compared to the former glazing technique using rubber seals or polysulfide materials, direct glazing has the following significant advantages: (1) Possibility of completely automatic application, (2) High-performance sealing … (3) Higher body strength, (4) Smoothly designed car bodies, (5) Improved aerodynamics." [Extracted, Ch. 50 §Direct Glazing]
- "The conventional epoxy–resin adhesives give good sheer strength results, but they show brittle fracture under impact loads … Polyurethane compounds are more flexible and tougher, but they give lower strength in adhesive bondings … That requires a toughened adhesive with as high a strength as possible and was the reason for the development of polymer blends." [Extracted, Ch. 50 §Polymer Blends]
- "For ecological and personnel safety reasons, the use of harmful adhesives (e.g., solvent-based cements) will be reduced." — Regulatory trend. [Extracted, Ch. 50 §Future]
- "In hybrid constructions different materials must be bonded. For both techniques adhesive bonding is preferred to welding or soldering. Adhesive bonding can also be combined with two new joining techniques, clinchen and toxen." [Extracted, Ch. 50 §Hybrid]

## 4. Chapter Connections（章节衔接）

### Part 4 在全书中的位置

- Part 1 (Fundamentals of Adhesion Science, Ch. 1–15) provides the theoretical backbone: surface energy, wetting, mechanical/chemical adhesion mechanisms, polymer physics, surface roughness, ageing, testing. Part 4's emphasis on wetting of wood (Ch. 47), bioadhesive substrate wetting (Ch. 48), and dental enamel/dentin wetting (Ch. 49) all directly reuse Part 1's wetting theory (Chapter 3–5).
- Part 2 (Adhesive Chemistry, Ch. 16–30) supplies the chemistry referenced throughout Part 4:

- Epoxy / acrylic / silicone / polyimide chemistry → reused in Ch. 46 (Electronics) [Line 71-90] and Ch. 50 (Automotive, structural polyurethanes/epoxies/acrylics).
- UF / PF / MUF / PMDI condensation-resin chemistry → reused in Ch. 47 (Wood) [Line 683-1793].
- Adhesive-tape & PSA chemistry → reused in Ch. 46 (adhesive tapes for keyboards, covering tapes, supply tapes) [Line 311-347] and Ch. 50 (interior lamination tapes) [Extracted, Ch. 50 Table 3].
- Part 3 (Substrates, Ch. 31–45) covers metals, ceramics, polymers, composites, papers, wood as substrates. Ch. 47's §11 "Wood as an Influential Parameter" [Line 2880-3071] extends Part 3 wood-substrate discussions; Ch. 50 (Automotive) draws on Part 3's metal and polymer-substrate treatments for multi-material hybrid bonding.
### Intra-Part 4 cross-links

- Shared chemistry: Epoxies, acrylates, silicones, polyurethanes, and isocyanates recur across Ch. 46, 47, 49, 50 — but with different formulations and performance targets (high-T solder resistance in electronics vs. low-T hot-melt fast-set in wood vs. moisture-cure for automotive hem flanges vs. acid-etch polymerization in dentistry).
- Shared phenomena:

- Wetting / contact angle: Ch. 46 (adhesive rheology adjustment) [Line 62-64], Ch. 47 (wood surface wetting, contact angles of UF resin on wood, MWBL/CWBL) [Line 2995-3023], Ch. 48 (bioadhesive "wetting" of mucosal surfaces) [Line 3961-3966], Ch. 49 ("complete surface wetting" as future target) [Extracted, Ch.49 §Future].

## 5. Business Connections（业务连接点）

用户背景：卫材（纸尿裤/湿巾）创新研发 + 木浆与纸品战略采购专家，关注芯体结构、SAP、无纺布、胶黏剂、采购成本。

Part 4 与卫材业务有 5 条直接相关通道和 3 条间接相关通道：

### A. 直接相关

-

无纺布 / 非织造材料与胶黏剂（Direct）

- Ch. 46 明确提到 "woven and nonwoven fabrics (cotton, glass)" as carrier materials for adhesive tapes [Line 342-346].
- Ch. 47 § VIII 详述 hot melts（热熔胶）[Line 2281-2397] 与 PVAc 胶乳 [Line 2398-2450] — 这两类正是卫生用品非织造复合、SMS 复合、芯体纤维粘合法最常用的胶种。
- 应用建议：

- 热熔胶体系 (EVA / APAO / polyamide / TPU) [Line 2316-2345]：影响芯体（Top-sheet + SAP + Backsheet 的复合）、侧边胶合、湿巾包装封口。不同聚合物决定耐热性（EVA 低）与粘性（polyamide 高）。
- Curing hot melt (NCO-端基 PU 预聚物) [Line 2361-2397]：对湿巾芯体和湿巾封口尤为相关 — 两步固化（冷却 + 湿气交联）提供抗湿气性能。
- PVAc 乳液胶 [Line 2398-2450]：适用于纸基 / 纤维基非织造材料的常温复合，但需注意 Tg ≈ 28 °C 带来的高温蠕变问题。
-

纤维素 / 纸品化学（Direct）

- Ch. 47 § XI "Wood as an Influential Parameter" 中 § C-D 讨论 cellulose, hemicellulose, lignin, chemical weak boundary layers (CWBL), mechanical weak boundary layers (MWBL) [Line 2963-2993].
- 直接业务链接：木浆（bleached kraft pulp）是非织造干法纸（fluff pulp、creped tissue）的核心原料，也是湿巾/纸尿裤 Top sheet 的关键原料。Ch. 47 中关于 cellulose 表面羟基、lignin 残留、提取物流失 / 蜡质覆盖、pH 缓冲能力 的讨论 [Line 2963-2978] 完全适用于浆粕品质评估、浆粕表面处理（湿法施胶、表面施胶、柔软剂吸附）。
- 季节性 / 原料产地波动（Ch. 47 [Line 3041-3071] "Seasonal Variations of Wood Quality"）：直接映射到浆粕采购中浆种、产地、季节导致的性能波动。
-

湿强树脂 — UF 化学用于纸增强（Direct）

- Ch. 47 明确指出 UF 树脂可用于 "enhance the wet strength of paper" [Line 791-792].
- 业务链接：湿巾基材（creped tissue、towel-type nonwoven）需要湿强度，常用 GA、PAE、MDI、UF/三聚氰胺 类湿强剂。Ch. 47 § IV (UF 化学) 与 § V (PF 化学) 提供湿强机理与固化动力学的教科书级解释。
-

胶黏剂分析 / 质量控制系统（Direct）

- Ch. 47 § X "Analysis of Wood Adhesives Based on Formaldehyde Condensation Resins" [Line 2548-2839] 提供从基础理化测试（固含量、粘度、pH、凝胶时间、pot life）[Table 18] 到 GPC–LALLS 分子量分布 [Line 2700-2772] 的完整方法学。
- 业务链接：浆厂 / 卫材厂对浆粕处理剂和胶粘剂的质量评估体系。
-

汽车 / 包装行业胶黏剂趋势（Direct）

- Ch. 50 讨论 direct glazing, hot melts, two-part acrylics, pressure-sensitive tapes [Extracted, Ch. 50 §Body Shop, §Assembly Shop] — 与卫材产品的包装封合（湿巾软包、纸尿裤腰围胶带）技术同宗。
### B. 间接相关（战略与采购视角）

-

生物基 / 天然胶黏剂（Indirect, 战略前景）

- Ch. 47 § VII "Wood Adhesives Based on Natural Resources" [Line 1974-2119] 涵盖 tannin, lignin, enzymatic laccase 系统 — 与当前非织造 / 湿巾行业"无胶复合 (glue-free bonding)" 和 "bio-based hot melt" 的研发方向高度相关。
- 采购战略信号：关注供应商是否在开发天然基胶黏剂替代石油基 EVA hot melt。
-

生物胶黏剂 / 医药卫生用品交叉（Indirect）

- Ch. 48 涉及 bioadhesives (mucoadhesives) — 主要用于药物传递，但其化学与卫生用品中的皮肤接触胶（nappy adhesive tapes、medipaste） 存在技术共通性。聚氨酯 / PVA / HPMC / PVP / Carbomer 等聚合物是湿巾和卫生用品常用的功能聚合物。
- 湿巾产品若涉及药用、护肤、皮肤贴敷场景，Ch. 48 的"biocompatibility、pKa、网状结构 / mesh size、水分活性"模型有参考价值。
-

甲醛释放法规 (E1) 与 VOC / 气味合规（Indirect）

- Ch. 47 详述 E1 甲醛排放限值 [Line 658-676]，虽主要针对木基人造板，但其中的 法规制定逻辑（室内暴露、婴幼儿产品风险偏好） 直接映射到纸尿裤 / 婴儿湿巾的气味 (odor) 与 VOC 合规要求，是合规战略采购的关键输入。
### C. 与 SAP（高吸收树脂）的关联

- Part 4 未直接讨论 SAP（SAP 属 Part 3 高分子化学范畴或专门的婴儿用品领域）。但存在 3 条间接技术接口：

- Ch. 47 § IX (UF/MUF/F/U 摩尔比 & 交联度) 与 SAP 的交联度 vs 吸收能力 trade-off 在概念上完全对应（降低 F/U → 交联度降低 → 性能下降；对 SAP 则是交联度降低 → 吸收倍率上升但吸收速度下降、凝胶堵塞）。
- Ch. 47 § VII (tannin + formaldehyde crosslinking) 中 formaldehyde 作为 crosslinker 的机制与 SAP 生产中的 crosslinker 化学同宗。
- Ch. 46 (electronics encapsulants) 与 Ch. 49 (dental resins) 中关于crosslinked hydrogel 网状结构 / mesh size 与性能关系的讨论（Ch. 48: "tensile strength of a mucoadhesive is directly proportional to the mesh size of the polymer network"）与 SAP 的凝胶网络动力学直接对应。
### D. 采购与成本策略要点

- 木浆 / 浆粕：Ch. 47 § XI (wood species variation, seasonal effects, MWBL/CWBL) → 用于与浆厂谈判时的质量索赔技术依据（浆种波动、含水率、pH、灰分）。
- 胶黏剂选型：

- 热熔胶（EVA/APAO/polyamide/TPU/curing hot melt） → 复合、封合、腰围胶合（成本 + 速度 + 耐温匹配）。
- UV-curable acrylates (Ch. 46) → 用于印刷标签、湿巾图案烫印、透明封口。
- PSA tapes / adhesive tapes (Ch. 46 § VI [Line 311-347] + Ch. 50 § Assembly) → 用于湿巾包装背胶条、湿巾卷芯背胶。
- 合规：甲醛 (E1) 与 VOC 排放框架 → 制定卫材产品原材料环保标准。
- 供应商评估：利用 Ch. 47 § X 的分析工具箱建立对胶粘剂供应商的理化参数 SLA（粘度、固含量、pH、凝胶时间、分子量分布）。
