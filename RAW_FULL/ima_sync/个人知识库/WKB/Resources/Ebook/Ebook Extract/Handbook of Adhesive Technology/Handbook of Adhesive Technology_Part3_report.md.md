# Handbook of Adhesive Technology – Part 3 深度拆书报告

## Part 3: Adhesive Classes（Chapter 20–45）

书名: Handbook of Adhesive Technology
作者: Anthony J. Pizzi & K. Mittal (Marcel Dekker, 2003, 680页, 50章/4Part)
Part 3 范围: Chapter 20–45，涵盖从天然蛋白胶到压敏胶及导电胶的全谱系胶黏剂化学与工艺

## 1. Core Arguments（核心论点）

### CA-1: 胶黏剂技术发展的基本驱动力量

"The dwindling availability of fossil reserves constitutes a driving force towards finding alternative resources which can substitute them, totally or partially, in order to prepare chemicals and materials that are normally produced from petroleum and coal." [Line 7002–7006]

"In this synthetic resin expansion, however, protein adhesives held onto their 'interior durable' product applications, due in large part to their unique combination of low cost and cold-curing capability or, alternatively, very fast hot press curing (about twice as fast as phenolics)." [Line 49–56]

### CA-2: 水性体系是环保转型的必然方向

"The use of waterborne polyurethane adhesives has grown in recent years as they have replaced solvent-based adhesives in a number of application areas... Because waterborne polyurethane adhesives have no VOC (volatile organic content) emissions and are nonflammable, they are more environmentally friendly." [Line 11056–11061]

### CA-3: 胶黏剂性能的根本取决于分子结构与交联机制

"For a given polyester formulation, the properties of the final compound are a function of its condensation (e.g., carboxyl and hydroxyl group concentration), viscosity and molecular weight distribution, and the structural features of the three-dimensional network obtained after free-radical copolymerization." [Line 12052–12055]

### CA-4: 表干/接触强度（Green Strength）是工艺可行性的关键

"Superior green strength: Applying a thin coat of either water-based or solvent-based adhesive onto each of the substrates to be bonded, drying to a tacky-to-the-touch condition, and mating the surfaces with moderate pressure provides excellent contact bond strength." [Line 3015–3019]

### CA-5: 甲醛释放问题正在推动替代胶黏剂技术发展

"Higher-molar-ratio resins tend to exhibit an overall better initial bond quality, but present an exponentially increased formaldehyde emission problem, most often disqualifying them from many, or most, modern uses." [Line 8011–8012]

"While pMDI wood binders are derived from formaldehyde, they are distinguished from other wood binders by a total lack of formaldehyde emission." [Line 10016–10018]

### CA-6: 压敏胶的核心机理是粘弹性而非简单的双相模型

"Tack is also believed to stem from the viscoelasticity of many polymers, allowing them to conform to the substrate to be adhered and 'wet' it even in the dry state. It follows that a fundamental requirement for tack is a glass transition temperature substantially below the application temperature to permit the necessary degree of flow." [Line 16064–16068]

### CA-7: 胶黏剂与基材的界面相互作用决定最终性能

"The primary goal of surface treatment is to remove any weak surface boundary layer on the substrate." [Line 11005–11010]

### CA-8: 天然可再生资源胶黏剂的未来取决于化学改性

"However, because the bonds formed by carbohydrate polymer adhesives are generally sensitive to water, future applications of these adhesives will increasingly depend on modifying the natural polymer to give components that can undergo further cross-linking to form water-insensitive bonds." [Line 2551–2554]

### CA-9: 热固性酚醛树脂的核心价值在于水、耐候、高温抗性的低成本实现

"The characteristic that renders these resins invaluable as adhesives is their capability to deliver water, weather, and high-temperature resistance to the cured glue line of the joint bonded with phenolic adhesives, at relatively low cost." [Line 4022–4025]

### CA-10: 压敏胶已成为最大且增长最快的胶黏剂细分市场

"Pressure-sensitive adhesives grew from 38% of total adhesive sales in the United States in 1980 to 44.6% in 1988, at an annual rate of 12%, to reach a sales value of $4.9 billion in 1989." [Line 16050–16052]

## 2. Key Concepts with Operationalized Definitions（关键概念操作化定义）

### KC-1: 蛋白胶（Protein Adhesives）

- 英文原文定义: "Human beings have apparently had a propensity for gluing things together since the dawn of recorded history and probably before. By experimentation, we learned over time that certain natural products or extractives could develop bonds in wood of sufficient strength to break in the material being glued rather than in the glue film itself." [Line 10–14]
- 出现行号范围: Line 1–1100
- 使用场景: "In this chapter we address three of the most widely used families of protein-based adhesives for wood: soybean, blood, and casein. The technology presented is drawn primarily from the years 1930 through about 1960, when the consumption and technical refinements of these adhesives were at their peak." [Line 69–74]
### KC-2: 接触强度（Green Strength）

- 英文原文定义: "Superior green strength: Applying a thin coat of either water-based or solvent-based adhesive onto each of the substrates to be bonded, drying to a tacky-to-the-touch condition, and mating the surfaces with moderate pressure provides excellent contact bond strength." [Line 3015–3019]
- 出现行号范围: Line 3015–3019
- 使用场景: 用于描述氯丁橡胶（polychloroprene）压敏胶的核心性能优势，即无需完全固化即可在接触时形成有效粘接。
### KC-3: 时间-温度-转变图（TTT Diagram）

- 英文原文定义: "The use of TMA techniques has allowed for the first time the determination of time–temperature-transformation (TTT) and continuous-heating-transformation (CHT) diagrams for UF and PF adhesives... and allowed the quantification of the wood/adhesive interface characteristics and parameter limits." [Line 5023–5027]
- 出现行号范围: Line 5021–5087
- 使用场景: 用于描述水性甲醛基树脂在木材基材上固化的动力学行为，揭示在200°C以上温度区间的凝胶和玻璃化转变曲线趋势。
### KC-4: 热固树脂（Thermoset）

- 英文原文定义: "These early resins were slow to cure and somewhat difficult to use, but they ushered in the era of 'thermoset' polymeric adhesives and true exterior durability." [Line 45–46]
- 出现行号范围: Line 44–46
- 使用场景: 用于描述酚醛和脲醛树脂的出现标志着真正热固性聚合胶黏剂的诞生，实现了真正的户外耐久性。
### KC-5: 压敏胶/压敏粘接（Pressure-Sensitive Adhesive, PSA）

- 英文原文定义: "Pressure-sensitive or permanent-tack adhesives are, as their name implies, adhesives that remain sticky even when dried or cured. This means that they are capable of bonding to surfaces simply by the application of light pressure." [Line 16044–16047]
- 出现行号范围: Line 16035–17600+
- 使用场景: 全书最大章节之一，系统论述水性、溶剂型和热熔型三大压敏胶体系的化学、工艺与应用。
### KC-6: 水性聚氨酯（Waterborne Polyurethane）

- 英文原文定义: "The use of waterborne polyurethane adhesives has grown in recent years as they have replaced solvent-based adhesives in a number of application areas... Because waterborne polyurethane adhesives have no VOC emissions and are nonflammable, they are more environmentally friendly." [Line 11056–11061]
- 出现行号范围: Line 11056–11087
- 使用场景: 作为溶剂型聚氨酯的环保替代方案，应用于鞋材、包装层压、纺织层压及刨花板。
### KC-7: 异氰酸酯木材胶黏剂（Isocyanate Wood Binders / pMDI）

- 英文原文定义: "While pMDI wood binders are derived from formaldehyde, they are distinguished from other wood binders by a total lack of formaldehyde emission. This performance advantage has not displaced the less expensive UF and melamine–urea–formaldehyde (MUF) adhesives." [Line 10016–10020]
- 出现行号范围: Line 9996–10130
- 使用场景: 用于OSB（定向刨花板）制造，替代PF树脂在细刨片高密度压实场景下的应用，提供无甲醛释放的外用级性能。
### KC-8: 聚氨酯胶粘剂（Polyurethane Adhesives）

- 英文原文定义: "The oldest types of one-component polyurethane adhesives were based on di- or triisocyanates that cured by reacting with active hydrogens on the surface of the substrate or moisture present in the air or substrate. The moisture reacts with the isocyanate groups to form urea and biuret linkages, building molecular weight, strength, and adhesive properties." [Line 11037–11041]
- 出现行号范围: Line 11003–11130
- 使用场景: 分为单组分（湿固化、热熔预聚物）和双组分（羟基固化、异氰酸酯固化），应用于软泡重组、轮胎帘线、家具和鞋材。
### KC-9: 不饱和聚酯树脂（Unsaturated Polyester Resins）

- 英文原文定义: "One of the main uses of polyester resin is to function as the adhesive for glass-fiber lamination. The cross-linking reaction of unsaturated polyesters is exothermic; that is, it is accompanied by a rise in temperature." [Line 12080–12084]
- 出现行号范围: Line 11800–12090
- 使用场景: 用作玻璃钢（GFRP）层压胶黏剂，通过自由基引发与乙烯基单体（如苯乙烯）共聚交联。
### KC-10: 厌氧胶（Anaerobic Adhesives）

- 英文原文定义: "Anaerobic adhesives are mixtures of acrylic esters that remain liquid when exposed to air but harden when confined between metal surfaces." [Line 13065–13066]
- 出现行号范围: Line 13056–13200
- 使用场景: 用于锁固螺纹紧固件、密封管道连接、固定圆柱形机器部件等。其固化机制为金属离子引发的自由基聚合，氧气抑制聚合。
### KC-11: 聚氨酯/酚醛混合胶黏剂（PU/PF Hybrid Adhesives）

- 英文原文定义: "The formation of urethane bridges derived by the reaction of the isocyanate group with the methylol group of the PF resin, which is perhaps one of the two dominant reactions introducing better dissipation of energy at the interface and hence contributing to strength improvement." [Line 5003–5006]
- 出现行号范围: Line 5000–5018
- 使用场景: 聚氨酯改性的酚醛树脂胶黏剂在木材粘接中通过异氰酸酯基团与羟甲基反应形成氨基甲酸酯桥，提升界面能量耗散和强度。
### KC-12: 导电胶黏剂（Conductive Adhesives）

- 英文原文定义: "Most commercially available anisotropically conductive adhesives are formulated on the bridging concept... A concentration of conductive particles far below the percolation threshold is dispersed in an adhesive... When a device is attached to a PWB, the placement force displaces the adhesive composite such that a layer the thickness of a single particle remains. Individual particles span the gap between device and PWB and form an electrical interconnection." [Line 17059–17068]
- 出现行号范围: Line 16950–17550
- 使用场景: 用于电子封装中的芯片贴装（die attach）和互连（interconnection），替代传统焊接工艺。

## 4. Chapter Connections（章节衔接）

### 4.1 Part 3 与 Part 1 的逻辑关系（前承）

- Part 1 (Adhesion and Surface Science) 提供了粘附理论、表面能与表面处理的物理化学基础，Part 3 的 Chapter "Surface Preparation and Primers" [Line 11003–11030] 直接引用了 Part 1 中的表面能概念和等离子处理、火焰处理等技术，说明"removing weak boundary layer"是获得可靠粘接的先决条件。
- Part 1 中的界面热力学 为 Part 3 中讨论各种胶黏剂的润湿行为和表面张力匹配提供了理论基础。
### 4.2 Part 3 与 Part 2 的逻辑关系（前承）

- Part 2 (Adhesive Formulation & Application) 讨论了胶黏剂的基础配方原理和应用工程，Part 3 则在此基础上逐一展开各类胶黏剂的具体化学、配方、工艺和应用实例。例如 Part 2 中的流变学原理直接应用于 Part 3 中PVA/EVA乳液的环管反应器设计 [Line 11500–11528]。
- Part 2 中的固化动力学框架（如玻璃化转变、扩散控制）被 Part 3 的 TTT 图讨论深化和扩展 [Line 5021–5087]。
### 4.3 Part 3 与 Part 4 的逻辑关系（后启）

- Part 4 (Testing and Characterization) 将测试方法标准化，Part 3 中提到的 lap shear strength [Line 3023–3024]、peel strength [Line 3506]、TMA分析 [Line 5023] 等在 Part 4 中被标准化为测试规程。
- Part 3 中讨论的各种胶黏剂类型在 Part 4 中将对应具体的测试标准和性能评估方法。
### 4.4 Part 3 内部章节间交叉引用

- 蛋白胶 (Chapter ~20) → 引出淀粉/纤维素胶 (Chapter ~21) [Line 1900–2500]：从蛋白质基扩展到碳水化合物基，逻辑一致。
- 酚醛树脂 (Chapter ~24) [Line 4000–4300] → 异氰酸酯木材胶 (Chapter ~27) [Line 9996–10130]：pMDI与PF在OSB领域的竞争直接对比 [Line 10036–10046]。
- UF/MUF (Chapter ~26) [Line 8000–9300] → pMDI (Chapter ~27)：甲醛释放问题的解决方案对比 [Line 10016–10019]。
- 聚氨酯 (Chapter ~28) [Line 11003–11130] → 有机硅 (Chapter ~31) [Line 14700–15400]：均涉及湿气固化机理，但化学基础完全不同。
- 丙烯酸 (Chapter ~30) [Line 11700–13100] → 厌氧胶 (Chapter ~38) [Line 13056–13200]：厌氧胶本质上是丙烯酸酯类，共享自由基聚合机理。
- 压敏胶 (Chapter ~44) [Line 16035–17600]：汇总了水性、溶剂型和热熔型三大PSA体系，是全书最大且最综合的章节。
## 5. Business Connections（业务连接点）

### 5.1 卫材（纸尿裤/湿巾）胶黏剂应用全景

Part 3 涵盖了卫材行业可能涉及的几乎所有胶黏剂类别。以下逐一分析其在卫材生产中的实际应用价值：

#### (a) 热熔胶（Hot Melt Adhesives）—— 卫材最核心胶黏剂

- 书中对应章节: 压敏胶章节（Chapter ~44）中热熔胶部分 [Line 16069–16075]，"Pressure-sensitive adhesives fall into three broad product categories: water based, solvent based, and hot melt." [Line 16069–16070]
- 书中数据: 聚烯烃/EVA市场 $1,290 million，是第二大胶黏剂市场 [Line 3526–3528]
- 卫材应用:

- 芯体与底膜复合: EVA或PO热熔胶用于无纺布与PE底膜的热压复合
- 耳贴（Tape/Elastic attachment）: 热熔胶点在弹性腰贴和侧贴固定中大量使用
- 湿巾封口: 热熔胶用于湿巾包装的密封
- 采购策略: 重点关注 EVA（乙烯-醋酸乙烯共聚物）、PO（聚烯烃）热熔胶的 Tg、熔点、开放时间参数；从书中可知"Acrylic dispersions, either with or without additional tackifiers, are the most widely used adhesives"用于标签，类似地热熔胶的选择需考虑基材兼容性 [Line 16525–16526]
#### (b) 水性丙烯酸胶（Water-based Acrylic Adhesives）

- 书中对应: 丙烯酸胶章节 [Line 11700–13050]，水性聚氨酯章节 [Line 11056–11087]
- 关键引述: "Because waterborne polyurethane adhesives have no VOC emissions and are nonflammable, they are more environmentally friendly." [Line 11060–11061]
- 卫材应用:

- 表层复合: 水性丙烯酸乳液用于SMS无纺布的表层-芯层复合
- 湿巾布面粘接: 水性丙烯酸用于湿巾的无纺布-水刺无纺布复合
- 纸尿裤表层亲水处理: 水性胶作为亲水整理剂的载体
- 采购策略: VOC法规驱动水性化趋势；关注固含量、干燥温度、开放时间。书中提到水性聚氨酯可"blended with other dispersions without problems" [Line 11062]
#### (c) 水性聚氨酯（Waterborne PU）

- 书中对应: Polyurethane Adhesives 章节 [Line 11003–11087]
