# 《Absorbent Technology》第10章深读报告

## 章名: Nonwovens in Absorbent Materials（无纺布在吸液材料中的应用）

原著: P.K. Chatterjee & B.S. Gupta 编, Absorbent Technology, Elsevier Science B.V., 2002
章作者: Bhupender S. Gupta (NCSU College of Textiles) 与 D.K. Smith (Smith, Johnson & Associates)
页码: pp. 366–405(书内页 350–387,约40页)
读者: 王总（上海,吸液产品R&D与战略采购）

## 1. Executive Summary（执行摘要）

- 无纺布(nonwoven)之所以成为吸液产品的主导材料,源于两项独特属性——"high bulk for imbibing and holding large amount of fluid per unit mass" 以及"low cost of converting raw material into final product",并兼具一次性(disposability)、舒适(comfort)与易加工(ease of fabrication)等优势,是Ch3(毛细管机制)和Ch6(纤维)工程化的"落地载体"。
- 全章的核心骨架是四大结构要素(four major structural elements):纤维(Fiber)、纤网排列(Web Assemblage)、纤网粘合(Bonding)、成品整理(Finish)。作者强调这四项因素"Each of these factors can have an effect on the absorbency of the resulting structure",对采购而言意味着必须按要素分层评价供应商,而不是单一价格比较。
- 工艺层面,章中系统梳理了12种无纺布成形工艺(Uni-Directional Dry Form、Bonding of Dry Form Web、Airlaid、Airlaid Pulp、Wetlaid、Spunbond、Needlepunch、Spunlace、Meltblown、Laminate、Polymer Web、Advanced Composites),并将每种工艺与吸液性能建立因果链:空气铺设(airlaid)→高蓬松高吸液量;水刺(spunlace)→柔软亲肤面材;SMS/SMMS复合→强韧面材与阻隔。
- 产品层面,作者对五类吸液制品(婴儿纸尿裤、女性卫生巾、护理垫 Underpad、成人失禁垫、伤口敷料、擦拭布、油污吸收剂)逐一拆解,给出"premium diaper"典型结构(Fig.12)——Nonwoven Top Sheet / ADL / Fluff Pulp + SAP / Barrier Sheet / Stand-up Leg Cuff——为Ch11 性能测试和Ch12 产品整合提供了结构参照。
- 数据层面,章内三张核心表格给出了1975–2000年无纺布与吸液产品全球销售额、北美合成纤维发货量、以及SMS/SS复合材料性能对照,为战略采购的"量-价-性能"谈判提供了历史坐标。
## 2. Core Arguments（核心论点）

Two-Property Thesis(双属性论): 作者开篇即定义无纺布适合作为吸液材料的两个根本原因——"Two characteristics of nonwovens make them particularly suitable for use in an absorbent structure: high bulk for imbibing and holding large amount of fluid per unit mass of material and low cost of converting raw material into final product." 高蓬松(high bulk,Ch3对应毛细管储液)与低成本低成本低加工成本(low cost of converting)构成了后续一切工艺-产品选择的底层约束。

Four-Element Architecture(四要素架构): "In discussing nonwovens, consideration must be given to the interrelationships of the four major structural elements that influence absorbency: Constituent fiber; Assembly structure; Type of bonding; Finish." 四要素(纤维 → 排列 → 粘合 → 整理)构成因果链,而非平行关系——采购评审必须按此顺序追问而非只看价格。

Fiber Absorbency Mechanism Duality(纤维吸液机制二元性): 亲水纤维与疏水纤维遵循两条不同吸液路径——"A hydrophilic, swellable fiber gives a structure that is able to absorb liquid via fiber imbibition, giving rise to fiber swelling... A hydrophobic fiber, on the other hand, has only the latter mechanism available to it normally." 前者通过纤维溶胀(fiber swelling)+毛细管储液;后者仅靠毛细管(capillary and structure voids)。这一论点直接决定面材(topsheet)与芯体(core body)选材策略的不同。

Bonding Trade-off Thesis(粘合-吸液权衡论): "Because most binder materials are hydrophobic, the net effect usually is some reduction in absorbency." 化学粘合牺牲吸液,机械粘合(needle felting)保持纤维本征吸液但改变结构弹性——"the entanglement may restrict the inherent ability of structure to swell" 且 "the fabric may become more resilient and resist collapse when subjected to external pressure"。这是面材/芯体粘合方式选择的核心权衡。

Airform Bulk Advantage(空气铺设蓬松优势): "The random orientation of the fibers in the structure with some tendency of fibers to be lifted out of the X-Y plane and become partially oriented in the Z direction results in a web with higher bulk and larger void volume." airlaid工艺因Z向排列获得更高蓬松和更大空隙体积,直接对应core body的高吸液量需求。

SMS Composite Thesis(SMS复合论): "Such a composite leads to a unique structure, claimed to provide desirable performance at lower basis weight and cost." 熔喷(meltblown)提供阻隔与均匀性,纺粘(spunbond)提供强韧,组合(SMS/SMMS)在10–25 g/m²的低克重下获得"high strike through with effective retention of superabsorbent particles (SAP) smaller than 100 microns"。这是Ch6纤维工程化在Ch10的结构化表达。

ADL Density-Gradient Thesis(ADL密度梯度论): ADL通过沿厚度方向的密度梯度实现"acquisition + distribution"双功能——"The top one-third... has low density... especially effective in the acquisition of the presented liquid... The higher density portions... develop greater capillary pressures"——这是Ch3毛细管压力原理的工程化产品落地。

Paradigm Shift(范式转移论): 作者反复强调工艺革新带来产品结构换代——"In a dynamic field of product development such as the absorbent sanitary materials, new technical innovations can result in a paradigm shift, giving rise to the use of a different, advanced technology." 从woven cotton到spunbond PP、从fluff pulp现场粉碎到preformed airlaid pulp fabric,采购需识别这些转折点。

Synergistic Integration Thesis(协同整合论): "the challenge in the designing of an efficient absorbent product is to organize the structural elements into an integrated whole that benefits from the synergistic interaction between the parts." 单一材料最优无意义,必须作为整体系统优化——这是Ch12(产品整合)的预告。

Cross-Industry Genesis(跨行业起源论): "nonwoven technology has had its genesis in three originating technologies/industries: textile, paper and polymer processing." 这一认知意味着吸液产品非织造供应链同时来自纺织、造纸、聚合物三大行业,采购需跨行业比价与整合。

## 3. Key Concepts & Operational Definitions（关键概念与操作化定义）

|||||
|---|---|---|---|
|Nonwoven|无纺布|非针织、非机织、非编织、非纸的,基于纤维或微纤维的纤网(fiber/fibril-based web),关键判别是"absence of a yarn element"|"the latter continues to be used worldwide to represent a broad range of materials that are not knitted, woven or braided, or classified as paper, but are webs based on fibers or fibrils" (p.350)|
|Web Assemblage / Fiberweb|纤网排列 / 纤网结构|纤维在纤网中的空间排列状态,决定取向度(ordered↔random),影响孔隙尺寸、毛细管方向、各向同性/异性|"the manner in which the fibers are assembled into a web has profound effect on the web properties" (p.355)|
|Web Formation|成网|纤维被布置/沉积为连续纤网的工艺过程,方法包括卡网、气铺、湿铺、纺粘、熔喷等|"Various types of web formers have been developed that allow air forming of webs of different materials and of a broad range of specifications" (p.360)|
|Basis Weight|克重(单位面积质量)|单位面积无纺布质量,g/m²,是SMS/SS等复合材料的成本与性能关键参数|"The most popular combination structure is SMS... in weight ranges from 10–25 g/m²" (p.378)|
|Permeability (Air/Liquid)|透气/透水率|空气或液体通过非织造材料的速率,表征纤维排列密度与毛细管结构,影响击穿时间(strike-through)与干爽性|"The differences are seen in the values of tensile properties, air permeability and pore size" (p.379)|
|Airlaid (Air Form)|空气铺设|利用气流悬浮纤维并在多孔带/网带上游离沉积,获得Z向取向、高蓬松、高孔隙体积的纤网|"The random orientation... with some tendency of fibers to be lifted out of the X-Y plane and become partially oriented in the Z direction results in a web with higher bulk and larger void volume" (p.360)|
|Airlaid Pulp|气铺木浆|airlaid工艺的专用形态,使用锤磨机(hammermill)将木浆片解纤至单纤维,气悬浮后气铺成网,乳胶喷胶粘合|"the air form or the airlaid pulp process, involves generation of an air stream filled with individualized pulp fibers and collecting them on a moving belt" (p.360)|
|Spunlace (Hydroentanglement)|水刺|高压水射流冲击纤网使纤维缠结(entangle)成布的机械粘合工艺,无化学粘合剂,可双面加工|"The system uses high energy, closely spaced, water jets that emerge from an injector and impinge on a web substrate and entangle loose arrays of fibers" (p.364)|
|Thermal Bonding|热粘合|利用热轧或热风使低熔点(thermal fusible)纤维熔融粘合纤维间接触点的工艺,可保留大部分空隙|"thermal fusible fibers are used in some airlaid nonwovens in order to bind the pulp web" (p.361)|
|Chemical Bonding|化学粘合|施加水基胶乳/树脂 binder(如latex spray/print/saturation)后烘干固化,使纤维粘结成布——多数binder疏水,会削弱吸液|"Because most binder materials are hydrophobic, the net effect usually is some reduction in absorbency" (p.355)|
|Needle Punching (Needlefelt)|刺针/针刺|通过刺针穿入纤网使纤维三维缠结的机械粘合,无需化学粘合,产生三维毛细管网络|"the needlepunching process itself results in consolidation of the fibrous web by creating an interconnected network of capillaries in three dimensions" (p.363)|
|High Bulk Nonwoven|高蓬松非织造|具有大空隙体积(large void volume)与非织造材料,通常由气铺工艺获得,Z向排列比例较高|"a web with higher bulk and larger void volume" (p.360)|
|Fiber Imbibition|纤维吸液|亲水纤维通过纤维本身吸水并膨胀(fiber swelling)吸收液体的机制|"A hydrophilic, swellable fiber gives a structure that is able to absorb liquid via fiber imbibition, giving rise to fiber swelling" (p.354)|
|Acquisition / Distribution Layer (ADL)|获取/分布层|位于面材与芯体间的非织造复合层,具有沿厚度方向的密度梯度——上薄下密——同时承担快速获取与横向分布液体|"The ADL generally takes the form of a composite fabric with a density gradient through the thickness" (p.380)|
|Superabsorbent Polymer (SAP)|高吸水性聚合物|遇液溶胀成凝胶(gelled mass)的聚合物粉末,单位质量吸水能力强,但凝胶化后堵塞毛细管,需位置优化|"the wetted, swollen, superabsorbent material is a gelled mass that would tend to block the capillaries and retard the progress of liquid" (p.382)|
|Strike-through|击穿时间|液体从面材顶部渗透至背面的时间(秒),表征面材导液速率|"'strike-through' (time in seconds for liquid to penetrate topsheet)" (p.380)|
|Wet-back / Rewet|反渗|在压力下液体从芯体返回面材的量,表征干爽性|"'wet-back' (volume of liquid squeezed out of an absorbent and back through the facing under standard conditions)" (p.380)|
|SMS / SMMS / SSMMS|纺粘-熔喷-纺粘复合|由纺粘(spunbond,SB)层和熔喷(meltblown,MB)微纤层交替叠合、热轧粘合成型的多层复合无纺布|"The most popular combination structure is SMS (or SMMS, SSMMS)... comprising 1–5 g/m² meltblown microfibers" (p.378)|
|Spunbond|纺粘|从聚合物熔体通过大牵伸直接纺出连续长丝并在气流中铺网、热轧/水刺成布的工艺|"Commercial spunbond lines are also providing polypropylene/polyethylene bicomponent material for diaper top sheet... denier lying in the 1.0–1.2 range for diaper use" (p.371)|
|Meltblown|熔喷|由微纤级(micro-denier)热塑性纤维组成的高密度、低强度随机纤网,自粘性+热粘合,常作复合夹层|"the meltblown process... produces a random web derived from micro-denier thermoplastic fiber, usually polypropylene" (p.378)|

- 行业增长预测: "the growth in the nonwoven sector, considering all materials, has been between 5–10% during the past decade and this trend is expected to be continued" (p.350)

### 6.2 ES vs Airlaid 对比(战略采购决策核心)

||||
|---|---|---|
|工艺起源|纺织(卡网)|造纸+气固两相流|
|纤维排列|高度取向(MD强、CD弱)|随机+Z向倾向(各向同性)|
|蓬松度|中等|高(higher bulk)|
|空隙体积|较小|大(larger void volume)|
|主要粘合|化学胶乳喷印|化学胶乳(13–25% wt)+可选热粘合纤维|
|吸液机制|毛细管+纤维溶胀|大空隙储液为主|
|典型应用|早期underpad顶面、擦拭布|纸尿裤/成人失禁Core Body|
|趋势|被PP spunbond替代(表2数据支持)|持续扩张,preformed airlaid fabric成趋势|

采购含义: 面材从rayon干法转向PP spunbond是不可逆趋势;芯体从现场粉碎fluff pulp转向preformed airlaid pulp fabric是"下一个范式转移"——"preformed airlaid pulp fabric can be supplied and used on the converting line directly, eliminating the large, noisy, and dusty pulp sheet grinding operation"。虽原材料单价略高,但"简化集成后的整机运营成本可抵消"(simplified, integrated converting operations make the change attractive)。

### 6.3 供应商评估框架(源自"四要素架构")

采购评审应按Ch10四要素分层打分:

||||
|---|---|---|
|Fiber(纤维)|纤维类型(PP/PE/棉/浆粕)、denier、截面形状、亲水处理剂|纤维TDS、截面SEM|

战略谈判要点(来自Table 4):
- 降克重策略:13 g/m² SMMS ≈ 15 g/m² SS 拉伸强度,但SMS在阻隔/SAP控制上显著更优 → "以SMMS替换SS"在维持同等功能下实现降本
- 粘合剂成本:airlaid pulp中binder 13–25% wt → 粘合剂成本占airlaid采购价约15–25%,是议价抓手
- 原料替代:PP从13→458百万磅 → PP供应端议价空间已成熟,但需关注原油价格传导

### 6.4 未来技术跟踪点(源自第5节 "Potential Future Developments")

- Preformed airlaid pulp fabric 取代现场粉碎木浆——供应链从"纸厂→粉碎机→整机厂"缩短为"纸厂→整机厂",需关注airlaid成品布厂商布局
- Multi-bonded airlaid 一体化产线——可在同一条气铺线上嵌入SAP、多层成型、完成芯体制造,直接改变整机厂投资结构与供应商格局
- Cotton-surfaced / Cotton-centered composites——将棉纤维与spunbond/meltblown在线复合,兼顾柔软亲肤(棉)与强韧(SB/MB),是"棉质感知+工业化"的新方向
- SMS多层复合(4–5层)——SSMMS进一步降低MB比例、优化成本/性能比,需跟踪SMMS/SSMMS的技术迭代
