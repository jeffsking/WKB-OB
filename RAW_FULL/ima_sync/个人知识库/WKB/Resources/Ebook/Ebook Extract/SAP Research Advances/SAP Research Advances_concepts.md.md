# SAP Research Advances — 核心概念词典

来源: Yang, Y. et al. Research Advances in Superabsorbent Polymers. Polymers 2024, 16, 501.
规则: 概念术语保留英文原文 + 中文释义；定义与使用场景的原文引用不翻译。

## 1. Superabsorbent polymer (SAP, 超吸水性聚合物)

操作性定义："Superabsorbent polymers are three-dimensional organic materials with moderately crosslinked structures that swell in aqueous solutions, absorb more than 1000 times their dry weight in liquids, and do not release absorbed liquid under pressure."（Page 2）

使用场景（原文）：
- Abstract (Page 1): "Superabsorbent polymers are new functional polymeric materials that can absorb and retain liquids thousands of times their masses."
- Introduction (Page 1): "In 1978, superabsorbent polymers were commercially produced in Japan for use in feminine products."
- Introduction (Page 1): "The resulting product was utilized in baby diapers, which increased their absorbency and minimized the quantity of superabsorbent polymers required."
- Section 2.1.3 (Page 7): "PVA is not as absorbent as PAA-based superabsorbent polymers but is biodegradable."
- Section 3 (Page 11): "Superabsorbent polymer particles are the most common and widely used form."
- Conclusions (Page 20): "Superabsorbent polymers have superabsorption abilities and specific retention ability for water or other body fluids."

## 2. Polyacrylic acid (PAA, 聚丙烯酸)

操作性定义：通过丙烯酸钾与丙烯酰胺在引发剂（K₂S₂O₈）和交联剂（MBA）作用下聚合交联成网络结构的高分子量 SAP 主链。"The molecular weight of the crosslinked PAA main chain can reach 10⁷ g/mol, and the content of water-absorbing groups is much higher than that of other polymer materials, such as polyvinyl alcohol and modified polyacrylonitrile, which provides a very high water-absorbing and long-lasting water-retaining capacity."（Page 5）

使用场景（原文）：
- Page 5: "PAA is the most commonly used superabsorbent."
- Page 5: "Acrylics dominate the superabsorbent market owing to their relatively inexpensive raw materials and ease of synthesis from high-molecular-weight products."
- Page 6: "The water absorption rate of the superabsorbent polymers tended to increase and then decrease with increasing crosslinker content."
- Page 7: "PAA-based superabsorbent polymers can agglomerate after absorbing water, resulting in hindered water absorption."
- Page 13: PAA/PVA 复合水凝胶、淀粉接枝 PAA 复合凝胶（772.4 g/g）。
- Page 20: "PAA polymers are the most common synthetic superabsorbent polymers."

关键性能参数：
- MW（单体）= 72；吸水 59 g/g；持水 >90%（Table 1）
- 亲水基团：–COOH（羧基）

## 3. Crosslinking density (交联密度)

操作性定义：由交联剂用量决定的网络交联程度；决定吸水率与机械性能的平衡。"When the content of crosslinking agent is too high, the crosslinking density is also too high, and although the molecular chain can expand smoothly, the intermolecular space is too small for water molecules to enter; thus, the water absorption of the superabsorbent polymers decreases."（Page 6）

使用场景（原文）：
- Page 6 (Figure 2B): 交联剂含量对吸水率影响——先升后降的典型抛物线。
- Page 6: "When the crosslinker content was low, the reaction system could not form a desirable three-dimensional crosslinked network, and some monomers did not form crosslinks."
- Page 17: "Electrostatically spun superabsorbent polymer nanofiber membranes... high porosities and small pore sizes"（间接相关）。

## 4. Crosslinker (交联剂)

操作性定义：在聚合反应中通过分子间桥连形成三维网络结构的试剂。文中典型代表：N,N-methylene bisacrylamide (MBA)、ethylene glycol diglycidyl ether、epichlorohydrin（环氧氯丙烷）。

使用场景（原文）：
- Page 5: "potassium persulfate (K₂S₂O₈) and the crosslinker N,N-methylene bisacrylamide (MBA)."
- Page 10: "epichlorohydrin as the crosslinking agent"（壳聚糖/羧甲基纤维素）。
- Page 13: "Polymers undergo crosslinking to directly form hydrogels, which involves chemical reactions or physical interactions (e.g., ionic interactions and hydrogen bonding)."

## 5. Initiator (引发剂)

操作性定义：产生自由基引发单体聚合的化合物。文中代表：potassium persulfate (K₂S₂O₈)、benzoyl peroxide、ammonium persulfate。

使用场景（原文）：
- Page 5: "Potassium acrylate and acrylamide can polymerize and crosslink... under the action of the initiator potassium persulfate (K₂S₂O₈)..."
- Page 5: "The type and amount of initiator used in the synthesis process have a strong influence on the water absorption properties of polyacrylate-type polymers."
- Page 5 (Figure 2A): 引发剂用量对聚合溶胀比的影响。

## 6. Hydrogel (水凝胶)

操作性定义："Polymers undergo crosslinking to directly form hydrogels, which involves chemical reactions or physical interactions (e.g., ionic interactions and hydrogen bonding)."（Page 13）

使用场景（原文）：
- Page 13: "a superabsorbent gel with the water uptake of 1914 g/g has been synthesized using monomers of 2-acrylamido-2-methyl-1-propanesulfonic acid and AA."
- Page 13: "Freeze-drying of hydrogels results in aerogels with ultrahigh porosity and ultralow thermal conductivity."
- Page 14: "Zhang et al. prepared superabsorbent hydrogel beads with sodium alginate and carboxymethyl chitosan with a maximum MB adsorption capacity of 2518 mg/g."
- Page 16: "Because hydrogels are sensitive to environmental conditions, the controlled release of drugs can be achieved by loading them onto superabsorbent polymer hydrogels."
- Page 18: "Varshney et al. used a physically crosslinked, highly absorbent poly(vinyl alcohol)/soybean isolate protein hydrogel as a dermal wound dressing."
- Page 20: "superabsorbent hydrogel should be a brilliant star due to its broad potential biomedical applications."

## 7. Nanofiber (纳米纤维)

操作性定义：通过静电纺丝制备的高比表面积纳米级纤维。"Electrostatically spun superabsorbent polymer nanofiber membranes have an extremely high specific surface area, which can provide more cell adhesion and growth sites."（Page 17）

使用场景（原文）：
- Page 12: "Chen et al. investigated the fractal dimension of the pore area of superabsorbent polymer fibers, and the results indicated that the larger the pore area, the stronger the adsorption capacity and the higher the water absorption rate."
- Page 17: "Superabsorbent nanofiber membranes prepared by electrostatic spinning have high porosities and small pore sizes, which can effectively block the invasion of external pathogens and provide good air and moisture permeability."
- Page 20: "Among various formats, electrospun fibrous film is the most anticipated one."

## 8. Polyacrylonitrile (PAN, 聚丙烯腈)

操作性定义：以腈基为主的合成聚合物，需经碱水解将疏水腈基转化为碱金属羧酸基团才能吸水。

原文摘选："Because the nitrile group in polyacrylonitrile is electronegative and cannot easily absorb water, it must be hydrolyzed by adding alkali to change the hydrophobic group into an alkali metal carboxylic acid group to obtain the ability to absorb water, after which the hydrophilic group in the three-dimensional network locks the water into the pores of the network by hydrogen bonding."（Page 7）

性能参数：MW（单体）= 53；吸水 95 g/g；持水 85%（Table 1）；亲水基团：–CH₃NO, –COOR。

## 9. Polyvinyl alcohol (PVA, 聚乙烯醇)

操作性定义：具有多个羟基的亲水性聚合物，可生物降解，需化学改性（引入羧基）提升超吸水性。

原文摘选："PVA is not as absorbent as PAA-based superabsorbent polymers but is biodegradable."（Page 7）

性能参数：MW（单体）= 44；吸水 74 g/g；持水 93%（Table 1）。

## 10. Cellulose (纤维素)

操作性定义：天然多糖，存在于植物纤维中；通过物理化学处理制成纳米纤维素和纳米晶（富含羟基）用于交联。

原文摘选："Cellulose usually exists in the form of fibrous strands with a large number of intramolecular and intermolecular hydrogen bonds."（Page 8）

性能参数：MW（单体）= 162；吸水 119 g/g；持水 61%；亲水基团：–COOH（Table 1）。

## 11. Chitosan (壳聚糖)

操作性定义："The second most abundant naturally occurring macromolecule after cellulose, has a linear polysaccharide structure, and is commonly found in fungi, arthropod exoskeletons, and molluscan teeth."（Page 8）

原文摘选：
- Page 9: "The amino groups on chitosan are less hydrophilic than the carboxyl or hydroxyl groups on cellulose and starch, and thus the water absorption capacity of pure chitosan is only 0.9 g distilled water/g."
- Page 10: "Tang et al. reported a material made of chitosan and carboxymethylcellulose with epichlorohydrin as the crosslinking agent, which had a water absorption capacity of 1300 g of distilled water/g and could be degraded in soil."

性能参数：MW（单体）= 161；吸水 670 g/g；持水 56%；亲水基团：–SO₃H（Table 1）。

## 12. Starch (淀粉)

操作性定义："The most prevalent polysaccharide in plants, has two main structures, straight-chain starch and branched-chain starch, both of which are readily hydrolyzed by enzymes or microorganisms into glucose."（Page 8）

原文摘选："By grafting acrylic acid or polyacrylamide onto starch, its water absorption capability can be enhanced."（Page 10）

性能参数：MW（单体）= 162；吸水 343 g/g；持水 80%；亲水基团：–OH, –COOH（Table 1）。

## 13. Alginate (藻酸盐)

操作性定义："Alginates are naturally occurring anionic polymers that are widely used in biomedical and industrial applications owing to their good biocompatibility, low toxicity, and low cost."（Page 9）

原文摘选："The carboxyl groups on the alginate backbone can be crosslinked by divalent metal ions, or a stabilizing network structure can be created using a crosslinking agent after graft copolymerization with vinyl monomers."（Page 9）

性能参数：MW（单体）= 176；吸水 1700 g/g；持水 70%；亲水基团：–COONa, –OH（Table 1）。

## 14. Graft copolymerization (接枝共聚)

操作性定义："Existing modification methods usually involve graft copolymerization of polymers rich in hydrophilic groups."（Page 10）

使用场景（原文）：
- Page 5: "the reaction mechanism for the formation of crosslinked acrylic hydrogels via radiation-induced graft polymerization."
- Page 8: "Poly(acrylic acid-co-acrylamide) grafted onto cellulose."
- Page 9: "Prepared a novel superabsorbent polymer by grafting copolymerization of sodium acrylate and 1-vinyl-2-pyrrolidone onto the chain of N,O-carboxymethyl chitosan; the measured water absorbency was 1268 g distilled water/g."
- Page 10: "Acrylic polymers are often grafted onto cellulose to enhance its water absorption capability while preserving its biodegradability."

## 15. Flory's network theory (Flory 网络理论)

操作性定义：解释聚合物吸水的热力学理论——渗透压差驱动吸水与膨胀。

原文摘选："Their water absorption mechanism follows Flory's network theory, in which the osmotic pressure difference drives the water absorption and expansion."（Page 2）

## 16. Osmotic pressure (渗透压)

操作性定义：由聚合物网络内外离子浓度差产生的驱动力，将水分子推入网络内部直至内外平衡。

原文摘选："The main reason for the water absorption is the osmotic pressure and the presence of numerous hydrophilic functional groups in the molecular chain."（Page 2）；"the ion concentration within the polymer is significantly greater than that outside, leading to an osmotic pressure that drives water from the exterior to penetrate the interior until an equilibrium is reached."（Page 2）

## 17. CRC — Centrifuge Retention Capacity (离心保持能力)

操作性定义：SAP 吸水后经离心（1200g, 3 min）保持的吸水量，是衡量 SAP 保持能力的重要指标。

原文摘选："The CRC of the polymers gradually decreases as the reaction temperature increases; however, the AUL shows a different trend, increasing with the reaction temperature but decreasing again at 160 °C."（Page 6）

## 18. AUL — Absorption Under Load (加压吸水率)

操作性定义：SAP 在特定压力（如 4 psi, 20 min）下吸收 0.9% NaCl 溶液的量，直接对应纸尿裤/卫生巾在实际使用中的吸水性能。

原文摘选："The CRC of the polymers gradually decreases as the reaction temperature increases; however, the AUL shows a different trend..."（Page 6）；"This is mainly due to the dehydration of the carboxylic acid groups at 160 °C."（Page 6）

## 19. Biodegradability (生物降解性)

操作性定义：材料在自然环境（如土壤）或生物体中被微生物降解的能力。

使用场景（原文）：
- Page 6: "Because of the high stability, very high molecular weight, and low water solubility of polyacrylates, which result in the inability of microorganisms to degrade them and a tendency to cause environmental pollution problems, the materials need to be modified."
- Page 10: "A biodegradation test in soil with 20% w/w moisture showed 46.7% weight loss of the material after 150 days."
- Page 9: "Alginate-based materials... demonstrate commendable biodegradability."

## 20. Electrostatic spinning / Electrospinning (静电纺丝)

操作性定义：通过高压电场将聚合物溶液拉伸成纳米纤维的加工技术，可制备 SAP 纳米纤维膜。

使用场景（原文）：
- Page 12: "Superabsorbent fibers are prepared by dry spinning, wet spinning, and electrostatic spinning."
- Page 16: "Combining drugs with superabsorbent polymer solutions for electrostatic spinning... resulting in personalized drug release profiles."
- Page 18: "super absorbent polymer nanofiber membranes have an extremely high specific surface area."
- Page 20: "electrospinning has moved forward from the single-fluid process, to coaxial, triaxial, side-by-side, and combination(s) thereof."

## 21. Protein (蛋白质)

操作性定义：由 n 条肽链组成的大分子；含 –CO–NH 基团。

性能参数：MW = 75–240；吸水 860 g/g；持水 30%（Table 1）。

## 22. Amino acid (氨基酸)

操作性定义："Amino acids are used as monomer units to form polymers with hydrophilic groups that can absorb water, and polyamino acids can be directly crosslinked to obtain a network structure."（Page 9）

典型数值：聚天冬氨酸最大吸水 1100 g/g；聚谷氨酸经 γ-辐射交联 3500 g/g（Page 9）。

## 23. Polycarboxylate (聚羧酸盐) — 相关概念

说明：本文未直接使用 "polycarboxylate" 术语，但其概念对应本文反复出现的含 –COOH/–COONa 基团的聚丙烯酸盐体系，是 PAA/PAN 水解产物及 SAP 的主要亲水官能团形式。

相关原文："the hydrophilic group in the three-dimensional network locks the water into the pores of the network by hydrogen bonding"（Page 7，PAN 水解后形成碱金属羧酸盐）；"the content of water-absorbing groups is much higher"（Page 5，PAA）。

[概念说明]：Polycarboxylate 是行业用语，本综述中以 PAA 及其钠盐（sodium polyacrylate）作为等价概念。

注：所有原文引文均直接来自 Yang, Y. et al., Polymers 2024, 16, 501；引文不翻译，保持英文原文。
