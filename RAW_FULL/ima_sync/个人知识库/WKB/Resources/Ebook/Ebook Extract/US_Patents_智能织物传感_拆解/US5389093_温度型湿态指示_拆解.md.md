## 2. 技术问题与背景

背景部分（lines 52-113）与母案 US 5,197,958 基本一致，要点：

- 单点指示、导流不足（lines 57-70）："Some of the prior art wetness indicators are located at a single location on the diaper."（lines 59-61）；"The prior art diapers are particularly unadvantageous when the original wetting location is significantly far from the indicator location and the amount of wetness is not substantial."（lines 65-70）。
- 难以查看、不美观（lines 71-79）："In many cases, the baby has to be virtually undressed to find the indicator mark, thus greatly limiting the utility of a wetness indicator. Furthermore, these single location indicators are not aesthetically pleasing."（lines 75-79）。
- 电子方案复杂/安全隐患（lines 80-94）：引 US 4,356,818（Macias et al.）短路检测，"These electronic wetness detectors are elaborate designs which require the addition of multiple components"（lines 89-91）；"mothers may fear possible electrocution of the child"（lines 92-94）。
- 一次性系统不可复用、不耐洗涤（lines 95-113）："The washing process repeatedly subjects the diapers to detergents and heat, and the indicating systems of the prior art are not able to withstand these harsh environments."（lines 110-113）；可复用尿布需"after drying will reindicate a dry condition and then reindicate a wet condition"（lines 104-107）。
- 环保诉求（右栏 lines 48-51）："Finally, the disposable diapers of the prior art are not reusable and therefore not recyclable."
## 3. 独立权利要求解析

### 3.1 权利要求 1（lines 371-390）—— 唯一产品独立权利要求（"diaper system"）（本报告核心）

英文原文（逐行引用，lines 371-390，右栏）：

"1. A diaper system for indicating wetness, comprising"（line 371）
"an outer shell having a front portion, a backportion and a connecting portion extending between said front and back portions, said outer shell defining an outer surface;"（lines 372-374）
"an insulation layer positioned between a wearer and said outer shell which, when dry is insufficiently thermally conductive for a normal body temperature to raise said outer surface above a given threshold, but being sufficiently thermally conductive when wet to raise said outer surface temperature above a given threshold, at least partially thermally insulates said outer surface from said inner surface;"（lines 375-382）
"a thermal indicator on said outer surface which provides a visual indication when said outer surface has a temperature above a given threshold; and said threshold being chosen such that said visual indication will generally not be present when said wearer is dry, but will generally be present when said wearer secretes waste material which is absorbed by said insulation layer."（lines 383-390）

与母案 US 5,197,958 权利要求 1 的差异（对比 lines 366-386 母案）：
1. 主体措辞由 "a diaper / a baby" 改为 "a diaper system / a wearer"——权利主体扩展为"系统"，且覆盖成人穿着者（失禁裤场景）；
2. E3 明确为 "an insulation layer positioned between a wearer and said outer shell"（母案为泛指 "material positioned between said interior and exterior surfaces"）；
3. E5 新增限定 "which is absorbed by said insulation layer"——排泄物必须被隔热层吸收（吸收与隔热功能合一）；
4. 删去母案 E2"内表面"要素，结构要素更收敛。
总体为针对母案授权范围重新划界的缩小版权利要求（continuation 常见做法，推演）。

### 3.2 权利要求 8（lines 409-427）—— 方法独立权利要求

"8. A method of detecting when a wearer has excreted moisture between its legs, comprising: providing an outer shell having a front portion, a back portion and a connecting portion extending between said front and back portions, said outer shell defining an outer surface on which is positioned a thermal indicator; positioning said outer shell on said wearer such that said connecting portion fits between the legs of said wearer; at least partially thermally insulating; wicking moisture excreted from said wearer from said wearer towards said outer surface to bring said moisture into thermal contact with said thermal indicator; and, providing a visual indication when said thermal indicator is exposed to a temperature above a given threshold."（lines 409-427）

要素：①提供带热指示器的三段式外壳 → ②将外壳穿戴于穿着者（连接部置于两腿间）→ ③至少部分热绝缘 → ④导流（把排泄湿气引向热指示器形成热接触）→ ⑤温度超阈值时给出可视指示。

## 7. 与卫材业务关联（湿态指示研发启示）

- "diaper system" 的系统化设计思路（Claim 1 E1，line 371）：把湿态指示作为"系统"而非尿布附加件设计——对卫材可启发"可拆卸/可更换指示模块"（指示贴、指示条独立于主体），降低整线改造难度（推演）。
- 隔热层兼吸收功能（E5，lines 385-390）："waste material which is absorbed by said insulation layer" 明确触发前提是排泄物被隔热层吸收——卫材设计上吸收芯体可同时承担"隔热+吸收"双职，导流层把尿液导向底膜热指示区（推演）。
- 方法权利要求 8 的全流程覆盖（lines 409-427）：穿着定位→隔热→导流→温度接触→可视指示，直接对应纸尿裤/失禁裤的"芯体导流 + 底膜外侧指示"量产工艺链，可作为工艺评审的检查清单。
- 阈值档位缺口（Claim 3-4，lines 396-398）：续案仅保护 >80°F、>87°F 两档——如卫材选用更低触发温度（如 30-35°C 接近室温区间），可避开其从属权利要求，且母案 60/70°F 档已随母案过期（推演）。
- 期限放弃声明的商业信号（Notice，lines 8-11）：权利人自愿将期限缩短至 2010-03-30（与母案 17 年自授权日对齐），说明其更关注与母案期限的协调而非延长独占——技术早已进入公有领域，可放心借鉴结构设计（推演）。
- 成人失禁场景（"wearer" 措辞，lines 375-390）：权利要求明确用 "wearer" 覆盖成人，成人失禁裤/成人纸尿裤的温度指示设计直接对标本专利（推演）。
## 8. 权利边界与规避启示

- 保护范围：较母案收窄——需要"三段式外壳 + 位于穿着者与外壳之间的隔热层（干/湿导热差异，且排泄物被其吸收）+ 外表面热指示器 + 阈值设定"的 diaper system；方法权利要求 8 覆盖穿戴与检测全流程。
- 关键局限：①E5 要求排泄物"被隔热层吸收"，若排泄物未被隔热层吸收（如被独立吸收层吸收）则不落入；②仍为温度阈值机制（非温度机制不侵权）；③从属阈值仅 >80°F/>87°F；④continuation 不能超出母案公开内容，说明书无新增实施例。
- 期限估算：申请日 1993-03-29（line 14）→ 20 年：2013-03-29；授权 1995-02-14（line 3）→ 17 年：2012-02-14；取长 2013-03-29。但 Notice（lines 8-11）声明 "portion of the term ... subsequent to Mar. 30, 2010 has been disclaimed" → 实际权利至 2010-03-30 终止。已过期。
- FTO 与规避提示（推演）：①本专利与母案均过期，美国市场温度型湿态指示 FTO 障碍主要转向 2007/0270773 A1（若其授权）及更新的第三方专利；②规避本专利（若仍有效）的路径：吸收层与隔热层分离（排泄物不被隔热层吸收）、非温度指示、指示器不置于外表面、阈值设定在 80°F 以下。
- 总结：US 5,389,093 是 US 5,197,958 的延续申请，权利要求收窄为"三段式外壳 + 吸收型隔热层 + 外表面热指示 + 阈值设定"的 diaper system（主体扩展至 wearer），并因权利人对 2010-03-30 之后期限的自愿放弃而提前届满，全部技术内容可自由实施。
