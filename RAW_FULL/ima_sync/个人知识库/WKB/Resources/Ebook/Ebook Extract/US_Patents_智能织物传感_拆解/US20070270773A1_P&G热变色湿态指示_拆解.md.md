## 2. 技术问题与背景

- 发明领域（lines 51-54）："This invention relates to absorbent articles and/or garments such as diapers, incontinent briefs, feminine pads, bandages and alike absorbent articles and/or garments with thermochromic wetness indicator(s)."——品类明确覆盖尿布、失禁裤、卫生巾、绷带。
- 健康问题（lines 60-66）：长时间接触尿液/排泄物导致 "skin irritation, diaper rash, skin sores and infection"（lines 65-66）；情绪影响（lines 70-73）；"The best cure for the problems is prevention."（line 74）。
- 现有指示方案的缺陷（lines 46-64）：

- 需移除产品才能查看 pH 指示剂/液体溶解油墨变色（lines 47-49）；
- "require extensive changes to the manufacturing process of such articles and garments to incorporate wetness indicating abilities to existing products, which in turn drives up the manufacturing costs"（lines 49-53）——改产线、推高成本；
- "the wetness indicating effects are wicked away into the absorbent materials making detection difficult or the wetness indicator effects do not have a lasting visual appearance"（lines 55-58）——指示效果被芯体吸走、不持久；
- 曾尝试加粘合剂/化学品延长变色（lines 59-62）；现有 "viewing window"（观察窗）方案（lines 62-64）。
- 背景引证（lines 86-96、31-42）：US 4,834,733（液体收缩机械式湿态计）；US 6,603,403（远程湿态信号系统）；US 4,484,573（湿态报警）；US 6,635,797（液体溶解油墨 + 互动图案如厕训练）；US 6,657,099（尿液溶解物片材）；并直接引证本批次前两件："U.S. Pat. Nos. 5,197,958 and 5,389,093 issued to Howell, describes reusable diapers with thermally sensitive indicators comprised of thermochromic ink(s) using conventional silk screening techniques as means of incorporation with the reusable diaper."（lines 41-44）。
## 3. 独立权利要求解析

### 3.1 权利要求 1（lines 211-218）—— 主权利要求（本报告核心）

英文原文（逐行引用，lines 211-218）：

"1. Absorbent articles and/or garments with thermochromic wetness indicator(s), with embodiments including a liquid-permeable top sheet with an internal and external side, a liquid-impermeable back sheet with an interior and exterior side, with an absorbent core/pad situated at least partially between the internal side of said top sheet and internal side of said back sheet."（lines 212-218）

|||
|---|---|---|
|E1 主题|"Absorbent articles and/or garments with thermochromic wetness indicator(s),"（lines 212-213）|带有热致变色湿态指示器的吸收制品和/或服装|
|E2 顶片|"with embodiments including a liquid-permeable top sheet with an internal and external side,"（lines 213-214）|实施例包括：液可透顶层片（top sheet），具内、外侧面|
|E3 底膜|"a liquid-impermeable back sheet with an interior and exterior side,"（lines 215-216）|液不可透底层片（back sheet，底膜），具内、外侧面|
|E4 吸收芯|"with an absorbent core/pad situated at least partially between the internal side of said top sheet and internal side of said back sheet."（lines 216-218）|至少部分位于顶片内侧面与底膜内侧面之间的吸收芯/垫|

解析要点：
1. 该权利要求极宽——只要求"吸收制品/服装 + 热致变色湿态指示器"叠加常规三明治结构（顶片/底膜/吸收芯），未限定指示器的位置、颜色、触发机制细节；
2. 写法上反复使用 "with embodiments including ... / with ..." 的可选性表述，更像对发明概念的公开而非限定的技术方案，权利稳定性弱（推演）；
3. 与 Howells 两案（5,197,958/5,389,093）的关键差异：不限 "diaper"、不限布料/可复用、不要求外表面温度阈值指示——品类扩展到 incontinent briefs、feminine pads、bandages（lines 51-54），指示器直接并入一次性结构。

### 3.2 权利要求 9（lines 207-218）—— 实质上的第二独立范围（依 claim 1）

"9. As per claim 1, the absorbent article and/or garment embodiment may include a liquid-permeable top sheet with an external and an internal side, a liquid-impermeable back sheet with an external and internal side, and an absorbent core/pad situated at least partially between the internal sides of the liquid-permeable top sheet and the liquid-impermeable back sheet, and a thermochromic wetness indicator(s) may include incorporation to the exterior side of the liquid impermeable back sheet, however the present invention offers a large range and variety of uses and products including diapers, incontinent briefs, feminine pads, bandages and any other suitable products and/or uses presently not discovered and/or described herein and the exact embodiment will be predetermined upon further product development."（lines 207-218）

要点：在三明治结构之上增加"指示器可并入底膜外侧面（exterior side of the liquid impermeable back sheet）"的限定；明确产品范围 "diapers, incontinent briefs, feminine pads, bandages and any other suitable products"。

## 5. 实施例与数据

本申请为概念性/方法学公开，无配方、脂肪酸表、微胶囊工艺或实验数据（与 Howells 两案的工程细节形成鲜明对比）：

- 0031 材料与配色（lines 107-117）：指示器材料 "may include various known/unknown thermochromic compound(s) and/or material(s) including thermochromic leuco dye(s) and/or thermochromic liquid crystal(s) or any suitable combination"（lines 109-111）；颜色示例："a possible color prior to activation may include tan and after activation occurs the color change may include blue."（lines 116-117）——干态 tan、激活后 blue 的配色示例。
- 0032 激活温度（lines 118-128）："the activation temperature range for color change of the wetness indicator(s) could include 95 F to 104 F, however due to the unknown insulation factors and/or RH factors and/or uses of future absorbent articles and/or garments developed with this invention, the actual activation temperature range of the wetness indicator(s) will be determined upon product development."——95-104°F（35-40°C，人体体温区间）为示例窗口；并明确因隔热因子/RH（湿度）因子未知，实际激活温度在产品开发时确定。
- 0033 施加方法与材料形态（lines 129-142）：施加方法 "could include printing, painting, dying, staining and/or melting and could be manually applied and/or mechanically applied"（lines 133-136）；材料形态 "may include inks, paints, powders, vinyl and/or any other thermochromic compound(s) and/or material(s) not currently available for use"（lines 138-140）。
- 0034 图形（lines 143-152）：shapes, figures, characters, animals, numbers, letters, people, planes, trains and/or automobiles, flowers, trees, insects 及任意组合。
- 0035 示例实施例（lines 153-164）：top sheet（液可透，具外/内侧面）+ back sheet（液不可透，具外/内侧面）+ absorbent core/pad（至少部分位于两者内侧面之间）+ 指示器并入底膜外侧面；产品 "diapers, incontinent briefs, feminine pads, bandages and any other suitable products and/or uses"（lines 161-164）。
- 0036-0037 扩展声明（lines 168-182）：早期湿态检测对预防 diaper rash/skin irritation/infection 的价值（lines 174-176）；宽泛的指示器替代声明："those articles and/or garments may include any suitable wetness indicator(s), including any suitable wetness indicator(s) that change color based, at least in part, on temperature, moisture-content, and/or other Suitable parameters."（lines 177-182）——即只要"至少部分基于温度/含水量/其他参数变色"的任意指示器都在其公开范围。

## 7. 与卫材业务关联（湿态指示研发启示）

- 品类扩展对标（lines 51-54、157-158、207-218）：明确覆盖 diapers、incontinent briefs、feminine pads、bandages——卫生巾/经期裤/成人失禁裤/医用敷料的湿态指示研发可直接以本案为公开技术背景（推演）。这是与 Howells 两案（仅 diaper）最大的增量信息。
- 指示位置与工艺落地（Claim 3/5/9，lines 168-170、183-189、207-218）：底膜外侧面 + 印刷/粘贴/熔融等成熟后加工工艺——纸尿裤/卫生巾底膜外侧印刷热致变色图案是当前产线可直接集成的方案，无需改动芯体结构（推演）。
- 触发温度窗口与干扰因子（0032，lines 118-128）：95-104°F（35-40°C）人体体温区间 + 明确提示"隔热因子/RH 因子"影响实际激活温度——卫材标定时需实测产品热阻与湿度环境，避免体热/环境温度误触发（推演）。
- 材料与配色选择（Claim 2/7，lines 219-222、197-199；0031，lines 107-117）：leuco dye（低成本、油墨形态成熟）与热致变色液晶两类；tan→blue 示例提示"干态浅色底衬 + 激活高对比色"的视觉设计逻辑，可沿用（推演）。
- 多位置/多色设计自由度（Claim 6/7，lines 191-199）：单一或多种位置、激活前后任意配色——为产品差异化（图案、品牌元素、多区指示）留出空间，且不增加专利风险（若该申请未授权，见第 8 节）。
- 成本主张的反向利用（lines 49-53）：本案将"现有方案需大幅改产线、推高成本"列为痛点——采购/工艺选型时应优先"不改芯体结构的外侧印刷"方案，这与 Claim 5 的施加方式（printing, painting, gluing, staining, dyeing, melting）吻合（推演）。
