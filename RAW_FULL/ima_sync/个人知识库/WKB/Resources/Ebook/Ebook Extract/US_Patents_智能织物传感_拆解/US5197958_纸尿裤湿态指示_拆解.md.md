## 2. 技术问题与背景

背景部分（lines 42-111）指出以下问题：

- 单点指示器需要导流、湿量小时失灵（lines 46-58）：
- "Some of the prior art wetness indicators are located at a single location on the diaper."（lines 48-50）
- "The prior art diapers are particularly unadvantageous when the original wetting location is significantly far from the indicator location and the amount of wetness is not substantial."（lines 54-58）—— 湿点距指示器远或湿量不足时无法触发。

- 单点指示难以观察、不美观（lines 60-69）：
- "In many cases, the baby has to be virtually undressed to find the indicator mark, thus greatly limiting the utility of a wetness indicator. Furthermore, these single location indicators are not aesthetically pleasing."（lines 64-69）

- 电子电路方案复杂且有安全顾虑（lines 70-88）：
- 引 US 4,356,818（Macias et al.），靠湿气使两条导线短路检测："These electronic wetness detectors are elaborate designs which require the addition of multiple components to the assembly and use of the diaper."（lines 82-85）；"mothers may fear possible electrocution of the child"（lines 86-88）。

- 一次性指示系统不可复用、不耐洗涤（lines 89-108）：
- "The washing process repeatedly subjects the diapers to detergents and heat, and the indicating systems of the prior art are not able to withstand these harsh environments."（lines 105-108）
- 可复用布尿布需求："A reusable cloth diaper needs a wetness indicating system that after drying will reindicate a dry condition and then reindicate a wet condition when the diaper is again wet."（lines 98-101）
环保诉求（lines 109-111）："the disposable diapers of the prior art are not reusable and therefore not recyclable."

核心需求：可复用的布尿布 + 耐洗涤、可逆、易于查看且美观的湿态指示。

## 3. 独立权利要求解析

### 3.1 权利要求 1（lines 366-386）—— 产品主权利要求（本报告核心）

英文原文（逐行引用，lines 366-386）：

"1. A diaper for indicating wetness, comprising:"（line 366）
"an inner surface adapted to be positioned against the skin of a baby;"（lines 367-368）
"an outer surface generally opposed to said inner surface adapted to form the exterior of said diaper;"（lines 369-370）
"material positioned between said interior and exterior surfaces, which, when dry is insufficiently thermally conductive for a normal body temperature to raise said outer surface above a given threshold, but being sufficiently thermally conductive when wet to raise said outer surface temperature above a given threshold, at least partially thermally insulates said outer surface from said inner surface;"（lines 371-378）
"a thermal indicator on said outer surface which provides a visual indication when said outer surface has a temperature above a given threshold; and said threshold being chosen such that said visual indication will generally not be present when a baby wearing said diaper is dry, but will generally be present when said baby secretes waste material into said diaper."（lines 379-386）

### 3.3 权利要求 18（lines 412-432）—— 可复用布尿布产品权利要求

"18. A diaper for indicating wetness, comprising: a cloth sufficiently durable to withstand multiple washings, said cloth defining an inner surface adapted to be positioned against the skin of a baby and an outer surface, said cloth comprising: a front portion; a back portion adapted to receive the buttocks of the wearer, said back portion defining a pair of opposing laterally extending tabs; and, a connecting portion connecting said front portion and said back portion adapted to extend between the legs of the wearer; fasteners secured to one of said front portion and said back portion adapted to connect said tabs to said front portion when said diaper is worn; and, a plurality of discrete thermal indicators distributed over substantially the entire outer surface of said diaper, each of said plurality of internal indicators providing a visual indication of wetness when said outer surface has a temperature above a given threshold."（lines 412-432）

要点：①布料须"耐多次洗涤"（可复用限定）；②前/后/连接三段式结构 + 横向延伸弹性搭扣；③"a plurality of discrete thermal indicators distributed over substantially the entire outer surface"——多点离散指示器覆盖几乎整个外表面（区别于现有单点指示）。原文 "internal indicators" 系笔误，更正证书已改为 "thermal indicators"（lines 476-477）。

### 3.4 权利要求 23（lines 446-458）—— 方法独立权利要求

"23. A method of detecting the wetness of a diaper having an inner surface, an outer surface and a thermal indicator, comprising: at least partially thermally insulating said inner surface of said diaper from said outer surface of said diaper; wicking any moisture from said inner surface towards said outer surface to bring said moisture into thermal contact with said thermal indicator; and, providing a visual indication when said thermal indicator is exposed to a temperature above a given threshold."（lines 446-458）

三步：隔热（inner 与 outer 之间）→ 导流（wicking 把湿气引至指示器热接触）→ 可视指示。

## 5. 实施例与数据

### 5.1 结构实施例（lines 167-195）

- 外壳：非吸收、耐用软织物，"The preferred embodiment of the outer shell 5 comprises Dupont style #22-2 material which is 97% Dacron and 6% Lycra"（lines 167-168；OCR 数字，97%+6% 疑为 97% Dacron + 3% Lycra）。
- 内垫三层（FIG. 3，lines 170-172）：

- protective covering 55（内表面，贴肤层）："a layer of BIRDSEYE 100% cotton"（line 181）；
- sorbent layer 50（吸收层）："looped terry 100% cotton having a thickness of at least of an inch"（lines 186-187，OCR 丢失分数）；
- wicking layer 60（导流层）："DuPont style #1178B (poly thyon) material"（lines 188-189，OCR 拼写），把湿气从臀部导至吸收层，并"quickly brings the waste into thermal contact with the heat sensitive ink on the outer shell 5"（lines 194-196）。
- 连接与洗涤（lines 230-247、199-209）：内垫仅在前 25/后 20 与外壳连接，留出通风通道，"allows the heat from the child's body to escape from the diaper and helps prevent the heat sensitive wetness indicating ink from changing color when the diaper is not wet"（lines 239-241）；洗涤时反穿（turned inside out）保护 VELCRO 钩面（lines 201-209）。
### 5.2 热致变色油墨（lines 195-337）

- 油墨来源："The thermochromic ink that is used is similar to the thermochromic ink of U.S. Pat. No. 4,826,550, assigned to Sakura Color Corporation"（lines 199-202）。
- 三组分体系（Table 1，lines 203-209）："(1) an electron donating coloring organic compound for determining the color, (2) an electron accepting compound for determining the concentration of the color, and (3) a fatty acid for determining the color-change temperature."——供电子显色有机化合物（leuco dye，决定颜色）+ 受电子化合物（phenol，决定浓度）+ 脂肪酸（决定变色温度）。
- 脂肪酸转变温度表（Table 2，lines 250-261）：capric acid (carbon No.10) 31°C；lauric acid (12) 44°C；myristic acid (14) 53°C；palmitic acid (16) 63°C；stearic acid (18) 69°C；behenic acid (22) 79°C。"it is possible to minutely regulate the discoloring temperature by using two or more kinds of fatty acids mixedly"（lines 245-247）。
- 显色物质与色相（lines 273-284）：3,6-dimethoxyfluoran（yellow 黄）；3-cyclohexylamino-6-chlorofluoran（orange 橙）；3-diethylamin-6-methyl-7-chlorofluoran（vermilion 朱红）；3-diethylaminobenzfluoran（pink 粉）；rhodamine B lactam（red 红）；crystal violet lactone（blue 蓝）；3-diethylamino-7-dibenzylaminofluoran（green 绿）；3-diethylamino-6-methyl-7-anilinofluoran（black 黑）；"if these compounds are mixed, their mixed colors can also be obtained"（lines 287-289）。
- 通用配比（lines 295-300）："1) Coloring matter 1 group; 2) Phenols 5 groups; 3) Fatty acids 50 groups"（1:5:50）。
- 微胶囊化（lines 301-317）：三组分须 microencapsulation 防脂肪酸液态流失（lines 308-317）；现用方法 "In-situ polymerization with urea formalin resin used as wall material"（lines 302-303）；TC slurry 为含约 "45% wt. of capsule particles" 的水分散体（lines 308-310），再与水基 binder 调配成热致变色水性油墨（lines 313-314）。Table 3（lines 322-330）列出化学法（界面聚合/原位聚合等）、物理化学法（凝聚、有机溶液凝聚等）、机械化学法（喷雾干燥等）多种封装工艺。
- 油墨性能（lines 316-337）：颜色可选（orange, pink, red, purple, blue, green, black）；"(2) The free setting of a discoloring temperature in the temperature range of -30° C. to +80 C."（lines 319-320）；基础变色为 color↔colorless，亦可 color A↔color B（lines 322-328）；"(5) Sensitivity of discoloration has a range of 1 to 2 C."（lines 329-330）；"(6) Repeatability of the color change, potentially tens of thousands of times"（lines 334-335）。
### 5.3 工作阈值与配色模式（lines 338-401）

- 阈值："It has been determined that the temperature threshold is advantageously about 60 F., is desirably above 70' F., is more desirably above 80' F. and is preferably above 87 F. for the indicators of the present invention."（lines 341-344）——约 60°F 起，优选 >87°F（≈30.6°C）。
- 三种油墨模式（lines 348-366）：①两种油墨混合、其一在阈值以上变无色（wet→变色）；②"colorless below a given specific threshold temperature, and is colorful above"（干燥无色、湿时有色）；③"colorless above a given threshold temperature, and is therefore colorful below"（湿时无色、干燥有色）。
- 防误触发设计（lines 368-392）："the inner pad 10 of the diaper is designed to insulate the baby's skin temperature from contacting the outer shell 5 where the heat sensitive dye is located, while still allowing the wetness to be drawn into thermal contact with the outer shell 5"（lines 376-381）；"This thickness of the inner pad 10 is therefore at least of an inch thick."（line 387，OCR 丢失分数）。
- 图案化变色（lines 393-401）："by screening the thermochromic inks onto the garment in a repeated pattern, such as a cartoon type design, it allows the color change to be limited to a controlled area"（lines 393-396）——避免先有技术整片变色形成难看的 blotch。

## 7. 与卫材业务关联（湿态指示研发启示）

- 温度触发机制的适用性评估：权利要求 1/17 用"干态导热不足—湿态导热足够 + 外表面热指示 + 阈值"的功能性限定，未限定布料/可复用（lines 366-386、405-411）。一次性纸尿裤若用透气底膜 + 尿液温热触发外表面热指示，结构上同样可能落入（推演）。若要在该方向自由设计，应改用"湿态化学响应"（pH 指示/液体溶解油墨）而非温度响应。
- 热致变色油墨选型与调校：脂肪酸熔点是变色温度开关（Table 2，lines 250-261）；卫材目标体温 35-40°C，可用月桂酸（44°C）/豆蔻酸（53°C）等混配把变色点下调至 30-40°C 区间，并利用 "-30°C 至 +80°C" 的可设定范围与 1-2°C 灵敏度（lines 319-330）设计窄窗口指示（推演）。微胶囊化（脲醛壁材原位聚合 + 45% wt 胶囊 slurry + 水基 binder，lines 302-314）是油墨供应商选型的技术底稿。
- 结构设计借鉴：隔热/导流协同防误触发（lines 368-392）——纸尿裤可用高蓬松吸收芯体兼作隔热，热风无纺布作导流层，把尿液热量导向底膜外侧指示区；内垫仅两端连接留通风通道（lines 230-247）减少体热累积误触发。
- 成本与工艺：丝网印刷（silk screening，lines 116-118、248-253）为低成本大批量施印方案；微胶囊 TC 油墨可耐多次洗涤/烘干（lines 199-209、338-348）——对可复用卫材（布尿裤、经期裤、可洗失禁垫）有直接价值。
- 图案化与多点覆盖：重复卡通图案既美观又限定变色区域（lines 393-401）；权利要求 18 要求"离散指示器覆盖几乎整个外表面"（lines 427-432）解决"脱衣找指示点"痛点（lines 60-69）——婴儿纸尿裤外衬整版印刷多区指示可直接借鉴（推演）。
- 湿巾/经期护理延伸（推演）：温度触发对经期用品（体温+血液温热）与湿巾（冷热双态）亦可移植，但需重新标定阈值并考虑皮肤贴合面的体热干扰——本专利的"隔热层"思想正是解决该干扰的关键。
## 8. 权利边界与规避启示

- 保护范围：核心为权利要求 1/17 的"隔热材料（干湿导热差异）+ 外表面热指示器 + 阈值设定"三要素组合；权利要求 18 覆盖"耐洗涤布料 + 三段式结构 + 扣件 + 全表面多点离散热指示"的可复用布尿布构型；权利要求 23 为方法（隔热→导流→可视指示）。属功能性权利要求，等同范围可能较宽（导热差异的等效隔热材料、温度指示的等效实现），但均以"温度阈值+外表面"为界。
- 关键局限：①限定 "diaper"，不含卫生巾/失禁垫/绷带（品类扩展由 2007 年申请补上）；②必须为"热指示器 + 温度阈值"——pH/液体溶解/电子式等非温度机制不侵权；③权利要求 18 要求"cloth ... sufficiently durable to withstand multiple washings"（可复用布料），一次性产品可抗辩不落入（推演）；④"inner pad" 类从属权利要求（10-16）限定内垫结构。
- 期限估算：申请日 1992-04-01（line 10）、授权 1993-03-30（line 3）。1995-06-08 前申请的美国专利按 17 年自授权（2010-03-30）与 20 年自申请（2012-04-01）取长者 → 最迟 2012-04-01 届满。已过期多年，在美国可自由实施（推演；注意同族延续案 US 5,389,093 另有放弃声明，见其报告）。
- FTO 与规避提示（推演）：①出口美国的纸尿裤若含温度型湿态指示，本专利已过期不构成障碍，但需排查其延续申请（5,389,093，已放弃至 2010-03-30）与 2007/0270773 A1 公开申请；②规避设计方向：非温度湿态指示（pH/液体溶解/导电传感）、一次性结构（避开"耐洗涤布料"）、指示器置于芯体/内层而非"outer surface"、电子湿度传感。
- 总结：本专利确立了"隔热层 + 热致变色油墨 + 外表面温度阈值"的尿布湿态指示范式，权利要求以功能性限定覆盖核心机理，已于 2012 年届满进入公有领域，其油墨配方（leuco dye/酚/脂肪酸 1:5:50、微胶囊 45% wt）、脂肪酸调温表与隔热防误触发设计可作为卫材湿态指示产品研发的免费技术底稿。
