# Seymour Polymer Chemistry — Batch 1 (Ch.1-6) 核心概念汇总

范围: Chapter 1–6
用途: 为 SAP（高吸水性树脂）与 PP（聚丙烯）卫材材料理解提供概念索引

## 一、基础定义类（Foundational Definitions）

||||||
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|Polymer|聚合物|Ch.1|"The word polymer is derived from the Greek poly and meros, meaning many and parts, respectively."|
|2|Macromolecule|大分子|Ch.1|"Some scientists prefer to use the word macromolecule, or large molecule, instead of polymer."|
|3|Biopolymer|生物聚合物|Ch.1|"naturally occurring polymers, or biopolymers, and synthetic polymers should be studied in different courses"|
|4|Oligomer|低聚物|Ch.3|"The word oligomer is derived from the Greek word oligos, meaning 'a few.'"|
|5|Addition Polymer|加聚物|Ch.6|"An addition polymer has the same atoms as the monomer in its repeat unit."|
|6|Condensation Polymer|缩聚物|Ch.6|"Condensation polymers contain fewer atoms within the polymer repeat unit than the reactants because of the formation of byproducts"|

## 二、材料分类类（Material Classification）

||||||
|---|---|---|---|---|
|7|Thermoplastic|热塑性聚合物|Ch.1|"Thermoplastics are two-dimensional molecules that may be softened by heating."|
|8|Thermoset|热固性聚合物|Ch.1|"Thermosets are materials that are three-dimensional networks that cannot be reshaped by heating. Rather than melting, thermosets degrade."|
|9|Elastomer / Rubber|弹性体/橡胶|Ch.1|"elastomers or rubbers"|
|10|Plastic / Fiber|塑料/纤维|Ch.1|"the differences between all polymers, including plastics, fibers, and elastomers"|

## 三、结构层级类（Structure Hierarchy）

||||||
|---|---|---|---|---|
|11|Primary Structure|一级结构|Ch.2|"describes the precise sequence of the individual atoms that compose the polymer chain"|
|12|Secondary Structure|二级结构|Ch.2|"describes the molecular shape or conformation of the polymer chain"|
|13|Tertiary Structure|三级结构|Ch.2|"describes the shaping or folding of the polymer"|
|14|Quaternary Structure|四级结构|Ch.2|"represents the overall shape of groups of the tertiary structures"|
|15|Tacticity|立体规整度|Ch.2|"the particular geometry about each chiral carbon"|
|16|Isotactic / Syndiotactic / Atactic|等规/间同/无规|Ch.2|"isotactic polypropylene (PP)"|
|17|Crosslink / Crosslinking|交联|Ch.1|"crosslinks between the individual polyisoprene chain-like molecules"|
|18|Chain Entanglement|链缠结|Ch.2|"chain entanglement is also an important contributory factor to the physical properties of polymers"|
|19|Spherulite|球晶|Ch.2|"Maltese cross-like structures with polarized light"|
|20|Lamella|片晶|Ch.2|"folded chain lamellae"|

|22|Tie Molecule|系带分子|Ch.2|"The individual spherulite lamellae are bound together by 'tie' molecules"|

## 五、分子量类（Molecular Weight）

||||||
|---|---|---|---|---|
|30|Polydisperse|多分散|Ch.3|"consist of molecules with different molecular weights and are said to be polydisperse"|
|31|Monodisperse|单分散|Ch.3|"consist of molecules with a specific molecular weight (M) and are said to be monodisperse"|
|32|Mn (Number-Average MW)|数均分子量|Ch.3|"Mn = Σ MiNi / Σ Ni"|
|33|Mw (Weight-Average MW)|重均分子量|Ch.3|"The weight-average molecular weight is similarly described, except that the capsules correspond in size to the size of the polymer chain"|
|34|Mv (Viscosity-Average MW)|粘均分子量|Ch.3|[通过 Mark-Houwink 方程定义]|
|35|MWD (Molecular Weight Distribution)|分子量分布|Ch.3|"Molecular weight distributions may be relatively broad... relatively narrow... or bimodal"|
|36|PDI (Polydispersity Index)|多分散指数|Ch.3|"PDI = Mw / Mn"|
|37|TMWV (Threshold MW Value)|阈值分子量|Ch.3|"lowest molecular weight where the desired property value is achieved"|
|38|GPC / SEC|凝胶渗透色谱|Ch.3|"based on separation by molecular size rather than chemical properties"|

||||||
|---|---|---|---|---|

|41|Newtonian Dashpot|牛顿粘壶|Ch.5|"σ = η dε/dt... independent of the strain ε"|
