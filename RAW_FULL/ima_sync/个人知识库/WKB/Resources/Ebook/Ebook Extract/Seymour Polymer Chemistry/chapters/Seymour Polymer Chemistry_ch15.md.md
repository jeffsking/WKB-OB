## 执行摘要（Executive Summary）

第15章是全书聚合物化学转化的综合手册，以"聚合物链本身作为反应底物"为核心视角，展开从取代基反应（polyolefin, polyene, aliphatic/ aromatic pendant, polyamide）到降解（depolymerization vs random chain scission）、交联、内环化、链端反应、大分子催化剂、能量吸收机制、乃至材料失效（crazing / shear yielding / ESC）的完整化学反应谱系。本章的学术价值在于把聚合物的"化学寿命"讲透——即聚合物在合成之后仍经历的所有可控制与不可控的化学变化。

对 SAP/PP 卫材业务的意义：
- SAP（聚丙烯酸交联网络）：§15.10（crosslinked materials + gel point）与 §15.12（macromer/telechelic）为 SAP 交联度调控与功能端基修饰提供理论基础
- PP 卫材：§15.7-15.8（降解）解释 PP 无纺布在紫外与氧化下的性能衰减；§15.16（crazing / ESC）解释 PP 薄膜在清洁剂环境下的应力开裂
- 聚合物改性：§15.1（polyolefin chlorination）、§15.4（聚苯乙烯磺化/硝化制离子交换树脂）、§15.11（PAN 热解制碳纤维）

## Step 1 — 宏观解读（Macro Interpretation）

### 1.1 核心论题

- 主论题：聚合物合成后的化学变化可被分类为"取代基反应 / 主链降解 / 交联 / 内环化 / 链端反应"五大类——每一类都可被定量描述与工程利用
- 副论题：（a）depolymerization（"unzipping"）与 random chain scission 是降解的两种根本机制，热力学倾向由单体稳定性决定；（b）交联通过 Flory 方程定量描述，凝胶点可预测；（c）酶作为大分子催化剂遵循 Michaelis-Menten 动力学；（d）能量吸收的三种机制（shear yielding, crazing, cracking）决定聚合物韧性
### 1.2 论证结构

- 取代基反应层（§15.1-15.5）：polyolefin（氯化/氟化）→ polyene（加氢/卤化/环氧化/臭氧化）→ aliphatic pendant（酯水解 PVAc→PVA）→ aromatic pendant（烷基化/卤化/硝化/磺化）→ polyamide（环氧/甲醛交联）
- 加工化学层（§15.6）：聚合物成型过程中的聚合/交联反应
- 降解理论层（§15.7-15.8）：depolymerization（unzipping）vs random chain scission
- 交联理论层（§15.10）：凝胶点、Flory 方程、DPw 演化
- 内环化层（§15.11）：PAN → "black orlon" → 碳纤维
- 链端与生物催化层（§15.12, §15.15）：macromer/telechelic、enzyme（lock-and-key, induced-fit, Michaelis-Menten）
- 能量吸收层（§15.16）：shear yielding / crazing / cracking / ESC
### 1.3 学术贡献

- Flory 交联方程体系：pgel = 1/(f−1) 与 DPw 演化方程构成定量预测凝胶点的核心工具
- 降解二分类：depolymerization vs random chain scission——这是理解 PMMA 与 PS 降解差异的热力学基础
- Michaelis-Menten 在聚合物化学中的应用：把酶动力学语言引入大分子催化
## Step 2 — 章节级拆解（Section-level Decomposition）

### §15.1 REACTIONS WITH POLYOLEFINS

氯化（chlorination）：
> "Polyolefins, like simple alkanes, may be chlorinated by chlorine at elevated temperatures or in the presence of ultraviolet light. This free radical reaction produces HCl and chlorinated polyolefins (Tyrin), which are used as plasticizers and flame retardants."

"Since a tertiary hydrogen atom is more readily replaced than a secondary hydrogen, polypropylene is more readily chlorinated than HDPE."

交联（crosslinking）：
> "Polyolefins may be crosslinked by heating with peroxides such as di-tert-butyl peroxide or by irradiation. It is advantageous to crosslink these polymers after they have been fabricated, as in wire coatings. The crosslinked products are less soluble and more resistant to heat than are the linear polyolefins."

### §15.3 REACTIONS OF ALIPHATIC PENDANT GROUPS

PVAc → PVA 水解：
> "Esters such as poly(vinyl acetate) (PVAc) may be hydrolyzed to produce alcohols such as poly(vinyl alcohol) (PVA), which should have the same DP as the ester."

"While the enol tautomer of acetaldehyde (vinyl alcohol) cannot be isolated, its water-soluble polymer can be obtained by the hydrolysis of any poly(vinyl carboxylic acid ester)."

"The solubility of the product in water is dependent on the extent of hydrolysis."

### §15.5 REACTIONS OF POLYAMIDES

甲醛交联：
> "Polyamides such as proteins or nylons may be reacted with ethylene oxide or formaldehyde. The latter serves as a crosslinking agent with proteins in embalming, leather production, and the stabilization of regenerated protein fibers."

### §15.7-15.8 GENERAL / SPECIFIC DEGRADATION

depolymerization (unzipping)：
> "In depolymerization monomer is split off from an activated end group. This is the opposite of the addition polymerization and is often referred to as 'unzipping.'"

random chain scission：
> "Chain scission is similar to the opposite of stepwise polycondensation where units are split apart in a random manner."

### §15.10 CROSSLINKED MATERIALS

凝胶点（gel point）：
> "As the extent of crosslinking increases there is a steady increase in the viscosity of the melt. At some extent of crosslinking, there is a rapid increase in viscosity and the mixture becomes elastic and begins to feel like a rubber. At this point the mixture is said to be 'gelled.' Beyond this point the polymer is insoluble."

Flory 交联方程：

"Mw,p = Mw,0 (1 − p) / ((1 − p)(f − 1))" (Eq. 15.37)

"pgel = 1/(f − 1)" (Eq. 15.38)

"(pgel)² = 1 / [r(f′ − 1)(f″ − 1)]" (Eq. 15.39)  ——两种官能度类型

"DPw = DPw,0(1 − p) / ((1 − p)(DPw,0 − 1))" (Eq. 15.41)

"pgel = 1/(DPw,0 − 1)" (Eq. 15.42)

"pgel = 1/DPn,0" (Eq. 15.44)

### §15.11 INTERNAL CYCLIZATION — 碳纤维合成

PAN → 碳纤维：
> "Carbon fibers and associated composite materials are the result of internal cyclization. Polyacrylonitrile, when heated, undergoes internal addition forming a condensed polycyclic material called 'black orlon' (Sec. 9.4). Further heating to about 1000°C removes the hydrogen atoms and most of the nitrogens giving a polyaromatic structure containing about 95% carbon. Further heating to about 2800°C gives a product with almost 99% carbon."

梯型聚合物：
> "When heated, polyacrylonitrile forms a complex ladder polymer containing a quina-zirine-like structure that approximates the structure shown below. This reaction is the first step in the formation of much of the carbon fibers (Sec. 15.11)."

### §15.12 REACTIVITIES OF END GROUPS

macromer（大分子单体）：
> "Polymers containing one reactive end group are often referred to as macromolecular monomers or macromers."

"Macromers are 'short' polymers that contain an active end group. This end group can be a site of unsaturation, heterocycle, or other group that can further react."

telechelic polymer（双官能端聚合物）：
> "Polymers that contain two active end groups are referred to as telechelic polymers."

> "Reactive group—MMMMMMM—reactive group"

### §15.15 NATURE'S MACROMOLECULAR CATALYSTS — 酶催化

锁钥 vs 诱导契合：
> "The two primary models currently employed to describe the formation of the complex between the reacting molecule(s) and the enzyme are the classical lock-and-key model and the induced-fit model."

"Briefly, the lock-and-key model calls for an exact or highly similar complementary fit between the enzyme and the reacting molecule(s). Geometry plays an essential part in permitting the electronic (polar and electrostatic) attractions to form the necessary complex."

"The induced-fit model is similar except that the enzyme originally does not fit the required shape. The required shape is achieved upon binding—the binding causing needed 'assisting factors' or proximity and orientation to effect a decrease in the energy of the transition state."

Michaelis-Menten 动力学：
> "The Michaelis-Menten model is commonly employed in describing nonallosteric enzyme reactions."

"Ri = k₂[EM]"

"Ratei = k₂[E]₀[M] / (K + [M])"

"Rm = k₂[E]₀"

"Since the enzyme concentration is constant, the rate of product formation under these conditions is independent of [M] and is said to be zero order."

### §15.16 MECHANISMS OF ENERGY ABSORPTION

三种机制：
> "There are three major mechanisms of energy absorption: shear yielding, crazing, and cracking. The latter two are often dealt with together and called normal stress yielding."

crack vs craze 区别：
> "A crack is an open fissure, whereas a craze is spanned top to bottom by fibrils that act to resist entrance of opportunistic molecules such as water."

shear yielding：
> "In shear yielding, oriented regions are formed at 45° angles to the stress. No void space is produced in shear yielding."

crazing：
> "Crazing often occurs prior to and in front of a crack tip. As noted before, the craze portion contains both fibrils and small voids that can be exploited after the stress is released or if the stress is maintained, since, while many materials are somewhat elastic, most plastics are not ideal elastomers and additional microscopic voids occur each time a material is stressed."

ESC（Environmental Stress Cracking）：
> "Aqueous solutions of surface-active materials, such as detergents, can produce brittle cracking, particularly in stressed materials. The term environmental stress cracking (ESC) was introduced to describe such situations. ESC is now more widely applied to describe the promotion of slow, brittle failure in stressed materials by organic substances."

"In both ESC and environmental crazing, direct chemical attack on the polymer chain is minor or not involved. It appears that the organic liquid or other promoting substance is absorbed or locally dissolved at the defects so as to assist further failure, possibly by plasticization of the stressed material or modification of the surface energy."

## Step 6 — RIA 实践转化

### 卡片 1：SAP 交联度通过 Flory 方程定量调控

- Read：原文 "pgel = 1/(f − 1)" 与 "pgel = 1/DPn,0"
- Interpret：SAP 中交联剂（如 MTEC，三官能度 f = 3）的添加量决定凝胶点；f = 3 → pgel = 0.5，即转化率超过 50% 时形成凝胶网络
- Apply：

- SAP 生产中：通过精确控制交联剂浓度（通常 < 0.5 mol%）将凝胶点推到高转化率附近，使线性聚丙烯酸链充分增长后再凝胶——最大化溶胀空间
- 交联剂选择：f = 3（三元丙烯酸酯）vs f = 4（四元交联剂）决定凝胶点位置；f 越大凝胶点越早（pgel 越小），网络越致密
### 卡片 2：PP 卫材的降解机制与抗老化配方

- Read：原文 "In depolymerization monomer is split off from an activated end group... referred to as 'unzipping.'" vs "Chain scission is similar to the opposite of stepwise polycondensation where units are split apart in a random manner."
- Interpret：PP 属"随机链断裂"型聚合物（不像 PMMA 那样 unzipping）；在紫外/热氧下发生 C–C 主链断裂 + 叔碳氢自由基链式氧化，导致分子量下降、脆化、黄变
- Apply：

- PP 无纺布货架期配方：主抗氧剂（BHT/1010）+ 副抗氧剂（168）+ UV 吸收剂（2-hydroxybenzophenone/benzotriazole）三重防护
- 与 Ch.14 §14.4 联用：PP 户外制品（婴儿车篷、户外地垫）需 > 100,000 吨级 UV 稳定剂配方
### 卡片 3：ESC——卫材薄膜在清洁剂/表面活性剂下的失效

- Read：原文 "Aqueous solutions of surface-active materials, such as detergents, can produce brittle cracking, particularly in stressed materials" + "possibly by plasticization of the stressed material or modification of the surface energy"
- Interpret：ESC 的关键在于"不是直接化学攻击"，而是表面活性剂"吸收/局部溶解于缺陷处"，通过塑化或表面能降低辅助断裂
- Apply：

- 湿巾塑料盖/防水膜在使用场景中直接接触表面活性剂——需选 ESC 抗性好的材料（HDPE 优于 LDPE，HDPE 优于 PP）
- 卫材热封条（PE）长期受表面活性剂浸泡的失效模式是 ESC，而非化学降解——配方需避免"链端应力集中"、增加结晶度
### 卡片 4：crazing vs shear yielding——韧性设计的科学依据

- Read：原文 "In shear yielding, oriented regions are formed at 45° angles to the stress. No void space is produced in shear yielding." vs "A crack is an open fissure, whereas a craze is spanned top to bottom by fibrils"
- Interpret：韧性聚合物通过 shear yielding 分散能量（无空穴）；脆性聚合物则发生 crazing（含微空洞，成为裂纹前驱体）
- Apply：

- 婴儿用品外壳（需抗冲）：应通过抗冲改性剂（§14.12 ABS/SBS/EVA/CPE）引入剪切屈服相，避免银纹化
- 卫材中的"应力集中"部位（腰围搭扣、湿巾翻盖铰链）需增加分子链缠结密度，促进 shear yielding 而非 crazing
### 卡片 5：macromer / telechelic 在功能聚合物设计中的应用

- Read：原文 macromer/telechelic 定义与 "Reactive group—MMMMMMM—reactive group"
- Interpret：链端官能化的"短聚合物"可作为"分子砖块"合成嵌段共聚物、接枝聚合物或功能交联剂
- Apply：

- SAP 中功能端基修饰：使用 telechelic 型丙烯酸低聚物作为"分子桥"，一端接主链、一端接抗菌基团/香精微胶囊
- 湿巾柔软剂接枝：以 macromer（一端活性）接枝到纤维素纳米纤维上
