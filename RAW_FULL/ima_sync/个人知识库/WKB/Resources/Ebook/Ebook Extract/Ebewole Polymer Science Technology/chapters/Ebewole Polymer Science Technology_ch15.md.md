# 第 15 章 · Polymer Properties and Applications（聚合物性能与应用）

## 一、执行摘要（Executive Summary）

本章是全书的"应用词典"，按聚合物族逐族列出结构特征、关键性能（Tg/Tm/强度）、加工方式、典型应用。覆盖 20+ 聚合物家族：PE（LD/HD/LL/Ultra-HD）、PP、PS/GP-PS/HIPS/SAN、PVC/CPVC、PMMA、PAN、PVC、ABS、PA（Nylon 6/6,6/4,6/6,10 等）、PC、PPO、PPS、PET/PBT、PTFE、NBR、PU、TPE、离子聚合物。

业务定位：卫材非织造布主要基于 PP、PE、PET、PAN、PVA、PU、PTFE——本章为每种原料的结构-性能-加工-应用链条提供原书级原始参考，是选型决策的第一手依据。

## 三、原文摘选与批注（English Excerpts with Citations）

### 3.1 Polyethylene（p. 418）

"Low-density polyethylene is a partially crystalline solid with a degree of crystallinity in the 50 to 70% range, melting temperature of 100 to 120°C, and specific gravity of about 0.91 to 0.94."

"High-density polyethylene, on the other hand, has few side chains, typically 1 per 200 carbon main chain atoms. Linear polyethylenes are highly crystalline, with a melting point over 127°C — usually about 135°C."

### 3.2 Polypropylene（p. 422）

"Isotactic polypropylene is essentially linear, with an ordered arrangement of propylene molecules in the polymer chain."

"Unlike polyethylene, isotactic polyethylene does not crystallize in a planar zigzag conformation due to steric hindrance from the relatively bulky methyl group."（*注：此处原文应为 isotactic polypropylene*）

"Isotactic polypropylene crystallizes with three methylene units per turn of the helix."

"Nonwovens … are important applications."（Table 15.3, p. 426）

### 3.3 PVC（p. 427）

"Unstabilized PVC undergoes dehydrochlorination when heated above its Tg (about 87°C) — for example, during melt processing."

### 3.4 PAN（p. 431）

"Acrylic and modacrylic fibers … have more than 85% acrylonitrile content."

"PAN … strong hydrogen bonding, stiff rod-like chains" — 见第 5 章引述。

"PAN is the starting material for carbon fiber production."

### 3.5 ABS（p. 432）

"ABS consists of a continuous SAN matrix with dispersed rubber (polybutadiene) domains."

"Toughness is achieved by the impact of rubber particles on the matrix — crack termination at the rubber particles."

### 3.6 Nylon / Polyamides（p. 436）

"Nylon 6,6 has a higher melting point (265°C) than Nylon 6 (220°C) due to the stronger hydrogen bonding between chains."

"Moisture absorption in nylons decreases with increasing hydrocarbon content between amide groups."

### 3.7 Polycarbonate（p. 437）

"PC is produced by interfacial polycondensation."

"PC is a glassy, amorphous polymer with excellent clarity, high impact strength, and good heat resistance."

### 3.8 PET / PBT（p. 443）

"PET is produced by direct esterification or transesterification followed by polycondensation."

"PET is a semicrystalline polymer with excellent mechanical properties and chemical resistance."

"PET is the primary fiber for textile and tire cord applications."

### 3.9 Fluoropolymers — PTFE（p. 444）

"PTFE has an extremely high melting point (327°C), is highly crystalline, and has exceptional chemical resistance."

### 3.10 Polyurethanes（p. 447）

"PU is a family of polymers formed by the reaction of diisocyanates with polyols."

"PU foams, elastomers, fibers, coatings, and adhesives" — 全线应用。

### 3.11 Thermoplastic Elastomers（p. 448–450）

"TPE's consist of block copolymers with hard and soft segments."

"Thermoplastic polyamides are a relatively new class of block-copolymer thermoplastic elastomers. They have amide linkages between the hard and soft segments. This class of thermoplastic elastomers has the highest performance and is the most expensive of the TPEs."

### 3.12 结尾段（Unsat. Network Polyester, p. 449）

"Unsaturated network polyesters may be produced by using an unsaturated dibasic acid or glycol or both."

"This prepolymer is then dissolved in styrene monomer, which participates in cross-linking with the double bonds in the prepolymer."

## 七、业务价值判断（Business Relevance — 卫材非织造布）

直接相关性（高）
- PP（Table 15.3, p. 426）：原书明确列出 "Nonwovens … are important applications"——卫材非织造布第一大原料。
- PET（p. 443）："primary fiber for textile and tire cord applications"；纺粘 PET 在湿巾、包装中大量使用。
- PAN（p. 431）：腈纶纤维——卫材保暖层（仿羊毛）、碳纤维前驱。
- PE（LD/HD）：纺粘 PE 用于低熔点共混层；HDPE 硬挺包装层。
- PU（p. 447）：非织造布粘合胶、弹性组件、涂层；TPU 用于高弹性卫材。
- PTFE（p. 444）：高性能过滤膜（疏水/化学稳定）——N95 滤材增强层。

间接相关性
- PVC（p. 427）：部分医用一次性手套、导管、防护服外壳（但 Tg 87°C 处需添加热稳定剂）。
- Nylon 6,6（p. 436）：高端缝合线、高性能织物——部分医用纺织品。

关键警示
- PVC 在 87°C 以上脱 HCl（"Unstabilized PVC undergoes dehydrochlorination"）——纺丝/加工温度需严格控制并添加热稳定剂。
- PA 吸湿性（p. 436）：非织造布在潮湿环境下的尺寸稳定性需评估。
- 本章未覆盖 PVA（聚乙烯醇）、EVA、POE 等卫材常用共混原料——需补读。
