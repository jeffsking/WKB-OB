## 一、执行摘要（Executive Summary）

本章覆盖聚合物从颗粒到最终制品的主流单元加工操作。原书自我定位："only extrusion and injection molding, the two predominant polymer processing methods, are treated in fairly great detail"（p. 282）。七大加工路线：Extrusion（挤出）、Injection Molding（注塑）、Blow Molding（吹塑）、Rotomolding（滚塑）、Thermoforming（热成型）、Compression/Transfer Molding（压缩/传递模塑）、Casting（铸造）。

业务提示：本章对非织造布核心工艺（spinbond melt spinning、meltblown、solvent-wet/dry spinning、calendering）仅隐含性提及（fiber extrusion 作为挤出产品、parison/bubble 作为吹塑内容）。这些缺口需在批注中标注 [未覆盖]，以避免误读。

## 三、原文摘选与批注（English Excerpts with Citations）

### 3.1 概览

"The choice of a particular processing technique is determined by part design, choice of material, production requirements, and, ultimately, cost–performance considerations."（p. 282）

"Extrusion is a processing technique for converting thermoplastic materials in powdered or granular form into a continuous uniform melt, which is shaped into items of uniform cross-sectional area by forcing it through a die."（p. 282）

### 3.2 挤出

"The screw, which is the moving part of the extruder, is designed to pick up, mix, compress, and move the polymer as it changes from solid granules to a viscous melt."（p. 283）

"The frictional force generated between the resin, the barrel wall, and the rotating screw (viscous energy dissipation) is an important energy source for melting the resin."（p. 284）

"Polymer melts, being viscoelastic, recover the stored elastic energy as they emerge from the die — a process known as die swell."（p. 285）

"Fibers of various gauges and lengths are obtained in fiber extrusion. These can range from monofilament such as fishing lines to hundreds of continuous filaments extruded from the same die and drawn to hair-sized thickness."（p. 285）

"The continuous filaments may be crimped for added bulk or made into staples."（p. 285）

"In coextrusion, two or more different materials or the same material with two different colors are extruded through the same die so that one material flows over and coats the other."（p. 285）

"In general, a high-speed, high-temperature process provides a more uniform melt than a low-speed, low-temperature process."（p. 285）

### 3.3 注塑

"Injection molding is a batch molding process in which a thermoplastic resin or a thermoset material is melted or plasticized, and then injected at high pressure into a closed, cooled mold."（p. 287）

"The plasticizing capacity of an injection molding machine is defined as the maximum weight of polymer that can be melted, accumulated, and stored in the barrel of the machine."（p. 291）

"The screw retracts into a position that allows a predetermined amount of melt to accumulate."（p. 291）

"Melt temperature must be sufficiently high to ensure that complete fusion of the polymer particles has been achieved."（p. 291）

"Melt temperatures in excess of this limit will cause the polymer to degrade."（p. 291）

"Polymer molecules generally assume a random orientation in the melt. As the melt is forced through the gate during mold filling, polymer molecules tend to lose this random orientation and align themselves along the direction of flow."（p. 296）

### 3.4 吹塑

"The blow molding process consists of a sequence of steps leading to the production of a hollow tube or parison from a molten thermoplastic resin. This is then entrapped between the two halves of a mold of the desired shape."（p. 298）

"(a) The molten, hollow tube — the parison or preform — is placed between the halves of the mold … (c) the parison, still molten, is pinched off and inflated by an air blast that forces its wall against the inside contours of the cooled mold."（Fig. 11.10 caption, p. 299）

"During blow molding, the polymer is stretched biaxially — in the axial and radial directions — causing the polymer molecules to align in a biaxial orientation."（p. 299）

### 3.5 滚塑

"Rotational molding (rotomolding) is used primarily for the manufacture of hollow articles that are not of complex shapes."（p. 300）

"While the heating cycle has virtually no effect on the properties of the finished part, the cooling rate determines part shrinkage, final density, brittleness, and other physical properties."（p. 300）

"A longer cooling time permits a greater ordered molecular arrangement in the crystalline polymer. The resultant enhanced crystallinity leads to higher specific gravity and shrinkage and reduced brittleness."（p. 300–301）

### 3.6 热成型

"In thermoforming, a thermoplastic sheet is heated to a pliable temperature and then formed against or into a mold."（p. 301）

"The basic difference between these methods is the means by which the sheet is drawn into the mold cavity."（p. 301）

### 3.7 压缩/传递模塑

"Compression molding involves placing a predetermined quantity of uncured thermosetting compound or a thermoplastic material in the cavity of an open mold, closing the mold under pressure, and heating to effect the flow and cure of the resin."（p. 304）

"Compression molding is limited to the fabrication of small parts and low production volumes because the process is slow."（p. 304）

"In transfer molding, the uncured thermosetting material is first plasticized in a hot chamber and then forced under pressure into a preheated closed mold."（p. 305）

### 3.8 铸造（含尼龙铸造）

"In the casting process, a liquid material is poured into a mold and allowed to solidify by physical (e.g., cooling) or chemical (e.g., polymerization) means resulting in a rigid object that generally reproduces the mold cavity detail with great fidelity."（p. 305）

"Cocatalyzed anionic polymerization is currently the most widely used nylon casting method."（p. 306）

## 七、业务价值判断（Business Relevance — 卫材非织造布）

直接相关性
- Fiber extrusion（p. 285）："hundreds of continuous filaments … drawn to hair-sized thickness"直接描述 spinbond 熔体纺丝的硬件基础。"Crimped for added bulk or made into staples"直接对应短纤（staple fiber）→ 针/punch 非织造布路线。
- Die swell（p. 285）：模口膨胀控制对熔喷纺丝嘴（meltblown die）孔尺寸设计至关重要。
- Biaxial orientation（p. 299）：吹塑中的双轴取向原理等价于非织造布热定型工序的取向机理。
- Plasticizing capacity / residence time（p. 291–293）：直接用于评估纺丝用螺杆挤出机的熔体均匀性，影响纺丝稳定性与纤维直径分布。
- Coextrusion（p. 285）：共挤出用于非织造布双组分纤维（sheath-core、side-by-side）纺丝——用于热复合（thermobonding）短纤。

业务警示
- 本章未覆盖 spinbond/meltblown 工艺细节、拉伸比、冷却气流、热辊定型等非织造专有参数——需配合专用非织造文献。
- 原书聚焦刚性制品，卫材柔性多层复合的加工经验需额外来源。
