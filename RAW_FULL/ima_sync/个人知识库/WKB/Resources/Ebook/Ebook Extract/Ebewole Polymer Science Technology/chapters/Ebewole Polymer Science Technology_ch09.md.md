## 一、执行摘要（Executive Summary）

本章阐述聚合物「非纯态使用」的工程现实："Very few polymers are used technologically in their chemically pure form"（p. 228）。全章围绕五大助剂/改性体系展开：Plasticizers（增塑剂）、Fillers and Reinforcements（填充与增强体）、Alloys and Blends（聚合物合金与共混）、Antioxidants and Thermal/UV Stabilizers（抗氧化剂与热/光稳定剂）、Flame Retardants（阻燃剂）。每条主线均以原书定义、机理解释、商业化实例三层次展开。工程价值在于回答"给定聚合物如何达到具体应用性能要求"，是连接聚合机理（前 8 章）与性能-应用（第 13–15 章）之间的关键桥梁。

## 三、原文摘选与批注（English Excerpts with Citations）

### 3.1 开篇：聚合物为何需助剂（Introduction）

"Very few polymers are used technologically in their chemically pure form; it is generally necessary to modify their behavior by the incorporation of additives."（p. 228）

"Additives are usually required to impart stability against the degradative effects of various kinds of aging processes and enhance product quality and performance."（p. 228）

"Nonreinforcing fillers are employed in large quantities to reduce overall formulation costs provided this does not result in significant or undesirable reduction in product quality or performance."（p. 228）

批注：开篇即划定助剂的功能边界——稳定化、性能增强、成本降低。

### 3.2 Plasticizers（增塑剂）

"The principal function of a plasticizer is to reduce the Tg of a polymer so as to enhance its flexibility over expected temperatures of application."（p. 228）

"The plasticizer molecules are inserted between the polymer molecules thereby pushing them apart. This reduces the intensity of the intermolecular cohesive forces."（p. 228）

"Internal plasticization involves copolymerization of the monomers of the desired polymer and that of the plasticizer so that the plasticizer is an integral part of the polymer chain."（p. 228）

"Phthalate, terephthalate, adipate, and phosphate esters are generally referred to as monomeric plasticizers."（p. 229）

"Linear polyesters … constitute the group of polymeric or permanent plasticizers. They have much higher molecular weights than the monomerics and as such are less volatile … less susceptible to migration, and less extractible."（p. 229）

"The ideal plasticizer must satisfy three principal requirements. These are compatibility, performance, and efficiency."（p. 229）

### 3.3 Fillers and Reinforcements（填充与增强体）

"Fillers or reinforcing agents may be incorporated in small amounts to modify processing or to improve mechanical or physical properties."（p. 229）

"The properties of filled polymers or composites are strongly dependent on the shape of the filler particles, their concentration in the matrix, their orientation within the matrix, the particle size distribution, and the interfacial bonding."（p. 229）

"Fillers of greater length and aspect ratio are much more efficient in reinforcing than fibers of shorter length or spherical particles."（p. 230）

"The principal advantage of reinforcing fillers is the high strength-to-weight ratio that is obtained when they are used in conjunction with a polymer matrix."（p. 230）

"The surface of the filler particles must be chemically bonded to the matrix … Coupling agents are used to provide such interfacial bonding."（p. 232）

"Silanes … are the most widely used coupling agents for glass and mineral fibers. … Titanates are similar to silanes but offer wider processing latitude."（p. 233）

"The properties of the composite are a function of the properties of the components, the volume fraction of each, and the state of adhesion between them."（p. 233）

### 3.4 Alloys and Blends（合金与共混）

"The word alloy refers to a combination of two or more polymeric components that exhibits a synergy of properties."（p. 235）

"A blend refers to a combination of two or more polymeric components in which the properties of the blend are equal to or less than the arithmetic average of the properties of the components."（p. 235）

"The miscibility of two polymers depends on the free energy of mixing."（p. 235）

"Polymers, in general, tend to be immiscible. A miscible polymer mixture is usually the exception rather than the rule."（p. 236）

"To achieve a true alloy, the interacting polymer chains must be intimately mixed and molecularly dispersed in the form of a homogeneous melt, a homogeneous solution, or a homogeneous solid."（p. 236）

"Compatibilization refers to the process of improving the interfacial adhesion between the components of a blend or alloy."（p. 236）

### 3.5 Polymer Degradation Mechanisms（降解机理）

"Polymer degradation is the deterioration of the properties of a polymer due to chemical or physical change during processing, storage, or end use."（p. 238）

"Nonchain-scission reactions involve the elimination of a small molecule from the polymer, leaving the polymer backbone essentially unchanged."（p. 238）

"In chain-scission reactions, bonds along the polymer backbone undergo homolytic cleavage, and radicals are generated."（p. 238）

"Oxidative degradation is initiated by free-radical attack by oxygen, producing peroxy radicals."（p. 239）

### 3.6 Stabilizers（稳定剂）

"Antioxidants are used to inhibit or retard the rate of polymer degradation in those instances in which oxygen is involved in the degradation process."（p. 239）

"Primary or chain-breaking antioxidants inhibit the propagation step of the free-radical chain reaction by reacting with the growing free radicals."（p. 239）

"Secondary or preventive antioxidants inhibit oxidative degradation by decomposing hydroperoxides before they decompose to initiate free-radical reactions."（p. 239）

"UV absorbers function by absorbing the harmful ultraviolet radiation."（p. 239）

"Quenchers function by dissipating the absorbed energy through nonproductive pathways."（p. 239）

### 3.7 Flame Retardants（阻燃剂）

"Flame retardants are additives that increase the resistance of a polymer to ignition and slow down the rate of burning."（p. 240）

"The halogens are most effective in the vapor phase; they act in the flame zone by forming a blanket of halogen vapor that interferes with the propagation of the flame."（p. 240）

"Others such as phosphorus or boron operate in the condensed or solid phase, minimizing the availability of fresh fuel."（p. 240）

"Alumina trihydrate, which dominates the flame retardant market in terms of sales volume, serves this function."（p. 240）

"In reactive flame retardants, retardancy is built into the polymer chain through appropriate polymerization or postpolymerization techniques."（p. 240）

"Intumescent flame retardance is based on the formation of a char on the surface of the resin on the application of heat, consequently insulating the substrate from further heat and flame."（p. 241）

## 七、业务价值判断（Business Relevance — 卫材非织造布评估）

直接相关性
- Plasticizer / Compatibilizer 体系：在卫材（卫生用品）中的 PP/PE/PU 复合层、涂布层广泛使用；增塑剂迁移、可萃取性直接关系到人体接触合规性（"less susceptible to migration, and less extractible"）。
- Flame retardants：部分高端卫材/医用纺织品需满足阻燃规范；需权衡阻燃剂的可迁移性。
- Antioxidants / UV stabilizers：户外一次性/半永久性非织造布（土工布、防护服外壳）的关键助剂。

间接相关性
- Coupling agents：玻璃纤维、矿物填充 PP（如 PP/滑石粉）在卫材的硬质包装、背板中常见。
- Filler 分类：CaCO₃、滑石粉等 non-reinforcing fillers 用于 PP/PE 非织造原料的成本控制。

关键缺失 / 需补：本章未覆盖非织造布专用的 spinfinish（纺丝油剂）、hydroentangling/thermobonding 助剂——这些见第 11 章及专用纺粘/熔喷文献。
