||||||
|---|---|---|---|---|

## B 组 · 链式聚合机理（Ch.2 / Ch.7）

||||||
|---|---|---|---|---|
|B1|Chain-reaction polymerization|链式聚合|"the addition of unsaturated molecules to a rapidly growing chain"|Ch.2, p.26|
|B2|Initiation|引发|"Initiation involves the acquisition of an active site by the monomer."|Ch.2, p.26|
|B3|Propagation|链增长|"the addition of a free radical to the double bond of a monomer, with regeneration of another radical. The active center is thus continuously relocated at the end of the growing polymer chain"|Ch.2, p.27|
|B4|Termination|终止|"the growth activity of a polymer chain radical is destroyed by reaction with another free radical in the system"|Ch.2, p.29|
|B5|Chain transfer|链转移|"a growing polymer chain is deactivated or terminated by transferring its growth activity to a previously inactive species"|Ch.2, p.29|
|B6|Initiator|引发剂|"A weak organic compound that can be decomposed thermally or by irradiation to produce free radicals. Initiators are consumed in the reaction while catalysts are regenerated after the reaction."|Ch.2, p.26 / Ch.7, p.182|
|B7|Free radical|自由基|"molecules containing atoms with unpaired electrons"|Ch.2, p.26|
|B8|Redox initiation|氧化还原引发|"the decomposition of peroxide or persulfate is greatly accelerated by the presence of a reducing system"|Ch.2, p.26|
|B9|Combination (coupling)|组合终止|"two growing polymer chains react with the mutual destruction of growth activity"|Ch.2, p.29|
|B10|Disproportionation|歧化终止|"a labile atom (usually hydrogen) is transferred from one polymer radical to another"|Ch.2, p.29|
|B11|Head-to-tail linkage|头-尾连接|"head-to-tail linkages in which the substituents occur on alternate carbon atoms"|Ch.2, p.28|
|B12|1,4-addition (cis / trans)|1,4 加成|"In 1,4 addition, however, the unsaturation is part of the backbone. Two configurations are possible — trans or cis."|Ch.7, p.184|
|B13|Active center|活性中心|"may be free radicals, carbanions, carbonium ions, or coordination complexes"|Ch.7, p.182|
|B14|Vinyl monomer|乙烯基单体|"These compounds constitute the so-called vinyl monomers where X and Y may be halogen, alkyl, ester, phenyl, or other groups."|Ch.7, p.182|
|B15|Ziegler–Natta catalyst|齐格勒-纳塔催化剂|"Stereoregularity can also be controlled by the use of coordination catalysts like Ziegler–Natta catalysts."|Ch.2, p.30|

## C 组 · 缩聚机理（Ch.6）

||||||
|---|---|---|---|---|
|C1|Condensation polymerization|缩聚反应|"Condensation polymerization is chemically the same as a condensation reaction that produces a small organic molecule. … the functionality of reactants must be at least 2."|Ch.6, p.161|
|C2|Step-reaction polymerization|逐步聚合|"polymerization processes are classified as step-reaction (condensation) or chain-reaction (addition) polymerization"|Ch.2, p.26|
|C3|Flory equal reactivity principle|弗洛里等反应性原理|"the intrinsic reactivity of all functional groups is constant, independent of the molecular size"|Ch.6, p.162|
|C4|Extent of reaction (p)|反应程度|"the fraction of the functional group that has reacted at time t"|Ch.6, p.163|
|C5|DP = 1/(1–p)|聚合度方程|"The quantity 1/(1–p) is called the degree of polymerization, DP"|Ch.6, p.164|
|C6|Third-order kinetics|三阶动力学|"In the absence of an added strong acid, a second molecule of the acid being esterified acts as the catalyst."|Ch.6, p.163|
|C7|Second-order kinetics|二阶动力学|"The kinetic expression can be greatly simplified if the polyesterification is carried out in the presence of a small amount of strong acid."|Ch.6, p.164|
|C8|Polycondensation|聚缩反应|"polycondensation (production of a macromolecule)"|Ch.6, p.161|

## D 组 · 共聚机理（Ch.8）

||||||
|---|---|---|---|---|
|D1|Copolymerization|共聚反应|"the simultaneous polymerization of two or more monomers"|Ch.8, p.211|
|D2|Reactivity ratio (r₁, r₂)|竞聚率|"r₁ = k₁₁/k₁₂, r₂ = k₂₂/k₂₁"|Ch.8, p.213 (Eq. 8.6)|
|D3|Copolymer equation (Mayo–Lewis)|共聚方程|"Equation 8.5 is the copolymer equation"（Eq. 8.9）|Ch.8, p.213|
|D4|F₁ / F₂|瞬时聚合物组成|"the mole fractions of monomers M₁ and M₂ in the increment of polymer formed at any instant"|Ch.8, p.213|
|D5|f₁ / f₂|瞬时供料组成|"the mole fractions of unreacted M₁ and M₂ in the monomer feed"|Ch.8, p.213|
|D6|Ideal copolymerization (r₁r₂ = 1)|理想共聚|"each radical displays the same preference for adding one monomer over the other … the sequence of monomer units in an ideal copolymer is necessarily random"|Ch.8, p.213|
|D7|Alternating copolymerization (r₁ = r₂ = 0)|交替共聚|"each radical reacts exclusively with the other monomer; that is neither radical can regenerate itself"|Ch.8, p.214|
|D8|Block copolymerization (r₁ > 1, r₂ > 1)|嵌段共聚|"each radical would prefer adding its own monomer"（"there are no established cases where r₁r₂ > 1"）|Ch.8, p.214|
|D9|Composition drift|组成漂移|"the composition of the feed, f₁, and that of the polymer, F₁, is neither equal nor constant throughout the polymerization"|Ch.8, p.215|
|D10|Azeotropic copolymerization|恒比共聚|"polymerization proceeds without a change in composition"（F₁ = f₁）|Ch.8, p.215|
|D11|Random / Block / Graft copolymer|随机/嵌段/接枝共聚物|"random copolymer exhibits a homogeneous single phase … block or graft copolymers … form heterogeneous multiphase systems"|Ch.5, p.119|

## E 组 · 结构与热行为（Ch.3 / Ch.4）

||||||
|---|---|---|---|---|
|E1|Glass transition temperature (Tg)|玻璃化转变温度|"the glass transition temperature, Tg"——聚合物物理性质发生急剧变化的特征温度|Ch.4, p.96|
|E2|Crystalline melting point (Tm)|结晶熔点|"The equilibrium crystalline melting point, Tm, for polymers corresponds to the temperature at which the last crystallite starts melting."|Ch.4, p.96|
|E3|Cohesive energy density (CED)|凝聚能密度|"the presence of strong intermolecular bonds in a polymer chain, i.e., a high value of cohesive energy density, will significantly increase Tg"|Ch.4, p.100|
|E4|Chain flexibility|链柔性|"Chain flexibility is determined by the ease with which rotation occurs about primary valence bonds."|Ch.4, p.100|
|E5|Free volume|自由体积|"The increased free volume results in a lower Tg."|Ch.4, p.100|
|E6|Steric hindrance|空间位阻|"Bulky side groups that are stiff and close to the backbone cause steric hindrance, decrease chain mobility, and hence raise Tg."|Ch.4, p.100|
|E7|Cis–trans configuration|顺反构型|"Double bonds in the cis form reduce the energy barrier for rotation of adjacent bonds, 'soften' the chain, and hence reduce Tg."|Ch.4, p.100|
|E8|Ionomer|离聚物|"The relatively new class of polymers known as ionomers contain ionic bonds"|Ch.3, p.61|
|E9|Dissociation energy|解离能|"that required to break the bond. It has a direct relationship with the thermal stability of polymers."|Ch.3, p.61|
