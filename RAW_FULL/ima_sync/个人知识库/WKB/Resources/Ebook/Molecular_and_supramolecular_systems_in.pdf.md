# Molecular and supramolecular systems in photochromism: Dihydroindolizines - New versatile molecules

H DLrRR*, C KRANZ, C SCHULZ, H KILBURG and H P JONSSON

FR 11.2, Organische Chemie, Universit~it des Saarlandes, D-66041 Saarbriicken, Germany

Abstract. A short summary of the known photochromic molecules is given. An  approach to the new class of photochromic 5-membered heterocycles, such as  dihydroindolizines (DHI), is presented. The important parameters of the photochromic 1,5-electrocyclization and its reverse of DHI's are discussed. Special DHI's  can undergo a photoreversible ring closure from the colored betaines to the  uncolored DHI. This reaction can be employed in photoswitches. Supramolecular  effects on reactivity of DHI's are studied with especially tailored DHI allowing for  host-guest-chemistry. An important commercial application of spiroxazines in  ophthalmic lenses is discussed.

Keywords. Photochromic dihydroindolizines; photochromic aza-heterocycles; photoswitches; supramolecular assemblies; host-guest-chemistry; photochromic  ophthalmic lenses.

Introduction

Photochromic molecules have received growing interest in the recent years (Diirr and  Bouas-Laurent 1990). With the development of new photochromic molecules (Diirr 1989) and the optimization of known systems such as spiroxazines (Mac Ardle 1992), being  commercially used in ophthalmic lenses, an important impetus was brought to the field.

The creation of highly photostable photochromic molecules in the series of hexatriene  derivatives (Irie and Kato 1985; Irie et al 1992) has been a large step forward for  information recording and information storage. The use of photochromic materials  such as bacteriorhodopsin in holography (Hampp and Br/iuchle 1990) and organic dyes in  hole burning (Wild 1990; Wild et al 1992) is in the same domain. Photoswitchable  photochromics were used in biosensors (WiUner and Rubin 1992) with various molecules.

In this paper, which is based on a talk, a short survey is made of the known  photochromic systems, preparation and properties of a new photochromic system, dihydroindolizines (DHI) and related compounds, photoswitches, supramolecular  photochromic assemblies and commercial application of spiroxazines. Photochromic  systems can involve unimolecular or bimolecular structural change (Diirr 1989).

Unimolecular photochromic systems can be roughly divided into six classes of  property or structural changes:

(1) singlet-triplet systems,

(2) cis-trans-isomerization,

(3) pericyclic processes,

# Structure-reactivity relationship

In order to establish a structure-reactivity relationship the DHI-molecule is divided into 3 regions A, B and C. To elucidate such a relationship as a typical parameter $\tau_{1/2} = \ln(2/k)$ ($k$ = rate of thermal back-reaction) is selected. Scheme 3 shows that on going from right to left with regard to substructures or substituents in regions A, B and C (in scheme 3), an increase in $\tau_{1/2}$ is observed.

The electrocyclic reaction of DHI's is however not limited to the pyrrolin ring only. According to Huisgen (1980) the ring-open form or betaine is classified with regard to the position of the heteroatom in the aza-pentadiene structure. Thus the dihydroindolizines belong to type-2 molecules cyclizing to a pyrrolin ring. We could show in our work that type-2, type-3, type-1, 2, type-2, 3 and type-1, 2, 3 systems are photochromic when properly substituted (Dürr and Bouas-Laurent 1990) affording the corresponding mono- or poly-aza 5-membered heterocycle (scheme 4).

# Photoswitches

Some examples of photochromic spirodihydroindolizines such as the dicyano-DHI derivatives exhibit special behavior (Schulz 1989). If 2,3-dicyano-dibenzo-dihydroindolizine 1b is irradiated with light $\lambda \sim 410$ nm it colorizes easily. The purple betaine 2b shows a half-life of $\tau_{1/2} = 213$ min. Irradiation reconverts this system back to the uncolored form 1b. The aza-derivative 2c behaves similarly (figure 1).

# Supramolecular assemblies

Supramolecular assemblies (Lehn 1988) can be employed to tune molecular or spectral properties in a selective way. We have tailored our molecules to allow for host-guest interactions. As anchor groups we chose polyethylene-glycol chains. Thus molecules of types A and B were designed.

The spectra of the uncolored form of NISO and those of the colored merocyanine form are presented in figure  5 . The absorption of the latter shows a maximum at about 610nm, resulting in the blue color of the merocyanine. Two firms-Rodenstock in Germany and Transitions in the USA -are using these or related spiroxazines in their photochromic lenses. The darkening and the bleaching curves of plastic lenses are shown in figures 6 and 7. These photochromic lenses are available in the market today. They are important products and demonstrate the use of photochromic molecules in commercial systems.

Abstract

Photochromic molecules present a high degree of interest both for basic and applied research. The development of a new class of photochromics, the dihydroindolizines (DHI) and related molecules, enlarges the field of well-studied photochromic materials and indicates a series of potential uses. With the advent of photochromic ophthalmic lenses a new era of commercializing photochromes on a large scale has begun.
