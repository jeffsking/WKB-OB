# MAXWELL MODEL

Maxwell was interested in creep and stress relaxation and developed a differential equation to describe these properties

Maxwell started with Hooke's law

$$\sigma = E\varepsilon,$$

Then allowed $\sigma$ to vary with $t$ time

$$ \begin{array}{c}d\boldsymbol{\sigma}\\\overline{d} t\end{array}=\mathrm{E}\begin{array}{c}d\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}\\\overline{d} t\end{array} $$

Writing for a Newtonian fluid

$$\sigma = \eta \frac{d\varepsilon}{dt}$$

Then assuming that the rate of strain is simply a sum of these two contributions

$$\frac{d\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}}{\bar{d} t} = \frac{\boldsymbol{\sigma}}{\bar{\boldsymbol{\eta}}} + \frac{1}{\bar{\mathbf{E}}}\frac{d\boldsymbol{\sigma}}{\bar{d} t}$$
