#   Absorbent system comprised of:

[图片]
$\bullet$ Liner: Baby-side material that covers the internal components of the diaper

$\bullet$ Surge - Acquisition & Distribution Layer (ADL): Lofty material under the liner to help fluid intake and transfer to the absorbent pad (LM+ NB-2 has a textured surge on top of the liner)

$\bullet$ Core Wrap/Spacer Layer: Wrap around the pad for help with skin health, fluid intake, and outer cover dampness

$\bullet$ Absorbent Pad: Made of SAM (superabsorbent material) and fluff (pulp) to absorb and distribute urine

#   Body Side Liner Material

* Dryness and SAM containment

* Soft/Dry layer against skin

* Barrier preventing SAM from getting on skin

* Do not impede fluid access to the absorbent core

* Little/no retention capability

$\bullet$ Spunbond-Melblown-Spunbond (SMS), Bonded Carded Web (BCW), Spunbond (SB)

* SMS is common – very good SAM barrier properties, low cost

* BCW or other textured liners for softness, aesthetics, dryness or BM handling

$\bullet$ Treated for wettability

$\bullet$ Pancake rolls

$\bullet$ Internal and external sourcing

$\bullet$ Recommended storage limit of ~1 year

#   Surge

* Fluid intake and dryness

* Resilient

* Fast intake and quick desorption to absorbent core

* Little/no retention capability

* Normally Through Air BCW (TABCW) or BCW

* Staple fibers (1-3" long)

* Primarily synthetic fibers (Polyester, Polypropylene, Bicomponent)

* Can contain natural fibers (cotton, wool)

* Fibers treated for wettability and/or bonding

$\bullet$ 50 – 130 gsm for BCC product forms

$\bullet$ Ships in Spools

$\bullet$ Internally and externally produced

* Berkeley, KC Mexico

* Shalag, TEXTOR, Freudenberg, PGI, Tenotex

$\bullet$ Recommended storage limit of ~1 year

#   Wrapsheet/Spacer Layer

* SAM containment and process aid

* Contains SAM and Fluff during usage to prevent gel on skin

* Protects the outer cover from SAM poke-through and dampness of
  breathable outer covers

* Can aid in transferring the absorbent off the drum in 'form-on substrate' processes

$\bullet$ Minimal basis weight

* SMS (BCC), treated for wettability

* Tissue (AFC)

$\bullet$ Treated for wettability

$\bullet$ Pancake rolls

$\bullet$ Internal and external suppliers

$\bullet$ Recommended storage limit of ~ 1 year

$\bullet$ Properties optimized for different applications

* Cross-linking: Controls resistance to deformation

* Surface treatments: Improve permeability, flowability (anti-caking), intake speed and other properties

* “Locks up” fluid better than pulp

* Swelling opens absorbent core structure

$\bullet$ External supply
