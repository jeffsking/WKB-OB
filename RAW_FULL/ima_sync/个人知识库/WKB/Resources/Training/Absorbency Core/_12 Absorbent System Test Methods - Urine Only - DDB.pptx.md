#   Void Volume – Pore Space Modulus

#   Test Description:

Measures the resiliency of the absorbent core and intake system materials after exposure to multiple compression cycles.

#   Output:

Specimen thickness as a function of pressure

* Output can be post-processed to determine void volume

#   Key Uses:

$\bullet$ Absorbent modeling inputs

$\bullet$ Determine the impact of material changes on void volume.

Reference: TTM-00038, TTM-00437

#   Capacity – Retention Capacity

#   Test Description:

Measures the total capacity of the absorbent system materials under free-swell conditions.

#   Output:

Capacity values in grams absorbed or grams absorbed/gram of material.

#   Key Uses:

$\bullet$ Verification of absorbent amounts

$\bullet$ Capacity of designated areas of the system (with x-ray)

Reference: STM-00033, STM-2415

#   Intake – Cradle Intake

#   Test Description:

Measures the intake time of multiple liquid insults in a non-horizontal configuration to account for gravity/pooling/free liquid stream.

#   Output:

Intake times in seconds

Rewet, in grams, can be an additional output (after last insult)

#   Key Uses:

$\bullet$ Measure intake capability of absorbent systems.

$\bullet$ Determine relative wettability of materials.

#   References:

TTM-00148

![](1/parseimages/figurea7a0c5c313684594a8dece935bc1fbf2_0_11.png)
#   Intake – 30 Degree Runoff

#   Test Description:

Measures the amount of liquid that does not immediately get captured by the absorbent system over multiple liquid insults.

#   Output:

Liquid runoff amounts in grams

#   Key Uses:

$\bullet$ Measure intake capability of absorbent systems.

$\bullet$ Determine relative wettability of materials.

$\bullet$ Benefit visualization/Demos

![30 Degree\nRunoff\nTTM 00279\n](1/parseimages/figureda70bc40069d488caa78f45833aab013_0_9.png)
#   References:

#   TTM-00279 (30 Degree Run-Off)

#   Surface Tension Reduction

#   Test Description:

Measures the amount of surface tension depression experienced after passing multiple liquid insults over a material.

#   Output:

Liquid surface tension after each insult.

#   Key Uses:

$\bullet$ Determine the durability of material treatments.

$\bullet$ Determine inputs into gasketing tests.

#   Rewet – Surface Moisture

#   Test Description:

Measures the amount of liquid residing above or near the liner surface through a low pressure rewet evaluation.

#   Output:

Multiple insult rewet amounts (grams)

Multiple insult intake time (seconds)

#   Key Uses:

$\bullet$ Determine how different liner materials or absorbent systems may change dryness feel and perception.

Reference: TTM-00302

#   Mannequins – Qualitative Observation

#   Test Description:

A full product evaluation on a mannequin designed to allow the tester to observe liquid entering into the product with the presence of a body.

#   Output:

Generally, qualitative
observations

Can be used to measure intake time

#   Key Uses:

$\bullet$ Determine leakage paths/mechanisms

$\bullet$ Observe how systems/materials handle liquid differently.

#   Test Description:

Measures the resistance forces during product deformation.

Peak compression forces and energy

Determine deformation forces that deliver consumer preference.

#   Fit/Comfort – X-Ray Densitometry

#   Test Description:

Use of x-ray image analysis to determine the location of amount of solid and/or liquid mass in the absorbent.

#   Output:

2-dimensional contour plots of mass distribution

1-dimensional line plots of mass distribution

#   Key Uses:

Determine dry mass distribution and/or fluid distribution

#   References:
