#   Pad Channels Process

Key design principles (used for both versions):

$\bullet$ Must form on a substrate

$\bullet$ Channel inserts taller than segments

$\bullet$ Air blowoff by scarfing removes contamination

$\bullet$ Channel adhesive pulsed onto carrier sheet

$\bullet$ Nip to drum bonds sheets together with glue
