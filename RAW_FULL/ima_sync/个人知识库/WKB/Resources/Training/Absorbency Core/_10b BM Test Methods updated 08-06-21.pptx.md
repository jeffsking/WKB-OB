$\bullet$ Development Status

- Standard method used for several years

$\bullet$ Status for Claims

- Used for claims of 'absorbs runny mess' in KCNA

$\bullet$ Outputs

1. Grams of Simulant remaining on Mannequin ('pooled on skin')

2. Grams of Simulant that come off of liner surface in a rewet test ('pooled on liner')

- Standard method requires Sat Cap box for rewet, but could use a weight instead to approximate the method

#   TTM 656: BM Flat Plate Absorption (EtQ Link)

![](1/parseimages/figure66495926ccfd433db29233a771177a73_0_2.png)
$\bullet$ Development Status

- Standard method used for several years

- 2021 improvements include equipment standardization and modifications to reduce run-off

$\bullet$ Status for Claims

- Not used for claims to date, used for research purposes only

$\bullet$ Outputs

1. Grams of Simulant remaining on upper plate and that come off of liner surface with blotter paper and weight ('pooled')

2. Grams of Simulant that reached each layer of the diaper

* Other Notes

- Requires BM Gun and mannequin to run standard method, but may be possible to use alternate simulant delivery method to approximate the method

- Recommended to use only A* simulant, as this is the only simulant which has been validated to partition through each layer the same as real BM

#   TTM 645: BM Run-Off (EtQ Link)

![](1/parseimages/figuree30ae3d0f1e84fa0bf2b5ef930d8a4cb_0_2.png)
* Development Status

- Method developed in 2016

- Standard and available for general use

$\bullet$ Status for Claims

- Not used for claims.

- Used for research purposes only.

$\bullet$ Outputs

1. Amount of run-off, (g).

2. Extent of BM spread, (MD and CD direction in mm).

3. Image of BM spread on product surface with image analysis, quantifying BM stain size. (If requested)

$\bullet$ Other Notes

- Initial use has been to investigate impact of liner topography on BM spread.

- While intended as a material test, the BM test lab noted in 2021 that this is more commonly run as a product test. Recommended updating the method.

#   Modified Material Flat Plate Test Method (Not Yet Published)

$\bullet$ Unpublished method originally developed for BVs for Cleanshield/project TOTAL:

$\bullet$ Modified from TTM 656 to test 3D & apertured materials:

- A glass plate is placed underneath the top plate

- A liner sample is placed in between the top plate and the glass plate

- Gap between the two plates is set to material thickness at 0.05psi.

- After 11 cc BM insult of Sim A*, BM residue on the top plate and the glass plate (bottom) are measured.

- BM amount on the liner sample is also measured.

- Photo is taken from underneath the glass plate.

$\bullet$ Current outputs (as of 8/5/2021):

- % of BM that is on the top plate

- % of BM that is on the bottom plate

- % of BM that is on + in the material

- Photos from underneath the sample, option to do image analysis

* Current gaps (as of 8/5/2021)

- Not yet published

- Method doesn't yet distinguish between how much BM is in vs. on the material

#   TTM 269 Runny BM Strike-though (EtQ Link)

$\bullet$ Purpose: Understand strikethrough of runny BM that the flap experiences

$\bullet$ Outputs:

- Amount of BM that remains on the flap (Film Percent)

- Amount of BM that stays in the flap (Flap Percent)

- Amount of BM that moves through the flap (Blotter Percent)
