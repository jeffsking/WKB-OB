# Category Strategy to deliver $36.5M value delivery 2022-2024

## Goals

1. Deliver improved product claims through SAM functionality or Core design

2. Drive bottom line improvement through DTV and Total Delivery Cost

3. Bring the Outside In & leverage Market Intelligence to win locally

4. Protect supply & Reduce price volatility

## 1

### Competitive Advantage

$\bullet$ Evolve MACH programs with LG and BASF on top of Evonik.

$\bullet$ Continue the work with Evonik in Project Cirrus.

$\bullet$ Pursue key innovation path with BASF, Evonik, LG and SDP.

## 2

## Short - term margin recovery

$\bullet$ DTV: Implement SAM grades Cost-In-Use optimization

$\bullet$ RFP: Leverage negotiation through SAM main grades

$\bullet$ Implement lower cost grades and suppliers where fits

$\bullet$ Recover from record freight costs. (2023+)

$\bullet$ Extend Payment Terms.

## 3

### Sustainable Competitiveness

$\bullet$ Joint-Development agreements with Evonik and BASF as priority – LG and SDP followers

$\bullet$ Consider BASF elevation to SRM program

$\bullet$ Leverage market intelligence to strength K-C's terms

## 4

### Risk Management

$\bullet$ Lock-in contract with Evonik (2023-2024) and work on unlocking volumes with other suppliers over next 2-3 years to mitigate any future access risk on Evonik volume

$\bullet$ Higher safety stocks in the supply chain

$\bullet$ Keep fixed commodity mechanism
