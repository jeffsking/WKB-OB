# Internal: Odor Ranking Panel (ORP)

$\bullet$ Rank-4 method. Panelist compare multiple subsets of four codes at a time.

$\bullet$ Attributes:

* overall odor intensity.

* objectionable odor intensity.

* nasal irritation (not typically requested).

$\bullet$ Results include panelists' odor descriptions.

$\bullet$ 12 panelists per study.

$\bullet$ Samples placed in jars or other receptacles.

$\bullet$ Not recommended for use with malodor simulants.
