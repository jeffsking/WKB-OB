## 模块1：核心论点（Core Arguments）

### CORE ARGUMENT 1 — Consumer need is a ranked 7-item list, and "Cleaning Efficacy" is structurally the first-listed need; the entire deck is organized to answer it.



"What are consumers looking for when they purchase baby wipes?"
— SLIDE 3

支撑依据：
- SLIDE 3 以问句立论，并给出需求清单（原文顺序）：Cleaning Efficacy / Safe & Gentle Skin Care / Good Quality / Convenience / Functional Packaging / Environmentally Friendly / Pleasant Scent（子项：Unscented for many）。
- 该清单带明确外部研究溯源：GL-90472 - Final Vendor Report KC BCC Wipes Benchmarking C&U（SLIDE 3），即这是 C&U（Consumer & Usage）Benchmarking 的_VENDOR 报告结论，不是内部臆测。
- 该论点在 SLIDE 7 被收束并升级：#1 Benefit Desired in a Wipe is CLEAN—these three parts work together to deliver it. —— 即 "Cleaning Efficacy"（SLIDE 3 首位需求）与 "CLEAN"（SLIDE 7 的 #1 Benefit）是同一条主线的首尾呼应。
- 可验证性：SLIDE 2 的 Agenda（Consumer → Basesheet → Formulation → Converting/Packaging → Product）证明后续 4 章正是为 SLIDE 7 的 CLEAN 三元（Basesheet / Formulation / Packaging）逐一展开。

### CORE ARGUMENT 2 — Wipes escape the diapering occasion: convenience extends usage to 7 non-diapering scenario families, which is what keeps the category alive long after diapering ends.



"The Convenience of Baby Wipes makes them Useful Beyond Diapering."
— SLIDE 4

支撑依据：
- SLIDE 4 将场景二元切分：Diapering-Related（4 项） vs Beyond Diapering（7 个一级项），来源 GL-87997 – Baby Wipes Usage Diary（SLIDE 4）。
- Beyond Diapering 的 7 个一级项：Baby/Kids Hands & Face（含 Mealtime、Out of Home 两个二级子项）/ Wiping Nose / Parent's Hands / Make-Up Removal / Adult/Child Toileting / Large and Small Surfaces / Pets & Pet Messes。
- 关键量化推论：SLIDE 5 称 "Many stay in category through child age 11"，而 SLIDE 5 的第三个决策节点仅为 "3-4 Years"。即换尿布期（约 0–4 岁）结束后，仍有约 7 年的在品类时间靠 Beyond-Diapering 场景支撑 [推导，非原文]。这使 SLIDE 4 的场景扩张不是"锦上添花"，而是品类生命周期的延续机制。

### CORE ARGUMENT 3 — Needs are non-stationary: three discrete decision-change points drive a predictable trade-down trajectory, and retention inside the brand is conditional on needs being met.



"…and keeps them in many homes for years after diapering is done. Needs also change over time."
— SLIDE 5

支撑依据：
- 原始图轴（Project JOURNEY Data Synthesis，SLIDE 5）三节点：1st Baby = Category Entry / 6 mos / 3-4 Years。
- 节点 1（Category Entry）的消费者心智与皮肤状态："Only the Best for Baby" + "Baby has very delicate/sensitive skin"（SLIDE 5 图）。
- 节点 2（6 mos）的生理与皮肤变化："BM consistency changes with solid food diet" + "Baby skin more developed/less delicate"（SLIDE 5 图）。
- 节点 3（3-4 Years）："More hands & face; surface usage" + "Lower volume consumed"（SLIDE 5 图）。
- 演讲者备注给出机制句：Consumers often start with a higher tier and trade down at the subsequent points.（SLIDE 5），以及留存的条件句：as long as their needs are being met（SLIDE 5）。
- 可验证性：该 trade-down 方向与 SLIDE 25 (Ch4) 的 Tier 阶梯（Economy Tier 3 → Mainstream Tier 4 → Premium Tier 5 → Super-Premium Tier 6）可逐档对应，使"trade-down"从定性描述升级为可落档的模型（详见模块 3 表 D、模块 5）。

### CORE ARGUMENT 4 — Segmentation is 2-dimensional: WILLINGNESS TO PAY (Y) × SKIN BENEFITS (X); these two attitudes "emerged as the key dimensions", and Huggies self-positions at the high-WTP end.



"Attitudes around willingness to pay and desired skin benefits emerged as the key dimensions that separated the segments."
— SLIDE 6 (Speaker Notes)

支撑依据：
- 坐标轴原文（SLIDE 6 图）：Y 轴 WILLINGNESS TO PAY（下端 VALUE → 上端 PREMIUM）；X 轴 SKIN BENEFITS（左端 NO ADDED INGREDIENTS → 右端 MANY SKINCARE INGREDIENTS）。
- 分群规模编码：*Size of circle represents segment size（SLIDE 6）—— 这是一个明确的三变量可视化（2 定位轴 + 1 规模变量）。
- 品牌定位句：As a premium brand, Huggies is targeted to those with greater willingness to pay.（SLIDE 6）
- 次级维度：Convenience and cleaning effectiveness also come into play and further develop segment identity（SLIDE 6）—— 说明 Convenience / Cleaning 是修饰性维度，不是切分轴。
- 边界声明：源文件仅明确点名一个分群 Bargain Buyers（左下象限）；其余 3 个象限的分群名称在源文件中 [未找到原句]，本报告不做补名。

### CORE ARGUMENT 5 — CLEAN is not a single attribute but a three-part coupled system: Basesheet + Formulation + Packaging, with a feedback loop between Formulation and Packaging.



"#1 Benefit Desired in a Wipe is CLEAN—these three parts work together to deliver it."
— SLIDE 7

支撑依据：
- SLIDE 7 图结构：中心蓝圈 CLEAN；三个红框 Basesheet / Formulation / Packaging 以箭头指向 CLEAN；Formulation ↔ Packaging 之间存在双向箭头。
- "work together" 是原文措辞，说明三元是协同而非加和；双向箭头进一步说明 Formulation 与 Packaging 之间存在相互约束/耦合（对应 SLIDE 20 (Ch4) 的 Packaging 功能之一 Retains moisture，即包装保水能力与配方液相量互为条件）。
- 可验证性：三元与后续章节一一对应 —— Basesheet → SLIDE 8–10 (Ch2)；Formulation → SLIDE 11–16 (Ch3)；Packaging → SLIDE 17–22 (Ch4)。

### 概念 1｜清洁效能 — Cleaning Efficacy

(b) English definition
The first-listed item on the consumer purchase-need list for baby wipes; restated at chapter level as the "#1 Benefit Desired in a Wipe" under the label CLEAN.

(c) 操作化定义
- Ch1 内：仅作为需求项/利益点列出，无测量口径、无公式、无阈值 → 就 Ch1 文本而言 [定义缺失/无法操作化]。
- 跨章可操作化锚点（供落地，均标注真实出处）：
  1. 结构路径锚：SLIDE 9 (Ch2) — Triple Layers help with hand protection because of the "torturous" path the mess must take. → 以"污物穿透路径曲折度"作为间接指标。
  2. 厚度锚：SLIDE 21 (Ch4) 图注 50% Thicker（脚注 *HUGGIES® Clean vs. Pampers® Baby Clean Fresh™，各 150 wipes 堆叠对比）。
  3. 化学锚：SLIDE 14 (Ch3) — Surfactant is the "cleaner" in the formulation. → 以表面活性剂体系作为清洁力的配方侧代理变量。
  4. 宣称锚：SLIDE 22 (Ch4) 标题 Unbeatable Clean；SLIDE 31 (Ch4) Aspiration 句 Own Differentiated Cleaning for Sensitive Skin Driven by Preferred Technology Platform。
- 建议判定式 [推导，非原文]：Cleaning Efficacy ≈ f(基材层结构与厚度, 表面活性剂体系, 单手单片取用的有效性)。Ch1 未给出权重，权重 [未找到原句]。

(d) 原文摘录
> "Cleaning Efficacy"（需求清单首项） — SLIDE 3

### 概念 2｜安全温和护肤 — Safe & Gentle Skin Care

(b) English definition
The second consumer need on the purchase list; the skin-tolerance / skin-health dimension of a wipe, operationalized downstream through formulation choice and third-party seals.

(c) 操作化定义
- Ch1 内测量维度：无量化 → Ch1 层面 [定义缺失/无法操作化]（仅有名称）。
- 跨章量化锚点：
  1. 纯度口径：SLIDE 11 (Ch3) / SLIDE 16 (Ch3) — over 99% of our formulation is purified water! Everything else makes up less than 1% of the formulation.（例外：Calm and Nourish are not 99% water due to the added ingredient (glycerin)）
  2. 排除项口径：SLIDE 27 (Ch4) — Free of alcohol and parabens；SLIDE 29 (Ch4) — Zero harsh ingredients / Free of Fragrance。
  3. 第三方认证口径（二值变量）：SLIDE 27/28/30 (Ch4) — National Eczema Association Seal of Acceptance；SLIDE 30 (Ch4) — Clinically proven more gentle than cloth and water（用于 NICU）。
  4. pH 口径：SLIDE 13 (Ch3) — Lower the solution pH (<5.0)。<5.0 是本章可引用的唯一硬阈值型参数（位于 Ch3）。
- 建议判定式 [推导，非原文]：Safe & Gentle = (配方水含量 ≥ 99%) ∧ (Free of 指定刺激物) ∧ (pH < 5.0) ∧ (NEA Seal = Yes)。

(d) 原文摘录
> "Safe & Gentle Skin Care" — SLIDE 3

### 概念 3｜便利性 — Convenience

(b) English definition
The fourth consumer need on the purchase list; in Ch1 it is the causal driver that makes wipes "Useful Beyond Diapering" and is one of two secondary dimensions that "further develop segment identity".

(c) 操作化定义
- 判定公式（可数）：Convenience 的场景覆盖度 = |Diapering-Related 场景集| + |Beyond Diapering 场景集| = 4 + 7 = 11 个一级使用场景（其中 Baby/Kids Hands & Face 下含 Mealtime、Out of Home 2 个二级子项，故二级口径为 13）[推导，非原文]。
- 非尿布场景占比：Beyond-Diapering 一级场景占比 = 7 / 11 = 63.6% [推导，非原文]。
- 行为学指标：SLIDE 5 节点 3 给出便利性的行为迁移 —— More hands & face; surface usage + Lower volume consumed。即"场景数上升 + 单位消耗量下降"是 Convenience 驱动的特征签名，可作为量化判据。
- 包装侧硬约束：SLIDE 17 (Ch4) — Package sheet counts must be multiples of 8 (24, 32, 40, 48, …)；SLIDE 20 (Ch4) — Dispensing 需 One-handed, one at a time；Easy to open and close 需 One-handed, closes securely。

(d) 原文摘录
> "Convenience" — SLIDE 3
> "The Convenience of Baby Wipes makes them Useful Beyond Diapering." — SLIDE 4
> "Convenience and cleaning effectiveness also come into play and further develop segment identity" — SLIDE 6 (Speaker Notes)

### 概念 5｜环保性 — Environmentally Friendly

(b) English definition
The sixth consumer need on the purchase list; the environmental-impact dimension of the wipe system.

(c) 操作化定义
- Ch1 内：仅列名称，无指标 → Ch1 层面 [定义缺失/无法操作化]。
- 跨章锚点（可量化）：
  1. 材料生物降解二元拆分：SLIDE 8 (Ch2) — Coform Typically 2/3rds pulp (biodegradable) and 1/3rd polymer (not biodegradable)；Spunlace Typically contains a blend of rayon (biodegradable) and polyester or polypropylene (not biodegradable) staple。→ 可用 可降解纤维重量占比 作为单一量化指标。

### 概念 6｜Project JOURNEY（消费者纵向旅程研究）

(b) English definition
A KC consumer data-synthesis program ("Project JOURNEY Data Synthesis") that maps how baby-wipe needs and purchase decisions evolve across child age, identifying the discrete moments when purchase decisions change.

(c) 操作化定义
- 时间轴节点（判定锚）：t₁ = 1st Baby（= Category Entry）｜ t₂ = 6 mos ｜ t₃ = 3-4 Years。
- 品类留存上限：Many stay in category through child age 11 → 品类生命周期上界 = child age 11。
- 决策变更点计数：The 3 specified points represent the times when purchase decisions are most likely to change. → 决策变更点 N = 3。
- 每个节点的双变量记录格式（皮肤状态 + 使用行为），见模块 3 表 A。
- 建议判定式 [推导，非原文]：JOURNEY 状态向量 S(t) = [皮肤敏感度, 场景结构, 单位消耗量, 价格档位]；在 t₁/t₂/t₃ 处对向量做重估。

(d) 原文摘录
> "Project JOURNEY Data Synthesis" — SLIDE 5
> "The 3 specified points represent the times when purchase decisions are most likely to change." — SLIDE 5 (Speaker Notes)

### 概念 7｜品类进入 — Category Entry

(b) English definition
The entry moment into the baby-wipes category, defined as the arrival of the first baby; characterized by a maximal-quality mindset and maximally delicate infant skin.

(c) 操作化定义
- 判定条件：1st Baby（首次生育）= Category Entry 触发事件。
- 心智维度（可直接转为问卷题项）："Only the Best for Baby" → 价格敏感度最低档、品质诉求最高档。
- 皮肤维度："Baby has very delicate/sensitive skin" → 对应 SLIDE 6 X 轴右端 MANY SKINCARE INGREDIENTS 与高 WTP 的组合。
- 档位判定：Category Entry 是 trade-down 模型中的 最高档起点（Consumers often start with a higher tier，SLIDE 5），对应 SLIDE 25 (Ch4) 的 Super-Premium Tier 6 / Premium Tier 5。
- 战略对应：SLIDE 31 (Ch4) — Accelerate POME through Trial with First-Time Parents（品牌 = SKIN ESSENTIALS），即以 First-Time Parents 作为 Category Entry 的运营化抓手。

(d) 原文摘录
> "Category Entry"；"1st Baby"；"Only the Best for Baby"；"Baby has very delicate/sensitive skin" — SLIDE 5

### 概念 8｜降级消费迁移 — Trade-down（Category Trade-down）

(b) English definition
The observed consumer behavior of entering the category at a higher price/quality tier and moving to lower tiers at subsequent decision-change points, typically while remaining inside the same brand.

(c) 操作化定义
- 方向判据（原文）：Consumers often start with a higher tier and trade down at the subsequent points.（SLIDE 5）
- 留存条件（原文，重要）：Data also shows that consumers often stay within a brand and move to lower tiers as their child(ren) age as long as their needs are being met.（SLIDE 5）→ 品牌留存是条件概率，条件变量 = "needs are being met"。
- 档位编码（与 Ch4 Tier 阶梯对接）：Economy Tier 3 / Mainstream Tier 4 / Premium Tier 5 / Super-Premium Tier 6（SLIDE 25 (Ch4)）。
- 判定公式 [推导，非原文]：设 Tier(t) ∈ {3,4,5,6}，则
  - Trade-down 判定：Tier(tₙ₊₁) ≤ Tier(tₙ)，且至少一次 Tier(tₙ₊₁) < Tier(tₙ)；
  - 品牌内降级（In-brand trade-down）= 品牌 ID 不变 ∧ Tier 递减；
  - 流失（Leakage）= 品牌 ID 改变（触发条件即 "needs NOT being met"）。
- 迁移窗口：3 个决策点 → 最多 2 次降级跃迁（t₁→t₂、t₂→t₃）[推导，非原文]。
- 缺失项：各档间的迁移概率、迁移比例、留存率在源文件中 [未找到原句]。

(d) 原文摘录
> "Consumers often start with a higher tier and trade down at the subsequent points. Data also shows that consumers often stay within a brand and move to lower tiers as their child(ren) age as long as their needs are being met." — SLIDE 5 (Speaker Notes)

### 概念 10｜皮肤益处诉求 — SKIN BENEFITS

(b) English definition
The second key attitudinal dimension separating segments; plotted as the horizontal (X) axis, running from NO ADDED INGREDIENTS (left) to MANY SKINCARE INGREDIENTS (right).

(c) 操作化定义
- 量表轴（原文）：X 轴 SKIN BENEFITS；左端 NO ADDED INGREDIENTS，右端 MANY SKINCARE INGREDIENTS（SLIDE 6）。
- 成分计数口径 [推导，非原文]：可用 INCI 表中"功能性护肤添加剂"的成分个数作为 X 轴的代理计数变量，锚点如下 ——
  - 左极（NO ADDED INGREDIENTS）：SLIDE 11/16 (Ch3) — 配方 over 99% 为纯化水，其余 <1%；SLIDE 29 (Ch4) — 99% water / Zero harsh ingredients。
  - 右极（MANY SKINCARE INGREDIENTS）：SLIDE 15 (Ch3) Variant Specific Materials —— Glycerin（Humectant）/ Aloe Barbadensis Leaf Juice、Tocopheryl Acetate（Botanicals & Vitamins）/ Fragrance / Theobroma Cacao (Cocoa) Extract and PEG-50 Shea Butter（HUGGIES® Nourish™）。
  - 完整 INCI 参照序列（SLIDE 13 (Ch3)）：Water, Butoxy PEG-4 PG-Amodimethicone, Polysorbate 20, Caprylyl Glycol, Sodium Benzoate, Coco-Betaine, Malic Acid, Sodium Citrate, Tocopheryl Acetate, Aloe Barbadensis Leaf Juice（10 项，无 Fragrance）。
- 气味作为正交子维度：Pleasant Scent（SLIDE 3）与其子项 Unscented for many（SLIDE 3）说明香型是极化变量，操作化应为二值 + 人群占比，而非线性偏好。SLIDE 15 (Ch3) 印证：For this reason, HUGGIES® offers both scented and unscented wipes to meet all consumers' needs.
- 缺失项：X 轴上各分群的具体成分数阈值、人数占比在源文件中 [未找到原句]。

可执行行动建议

- 建立"单片浆重 → 年度浆量"的换算基线：







- 单片浆重 = 1.34 g（SLIDE 10 (Ch2)）
- 年产能 ≈ 40 billion 片（SLIDE 10 (Ch2)）
- 年浆需求量 ≈ 1.34 g × 40×10⁹ = 53,600 吨/年；年 PP 需求量 ≈ 0.5 g × 40×10⁹ = 20,000 吨/年 [推导计算，非原文]
