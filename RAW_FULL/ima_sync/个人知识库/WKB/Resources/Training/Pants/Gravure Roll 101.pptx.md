## Gravure Coating

$\bullet$ Process that uses a gravure roll to apply a thin, uniform liquid film to a moving substrate

$\bullet$ Consistent coating weights

$\bullet$ Suitable for high-speed processes

## Main Forms of Gravure Coating

$\bullet$ Direct = gravure roll to substrate ...... DRC and OLU

$\bullet$ Indirect = gravure roll to applicator roll to substrate.... Facial and Embossing winders

# Laser Engraving

$\bullet$ Circular laser beam that creates ~10 um round hole

$\bullet$ Engraving each subsequent hole pushes the cell walls to create a hexagon shape

$\bullet$ Each corner is exposed to the laser a minimum of 3 times compared to the side walls

* Raised peaks are created in the corners

$\bullet$ Polishing is required to remove the corner peaks and eliminate excessive doctor blade wear

# Gravure Roll Volume

$\bullet$ Total volume of all the engraved cells on the roll surface per unit area of the surface

$\bullet$ USA = BCM/in2 ...... More commonly just referred to as BCM

$\bullet$ European = cm3/m2

$\bullet$ Conversion:

* 1 BCM/in2 = 1.55 cm3/m2

# Cell Volume Terminology – BCM/in2

$\bullet$ 1 Billion Cubic Microns (Micrometers)

* 1 BCM = 1,000,000,000 um3

# UCTAD Print Crepe Product Properties

$\bullet$ X and Y direction sheet surface coverage - Key to GMT (Cell diameter)

$\bullet$ Z direction sheet penetration - Key to VIVA/WypAll Z peel strength (Cell depth)

* High Z direction penetration can also negatively impact bulk/caliper

# Cell Diameter vs Cell Depth

$\bullet$ Gravure roll design is an iterative process

$\bullet$ What cell design is necessary for desired product attributes

$\bullet$ Same volume from different combinations of diameter vs depth

$\bullet$ Engraver must have at least 1 degree of freedom. Can only specify 2 from the key engraving parameters of volume, depth, and diameter

$\bullet$ Banded gravure rolls can help speed up the iterative process – typically don't produce sellable product – RSR not MSR

# Summary

Gravure rolls are engraved
rolls that deliver a precise
amount of fluid to a
substrate

GRs are critical to many of FCP's premium products

Ceramic laser engraved
rolls dominate the market

Gravure roll design is an iterative process

KC's engineered patterns create a tight engraving window

Product properties can change dramatically with engraving changes/inconsistencies

New vendors are learning with KC as we attempt to get back to healthy gravure roll inventories and look to ignite innovation
