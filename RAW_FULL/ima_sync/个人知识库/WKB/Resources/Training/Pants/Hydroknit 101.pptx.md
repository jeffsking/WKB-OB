$\bullet$ The consistency of the water that is being fed into the HK2 Headbox is .3% for most of the codes that will be run on HK2 when the Headbox is in Single Layer mode (X40, X50, etc.)

$\bullet$ When the machine is running x80 codes in Single Layer mode, the consistency in the Headbox is expected to be .55 to .6%

$\bullet$ The Headbox on HK2 is a 2 mode Headbox; Single and Dual Mode

* When the machine is running Staple Knit codes in Dual Layer mode, the consistency in the Headbox is expected to be .03%

* HK2 water system is not capable of supporting Dual Layer mode as of 2024

$\bullet$ The Headbox on HK supplies a fibrous suspension to the sheet forming zone inside the Headbox as uniformly as possible

$\bullet$ The fibrous suspension is the stock inside of the Headbox on top of the Apron Board before it reaches the sheet forming zone

$\bullet$ The suspension is filtered by means of the forming wire moving across the dewatering box

$\bullet$ Through the dewatering box, the white water is returned to the Silos, while the pulp fibers form a pulp sheet on the forming wire

# CAN DRYING THEORY

When steam condenses and gives up its latent heat, it passes via the cylinder wall to the web. However there are several restrictions that slow down this heat transfer because of differences of conductivity. So some of the latent heat liberated is lost and the temperature reduced.

The restrictions are: -

1. The Steam Side layer. This comprises a layer of condensate, scale, rust and other material that has been deposited on the inside of the drying cylinder.

2. The Dryer Shell itself

3. Dirt on the face of the Dryer

4. The air gap between the face of the dryer and the web itself.

5. The web itself.

# External Contract Manufacturing (ECM) - Outsourcing

## KCP uses External Contract Manufacturers to provide goods or services

$\bullet$ Purchase Basepaper or Nonwovens from an ECM

$\bullet$ Contract an ECM to Convert purchased basepaper or nonwovens

$\bullet$ Contract an ECM to Convert Internally produced basepaper or nonwovens

$\bullet$ Contract an ECM to Provide a complete purchased finished good

## ECM Management

$\bullet$ Identification of an ECM for a Specific Need: Procurement works with R&E to search for an ECM to deliver a specific good or service. R&E provides technical requirements and assists with identifying ECM options and participates in initial discussion and evaluation of the ECMs for fit and capabilities. R&E supports through the entire process leading up to RFQs and selection of best option. In many cases R&E will provide Procurement with the initial list of ECM options based on the need.

$\bullet$ Qualification of an ECM: Once an ECM is selected, KC ECM Team works with R&E to define qualification requirements for outsourced materials or services. R&E provides specifications for outsourced materials and/or finished products. R&E conducts RSRs to validate materials, processes or finished products.
