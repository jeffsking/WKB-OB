### Stack of Folded Wipes

Individual sheets folded and stacked; wipes are compressed, so it can be more difficult for the user to find the end of the wipe

![](2/Oy0YFe4o874ND9Udefofxe/ppt_3867e2b60070d7dc3f413086d0b0036e_b9526ce20e7d06420cad8bb5e60a7ad2001a693ee6c0644e/tmp_zip_img/42eeea8d-f4ea-4bd7-9842-76a9f1ad7b08.png)
### Interfolded Wipes

Individual sheets folded and stacked so the trailing edge of one wipe is overlapping the leading edge of the next wipe; easier to find the edge of the wipe, but the sheets often stick together, resulting in multiple wipes dispensing at once

Note: There are different folding configurations when wipes are folded/interfolded
