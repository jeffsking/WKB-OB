## Status & Challenge

Application in a 100% SAP fluff-less core, REL sits between two SAP layers → creates void space, prevents gel-block.

Pain points: SAP uptake insufficient - no space as SAP swells - REL clean-water wicking sub-optimal.

5 failure modes without REL: gel-block / no void / Z-collapse / poor MD distribution / rewet.

No one in the industry does this → opportunity, 3rd Intake-Speed data pending Tianjin
