# Meeting Minutes: New Pilot Line High-Speed Running Solution

* **Meeting Date**: October 13, 2026 (the recording does not mention a date; filled in based on the date the minutes were generated — please advise if this differs from the actual meeting date)

* **Topics**: New pilot line high-speed machine solution, process structure, SAP dosing configuration, and four product/process routes

## 1. New Pilot Line Planning

* A new high-speed pilot line is planned, with a target running speed of **1000 PPM** and a design speed of **1200 PPM**
* The pilot machine is expected to be ready in **the second half of next year (H2 2027)**
* The machine layout was shared on screen, and the team confirmed the visuals were visible

## 2. Machine Process Structure & Dosing System

* The machine adopts a **five-structure process**, with two cores: the light side and the poly side
* **Three separate dosing systems** are planned on top of the machine; exact positions are not yet defined. Design intent:
  1. If the **hydrogen block** issue (transcribed as-is; likely hydration/gel blocking) is to be solved from the SAP side, two different SAPs could be used on the poly side or even the liner side, adding process flexibility;
  2. Alternatively, SAP is kept on only one side (liner or poly), with the extra dosing system reserved for future development — e.g., control granules or PCM phase-change material granules for temperature regulation or functional modification.
* Absorbent material configuration: the demo machine design supports **4 different absorbent materials** (2 on top + 2 on bottom); production machines can be configured per country needs, keeping as few as 2 or even 1 SAP type; dosing systems can be simply removed or added as required; the mixing chamber supports up to **4 dosing streams**

## 3. Product Structure & Process Routes

Base product structure (upper left of the layout): core + skin-side proprietary SAP + AI/EL reinforced layer + poly-side proprietary SAP

### Process 1: HCH Process

* **10% fluff** is added on the poly side via the HCH process to increase caliper/absorbency
* Purpose: a minimal fluff addition to mitigate the SAP hydrogen block issue
* Equipment: a heavy hammer mill on top; the 10% fluff enters the poly-layer forming zone, added on the second poly-layer SAP side to improve absorbency

### Process 2: Top VQ + Pattern Process

* For certain country markets in the near future: the product structure only requires top **VQ** and **patterns** on the top SAP, while retaining the existing blaz process (transcribed as blazard / casec blaz / kc bla process)
* One forming zone can be dismantled/removed and kept as a "blanket black box" spare block, allowing switching between poly-side forming and the kc bla process
* Space impact: the blaz process requires roughly an additional **2.7 meters** of machine length

### Process 3: High-Absorbency Fluff + SAP Combination

* Not yet confirmed/commissioned (transcribed as "not in neutral yet"); targets high-absorbency demand in certain countries
* The bottom SAP layer on the poly side becomes a conventional fluff + SAP combination:
  * **50% SAP + 50% fluff**, or **70% SAP + 30% fluff**
* Similar to a conventional second forming draw, except that this forming station has a large screen opening — fluff and SAP do not land directly on the drum but onto a substrate (nonwoven) wrapped around the drum
* Differences vs. Process 1:
  * Process 1 hammer mill capacity is about **200 kg/h**; Process 3 hammer mill (CA mill) requires at least **800 kg/h**
  * Latex patterns need no scaling roll; Process 3, with its high fluff content, requires an additional scaling roll (transcribed as "scaling row") to ensure correct basis/caliper of the bottom layer — this is the only difference

### Product 4: Co-Development

* Fluff + SAP patterned development (transcribed as "code development", likely co-development)
* A new development for **Phase 2** on the cocoa v6 (transcribed as "cocoa v six"), providing additional VQ and capability

## 4. Adhesive System

* Before the core roll is fed in and wrapped onto the drum: a basic glue application; another adhesive system follows after the forming zone
* Previously, the casec blaz process glued the blaz onto the conveyor; cocoa v6 intends to try wrapping the blaz process **directly onto the drum** instead of the conveyor
* The machine carries **1–5 adhesive applicators**, roughly two above each forming drum (job)
* Process requirement: **high temperature and pressure**, giving the adhesive a waxy (wax adhesive) behavior with finer particle size, ensuring good penetration into the SAP gaps

## 5. Substrate & Screen Design

* Current state: no substrate outside the forming drum; the core wrap is wrapped directly around the drum, and SAP lands directly on the track
* Fluff screens have very small openings; cocoa v6 requires **larger openings** for higher airflow/pressure
* Therefore, the substrate (nonwoven) is wrapped outside the forming drum, avoiding contamination issues
* Video demonstration: the white part is the substrate wrapped around the drum; SAP lands on the substrate and accumulates only in the open-area regions

## 6. Forming Zone Design Direction

* To keep the process lean, the cocoa v6 forming zone is designed to be **significantly smaller than conventional forming zones**

# 会议纪要：新生产线（Pilot Line）高速运行方案讨论

* **会议日期**：2026年10月13日（录音中未提及日期，暂按纪要生成日期填写；如与实际会议日期不符，请告知更正）

* **议题**：新 Pilot Line 高速设备方案、工艺结构、SAP dosing 配置及四种产品/工艺路线

## 一、新试点生产线规划

* 拟投建一条新的高速试点线（pilot line），目标运行速度 **1000 PPM**，设计速度 **1200 PPM**
* 试点机器预计 **明年下半年（2027 年下半年）** 准备就绪
* 会上共享了机器布局图（machine layout），并确认团队可正常看到画面

## 二、机器工艺结构与 dosing 系统

* 机器采用 **5 段结构工艺**（five structure process），含两个 core：轻质侧（light side）与 poly 侧（poly side）
* 计划在机器顶部配置 **三套不同的 dosing（计量添加）系统**，具体位置尚未定义。设计目的：
  1. 若需从 SAP 端解决**水合阻滞问题**（hydrogen block，转写原文，疑为 hydration/gel blocking），可在 poly 侧甚至 liner 侧使用两种不同 SAP，从而增加工艺灵活性；
  2. 或 SAP 仅保留在 liner/poly 单侧，另一套 dosing 留作未来开发，例如控释颗粒（control granules）或 PCM 相变材料颗粒，用于温度调节或功能改性。
* 吸收材料配置：演示机（demo machine）设计支持 **4 种不同吸收材料**（顶部 2 种 + 底部 2 种）；生产机按各国需求配置，可只保留 2 种甚至 1 种 SAP，dosing 系统可简单移除或按需添加；混合腔（mixing chamber）最多支持 **4 路 dosing**

## 三、产品结构与工艺路线

基础产品结构（图左上）：core + 皮肤侧（skin side）专利 SAP + AI/EL 增强层 + poly 侧专利 SAP

### 工艺一（Process 1）：HCH 工艺

* poly 侧通过 HCH 工艺添加 **10% 绒毛浆（fluff）**，提高厚度/吸收容量（caliper absorbency）
* 目的：以极少量绒毛浆添加改善 SAP 水合阻滞问题
* 设备配置：顶部重型锤磨机（hammer mill），10% 绒毛浆进入 poly 层成形区，添加至第二层 poly 层 SAP 侧，改善吸收性能

### 工艺二（Process 2）：顶部 VQ + 图案工艺

* 针对未来某些国家市场：产品结构只需顶部 **VQ** 与顶部 SAP 上的**图案（patterns）**，同时保留现有 blaz 工艺（转写原文：blazard / casec blaz / kc bla process）
* 可拆卸/移除一个成形区（forming zone），保留为 "blanket black box" 备用区块，实现 poly 侧成形与 kc bla 工艺的切换
* 空间影响：blaz 工艺约需额外 **2.7 米**机长

### 工艺三（Process 3）：高吸收性绒毛浆+SAP 组合

* 目前尚未确定/未投产（转写原文 "not in neutral yet"），针对部分国家的高吸收性需求
* poly 侧底部 SAP 层做成传统绒毛浆+SAP 组合，配比方案：
  * **50% SAP + 50% 绒毛浆**，或 **70% SAP + 30% 绒毛浆**
* 类似传统工艺的第二成形工位（forming draw），区别在于：该工位筛网开孔大，绒毛浆+SAP 不直接落鼓，而是落在包覆于鼓外的衬底（无纺布）上
* 与工艺一的差异：
  * 工艺一锤磨机产能约 **200 kg/h**；工艺三锤磨机（CA mill）至少需 **800 kg/h**
  * 用乳胶（latex）做图案时无需整平辊；工艺三绒毛浆量大，需增设整平辊（scaling roll，转写为 scaling row）以保证底层厚度——这是两者唯一差异

### 工艺四（Product 4）：联合开发

* 绒毛浆+SAP 图案化开发（转写原文 "code development"，疑为 co-development）
* 属 cocoa v6（转写原文 cocoa v six）**第二阶段（Phase 2）**&\#x65B0;开发，用于提供额外 VQ 及相关能力

## 四、胶粘剂（adhesive）系统

* core 卷材喂入并包覆到鼓（drum）之前：设置基础胶（basic glue）涂布；成形区之后另有一组胶系统
* 以往 casec blaz 工艺是将 blaz 粘合到传送带（conveyor）上；cocoa v6 计划尝试 blaz 工艺**直接包覆到鼓上**而非传送带
* 设备上共 **1–5 个胶涂布器**（adhesive applicators），约每两个位于每个成形鼓（job）上方
* 工艺要求：**高温高压**，使胶呈蜡状（wax adhesive）特性，胶粒粒径更细，确保胶能良好渗透进 SAP 间隙（SAP gaps）

## 五、衬底（substrate）与筛网（screen）设计

* 现状：成形鼓外部无衬底，core wrap 直接包覆在鼓外，SAP 直接落至 track
* 绒毛浆用筛网开孔很小；cocoa v6 需要**更大开孔**以保证更大气流/压力
* 因此衬底（无纺布）包覆在成形鼓外部，可避免污染问题
* 视频演示：白色部分为衬底包覆鼓外，SAP 落在衬底上、仅在开孔区域累积

## 六、成形区设计方向
