#   Process Steps

01. Preparation: A polymer solution is loaded into a syringe.

02. Electric Field:High voltage is applied between the needle & collector.

03. Jet Formation:Electric force overcomes surface tension to form a jet.

04. Elongation & Drying:Jet stretches, whips, and solidifies as solvent evaporates.

05. Collection:Solidified nanofibers are deposited randomly to form a membrane.
