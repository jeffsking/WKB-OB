# DARCO® KB-G

## Powdered Activated Carbon

### WHY CABOT

Cabot Norit Activated Carbon is a premier activated carbon manufacturer respected for experienced people, diverse products and strong customer relationships. Cabot's history of innovation, product performance, technical expertise and customer focus ensure that you receive the right products and solutions for your specific purification needs.

[图片: STERUKASMACANN003FM 14575]
DARCO KB-G is excellent for the purification of highly colored and/or foaming process liquids in the food, chemical, and pharmaceutical industries. DARCO KB-G combines a superior adsorptive capacity with good filtration characteristics. It is especially effective in adsorbing high molecular weight organics such as large color bodies and proteins, at low carbon dosage rates. DARCO KB-G is produced by chemical activation of wood using the phosphoric acid process.

DARCO KB-G is Kosher certified and meets the requirements of the latest version of the U.S. Food Chemicals Codex.
