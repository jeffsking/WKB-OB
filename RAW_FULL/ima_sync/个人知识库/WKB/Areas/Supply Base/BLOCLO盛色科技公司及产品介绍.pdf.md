## 光变产品变色原理：

光变材料在没有阳光或紫外光照射下为无色，当阳光或紫外光照射时吸收了特定波长的紫外光能量而产生分子结构变化，从而产生颜色变化；当失去这种特定波长的紫外光照射则恢复到原来的分子结构，又变回原来的无色。

When the photochromic compound is excited by the light with certain wavelength and intensity, the molecular structure will change reversibly, resulting in a visible color change.

When the excitation is stopped, the molecular structure will return to the initial state, and the color will also fade to the original color.

# 光变染料
Photochromic dye

光变染料为有机化合物，可溶解在有机体系中，从而实现体系完全透光。光变染料需溶解在合适介质(如油性溶剂、树脂)中才能产生光变现象。介质的极性对光变染料的变色性能有较大影响。

## 建议用途及用量：

用于油性油墨及涂料：0.3 ~ 3.0 WT%

用于制膜：0.2~1 WT%

用于注塑: 0.1 ~ 0.8 WT%

## 产品特性：

光变染料为有机化合物，可溶解在有机体系中，从而实现体系完全透光。

## 注意事项：

光变染料极易受环境中光线、氧气、PH值等因素影响，产生光老化现象，直观表现为使用寿命缩短甚至丧失光致变色能力。建议在使用过程中添加抗氧剂和紫外吸收剂来提高光变染料的抗光老化性能。值得注意的是，不同的使用介质所适用的助剂种类和用量会有较大差异，错误的使用助剂可能会加剧光老化现象。光变染料的分解温度较低，若有高温工序，建议加工温度不高于220℃，加工时间不超过30 MIN。降低加工温度或缩短高温加工时间有助于提升最终产品的变色性能。建议客户使用前先进行小试，若有进一步技术支持需要，请联系我们。

The photochromic process can happen only when dye is dissolved in suitable medium, such as oily solvent and resin. The polarity of the medium has great influence on the color, so a test before use to confirm the color is necessary.

## SUGGESTED APPLICATION AND DOSAGE:

For oily ink and coating:0.3~3.0 wt%

For film-making:0.2~1 wt%

For injection molding:0.1~0.8 wt%

## PRODUCT FEATURES:

Photochromic dye is an organic compound and can be dissolved in an organic solvent to provide a transparent function.

## CAUTIONS:

Photochromic dye is easily affected by factors of the environment, such as light, oxygen, and pH value, causing photo aging. This will result in short lifetime and even loss of photochromic function. To improve longevity and achieve anti-aging of the DY power, it is beneficial to add antioxidant and ultraviolet absorbent during use. The type and amount of the additives applicable to various mediums may be different. The incorrect additives might shorten the lifespan of DY powder. The decomposition temperature of the DY powder is low. It is beneficial for the photochromic property when the processing temperature is not higher than 220°C and the processing time is not longer than 30 mins. The lower processing temperature and the shorter processing time is beneficial to improve the photochromic property of the products. A test sample before use is necessary. ## 建议用途及用量：

用于油墨及涂料：2~20 WT%
用于注塑：0.2~2 WT%

## 产品外观:固体粉末

光变粉的平均粒径为5±1μm

## 注意事项：

光变粉使用简单，适用性广，可用于油墨、涂料、注塑等多个领域。若有高温工序，建议加工温度不高于220℃，加工时间不超过30。添加热稳定剂、降低加工温度或缩短高温加工时间均有助于提升最终产品的变色性能。

建议客户使用前先进行小试，若需要进一步技术支持，请联系我们。

The photochromic pigment is produced by encapsulating photochromic dye in microcapsules and processing it through special techniques.

It changes color under ultraviolet light or sunlight. It can be directly added to the corresponding application products in proportion. It has the characteristics of bright color, high temperature resistance, solvent resistance, and high fatigue resistance. bright color, high temperature resistance, solvent resistance, and high fatigue resistance.

# 光变纤维
Photochromic fiber

光变丝是一种在太阳光或紫外线灯光照射下产生颜色或变色的聚酯纤维。

## 应用领域：

服装、面料、鞋制品等纺织领域。

## 产品规格：

FDY: 150D, 75D, 50D

DTY: 150D,100D,75D

可根据客户需求定制。

## 注意事项：

本产品为特殊商品，具有一定寿命，加工及后处理时需要注意下列事宜：

1.避免高温处理，长时间高温处理会对光变产品产生影响;

2.建议将温度控制在140℃以下，时间20分钟以内进行后整处理;

3. 避免强酸强碱处理，建议可使用的PH值6-8；

4.不建议染色处理。

## 包装及储存方法

1. 避光储存，包装材料建议选择不透光的材料，以防止光变产品暴露在紫外线下而缩短其寿命；

2.保存温度最好控制在0-40℃;

Photochromic fiber is a polyester fiber that can change color under sunlight or ultraviolet light.

## APPLICATION SCOPE:

Clothes, fabric, shoes and so on.

## PRODUCT SPECIFICATIONS:

FDY: 150D, 75D, 50D

DTY: 150D, 100D, 75D

Also customizable according to the requirement.

## CAUTIONS:

The photochromic fiber is the special product and has a certain life-span. Please pay attention to the following matters.

1. Avoid the high-temperature treatment, which will affect the product's functionality

2. For post-processing, the suggested temperature is below 140°C, and the time is within 20 mins after processing

3. Avoid the strong acid and alkali treatment, the suggested pH value is 6 to 8.

4. Avoid the dyeing process.

## CAUTIONS FOR PACKAGING AND STORAGE

用于油黑及涂料：2 ~ 20 WT%

用于注塑：0.2~2 WT%

## 产品说明：

温变粉的平均粒径为5±1μm，使用简单，适用性广，可用于油墨、涂料、注塑等多个领域。

## 注意事项：

请勿长时间浸泡在高极性溶剂中，避免由于渗透作用影响胶囊内部环境，从而影响变色性能。若有高温工序，建议加工温度不高于220℃，加工时间不超过30分钟。添加热稳定剂、降低加工温度或缩短高温加工时间均有助于提升最终产品的变色性能。建议客户使用前先进行小试，若有进一步技术支持需要，请联系我们。
