# Reverse Thermochromic Microcapsule Reverse Thermochromic Series

Reverse thermochromic microcapsule is a product with thermochromic dye, which reveals its color according to increasing temperature. When the heat is applied, the color appears. When it cools again, the color disappears.

# Photochromic Microcapsule Chameleon UVC Series

This is a microcapsule product with a photochromic dye which changes its color by light. The photochromic dye causes color change reversibly by light. When it is exposed to ultraviolet rays (sunlight), it causes color formation, and when a light is blocked, it has its original color.
