# Introduction of Super Absorbent Polymer (SAP) HySorb®N7059

## HySorb®N7059

Product description: HySorb®N7059 is a super-absorbent polymer with high Centrifuge Retention Capacity and high Absorption Against Pressure.

Features: The hygiene products which adopts HySorb®N7059 offer high saline water retention, strong absorption, long-lasting dryness, and strong liquid retention, etc.

Applicable scope: Hygiene care products such as baby diapers, adult incontinence products, and female hygiene products, can be used in absorbed paper cores, as well as traditional core products.
