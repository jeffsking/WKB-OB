# 01 CPE series

## APPLICATIONS

sanitany napkins, diapers, etc

Our CPE series product line includes ultra-frosted series, fantastic-color series (first in China), metallized ink printing series, pearl ink printing series, registered printing series, dazzling glitter series, drawing technology series, laser series, and many other customized high-tech products. We can produce products between 13 gsm to 90 gsm, and our flexography printing machine can support up to six colors. The CD tension can reach over 7N and the MD tension can reach over 10N with registration accuracy 0.5mm. In terms of patterns, we have frosted pattern, fine patterns and middle-sized patterns. The flm stiffness can be adjusted accordingly to adapt to the client's machines. The pitch length can be controlled within +/-2 mm. The products can be laminated with adhesive-bonded fabric and air-through thermal-bonded nonwoven.

# 02 Breathable film series

## APPLICATIONS

sanitary napkins, baby diapers, adult diopers, maternal towels, etc.

Our PE breathable film series are specialized for hygienic film materials include diapers and sanitary napkins. Our printing machine can print 6 colors in the front side and 2 colors in the back side of film plus urine discolor feature. The max printing color is up to 10 colors. The products con be laminated with adhesive-bonded fabric and air-through thermal-bonded norwovens.

For diapers: The registered printing film basis weight is between 13g to 17g. The CD tension can reach over 2.3N, and the MD tension can reach over 7N. The pitch length can be controlled witin +/-2 mm with registration accuracy 0.5mm. WVTR 1800 -4000 g/24H/sqm.

For Sanitary Napkins: film basis weight is between 28g to 35g. The CD tension can reach over 3N. We have embossing and plain pattern styles. The embossing is clear and uniform.
