# Organic growth of 2.0% in the first half of 2026

## Adjusted EBITDA margin of 21.8 %

Sequential improvement of organic growth supported by market success in the North America and Asia/Pacific regions

## Acceleration of the ONE SYM Transformation program as the driver of profitable organic growth

## Forecast for fiscal year 2026 confirmed:

· Organic sales growth of 2 % to 4 %

· Adjusted EBITDA margin of 21.5 % to 22.5 %

· Adjusted business free cash flow as a percentage of sales of more than 14%

Symrise AG, a leading global supplier of fragrances, flavors, cosmetic active ingredients and raw materials, as well as functional ingredients, successfully continued implementing the ONE SYM Transformation program in the first half of 2026.

Symrise achieved organic sales growth of 2.0% in the first half of 2026. Taking into account portfolio and currency translation effects, sales amounted to EUR 2,539 million, 0.6% lower than in the same period the previous year (H1 2025: EUR 2,554 million).

Adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization (EBITDA) amounted to EUR 553 million, almost matching the previous year's figure of EUR 564 million. Profitability (adjusted EBITDA margin) amounted to 21.8%, which was slightly lower than the previous year's figure of 22.1%, mainly due to targeted investments in the ONE SYM Transformation program and ongoing geopolitical tension in the Middle East.

The consolidated net income attributable to the shareholders of Symrise AG for the first six months amounted to EUR 265 million, which was 1.2 % below the previous year's figure of EUR 268 million.

# About
Symrise

Symrise is a global supplier of fragrances and flavors, cosmetic active ingredients as well as functional ingredients. The customers of Symrise include large multinational groups as well as important regional and local manufacturers of food, beverages, pet food, perfumes, cosmetics, personal care products, cleaning products and laundry detergents.

With sales of around EUR 4.9 billion in the 2025 financial year, the company is one of the world's leading providers. Headquartered in Holzminden, Germany, the Group is represented by more than 100 companies in Europe, Africa, the Middle East, Asia, the United States and Latin America.

Symrise works with its customers to develop new ideas and market-ready concepts for products that form an indispensable part of everyday life. Economic success and corporate responsibility are inextricably linked as part of this process. Symrise – always inspiring more...

## Business environment

Despite robust development in previous years, global growth remains below the long-term historical average. Whereas the world economy expanded by 3.4 % in 2025, the International Monetary Fund (IMF) is forecasting growth of 3.1 % for 2026 and 3.2 % for 2027. Economic development is being impacted particularly by geopolitical tensions in the Middle East, higher raw material prices, rising inflation forecasts and increasing geopolitical and trade policy fragmentation. At the same time, investment in future technologies – especially artificial intelligence – and supportive financial conditions are having a stabilizing effect. According to IMF estimates, global inflation rates will be 4.4 % in 2026 and 3.7 % in 2027.

Growth forecasts for the industrialized countries remain cautious at 1.8%. The IMF anticipates economic growth of 1.1% in the Eurozone, while the USA will continue to record comparatively higher growth of 2.3%. With a growth rate of 0.8% in 2026, Germany is expected to underperform the Eurozone. In this connection, economic performance will be influenced by greater uncertainty in respect of energy prices, geopolitical risks and global trade relations.

The emerging and developing countries remain the drivers of global economic expansion and the IMF is forecasting growth of 3.9 % for this group of countries in 2026. Despite robust growth overall, the outlook varies from region to region. Those economies that are particularly dependent on raw material imports remain vulnerable to rising energy prices and geopolitical upheaval.

Overall, there is a continued assumption that global growth will remain moderate. However, the forecasts are associated with substantial downward risks, especially in the event of a prolonged or broader escalation of the Middle East conflict, increasing geopolitical fragmentation, renewed trade tensions or a reassessment of the anticipated effects of artificial intelligence on productivity.

Interim Group Management Report

Symrise has a proven and stable business model with comparatively low risk content. The Group is broadly diversified across all stages of the value chain – from the procurement of raw materials on the basis of long-term agreements to on-site production in the sales markets and a global customer structure. Parts of the product portfolio serve to meet basic needs. Our group of companies is therefore well-equipped to deal with the numerous risks currently affecting the environment and is in a position to quickly and systematically exploit business opportunities as they arise.

Symrise AG continued on its profitable growth path in the first half of the 2026 fiscal year. Despite the persistently challenging global environment, Symrise was able to grow sales organically while maintaining profitability at an almost constant level.

In the first half of 2026, the Symrise Group achieved organic sales growth of 2.0 %. Taking into account portfolio and currency translation effects, reported sales growth was down 0.6 % from the previous year.

In the first half of 2026, the Taste, Nutrition & Health segment achieved organic sales growth of 3.2 %. Taking into account portfolio and currency translation effects, the segment's sales in the reporting currency amounted to EUR 1,530 million and were thus 0.2 % above the previous year's figure (H1 2025: EUR 1,527 million).

The Food & Beverage division achieved mid single-digit organic growth in the first half of 2026. Demand for beverage flavorings delivered mid single-digit organic growth. The Naturals business unit and the business unit for savory products posted high single-digit organic growth rates. In the Food & Beverage division, the North America and Asia/Pacific regions posted low double-digit percentage growth.

# Operating result

To ensure better comparability, the following information references key financial figures adjusted for one-time effects.$^{1}$ Despite geopolitical tensions and a challenging market environment, earnings performance in the first half of 2026 was satisfactory overall. At Group level, gross profit almost matched the previous year's level at EUR 1,049 million, a decrease of -0.8 % (H1 2025: EUR 1,057 million). The gross margin of 41.3 % was also at the previous year's level (H1 2025: 41.4 %). The cost of goods sold of EUR 1,490 million was 0.4 % below the previous year's figure (H1 2025: EUR 1,497 million). Compared to the previous year, selling and marketing expenses decreased by 2.8 %. R&D expenses were EUR 144 million, 1.6 % above the previous year's figure. At 5.7 %, the R&D ratio was almost level with the figure for the first half of 2025 (5.5 %). Administration expenses totaled EUR 164 million and were 0.8 % higher than in the previous year (H1 2025: EUR 163 million). Other operating income and expenses remained nearly constant compared to the previous year.

In the first six months of 2026, the Group generated adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization (EBITDA) of EUR 553 million. As a result, EBITDA for the current fiscal year was EUR 11 million, or 1.9% lower than in the same period the previous year (H1 2025: EUR 564 million). The Group's profitability (adjusted EBITDA margin) amounted to 21.8%, a decrease of 0.3 percentage points compared to the previous year (H1 2025: 22.1%). Operational improvements resulting from profitable sales and efficiency measures were offset by impacts from higher material and logistics costs in connection with the ongoing geopolitical tension in the Middle East.

In the first six months of the current year, adjusted earnings before interest and taxes (EBIT) amounted to EUR 412 million (H1 2025: EUR 413 million). Thus, adjusted EBIT in the current fiscal year is 0.1% below the previous year's figure. The adjusted EBIT margin of 16.2% stands at the same level as the previous year.

# Alternative performance indicators

Symrise uses alternative performance indicators in addition to the disclosures required by the applicable accounting standards. These indicators serve to ensure the periodical and industry-related comparability of the company's performance. They are also used for internal management purposes and for the external assessment of business performance by investors and analysts.

Symrise formalized its system of adjustments as of the end of fiscal year 2025. The system incorporates adjustments to earnings or cash flow for one-time effects of a non-operational and non-recurring nature that impact the performance analysis. These one-time effects mainly relate to portfolio changes (for example, acquisitions and disposals), restructuring and optimization projects and extraordinary business transactions.

A more detailed description of the alternative performance indicators and the system of adjustments can be found in the Corporate Report 2025.

In the first half of the 2026 fiscal year, adjustments were made to the aforementioned indicators for the following one-time effects:

· Costs associated with the antitrust investigation (around EUR 2 million).

· Loss and impairment, the reversal of provisions and consulting costs associated with the sale and discontinuation of businesses and strategic considerations in the context of portfolio optimization of the aqua and terpene businesses (around EUR -1 million)

· Costs in connection with the ONE SYM transformation in the strategic areas of sourcing, productivity, portfolio and global asset management (around EUR 9 million)

# Opportunities and risk report

No risks in accordance with Section 91 (2) of the German Stock Corporation Act (AktG) that could endanger the continued existence of the Symrise Group can be identified at present.

A detailed discussion of the opportunities and risks as well as a description of the risk management system can be found in the Group management report 2025 (see the Corporate Report 2025 on pages 60 et seq.). The statements made there remain essentially unchanged.

The business activities of the two Russian Symrise Group companies must be viewed on a differentiated basis. Whereas the economic activities of the Rogovo plant, close to Moscow, can still be continued, especially to fulfill international contracts, the plant in Shebekino, close to the border with Ukraine, has been affected repeatedly and indirectly by the conflict. Ongoing fighting in Russia's Belgorod region means that there is a risk that the region may be evacuated repeatedly and temporarily by the government of the Russian Federation. This may result in temporary disruptions to production. It is currently not possible to predict with any certainty the extent to which the further development of the conflict could impact business activities at the Shebekino plant. Symrise is monitoring the situation continuously and will take suitable action if the need arises.

Since March 2023, a number of antitrust authorities (including the European Commission, WEKO in Switzerland, the US Department of Justice, SAMR in China, CCI in India and CMA in the UK) have launched preliminary investigations against Symrise and its three largest competitors – IFF, Givaudan and DSM-Firmenich – in respect of antitrust violations. Symrise is cooperating fully and has provided all authorities with any documents they have requested, which are the same in most cases. In Symrise's opinion, there is still no indication of unlawful conduct by Symrise. To date, none of the investigating authorities has made a formal request for information, communicated any objections or laid any official charges. By contrast, the CMA in the UK suspended its investigation of Symrise in May 2025. However, it is continuing to investigate IFF, Givaudan and DSM-Firmenich. Depending on the further development of events, the continuing and dynamic conflicts in the Middle East could impact Symrise's supply chains if attacks resume on cargo ships transiting the Horn of Africa and the Red Sea. This would lead to the continued use of alternative shipping routes and prolong the general delivery times of raw materials and goods to Europe. In this case, shipping companies would pass their additional costs on to their customers. Moreover, the renewed closure of the Strait of Hormuz by Iran would drive up global energy market costs once more. In addition, if the conflict in the region reignites, it may no longer be possible to supply customers and markets there. This could result in a temporary loss of customers in the affected regions if local suppliers were able to ensure supplies. Alternative land-based supply routes might only offset this in part. In this case, Symrise would be impacted by the resulting effects because of its global presence. Whether this scenario materializes is largely dependent on the future outcomes of negotiations between the parties in the conflict.

· Organic sales growth for the full year is expected to be in a corridor of 2 % to 4 %.

· The adjusted EBITDA margin is predicted to be in a corridor of 21.5 % to 22.5 %.

· The goal remains to achieve business free cash flow as a percentage of sales of more than 14 %.

### Share buyback program from February 1 to October 31, 2026

In January 2026, Symrise announced a share buyback program of up to EUR 400.0 million that would be active between February 1 and October 31, 2026, with the purpose of redeeming shares. Of the 139,772,054 no-par-value bearer shares with a calculated nominal value of EUR 1.00 per share outstanding as of January 1, 2026, 2,591,875 treasury shares were purchased for a total price of EUR 192.2 million in the period from February 1, 2026, to June 30, 2026. This represents 1.9 % of the share capital. The shares were purchased at a weighted average price of EUR 74.16 per share. After deduction of the treasury shares acquired, 137,180,179 no-par-value bearer shares remain outstanding as of June 30, 2026. As of the reporting date, Symrise holds 2,591,875 treasury shares.

## Investment in Swedencare AB, Malmö, Sweden

In the context of continuously monitoring the recoverability of the investment in Swedencare AB, Malmö, Sweden, an impairment test was performed again as of June 30, 2026, by discounting the estimated future cash flows. ## Divestment of the Aqua Feed business in Ecuador

In 2024, in the context of portfolio optimization and a stronger focus on profitability, the Executive Board of Symrise AG decided to divest the Aqua Feed line of additives for aquafeed products that was assigned to the Taste, Nutrition & Health segment. With the closing of the transaction on June 24, 2026 (closing date), after approval by the Ecuadororean antitrust authorities, the non-current assets that were connected with the already closed Aqua Feed business of the entity Proteinas Del Ecuador Ecuaprotein SA, Durán, Ecuador, and the inventories were sold in an asset deal for a price of USD 3.45 million, respectively EUR 3.0 million. Based on the impairment losses recognized in previous fiscal years, the sale of the assets resulted in no substantial earnings effect in the fiscal year 2026.
