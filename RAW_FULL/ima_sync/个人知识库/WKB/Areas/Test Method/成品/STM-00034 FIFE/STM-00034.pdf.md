# 1.0 PURPOSE

1.1 This test is used to measure a diaper, training pant, youth pant, POISE® Pad, DEPEND® Fitted Brief (FB), and Guards for Men (GFM) absorbency time, to the nearest 0.01 second, and the flowback, to the nearest 0.01 gram.

1.2 The absorbency time is measured using a stopwatch and visually estimating the length of time required to absorb three individual testing fluid insults. A layer of pre-weighed blotter paper is placed under the product to collect and quantify any testing fluid that may flow over the side of the product. The flowback test measures the amount of testing fluid that emerges from the body side of the product after it has absorbed two testing fluid insults and pressure has been applied.

1.3 The higher the flowback value, the less fluid the product can retain under pressure.

# 1.4 Definitions

## 1.4.1 flowback

The amount of testing fluid that is forced back through the body side of a fluid-insulted product when it is subjected to a specified pressure.

## 1.4.2 Fluid Intake Flowback Evaluation (FIFE)

Fluid intake is the amount of time it takes for a predetermined amount of testing fluid to be absorbed into the specimen. The flowback evaluation is the amount of testing fluid that is forced out of the specimen at a 0.5 pound-force per square inch (psi) loading.

## 1.4.3 testing fluid insult

Predetermined amount of liquid that is poured or dispensed onto the product.

# 10.0 SAFETY

10.1 Training is essential for the proper execution of this method.

10.2 Prior to performing any test method for the first time, and in support of good laboratory practices, each location must conduct a Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Needs Assessment to determine if protective equipment is necessary to ensure the safety of personnel. Each facility's safety and hygiene resource should be able to assist in this assessment. Contact the Corporate Occupational Safety and Hygiene team if additional help is needed.

10.3 Safety precautions are not intended to be all-inclusive. It is the user's responsibility to use safe and proper techniques in handling the materials and apparatus associated with this test method.

10.4 When using a cutting device, use proper hand placement to avoid injury.

10.5 The Saturated Capacity (SAT CAP) tester is designed for testing with a 0.5 psi loading. Exceeding a 2.0 psi loading may cause the equipment to implode (collapse inward).

10.6 Refer to the appropriate Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) for the proper handling of the chemicals associated with this test method. Follow the safety requirements stated on the Hazardous Material Identification System (HMIS) label for each chemical.

10.7 Do not allow water to collect on the floor as it is a slip hazard.

10.8 It is strongly recommended that all test instruments operating near any testing fluid and using an alternating current power source should be connected to a ground fault interrupter (GFI) outlet.
